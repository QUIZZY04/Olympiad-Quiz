IMO Syllabus for Class 6 (2025-2026)

A comprehensive breakdown of chapters and topics required to conquer the SOF International Mathematics Olympiad.

Section-wise Breakdown

The exam consists of 50 questions worth 60 marks, divided into 4 main sections.

Section Topics Covered Questions
1. Logical Reasoning Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning, Series, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Coding-Decoding. 15
2. Mathematical Reasoning Knowing our Numbers, Whole Numbers, Playing with Numbers, Basic Geometrical Ideas, Integers, Fractions, Decimals, Data Handling, Mensuration, Algebra, Ratio and Proportion. 20
3. Everyday Mathematics Syllabus of Section 2 applied to real-world word problems. 10
4. Achievers Section Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions from Section 2 syllabus. 5
