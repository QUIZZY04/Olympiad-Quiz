Chapter 11: Air Around Us

NCERT Science (Classic) (Class 6) — Atmosphere & Composition of Air (Nitrogen 78%, Oxygen 21%, Carbon Dioxide 0.04%, Water Vapour & Dust), Importance of Oxygen for Respiration & Combustion, Exchange of Gases in Plants & Animals, Wind Energy | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Atmosphere: Thick blanket of air surrounding Earth, extending up to hundreds of kilometres.
  • Composition of Air: Nitrogen (~78%), Oxygen (~21%), Carbon dioxide, Noble gases, Water vapour, and Smoke/Dust particles (~1%).
  • Oxygen: Supports burning and respiration. Nitrogen: Does not support burning.
  • Respiration in Organisms: Earthworms (breathe through skin), Fish (gills breathe dissolved oxygen), Plants (stomata exchange gases).
  • Carbon Cycle: Plants consume CO2 and release O2 during photosynthesis; animals consume O2 and exhale CO2 during respiration.
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
What is the composition of air? Which gas in the atmosphere is essential for respiration and burning? Describe an experiment showing that air contains oxygen.
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Atmospheric Composition & Oxygen Experiment:
• Composition: Nitrogen (78%), Oxygen (21%), Carbon Dioxide (0.04%), Argon, Water vapour, and Smoke/dust particles (remaining ~1%).
• Essential Gas: Oxygen (O₂) is essential for both respiration and combustion.
• Inverted Tumbler Experiment:
1. Fix a lighted candle in a trough filled with coloured water.
2. Cover the burning candle with an inverted glass tumbler.
3. Observation: After a few moments, the candle flame flickers and goes out, and water rises to fill approximately 1/5th of the tumbler's volume.
4. Conclusion: Oxygen occupied 1/5th (20%) of the air inside the tumbler; once consumed by combustion, burning ceased and water rose to occupy the vacuum.

Q2
How do plants and animals help each other in the exchange of gases in the atmosphere? Why do mountaineers carry oxygen cylinders?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Ecological Gas Balance & High Altitude:
• Mutual Gas Exchange:
1. Animals consume oxygen during respiration and exhale carbon dioxide.
2. Green plants absorb carbon dioxide from air to prepare food by photosynthesis in sunlight and release oxygen back into the atmosphere.
3. Because plants produce far more oxygen during photosynthesis than they consume in respiration, the atmospheric balance between O₂ and CO₂ is maintained.
• Mountaineers: As altitude increases, atmospheric pressure drops and air becomes progressively thinner with lower oxygen density. To prevent altitude sickness and hypoxia, mountaineers carry oxygen cylinders.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Photosynthesis vs Respiration ecological balance2 Marks
Thin air and low oxygen density at high altitude1 Mark
CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Environmental Science: Urban Smog & Air Quality Index (AQI):
During winter months in metropolitan cities, a thick haze of smog blankets the city. (a) What two components combine to form smog? (b) Why are tall chimneys installed in factories with electrostatic precipitators? (c) Why do traffic police officers stationed at busy crossroads wear respirators?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Smog Composition: Smoke (unburnt carbon particles, sulfur dioxide) combined with Fog (condensed water droplets near ground level).
(b) Tall Chimneys: Discharge hot toxic gases and smoke high into the upper atmosphere away from human settlements, while electrostatic precipitators filter out 99% of particulate soot.
(c) Traffic Police Respirators: High concentration of vehicular exhaust contains toxic Carbon Monoxide (CO), nitrogen oxides, and fine PM2.5 particles that enter deep into pulmonary lung alveoli, causing respiratory disorders.
