Chapter 7: Motion and Measurement of Distances

NCERT Science (Classic) (Class 6) — Story of Transport, Standard Units of Measurement, International System of Units (SI Units), Measuring Length with Ruler, Types of Motion (Rectilinear, Circular, Periodic, Rotational) | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Standard Unit: A unit of measurement that has a fixed value and does not change from person to person.
  • SI Units: Length (Metre, m), Mass (Kilogram, kg), Time (Second, s). 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 m = 100 cm; 1 cm = 10 mm.
  • Rectilinear Motion: Motion along a straight line (March-past of soldiers, falling apple).
  • Circular Motion: Motion along a circular path where distance from center remains constant (Hands of clock, blades of fan).
  • Periodic Motion: Motion that repeats itself at regular intervals of time (Pendulum, swing, strings of guitar).
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Why can a pace or a footstep not be used as a standard unit of length? What is the SI unit of length?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Standard Units of Measurement:
• Footstep Variation: The length of a footstep, handspan, or cubit varies from person to person based on physical stature. If footsteps were used, the measured distance would differ depending on who measured it, leading to commercial confusion.
• SI Unit: The International System of Units (SI unit) of length is the Metre (m).
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Explanation of individual human variability1.5 Marks
Stating SI unit = Metre (m)1.5 Marks
Q2
Identify the type of motion in each case: (a) Motion of a child on a swing. (b) Motion of the needle of a sewing machine. (c) Motion of a vehicle on a straight road. (d) Motion of the wheel of a moving bicycle.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Classification of Motion:
• (a) Motion of a child on a swing: Periodic motion (and oscillatory motion).
• (b) Motion of needle of sewing machine: Periodic motion.
• (c) Motion of vehicle on straight road: Rectilinear motion.
• (d) Wheel of moving bicycle: Exhibits both Rotational motion (about its axle) and Rectilinear motion (advancing forward on road).
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Correct types for (a), (b), (c)1.5 Marks
Dual motion identification for bicycle wheel1.5 Marks
CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Precision Metrology: Industrial Measurement Accuracy:
While measuring the length of a metal rod with a broken ruler whose zero mark is damaged, a student aligns the end with the 2.5 cm mark and reads the other end at 18.2 cm. (a) What is the true length of the rod? (b) State two essential precautions to avoid parallax error while reading a ruler.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) True Length: Actual Length = Final Reading - Initial Reading = 18.2 cm - 2.5 cm = 15.7 cm.
(b) Precautions:
1. Keep the eye positioned vertically directly above the point where the measurement is taken.
2. Place the ruler strictly parallel and in contact along the edge of the object being measured.
