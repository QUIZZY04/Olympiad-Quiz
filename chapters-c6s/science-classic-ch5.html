Chapter 5: Body Movements

NCERT Science (Classic) (Class 6) — Human Skeleton & Cartilage, Joint Mechanics (Ball and Socket, Hinge, Pivotal, Fixed Joints), Muscles & Contraction, Locomotion in Animals (Earthworm, Snail, Cockroach, Birds, Fish, Snakes) | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Ball and Socket Joint: Allows movement in all directions (Shoulder and Hip joints).
  • Pivotal Joint: Cylindrical bone rotates in a ring (Neck joint where head joins neck).
  • Hinge Joint: Allows movement only back and forth like a door hinge (Elbow and Knee).
  • Fixed Joint: Bones cannot move (Cranium / upper jaw in skull).
  • Animal Locomotion: Earthworm (hydrostatic skeleton & bristles), Snail (muscular foot), Fish (streamlined body & caudal fin), Snake (curving loops).
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Fill in the blanks: (a) The joint where our neck joins the head is a _____ joint. (b) The joint at the elbow is a _____ joint. (c) The contraction of the _____ pulls the bones during movement. (d) The skull bones are joined by _____ joints.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Skeletal System Blanks:
• (a) The joint where our neck joins the head is a pivotal joint.
• (b) The joint at the elbow is a hinge joint.
• (c) The contraction of the muscle pulls the bones during movement.
• (d) The skull bones are joined by fixed joints.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Correct answers: Pivotal, Hinge, Muscle, Fixed3 Marks
Q2
Explain how a fish moves in water. How does a snake slither forward?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Locomotion in Fish and Snake:
• Fish: Possesses a streamlined body shape which minimizes water resistance. During swimming, muscles on one side of the body contract, bending the body into a curve while the tail swings to the opposite side. The sudden jerk of the tail fin (caudal fin) propels the fish forward.
• Snake: Has a long backbone and interconnected thin muscles. The snake's body curves into multiple side-to-side loops. Each loop presses against the ground to give a forward push, moving the snake forward rapidly in a wave-like slithering motion.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Streamlined shape and caudal fin mechanism of fish1.5 Marks
Backbone and lateral loop mechanics of snake1.5 Marks
CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Biomechanics & Sports Science: Knee Joint Rehabilitation:
An athlete suffers an injury to the knee ligament. (a) What type of joint is the knee, and what planes of motion does it allow? (b) Explain why muscles always work in antagonistic pairs (such as biceps and triceps) to move a limb.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Knee Joint: A hinge joint that allows movement restricted to one plane (flexion and extension, like opening and closing a door).
(b) Antagonistic Muscle Pairs: A muscle can only pull; it cannot push. Therefore, two muscles are required to move a bone in opposite directions: when one muscle contracts to pull the bone, the partner muscle relaxes; to return the bone, the partner contracts while the first relaxes.
