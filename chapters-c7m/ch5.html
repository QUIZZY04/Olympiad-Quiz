📌 Key Concepts & Angle Properties Summary
  • Complementary Angles: Two angles whose sum of measures equals 90°. Each angle is called the complement of the other.
  • Supplementary Angles: Two angles whose sum of measures equals 180°. Each angle is called the supplement of the other.
  • Adjacent Angles: Angles that have a common vertex, a common arm, and non-common arms on opposite sides of the common arm.
  • Linear Pair: A pair of adjacent angles whose non-common sides form opposite rays (a straight line). Sum = 180°.
  • Vertically Opposite Angles: When two lines intersect at a point, vertically opposite angles are formed and are always equal.
  • Parallel Lines and Transversal: When two parallel lines l ∥ m are cut by a transversal t:
    • Corresponding angles are equal (∠ 1 = ∠ 5, etc.).
    • Alternate interior angles are equal (∠ 3 = ∠ 5, ∠ 4 = ∠ 6).
    • Alternate exterior angles are equal (∠ 1 = ∠ 7, ∠ 2 = ∠ 8).
    • Co-interior angles (interior angles on same side of transversal) are supplementary (∠ 4 + ∠ 5 = 180°).
Exercise 5.1
Q1
Find the complement of each of the following angles:
(i) 20°
(ii) 63°
(iii) 57°
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

Two angles are complementary if their sum is 90°. The complement of θ = 90° − θ.

(i) Complement of 20°:
= 90° − 20° = 70°.
(ii) Complement of 63°:
= 90° − 63° = 27°.
(iii) Complement of 57°:
= 90° − 57° = 33°.

Q4
(a) Find the angle which is equal to its complement.
(b) Find the angle which is equal to its supplement.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) Angle equal to its complement:
Let the angle be x.
Its complement is 90° − x.
According to question: x = 90° − x ⇒ 2x = 90° ⇒ x = 45°.
(b) Angle equal to its supplement:
Let the angle be y.
Its supplement is 180° − y.
According to question: y = 180° − y ⇒ 2y = 180° ⇒ y = 90°.

Q5
Two lines intersect at a point. If one angle is 55°, find the values of all other angles x, y and z.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Vertically opposite angle:
Angle x is vertically opposite to 55°.
Therefore, x = 55°.
Step 2: Linear pair:
Angle y and 55° form a linear pair on a straight line:
y + 55° = 180° ⇒ y = 180° − 55° = 125°.
Step 3: Vertically opposite to y:
Angle z is vertically opposite to y:
z = y = 125°.

Final Answer: x = 55°, y = 125°, z = 125°.

Q2
Find the value of x in each of the following figures if l ∥ m:
(i) One angle is 110° and alternate interior to x with linear pair.
(ii) Co-interior angles are (2x)° and (3x)°.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Figure with angle 110°:
The angle adjacent to 110° on the straight line is: 180° − 110° = 70°.
Since l ∥ m, angle x is corresponding (or alternate) to this angle:
x = 180° − 110° = 70°.
(ii) Co-interior angles 2x and 3x:
Interior angles on the same side of a transversal are supplementary:
2x + 3x = 180° ⇒ 5x = 180° ⇒ x = 180°/5 = 36°.
The angles are 2(36°) = 72° and 3(36°) = 108°.

Q7
Can two angles be supplementary if both of them are:
(i) acute?
(ii) obtuse?
(iii) right?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) No: If both angles are acute (< 90°), their sum is always strictly less than 90° + 90° = 180°.
(ii) No: If both angles are obtuse (> 90°), their sum is always strictly greater than 90° + 90° = 180°.
(iii) Yes: If both angles are right angles (90° each), their sum is 90° + 90° = 180°.

2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

In the following figure, is ∠1 adjacent to ∠2? Give reasons.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
No, ∠1 is not adjacent to ∠2.

Here, ∠1 and ∠2 do not have a common vertex (their vertices are at two completely different points).

HOTS: Ratio of Supplementary Angles
Problem: Two supplementary angles are in the ratio 4 : 5. Find the measure of both angles.

Let the common ratio multiplier be x.
The two angles are 4x and 5x.
Since they are supplementary:
4x + 5x = 180° ⇒ 9x = 180° ⇒ x = 180°/9 = 20°.
First angle = 4 × 20° = 80°.
Second angle = 5 × 20° = 100°.
Check: 80° + 100° = 180° (verified).

⚡ Assertion & Reason
Assertion (A): If two parallel lines are intersected by a transversal, then each pair of alternate interior angles are equal.
Reason (R): If two parallel lines are intersected by a transversal, the interior angles on the same side of the transversal are supplementary.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. (b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Answer: (b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
Explanation: Both statements are fundamental, correct geometric properties of parallel lines and transversals, but Reason (R) states the co-interior angle property, which is not the explanation for why alternate interior angles are equal.
