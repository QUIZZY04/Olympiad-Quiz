Chapter 9: Perimeter and Area

NCERT Exercise 9.1 to 9.2 — Complete Solutions as per CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Mensuration Formulas Summary
  • Square & Rectangle:
    • Perimeter of square = 4 × side; Area of square = side².
    • Perimeter of rectangle = 2(l + b); Area of rectangle = l × b.
  • Area of Parallelogram: Area = Base × Height = b × h
  • Area of Triangle: Area = 1/2 × Base × Height = 1/2 b h (Every triangle can be viewed as half of a parallelogram with the same base and height).
  • Circle:
    • Circumference = 2π r = π d (where π ≈ 22/7 or 3.14).
    • Area of circle = π r².
    • Area of circular ring / path = π (R² - r²) = π(R + r)(R - r).
  • Unit Conversions: 1 cm² = 100 mm², 1 m² = 10,000 cm², 1 hectare = 10,000 m².

Q3
ΔABC is right angled at A. AD is perpendicular to BC. If AB = 5 cm, BC = 13 cm and AC = 12 cm, find the area of ΔABC. Also find the length of AD.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Calculate Area of ΔABC using legs AB and AC:
Since right angle is at A, legs AB = 5 cm and AC = 12 cm serve as base and height.
Area = 1/2 × AB × AC = 1/2 × 5 × 12 = 5 × 6 = 30 cm².
Step 2: Find altitude AD using hypotenuse BC as base:
Area = 1/2 × BC × AD
30 = 1/2 × 13 × AD ⇒ AD = (30 × 2)/13 = 60/13 = 4 8/13 cm ≈ 4.62 cm.

Conclusion: Area of ΔABC is 30 cm² and length of AD is 60/13 cm (4.62 cm).

Q4
From a circular sheet of radius 4 cm, a circle of radius 3 cm is removed. Find the area of the remaining sheet. (Take π = 3.14)
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Area of outer circular sheet (R = 4 cm):
Area₁ = π R² = 3.14 × 4² = 3.14 × 16 = 50.24 cm².
Step 2: Area of removed circle (r = 3 cm):
Area₂ = π r² = 3.14 × 3² = 3.14 × 9 = 28.26 cm².
Step 3: Area of remaining sheet:
Area = Area₁ − Area₂ = 50.24 − 28.26 = 21.98 cm².

Conclusion: The area of the remaining sheet is 21.98 cm².

Q13
A circular flower bed is surrounded by a path 4 m wide. The diameter of the flower bed is 66 m. What is the area of this path? (π = 3.14)
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Diameter of circular flower bed = 66 m.
Radius of flower bed (r) = 66 / 2 = 33\text{ m}.
Width of path = 4 m.
Radius of flower bed including path (R) = 33 + 4 = 37\text{ m}.

Area of the path = \pi R^2 − \pi r^2 = \pi (R^2 − r^2)
= 3.14 × (37^2 − 33^2)
Using a^2 − b^2 = (a − b)(a + b):
= 3.14 × (37 − 33)(37 + 33) = 3.14 × 4 × 70 = 3.14 × 280 = 879.2 m².

HOTS: Wire Rebent from Square to Rectangle
Problem: A wire is in the shape of a square of side 10 cm. If the wire is rebent into a rectangle of length 12 cm, find its breadth. Which figure encloses more area and by how much?

1. Length of wire (Perimeter):
Perimeter of square = 4 × side = 4 × 10 = 40 cm.
When rebent into a rectangle, perimeter is still 40 cm:
2(l + b) = 40 ⇒ 2(12 + b) = 40 ⇒ 12 + b = 20 ⇒ b = 20 − 12 = 8 cm.

2. Compare Areas:
Area of square = side² = 10 × 10 = 100 cm².
Area of rectangle = l × b = 12 × 8 = 96 cm².
Difference = 100 − 96 = 4 cm².
Conclusion: The square encloses more area by 4 cm².
