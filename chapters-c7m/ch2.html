📌 Key Concepts & Formulas Summary
  • Types of Fractions:
    • Proper Fraction: Numerator < Denominator (e.g. 3/5). Value < 1.
    • Improper Fraction: Numerator ≥ Denominator (e.g. 7/4). Value ≥ 1. Can be written as Mixed Fraction: 1 3/4.
  • Multiplication of Fractions: (Numerator₁ × Numerator₂)/(Denominator₁ × Denominator₂) The operator 'of' signifies multiplication: 1/2 of 24 = 1/2 × 24 = 12.
  • Reciprocal of a Fraction: The non-zero number obtained by interchanging numerator and denominator. Product with original fraction equals 1: a/b × b/a = 1.
  • Division of Fractions: Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor: a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c.
  • Decimal Operations:
    • Multiplying by 10, 100, 1000: Shift decimal point to the right by as many places as there are zeros.
    • Dividing by 10, 100, 1000: Shift decimal point to the left by as many places as there are zeros.
    • Division by decimal: Multiply both dividend and divisor by suitable powers of 10 to make divisor a whole number.

Q1
Solve the following fractions:
(i) 2 − 3/5
(ii) 4 + 7/8
(iii) 3/5 + 2/7
(iv) 9/11 − 4/15
(v) 7/10 + 2/5 + 3/2
(vi) 2⅔ + 3½
(vii) 8½ − 3⅝
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 2 − 3/5:
= 2/1 − 3/5 = (2 × 5 − 3)/5 = (10 − 3)/5 = 7/5 = 1 2/5.
(ii) 4 + 7/8:
= (4 × 8 + 7)/8 = (32 + 7)/8 = 39/8 = 4 7/8.
(iii) 3/5 + 2/7:
LCM of 5 and 7 is 35.
= (3 × 7 + 2 × 5)/35 = (21 + 10)/35 = 31/35.
(iv) 9/11 − 4/15:
LCM of 11 and 15 is 165.
= (9 × 15 − 4 × 11)/165 = (135 − 44)/165 = 91/165.
(v) 7/10 + 2/5 + 3/2:
LCM of 10, 5, 2 is 10.
= (7 + (2 × 2) + (3 × 5))/10 = (7 + 4 + 15)/10 = 26/10 = 13/5 = 2 3/5.
(vi) 2 2/3 + 3 1/2:
Convert to improper fractions: 8/3 + 7/2. LCM of 3, 2 is 6.
= (8 × 2 + 7 × 3)/6 = (16 + 21)/6 = 37/6 = 6 1/6.
(vii) 8 1/2 − 3 5/8:
Convert to improper fractions: 17/2 − 29/8. LCM of 2, 8 is 8.
= (17 × 4 − 29)/8 = (68 − 29)/8 = 39/8 = 4 7/8.

Q2
Arrange the following in descending order:
(i) 2/9, 2/3, 8/21
(ii) 1/5, 3/7, 7/10
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 2/9, 2/3, 8/21:
LCM of denominators 9, 3, 21 is 63.
Convert each to like fractions:
2/9 = (2 × 7)/63 = 14/63
2/3 = (2 × 21)/63 = 42/63
8/21 = (8 × 3)/63 = 24/63
Comparing numerators: 42 > 24 > 14.
Descending order: 2/3 > 8/21 > 2/9.
(ii) 1/5, 3/7, 7/10:
LCM of 5, 7, 10 is 70.
1/5 = (1 × 14)/70 = 14/70
3/7 = (3 × 10)/70 = 30/70
7/10 = (7 × 7)/70 = 49/70
Comparing numerators: 49 > 30 > 14.
Descending order: 7/10 > 3/7 > 1/5.

Q3
A car runs 16 km using 1 litre of petrol. How much distance will it cover using 2¾ litres of petrol?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Quantity of petrol given:
2 3/4 litres = (2 × 4 + 3)/4 = 11/4 litres.
Step 2: Calculate total distance:
Distance covered in 1 litre = 16 km.
Total distance = 16 × 11/4 = (16 × 11)/4 = 4 × 11 = 44 km.

Conclusion: The car will cover 44 km using 2¾ litres of petrol.

(i) How much water did Vidya drink?
(ii) What fraction of the total quantity of water did Pratap drink?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Total quantity of water in the bottle = 5 litres.
(i) Water consumed by Vidya:
2/5 of 5 litres = (2/5) × 5 = 2 litres.
(ii) Fraction of water consumed by Pratap:

Final Answer: Vidya drank 2 litres of water, and Pratap drank 3/5 of the total quantity of water.

Q4
A rectangular sheet of paper is 12 ½ cm long and 10 ⅔ cm wide. Find its perimeter.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Length of the sheet (l) = 12 \frac{1}{2}\text{ cm} = \frac{25}{2}\text{ cm}.
Breadth of the sheet (b) = 10 \frac{2}{3}\text{ cm} = \frac{32}{3}\text{ cm}.

Perimeter of a rectangle = 2(l + b)
= 2 × \left(\frac{25}{2} + \frac{32}{3}\right)
LCM of 2 and 3 is 6.
= 2 × \left(\frac{25 × 3 + 32 × 2}{6}\right) = 2 × \left(\frac{75 + 64}{6}\right) = 2 × \frac{139}{6} = \frac{139}{3} = 46 \frac{1}{3}\text{ cm}.

Q5
Find the perimeters of (i) ΔABE with sides 5/2 cm, 3 3/5 cm, 2 3/4 cm (ii) the rectangle BCDE in this figure with sides 2 3/4 cm and 7/6 cm. Whose perimeter is greater?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Perimeter of ΔABE:
AB = 5/2\text{ cm}, BE = 2 \frac{3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}\text{ cm}, AE = 3 \frac{3}{5} = \frac{18}{5}\text{ cm}.
Perimeter = \frac{5}{2} + \frac{11}{4} + \frac{18}{5}. LCM(2, 4, 5) = 20.
= \frac{50 + 55 + 72}{20} = \frac{177}{20} = 8 \frac{17}{20}\text{ cm} = 8.85\text{ cm}.

(ii) Perimeter of rectangle BCDE:
Length = 11/4\text{ cm}, Breadth = 7/6\text{ cm}.
Perimeter = 2 × \left(\frac{11}{4} + \frac{7}{6}\right) = 2 × \left(\frac{33 + 14}{12}\right) = 2 × \frac{47}{12} = \frac{47}{6} = 7 \frac{5}{6}\text{ cm} ≈ 7.83\text{ cm}.

Comparison: Since 8.85 > 7.83, the perimeter of ΔABE is greater.

Case Study: Uniform Tailoring
Scenario: A school needs 1.8 metres of cloth to stitch one uniform shirt. A roll of fabric contains 45 metres of cloth. (i) How many complete shirts can be tailored from this roll? (ii) What length of fabric will be left? (iii) If each shirt requires 6 buttons, how many buttons will be needed in total?

(i) Number of shirts:
Number of shirts = (Total cloth)/(Cloth per shirt) = 45 ÷ 1.8 = 450/18 = 25 complete shirts.

(ii) Leftover fabric:
Total used = 25 × 1.8 = 45 m. Leftover = 45 − 45 = 0 metres.

(iii) Total buttons needed:
25 × 6 = 150 buttons.

⚡ HOTS Question
Each side of a regular polygon is 2.5 cm in length. The perimeter of the polygon is 12.5 cm. How many sides does the polygon have?

Let number of sides be n.
In a regular polygon, all sides are equal. Perimeter = n × side.
n × 2.5 = 12.5 ⇒ n = 12.5/2.5 = 125/25 = 5.
Therefore, the polygon has 5 sides (it is a regular pentagon).
