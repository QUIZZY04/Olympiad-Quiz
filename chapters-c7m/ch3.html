📌 Key Concepts & Formulas Summary
  • Data & Range: Range = Highest Value - Lowest Value
  • Arithmetic Mean (Average): Mean = (Sum of all observations)/(Number of observations)
  • Mode: The observation that occurs most frequently in the given data. A data set can have more than one mode (multimodal).
  • Median: The middle observation when data is arranged in ascending or descending order.
    • For odd n: Median = ((n+1)/2)th observation.
  • Bar Graphs: Representation of numbers using bars of uniform width where lengths/heights are proportional to the frequencies. Double bar graphs compare two sets of data simultaneously.
  • Probability (P): Measure of chance of an event happening: P(Event) = (Number of favourable outcomes)/(Total number of possible outcomes) 0 ≤ P ≤ 1. Impossible event = 0, Certain event = 1.

Q1
Find the range of heights of any ten students of your class.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Sample Data (Heights in cm):
Let the heights of 10 students be: 128, 132, 135, 139, 140, 142, 145, 148, 150, 152 cm.
Calculation of Range:
Highest height = 152 cm
Lowest height = 128 cm
Range = Highest height − Lowest height = 152 − 128 = 24 cm.

✅ CBSE Standard Answer

Q3
A cricketer scores the following runs in eight innings: 58, 76, 40, 35, 46, 45, 0, 100. Find the mean score.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Sum of runs:
Total runs = 58 + 76 + 40 + 35 + 46 + 45 + 0 + 100 = 400.
Step 2: Number of innings:
n = 8.
Step 3: Mean score:
Mean = (Total runs)/(Number of innings) = 400/8 = 50.

Conclusion: The mean score of the cricketer is 50 runs.

The scores in mathematics test (out of 25) of 15 students is as follows: 19, 25, 23, 20, 9, 20, 15, 10, 5, 16, 25, 20, 24, 12, 20. Find the mode and median of this data. Are they same?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Arrange data in ascending order:
5, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 19, 20, 20, 20, 20, 23, 24, 25, 25.
Total students, n = 15.
Step 2: Find Mode:
The score 20 occurs 4 times (highest frequency).
Therefore, Mode = 20.
Step 3: Find Median:
Since n = 15 is odd:
Median = ((15+1)/2)th observation = 8th observation = 20.
Conclusion:
Yes, the mode and median are the same (both equal 20).

Q2
There are 6 marbles in a box with numbers from 1 to 6 marked on each of them.
(i) What is the probability of drawing a marble with number 2?
(ii) What is the probability of drawing a marble with number 5?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Sample Space:
Total number of marbles = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6} ⇒ n(S) = 6.
(i) Probability of drawing marble number 2:
Number 2 appears on only 1 marble ⇒ n(E) = 1.
P(Number 2) = n(E)/n(S) = 1/6.
(ii) Probability of drawing marble number 5:
Number 5 appears on only 1 marble ⇒ n(E) = 1.
P(Number 5) = n(E)/n(S) = 1/6.

Case Study: Die Roll Outcomes
Problem: When a standard 6-faced die is thrown once, list the outcomes and find the probability of an event of getting: (i) a prime number, (ii) not a prime number, (iii) a number greater than 5, (iv) a number not greater than 5.

Total possible outcomes = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}, total = 6.

(i) Prime number: Outcomes are {2, 3, 5} (3 outcomes) ⇒ P = 3/6 = 1/2.

(ii) Not a prime number: Outcomes are {1, 4, 6} (3 outcomes) ⇒ P = 3/6 = 1/2. (Note: 1 is neither prime nor composite).

(iii) Number greater than 5: Only {6} (1 outcome) ⇒ P = 1/6.

(iv) Number not greater than 5: Outcomes {1, 2, 3, 4, 5} (5 outcomes) ⇒ P = 5/6.
