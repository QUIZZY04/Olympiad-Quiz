NCERT Exercise 7.1 to 7.2 — Complete Solutions as per CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Commercial Mathematics Formulas
  • Ratio: A comparison of two quantities of the same kind by division (a : b = a/b). Both quantities must have the same unit.
  • Percentage: Ratios with denominator 100.
    • Fraction/Decimal to % ⇒ Multiply by 100%.
    • % to Fraction/Decimal ⇒ Divide by 100.
  • Percentage Change: Percentage Increase / Decrease = (Amount of change)/(Original value) × 100%
  • Profit and Loss:
    • When SP > CP ⇒ Profit = SP - CP; Profit % = Profit/CP × 100%.
    • When CP > SP ⇒ Loss = CP - SP; Loss % = Loss/CP × 100%.
  • Simple Interest: SI = (P × R × T)/100, Total Amount A = P + SI where P = Principal (sum borrowed/invested), R = Rate of interest per annum, T = Time in years.
Exercise 7.1 (Ratios & Percentages)
Q1
Find the ratio of:
(a) ₹5 to 50 paise
(b) 15 kg to 210 g
(c) 9 m to 27 cm
(d) 30 days to 36 hours
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) ₹5 to 50 paise:
Convert to paise: ₹5 = 5 × 100 = 500 paise.
Ratio = 500/50 = 10/1 = 10 : 1.
(b) 15 kg to 210 g:
Convert to grams: 15 kg = 15 × 1000 = 15000 g.
Ratio = 15000/210 = 1500/21 = 500/7 = 500 : 7.
(c) 9 m to 27 cm:
Convert to cm: 9 m = 900 cm.
Ratio = 900/27 = 100/3 = 100 : 3.
(d) 30 days to 36 hours:
Convert to hours: 30 days = 30 × 24 = 720 hours.
Ratio = 720/36 = 20/1 = 20 : 1.

Q2
In a computer lab, there are 3 computers for every 6 students. How many computers will be needed for 24 students?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Unitary Method:
Computers required for 6 students = 3.
Computers required for 1 student = 3/6 = 1/2.
Computers required for 24 students = 24 × 1/2 = 12 computers.

Conclusion: Exactly 12 computers will be needed for 24 students.

Exercise 7.2 (Profit & Loss, Simple Interest)
Q1
Tell what is the profit or loss in the following transactions. Also find profit per cent or loss per cent in each case:
(a) Gardening shears bought for ₹250 and sold for ₹325.
(b) A refrigerator bought for ₹12,000 and sold at ₹13,500.
(c) A cupboard bought for ₹2,500 and sold at ₹3,000.
(d) A skirt bought for ₹250 and sold at ₹150.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) Gardening shears:
CP = ₹250, SP = ₹325. Since SP > CP, there is a profit.
Profit = 325 − 250 = ₹75.
Profit % = 75/250 × 100 = 3/10 × 100 = 30%.
(b) Refrigerator:
CP = ₹12,000, SP = ₹13,500. Profit.
Profit = 13500 − 12000 = ₹1,500.
Profit % = 1500/12000 × 100 = 150/12 = 12.5%.
(c) Cupboard:
CP = ₹2,500, SP = ₹3,000. Profit.
Profit = 3000 − 2500 = ₹500.
Profit % = 500/2500 × 100 = 1/5 × 100 = 20%.
(d) Skirt:
CP = ₹250, SP = ₹150. Since CP > SP, there is a loss.
Loss = 250 − 150 = ₹100.
Loss % = 100/250 × 100 = 2/5 × 100 = 40%.
CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27
Each part: Profit/Loss calculation (0.5 Mark) + % calculation (0.5 Mark)1 Mark each (4 Marks total)
Q2
Find the amount to be paid at the end of 3 years in each case:
(a) Principal = ₹1,200 at 12% p.a.
(b) Principal = ₹7,500 at 5% p.a.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) P = ₹1,200, R = 12% p.a., T = 3 years:
SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (1200 × 12 × 3)/100 = 12 × 36 = ₹432.
Amount = P + SI = ₹1200 + ₹432 = ₹1,632.
(b) P = ₹7,500, R = 5% p.a., T = 3 years:
SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (7500 × 5 × 3)/100 = 75 × 15 = ₹1,125.
Amount = P + SI = ₹7500 + ₹1125 = ₹8,625.

Q3
What rate gives ₹280 as interest on a sum of ₹56,000 in 2 years?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given:
SI = ₹280, P = ₹56,000, T = 2 years.
Formula for Rate:
R = (SI × 100)/(P × T) = (280 × 100)/(56000 × 2) = 28000/112000 = 28/112 = 1/4% = 0.25%.

Conclusion: The rate of interest is 0.25% (or ¼%) per annum.

CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27
Formula R = (SI × 100) / (P × T)1 Mark
R = 0.25% p.a. with statement1 Mark

Case Study: Population Growth & Depreciation
Scenario: The population of a city decreased from 25,000 to 24,500. (i) Find the percentage decrease in population. (ii) If the population decreases by the same percentage next year, what will the new population be?

(i) Percentage decrease:
Original population = 25,000.
Decrease = 25,000 − 24,500 = 500.
Percentage Decrease = 500/25000 × 100% = 50/25% = 2%.

(ii) Next year's population:
Decrease next year = 2% of 24,500 = 2/100 × 24500 = 490.
New population = 24,500 − 490 = 24,010.
