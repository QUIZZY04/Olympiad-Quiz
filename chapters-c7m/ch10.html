📌 Key Concepts & Algebraic Rules Summary
  • Terms and Factors: An algebraic expression is formed by terms. For example, in 4x² - 3xy, the terms are 4x² and -3xy. The factors of 4x² are 4, x, x and of -3xy are -3, x, y.
  • Coefficients: The numerical factor of a term is called its numerical coefficient (e.g. In -5xy, the coefficient is -5).
  • Like and Unlike Terms:
    • Like terms: Terms having the same algebraic factors with identical exponents (e.g. 7x and -12x, 4x^2y and -9x^2y).
    • Unlike terms: Terms having different algebraic factors (e.g. 7x and 7y, 4x² and 4x).
  • Classification of Expressions:
    • Monomial: Exactly one term (e.g. 7xy, -5).
    • Binomial: Exactly two terms (e.g. a + b, 4x² - 3).
    • Trinomial: Exactly three terms (e.g. a + b + c, x² + 5x + 6).
    • Polynomial: An expression with one or more terms having non-negative integral exponents.
  • Addition and Subtraction: Only like terms can be combined (added or subtracted) by adding their numerical coefficients.
  • Evaluating Expressions: Substitute the given numerical values of variables into the expression and apply BODMAS.
Exercise 10.1
Q1
Identify the terms and their factors in the following expressions:
(a) x − 3
(b) 1 + x + x²
(c) y − y³
(d) 5xy² + 7x²y
(e) −ab + 2b² − 3a²
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) x − 3:
• Term: x ⇒ Factors: x
• Term: −3 ⇒ Factors: −3 (or −1, 3)
(b) 1 + x + x²:
• Terms: 1, x, x²
• Factors of x²: x, x
(c) y − y³:
• Terms: y and −y³
• Factors of −y³: −1, y, y, y
(d) 5xy² + 7x²y:
• Term: 5xy² ⇒ Factors: 5, x, y, y
• Term: 7x^2y ⇒ Factors: 7, x, x, y
(e) −ab + 2b² − 3a²:
• Term −ab: −1, a, b
• Term 2b²: 2, b, b
• Term −3a²: −3, a, a

Q3
Simplify by combining like terms:
(i) 21b − 32 + 7b − 20b
(ii) −z² + 13z² − 5z + 7z³ − 15z
(iii) p − (p − q) − q − (q − p)
(iv) 3a − 2b − ab − (a − b + ab) + 3ab + b − a
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 21b − 32 + 7b − 20b:
Group terms with variable b:
= (21 + 7 − 20)b − 32 = (28 − 20)b − 32 = 8b − 32.
(ii) −z² + 13z² − 5z + 7z³ − 15z:
Arrange by descending powers of z:
= 7z³ + (−1 + 13)z² + (−5 − 15)z = 7z³ + 12z² − 20z.
(iii) p − (p − q) − q − (q − p):
Open parentheses with sign rules:
= p − p + q − q − q + p = (p − p + p) + (q − q − q) = p − q.
(iv) 3a − 2b − ab − (a − b + ab) + 3ab + b − a:
Open brackets: = 3a − 2b − ab − a + b − ab + 3ab + b − a.
Group like terms:
• Terms in a: (3 − 1 − 1)a = a
• Terms in b: (−2 + 1 + 1)b = 0b = 0
• Terms in ab: (−1 − 1 + 3)ab = ab
= a + ab.

Exercise 10.2 (Evaluation of Expressions)
Q1
If m = 2, find the value of:
(i) m − 2
(ii) 3m − 5
(iii) 9 − 5m
(iv) 3m² − 2m − 7
(v) 5m/2 − 4
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

Substitute m = 2:

(i) m − 2 = 2 − 2 = 0
(ii) 3m − 5 = 3(2) − 5 = 6 − 5 = 1
(iii) 9 − 5m = 9 − 5(2) = 9 − 10 = −1
(iv) 3m² − 2m − 7 = 3(2)² − 2(2) − 7 = 3(4) − 4 − 7 = 12 − 11 = 1
(v) 5m/2 − 4 = 5(2)/2 − 4 = 5 − 4 = 1

Q2
If a = 2, b = −2, find the value of:
(i) a² + b²
(ii) a² + ab + b²
(iii) a² − b²
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

Given: a = 2, b = −2.

(i) a² + b² = (2)² + (−2)² = 4 + 4 = 8
(ii) a² + ab + b² = (2)² + (2)(−2) + (−2)² = 4 − 4 + 4 = 4
(iii) a² − b² = (2)² − (−2)² = 4 − 4 = 0

HOTS: Constant Determination
Problem: What should be the value of a if the value of 2x² + x − a equals 5, when x = 0?

Given equation condition: 2x² + x − a = 5 when x = 0.

Substitute x = 0 into the expression:
2(0)² + 0 − a = 5
0 + 0 − a = 5 ⇒ −a = 5 ⇒ a = −5.

Conclusion: The value of a is −5.
