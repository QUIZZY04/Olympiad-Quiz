NCERT Exercise 13.1 to 13.3 — Complete Solutions as per CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & 3-D Solid Properties Summary
  • 3-Dimensional Shapes (Solids): Figures with length, breadth, and height/depth (e.g. Cube, Cuboid, Cylinder, Cone, Sphere, Prism, Pyramid).
  • Polyhedrons: Solids formed entirely of polygonal planar regions called Faces (F). The line segments where faces meet are Edges (E). The points where edges meet are Vertices (V).
  • Euler’s Formula: For any convex polyhedron: F + V - E = 2
  • Net of a Solid: A 2-D outline skeleton on paper that can be folded along its edges to construct the 3-D solid shape.
  • Visualising Cross-Sections:
    • Horizontal cut (parallel to base): Slicing through the height.
    • Vertical cut (perpendicular to base): Slicing through the cross-axis.

Identify the nets which can be used to make cubes (cut out copies of the nets and try it). How many faces does a cube have?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Properties of a Cube Net:
• A cube has exactly 6 identical square faces, 12 edges, and 8 vertices.
• In a valid cube net, no two folded squares overlap, and opposite faces never share a common edge in the net.
• Standard valid nets include the "T-shape" (1-4-1 pattern), the cross-shape, and the step patterns (2-3-1 pattern).

Exercise 13.2 (Isometric Sketches & Dimension Combining)
Q1
Three cubes each with 2 cm edge are placed side by side to form a cuboid. What would be the dimensions (length, breadth, and height) of this cuboid?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Geometry Analysis:
Edge of each cube = 2 cm.
When 3 cubes are placed side by side in a row:
• Length (l) = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 cm.
• Breadth (b) remains unchanged = 2 cm.
• Height (h) remains unchanged = 2 cm.

Conclusion: The dimensions of the resulting cuboid are 6 cm × 2 cm × 2 cm.

Q2
Verify Euler’s formula (F + V − E = 2) for:
(a) A Cuboid
(b) A Triangular Prism
(c) A Square Pyramid
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

Euler's formula: F + V − E = 2.

(a) Cuboid:
• Faces (F) = 6
• Vertices (V) = 8
• Edges (E) = 12
Verification: F + V − E = 6 + 8 − 12 = 14 − 12 = 2. Verified!
(b) Triangular Prism:
• Faces (F) = 5 (2 triangular + 3 rectangular)
• Vertices (V) = 6
• Edges (E) = 9
Verification: F + V − E = 5 + 6 − 9 = 11 − 9 = 2. Verified!
(c) Square Pyramid:
• Faces (F) = 5 (1 square + 4 triangular)
• Vertices (V) = 5 (1 apex + 4 base corners)
• Edges (E) = 8 (4 base edges + 4 slant edges)
Verification: F + V − E = 5 + 5 − 8 = 10 − 8 = 2. Verified!

Q4
Two dice are placed side by side with 5 + 2 on top faces and 6 + 1. What would be the total on the face opposite to:
(a) 5 + 6    (b) 1 + 2?
2 Marks (1 Mark each)
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
On standard dice, the sum of numbers on opposite faces is always 7.
• Face opposite to 5 is 7 − 5 = 2.
• Face opposite to 6 is 7 − 6 = 1.
• Face opposite to 1 is 7 − 1 = 6.
• Face opposite to 2 is 7 − 2 = 5.

(a) Face opposite to (5 and 6) = 2 + 1 = 3.
(b) Face opposite to (1 and 2) = 6 + 5 = 11.

Q1
Explain how isometric dot paper is used to make an isometric sketch of a 3D solid shape like a cuboid.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
On isometric dot paper, dots are arranged at the vertices of equilateral triangles, forming axes at 120° to each other:
1. Draw the base edges along the slanted isometric axes proportional to the given length and breadth.
2. Draw vertical line segments at the vertices equal to the height of the cuboid.
3. Join the top endpoints to form the top face.
In an isometric sketch, measurements are drawn to exact scale along the isometric axes, preserving proportional dimensions.

Euler's Formula Polyhedron Test
Problem: Can a polyhedron have 10 faces, 20 edges and 15 vertices? Justify your answer using Euler’s formula.

Given parameters:
Faces, F = 10
Edges, E = 20
Vertices, V = 15.

By Euler's Formula for any polyhedron:
F + V − E = 2.

Substituting given values:
LHS = F + V − E = 10 + 15 − 20 = 25 − 20 = 5.

Since 5 ≠ 2, the given values violate Euler’s formula.

Conclusion: No, a polyhedron cannot have 10 faces, 20 edges, and 15 vertices.
