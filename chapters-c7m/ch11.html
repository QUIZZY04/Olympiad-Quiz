📌 Key Concepts & Laws of Exponents Summary
  • Exponential Form: Repeated multiplication written concisely: a × a × a ... (n times) = aⁿ, where a is the base and n is the exponent.
  • Laws of Exponents (for non-zero integers a, b and whole numbers m, n):
    • Multiplying powers with same base: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ.
    • Dividing powers with same base: aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ (m > n).
    • Power of a power: (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ.
    • Multiplying powers with same exponents: aᵐ × bᵐ = (a × b)ᵐ = (ab)ᵐ.
    • Dividing powers with same exponents: aᵐ ÷ bᵐ = (a/b)ᵐ.
    • Zero Exponent: a⁰ = 1.
    • Powers of -1: (-1)eveⁿ = +1, (-1)odd = -1.
  • Standard Form (Scientific Notation): Any number can be expressed as k × 10ⁿ, where 1 ≤ k < 10 and n is an integer. Used to write very large numbers compactly.

Q3
Identify the greater number, wherever possible, in each of the following:
(i) 4³ or 3⁴
(ii) 5³ or 3⁵
(iii) 2⁸ or 8²
(iv) 100² or 2¹⁰⁰
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 4³ vs 3⁴:
4³ = 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.
3⁴ = 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81.
Since 81 > 64 ⇒ 3⁴ > 4³.
(ii) 5³ vs 3⁵:
5³ = 125.
3⁵ = 243.
Since 243 > 125 ⇒ 3⁵ > 5³.
(iii) 2⁸ vs 8²:
2⁸ = 256.
8² = 64.
Since 256 > 64 ⇒ 2⁸ > 8².
(iv) 100² vs 2¹⁰⁰:
100² = 10,000.
Notice 2¹⁰ = 1024 > 1000 ⇒ 2¹⁰⁰ = (2¹⁰)¹⁰ > (10³)¹⁰ = 10³⁰.
Hence, 2¹⁰⁰ > 100² by an enormous margin.

Q1
Using laws of exponents, simplify and write the answer in exponential form:
(i) 3² × 3⁴ × 3⁸
(ii) 6¹⁵ ÷ 6¹⁰
(iii) a³ × a²
(iv) 7ˣ × 7²
(v) (5²)³ ÷ 5³
(vi) 2⁵ × 5⁵
(vii) (2²⁰ ÷ 2¹⁵) × 2³
(viii) 8ᵗ ÷ 8²
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 3² × 3⁴ × 3⁸ = 3² ⁺ ⁴ ⁺ ⁸ = 3¹⁴  [Law: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ]
(ii) 6¹⁵ ÷ 6¹⁰ = 6¹⁵ ⁻ ¹⁰ = 6⁵  [Law: aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ]
(iii) a³ × a² = a³ ⁺ ² = a⁵
(iv) 7ˣ × 7² = 7ˣ⁺²
(v) (5²)³ ÷ 5³ = 5² × ³ ÷ 5³ = 5⁶ ÷ 5³ = 5⁶ ⁻ ³ = 5³
(vi) 2⁵ × 5⁵ = (2 × 5)⁵ = 10⁵  [Law: aᵐ × bᵐ = (ab)ᵐ]
(vii) (2²⁰ ÷ 2¹⁵) × 2³ = 2²⁰ ⁻ ¹⁵ × 2³ = 2⁵ × 2³ = 2⁵ ⁺ ³ = 2⁸
(viii) 8^t ÷ 8² = 8t⁻²

Q2
Simplify and express each of the following in exponential form:
(i) (2³ × 3⁴ × 4) / (3 × 32)
(ii) [(5²)³ × 5⁴] ÷ 5⁷
(iii) 25⁴ ÷ 5³
(iv) (3 × 7² × 11⁸) / (21 × 11³)
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) (2³ × 3⁴ × 4)/(3 × 32):
Express in prime factors: 4 = 2², 32 = 2⁵.
= (2³ × 3⁴ × 2²)/(3¹ × 2⁵) = (2³⁺² × 3⁴)/(2⁵ × 3¹) = (2⁵ × 3⁴)/(2⁵ × 3¹) = 2⁵⁻⁵ × 3⁴⁻¹ = 2⁰ × 3³ = 1 × 3³ = 3³.
(ii) [(5²)³ × 5⁴] ÷ 5⁷:
= [5² × ³ × 5⁴] ÷ 5⁷ = [5⁶ × 5⁴] ÷ 5⁷ = 5⁶⁺⁴ ÷ 5⁷ = 5¹⁰ ÷ 5⁷ = 5¹⁰⁻⁷ = 5³.
(iii) 25⁴ ÷ 5³:
Write 25 = 5² ⇒ (5²)⁴ ÷ 5³ = 5⁸ ÷ 5³ = 5⁸⁻³ = 5⁵.
(iv) (3 × 7² × 11⁸)/(21 × 11³):
Write 21 = 3 × 7.
= (3¹ × 7² × 11⁸)/(3¹ × 7¹ × 11³) = 3¹⁻¹ × 7²⁻¹ × 11⁸⁻³ = 3⁰ × 7¹ × 11⁵ = 1 × 7 × 11⁵ = 7 × 11⁵.

Q4
Identify the greater number, wherever possible, in each of the following:
(i) 4³ or 3⁴    (ii) 5³ or 3⁵    (iii) 2⁸ or 8²    (iv) 100² or 2¹⁰⁰    (v) 2¹⁰ or 10²
5 Marks (1 Mark each)
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 4³ or 3⁴:
4^3 = 4 × 4 × 4 = 64
3^4 = 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81
Since 81 > 64, 3⁴ > 4³.

(ii) 5³ or 3⁵:
5^3 = 125, 3^5 = 243. Since 243 > 125, 3⁵ > 5³.

(iii) 2⁸ or 8²:
2^8 = 256, 8^2 = 64. Since 256 > 64, 2⁸ > 8².

(iv) 100² or 2¹⁰⁰:
100^2 = 10,000.
2^{10} = 1024 > 10^3 ⟹ 2^{100} = (2^{10})^{10} > (10^3)^{10} = 10^{30}.
Clearly, 2¹⁰⁰ is vastly greater than 100².

(v) 2¹⁰ or 10²:
2^{10} = 1024, 10^2 = 100. Since 1024 > 100, 2¹⁰ > 10².

Q3
Say true or false and justify your answer:
(i) 10 × 10¹¹ = 100¹¹
(ii) 2³ > 5²
(iii) 2³ × 3² = 6⁵
(iv) 3⁰ = (1000)⁰
4 Marks (1 Mark each)
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 10 × 10¹¹ = 100¹¹: False.
LHS = 10^1 × 10^{11} = 10^{1+11} = 10^{12}.
RHS = 100^{11} = (10^2)^{11} = 10^{22}. Since 10^{12} ≠ 10^{22}, statement is false.

(ii) 2³ > 5²: False.
LHS = 2^3 = 8, RHS = 5^2 = 25. Since 8 < 25, statement is false.

(iii) 2³ × 3² = 6⁵: False.
LHS = 8 × 9 = 72, RHS = 6^5 = 7776. Since 72 ≠ 7776, statement is false.

(iv) 3⁰ = (1000)⁰: True.
Any non-zero number raised to exponent 0 equals 1: 3^0 = 1 and (1000)^0 = 1. Both sides equal 1.

Problem: Find the value of x in the equation: (2/5)³ × (2/5)⁶ = (2/5)³ˣ.

Using law aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ on LHS:
(2/5)³ ⁺ ⁶ = (2/5)³ˣ
(2/5)⁹ = (2/5)³ˣ.

Equating exponents since bases are identical:
3x = 9 ⇒ x = 9/3 = 3.
