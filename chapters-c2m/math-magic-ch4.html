Chapter 4: Counting in Tens

Chickens and Fox, Garlands of 10, Packets of Pencils and Bindis | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Counting large collections efficiently by grouping into tens and loose units (ones).
  • Tikloo farmer's chickens: counting baskets of 10 chickens and finding how many the fox ate.
  • Malti's flower garlands: making garlands of 10 flowers each and finding total flowers.
  • Kanak's bindi collection: counting sheets of 10 bindis in artistic patterns (3+2+5, 4+3+3).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Garlands of 10
Malti has to make flower garlands for a wedding. She strings 10 marigold flowers in each garland.
(a) In picture 1, there are 8 loose flowers and 5 garlands. How many flowers in all?
(b) In picture 2, there are 6 garlands and 7 loose flowers. How many flowers in all?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
(a) 5 garlands of 10 = 50 flowers. Plus 8 loose flowers = 50 + 8 = 58 flowers.
(b) 6 garlands of 10 = 60 flowers. Plus 7 loose flowers = 60 + 7 = 67 flowers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) 58 flowers calculated 1 M
(b) 67 flowers calculated 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Making Garlands
If you have 35 flowers, how many garlands of 10 flowers each can you make? How many flowers will be left loose?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• In 35, there are 3 tens and 5 ones.
• Number of garlands of 10 = 3 garlands.
• Loose flowers remaining = 5 flowers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
3 garlands of 10 identified 1 M
5 loose flowers remaining 1 M
Q3 Textbook Exercise • Packets of Pencils
Look at the pencil boxes. There are 6 packets of 10 pencils each, and 4 loose pencils outside.
(a) How many packets of 10 are there?
(b) How many pencils outside the packets?
(c) How many total pencils are there?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
(a) Number of packets of 10 = 6 packets.
(b) Pencils outside = 4 pencils.
(c) Total pencils = 60 + 4 = 64 pencils.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) 6 packets 1 M
(b) 4 loose 1 M
(c) 64 total 1 M
Q4 Textbook Exercise • Kanak's Bindis
Kanak loves collecting bindis. She has 7 packets of bindis. Each packet contains 10 bindis arranged in groups of 3 + 2 + 5.
(a) How many packets does Kanak have?
(b) How many bindis are there in all?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Kanak has 7 packets.
(b) Total bindis = 7 packets × 10 bindis = 70 bindis.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) 7 packets identified 1 M
(b) 70 total bindis calculated 1 M
Q5 Creative Arrangement • Bindi Patterns
Draw or describe 10 bindis in a different pattern than 3 + 2 + 5. Give two different ways to group 10 bindis.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Groupings:
1. Way 1: 4 + 3 + 3 = 10 (4 bindis in top row, 3 in middle, 3 in bottom row).
2. Way 2: 5 + 5 = 10 (two equal rows of 5 bindis each).

First grouping of 10 (e.g. 4+3+3) 1 M
Second grouping of 10 (e.g. 5+5) 1 M
Q6 Tikloo Farmer Morning Check
Tikloo counted his chickens on Monday morning. He filled 3 baskets of 10 chickens and had 8 loose chickens. Write the total number in tens and ones format.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Tens = 3 (30 chickens)
• Ones = 8 (8 chickens)
• Total chickens = 30 + 8 = 38 chickens.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tens and ones split: 3 tens + 8 ones 1 M
Total 38 chickens 1 M

Q9 Counting Bundles • Matchsticks
A matchstick bundle contains 10 sticks. If Rohan makes 4 bundles and has 9 loose sticks, how many sticks does he have? How many more sticks does he need to make a 5th bundle?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. 4 bundles of 10 = 40. Plus 9 loose = 40 + 9 = 49 sticks.
2. To make a bundle of 10, he needs: 10 - 9 = 1 more stick.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total 49 sticks calculated 1 M
1 more stick needed for 5th bundle 1 M
Q10 Word Problem • Sweet Box
A baker puts 10 laddoos in every box. A customer buys 5 boxes and 6 loose laddoos. How many laddoos did the customer buy in total?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• 5 boxes of 10 laddoos = 5 × 10 = 50 laddoos.
• 6 loose laddoos = 6 laddoos.
• Total laddoos = 50 + 6 = 56 laddoos.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
5 boxes = 50 laddoos shown 1 M
Total 56 laddoos calculated 1 M
Q11 Comparative Problem • Bindi Collection
Sita has 4 packets of 10 bindis. Geeta has 3 packets of 10 bindis and 8 loose bindis. Who has more bindis and by how many?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Sita's bindis = 4 × 10 = 40 bindis.
• Geeta's bindis = (3 × 10) + 8 = 30 + 8 = 38 bindis.
• Since 40 > 38, Sita has more bindis.
• Difference = 40 - 38 = 2 bindis more.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Totals 40 and 38 calculated 1 M
Sita identified with difference 2 1 M

Dinesh wants to buy a toy car costing ₹50. How many 10-rupee coins must he give to the shopkeeper?

Q17 Chapter Reflection
Why is 10 considered a very special number in our numbering system?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Explanation:
• Our number system is based on groups of 10 (base-10 system).
• Whenever we reach 10 ones, they combine to form 1 ten. Whenever we reach 10 tens, they combine to form 1 hundred (100). This allows us to write any large number easily using place values.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Base-10 grouping explained 1 M
Transition from ones to tens to hundreds 1 M
Q18 Quick Quiz Problem
Which is greater: 6 tens and 3 ones OR 3 tens and 6 ones? Explain by calculating both values.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• 6 tens and 3 ones = 60 + 3 = 63.
• 3 tens and 6 ones = 30 + 6 = 36.
• Since 63 > 36, 6 tens and 3 ones (63) is greater.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Values 63 and 36 calculated 1 M
63 identified as greater 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Tikloo Farmer and Clever Fox Problem

Tikloo farmer has many chickens on his farm. A clever fox started stealing and eating chickens. Tikloo decided to put 10 chickens in each basket every morning. In the morning, there were 5 baskets of 10 chickens and 4 chickens outside. In the evening, there were 4 baskets of 10 chickens and 3 chickens outside.

Analytical Task: How many chickens were there in the morning? How many in the evening? How many chickens did the clever fox eat during the day?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Morning count: 5 baskets of 10 = 50, plus 4 outside = 50 + 4 = 54 chickens.
2. Evening count: 4 baskets of 10 = 40, plus 3 outside = 40 + 3 = 43 chickens.
3. Chickens eaten by fox: 54 - 43 = 11 chickens.
Rubric: 1 M for morning count (54) + 1 M for evening count (43) + 1 M for difference eaten (11)
