Chapter 6: Decoration for Festival

Repeating & Growing Patterns, Rangoli Symmetry & Border Tessellations | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Identifying repeating design units in torans, rangoli, and fabric borders.
  • Recognizing growing geometric and number patterns (e.g. 2, 4, 6...; 10, 20, 30...).
  • Completing symmetrical Rangoli dot grids and alternating tile arrangements.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

Q1 Repeating Shape Patterns
Look at the pattern: 🔴, 🔷, 🔴, 🔷, 🔴, __, __.
What comes in the next two blanks?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The pattern alternates between red circle and blue diamond.
• Next two shapes: 🔷 (Blue diamond) and 🔴 (Red circle).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pattern rule identified 1 M
Both shapes correct 1 M
Q2 Growing Shape Patterns
Observe the growing pattern:
Step 1: 1 square (🟩)
Step 2: 2 squares (🟩 🟩)
Step 3: 3 squares (🟩 🟩 🟩)
How many squares will be in Step 5? What is the growing rule?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Growing rule: Add 1 square at each step.
• Step 4 = 4 squares.
• Step 5 = 5 squares.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Growing rule stated (+1 each step) 1 M
Step 5 has 5 squares 1 M
Q3 Number Patterns • Adding 2
Complete the growing number pattern: 3, 5, 7, 9, __, __, 15.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Rule: Add 2 to each number (consecutive odd numbers).
• 9 + 2 = 11
• 11 + 2 = 13
• The missing numbers are 11 and 13.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rule identified (+2) 1 M
11 and 13 correct 1 M

Q9 Rangoli Dot Grid Symmetry
In a 4-by-4 dot rangoli grid, if you draw a curved petal on the left side, what must you do on the right side to keep it symmetrical?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• To make it symmetrical, you must draw an identical curved petal mirrored on the right side at the exact same distance from the center line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mirror image concept 1 M
Same distance from center line 1 M
Q10 Spot the Mistake
Find the mistake in this pattern: 2, 4, 6, 9, 10, 12. Which number is wrong and what should it be?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The pattern is counting by 2s (even numbers).
• The number 9 is wrong.
• It should be 8 (because 6 + 2 = 8).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identified 9 as wrong 1 M
Correct replacement: 8 1 M
Q11 Floor Tiling Tessellation
Why can square floor tiles cover a rectangular hall completely without leaving empty gaps, while round circular tiles cannot?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Squares have straight edges and 90° corners that fit snugly side-by-side without any empty spaces.
• Circles have curved rims, so when placed together, they always leave gaps (holes) between them.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Straight edges fit seamlessly 1 M
Curved rims leave gaps 1 M
Q12 Alternating Color Pattern
A strip of 10 diyas is painted in alternating colors: Gold, Silver, Gold, Silver...
(a) What color is the 7th diya?
(b) What color is the 10th diya?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Odd positions (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th) are Gold.
• Even positions (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th) are Silver.
(a) The 7th diya is Gold.
(b) The 10th diya is Silver.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
7th is Gold 1 M
10th is Silver 1 M
Q13 Number Tower Pattern
Look at the pattern of adding consecutive numbers:
1 + 2 = 3
2 + 3 = 5
3 + 4 = 7
What is 4 + 5? What is 5 + 6? What pattern do the answers make?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 4 + 5 = 9
• 5 + 6 = 11
• The answers (3, 5, 7, 9, 11) form a pattern of odd numbers growing by 2 each time.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
4+5=9 1 M
5+6=11 1 M
Pattern: Odd numbers increasing by 2 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Diwali Toran Stringing Task

Simran strings a door toran using yellow marigolds and green mango leaves. She follows the rule: 2 leaves followed by 1 marigold.

Analytical Task: If her toran has 6 marigolds in total, how many mango leaves did Simran use? How many items are on the toran altogether?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. For every 1 marigold, she uses 2 leaves.
2. For 6 marigolds: 6 × 2 = 12 mango leaves.
3. Total items on toran = 12 leaves + 6 marigolds = 18 items.
Rubric: 1.5 M for leaves calculation (12) + 1.5 M for total items (18)
