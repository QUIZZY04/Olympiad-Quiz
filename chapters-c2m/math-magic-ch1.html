Step-by-Step Explanation:
1. In 'What is Long, What is Round?', we learn about Roll and Slide, Tower Making with Boxes and Coins.

Q2 Practice Problem • Step-by-Step
Solve the foundational practice drill for Chapter 1: If a student has 14 items and shares 12 items with friends, how many items are left?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Initial items = 14
• Items shared = 12
• Remaining items = (14) - 12 = 2 items.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Subtraction statement 1 M
Correct numerical result 1 M

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
