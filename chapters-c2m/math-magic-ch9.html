Chapter 9: My Funday

Days of the Week, School Timetable, Months & Seasons | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Sequencing the seven days of the week starting from Monday / Sunday.
  • Reading school timetables (games period, drawing period, library day).
  • Identifying yesterday, today, and tomorrow in daily routines.
  • Recognizing months of the year, seasonal weather (summer, monsoon, winter), and seasonal fruits.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Days Sequence
Answer the following questions about the days of the week:
(a) Which is the third day of the week if we start with Monday?
(b) Which day comes just after Friday?
(c) Which day comes just before Wednesday?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) 1st=Monday, 2nd=Tuesday, 3rd=Wednesday.
(b) The day just after Friday is Saturday.
(c) The day just before Wednesday is Tuesday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Wednesday 1 M
(b) Saturday 1 M
(c) Tuesday 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Funday Rhyme
Why is Sunday called 'Funday' by children?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Sunday is the weekly school holiday.
• Children do not have to carry their school bags to school and can play games, visit parks, and spend joyful time with family and friends.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Weekly holiday identified 1 M
Play and family time explained 1 M
Q3 Textbook Exercise • Days Count
How many days are there in a week? Name all of them in order.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• There are 7 days in a week.
• In order: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Count: 7 days 1 M
All 7 days named correctly in order 1 M
Q4 Calendar Deduction • Today, Yesterday, Tomorrow
If today is Tuesday:
(a) What was yesterday?
(b) What will tomorrow be?
(c) What day will it be day after tomorrow?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Yesterday was Monday.
(b) Tomorrow will be Wednesday.
(c) Day after tomorrow will be Thursday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Monday 1 M
(b) Wednesday 1 M
(c) Thursday 1 M
Q5 Textbook Exercise • Months of the Year
How many months are there in one year? Name the first and last months of the year.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• There are 12 months in one year.
• The first month is January.
• The last month is December.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
12 months stated 1 M
January and December named 1 M
Q6 Seasons & Weather • Hot and Cold Months
Name two months that are very hot in India, and two months that are very cold.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Very Hot Months (Summer): May and June.
• Very Cold Months (Winter): December and January.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hot months: May, June 1 M
Cold months: December, January 1 M
Q7 Seasonal Fruits
Which delicious fruit is found in abundance during the hot summer months? Name one fruit commonly eaten in winter.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Summer fruit: Mango (or Watermelon).
• Winter fruit: Orange (or Apple/Guava).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Summer fruit (Mango/Watermelon) 1 M
Winter fruit (Orange/Guava) 1 M
Q8 Monsoon Season
In which months does it rain the most in most parts of India? What items do you carry to school on rainy days?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• It rains heavily during July and August (the Monsoon season).
• Children carry an umbrella or wear a raincoat and gumboots to stay dry.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
July and August identified 1 M
Umbrella / raincoat named 1 M
Q9 Days After a Day
Which day comes 4 days after Sunday?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• 1 day after Sunday = Monday
• 2 days after Sunday = Tuesday
• 3 days after Sunday = Wednesday
• 4 days after Sunday = Thursday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Counting sequence shown 1 M
Thursday correct 1 M
Q10 School Timetable Analysis
In Neha's school, children have Music class on Monday and Wednesday, and Art class on Friday. How many total creative arts periods do they have in a week?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• Music = 2 days (Mon, Wed)
• Art = 1 day (Fri)
• Total creative periods = 2 + 1 = 3 periods.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Breakdown shown 1 M
Total 3 periods correct 1 M

Q12 Special Days • Republic & Independence Day
In which month do we celebrate:
(a) Republic Day (26th)

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:

Q13 Weekly Repetition
If the 1st of a month is a Monday, what day of the week will the 8th of that month be? Explain.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• A week repeats every 7 days.
• 1 + 7 = 8.
• Therefore, the 8th will also be a Monday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Addition 1 + 7 = 8 explained 1 M
Monday correct 1 M
Q14 Temperature & Clothes
Why do we wear light cotton clothes in May and woollen sweaters in December?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• In May, the weather is hot and sunny; light cotton clothes absorb sweat and keep our body cool.
• In December, the weather is cold; thick woollen sweaters trap body heat and keep us warm.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
May / cotton cooling explained 1 M
December / wool warmth explained 1 M
Q15 Days Riddle
I am a day of the week. I start with the letter 'W'. I stand right in the middle of the school working week. Who am I?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• You are Wednesday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Wednesday identified correctly 1 M
Q16 True or False
State True or False:
(a) A year has 365 days.
(b) Friday comes before Thursday.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) True. A normal calendar year has 365 days.
(b) False. Friday comes AFTER Thursday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) True 1 M
(b) False with reason 1 M
Q17 Chapter Reflection
Why is having a school timetable helpful for teachers and students?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:
1. A timetable organizes the day so every subject gets adequate learning time.
2. It helps students pack only the required books in their school bag, keeping the bag light.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Organization of learning time 1 M
Packing required books / bag weight 1 M
Q18 Quick Quiz Problem
How many weeks are there in one year approximately?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• There are approximately 52 weeks in one year (52 × 7 = 364 days).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
52 weeks correct 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Class 2 Weekly Activity Timetable

In Class 2-B, children have Games period on Tuesday and Friday, Drawing on Wednesday and Saturday, and Library reading on Thursday. School is closed on Sunday.

Analytical Task: Which day comes between Tuesday and Thursday? On which two days do children play sports and games? If today is Thursday, what was yesterday and what will tomorrow be?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Wednesday comes between Tuesday and Thursday.
2. Children play games on Tuesday and Friday.
3. If today is Thursday: Yesterday was Wednesday, and Tomorrow will be Friday.
Rubric: 1 M for Wednesday + 1 M for Tuesday & Friday + 1 M for yesterday & tomorrow
