Chapter 12: Give and Take

Necklace of 10 Beads, Regrouping, Carrying & Borrowing | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Regrouping ones into tens when adding: 10 loose beads make 1 new necklace.
  • Regrouping tens into ones when subtracting: untying 1 necklace to get 10 loose beads.
  • Solving 2-digit addition with carry-over (e.g. 17 + 24 = 41).
  • Solving 2-digit subtraction with borrowing (e.g. 35 - 17 = 18).
  • Real-world canteen bills and money transactions.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Bead Necklace Addition
Razia has 12 beads (1 necklace and 2 loose). Sonu has 35 beads (3 necklaces and 5 loose). How many beads do they have altogether?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• Tens (necklaces): 1 + 3 = 4 necklaces (40 beads)
• Ones (loose beads): 2 + 5 = 7 beads
• Total beads = 40 + 7 = 47 beads (4 necklaces and 7 loose beads).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tens and ones addition shown 1 M
Total 47 beads correct 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Regrouping Loose Beads
Aarif has 24 beads and Razia has 12 beads. How many beads do they have together?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• Add ones: 4 + 2 = 6 loose beads
• Add tens: 2 + 1 = 3 necklaces
• Total = 36 beads.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
24 + 12 step shown 1 M
36 beads correct 1 M
Q3 Textbook Exercise • Addition with Carrying Over
Reema has 17 beads and Aarif has 24 beads.
(a) How many loose beads are there in all?
(b) Can they make a new necklace from the loose beads?
(c) What is their total bead count?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Loose beads: 7 + 4 = 11 loose beads.
(b) Yes, 10 of these 11 loose beads make 1 new necklace, leaving 1 loose bead.
(c) Necklaces: 1 (Reema) + 2 (Aarif) + 1 (new) = 4 necklaces.
Total beads = 40 + 1 = 41 beads.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Loose beads: 11 1 M
Regrouping 10 beads into 1 necklace 1 M
Total 41 beads 1 M
Q4 Textbook Exercise • How Many More Beads?
Aarif has 24 beads and Razia has 12 beads. How many more beads does Aarif have than Razia?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Aarif's beads = 24
• Razia's beads = 12
• Difference = 24 - 12 = 12 more beads.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Subtraction 24 - 12 shown 1 M
12 beads correct 1 M
Q5 Textbook Exercise • Subtraction with Borrowing
Simar has 31 beads (3 necklaces and 1 loose bead). She gives 17 beads to Reema. How many beads are left with Simar?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Simar has 1 loose bead and needs to give 7 loose beads. She cannot give 7 from 1.
2. She unties 1 necklace (10 beads). Now she has 2 necklaces and (10 + 1) = 11 loose beads.
3. Subtract loose beads: 11 - 7 = 4 loose beads.
4. Subtract necklaces: 2 - 1 = 1 necklace.
5. Beads remaining = 10 + 4 = 14 beads.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Untying 1 necklace into 10 beads explained 1 M
Loose subtraction 11 - 7 = 4 1 M
Final answer 14 beads correct 1 M
Q6 NCERT Story • Canteen Bill Problem
At the school snack bar, Dosa costs ₹23 and Uttpam costs ₹28. If Aman orders both, what is his total bill?

Q7 Canteen Bill • Change Calculation
Aman's canteen bill is ₹51. He hands a ₹100 note to the cashier. How much change will he get back?

• Bill amount = ₹51
• Change back = 100 - 51 = ₹49.

Q8 Practice Problem • Column Addition
Solve by column addition with carry-over:
  4 7
+ 2 6
-----
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Ones column: 7 + 6 = 13 (Write 3 in ones place, carry 1 ten to tens column).
• Tens column: 4 + 2 + 1 (carried) = 7 tens.
• Answer = 73.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Carrying 1 ten shown 1 M
Sum 73 correct 1 M
Q9 Practice Problem • Column Subtraction
Solve by column subtraction with borrowing:
  6 2
- 2 8
-----
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Ones column: 2 is smaller than 8. Borrow 1 ten from 6 tens (leaving 5 tens).
• 2 becomes 12. 12 - 8 = 4.
• Tens column: 5 - 2 = 3.
• Answer = 34.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Borrowing step shown (12 - 8) 1 M
Answer 34 correct 1 M
Q10 Word Problem • Mango Tree

Q11 Word Problem • Cricket Bats
A sports shop had 54 cricket balls. On Sunday, 29 cricket balls were sold. How many cricket balls were left in the shop?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Initial cricket balls = 54
• Balls sold = 29
• Remaining balls = 54 - 29 = 54 - 30 + 1 = 24 + 1 = 25 cricket balls.

How many necklaces of 10 beads can you make from 60 beads? Will any beads be left loose?

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• 60 ÷ 10 = 6 complete necklaces.
• There are 0 loose beads left.

Q13 Mental Check • Addition Check with Subtraction
If 34 + 25 = 59, how can you check this using subtraction?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• Subtract one addend from the total sum:
• 59 - 25 = 34 (or 59 - 34 = 25).
• Since the difference equals the other number, the addition is confirmed correct.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Inverse operation 59 - 25 = 34 shown 1 M
Verification rationale explained 1 M
Q14 Regrouping Rule in Words
What do we do when the sum of digits in the ones place is 10 or more?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• When the ones place sum is 10 or more, we regroup 10 ones into 1 ten.
• We write the remaining ones in the ones place and carry over the 1 ten to the tens place.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Regroup 10 ones into 1 ten explained 1 M
Carry over to tens place described 1 M
Q15 Word Problem • Bus Passengers
A bus starts with 36 passengers. At the next stop, 15 passengers get off and 8 new passengers get on. How many passengers are in the bus now?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. After 15 get off: 36 - 15 = 21 passengers.
2. After 8 get on: 21 + 8 = 29 passengers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Subtraction 36 - 15 = 21 shown 1 M
Addition 21 + 8 = 29 correct 1 M
Q16 True or False
State True or False:
(a) 50 - 25 = 25.
(b) In subtraction, we can borrow 1 ten, which is equal to 10 ones.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) True. 25 + 25 = 50, so 50 - 25 = 25.
(b) True. 1 ten always equals 10 ones.

(a) True 1 M

• 'Take' represents receiving items or adding.
• Together, addition and subtraction model all give-and-take exchanges in daily life, shopping, and games.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Simar and Reema's Bead Necklace Project

Children in the village craft bead necklaces. 1 necklace takes exactly 10 beads. Razia has 12 beads (1 necklace and 2 loose beads). Reema has 17 beads (1 necklace and 7 loose beads). Aarif has 24 beads (2 necklaces and 4 loose beads).

Analytical Task: How many total beads do Razia and Reema have together? When they put their loose beads together, do they get enough to make a new necklace?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Razia: 12 beads. Reema: 17 beads.
2. Combined beads: 12 + 17 = 29 beads.
3. Loose beads check: 2 loose + 7 loose = 9 loose beads. Since 9 < 10, they need 1 more bead to make a new necklace (so they have 2 necklaces and 9 loose beads).
Rubric: 1 M for total beads (29) + 1 M for loose beads analysis (9) + 1 M for new necklace condition explanation
