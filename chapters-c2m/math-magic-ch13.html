Chapter 13: The Longest Step

Footsteps, Handspans, Fingers, Cat & Mouse Distance Puzzle | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Measuring length and distance using body parts (fingers, handspan, pace/footsteps).
  • Comparing distances: who takes the longest step (deer vs rabbit vs elephant).
  • The Cat, Rat, and Milk Bowl puzzle: estimating who reaches the milk first.
  • Understanding why non-standard body measurements differ between a child and an adult.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Animal Steps
Three friends — an elephant, a deer, and a rabbit — walk in a park. Who takes the longest step? Who takes the shortest step?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• The elephant has huge long legs and takes the longest step.
• The rabbit hops with small bounds and takes the shortest step.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Elephant: longest step 1 M
Rabbit: shortest step 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Measuring with Handspans
Rajat measures the classroom study desk using his handspan. His desk is 6 handspans long. His teacher measures the same desk and finds it is 4 handspans long. Why did the teacher get a smaller count?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• The teacher is an adult, so her handspan is much bigger than Rajat's child handspan.
• Because the teacher's handspan is longer, fewer handspans are needed to cover the same length.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Teacher's handspan is larger explained 1 M
Fewer units needed for longer measure 1 M
Q3 Textbook Exercise • Measuring with Steps (Paces)
How can you measure the distance between the classroom door and the teacher's blackboard without using a measuring tape?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• You can measure it by walking normally and counting your footsteps (paces) heel-to-toe from the door to the blackboard.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Footsteps / paces method described 1 M
Counting from start to end point explained 1 M
Q4 Textbook Activity • Measuring School Items
Which body unit (fingers, handspan, or footsteps) is most suitable to measure:
(a) The thickness of your pencil
(b) The length of your school math book
(c) The length of your school playground
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Thickness of a pencil: Fingers.
(b) Length of a math book: Handspan.
(c) Length of a playground: Footsteps (paces).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Fingers 1 M
(b) Handspan 1 M
(c) Footsteps 1 M
Q5 Body Proportion Investigation
Is your forehead longer than your palm? Measure with your fingers and compare.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Forehead height is usually about 3 to 4 fingers.
• Palm length is usually about 6 to 8 fingers.
• Therefore, the palm is longer than the forehead.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Finger measurement comparison 1 M
Palm identified as longer 1 M
Q6 Footprint Length Comparison
Who will have a longer foot: you or your father? How can you verify this at home?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Father's foot will be longer.
• You can verify this by standing side-by-side on paper and tracing your foot outlines, or placing your heel against your father's heel.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Father's foot identified 1 M
Heel-to-heel or tracing verification method 1 M
Q7 Pencil Length Drill
An eraser is 2 fingers long. A new wooden pencil is equal to 4 erasers placed end-to-end. How many fingers long is the pencil?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• 1 eraser = 2 fingers
• Pencil = 4 erasers = 4 × 2 = 8 fingers long.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Multiplication 4 × 2 shown 1 M
8 fingers correct 1 M
Q8 Ribbon Comparison
Priya has a red ribbon that is 5 handspans long. Sneha has a blue ribbon that is 7 handspans long (measured using Priya's hand). Whose ribbon is longer and by how many handspans?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Red ribbon = 5 handspans
• Blue ribbon = 7 handspans
• Since 7 > 5, the blue ribbon is longer.
• Difference = 7 - 5 = 2 handspans longer.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Blue ribbon identified 1 M
Difference of 2 handspans calculated 1 M
Q9 Cat & Mouse Puzzle Variation
In the cat and mouse game, if the rat is 4 steps away from the cheese and the cat is 9 steps away, how many steps head start does the rat have?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Cat distance = 9 steps
• Rat distance = 4 steps
• Head start = 9 - 4 = 5 steps.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Subtraction 9 - 4 shown 1 M
5 steps correct 1 M
Q10 Hands-on Activity • Nose to Ear
Guess the distance between your nose and your ear in fingers. Check with your hand.
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The distance from the tip of the nose to the ear in a 7-year-old child is approximately 4 to 6 fingers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Reasonable estimate (4-6 fingers) 1 M
Q11 Why Standard Units Matter
Why can carpenters and tailor uncles not use only handspans to make tables and shirts?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• Everyone's hands and bodies are different sizes.
• If a tailor used handspans, clothes would turn out too big or too small. They use a standard measuring tape (centimetres/metres) so measurements are exact for everyone.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Variation in body sizes explained 1 M
Need for standard measuring tape explained 1 M
Q12 Walking Race • Steps Counting
Tarun walks across the verandah in 18 steps. His younger sister walks the same verandah in 26 steps. Who took more steps, and why?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• The younger sister took more steps (26 steps vs 18 steps).
• Reason: Her legs are shorter, so each of her steps covers a smaller distance, requiring more steps to cross the verandah.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Younger sister identified 1 M
Shorter stride length reason provided 1 M
Q13 True or False
State True or False:
(a) A handspan is the distance between the tip of the thumb and the tip of the little finger when stretched open.
(b) An ant takes longer steps than a dog.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) True. That is the exact anatomical definition of a handspan.
(b) False. An ant has tiny legs and takes microscopically small steps compared to a dog.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) True 1 M
(b) False with reason 1 M
Q14 Chapter Reflection
What have you learned about measuring distances in Chapter 13?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:
1. Distances can be measured using natural units like fingers, handspans, and footsteps.
2. Larger objects take fewer measurement units; smaller units take more counts.
3. Because human body sizes vary, standard tools like rulers and measuring tapes ensure universal precision.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Non-standard units named 1 M
Inverse unit-count relationship 1 M
Need for standard units 1 M

Answer:

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Cat and Mouse Distance Investigation

In the NCERT picture, a bowl of fresh milk sits between a cat and a little mouse. A girl uses her fingers to measure distance on the page. From the rat's nose to the milk bowl is 3 fingers. From the cat's paw to the milk bowl is 5 fingers. The rat is 2 fingers away from its underground hole.

Analytical Task: Who is closer to the milk bowl: the cat or the rat? Will the rat reach the milk bowl or its safety hole first? What will happen if the cat runs twice as fast?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The rat is closer to the milk bowl (3 fingers vs 5 fingers for the cat).
2. The rat will reach its hole first because the hole is only 2 fingers away.
3. If the cat runs twice as fast, it covers distance rapidly, but the smart rat can quickly jump into its hole 2 fingers away and stay safe!
Rubric: 1 M for rat closer to milk + 1 M for hole distance comparison + 1 M for cat speed reasoning
