Chapter 3: Fun with Numbers

2-Digit Numbers (1–100), Tens & Ones Place Value & Ordering | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Building numbers 1 to 100 with bundles of 10 and single units (ones).
  • Finding predecessor (before), successor (after), and between numbers.
  • Comparing 2-digit numbers using <, >, and = symbols.
  • Expanding numbers into Tens and Ones (e.g. 64 = 60 + 4).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Tens and Ones Place Value
Write the number shown by 5 bundles of 10 sticks and 8 loose sticks. What is the value of the digit in the tens place?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• 5 bundles of 10 = 5 tens = 50
• 8 loose sticks = 8 ones = 8
• Number = 50 + 8 = 58.
• The digit in the tens place is 5, and its value is 50.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Number identification: 58 1 M
Value of tens digit: 50 1 M
Q2 Number Expansion
Expand the following numbers into Tens and Ones:
(a) 43 = __ tens + __ ones = __ + __
(b) 76 = __ tens + __ ones = __ + __
(c) 90 = __ tens + __ ones = __ + __
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) 43 = 4 tens + 3 ones = 40 + 3
(b) 76 = 7 tens + 6 ones = 70 + 6
(c) 90 = 9 tens + 0 ones = 90 + 0.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a) correct 1 M
Part (b) correct 1 M
Part (c) correct 1 M
Q3 Before, After and Between
Fill in the missing numbers:
(a) What comes just before 60? ______
(b) What comes just after 89? ______
(c) What comes between 74 and 76? ______
(d) What comes between 98 and 100? ______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Just before 60 is 59.
(b) Just after 89 is 90.
(c) Between 74 and 76 is 75.
(d) Between 98 and 100 is 99.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 correct (0.5 M each) 2 M
Q4 Comparing 2-Digit Numbers
Insert <, >, or = in the boxes:
(a) 47 [ ] 74
(b) 85 [ ] 58
(c) 63 [ ] 60 + 3
(d) 91 [ ] 99
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) 47 < 74 (4 tens is less than 7 tens)
(b) 85 > 58 (8 tens is greater than 5 tens)
(c) 63 = 60 + 3 (both equal 63)
(d) 91 < 99 (same tens, but 1 one is less than 9 ones).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 correct comparison signs (0.5 M each) 2 M
Q5 Ordering Numbers • Increasing Order
Arrange the given numbers in ascending order (smallest to greatest): 54, 21, 88, 37, 19.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Compare tens digits: 19 (1 ten), 21 (2 tens), 37 (3 tens), 54 (5 tens), 88 (8 tens).
• Ascending Order: 19, 21, 37, 54, 88.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Method of comparing tens digits 1 M
Correct order: 19, 21, 37, 54, 88 1 M
Q6 Ordering Numbers • Decreasing Order
Arrange the given numbers in descending order (greatest to smallest): 62, 95, 34, 79, 41.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:

Q11 Abacus Representation
An abacus has 6 green beads on the Tens spike and 3 blue beads on the Ones spike. What number does the abacus represent?

Q15 Word Problem • Marble Collection
Kabir has 47 glass marbles. His cousin Aman has 52 glass marbles. Who has more marbles and by how many?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
1. Aman has 52, Kabir has 47.
2. Since 52 > 47, Aman has more marbles.
3. Difference = 52 - 47 = 5 more marbles.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Difference = 5 marbles 1.5 M

Q18 True or False • Place Value
Write True or False:
(a) In the number 83, the place value of 8 is 8.
(b) 10 tens make 100.
(c) The smallest 2-digit number is 10.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) False (The place value of 8 in 83 is 80).
(b) True (10 × 10 = 100).
(c) True (10 is the first 2-digit number).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 statements evaluated correctly (1 M each) 3 M

Q20 Real-Life Math • Bead Necklace
Meera makes necklaces using 10 beads in each necklace. If she has 38 beads, how many complete necklaces can she make? How many beads are left over?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• Total beads = 38
• Beads per necklace = 10
• 38 = 30 + 8 = (3 tens) + (8 ones).
• Meera can make 3 complete necklaces and 8 beads are left over.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
3 complete necklaces 1 M
8 beads remainder 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Classroom Pencil Inventory

The class teacher has 4 boxes of 10 pencils each, and 7 loose pencils on her desk. She needs 50 pencils for an art contest.

Analytical Task: How many total pencils does the teacher have right now? How many more pencils does she need to reach 50?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. 4 boxes of 10 = 4 × 10 = 40 pencils.
2. Plus 7 loose pencils = 40 + 7 = 47 pencils.
3. Shortage to reach 50 = 50 - 47 = 3 more pencils needed.
Rubric: 1.5 M for total inventory (47) + 1.5 M for shortage calculation (3)
