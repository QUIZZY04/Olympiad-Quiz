Chapter 10: Add Our Points

Toss the Ball, Snake Number Strip, Coin Toss & Killer Lizard | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Mental addition of two and three single-digit and 2-digit numbers.
  • Using the snake number board (number strip) to count forward when adding points.
  • Tossing coins (Heads / Tails) and calculating running game scores.
  • The Killer Lizard puzzle: finding paths through numbered holes to eat insects.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (19 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Adding on Snake Strip
A player stands on number 14 on the snake strip board. He tosses the ball 7 times without dropping it. To add 7, he jumps 7 steps forward. On which number does he land?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Starting point = 14
• Steps forward = 7
• Landing number = 14 + 7 = 21.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
14 + 7 shown 1 M
Landing number 21 correct 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Animal Scores Addition
Add the points mentally:
(a) Monkey: 12 + 8 = ___
(b) Deer: 15 + 13 = ___
(c) Fox: 23 + 9 = ___
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) 12 + 8 = 20
(b) 15 + 13 = (15 + 10 + 3) = 25 + 3 = 28
(c) 23 + 9 = (23 + 10 - 1) = 33 - 1 = 32
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) 20 1 M
(b) 28 1 M
(c) 32 1 M
Q3 Textbook Exercise • Killer Lizard Puzzle
A lizard on a wall moves between numbered holes to eat insects. From hole 0, the lizard can only walk along straight lines. Which 3 holes can the lizard visit to eat exactly 12 insects if hole numbers are 3, 5, 4?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Check the sum of 3, 5, 4:
• 3 + 5 + 4 = 8 + 4 = 12.
• The lizard can walk through holes 3, 5, and 4 (in any order) to eat 12 insects.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sum 3 + 5 + 4 = 12 verified 1 M
Holes 3, 5, 4 identified 1 M
Q4 Killer Lizard • Target 18
Find 3 holes whose numbers add up to 18 using digits from: 8, 5, 2, 1, 9, 7.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Try 9 + 8 + 1: 9 + 8 = 17, 17 + 1 = 18.
• Alternatively: 9 + 7 + 2 = 18.
• Holes 9, 8, and 1 (or 9, 7, 2) add up to 18.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Valid 3-digit combination selected 1 M
Sum 18 verified 1 M
Q5 Textbook Game • Heads and Tails
Two children play a game with a 1-rupee coin. If Heads comes, they score 10 points. If Tails comes, they score 1 point.
Suraj gets 3 Heads and 4 Tails. What is his total score?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• 3 Heads = 3 × 10 = 30 points
• 4 Tails = 4 × 1 = 4 points
• Suraj's total score = 30 + 4 = 34 points.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Heads (30) and Tails (4) calculated 1 M
Total 34 points correct 1 M
Q6 Three Numbers Addition
Add three numbers mentally:
(a) 6 + 5 + 4 = ___
(b) 7 + 8 + 3 = ___
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Mental Strategy:
(a) Make 10 first: (6 + 4) = 10; then 10 + 5 = 15.
(b) Make 10 first: (7 + 3) = 10; then 10 + 8 = 18.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) 15 with make-10 strategy 1 M
(b) 18 with make-10 strategy 1 M
Q7 Two at a Time • Card Sums
Look at number cards: [11], [25], [17], [32]. Pick two cards that give the largest sum. Pick two cards that give the smallest sum.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Largest sum: Pick the two largest numbers: 32 + 25 = 57.
2. Smallest sum: Pick the two smallest numbers: 11 + 17 = 28.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Largest sum: 32 + 25 = 57 1 M
Smallest sum: 11 + 17 = 28 1 M
Q8 Scoreboard Comparison
Team Red scored 46 runs. Team Blue scored 38 runs. How many more runs did Team Red score than Team Blue?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Team Red = 46 runs
• Team Blue = 38 runs
• Difference = 46 - 38 = 8 runs more.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Q9 Double Digits Mental Addition
Calculate mentally: 33 + 14.
Explain how you added without using paper and pencil.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
1. Break 14 into 10 + 4.
2. First add 10 to 33: 33 + 10 = 43.
3. Then add the remaining 4: 43 + 4 = 47.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mental strategy (33 + 10 + 4) explained 1 M
Answer 47 correct 1 M

Q12 Word Problem • Fruit Basket
A basket has 16 red apples and 18 green apples. How many apples are there in all?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• Red apples = 16
• Green apples = 18
• Total = 16 + 18 = 16 + 14 + 4 = 30 + 4 = 34 apples.

Q14 Fast Math • Add 9 Strategy
What is an easy shortcut to add 9 to any number like 35?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Explanation:
• Since 9 is just 1 less than 10, first add 10, then subtract 1.
• For 35 + 9: 35 + 10 = 45; 45 - 1 = 44.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Add 10, subtract 1 shortcut explained 1 M
Example 35 + 9 = 44 shown 1 M
Q15 Comparison Problem • Game Turns
In a board game, Rohit scored 24 in round 1 and 19 in round 2. Sameer scored 21 in round 1 and 23 in round 2. Who won the match?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Rohit's total = 24 + 19 = 43 points.
• Sameer's total = 21 + 23 = 44 points.
• Since 44 > 43, Sameer won the match (by 1 point).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Totals 43 and 44 calculated 1 M
Sameer identified as winner 1 M
Q16 True or False
State True or False:
(a) 15 + 0 = 15.
(b) Changing the order of numbers changes the sum (e.g. 7 + 9 is different from 9 + 7).
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) True. Adding zero to any number leaves it unchanged.
(b) False. In addition, changing the order gives the exact same sum (7 + 9 = 16 and 9 + 7 = 16).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) True 1 M
(b) False with commutative property explanation 1 M

Q19 Textbook Game • Toss the Ball
Animals in the forest played toss-the-ball. Rabbit scored 14 in the first turn and 7 in the second turn. Tortoise scored 11 in the first turn and 12 in the second turn. Who won the game?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Rabbit's score = 14 + 7 = 21 points.
• Tortoise's score = 11 + 12 = 23 points.
• Since 23 > 21, the Tortoise won the game.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rabbit: 21 1 M
Tortoise: 23 1 M
Tortoise won 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Jungle Animals 'Toss the Ball' Championship

Animals of the forest played 'Toss the Ball'. Each player had two turns. Bunu rabbit added their points on his snake strip board. Bear scored 16 + 7 points. Elephant scored 18 + 9 points. Tortoise scored 14 + 7 points. Bunu rabbit scored 19 + 15 points.

Analytical Task: Calculate the total points scored by each player. Who won the toss game, and who lost the game?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Bear: 16 + 7 = 23 points.
2. Elephant: 18 + 9 = 27 points.
3. Tortoise: 14 + 7 = 21 points.
4. Bunu Rabbit: 19 + 15 = 34 points.
5. Winner: Bunu Rabbit (34 points).
6. Loser: Tortoise (21 points).
Rubric: 1 M for all four sums + 1 M for identifying winner (Bunu) + 1 M for identifying loser (Tortoise)
