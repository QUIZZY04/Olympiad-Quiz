Chapter 8: Tens and Ones

Bundles of 10, Currency Notes (₹10, ₹20, ₹50) & Coins | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Grouping loose items into bundles of ten.
  • Recognizing Indian currency notes and coins: ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100.
  • Combining ₹10 notes and ₹1 coins to pay exact amounts in a shop.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (2 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Money Representation • Notes and Coins
How many ₹10 notes and ₹1 coins are needed to make:
(a) ₹46?
(b) ₹63?
(c) ₹80?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• (a) ₹46: 4 notes of ₹10 and 6 coins of ₹1.
• (b) ₹63: 6 notes of ₹10 and 3 coins of ₹1.
• (c) ₹80: 8 notes of ₹10 and 0 coins of ₹1.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct breakdown for all three amounts 3 M
Q2 Mental Arithmetic • Total Value
Grandma gave Riya two ₹20 notes and one ₹10 note. How much money did Riya receive in total?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Two ₹20 notes = ₹20 + ₹20 = ₹40
• One ₹10 note = ₹10
• Total amount = ₹40 + ₹10 = ₹50.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Addition step shown 1 M
Answer = ₹50 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Stationery Shopping Spree

Harman goes to a bookshop to buy a notebook for ₹35 and a pencil box for ₹42. The shopkeeper only accepts ₹10 notes and ₹1 coins.

Analytical Task: How much is the total bill? How many ₹10 notes and ₹1 coins should Harman give the shopkeeper?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total bill = ₹35 + ₹42 = ₹77.
2. Breaking ₹77 into tens and ones:
• ₹77 = 7 Tens and 7 Ones.
3. Harman gives 7 notes of ₹10 and 7 coins of ₹1.
Rubric: 1.5 M for total sum + 1.5 M for 7 notes and 7 coins
