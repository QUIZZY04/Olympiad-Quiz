Chapter 9: Which Season is It?

Days of the Week, 12 Months of the Year, Clock Hours & Seasonal Cycles | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Naming and sequencing the 7 days of the week (Monday to Sunday).
  • Knowing the 12 months in a solar year and months with 30, 31, or 28 days.
  • Reading whole-hour clock times (e.g. 3:00, 7:00) and understanding daily routines.
  • Connecting weather patterns, clothing, and seasonal foods to Indian seasons.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Days of the Week Sequence
Name the 7 days of the week in chronological order starting from Monday.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Monday
2. Tuesday
3. Wednesday
4. Thursday
5. Friday
6. Saturday
7. Sunday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 7 days in correct order 2 M
Q2 Days Before and After
Answer the following:
(a) Which day comes just after Friday?
(b) Which day comes just before Tuesday?
(c) Which day falls between Wednesday and Friday?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Just after Friday is Saturday.
(b) Just before Tuesday is Monday.
(c) Between Wednesday and Friday is Thursday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 correct (1 M each) 3 M
Q3 Months of the Year
How many months are there in a calendar year? Which is the first month and which is the last month?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• There are 12 months in a calendar year.
• The first month is January.
• The last month is December.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
12 months named 1 M
January and December identified 1 M
Q4 Shortest Month
Which month of the year has the fewest number of days? How many days does it usually have?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• February has the fewest days.
• It usually has 28 days (and 29 days in a leap year).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
February identified 1 M
28 days (29 in leap year) 1 M
Q5 Months with 31 Days
Name 4 months of the year that have exactly 31 days.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Any four of the following:
1. January
2. March
3. May
4. July
*(Also: August, October, December)*.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four valid 31-day months named 2 M
Q6 Months with 30 Days
Name the 4 months that have exactly 30 days.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
The four 30-day months are:
1. April
2. June
3. September
4. November.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 thirty-day months named correctly 2 M
Q7 Reading Clock Hours
On a wall clock, the short hour hand points to 4 and the long minute hand points straight up to 12. What time is it?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Short hand at 4 = Hour 4
• Long hand at 12 = 00 minutes
• The time is 4:00 o'clock (4 o'clock).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hour and minute hand identified 1 M
Time: 4:00 o'clock 1 M
Q8 Clock Hands at School Opening
If your school morning assembly begins at 8 o'clock in the morning, where should the short hand and long hand be on the clock face?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The short hour hand should point to 8.
• The long minute hand should point to 12.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Short hand at 8 1 M
Long hand at 12 1 M
Q9 Seasons and Clothing
Match each season with the clothing item we use:
(1) Winter — (A) Cotton T-shirt & shorts
(2) Monsoon — (B) Woolen muffler and sweater
(3) Summer — (C) Raincoat and gumboots
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Correct Matching:
1. Winter ➔ (B) Woolen muffler and sweater
2. Monsoon ➔ (C) Raincoat and gumboots
3. Summer ➔ (A) Cotton T-shirt & shorts.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 matched correctly (1 M each) 3 M
Q10 Seasonal Fruits
In which season do we enjoy juicy sweet mangoes and chilled watermelon in India?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
We enjoy mangoes and watermelons during the Summer season (April, May, June).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Summer season identified 1 M
Q11 National Festivals and Months
In which months do we celebrate:
(a) Independence Day (15th August)?
(b) Republic Day (26th January)?
(c) Gandhi Jayanti (2nd October)?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Independence Day: August
(b) Republic Day: January
(c) Gandhi Jayanti: October.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 national festival months correct (1 M each) 3 M
Q12 Daily Routine Sequencing
Put these daily events in chronological order from morning to night:
[ Go to sleep, Wake up from bed, Eat dinner, Attend school, Play in the evening ].
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Correct Chronological Order:
1. Wake up from bed (Morning)
2. Attend school (Morning to Afternoon)
3. Play in the evening (Evening)
4. Eat dinner (Night)
5. Go to sleep (Bedtime).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Proper logical sequence 2 M
Q13 Calendar Math
If today is Tuesday, October 6, what day of the week will it be exactly one week later (October 13)?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• A complete cycle of days repeats every 7 days.
• Since 6 + 7 = 13, it will be Tuesday again.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
7-day cycle explained 1 M
Answer: Tuesday 1 M
Q14 True or False • Time and Calendar
Write True or False:
(a) There are 24 hours in one full day and night.
(b) Sunday is the day after Monday.
(c) July and August both have 31 days.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) True (12 hours day + 12 hours night = 24 hours).
(b) False (Sunday is the day *before* Monday).
(c) True (July and August are consecutive 31-day months).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 correct (1 M each) 3 M
Q15 Time Riddle
I have two hands that always chase each other, but no feet to walk. I tell you when to wake up and when to play. What am I?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
I am a Clock (or Watch).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clock identified 1 M
Q16 Let Us Think • Sun and Seasons
During which season do the days feel short and the nights feel very long and chilly?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
During the Winter season, the sun sets early, making days shorter and nights longer.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Winter season identified 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Annual Sports Week Schedule

The school sports week starts on Monday, October 12, and runs for 5 continuous school days.

Analytical Task: On which day of the week will the sports competition conclude? If Saturday and Sunday are holidays, how many total days off do students get that weekend?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Day 1 is Monday. Day 2 is Tuesday. Day 3 is Wednesday. Day 4 is Thursday. Day 5 is Friday. The competition concludes on Friday, October 16.
2. Saturday + Sunday = 2 weekend days off.
Rubric: 1.5 M for concluding on Friday + 1.5 M for 2 weekend holidays
