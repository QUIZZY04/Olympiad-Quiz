Class 5 IGKO Syllabus & Exam Pattern

The Class 5 General Knowledge Olympiad assesses students across four major sections. Our practice aligns with the latest global updates.

  • Section 1
    General Awareness

    Our Body and Health, Plants and Animals, India and the World, Science and Technology, Environment and its Conservation, Language and Literature, Entertainment, Sports, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning.

  • Section 2
    Current Affairs

    Important national and international events, news, and developments.

  • Section 3
    Life Skills

    Empathy, Effective Communication, Critical Thinking, Creative Thinking, Coping with Stress, Decision Making, Problem Solving.

  • Section 4
    Achievers Section

    Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions based on the complete syllabus. These complex questions carry 3 marks each.

Why General Knowledge is Crucial in Class 5

In Class 5, students transition from learning basic facts to understanding how the world connects. Participating in the Class 5 General Knowledge Olympiad builds a broad perspective on science, society, and global events.

By practicing our IGKO Class 5 mock tests, students develop a natural curiosity. Learning about current affairs and life skills at this age trains young minds to be better global citizens and aids in comprehension for all other academic subjects.

Our free Olympiad practice for Class 5 ensures that students can identify gaps in their awareness instantly using our detailed post-test analytics and step-by-step solutions.
