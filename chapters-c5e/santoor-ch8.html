Chapter 8: The Decision of the Panchayat

Classic Indian folktale on truth, justice, rural jurisprudence & outsmarting deceit | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Themes of ethical honesty, village community justice (Panchayati Raj), and exposing crafty loopholes.
  • A farmer purchases a well to irrigate his fields, but the dishonest neighbor claims he sold only the well, not the water.
  • The Village Panchayat's witty, brilliant verdict: if you own the water, pay rent for storing it in the farmer's well or empty it immediately!
  • Grammar: prepositions of place and direction, and deriving abstract nouns using suffixes.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Comprehension
Why did the hardworking farmer buy the well from his neighbor?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The farmer had purchased an adjoining agricultural field and needed a dependable, perennial source of freshwater to irrigate his growing crops and sustain his family.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Purchased adjoining farmland 1 Mark
Needed reliable water for crop irrigation 1 Mark
Q2 Let us Think • Comprehension
Why did the farmer become deeply distressed the day after completing the purchase?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
When the farmer went to draw water the following morning, the cunning neighbor stopped him aggressively, claiming deceitfully that he had sold only the physical well structure, but not the water contained inside it.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Neighbor stopping farmer from drawing water 1 Mark
Cunning claim: sold well structure, not the water 1 Mark
Q3 Let us Think • Comprehension
How did the Village Panchayat use clever logical reasoning to resolve the dispute?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The Panchayat elders used the neighbor's own dishonest logic against him. They declared that since the physical well now lawfully belonged to the farmer, the neighbor had no legal right to store his water inside the farmer's property. Therefore, the neighbor must either pay daily rent to the farmer for storing his water, or remove all his water immediately!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Panchayat accepting that the well belongs to farmer 1 Mark
Counter-argument: neighbor cannot store water in farmer's property 1 Mark
Ultimatum: pay storage rent or remove water immediately 1 Mark
Q4 Let us Think • Comprehension
What was the final judgment delivered by the Panchayat, and what was its effect on the neighbor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The Panchayat ordered the neighbor to either vacate his water from the well at once without spilling a drop onto the farmer's land, or surrender full water rights to the farmer. Realizing his deceit had been completely foiled by superior wisdom, the neighbor hung his head in shame and conceded unconditional access to the farmer.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Panchayat's binding ultimatum delivered 1.5 Marks
Neighbor conceding defeat in shame, full rights given to farmer 1.5 Marks
Q5 Let us Think • Think & Discuss
Do you agree that the decision of the Panchayat was fair, just, and morally sound? Why?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Yes, the decision was exemplary. It upheld justice by exposing the neighbor's bad-faith technicality. Selling a well naturally implies selling access to its water. By turning the trickster's own words back upon him, the elders protected the honest farmer without violence or prolonged litigation.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Affirmation of justice and fairness 1.5 Marks
Insight on dismantling dishonest technicalities with wit 1.5 Marks
Q6 Let us Think • Think & Discuss
What moral values does this traditional story teach regarding business dealings and community life?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The story teaches that integrity and honesty are paramount in contracts. Attempting to exploit loopholes to cheat others ultimately leads to public humiliation. True justice requires wisdom and courageous community arbitration where the voice of truth triumphs over greed.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Integrity in agreements / contracts 1.5 Marks
Truth prevailing over greed and trickery 1.5 Marks

Q11 Let us Explore • Civics & Panchayati Raj
Project Work: Describe the structure of a Village Gram Panchayat in India and its role in resolving local disputes.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Panchayati Raj Grassroots Governance:
  • Gram Sabha: The general body comprising all adult voting residents of the village.
  • Gram Panchayat: The elected executive council headed by the Sarpanch (village head) and ward members (Panchs).
  • Dispute Resolution: Panchayats resolve family, property, and boundary disputes locally through mediation, avoiding costly police and court litigation while preserving community harmony.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Gram Sabha and Gram Panchayat structure defined 1.5 Marks
Local dispute resolution function explained 1.5 Marks

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Consumer Rights, Contractual Integrity & Legal Loopholes

A shopkeeper sells a brand-new desktop computer to a student. When the student opens the package at home, the power cable and keyboard are missing. When confronted, the shopkeeper cunningly argues: 'The bill says computer screen and CPU box; accessories require an additional fee.'

Analytical Task: Drawing a parallel to the trickery in 'The Decision of the Panchayat', identify the unfair trade practice and explain how consumer protection forums deliver justice in such cases.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Legal & Ethical Resolution under Consumer Protection:
  1. Identification of Deceit: Just as the neighbor tried to sell an empty well while denying its essential water, the shopkeeper is engaging in an 'Unfair Trade Practice' by selling an unusable product that cannot operate without its essential basic power cable.
  2. Doctrine of Implied Fitness: In commercial law, goods must be fit for their intended purpose. A computer sold without a power cord violates basic contractual good faith.
  3. Consumer Forum Remedy: The student can file a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline or District Consumer Commission. The forum will order the retailer to immediately supply all missing operational accessories and pay compensation for harassment.
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for connecting well/water deceit to modern consumer rights + 3.5 Marks for legal doctrine of fitness and redressal mechanism = 5 Marks
