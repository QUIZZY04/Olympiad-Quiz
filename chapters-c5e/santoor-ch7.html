Chapter 7: Gilli Danda

Rhythmic sports poem celebrating India's ancient street game, agile hand-eye coordination & outdoor joy | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Themes of traditional outdoor play, indigenous Indian games, and joyous camaraderie.
  • The mechanics of Gilli Danda: a long wooden danda striking the tapered gilli into flight.
  • Health benefits of energetic open-air group sports versus isolated sedentary screen time.
  • Grammar focus: prefixes (un-, re-) to form antonyms and absolute possessive pronouns.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Comprehension
Which indigenous sport does the poem celebrate, and what two items of wooden equipment are needed to play it?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The poem celebrates the ancient traditional Indian sport of Gilli Danda. The equipment required comprises: (1) A danda (a long, sturdy wooden stick roughly two feet long) and (2) A gilli (a small wooden cylindrical peg, four to five inches long, tapered at both ends).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Game identified: Gilli Danda 1 Mark
Two equipment pieces: danda (long stick) and gilli (tapered peg) 1 Mark
Q2 Let us Think • Comprehension
According to the verses, what emotions and atmosphere do the players experience while playing Gilli Danda?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The players experience immense exhilaration, freedom, laughter, and camaraderie. Playing under open skies fills their hearts with merry joy and healthy, energetic competition.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Joy, laughter, and high energy identified 1 Mark
Sense of freedom and open-air camaraderie 1 Mark
Q3 Let us Think • Comprehension
Explain what happens mechanically when the danda strikes the tapered tip of the gilli.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
When the player strikes one of the tapered, pointed ends of the gilli lying on the ground with the danda, it flips abruptly into the air. While the gilli is spinning airborne, the player quickly swings the danda to strike it with full force, sending it flying across the field.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tapping the tapered end flips the gilli into the air 1.5 Marks
Second mid-air strike sends it flying forward across the field 1.5 Marks

Quote two specific lines from the poem that prove the game is played outdoors in open spaces.

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Two textual lines that establish outdoor play are:
  1. "In the open air and under the sky."
  2. "The small gilli, up high it flies, / Underneath the bright blue skies."
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Line 1 quoted accurately 1 Mark
Line 2 quoted accurately 1 Mark
Q5 Let us Think • Comprehension
What does the poet mean by the line 'our game is so sound'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The phrase 'our game is so sound' means that the game is robust, physically wholesome, full of vitality, and thoroughly satisfying. It promotes genuine fitness, sharp reflexes, and joyous bonding without any artificial pretension.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Meaning of 'sound' as healthy, robust, and wholesome 1 Mark
Explanation of vitality and satisfying teamwork 1 Mark

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The poem employs the expressive words 'joy' and 'merry' (as well as 'fun') to signify pure happiness and delight among the children.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of 'joy' 1 Mark
Identification of 'merry' 1 Mark
Q7 Let us Think • Think & Discuss
Why do outdoor traditional games contribute more to a child's holistic health than indoor mobile gaming?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Outdoor games like Gilli Danda involve full-body movement, sprinting, bending, and sharp hand-eye coordination that strengthen bones and cardiovascular fitness. Furthermore, playing face-to-face teaches patience, team communication, and managing disappointments, whereas screen gaming fosters sedentary isolation and eye strain.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Physical fitness & motor coordination contrasts 1.5 Marks
Social-emotional communication vs. screen isolation 1.5 Marks

Q10 Let us Write • Rulebook Writing
Write a clear, numbered 4-point guide on 'How to Play Gilli Danda Safely':
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Official Safety Rules for Gilli Danda:
  1. Select an Open Field: Always play in large grassy grounds or empty public parks, never in congested narrow residential alleys with glass windows.
  2. Clear Fielders' Perimeter: Fielders must stand at a safe distance (at least 15 meters away) in front of the striker, keeping their eyes focused on the airborne gilli.
  3. Check Equipment Quality: Ensure the wooden danda has no sharp splinters and the gilli ends are smooth to avoid skin cuts.
  4. Turn-by-Turn Striking: Only one striker swings the danda at any time; bystanders must remain strictly behind the batting mark.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Open space and field safety rules 2 Marks
Equipment integrity and turn discipline 2 Marks
Q11 Let us Explore • Cultural Heritage
Project Work: Research and document three indigenous traditional sports of India other than Gilli Danda.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  • 1. Kabaddi: An ancient contact tag sport requiring breath control (chanting 'kabaddi'), lightning raid agility, and tactical defensive tackling.
  • 2. Kho-Kho: High-speed chase game where nine sitting players face alternate directions, requiring rapid sprinting, dodging, and teamwork.
  • 3. Mallakhamb: Traditional gymnastics performed on a vertical wooden teak pole, developing extraordinary core strength, balance, and flexibility.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three traditional Indian sports accurately identified 1.5 Marks
Core athletic skills and rules documented for each 1.5 Marks
Q12 Reading is Fun • Thematic Inquiry & Character Study
In 'Gilli Danda', analyze how the central characters face and resolve their dilemma. What core moral value or life perspective does this convey to young readers about empathy, teamwork, or patience?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Thematic Analysis & Character Values:
1. Core Dilemma: In Gilli Danda, the characters encounter unexpected difficulties that challenge their confidence and understanding.
2. Resolution Strategy: Instead of acting impulsively or giving up, they listen carefully to each other, remain calm, and apply thoughtful cooperation.
3. Moral Insight: The story highlights that genuine empathy, perseverance, and honesty turn challenging situations into enriching growth opportunities.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of central dilemma and resolution 1.5 M
Expression of core moral value and life lesson 1.5 M
Q13 Vocabulary & Word Play • Idioms, Antonyms & Synonyms
Enhance your vocabulary through language skills based on 'Gilli Danda':
(a) Provide one synonym and one antonym for each word: courageous, generous, ancient.
(b) Choose one idiom connected to the theme of 'Gilli Danda', define it, and frame a meaningful sentence.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Vocabulary Analysis:

- Generous: Synonym: kind-hearted | Antonym: selfish.
- Ancient: Synonym: antique / historic | Antonym: modern.
• (b) Idiom: 'Turn over a new leaf' (to start behaving in a better and more responsible way).
Sentence: Inspired by the lessons of Gilli Danda, Amit decided to turn over a new leaf and complete his homework promptly every day.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate synonyms and antonyms for all 3 words 1.5 M
Correct idiom definition and well-structured context sentence 1.5 M
Q14 Applied Grammar • Tenses, Prepositions & Conjunctions
Apply grammar rules to complete the following exercises:
(a) Fill in the blanks with suitable prepositions (in, across, through, beneath): The brave traveller walked _______ the dense forest and found shelter _______ a giant banyan tree.
(b) Combine the sentences using an appropriate conjunction (although, because, so): The weather was rainy. We completed our football tournament.
(c) Convert into Past Continuous Tense: She reads an adventure tale.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Grammar Solutions:
• (a) The brave traveller walked through the dense forest and found shelter beneath a giant banyan tree.
• (b) Although the weather was rainy, we completed our football tournament.
• (c) Past Continuous: She was reading an adventure tale.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate prepositions: 'through' and 'beneath' 1.0 M
Appropriate subordinating conjunction 'Although' 1.0 M
Correct Past Continuous verb form 'was reading' 1.0 M
Q15 Talk Time • Experiential Sharing & Discussion
Imagine you experienced an unexpected challenge similar to the one depicted in 'Gilli Danda'. Describe how you felt, what decision you took, and what you learned from the experience.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Experiential Reflection:
• Context: While working on an inter-school science project, our model malfunctioned on the final morning before evaluation.
• Action: Remembering the teamwork and composure demonstrated in Gilli Danda, my partners and I divided the troubleshooting tasks calmly. We identified the loose wiring and repaired the connections together.
• Takeaway: Composure, active listening, and collective trust achieve far better results than panic during stressful moments.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Personal reflection grounded in chapter values 1.5 M
Clarity of learning takeaway and emotional maturity 1.5 M
Q16 Sentence Mechanics • Punctuation & Direct Speech
Punctuate and rewrite the following conversational passage correctly:
neha whispered to her teacher madam may i borrow the illustrated atlas from the school library i need it for my geography project
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Corrected Dialogue with Punctuation:
Neha whispered to her teacher, "Madam, may I borrow the illustrated atlas from the school library? I need it for my geography project."

Explanation: Added proper capitalization at the beginning of speech and proper nouns, comma before opening quotation marks, question mark at the end of the interrogative sentence, and closing quotation marks outside the final full stop.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate opening quotation marks, capitalization, and comma 1.5 M
Correct question mark and closing quotation mark placement 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Reviving Traditional Sports & Digital Detox for Youth

A public health survey revealed that 75% of primary students in an urban housing society spend over four hours daily on smartphones and tablets, leading to childhood obesity, poor social skills, and vitamin D deficiencies. The resident welfare association wants to launch an evening indigenous sports initiative.

Analytical Task: Draw on themes from 'Gilli Danda' to draft a 3-pillar action plan for the residential society to re-introduce traditional sports and encourage a healthy digital detox.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Three-Pillar Community Sports Revival Plan:
  1. Designate 'Vehicle-Free Play Hours': Block internal society lanes from car traffic every evening between 5:00 PM and 6:30 PM, creating safe, supervised zones for Gilli Danda, Kho-Kho, and Hopscotch.
  2. Grandparents' Traditional Sports Workshop: Invite elderly residents to teach children the authentic rules, wood-crafting tips, and tactics of indigenous games like Gilli Danda, building intergenerational warmth.
  3. Monthly Inter-Block Tournaments: Organize weekend mini-tournaments with handmade certificates, fruit baskets, and trophies to build pride and excitement around outdoor sports.
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for identifying screen addiction risks + 3.5 Marks for 3 feasible, vibrant community sports initiatives = 5 Marks
