Chapter 10: Glass Bangles

Inspirational narrative on traditional Indian handicrafts, creative innovation & community upliftment | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Themes of traditional artisanal craftsmanship, family entrepreneurship, and rural innovation.
  • The glassmaking heritage of Firozabad, Uttar Pradesh: turning recycled glass and sand into vibrant jewelry.
  • Ananya's artistic innovation: blending modern geometric and floral motifs with ancient furnace techniques.
  • The ripple effect: increased business uplifts local artisans, packers, traders, and women workers.
  • Grammar: compound words, process writing, and descriptive vocabulary.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (22 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Comprehension
Describe the traditional step-by-step process of making glass bangles inside Ravi's workshop.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
In Ravi's workshop, raw glass and recycled colorful shards are first placed inside high-temperature clay furnaces until they melt into glowing, malleable red liquid. Artisans dip long iron rods into the molten glass, wind the threads around rotating spiraled metal mandrels to form coils, cut them into individual rings with diamond cutters, and seamlessly solder the joints over narrow flame torches.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Melting raw/recycled glass in clay furnaces 1 Mark
Drawing molten threads onto rotating mandrels to form coils 1 Mark
Cutting and flame-soldering individual circular joints 1 Mark
Q2 Let us Think • Comprehension
Who was Ananya, and how did her creative artistic vision transform her father's traditional craft?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Ananya was Ravi's young, observant daughter. Recognizing that younger generations desired contemporary styles, she designed fresh floral motifs, delicate geometric patterns, and elegant pastel color combinations. By blending traditional glass craftsmanship with modern aesthetics, she created bangles that appealed to modern markets.

Ananya identified as Ravi's daughter 1 Mark

Blending ancient technique with contemporary aesthetic 1 Mark
Q3 Let us Think • Comprehension
How did the booming market success of the new bangle designs uplift the broader local community?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The huge surge in demand created extensive employment across the entire village: more local craftsmen were hired at fair wages to mold bangles; neighborhood women gained income decorating and packaging them in padded gift boxes; and local transport drivers prospered shipping cartons to city festival markets for Diwali.

Q8 Let us Write • Process Writing
Write a 4-step instructional guide on 'How to Safely Make a Decorative Paper Lantern for Festivals':
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Crafting a Festive Paper Lantern:
  1. Select & Fold Paper: Take a colorful rectangular sheet of craft paper and fold it in half lengthwise.
  2. Make Parallel Slits: Use safety scissors to cut parallel slits along the folded edge, stopping one inch before the outer margins.
  3. Roll into Cylinder: Unfold the sheet, curve it into a circular cylinder so the slits flare outward gracefully, and staple the edges together.
  4. Attach Handle & Hanger: Glue a contrasting paper strip to the top rim as a handle, and hang it near a doorway with fairy lights.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear 4-step chronological craft process 2 Marks
Action verbs, safety precautions, and precise instructions 2 Marks
Q9 Let us Explore • Cultural Geography
Research & Fact Card: Why is the city of Firozabad in Uttar Pradesh celebrated across the world as 'The Bangle City'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Firozabad: The Glass Heritage Capital of India:
  • Centuries-Old History: Glassmaking in Firozabad dates back over 200 years, producing glassware, chandeliers, and bangles for Mughal and Indian royal courts.
  • Global Hub: It is the largest producer of glass bangles in the world, employing hundreds of thousands of traditional glassblowers and artisans.
  • Cultural Symbolism: Glass bangles are an auspicious symbol of matrimonial happiness, prosperity, and joy celebrated across Indian festivals like Karwa Chauth, Teej, and Diwali.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Historical heritage of Firozabad explained 1.5 Marks
Economic scale and cultural significance of bangles 1.5 Marks

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Handloom & Handicraft Revitalisation in the Digital Age

Many young members of traditional artisan families across India are abandoning ancestral crafts like handloom weaving, pottery, and brass sculpting to take up unfulfilling low-wage urban labor, citing low income and exploitative middlemen in local bazaars.

Analytical Task: Drawing inspiration from Ananya's entrepreneurial success in 'Glass Bangles', formulate three innovative strategies that can empower traditional rural artisans to prosper in the digital era.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Three Strategies for Handicraft Revitalisation:
  1. Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) E-Commerce & Artisan Platforms: Establish village cooperative portals where artisans list their products directly on national and global e-commerce marketplaces (like ONDC, Etsy, and Amazon Karigar), bypassing predatory middlemen and retaining 90%+ of sales value.
  2. Design Mentorship & Design School Collaborations: Connect rural workshops with fashion and industrial design institutes (like NID and NIFT) to co-create modern lifestyle products (home decor, minimalist jewelry, tech sleeves) using traditional handmade techniques.
  3. Fair-Trade Artisan Storytelling: Attach a verifiable 'Handmade in India' tag and QR code to every product, sharing video stories of the specific artisan family who crafted it, appealing to ethical global consumers willing to pay premium prices.
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for identifying middleman exploitation and artisan abandonment + 3.5 Marks for 3 actionable digital/design/fair-trade business strategies = 5 Marks
