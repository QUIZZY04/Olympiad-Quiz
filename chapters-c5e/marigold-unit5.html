Chapter 5: The Lazy Frog & Rip Van Winkle

Humorous poem on sloth & Washington Irving's classic American folktale | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'The Lazy Frog': Fred the frog refuses to budge an inch, ignoring his elderly mother's calls for help and feigning deep sleep even when a lady frog hops past.
  • Prose 'Rip Van Winkle': Set in the Kaatskill mountains; Rip is a kind, good-natured man loved by village children and dogs, but hopelessly lazy about tending his own farm.
  • The Encounter with Strange Mountain Men: Rip assists a short, stout man dressed in antique Dutch clothing carrying a heavy barrel; watches a silent game of ninepins.
  • The 20-Year Slumber: Rip drinks from the sweet barrel and falls into a profound two-decade sleep; wakes to find his dog gone, gun rusted, beard a foot long, and the village transformed.
  • Themes & Grammar: Consequences of chronic procrastination, silent letters in English spelling, prefixes of negation (un-, dis-, in-).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (21 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem (The Lazy Frog)
What does Fred the frog do all day? How does he react when his elderly mother calls for help or when a lady frog hops past?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Fred does absolutely nothing all day; he lies sprawled like a useless lump on a silent log. When his poor old mother calls him to do errands, he refuses to move even a single inch and pretends to be hard of hearing. Even when a graceful lady frog hops past, he does not open an eye or bow politely.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sprawls on a log doing zero work 1 Mark
Ignores elderly mother's calls 1 Mark
Refuses to bow or open an eye for lady frog 1 Mark
Q2 Reading is Fun • Story (Rip Van Winkle)
What kind of man was Rip Van Winkle? Mention two positive traits and one major personal flaw.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Rip was a simple, kind-hearted, and friendly neighbour who was universally loved by village children because he played marbles with them, made kites, and told endless stories. His dog Wolf was his faithful companion. However, his fatal flaw was that he was incorrigibly lazy regarding his own work—he let fences fall apart, weeds choke his crops, and avoided all farm labour.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Positive traits: kind, friendly to children/neighbours 1.5 Marks
Major flaw: extreme laziness regarding his own farm and livelihood 1.5 Marks
Q3 Reading is Fun • Story (Rip Van Winkle)
Describe the strange men playing ninepins in the Kaatskill mountains.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The strange men were short, odd-looking beings dressed in quaint antique Dutch doublets with big buttons. They had long, bushy beards of diverse colours and shapes. Though playing a noisy bowling game (ninepins) whose sounds echoed like rolling thunder through the peaks, their faces remained utterly solemn and silent.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Odd short figures in antique Dutch clothing 1.5 Marks
Solemn, silent faces despite playing thunderous ninepins 1.5 Marks
Q4 Reading is Fun • Story (Rip Van Winkle)
What startling changes did Rip Van Winkle discover when he woke up and returned to his village?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Upon waking, Rip discovered: (1) His clean gun was replaced by a rusty, corroded barrel, (2) His companion dog Wolf had disappeared, (3) His grey beard had grown a foot long, and (4) In the village, all houses were new, the people wore strange clothes, and not a single old acquaintance recognized him.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rusted gun and foot-long white beard 1.5 Marks
Village transformed, old friends replaced by strangers 1.5 Marks

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Procrastination and Time Management for Youth

Rip Van Winkle was eager to help any neighbor in the village run errands, but when it came to maintaining his own home and family fields, he continually postponed chores until twenty years slipped away while he slept.

Analytical Task: Analyze Rip's habit of chronic avoidance. What three actionable strategies can students employ to prevent procrastination and cultivate daily self-discipline?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Three Strategies to Overcome Chronic Procrastination:
  1. The 'Two-Minute Rule': If a pending task takes less than two minutes (putting away books, organizing a desk, submitting a notebook), do it immediately without postponing.
  2. Breaking Large Goals into Bite-Sized Milestones: Chronic delay happens when chores feel overwhelming. Divide a bulky assignment into three 15-minute study sprints with 5-minute reward breaks.
  3. Ownership and Personal Duty First: Learn Rip's vital life lesson: being pleasant to strangers does not compensate for neglecting one's primary responsibilities at home and school.
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for analyzing Rip's avoidance behavior + 3.5 Marks for 3 practical psychological productivity techniques = 5 Marks
