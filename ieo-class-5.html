Class 5 IEO Syllabus & Exam Pattern

The Class 5 English Olympiad test assesses students across four major sections. Our mock tests align with the latest SOF pattern.

  • Section 1
    Word and Structure Knowledge

    Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms and Phrases, Nouns, Pronouns, Verbs, Adverbs, Adjectives, Prepositions, Conjunctions, Tenses, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech.

  • Section 2
    Reading

    Search for and retrieve information from various text types like news reports, time tables, messages, and factual passages.

  • Section 3
    Spoken and Written Expression

    Ability to understand situation-based variations in functions like requests, apologies, and greetings.

  • Section 4
    Achievers Section

    Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions based on the grammar and vocabulary syllabus. These carry 3 marks each.

Why English Olympiads are Crucial in Class 5

In Class 5, English moves beyond basic grammar to more complex structures like active/passive voice, idioms, and advanced reading comprehension. The Class 5 English Olympiad helps solidify these advanced concepts.

By practicing our IEO Class 5 mock tests, students develop a rich vocabulary and a keen eye for grammatical errors. The Reading section specifically trains students to process information quickly—a vital skill for higher education.
