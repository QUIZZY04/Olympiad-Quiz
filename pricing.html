Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I run out of tests on the Free plan?
You get 1 test a day, up to 4 a week, on the Free plan. Once you hit that limit, you'll see a countdown until your next test unlocks - or you can upgrade to Silver, Gold or Diamond for unlimited or much higher limits right away.
Is Silver really capped, unlike Gold and Diamond?
Yes. Silver (₹199/month) gives you up to 4 tests a day and 40 a month, with a 1-hour wait between attempts - a big step up from Free, but not unlimited. Gold and Diamond remove the daily/monthly cap and the wait time entirely, so you can attempt as many chapterwise and mock tests as you like, back to back.
What are "All India Live Tests" and how do the credits work?
Live Tests are timed, All India ranked tests held on a schedule (see Live Arena). Each normally costs ₹99 to enter. Gold includes 2 Live Test credits every month (worth ₹198) and Diamond includes 4 every month (worth ₹396) - both automatically, at no extra cost.
What does "Personal Olympiad guidance" in Diamond include?
Diamond members get personal guidance for upcoming Olympiad exams - help with exam selection, preparation strategy, and priority support from our team, on top of everything included in Gold.
Can I cancel or change my plan later?
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your dashboard. You'll keep premium access until the end of your current billing period (monthly for Silver, every 3 months for Gold, yearly for Diamond) - no partial refunds, but no further charges either.
