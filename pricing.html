Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I run out of tests on the Free plan?
You get 1 test a day, up to 4 a week, on the Free plan. Once you hit that limit, you'll see a countdown until your next test unlocks - or you can upgrade to Silver, Gold or Diamond for unlimited tests right away.
What's the difference between Silver, Gold and Diamond?
All three paid plans give unlimited chapterwise, mock and HOTS tests with full analytics. Silver has a 1-hour wait between tests; Gold and Diamond remove that wait entirely. The difference is how long you pay for at once and the extras: Silver renews every month (₹149), Gold every 3 months (₹349, just ₹116/month, no waiting, plus 2 free Live Tests a month) and Diamond every 6 months (₹599, just ₹100/month, no waiting, plus 4 free Live Tests a month, personal Olympiad guidance and priority support).
Why does Silver have a 1-hour wait between tests?
Silver still gives you unlimited test attempts overall - there's just a 1-hour gap required between the start of one test and the next, which encourages reviewing your results before jumping into another attempt. If you want to take tests back-to-back with zero waiting, Gold and Diamond remove this wait completely.
What are "All India Live Tests" and how do the credits work?
Live Tests are timed, All India ranked tests held on a schedule (see Live Arena). Each normally costs ₹99 to enter. Gold includes 2 Live Test credits every month (worth ₹198) and Diamond includes 4 every month (worth ₹396) - both automatically, at no extra cost.
What does "Personal Olympiad guidance" in Diamond include?
Diamond members get personal guidance for upcoming Olympiad exams - help with exam selection, preparation strategy, and priority support from our team, on top of everything included in Gold.
Can I cancel or change my plan later?
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your dashboard. You'll keep premium access until the end of your current billing period (monthly for Silver, every 3 months for Gold, every 6 months for Diamond) - no partial refunds, but no further charges either.
