Chapter 10: Tenths and Hundredths

दसवाँ और सौवाँ भाग (दशमलव, मिलीमीटर-सेंटीमीटर एवं मुद्रा गणना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Understanding Decimals: Tenths (1/10 = 0.1) and Hundredths (1/100 = 0.01).
  • Length measurement: 1 cm = 10 mm ⇒ 1 mm = 0.1 cm.
  • Currency notation: 1 Rupee = 100 paise ⇒ 1 paisa = ₹0.01; 75 paise = ₹0.75.
  • Temperature reading and comparing decimal values across different Indian cities.
  • (a) 4 cm 8 mm: Since $1\text{ mm} = 0.1\text{ cm}$, $8\text{ mm} = 0.8\text{ cm}$. Total = $4 + 0.8 = \mathbf{4.8\text{ cm}}$.
  • (b) 75 paise: Since $100\text{ paise} = ₹1$, $75\text{ paise} = \frac{75}{100} = \mathbf{₹0.75}$.
  • (c) 3 m 45 cm: Since $100\text{ cm} = 1\text{ m}$, $45\text{ cm} = 0.45\text{ m}$. Total = $3 + 0.45 = \mathbf{3.45\text{ m}}$.
  • (d) 5 mm: $\frac{5}{10} = \mathbf{0.5\text{ cm}}$.

Q1 Textbook Exercise • Pencil Measurement in Millimetres
Look at a ruler: (a) How many millimetres are there in 1 centimetre? (b) What fraction of a centimetre is 1 mm? (c) A small pencil measures 3 cm and 6 mm. Write its length in centimetres using decimals.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Metric Conversions:
• (a) Millimetres in 1 cm: 10 millimetres (10 mm).
• (b) Fraction: 1 mm = 1/10 of a cm.
• (c) Decimal notation: 3 cm + 6/10 cm = 3.6 cm.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 10 mm 1 M
Part (b): 1/10 cm 1 M
Part (c): 3.6 cm 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Frog Jump Lengths
Two species of frogs are found in India: A Gold Frog is 0.9 cm long. A Bull Frog is 30.5 cm long. (a) How many millimetres long is the Gold Frog? (b) About how many Gold Frogs can sit on the body of one Bull Frog?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations:
• (a) Gold Frog in mm: 0.9 cm = 0.9 × 10 = 9 mm.
• (b) Number of Gold Frogs: 30.5 cm ÷ 0.9 cm ≈ 305 ÷ 9 = 33.88 (about 33 or 34 frogs).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 9 mm 1.5 M
Part (b): About 33-34 frogs 1.5 M
Q3 Textbook Exercise • Money in Decimals (Paise to Rupee)
Convert the following paise amounts into rupees using decimals:
(a) 50 paise
(b) 75 paise
(c) 5 paise
(d) 250 paise
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Rupee Conversions (1 Rupee = 100 paise; divide by 100):
• (a) 50 paise = 50 ÷ 100 = ₹0.50.
• (b) 75 paise = 75 ÷ 100 = ₹0.75.
• (c) 5 paise = 5 ÷ 100 = ₹0.05 (note the zero in tenths place!).
• (d) 250 paise = 250 ÷ 100 = ₹2.50.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
₹0.50 1 M
₹0.75 1 M
₹0.05 (vital zero check) 1 M
₹2.50 1 M
Q4 Textbook Exercise • City Temperature Readings
The temperature recorded at 3:00 PM in different cities on 16 January were:
• Chennai: 29.9°C
• Mumbai: 35.1°C
• Srinagar: 8.1°C
• Jaipur: 37.9°C
(a) Which place was the hottest at 3 PM? (b) Which place was the coolest? (c) How much higher was Mumbai's temperature than Srinagar's?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Temperature Analysis:
• (a) Hottest place: Jaipur (37.9°C).
• (b) Coolest place: Srinagar (8.1°C).
• (c) Difference between Mumbai and Srinagar: 35.1°C - 8.1°C = 27.0°C higher.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hottest: Jaipur (37.9°C) 1 M
Coolest: Srinagar (8.1°C) 1 M
Difference: 27.0°C 1 M
Q5 Textbook Exercise • Sports Day Long Jump
In the School Sports Day long jump event: Teena jumped 3.50 m, Meena jumped 4.05 m, and Rehana jumped 4.50 m. (a) Who jumped the longest distance? (b) By how many metres did Rehana beat Teena? Express your answer in metres and centimetres.

Q9 Textbook Exercise • Comparing Decimals
Put >, < or = between the decimal numbers:
(a) 0.5 and 0.05
(b) 3.45 and 3.54
(c) 1.20 and 1.2
(d) 0.89 and 0.9
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Decimal Comparisons:
• (a) 0.5 & 0.05: 5 tenths > 0 tenths. 0.5 > 0.05.
• (b) 3.45 & 3.54: 4 tenths < 5 tenths. 3.45 < 3.54.
• (c) 1.20 & 1.2: Trailing zeros do not change value. 1.20 = 1.2.
• (d) 0.89 & 0.9: 0.89 < 0.90 (8 tenths < 9 tenths). 0.89 < 0.9.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
0.5 > 0.05 1 M
3.45 < 3.54 1 M
1.20 = 1.2 1 M
0.89 < 0.9 1 M
Q10 Textbook Exercise • Place Value in Decimals
In the number 45.68:
(a) What is the place value of digit 6?
(b) What is the place value of digit 8?
(c) Write 45.68 in expanded form.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Decimal Place Value:
• (a) Digit 6: In tenths place = 6/10 = 0.6.
• (b) Digit 8: In hundredths place = 8/100 = 0.08.
• (c) Expanded form: 40 + 5 + 6/10 + 8/100 (or 40 + 5 + 0.6 + 0.08).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tenths / 0.6 1 M
Hundredths / 0.08 1 M
Expanded notation 1 M

Q17 Textbook Exercise • Hundredths Grid Model
A large square is divided into 100 small equal squares. (a) What fraction and what decimal does 1 small square represent? (b) If 38 squares are shaded green, what decimal does the shaded region represent? (c) What decimal represents the unshaded region?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Hundredths Grid:
• (a) 1 small square: Fraction = 1/100, Decimal = 0.01.
• (b) 38 shaded squares: 38/100 = 0.38.
• (c) Unshaded region: 100 - 38 = 62 squares. Decimal = 62/100 = 0.62.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Unshaded: 0.62 1 M

Q20 Textbook Exercise • Rounding Off Decimals
Round off the following decimals to the nearest whole number:
(a) 7.3
(b) 12.8
(c) 24.5
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Rounding Rules:
• (a) 7.3: Tenths digit is 3 (< 5), rounds down to 7.
• (b) 12.8: Tenths digit is 8 (≥ 5), rounds up to 13.
• (c) 24.5: Tenths digit is 5 (halfway), standard rule rounds up to 25.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
7 1 M
13 1 M
25 1 M
