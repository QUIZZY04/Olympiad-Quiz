Chapter 9: Boxes and Sketches

डिब्बे और रेखाचित्र (त्रिविमीय आकृतियाँ, जालक एवं गहरे चित्र) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • 2D representations of 3D objects: Floor maps vs Deep drawings (perspective views).
  • Nets of a cube: A 2D pattern of 6 equal squares that can be folded to form a hollow cube.
  • Opposite faces of a standard dice always add up to 7 (1+6, 2+5, 3+4).
  • Open boxes (nets with 5 squares) vs closed boxes (nets with 6 squares).
  • (a) Bottom face: Opposite to 3 is $7 - 3 = \mathbf{4}$.
  • (b) Back face: Opposite to 1 is $7 - 1 = \mathbf{6}$.
  • (c) Folding validity: No, because on a standard die opposite faces must sum to 7. Here $4 + 2 = 6 \neq 7$. Opposite to 4 must be 3, and opposite to 2 must be 5.

(a) How many faces, edges, and vertices does a cube have?
(b) In a standard playing dice, what is the sum of dots on opposite faces? If face 4 is at the top, what number is on the bottom face?
(c) Will a flat strip of 6 squares arranged in a single straight row fold into a cube? Explain why or why not.
[3 Marks]

Q5 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Boxes and Sketches', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 143 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 15. Find the next three terms (158, 173, 188) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{143}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{15}$.
3. Term 2: 143 + 15 = 158.
4. Term 3: 158 + 15 = 173.
5. Term 4: 173 + 15 = 188.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 143 + (n - 1) \times 15$. Successive addition of 15 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (158, 173, 188) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+15) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q7 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Boxes and Sketches' has length $L = 14\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (14 + 5) = 2 \times 19 = \mathbf{38\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 14 \times 5 = \mathbf{70\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 38 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 70 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Boxes and Sketches': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 172. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 172$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 172 + 16 = 188$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{188}{4} = \mathbf{47}$.
4. Verification: (47 × 4) - 16 = 188 - 16 = 172. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 188 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 47 with full verification 1.5 M
