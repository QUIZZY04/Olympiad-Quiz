Chapter 14: How Big? How Heavy?

कितना बड़ा? कितना भारी? (आयतन, घन सेमी, जल विस्थापन एवं भार तुलना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Volume is the 3D space occupied by an object: Volume of Cuboid = Length × Breadth × Height.
  • Standard unit of volume: Cubic centimetre (cu cm or cm³).
  • Water displacement method: Volume of an irregular solid = Volume of water displaced.
  • Packing volume: Number of small unit cubes that can fit into a larger carton box.
  • Given: Volume displaced by 6 marbles = 12 ml.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
(a) A measuring cylinder contains water up to the 50 mL mark. When 5 identical glass marbles are dropped into it, the water level rises to 65 mL. What is the volume of all 5 marbles together, and what is the volume of 1 marble?
(b) A matchbox has length 5 cm, breadth 3 cm, and height 1 cm. What is its volume in cubic centimetres?
(c) How many such matchboxes can be packed into a carton measuring 15 cm by 9 cm by 10 cm?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Marble Volume: Total = 65 - 50 = 15 cu cm. One marble = 15/5 = 3 cu cm.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 15 cu cm & 3 cu cm 1.5 Marks
Part (b): 15 cu cm 1 Mark
Part (c): 90 matchboxes 1.5 Marks

Q3 Textbook Exercise
A measuring glass is filled with water. Dropping 6 identical glass marbles into the water raises the water level by 12 ml. (a) What is the volume of 1 marble in ml? (b) How many ml of water will be displaced if 25 marbles are dropped?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Given: Volume displaced by 6 marbles = 12 ml.
  2. (a) Volume of 1 marble:
    $\text{Volume} = \frac{12}{6} = \mathbf{2\text{ ml}}$.
  3. (b) Volume displaced by 25 marbles:
    $\text{Volume} = 25 \times 2\text{ ml} = \mathbf{50\text{ ml}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 12 / 6 = 2 ml 1.5 Marks
Part (b): 25 × 2 = 50 ml 1.5 Marks
Q4 Textbook Exercise
A big cardboard carton has internal dimensions: length = 30 cm, width = 20 cm, and height = 10 cm. How many small cubic blocks of side 2 cm can be completely packed inside this carton?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Volume of the cardboard carton:
    $\text{Carton Volume} = 30 \times 20 \times 10 = \mathbf{6,000\text{ cm}^3}$.
  2. Volume of one cubic block:
    $\text{Block Volume} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = \mathbf{8\text{ cm}^3}$.
  3. Number of blocks packed:
    $\text{Number} = \frac{\text{Carton Volume}}{\text{Block Volume}} = \frac{6,000}{8} = \mathbf{750\text{ blocks}}$.

Grid Check: Along length: $30 \div 2 = 15$. Along width: $20 \div 2 = 10$. Along height: $10 \div 2 = 5$. Total $= 15 \times 10 \times 5 = 750$ blocks.

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Carton volume = 30 × 20 × 10 = 6,000 cm³ 1.5 Marks
Block volume = 2³ = 8 cm³ 1 Mark
Total blocks = 6000 / 8 = 750 blocks 1.5 Marks

Q7 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'How Big? How Heavy?', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 218 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 20. Find the next three terms (238, 258, 278) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{218}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{20}$.
3. Term 2: 218 + 20 = 238.
4. Term 3: 238 + 20 = 258.
5. Term 4: 258 + 20 = 278.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 218 + (n - 1) \times 20$. Successive addition of 20 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (238, 258, 278) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+20) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q9 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'How Big? How Heavy?' has length $L = 13\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (13 + 5) = 2 \times 18 = \mathbf{36\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 13 \times 5 = \mathbf{65\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 36 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 65 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625 1.0 M

Q11 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'How Big? How Heavy?': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 212. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 212$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 212 + 16 = 228$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{228}{4} = \mathbf{57}$.
4. Verification: (57 × 4) - 16 = 228 - 16 = 212. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 228 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 57 with full verification 1.5 M
