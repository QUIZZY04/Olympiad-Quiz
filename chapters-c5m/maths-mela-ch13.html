Chapter 13: Animal Jumps

जानवरों की छलाँग (गुणज, सार्व गुणज एवं लघुत्तम समापवर्त्य - LCM) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Multiples of a number: The numbers obtained by multiplying it by natural numbers (1, 2, 3, …).
  • Common Multiples: Numbers that appear in the multiple lists of two or more given numbers.
  • Lowest Common Multiple (LCM): The smallest non-zero common multiple of given numbers.
  • Step/jump word problems on a number line representing periodic cycles.
  • Frog lands on multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30, 33, 36, 39, 42, 45, 48...
  • Rabbit lands on multiples of 7: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49...
  • (a) Common steps under 50: 21 and 42.
  • (b) First common step: $\text{LCM}(3, 7) = \mathbf{21}$.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
A frog jumps 3 steps at a time, and a rabbit jumps 5 steps at a time. Both start at step 0.
(a) List the first six steps landed on by the frog.
(b) List the first six steps landed on by the rabbit.
(c) At which common steps will both land? What is the first common step?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Frog: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 1 Mark
Part (b): 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 1 Mark
Part (c): 15, 30... and LCM 15 2 Marks

Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of:
(a) 4 and 6
(b) 6 and 9
(c) 3, 4, and 6

Q3 Textbook Exercise
(a) Write all the factors of 18 and 24.
(b) What are their common factors?

[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18.
Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.

Part (a): Complete factor lists 1 Mark
Part (b): Common factors 1, 2, 3, 6 1 Mark
Part (c): HCF = 6 1 Mark
Q4 Textbook Exercise
A frog and a rabbit start jumping together from step 0. The frog jumps 3 steps at a time, while the rabbit jumps 7 steps at a time. (a) At what common steps between 0 and 50 will they both land? (b) What is the first step where both will land together?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Frog lands on multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30, 33, 36, 39, 42, 45, 48...
  2. Rabbit lands on multiples of 7: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49...
  3. (a) Common steps under 50: 21 and 42.
  4. (b) First common step: $\text{LCM}(3, 7) = \mathbf{21}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Listing multiples and finding 21 and 42 2 Marks
First common step = 21 1 Mark

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency Question: Traffic Light Synchronization

Three traffic signals at a busy junction change their lights after every 40 seconds, 60 seconds, and 75 seconds respectively. If they all change simultaneously at 8:00 AM, when will they change together next?

Analytical Task: Find the time at which all three traffic lights will change together again.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
LCM(40, 60, 75) = 600 seconds = 10 minutes.
Next change time = 8:00 AM + 10 min = 8:10 AM.
