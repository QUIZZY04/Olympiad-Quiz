Chapter 4: Parts and Wholes

हिस्से और पूरे (राष्ट्रीय ध्वज, चॉकलेट बंटवारा एवं रामू का खेत) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Fractions in everyday life: tricolour flags, chocolate bars, and garden plots.
  • Fraction of a collection: e.g., 1/4 of 16 balls = 4 balls.
  • Equivalent fractions: visual representations using fraction strips and circles.
  • Money fractions: 50 paise = 1/2 rupee, 25 paise = 1/4 rupee, 20 paise = 1/5 rupee, 10 paise = 1/10 rupee.
  • Total equal parts: 9.
  • Total pieces of chocolate: 12.
  • Raji's share: $\frac{1}{4} \times 12 = \mathbf{3\text{ pieces}}$.
  • Sugatha's share: $\frac{1}{3} \times 12 = \mathbf{4\text{ pieces}}$.
  • Sheela's share: $\frac{1}{6} \times 12 = \mathbf{2\text{ pieces}}$.
  • Total pieces given away: $3 + 4 + 2 = 9\text{ pieces}$.
  • Pieces left for Manju: $12 - 9 = \mathbf{3\text{ pieces}}$.
  • Fraction left for Manju: $\frac{3}{12} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{4}}$.

Q4 Textbook Exercise
Ramu's field has 9 equal parts. In these parts he grows vegetables: 3 parts potatoes, 2 parts brinjals, 1 part spinach, and 3 parts tomatoes. (a) What fraction of the field is used for growing tomatoes? (b) What vegetable occupies the smallest part of the field? (c) What fraction is used for potatoes and brinjals combined?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Total equal parts: 9.
  2. (a) Fraction for tomatoes:
    $\text{Fraction} = \frac{3}{9} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{3}}$.
  3. (b) Smallest vegetable part:
    Spinach occupies 1 part out of 9 ($\frac{1}{9}$), which is the smallest fraction.
  4. (c) Fraction for potatoes and brinjals combined:
    $\text{Combined Fraction} = \frac{3}{9} + \frac{2}{9} = \mathbf{\frac{5}{9}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 3/9 = 1/3 for tomatoes 1.5 Marks
Part (b): Spinach (1/9) is the smallest part 1 Mark
Part (c): 3/9 + 2/9 = 5/9 combined fraction 1.5 Marks

Q8 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Parts and Wholes', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 68 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 10. Find the next three terms (78, 88, 98) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{68}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{10}$.
3. Term 2: 68 + 10 = 78.
4. Term 3: 78 + 10 = 88.
5. Term 4: 88 + 10 = 98.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 68 + (n - 1) \times 10$. Successive addition of 10 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (78, 88, 98) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+10) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q10 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Parts and Wholes' has length $L = 15\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (15 + 5) = 2 \times 20 = \mathbf{40\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 15 \times 5 = \mathbf{75\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 75 × ₹25 = ₹1875.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 40 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 75 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 75 × ₹25 = ₹1875 1.0 M

Q12 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Parts and Wholes': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 132. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 132$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 132 + 16 = 148$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{148}{4} = \mathbf{37}$.
4. Verification: (37 × 4) - 16 = 148 - 16 = 132. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 148 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 37 with full verification 1.5 M
