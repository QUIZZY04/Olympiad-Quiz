Chapter 7: Can You See the Pattern?

क्या तुम पैटर्न देख सकते हो? (जादुई वर्ग, षट्कोण, पैलिंड्रोम एवं संख्या पहेलियाँ) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Rotational patterns of motifs: 1/4 turn, 1/2 turn, 3/4 turn.
  • 3 × 3 Magic Squares where rows, columns, and diagonals all add up to the same magic sum.
  • Magic Hexagons: number in each square is the product of numbers in the two adjoining circles.
  • Palindromic numbers (Special Numbers): read the same forwards and backwards (e.g., 121, 656, 1331) formed by reversing and adding.

Fill in the numbers from 46 to 54 in a 3 × 3 grid so that the sum of each row, column, and diagonal is 150. (Hint: Central number is (46 + 54) ÷ 2 = 50).

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Magic sum = 150. The center number must be 150 ÷ 3 = 50.

Row 1: 53, 48, 49 ⇒ 53 + 48 + 49 = 150

Q4 Textbook Exercise
Fill the numbers from 46 to 54 in a 3×3 Magic Square grid such that the sum of the numbers in every row, column, and diagonal equals 150.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Center Number Rule: The middle cell is the average of the 9 numbers: $\frac{46 + 54}{2} = \mathbf{50}$.

Completed Magic Square Grid:

53 46 51
48 50 52
49 54 47

Check Sums:
Row 1: $53 + 46 + 51 = 150$.
Row 2: $48 + 50 + 52 = 150$.
Row 3: $49 + 54 + 47 = 150$.
Diagonals: $53 + 50 + 47 = 150$, $51 + 50 + 49 = 150$.

Correct cell placements satisfying row, column, diagonal sums = 150 3 Marks

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Can You See the Pattern?', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 113 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 13. Find the next three terms (126, 139, 152) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{113}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{13}$.
3. Term 2: 113 + 13 = 126.
4. Term 3: 126 + 13 = 139.
5. Term 4: 139 + 13 = 152.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 113 + (n - 1) \times 13$. Successive addition of 13 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (126, 139, 152) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+13) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Can You See the Pattern?' has length $L = 12\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (12 + 5) = 2 \times 17 = \mathbf{34\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 12 \times 5 = \mathbf{60\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 60 × ₹25 = ₹1500.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 34 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 60 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 60 × ₹25 = ₹1500 1.0 M

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Can You See the Pattern?': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 156. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 156$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 156 + 16 = 172$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{172}{4} = \mathbf{43}$.
4. Verification: (43 × 4) - 16 = 172 - 16 = 156. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 172 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 43 with full verification 1.5 M
