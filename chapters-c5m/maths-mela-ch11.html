Chapter 11: Grandmother's Quilt

दादी की रजाई (क्षेत्रफल, परिमाप एवं वर्ग ग्रिड) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Difference between Perimeter (length of outer boundary) and Area (measure of flat surface enclosed).
  • Formulas: Perimeter of Rectangle = 2 × (Length + Breadth); Area of Rectangle = Length × Breadth.
  • Formulas for Squares: Perimeter = 4 × Side; Area = Side × Side.
  • Counting 1 cm grid squares to estimate areas of irregular shapes and quilt patches.
  • Definition: Tessellation is the tiling of a flat two-dimensional plane using one or more geometric shapes with zero gaps and zero overlaps.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
Grandmother stitches a rectangular patchwork quilt measuring 200 cm long and 150 cm wide.
(a) What is the total length of border lace required to stitch around the boundary?
(b) What is the total surface area of the quilt in square centimetres and square metres?
(c) If the lace costs ₹15 per metre, what is the cost of the lace?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Border Lace (Perimeter): 2 × (200 + 150) = 2 × 350 = 700 cm = 7 metres.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 700 cm / 7 m 1.5 Marks
Part (b): 30,000 sq cm / 3 sq m 1.5 Marks
Part (c): ₹105 1 Mark
Q2 Textbook Exercise
Two patches have the following dimensions:
• Patch 1: Square of side 6 cm.
• Patch 2: Rectangle measuring 9 cm by 4 cm.
(a) Find the area of Patch 1 and Patch 2. Are their areas equal?
(b) Find the perimeter of both patches. Which patch has a greater perimeter and by how much?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Areas:
Patch 1: 6 × 6 = 36 sq cm.
Patch 2: 9 × 4 = 36 sq cm.
Yes, both areas are equal.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): Both 36 sq cm 2 Marks
Part (b): 24 cm vs 26 cm, diff 2 cm 2 Marks
Q3 Textbook Exercise
A rectangular park is 40 m long and 25 m wide.
(a) Find its perimeter and area.
(b) A square park has the same perimeter as this rectangular park. What is the length of each side of the square park?
(c) Find the area of the square park. Which park encloses more space and by how much?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Rectangular Park:
Perimeter = 2 × (40 + 25) = 2 × 65 = 130 metres.
Area = 40 × 25 = 1,000 sq metres.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): P = 130 m, A = 1,000 sq m 1.5 Marks
Part (b): Side = 32.5 m 1 Mark
Part (c): Area = 1,056.25 sq m; Square larger by 56.25 sq m 1.5 Marks
Q4 Textbook Exercise
What is tessellation? Can regular pentagons tile a flat surface without gaps or overlaps? Give mathematical reasons based on interior angles.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Definition: Tessellation is the tiling of a flat two-dimensional plane using one or more geometric shapes with zero gaps and zero overlaps.
  2. Regular Pentagons: No, regular pentagons cannot tessellate.
    Each interior angle of a regular pentagon is $108^\circ$. To tile around a vertex, the sum of angles must be exactly $360^\circ$.
    $3 \times 108^\circ = 324^\circ$ (leaving a $36^\circ$ gap).
    $4 \times 108^\circ = 432^\circ$ (overlapping). Since 360 is not divisible by 108, regular pentagons cannot form a tiling pattern.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tessellation definition 1 Mark
Proof that 360 is not divisible by 108° 2 Marks

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency Question: School Garden Lawn Turfing

A school garden has a square lawn of side 15 metres. Grass turf tiles of size 50 cm by 50 cm cost ₹40 each.

Analytical Task: (a) What is the area of the lawn in square metres?
(b) How many turf tiles are needed to cover the entire lawn?
(c) What is the total cost of turfing?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Lawn Area: 15 × 15 = 225 sq metres.
(b) Number of tiles: Tile = 0.25 sq m ⇒ 225/0.25 = 900 tiles.
(c) Total Cost: 900 × ₹40 = ₹36,000.
