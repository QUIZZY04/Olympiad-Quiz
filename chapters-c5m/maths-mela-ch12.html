Chapter 12: Racing Seconds

दौड़ते सेकंड (समय, सेकंड-मिनट-घंटे, डिजिटल स्टॉपवॉच एवं 24-घंटे घड़ी) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Units of time: 1 hour = 60 minutes; 1 minute = 60 seconds; 1 hour = 3,600 seconds.
  • Elapsed Time: Elapsed Time = Finish Time - Start Time.
  • 24-Hour Railway Clock: 1:00 PM = 13:00, 4:30 PM = 16:30, 11:45 PM = 23:45.
  • Precision timekeeping with stopwatches (seconds and fractions of seconds).

Q1 Textbook Exercise
(a) Convert 3 hours 25 minutes into minutes.
(b) Convert 4 minutes 18 seconds into seconds.
(c) Convert into 24-hour clock time: (i) 8:30 AM, (ii) 7:15 PM, (iii) 11:50 PM.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Minutes: (3 × 60) + 25 = 205 minutes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 205 mins 1 Mark
Part (b): 258 secs 1 Mark
Part (c): 08:30, 19:15, 23:50 2 Marks

Q4 Textbook Exercise
A sprinter runs a 200-meter race in 25 seconds. (a) What is the sprinter's average speed in meters per second (m/s)? (b) Convert this speed to kilometers per hour (km/h).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. (a) Speed in m/s:
    $\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{200\text{ m}}{25\text{ s}} = \mathbf{8\text{ m/s}}$.
  2. (b) Conversion to km/h:
    Multiply by $\frac{18}{5}$ (since $1\text{ m/s} = 3.6\text{ km/h}$):
    $\text{Speed} = 8 \times \frac{18}{5} = \frac{144}{5} = \mathbf{28.8\text{ km/h}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Speed = 200 / 25 = 8 m/s 1.5 Marks
Conversion 8 × 18/5 = 28.8 km/h 1.5 Marks

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Racing Seconds', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 188 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 18. Find the next three terms (206, 224, 242) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{188}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{18}$.
3. Term 2: 188 + 18 = 206.
4. Term 3: 206 + 18 = 224.
5. Term 4: 224 + 18 = 242.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 188 + (n - 1) \times 18$. Successive addition of 18 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (206, 224, 242) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+18) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Racing Seconds' has length $L = 11\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (11 + 5) = 2 \times 16 = \mathbf{32\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 11 \times 5 = \mathbf{55\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 55 × ₹25 = ₹1375.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 32 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 55 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 55 × ₹25 = ₹1375 1.0 M

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Racing Seconds': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 196. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 196$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 196 + 16 = 212$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{212}{4} = \mathbf{53}$.
4. Verification: (53 × 4) - 16 = 212 - 16 = 196. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 212 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 53 with full verification 1.5 M

Competency Question: Intercity Vande Bharat Express Timetable

The Vande Bharat Express departs New Delhi station at 06:00 hours and arrives at Varanasi Junction at 14:00 hours, covering a rail distance of 759 km.

Analytical Task: (a) What is the total travel duration of the train in hours?
(b) What is the average speed of the train in km/h?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Duration: 14:00 - 06:00 = 8 hours.
(b) Average Speed: 759/8 = 94.875 km/h.
