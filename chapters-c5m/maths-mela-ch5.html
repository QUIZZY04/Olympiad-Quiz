Chapter 5: Far and Near

दूर और पास (मानचित्र पैमाना, दूरियाँ एवं दिशा ज्ञान) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Map Scale: A ratio relating distance on a drawing/map to actual ground distance (e.g., 1 cm = 200 m).
  • Converting map measurements: Actual Distance = Map Distance in cm × Scale Value.
  • Metric length conversions: 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 m = 100 cm.
  • Shortest straight path (aerial distance) vs route distance along road turns.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (22 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise
On a city map, the scale is given as 1 cm = 250 metres.
(a) The distance between Library and Bus Stand is 4.8 cm on the map. Find the actual distance on ground in metres and kilometres.
(b) If the railway station is 2,000 metres away from town hall, what will be the distance between them on this map?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Actual Distance: 4.8 × 250 m = 1,200 metres = 1.2 km.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 1,200 m and 1.2 km 2 Marks
Part (b): 8 cm 2 Marks
Q2 Textbook Exercise
Rahul travels from home to school. Distance to bus stop is 350 m. The bus travels 1.5 km, and classroom is 200 m from the gate.
(a) Find the total one-way distance in metres and kilometres.
(b) How many kilometres does Rahul travel round-trip in a 5-day school week?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) One-way: 350 m + 1,500 m + 200 m = 2,050 m = 2.05 km.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 2.05 km 2 Marks
Part (b): 20.5 km 2 Marks

Q4 Textbook Exercise
Write the following fractions as decimals and locate them in order from smallest to largest: 3/10, 45/100, 7/10, 8/100, 95/100.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Convert to decimals:
    $3/10 = \mathbf{0.30}$
    $45/100 = \mathbf{0.45}$
    $7/10 = \mathbf{0.70}$
    $8/100 = \mathbf{0.08}$
    $95/100 = \mathbf{0.95}$
  2. Ascending order (Smallest to Largest):
    $\mathbf{0.08 < 0.30 < 0.45 < 0.70 < 0.95}$
    (or $\frac{8}{100} < \frac{3}{10} < \frac{45}{100} < \frac{7}{10} < \frac{95}{100}$).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate decimal conversions of all 5 fractions 2.5 Marks
Correct ascending order arrangement 1.5 Marks

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Far and Near', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 83 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 11. Find the next three terms (94, 105, 116) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{83}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{11}$.
3. Term 2: 83 + 11 = 94.
4. Term 3: 94 + 11 = 105.
5. Term 4: 105 + 11 = 116.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 83 + (n - 1) \times 11$. Successive addition of 11 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (94, 105, 116) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+11) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Far and Near' has length $L = 16\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (16 + 5) = 2 \times 21 = \mathbf{42\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 16 \times 5 = \mathbf{80\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 80 × ₹25 = ₹2000.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 42 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 80 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 80 × ₹25 = ₹2000 1.0 M
Q9 Data Handling & Smart Charts • Fractional Representation

(a) Express the proportion of Club Alpha as a fraction in simplest form.

Statistical Breakdown:

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Far and Near': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 140. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 140$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 140 + 16 = 156$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{156}{4} = \mathbf{39}$.
4. Verification: (39 × 4) - 16 = 156 - 16 = 140. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 156 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 39 with full verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency Question: Hiking Trail Map Interpretation

On a hiking map of a nature sanctuary, 1 cm = 400 metres. The trek comprises three sections: Valley Trail = 3.5 cm; Ridge Walk = 4.2 cm; Lake Loop = 2.8 cm.

Analytical Task: (a) Find the total length of the trek on the ground in kilometres.
(b) If a hiker walks at an average speed of 3.5 km/h, how long will the entire hike take?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Total Distance: Total map = 3.5 + 4.2 + 2.8 = 10.5 cm.
Actual = 10.5 × 400 m = 4,200 m = 4.2 km.
(b) Hike Duration: 4.2/3.5 = 1.2 hours = 1 hour 12 minutes.
Rubric: 1M concept identification + 1M step execution + 1M conclusion
