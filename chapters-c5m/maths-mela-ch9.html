Chapter 9: Coconut Farm

नारियल का बाग (गुणन व्यूह, गुणनखंड वृक्ष एवं विभाजन) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Rectangular arrays: Visualizing multiplication as Rows × Columns.
  • Box/Grid Multiplication Method: Breaking two-digit numbers by expanded form.
  • Factor Trees: Finding prime factorizations and factor pairs of composite numbers.
  • Division with remainder: Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder.
  • (a) Number of faces: An open box without a top lid has 1 base + 4 side walls = 5 square faces.
  • (b) Area of 1 face: $5\text{ cm} \times 5\text{ cm} = 25\text{ cm}^2$.
  • Total surface area: $5 \times 25\text{ cm}^2 = \mathbf{125\text{ cm}^2}$.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
A coconut grove has 24 rows of trees, with 18 trees in each row.
(a) Find the total number of trees using the box method.
(b) If each tree yields 45 coconuts, find the total harvest.
(c) The farmer packs 35 coconuts in each gunny bag. How many full bags can be packed, and how many coconuts will be left over?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Box Method (24 × 18): 200 + 40 + 160 + 32 = 432 trees.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 432 trees 1.5 Marks
Part (b): 19,440 coconuts 1 Mark
Part (c): 555 bags and 15 remainder 1.5 Marks

Q4 Textbook Exercise
A cubical open box (without lid) has an edge of 5 cm. (a) How many square faces does an open box have? (b) What is the total surface area of cardboard required to make this box?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. (a) Number of faces: An open box without a top lid has 1 base + 4 side walls = 5 square faces.
  2. (b) Area of 1 face: $5\text{ cm} \times 5\text{ cm} = 25\text{ cm}^2$.
  3. Total surface area: $5 \times 25\text{ cm}^2 = \mathbf{125\text{ cm}^2}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of 5 faces 1 Mark
Surface area = 5 × 25 = 125 cm² 2 Marks

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Coconut Farm', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 143 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 15. Find the next three terms (158, 173, 188) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{143}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{15}$.
3. Term 2: 143 + 15 = 158.
4. Term 3: 158 + 15 = 173.
5. Term 4: 173 + 15 = 188.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 143 + (n - 1) \times 15$. Successive addition of 15 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (158, 173, 188) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+15) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Coconut Farm' has length $L = 14\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (14 + 5) = 2 \times 19 = \mathbf{38\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 14 \times 5 = \mathbf{70\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 38 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 70 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750 1.0 M

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Coconut Farm': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 172. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 172$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 172 + 16 = 188$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{188}{4} = \mathbf{47}$.
4. Verification: (47 × 4) - 16 = 188 - 16 = 172. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 188 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 47 with full verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency Question: Coconut Water Cooperative Dispatch

A cooperative dispatches 2,400 tender coconuts to market. 35% are sold directly to street stalls, and the remainder are supplied to juice bottling plants in crates of 24 coconuts each.

Analytical Task: (a) How many coconuts are sold to street stalls?
(b) How many crates are supplied to the bottling plant?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Street stalls: 35/100 × 2400 = 840 coconuts.
(b) Bottling crates: 2400 - 840/24 = 1560/24 = 65 crates.
