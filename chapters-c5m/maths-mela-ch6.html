Chapter 6: The Dairy Farm

दुग्धशाला (धारिता, लीटर-मिलीलीटर एवं व्यापारिक गणना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Standard units of liquid volume: 1 Litre (L) = 1,000 millilitres (mL).
  • Common fractional benchmarks: 1/2 L = 500 mL, 1/4 L = 250 mL, 3/4 L = 750 mL.
  • Adding and subtracting compound units of Litres and Millilitres.
  • Packaging milk into pouches and cartons; commercial billing at given rates per litre.
  • (a) True: Since $6 = 2 \times 3$, any number divisible by 6 is divisible by both of its factors (2 and 3).
  • (b) False: 1 has only one factor (itself). A prime number must have exactly two distinct factors (1 and the number itself).
  • (c) True: 1 is a factor of every integer, hence it is always the smallest common factor.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
A dairy cooperative farm has 12 cows. Each cow yields 8 litres 250 mL of milk daily.
(a) What is the daily milk yield of each cow in millilitres?
(b) What is the total milk collected from all 12 cows in one day (in litres and millilitres)?
(c) If milk is sold at ₹56 per litre, what is the farm's total daily income from milk?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Yield in Millilitres: 8 L 250 mL = 8,250 mL.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 8,250 mL 1 Mark
Part (b): 99 Litres 1.5 Marks
Part (c): ₹5,544 1.5 Marks
Q2 Textbook Exercise
A milk booth receives 65 litres of milk in the morning. They package it into:
• 40 pouches of 500 mL each
• 80 pouches of 250 mL each
• The remaining milk is packaged in 200 mL tetra packs.
(a) How many litres are packed into 500 mL pouches?
(b) How many litres are packed into 250 mL pouches?
(c) How many 200 mL tetra packs can be filled from the remaining milk?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) 500 mL pouches: 40 × 500 mL = 20,000 mL = 20 Litres.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 20 Litres 1 Mark
Part (b): 20 Litres 1 Mark
Part (c): 125 packs 2 Marks
Q3 Textbook Exercise
Calculate:
(a) Add: 15 L 450 mL + 8 L 850 mL
(b) Subtract: 25 L 200 mL - 17 L 650 mL
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Addition:
mL part: 450 + 850 = 1,300 mL = 1 L 300 mL.
L part: 15 + 8 + 1 = 24 L.
Result = 24 L 300 mL.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 24 L 300 mL 1.5 Marks
Part (b): 7 L 550 mL 1.5 Marks

Q6 Common Multiples • Number Line Intersections
Find the first three common multiples of 4 and 6. Explain how you find them using lists of multiples and locate their Least Common Multiple (LCM).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Listing Multiples:
• Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40...
• Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42...

2. Common Multiples:
The numbers appearing in both lists are 12, 24, 36.

3. Least Common Multiple (LCM):
The smallest among these common multiples is 12. Thus, LCM(4, 6) = 12.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct lists of multiples 1.0 M
First three common multiples (12, 24, 36) 1.0 M
Identification of LCM as 12 1.0 M
Q7 Factor Trees • Prime Factorization
Draw two different factor trees for the number 60 and write down its prime factorization.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Factor Tree A (Starting with 6 × 10):
• 60 = 6 × 10
• 6 splits into 2 × 3
• 10 splits into 2 × 5
• Prime leaves: 2, 3, 2, 5.

2. Factor Tree B (Starting with 4 × 15):
• 60 = 4 × 15
• 4 splits into 2 × 2
• 15 splits into 3 × 5
• Prime leaves: 2, 2, 3, 5.

3. Prime Factorization:
60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5. Both trees yield the exact same prime factors.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two distinct factor tree branches 2.0 M
Correct prime factorization (2 × 2 × 3 × 5) 1.0 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency Question: Community Milk Tea Stall Procurement

A tea vendor makes 1 cup of special masala chai using 75 mL of milk. On Sunday, he prepares 400 cups of tea. Milk is bought in 1-litre bottles costing ₹62 each.

Analytical Task: (a) How much milk in litres is needed for 400 cups?
(b) How many whole 1-litre bottles must he buy, and how much will he spend?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Total Milk: 400 × 75 mL = 30,000 mL = 30 Litres.
(b) Bottles and Cost: 30 bottles needed ⇒ 30 × ₹62 = ₹1,860.
