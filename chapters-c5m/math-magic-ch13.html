Chapter 13: Ways to Multiply and Divide

गुणा और भाग के तरीके (बेला का गुणन, शांताराम का वेतन एवं दिन गणना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Bela's method of columnar multiplication using expanded place-value distributive property.
  • Multiplying 2-digit and 3-digit numbers by multi-step partial products.
  • Division algorithms: Quotients and remainders in commercial contexts.
  • Daily wage calculation, monthly salaries, and yearly earnings.
  • Total notebooks: 576.
  • Total boxes: 18.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
(a) Multiply using Bela's method: 65 × 32.
(b) Shantaram is a cook on a farm. He is paid ₹165 per day. If he works for all 365 days of the year, what will be his total annual earnings?
(c) 576 books are to be packed in boxes. If one box holds 24 books, how many boxes are needed?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Bela's Method: 65 × 2 = 130; 65 × 30 = 1,950; Sum = 2,080.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 2,080 1.5 Marks
Part (b): ₹60,225 1.5 Marks

Q3 Textbook Exercise
Sohanlal drinks 8 glasses of water every day. (a) How many glasses of water will he drink in one non-leap year (365 days)? (b) If 125 people living in a residential colony drink 8 glasses each day, how many glasses will they drink altogether in one month of 30 days?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. (a) Glasses in 365 days:
    $\text{Total glasses} = 365 \times 8 = \mathbf{2,920\text{ glasses}}$.
  2. (b) Glasses by 125 people in 30 days:
    Glasses drunk by colony in 1 day = $125 \times 8 = 1,000\text{ glasses}$.
    Glasses in 30 days = $1,000 \times 30 = \mathbf{30,000\text{ glasses}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 365 × 8 = 2,920 glasses 2 Marks
Part (b): 125 × 8 × 30 = 30,000 glasses 2 Marks
Q4 Textbook Exercise
A school ordered 576 notebooks to be packed equally into 18 boxes. How many notebooks were packed in each box? Check your answer using multiplication.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Total notebooks: 576.
  2. Total boxes: 18.
  3. Notebooks per box:
    $576 \div 18 = \mathbf{32\text{ notebooks}}$.
  4. Verification Check:
    $\text{Quotient} \times \text{Divisor} = 32 \times 18 = 32 \times (10 + 8) = 320 + 256 = 576$ (Correct).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Division: 576 / 18 = 32 notebooks 2 Marks
Multiplication verification: 32 × 18 = 576 1 Mark

Q7 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Ways to Multiply and Divide', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 203 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 19. Find the next three terms (222, 241, 260) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{203}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{19}$.
3. Term 2: 203 + 19 = 222.
4. Term 3: 222 + 19 = 241.
5. Term 4: 241 + 19 = 260.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 203 + (n - 1) \times 19$. Successive addition of 19 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (222, 241, 260) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+19) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q9 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Ways to Multiply and Divide' has length $L = 12\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (12 + 5) = 2 \times 17 = \mathbf{34\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 12 \times 5 = \mathbf{60\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 60 × ₹25 = ₹1500.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 34 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 60 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 60 × ₹25 = ₹1500 1.0 M

Q11 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Ways to Multiply and Divide': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 204. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 204$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 204 + 16 = 220$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{220}{4} = \mathbf{55}$.
4. Verification: (55 × 4) - 16 = 220 - 16 = 204. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 220 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 55 with full verification 1.5 M
