Chapter 14: Maps and Locations

मानचित्र और स्थितियाँ (निर्देशांक ग्रिड, दिशाएँ एवं स्थल पहचान) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Grid coordinates: Identifying positions using Column-Row coordinate pairs (e.g., (C, 3)).
  • Horizontal axis (Columns A, B, C, D, E) and Vertical axis (Rows 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
  • Navigating grid paths: Counting steps North, South, East, and West.
  • Shortest path on a Manhattan grid (taxicab distance).

Refer to the town grid where rows are 1 to 4 and columns are A to E:
(a) What are the coordinates of School and Central Park?
(b) If Amit walks from School (A, 1) to Central Park (C, 3), how many blocks East and North must he walk?

Q3 Textbook Exercise
On a city grid map, Point A (Library) is at coordinates (2, 3) and Point B (Hospital) is at coordinates (8, 3). If each grid unit represents 150 meters: (a) What is the grid distance between Library and Hospital? (b) What is the actual distance in meters and in kilometers?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. (a) Grid distance:
    Both points have same y-coordinate (3).
    $\text{Horizontal distance} = 8 - 2 = \mathbf{6\text{ grid units}}$.
  2. (b) Actual distance:
    $\text{Distance in meters} = 6 \times 150\text{ m} = \mathbf{900\text{ meters}}$.
    $\text{Distance in kilometers} = \frac{900}{1000} = \mathbf{0.9\text{ km}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Grid units = 8 - 2 = 6 units 1 Mark
Actual distance = 900 m = 0.9 km 2 Marks

Q5 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Maps and Locations', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 218 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 20. Find the next three terms (238, 258, 278) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{218}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{20}$.
3. Term 2: 218 + 20 = 238.
4. Term 3: 238 + 20 = 258.
5. Term 4: 258 + 20 = 278.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 218 + (n - 1) \times 20$. Successive addition of 20 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (238, 258, 278) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+20) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q7 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Maps and Locations' has length $L = 13\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (13 + 5) = 2 \times 18 = \mathbf{36\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 13 \times 5 = \mathbf{65\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 36 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 65 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Maps and Locations': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 212. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 212$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 212 + 16 = 228$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{228}{4} = \mathbf{57}$.
4. Verification: (57 × 4) - 16 = 228 - 16 = 212. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 228 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 57 with full verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency Question: Emergency Ambulance Route Optimization

Each grid block in the town layout represents a distance of 300 metres along street avenues. An emergency ambulance departs from Community Clinic at (B, 1) to reach an accident site at (E, 4).

Analytical Task: (a) What is the total road distance traveled by the ambulance?
(b) If the ambulance drives at an average speed of 36 km/h, how many minutes will the drive take?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Road Distance: 3 + 3 = 6 blocks = 6 × 300 m = 1,800 m = 1.8 km.
(b) Travel Time: 1.8 km/36 km/h = 0.05 hours = 0.05 × 60 = 3 minutes.
