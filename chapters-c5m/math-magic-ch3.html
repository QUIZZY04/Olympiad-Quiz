Chapter 3: How Many Squares?

कितने वर्ग? (क्षेत्रफल, परिमाप, डाक टिकट एवं ग्रिड पहेलियाँ) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Measuring area by counting 1 cm grid squares: full squares, more than half squares as 1, exactly half squares as 1/2, less than half ignored.
  • Comparing areas of postal stamps, footprints, and coin impressions.
  • Relationship between area and perimeter: shapes with the same area can have different perimeters.
  • Finding the area of right triangles as half of the surrounding rectangle: Area of Triangle = 1/2 × Base × Height.
  • Given: $\text{Perimeter} = 24\text{ cm}$, $\text{Width} (w) = 4\text{ cm}$.

On a 1 cm square grid sheet: Stamp A covers 18 squares (6 × 3), Stamp B covers 8 squares (4 × 2), Stamp C covers 6 squares (3 × 2). (a) What is the area of Stamp A? (b) Which stamp has the smallest area? (c) What is the difference between the areas of the largest and smallest stamps?

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Area of Stamp A: Covers 18 grid squares of 1 cm² each = 18 cm².

Q4 Textbook Exercise
A rectangular postage stamp has length 4 cm and width 3 cm. (a) What is the area of this stamp? (b) How many such stamps are needed to completely cover a sheet of paper measuring 24 cm in length and 18 cm in width?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. (a) Area of the stamp:
    $\text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} = 4\text{ cm} \times 3\text{ cm} = \mathbf{12\text{ cm}^2}$.
  2. (b) Area of the paper sheet:
    $\text{Sheet Area} = 24\text{ cm} \times 18\text{ cm} = \mathbf{432\text{ cm}^2}$.
  3. Number of stamps required:
    $\text{Number of stamps} = \frac{\text{Sheet Area}}{\text{Stamp Area}} = \frac{432}{12} = \mathbf{36\text{ stamps}}$.

Alternative grid check: Along the length: $24 \div 4 = 6$ stamps. Along width: $18 \div 3 = 6$ stamps. Total $= 6 \times 6 = 36$ stamps.

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Stamp area = 4 × 3 = 12 cm² 1.5 Marks
Sheet area = 24 × 18 = 432 cm² and division 432 / 12 = 36 stamps 2.5 Marks
Q5 Textbook Exercise
A rectangle has a perimeter of 24 cm. If its width is 4 cm, find its: (a) Length, (b) Area.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Given: $\text{Perimeter} = 24\text{ cm}$, $\text{Width} (w) = 4\text{ cm}$.
  2. Formula: $\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (l + w)$.
    $24 = 2 \times (l + 4) \implies l + 4 = 12 \implies l = 12 - 4 = \mathbf{8\text{ cm}}$.
  3. Area:
    $\text{Area} = l \times w = 8\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} = \mathbf{32\text{ cm}^2}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Finding length = 12 - 4 = 8 cm 1.5 Marks
Finding area = 8 × 4 = 32 cm² 1.5 Marks
