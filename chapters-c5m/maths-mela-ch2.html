Chapter 2: Fractions

भिन्न (अंश, हर, तुल्य भिन्न एवं भिन्नों की तुलना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Fraction as part of a whole: Fraction = Numerator (parts taken)/Denominator (total equal parts).
  • Fraction Kits: Combining unit fractions: two 1/6 pieces make 1/3, two 1/4 pieces make 1/2.
  • Equivalent Fractions: Fractions that have the same value: a/b = a × k/b × k.
  • Cross-multiplication rule for comparing fractions: a/b vs c/d ⇒ compare a × d and b × c.
  • Addition and subtraction of fractions with common denominators.

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Fractions', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 38 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 8. Find the next three terms (46, 54, 62) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{38}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{8}$.
3. Term 2: 38 + 8 = 46.
4. Term 3: 46 + 8 = 54.
5. Term 4: 54 + 8 = 62.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 38 + (n - 1) \times 8$. Successive addition of 8 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (46, 54, 62) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+8) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Fractions' has length $L = 13\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (13 + 5) = 2 \times 18 = \mathbf{36\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 13 \times 5 = \mathbf{65\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 36 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 65 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625 1.0 M

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Fractions': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 116. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 116$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 116 + 16 = 132$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{132}{4} = \mathbf{33}$.
4. Verification: (33 × 4) - 16 = 132 - 16 = 116. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 132 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 33 with full verification 1.5 M

Competency Question: Farmer Sohan's Crop Field Partition

Farmer Sohan has a square field measuring 24 acres. He cultivates wheat on 1/3 of the land, tomatoes on 1/4 of the land, and mustard on 1/6 of the land. The remaining land is kept as grazing meadow for cattle.

Analytical Task: (a) How many acres are used for each of the three crops?
(b) How many acres remain for cattle grazing?
(c) What fraction of the total field is left for cattle?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Acres for each crop:
- Wheat: 1/3 × 24 = 8 acres.
- Tomatoes: 1/4 × 24 = 6 acres.
- Mustard: 1/6 × 24 = 4 acres.
(b) Acres for Cattle Grazing:
Remaining area = 24 - (8 + 6 + 4) = 24 - 18 = 6 acres.
(c) Fraction for Cattle: 6/24 = 1/4.
