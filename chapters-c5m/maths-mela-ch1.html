Chapter 1: We the Travellers – I

हम यात्री – १ (बड़ी संख्याएँ एवं यात्रा गणना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Reading, writing and representing 4-digit, 5-digit, and 6-digit numbers using the Indian Place Value System.
  • Indian Numbering System comma placement: Ones, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands (Th, T-Th), Lakhs (L, T-L).
  • Place Value and Face Value: Place value of a digit = Face value × Value of place position.
  • Expanded notation: e.g., 58,406 = 50,000 + 8,000 + 400 + 0 + 6.
  • Forming smallest and greatest numbers from given digits and solving digit-swap puzzles.
  • Travel arithmetic: Distance, Speed, Time relationship: Distance = Speed × Time.

Q2 Number Sequences • Pattern Finding
Find the missing terms in the following number patterns by identifying the sequence rule:
(a) 456, 567, 678, _____, _____
(b) 10,100, 10,200, 10,300, _____, _____, 10,600
(c) 73,005, 72,004, 71,003, _____, _____
(d) 2,500, 5,000, 7,500, _____, _____
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Pattern Rules & Solutions:
• (a) 456, 567, 678: Rule is add 111 (+111). Next: 678 + 111 = 789; then 789 + 111 = 900.
• (b) 10,100, 10,200, 10,300: Rule is add 100 (+100). Next: 10,400, then 10,500.
• (c) 73,005, 72,004, 71,003: Rule is subtract 1,001 (-1,001). Next: 70,002, then 69,001.
• (d) 2,500, 5,000, 7,500: Rule is add 2,500 (+2,500). Next: 10,000, then 12,500.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 789, 900 0.75 M
Part (b): 10,400, 10,500 0.75 M
Part (c): 70,002, 69,001 0.75 M
Part (d): 10,000, 12,500 0.75 M
Q3 Comparing Numbers • Ordering
(a) Compare using > or < signs: 68,950 and 68,905.
(b) Arrange in ascending order: 23,516; 52,823; 30,512; 30,125; 42,222.
(c) Riya took the number 26,053 and swapped two digits to make a new number between 50,000 and 60,000. Which digits did she swap, and what is the new number?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Working:
• (a) Comparison: In ten-thousands, thousands, and hundreds places, both numbers are equal (6, 8, 9). Looking at tens place, 5 > 0. Therefore: 68,950 > 68,905.
• (b) Ascending Order: Smallest to largest: 23,516 < 30,125 < 30,512 < 42,222 < 52,823.
• (c) Digit Swap: To get a number in the 50,000s, the ten-thousands digit (2) must be replaced by 5. Swapping digits 2 and 5 gives: 56,023 (which lies between 50,000 and 60,000).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 68,950 > 68,905 1.0 M
Part (b): Correct ascending order 1.0 M
Part (c): Swapped 2 & 5 to get 56,023 1.0 M
Q4 Travel Arithmetic • Distance & Speed
A family travels from New Delhi to Jaipur covering 280 km in 5 hours, then from Jaipur to Udaipur covering 395 km in 7 hours.
(a) Find the total distance traveled.
(b) Find their average speed during the New Delhi to Jaipur journey.
(c) Find the total travel time for the entire journey.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations:
• (a) Total Distance: 280 km + 395 km = 675 km.
• (b) Average Speed (Delhi to Jaipur): Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 280 ÷ 5 = 56 km/h.
• (c) Total Travel Time: 5 hours + 7 hours = 12 hours.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total distance = 675 km 1.0 M
Speed calculation = 56 km/h 1.0 M
Total travel time = 12 hours 1.0 M

Q7 Place Value Comparison • City Populations
The population of City A is 7,85,420 and City B is 7,82,990.
(a) Which city has a greater population?
(b) By how much is it greater?
(c) Round both populations to the nearest thousand.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Analysis:
• (a) Comparison: Lakhs (7) and Ten-Thousands (8) are equal. At Thousands place, City A has 5 and City B has 2. Since 5 > 2, City A has a greater population (7,85,420 > 7,82,990).
• (b) Difference: 7,85,420 - 7,82,990 = 2,430 people.
• (c) Rounding to Nearest Thousand:
- City A (7,85,420): Hundreds digit is 4 (< 5) → 7,85,000.
- City B (7,82,990): Hundreds digit is 9 (≥ 5) → 7,83,000.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct comparison 1.0 M
Difference = 2,430 1.0 M
Accurate rounding (7,85,000 & 7,83,000) 1.0 M
Q8 Number Operations • Predecessor & Successor
Find the predecessor and successor of the largest 6-digit number. What is their difference?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Largest 6-digit number: 9,99,999.
• Predecessor (Number - 1): 9,99,999 - 1 = 9,99,998.
• Successor (Number + 1): 9,99,999 + 1 = 10,00,000 (Ten Lakhs, smallest 7-digit number).
2. Difference: 10,00,000 - 9,99,998 = 2.
Mathematical property: The difference between the successor and predecessor of any whole number is always 2.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct predecessor and successor 2.0 M
Accurate difference = 2 1.0 M

Q10 Travel Budget • Railway Timetable

Q11 Number Line • Interval Estimation
On a number line marked from 50,000 to 60,000 with major ticks at intervals of 2,000:
(a) How many equal subdivisions lie between 50,000 and 60,000?
(b) Between which two markings does 57,400 lie?
(c) Which major marking is 57,400 closest to?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Number Line Analysis:
• (a) Subdivisions: Total interval = 60,000 - 50,000 = 10,000. Subdivisions = 10,000 ÷ 2,000 = 5 equal intervals (50,000; 52,000; 54,000; 56,000; 58,000; 60,000).
• (b) Position: 57,400 lies between 56,000 and 58,000.
• (c) Proximity: Distance to 56,000 = 57,400 - 56,000 = 1,400. Distance to 58,000 = 58,000 - 57,400 = 600. Since 600 < 1,400, it is closest to 58,000.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 5 intervals identified 1.0 M
Part (b): Between 56,000 and 58,000 1.0 M
Part (c): Closest to 58,000 1.0 M
Q12 Mental Arithmetic • Number Puzzles
I am a 5-digit number. My ten-thousands digit is 7. My thousands digit is 3 less than ten-thousands digit. My hundreds digit is 0. My tens digit is double my thousands digit, and my ones digit is 1. What number am I?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Deduction Step-by-Step:
1. Ten-thousands digit = 7.
2. Thousands digit = 7 - 3 = 4.
3. Hundreds digit = 0.
4. Tens digit = 2 × 4 = 8.
5. Ones digit = 1.
The secret number is 74,081 (Seventy-four thousand eighty-one).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct deduction of individual place values 1.5 M
Final number: 74,081 1.5 M
Q13 Distance Comparison • Highway Markers
A highway distance stone shows: Chandigarh 145 km, Shimla 260 km, Manali 495 km from Delhi.
(a) What is the distance between Chandigarh and Shimla?
(b) How much further is Manali from Chandigarh than Shimla is from Chandigarh?
(c) If an electric bus travels 45 km per hour, how long will it take to reach Shimla from Chandigarh?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations:
• (a) Distance Chandigarh to Shimla: 260 km - 145 km = 115 km.
• (b) Manali to Chandigarh vs Shimla to Chandigarh:
- Manali to Chandigarh = 495 km - 145 km = 350 km.
- Extra distance = 350 km - 115 km = 235 km.
• (c) Travel Time: Time = Distance ÷ Speed = 115 ÷ 45 = 2.55 hours = 2 hours 33 minutes (approx 2.56 h).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 115 km 1.0 M
Part (b): 235 km extra 1.0 M
Part (c): Time calculation (approx 2 h 33 min) 1.0 M

Q21 Travel Estimation • Walking vs Cycling
Rohan's school is 3,600 metres from his house.
(a) Express this distance in kilometres.
(b) Rohan walks at 60 metres per minute. How many minutes does he take to walk to school?
(c) If he rides his bicycle at 180 metres per minute, how much time does he save?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Steps & Solutions:
• (a) In Kilometres: 3,600 m ÷ 1,000 = 3.6 km.

Q23 Practical Application • Bus Capacity
A state transport bus has 52 passenger seats. For an inter-city tour, 380 people register.
(a) How many buses are required to seat all passengers comfortably?
(b) How many vacant seats will remain in the last bus?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Division with Remainders:
• 380 ÷ 52: 52 × 7 = 364 passengers.
• Remaining passengers = 380 - 364 = 16 passengers.
• (a) Buses Required: Since 16 passengers cannot be left behind, 7 + 1 = 8 buses are needed.
• (b) Vacant Seats in 8th Bus: 52 - 16 = 36 vacant seats.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Division: 380 ÷ 52 gives quotient 7, remainder 16 1.5 M
Total 8 buses & 36 vacant seats 1.5 M

Suggested Model Solution:
