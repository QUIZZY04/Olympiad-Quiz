Chapter 1: We the Travellers – I

हम यात्री – १ (बड़ी संख्याएँ एवं यात्रा गणना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Reading, writing and representing 4-digit, 5-digit, and 6-digit numbers using the Indian Place Value System.
  • Indian Numbering System comma placement: Ones, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands (Th, T-Th), Lakhs (L, T-L).
  • Place Value and Face Value: Place value of a digit = Face value × Value of place position.
  • Expanded notation: e.g., 58,406 = 50,000 + 8,000 + 400 + 0 + 6.
  • Forming smallest and greatest numbers from given digits and solving digit-swap puzzles.
  • Travel arithmetic: Distance, Speed, Time relationship: Distance = Speed × Time.

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'We the Travellers – I', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 23 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 7. Find the next three terms (30, 37, 44) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{23}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{7}$.
3. Term 2: 23 + 7 = 30.
4. Term 3: 30 + 7 = 37.
5. Term 4: 37 + 7 = 44.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 23 + (n - 1) \times 7$. Successive addition of 7 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (30, 37, 44) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+7) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'We the Travellers – I' has length $L = 12\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (12 + 5) = 2 \times 17 = \mathbf{34\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 12 \times 5 = \mathbf{60\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 60 × ₹25 = ₹1500.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 34 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 60 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 60 × ₹25 = ₹1500 1.0 M

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'We the Travellers – I': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 108. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 108$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 108 + 16 = 124$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{124}{4} = \mathbf{31}$.
4. Verification: (31 × 4) - 16 = 124 - 16 = 108. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 124 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 31 with full verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency-Based Question: Highway Toll & Fuel Economy

A tour bus travels across 3 states covering 1,840 km in 4 days. The bus has a fuel tank of 150 litres and gives an average mileage of 8 km per litre of diesel. Diesel costs ₹90 per litre.

Analytical Task: (a) How many litres of diesel were consumed during the 1,840 km trip?
(b) What was the total cost of diesel consumed?
(c) If 40 tourists shared the diesel expense equally, how much did each passenger pay?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Diesel Consumption: 1840/8 = 230 litres.
(b) Total Fuel Cost: 230 × ₹90 = ₹20,700.
(c) Share per Passenger: ₹20,700/40 = ₹517.50.
Rubric: 1M concept identification + 1M step execution + 1M conclusion
