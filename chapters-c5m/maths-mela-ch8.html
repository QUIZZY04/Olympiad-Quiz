Chapter 8: Weight and Capacity

भार और धारिता (किलोग्राम-ग्राम, बाट संतुलन एवं तराजू) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Units of mass: 1 Kilogram (kg) = 1,000 grams (g).
  • Benchmark weights: 1/2 kg = 500 g, 1/4 kg = 250 g, 3/4 kg = 750 g.
  • Balancing two-pan scales: Left Pan Weight = Right Pan Weight.
  • Solving multi-step grocery word problems involving weights and prices per kg.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (24 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Standard Weights • Balancing Beam Balance
(a) How many 200 g weights are needed to balance a 1 kg block of sugar?
(b) How many 250 g weights balance a 2 kg weight?
(c) A grocer has weights of 500 g, 200 g, and 100 g only. How can he weigh exactly 800 g of dal using minimum weights?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Weight Balancing (1 kg = 1,000 g):
• (a) For 1 kg (1,000 g): 1,000 ÷ 200 = 5 weights of 200 g.
• (b) For 2 kg (2,000 g): 2,000 ÷ 250 = 8 weights of 250 g.
• (c) Weighing 800 g: Combine one 500 g weight, one 200 g weight, and one 100 g weight: 500 + 200 + 100 = 800 g (3 weights total).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 5 weights 1.0 M
Part (b): 8 weights 1.0 M
Part (c): 500 g + 200 g + 100 g 1.0 M
Q2 Vegetable Shopping • Multi-Item Bill
Mrs. Sharma buys:
• 2.5 kg onions at ₹36 per kg
• 1.5 kg tomatoes at ₹40 per kg
• 750 g ginger at ₹120 per kg
(a) Find the cost of each item.
(b) Calculate her total bill.
(c) If she pays with a ₹500 note, how much change is refunded?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Cost Calculations:
• (a) Individual Costs:
- Onions: 2.5 × ₹36 = ₹90.
- Tomatoes: 1.5 × ₹40 = ₹60.
- Ginger: 750 g = 0.75 kg. Cost = 0.75 × ₹120 = ₹90.
• (b) Total Bill: ₹90 + ₹60 + ₹90 = ₹240.
• (c) Change: ₹500 - ₹240 = ₹260.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Onions ₹90, Tomatoes ₹60, Ginger ₹90 1.5 M
Total bill = ₹240 0.75 M
Change = ₹260 0.75 M
Q3 Pan Balance • Subtraction Weighing Trick
A grocer has weights of 5 kg and 2 kg only. Explain how he can weigh exactly 3 kg of rice using a two-pan balance in a single measurement.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Pan Balance Subtraction Principle:
1. Place the 5 kg weight on the left pan.
2. Place the 2 kg weight on the right pan.
3. Pour rice into the right pan (alongside the 2 kg weight) until both pans balance perfectly.
4. Since Left Pan = Right Pan:
5 kg = 2 kg + Rice Weight → Rice Weight = 5 kg - 2 kg = 3 kg of rice.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
5 kg on one pan, 2 kg on other pan 1.5 M
5 - 2 = 3 kg balance equation 1.5 M
Q4 Unit Conversions • Kilograms & Grams
Convert:
(a) 4 kg 350 g into grams
(b) 8,075 g into kilograms and grams
(c) 3/4 kg into grams
(d) 2.25 kg into grams
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Conversions (1 kg = 1,000 g):
• (a) 4 kg 350 g: (4 × 1,000) + 350 = 4,350 g.
• (b) 8,075 g: 8,000 g + 75 g = 8 kg 75 g.
• (c) 3/4 kg: 3/4 of 1,000 = 1,000 × 3 ÷ 4 = 750 g.
• (d) 2.25 kg: 2.25 × 1,000 = 2,250 g.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Parts (a) & (b): 4,350 g, 8 kg 75 g 1.5 M
Parts (c) & (d): 750 g, 2,250 g 1.5 M
Q5 Water Tank Capacity • In-Text Problem
An overhead rooftop water tank has a capacity of 1,200 litres.
(a) A family uses 350 litres for washing, 180 litres for cooking, and 420 litres for bathing daily. How many litres are consumed in total?
(b) How many litres of water remain in the tank?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Water Consumption:
• (a) Total Used: 350 L + 180 L + 420 L = 950 litres.
• (b) Remaining Water: 1,200 L - 950 L = 250 litres.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total used = 950 L 1.5 M
Remaining = 250 L 1.5 M
Q6 Grain Bag Packaging • Division
A farmer harvests 6 quintals of wheat (1 quintal = 100 kg). He packs the wheat into smaller sacks of 25 kg each.
(a) Find the total weight of wheat in kilograms.
(b) How many 25 kg sacks does he fill completely?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Working:
• (a) Total Kilograms: 6 quintals × 100 kg/quintal = 600 kg.
• (b) Number of Sacks: 600 kg ÷ 25 kg/sack = 24 sacks.

Total = 600 kg 1.5 M
Sacks = 24 1.5 M
Q7 Medicine Dropper • Dosage Calculations
A doctor prescribes 5 mL of cough syrup 3 times a day for a child for 5 days.
(a) How many mL does the child take in one day?
(b) What is the total volume of cough syrup needed for the 5-day course?
(c) Will a 100 mL bottle be sufficient? How much syrup will remain in the bottle?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Dosage Calculations:
• (a) Daily Dose: 3 times × 5 mL = 15 mL per day.
• (b) Total for 5 Days: 5 days × 15 mL = 75 mL.
• (c) Bottle Sufficiency: Yes, 75 mL < 100 mL, so a 100 mL bottle is sufficient.
- Remaining syrup = 100 mL - 75 mL = 25 mL remaining.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Daily dose = 15 mL 1.0 M
Total 5-day dose = 75 mL 1.0 M
Sufficient with 25 mL left 1.0 M
Q8 School Mid-Day Meal • Ration Accounting
In a primary school with 140 students, each student receives 100 g of rice and 30 g of dal daily under the Mid-Day Meal scheme.
(a) How many kilograms of rice are cooked daily?
(b) How many kilograms of dal are cooked daily?
(c) Calculate the total monthly requirement of rice for 22 school working days.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations:
• (a) Daily Rice: 140 × 100 g = 14,000 g = 14 kg of rice.
• (b) Daily Dal: 140 × 30 g = 4,200 g = 4.2 kg (4 kg 200 g) of dal.
• (c) Monthly Rice (22 days): 22 × 14 kg = 308 kg of rice.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Daily rice = 14 kg 1.0 M
Daily dal = 4.2 kg 1.0 M
Monthly rice = 308 kg 1.0 M
Q9 Weight of Money • Coin Stacks
A new 5-rupee coin weighs 9 grams.
(a) What is the weight of a sack containing 1,000 such 5-rupee coins in kilograms?
(b) If a bag of 5-rupee coins weighs 4 kg 500 g, how many coins are in the bag?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Working:
• (a) Weight of 1,000 Coins: 1,000 × 9 g = 9,000 g = 9 kg.
• (b) Number of Coins:
- Total weight = 4 kg 500 g = 4,500 grams.
- Number of coins = 4,500 ÷ 9 g = 500 coins.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Weight of 1,000 coins = 9 kg 1.5 M
Number of coins = 500 1.5 M
Q10 Bucket and Mug Problem
A bathing bucket has a capacity of 15 litres. A plastic mug holds 300 mL.
(a) How many full mugs of water are needed to fill the bucket completely?
(b) If 12 mugs of water are taken out of a full bucket, how many litres remain?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Capacity Calculations (15 L = 15,000 mL):
• (a) Mugs to Fill: 15,000 mL ÷ 300 mL = 50 mugs.
• (b) Remaining Volume:
- Water removed = 12 × 300 mL = 3,600 mL = 3.6 L.
- Water left = 15 L - 3.6 L = 11.4 litres (11 L 400 mL).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
50 mugs to fill 1.5 M
Water left = 11.4 L 1.5 M
Q11 Comparing Heaviness • Metric Units
Arrange the following weights from heaviest to lightest:
2.8 kg, 2,750 g, 3 kg 50 g, 2,890 g, 3,000 g
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Converting all to Grams:
• 2.8 kg = 2,800 g
• 2,750 g = 2,750 g
• 3 kg 50 g = 3,050 g
• 2,890 g = 2,890 g
• 3,000 g = 3,000 g
Ordering from largest to smallest: 3,050 g > 3,000 g > 2,890 g > 2,800 g > 2,750 g.
Descending Order: 3 kg 50 g > 3,000 g > 2,890 g > 2.8 kg > 2,750 g.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All converted to grams 1.5 M
Correct descending order 1.5 M

Q13 Elephant Weight vs Children Weight
A baby elephant weighs 240 kg. A Class 5 child has an average weight of 30 kg.
(a) How many children together weigh the same as the baby elephant?
(b) If an adult elephant weighs 3,600 kg, how many baby elephants equal the weight of one adult elephant?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Comparison:
• (a) Children Equivalent: 240 kg ÷ 30 kg = 8 children.
• (b) Adult vs Baby Elephant: 3,600 kg ÷ 240 kg = 15 baby elephants.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
8 children 1.5 M
15 baby elephants 1.5 M
Q14 Teaspoon and Tablespoon Volumes
In household cooking: 1 teaspoon holds 5 mL, and 1 tablespoon holds 15 mL.

Q15 Postal Parcel Weight Tariff
India Post parcel charges are: ₹20 for the first 50 grams, and ₹10 for every additional 50 grams (or fraction thereof).
(a) Find the postal charge for a book parcel weighing 180 grams.
(b) Find the postal charge for a parcel weighing 320 grams.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Postal Step Tariff:
• (a) 180 g Parcel:
- First 50 g: ₹20.
- Remaining weight = 180 - 50 = 130 g.
- Additional 50 g slabs = 130 ÷ 50 = 2.6 → 3 slabs.
- Surcharge = 3 × ₹10 = ₹30.
- Total postage = ₹20 + ₹30 = ₹50.
• (b) 320 g Parcel:
- First 50 g: ₹20.
- Remaining = 320 - 50 = 270 g.
- Additional slabs = 270 ÷ 50 = 5.4 → 6 slabs.
- Surcharge = 6 × ₹10 = ₹60.
- Total postage = ₹20 + ₹60 = ₹80.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
180 g parcel = ₹50 1.5 M
320 g parcel = ₹80 1.5 M
Q16 Dripping Tap Water Waste
A leaking tap drips 20 mL of water every minute.
(a) How many mL of water are wasted in 1 hour?
(b) How many litres of water are wasted in a 24-hour day?
(c) If water is valued at ₹5 per 1,000 litres, what is the cost of water lost in a month of 30 days?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Water Wastage Calculations:
• (a) In 1 Hour (60 min): 20 mL × 60 = 1,200 mL (1.2 Litres).
• (b) In 1 Day (24 hours): 1.2 L × 24 = 28.8 litres wasted daily.
• (c) In 30 Days: 28.8 L × 30 = 864 litres.
- Financial loss = (864 ÷ 1,000) × ₹5 = ₹4.32.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
1 hour = 1.2 L 1.0 M
1 day = 28.8 L 1.0 M
Monthly wastage = 864 L 1.0 M
Q17 Weight of Liquids • Water vs Honey
A glass jar holds 500 mL of liquid. When filled with pure water, the liquid weighs 500 g. When filled with pure bee honey, the liquid weighs 710 g.
(a) Which liquid is denser/heavier for the same volume?
(b) By how many grams does 1 litre of honey weigh more than 1 litre of water?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Density Analysis:
• (a) Heavier Liquid: For 500 mL, honey weighs 710 g while water weighs 500 g. Therefore, honey is denser and heavier.
• (b) Difference per Litre (1,000 mL):
- Weight of 1 L water = 2 × 500 g = 1,000 g.
- Weight of 1 L honey = 2 × 710 g = 1,420 g.
- Difference = 1,420 g - 1,000 g = 420 grams heavier per litre.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Honey is heavier 1.5 M
1 L honey is 420 g heavier than water 1.5 M
Q18 Flour Mill Packing • Sacks and Bags
A flour mill processes 1,500 kg of wheat into atta. 5% weight is lost as bran during husk separation.
(a) Find the weight of atta produced.
(b) If the atta is packed into bags of 10 kg each, how many bags are packed?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Working:
• (a) Atta Produced:
- Loss = 5% of 1,500 kg = 75 kg.
- Net atta = 1,500 kg - 75 kg = 1,425 kg.
• (b) Bags Packed: 1,425 kg ÷ 10 kg/bag = 142 full bags (with 5 kg remaining).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Net atta = 1,425 kg 1.5 M
142 bags with 5 kg remainder 1.5 M
Q19 HOTS • Weight Riddle
A brick balances with 3/4 of a brick plus 3/4 of a kilogram on a beam balance. How much does the brick weigh in kilograms?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Algebraic Derivation:
1. Let the weight of 1 whole brick be $W$ kg.
2. Given balance condition: $W = \frac{3}{4}W + \frac{3}{4}\text{ kg}$.
3. Subtract $\frac{3}{4}W$ from both sides:
$W - \frac{3}{4}W = \frac{3}{4}\text{ kg} \rightarrow \frac{1}{4}W = \frac{3}{4}\text{ kg}$.
4. Multiply both sides by 4: $W = 4 \times \frac{3}{4} = \mathbf{3\text{ kg}}$.
5. Verification: 3/4 of 3 kg = 2.25 kg. 2.25 kg + 0.75 kg = 3.0 kg. Balanced!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Equation: W = (3/4)W + 0.75 1.5 M
W = 3 kg with verification 1.5 M
Q20 Juice Dispenser Refill
A school sports day beverage cooler holds 25 litres of fresh lemon squash. The school serves the drink in 200 mL paper cups.
(a) How many students can be served from one full cooler?
(b) If 160 cups are consumed, how many litres of lemon squash remain in the cooler?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations (25 L = 25,000 mL):
• (a) Total Cups: 25,000 mL ÷ 200 mL = 125 cups.
• (b) Consumption Analysis: Since 1 cooler holds only 125 cups, serving 160 cups requires more than 1 cooler (32 litres total). For 125 cups from 1 cooler, all 25 L are used. If 160 cups were served from a larger reservoir, 160 × 200 mL = 32 L were used.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
125 cups per 25 L cooler 1.5 M
160 cups requires 32 L (or 0 left with refill noted) 1.5 M

Q23 True or False with Measurement Reason
State True or False with reasoning:
(a) 1,500 grams is heavier than 1.45 kilograms.
(b) 1 millilitre equals 1 cubic centimetre (1 cm³).
(c) A standard 200 mL glass holds more than 1/4 litre of water.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Justifications:
• (a) True: 1.45 kg = 1,450 g. Since 1,500 g > 1,450 g, it is heavier.
• (b) True: By scientific and metric definition, 1 mL is exactly the volume of a cube with 1 cm sides (1 cm × 1 cm × 1 cm = 1 cm³).
• (c) False: 1/4 Litre = 1,000 ÷ 4 = 250 mL. Since 200 mL < 250 mL, it holds less than 1/4 litre.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): True with gram comparison 1.0 M
Part (b): True with 1 cm³ definition 1.0 M
Part (c): False (200 mL < 250 mL) 1.0 M
Q24 Comprehensive Chapter Review
A relief camp receives a consignment of provisions:
• 1.2 tonnes of rice (1 tonne = 1,000 kg)
• 450 kg of pulses
• 200 cans of cooking oil of 5 Litres each
(a) Find the total weight of food grains (rice + pulses) in kilograms.
(b) Find the total volume of cooking oil in Litres.
(c) If 500 families share the provisions equally, how much rice and oil does each family receive?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Relief Camp Provision Distribution:
• (a) Food Grains Weight:
- Rice = 1.2 × 1,000 kg = 1,200 kg.
- Total grains = 1,200 kg + 450 kg = 1,650 kg.
• (b) Total Oil: 200 cans × 5 L = 1,000 Litres.
• (c) Per Family Share (500 Families):
- Rice = 1,200 kg ÷ 500 = 2.4 kg of rice per family.
- Oil = 1,000 L ÷ 500 = 2 Litres of oil per family.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Grains = 1,650 kg 1.0 M
Oil = 1,000 L 1.0 M
Per family: 2.4 kg rice, 2 L oil 1.0 M

Competency Question: Post Office Parcel Weighing Slab

A post office charges ₹20 for a parcel weighing up to 50 g, and ₹10 for every additional 50 g or fraction thereof.

Analytical Task: How much postage stamp value must be affixed to send a book parcel weighing 380 grams?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
First 50 g = ₹20. Remaining = 380 - 50 = 330 g.
Additional slabs = 330/50 = 6.6 ⇒ 7 slabs.
Cost = ₹20 + (7 × ₹10) = ₹90.
