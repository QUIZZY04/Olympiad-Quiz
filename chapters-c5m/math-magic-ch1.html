Q4 Textbook Exercise
A log boat travels at a speed of 4 km per hour. (a) How far will it go in 6 hours? (b) How long will it take to travel a distance of 10 km?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Given: Speed of log boat = 4 km/h.

(a) Distance in 6 hours:
$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time} = 4 \text{ km/h} \times 6 \text{ h} = \mathbf{24\text{ km}}$.

(b) Time to travel 10 km:
$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{10}{4} = 2.5 \text{ hours} = \mathbf{2\text{ hours and } 30\text{ minutes}}$.

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula: Distance = Speed × Time & calculation of 24 km 1.5 Marks
Formula: Time = Distance / Speed & calculation of 2.5 hours (2 hrs 30 min) 1.5 Marks
Q5 Textbook Exercise
Gracy took a bank loan of ₹4,000 to buy a fishing net. She paid back ₹345 every month for one year. How much total money did she pay back to the bank, and how much interest did she pay?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Principal Loan Amount: $P = ₹4,000$.
  2. Monthly Installment: ₹345 per month.
  3. Number of months in 1 year: 12 months.
  4. Total Amount Paid Back:
    $\text{Total Repaid} = ₹345 \times 12 = \mathbf{₹4,140}$.
  5. Interest Paid to Bank:
    $\text{Interest} = \text{Total Repaid} - \text{Principal Loan} = ₹4,140 - ₹4,000 = \mathbf{₹140}$.

Conclusion: Gracy paid a total of ₹4,140 to the bank, which includes ₹140 as interest.

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Calculation of total monthly repayments: 345 × 12 = ₹4,140 2 Marks
Subtraction of principal loan to find interest: ₹4,140 - ₹4,000 = ₹140 2 Marks
Q6 Textbook Exercise
When fresh fish is dried, it becomes 1/3 of its weight. Floramma wants to dry 6,000 kg of fresh fish. She bought fresh fish at ₹15 per kg and sells dried fish at ₹70 per kg. Calculate: (a) Weight of dried fish obtained, (b) Cost of fresh fish, (c) Money earned by selling dried fish, (d) Net profit earned.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. (a) Weight of dried fish:
    $\text{Weight} = 6,000 \times \frac{1}{3} = \mathbf{2,000\text{ kg}}$.
  2. (b) Total buying cost of 6,000 kg fresh fish:
    $\text{Cost} = 6,000 \times ₹15 = \mathbf{₹90,000}$.
  3. (c) Money earned by selling 2,000 kg dried fish:
    $\text{Selling Price} = 2,000 \times ₹70 = \mathbf{₹1,40,000}$.
  4. (d) Net Profit:
    $\text{Profit} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Cost Price} = ₹1,40,000 - ₹90,000 = \mathbf{₹50,000}$.

Final Answer: Floramma earns a net profit of ₹50,000.

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 6000 × 1/3 = 2000 kg dried fish 1 Mark
Part (b): 6000 × ₹15 = ₹90,000 buying cost 1 Mark
Part (c): 2000 × ₹70 = ₹1,40,000 selling amount 1.5 Marks
Part (d): Profit = ₹1,40,000 - ₹90,000 = ₹50,000 1.5 Marks
