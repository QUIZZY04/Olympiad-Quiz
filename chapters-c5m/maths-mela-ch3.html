Chapter 3: Angles as Turns

कोण और घुमाव (समकोण, न्यूनकोण, अधिककोण एवं घूर्णन) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Angle definition: Formed when two rays or line segments meet at a common vertex point.
  • Types of Angles: Right Angle = 90° (L-shape); Acute Angle < 90°; Obtuse Angle between 90° and 180°; Straight Angle = 180°.
  • Angles as Turns: 1/4 turn = 90° (one right angle); 1/2 turn = 180° (two right angles); 3/4 turn = 270°; 1 full turn = 360°.
  • Clock Angles: Clock dial is 360° divided into 12 hours. Angle between consecutive numbers = 30°.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
What type of angle is formed between the hour hand and minute hand of a clock at:
(a) 3:00
(b) 6:00
(c) 2:00
(d) 5:00
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) At 3:00: Angle = 3 × 30° = 90° ⇒ Right Angle.

Q4 Textbook Exercise
On a 1 cm square grid, draw two different polygons each having an area of exactly 12 sq cm. (a) What is the perimeter of each shape? (b) Can two different shapes have the same area but different perimeters? Explain with calculation.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Shape A (Rectangle 4 cm × 3 cm):
    $\text{Area} = 4 \times 3 = \mathbf{12\text{ cm}^2}$.
    $\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (4 + 3) = \mathbf{14\text{ cm}}$.
  2. Shape B (Rectangle 6 cm × 2 cm):
    $\text{Area} = 6 \times 2 = \mathbf{12\text{ cm}^2}$.
    $\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (6 + 2) = \mathbf{16\text{ cm}}$.
  3. (b) Conclusion:
    Yes. Both shapes have an identical area of $12\text{ cm}^2$, but Shape A has perimeter $14\text{ cm}$ while Shape B has perimeter $16\text{ cm}$. As shapes become longer and narrower, their perimeter increases even though area remains constant.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Area and perimeter of Shape A (12 cm², 14 cm) 1.5 Marks
Area and perimeter of Shape B (12 cm², 16 cm) 1.5 Marks
Mathematical explanation of differing perimeters 1 Mark

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Angles as Turns', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 53 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 9. Find the next three terms (62, 71, 80) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{53}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{9}$.
3. Term 2: 53 + 9 = 62.
4. Term 3: 62 + 9 = 71.
5. Term 4: 71 + 9 = 80.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 53 + (n - 1) \times 9$. Successive addition of 9 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (62, 71, 80) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+9) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Angles as Turns' has length $L = 14\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (14 + 5) = 2 \times 19 = \mathbf{38\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 14 \times 5 = \mathbf{70\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 38 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 70 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750 1.0 M

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Angles as Turns': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 124. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 124$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 124 + 16 = 140$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{140}{4} = \mathbf{35}$.
4. Verification: (35 × 4) - 16 = 140 - 16 = 124. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 140 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 35 with full verification 1.5 M

Competency Question: Yoga Asana Angle Identification

During morning physical training, students perform various yoga postures: Trikonasana (Triangle pose), Bhujangasana (Cobra pose), and Dandasana (Staff pose).

Analytical Task: Identify the angles formed by the body in each case:
(a) In Staff pose, what angle is made between the torso and legs?
(b) In Triangle pose, what kind of angles are made between legs and floor?
(c) In a deep backbend, what type of angle is formed?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Staff Pose: Torso vertical, legs horizontal ⇒ Right Angle (90°).
(b) Triangle Pose: Sharp angles at feet ⇒ Acute Angles (< 90°).
(c) Backbend: Opening greater than 90° ⇒ Obtuse Angle.
