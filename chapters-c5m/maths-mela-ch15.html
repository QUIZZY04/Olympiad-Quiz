Chapter 15: Data Through Pictures

चित्रों से आँकड़े (चित्रालेख, टैली मार्क्स एवं दण्ड आलेख) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Pictographs: Visual representation of data using pictures/icons where 1 icon represents a fixed key value.
  • Tally marks grouping: Groups of five (|||| with a diagonal strike through).
  • Bar Graphs: Graphical displays using rectangular bars of uniform width whose heights represent quantities.
  • Interpreting trends, identifying maximum/minimum values, and computing totals from charts.
  • Scale: $1\text{ symbol} = 25\text{ books}$.
  • (a) Storybooks: $5 \times 25 = \mathbf{125\text{ books}}$.

Q1 Library Books Pictograph • Reading Data
A school library records books borrowed by students: Grade 3 (20 books), Grade 4 (35 books), Grade 5 (45 books).
(a) If a symbol 📖 represents 5 books, how many book symbols will represent Grade 3, Grade 4, and Grade 5?
(b) Which class borrowed the maximum number of books?
(c) What is the total number of books borrowed by all three classes?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Pictograph Calculations (1 📖 = 5 Books):
• (a) Symbol Counts:
- Grade 3: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 symbols (📖📖📖📖).
- Grade 4: 35 ÷ 5 = 7 symbols.
- Grade 5: 45 ÷ 5 = 9 symbols.
• (b) Maximum Class: Grade 5 borrowed the maximum (45 books).
• (c) Grand Total: 20 + 35 + 45 = 100 books.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Symbols: 4, 7, 9 1.5 M
Maximum = Grade 5 0.75 M
Total = 100 books 0.75 M

Q3 School Attendance Bar Chart
A bar graph shows student attendance in Grade 5 over one week:
Monday: 42, Tuesday: 45, Wednesday: 40, Thursday: 44, Friday: 38 (Class strength = 45).
(a) On which day was attendance 100% full?
(b) On which day was attendance lowest, and how many students were absent?
(c) What was the average daily attendance for the week?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Data Interpretation:
• (a) 100% Attendance: Tuesday (45 students present).
• (b) Lowest Day: Friday (38 students).
- Absentees = 45 - 38 = 7 students absent.
• (c) Average Daily Attendance:
- Total attendance = 42 + 45 + 40 + 44 + 38 = 209 students.
- Average = 209 ÷ 5 = 41.8 students per day.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Full attendance = Tuesday 1.0 M
Lowest = Friday with 7 absentees 1.0 M
Average = 41.8 students 1.0 M

Q6 Family Electricity Consumption Graph
A family's electricity bill shows units consumed over 4 months: January: 180 units, February: 160 units, March: 240 units, April: 320 units.
(a) In which month was consumption highest? Why did it increase in March and April?
(b) What was the total units consumed over the 4 months?

Energy Consumption Analysis:
• (a) Highest Month: April (320 units).

• (b) Total Units: 180 + 160 + 240 + 320 = 900 units.
• (c) April Bill: 320 units × ₹7 = ₹2,240.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Highest = April (summer explanation) 1.0 M
Total = 900 units 1.0 M
April bill = ₹2,240 1.0 M
Q7 Choosing an Appropriate Pictograph Scale
A village dairy records daily milk collection in litres: Monday (240 L), Tuesday (360 L), Wednesday (300 L), Thursday (180 L).
(a) What would be a convenient scale for a milk can symbol 🛢️ (e.g. 1 symbol = ? litres)?
(b) How many symbols will be drawn for each day using that scale?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Scale Selection:
• (a) Convenient Scale: The numbers (240, 360, 300, 180) have HCF = 60. A scale of 1 symbol 🛢️ = 60 Litres is ideal.
• (b) Symbol Count:
- Monday: 240 ÷ 60 = 4 symbols.
- Tuesday: 360 ÷ 60 = 6 symbols.
- Wednesday: 300 ÷ 60 = 5 symbols.
- Thursday: 180 ÷ 60 = 3 symbols.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Scale: 1 symbol = 60 L 1.5 M
Counts: 4, 6, 5, 3 symbols 1.5 M

Q11 Mid-Term Exam Marks Distribution
Marks obtained by 5 friends in Mathematics (out of 50):
Aarav: 48, Divya: 42, Farhan: 45, Gurpreet: 39, Tanya: 46.
(a) Find the range of marks (Highest mark - Lowest mark).
(b) Calculate the mean (average) mark of the 5 students.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Statistical Analysis:
• (a) Range:
- Highest = 48 (Aarav).
- Lowest = 39 (Gurpreet).
- Range = 48 - 39 = 9 marks.
• (b) Mean (Average):
- Sum = 48 + 42 + 45 + 39 + 46 = 220 marks.
- Average = 220 ÷ 5 = 44 marks out of 50.

Q19 HOTS • Data Inference Riddle
In a class of 28 students, all students play either Chess or Carrom or both. 18 students play Chess, and 16 students play Carrom. How many students play both Chess and Carrom?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Set Principle Formula ($N(A \cup B) = N(A) + N(B) - N(A \cap B)$):
1. Total unique students = 28.
2. Sum of individual players = 18 (Chess) + 16 (Carrom) = 34.
3. Excess count due to double-counting students who play both games = 34 - 28 = 6 students.
4. Therefore, exactly 6 students play both Chess and Carrom.
(Check: Chess only = 12, Carrom only = 10, Both = 6. 12 + 10 + 6 = 28. Verified!)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sum of sets = 18 + 16 = 34 1.5 M
Students playing both = 6 with proof 1.5 M

Q23 True or False with Statistics Reason
State True or False with reasoning:
(a) A bar graph must always have equal spacing between its bars.
(b) The scale of a bar graph must always start from zero.
(c) A pictograph can only display whole numbers and cannot represent fractions.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Justifications:
• (a) True: Equal uniform spacing between bars prevents visual distortion and maintains aesthetic and statistical consistency.
• (b) True: Starting bar graphs at zero prevents exaggerating differences between categories.
• (c) False: A pictograph can represent fractional quantities using half or partial symbols (e.g. half a symbol represents half the scale value).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): True with spacing reason 1.0 M
Part (b): True with baseline reason 1.0 M
Part (c): False with half-symbol proof 1.0 M
Q24 Comprehensive Data Handling Project

Competency Question: Van Mahotsav Sapling Planting Audit

During the annual Van Mahotsav tree plantation drive, four houses of a school planted neem, peepal, and gulmohar saplings. Red House planted 85, Blue House planted 110, Green House planted 125, and Yellow House planted 80 saplings.

Analytical Task: (a) What is the total count of saplings planted by all four houses?
(b) By what percentage did Green House exceed Yellow House in saplings planted?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Total Saplings: 85 + 110 + 125 + 80 = 400 saplings.
(b) Percentage Difference: 125 - 80/80 × 100 = 45/80 × 100 = 56.25%.
