Chapter 15: Data Through Pictures

चित्रों से आँकड़े (चित्रालेख, टैली मार्क्स एवं दण्ड आलेख) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Pictographs: Visual representation of data using pictures/icons where 1 icon represents a fixed key value.
  • Tally marks grouping: Groups of five (|||| with a diagonal strike through).
  • Bar Graphs: Graphical displays using rectangular bars of uniform width whose heights represent quantities.
  • Interpreting trends, identifying maximum/minimum values, and computing totals from charts.
  • Scale: $1\text{ symbol} = 25\text{ books}$.
  • (a) Storybooks: $5 \times 25 = \mathbf{125\text{ books}}$.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
A school library records books borrowed by students: Grade 3 = 20 books; Grade 4 = 35 books; Grade 5 = 40 books; Grade 6 = 25 books.
(a) Which grade borrowed the most books?
(b) Which grade borrowed the fewest books?
(c) What is the total number of books borrowed?
(d) If each 📚 symbol in a pictograph represents 5 books, how many symbols are drawn for Grade 5 and Grade 4?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Most Books: Grade 5 (40 books).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): Grade 5 1 Mark
Part (b): Grade 3 1 Mark
Part (c): 120 books 1 Mark
Part (d): 8 and 7 symbols 1 Mark

Q3 Textbook Exercise
In a village school library, a pictograph represents books where 1 📚 symbol = 25 books. The library has: Storybooks: 📚📚📚📚📚; Science books: 📚📚📚; Mathematics books: 📚📚📚📚. (a) How many storybooks are there? (b) How many more storybooks are there than science books? (c) What is the total number of books?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Scale: $1\text{ symbol} = 25\text{ books}$.
  2. (a) Storybooks: $5 \times 25 = \mathbf{125\text{ books}}$.
  3. Science books: $3 \times 25 = 75\text{ books}$.
    Mathematics books: $4 \times 25 = 100\text{ books}$.
  4. (b) Difference between storybooks and science books:
    $125 - 75 = \mathbf{50\text{ books}}$ (or $2 \text{ symbols} \times 25 = 50$).
  5. (c) Total books in library:
    $125 + 75 + 100 = \mathbf{300\text{ books}}$ (or $12 \text{ symbols} \times 25 = 300$).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 5 × 25 = 125 storybooks 1.5 Marks
Part (b): 125 - 75 = 50 books 1 Mark
Part (c): Total = 300 books 1.5 Marks

Q5 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Data Through Pictures', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 233 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 21. Find the next three terms (254, 275, 296) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{233}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{21}$.
3. Term 2: 233 + 21 = 254.
4. Term 3: 254 + 21 = 275.
5. Term 4: 275 + 21 = 296.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 233 + (n - 1) \times 21$. Successive addition of 21 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (254, 275, 296) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+21) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q7 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Data Through Pictures' has length $L = 14\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (14 + 5) = 2 \times 19 = \mathbf{38\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 14 \times 5 = \mathbf{70\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 38 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 70 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 70 × ₹25 = ₹1750 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Data Through Pictures': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 220. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 220$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 220 + 16 = 236$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{236}{4} = \mathbf{59}$.
4. Verification: (59 × 4) - 16 = 236 - 16 = 220. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 236 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 59 with full verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Competency Question: Van Mahotsav Sapling Planting Audit

During the annual Van Mahotsav tree plantation drive, four houses of a school planted neem, peepal, and gulmohar saplings. Red House planted 85, Blue House planted 110, Green House planted 125, and Yellow House planted 80 saplings.

Analytical Task: (a) What is the total count of saplings planted by all four houses?
(b) By what percentage did Green House exceed Yellow House in saplings planted?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Total Saplings: 85 + 110 + 125 + 80 = 400 saplings.
(b) Percentage Difference: 125 - 80/80 × 100 = 45/80 × 100 = 56.25%.
