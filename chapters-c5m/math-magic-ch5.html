Chapter 5: Does it Look the Same?

क्या यह एक जैसा दिखता है? (दर्पण रेखाएँ, आधा घुमाव एवं घूर्णन सममिति) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Mirror halves: lines dividing a shape such that one half reflects exactly onto the other half.
  • Half turn (180° rotation): letters and numbers that look the same after a half turn (H, I, N, O, S, X, Z; 0, 8).
  • Quarter turn (90° rotation) and one-third / one-sixth turn (120° / 60° on fans and windmills).
  • Distinguishing between line symmetry and rotational symmetry.
  • H: Turns upside down to form H (Yes).
  • I: Turns upside down to form I (Yes).
  • N: Turns upside down to form N (Yes).
  • O: Turns upside down to form O (Yes).
  • S: Turns upside down to form S (Yes).
  • X: Turns upside down to form X (Yes).
  • Z: Turns upside down to form Z (Yes).

Q3 Textbook Exercise
Consider a ceiling fan with 3 blades, and another ceiling fan with 4 blades. (a) By what fraction of a turn will the 3-blade fan look exactly the same as its original position? (b) By what fraction of a turn will the 4-blade fan look the same? (c) Does the 3-blade fan look the same on a half turn?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) 3-blade fan: The 3 blades are placed at equal angles of 360° ÷ 3 = 120°.
Therefore, it looks the same after a one-third turn (1/3 turn or 120°).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Q4 Textbook Exercise
Which of the following capital English letters look exactly the same after a half-turn (180° rotation): A, H, I, M, N, O, S, T, X, Z?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

A letter looks identical after a half-turn (1/2 turn or 180° rotation) if it possesses point symmetry (order-2 rotational symmetry) about its center.

  • H: Turns upside down to form H (Yes).
  • I: Turns upside down to form I (Yes).
  • N: Turns upside down to form N (Yes).
  • O: Turns upside down to form O (Yes).
  • S: Turns upside down to form S (Yes).
  • X: Turns upside down to form X (Yes).
  • Z: Turns upside down to form Z (Yes).

Letters that do NOT look the same: A (becomes upside down ∀), M (becomes W), T (becomes ⊥).

Final Answer: The letters are H, I, N, O, S, X, and Z.

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identifying symmetric letters: H, I, N, O, S, X, Z 3 Marks
Explaining rotational symmetry rule 1 Mark

Q6 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Does it Look the Same?', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 83 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 11. Find the next three terms (94, 105, 116) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{83}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{11}$.
3. Term 2: 83 + 11 = 94.
4. Term 3: 94 + 11 = 105.
5. Term 4: 105 + 11 = 116.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 83 + (n - 1) \times 11$. Successive addition of 11 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (94, 105, 116) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+11) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q8 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Does it Look the Same?' has length $L = 16\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (16 + 5) = 2 \times 21 = \mathbf{42\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 16 \times 5 = \mathbf{80\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 80 × ₹25 = ₹2000.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 42 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 80 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 80 × ₹25 = ₹2000 1.0 M

Q10 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Does it Look the Same?': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 140. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 140$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 140 + 16 = 156$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{156}{4} = \mathbf{39}$.
4. Verification: (39 × 4) - 16 = 156 - 16 = 140. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 156 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 39 with full verification 1.5 M
