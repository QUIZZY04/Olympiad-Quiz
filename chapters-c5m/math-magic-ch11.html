Chapter 11: Area and its Boundary

क्षेत्रफल और घेरा (परिमाप, डाक टिकट क्षेत्रफल एवं राजा का सोने का तार) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Perimeter = Total length of the boundary: P = 2 × (L + B) for rectangles, P = 4 × S for squares.
  • Area = Measure of region enclosed: A = L × B for rectangles, A = S × S for squares.
  • Shapes with the SAME perimeter can enclose DIFFERENT areas.
  • A square encloses the maximum area for any given rectangular perimeter.

Q3 Textbook Exercise
Arbaz plans to tile his kitchen floor with green square tiles of size 10 cm × 10 cm. The length of the floor is 200 cm and the width is 150 cm. (a) What is the area of the kitchen floor? (b) What is the area of one tile? (c) How many tiles will he need?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. (a) Area of the floor:
    $\text{Floor Area} = 200\text{ cm} \times 150\text{ cm} = \mathbf{30,000\text{ cm}^2}$.
  2. (b) Area of one tile:
    $\text{Tile Area} = 10\text{ cm} \times 10\text{ cm} = \mathbf{100\text{ cm}^2}$.
  3. (c) Number of tiles needed:
    $\text{Number of tiles} = \frac{\text{Floor Area}}{\text{Tile Area}} = \frac{30,000}{100} = \mathbf{300\text{ tiles}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Floor Area = 200 × 150 = 30,000 cm² 1.5 Marks
Tile Area = 100 cm² 1 Mark
Total tiles = 30,000 / 100 = 300 tiles 1.5 Marks
Q4 Textbook Exercise
A square has a side of 10 cm. A small square of side 1 cm is cut out from one corner. Does the boundary (perimeter) increase, decrease, or remain the same? What happens to the area?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Original Square:
    $\text{Perimeter} = 4 \times 10 = 40\text{ cm}$.
    $\text{Area} = 10 \times 10 = 100\text{ cm}^2$.
  2. After cutting 1 cm square from corner:
    The two cut edges inside the corner measure $1\text{ cm} + 1\text{ cm} = 2\text{ cm}$, which replace the two outer 1 cm segments removed.
    Therefore, the boundary (perimeter) remains exactly the same (40 cm).
  3. Area:
    Area decreases by $1\text{ cm}^2$: $\text{New Area} = 100 - 1 = \mathbf{99\text{ cm}^2}$ (Decreases).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation that perimeter remains unchanged at 40 cm 1.5 Marks
Explanation that area decreases to 99 cm² 1.5 Marks

Q7 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Area and its Boundary', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 173 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 17. Find the next three terms (190, 207, 224) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{173}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{17}$.
3. Term 2: 173 + 17 = 190.
4. Term 3: 190 + 17 = 207.
5. Term 4: 207 + 17 = 224.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 173 + (n - 1) \times 17$. Successive addition of 17 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (190, 207, 224) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+17) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q9 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Area and its Boundary' has length $L = 16\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (16 + 5) = 2 \times 21 = \mathbf{42\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 16 \times 5 = \mathbf{80\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 80 × ₹25 = ₹2000.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 42 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 80 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 80 × ₹25 = ₹2000 1.0 M

Q11 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Area and its Boundary': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 188. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 188$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 188 + 16 = 204$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{204}{4} = \mathbf{51}$.
4. Verification: (51 × 4) - 16 = 204 - 16 = 188. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 204 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 51 with full verification 1.5 M
