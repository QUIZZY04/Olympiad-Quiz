Chapter 12: Smart Charts

स्मार्ट चार्ट (चपाती चार्ट / पाई चार्ट, टैली चिह्न एवं पेड़ आरेख) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Tally marks for rapid counting: 5 is represented by four vertical lines and one cross slash (||||).
  • Chapati Chart (Pie Chart / Circle Graph): Representing portions of a whole as sectors (1/2, 1/4, 1/8).
  • Growth bar graphs: Tracking seed/plant growth over consecutive days.
  • Family trees and generation tracking.
  • Total students: 40.
  • (a) Cricket: $\frac{1}{2} \times 40 = \mathbf{20\text{ students}}$.
  • (b) Football: $\frac{1}{4} \times 40 = \mathbf{10\text{ students}}$.
  • (c) Badminton: $\frac{1}{4} \times 40 = \mathbf{10\text{ students}}$.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
In a class of 40 children, a teacher asks each child which sport they like most. The responses are shown in a Chapati chart:
• Half of the children like Cricket.
• One-fourth like Football.
• The remaining like Badminton.
(a) How many children like Cricket?
(b) How many children like Football?
(c) What fraction and how many children like Badminton?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Cricket: 1/2 × 40 = 20 children.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 20 children 1 Mark
Part (b): 10 children 1 Mark
Part (c): 1/4 and 10 children 2 Marks

Q3 Textbook Exercise
In a class of 40 students, a Chapati Chart (Pie Chart) shows their favorite sports: 1/2 of the students like Cricket, 1/4 like Football, and 1/4 like Badminton. (a) How many students like Cricket? (b) How many like Football? (c) How many like Badminton?

  2. (a) Cricket: $\frac{1}{2} \times 40 = \mathbf{20\text{ students}}$.

Check: $20 + 10 + 10 = 40$ students.

Football: 1/4 × 40 = 10 1 Mark

Q5 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Smart Charts', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 188 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 18. Find the next three terms (206, 224, 242) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{188}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{18}$.
3. Term 2: 188 + 18 = 206.
4. Term 3: 206 + 18 = 224.
5. Term 4: 224 + 18 = 242.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 188 + (n - 1) \times 18$. Successive addition of 18 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (206, 224, 242) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+18) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q7 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Smart Charts' has length $L = 11\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (11 + 5) = 2 \times 16 = \mathbf{32\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 11 \times 5 = \mathbf{55\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 55 × ₹25 = ₹1375.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 32 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 55 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 55 × ₹25 = ₹1375 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Smart Charts': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 196. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 196$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 196 + 16 = 212$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{212}{4} = \mathbf{53}$.
4. Verification: (53 × 4) - 16 = 212 - 16 = 196. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 212 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 53 with full verification 1.5 M
