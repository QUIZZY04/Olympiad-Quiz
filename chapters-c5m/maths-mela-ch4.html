Q1 Travel Budget • Hotel Package Comparison
A hotel offers two package options for a 3-night stay for a family of 4:
• Package A: Flat rate of ₹2,800 per night including breakfast.
• Package B: Room rent of ₹2,200 per night plus ₹250 per person per day for breakfast.
(a) Calculate the total cost for Package A.
(b) Calculate the total cost for Package B.
(c) Which package is more economical, and by how much?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations (Duration = 3 nights, 4 persons):
• (a) Package A Total: 3 nights × ₹2,800 = ₹8,400.
• (b) Package B Total:
- Room rent: 3 × ₹2,200 = ₹6,600.
- Breakfast cost: 4 persons × ₹250 × 3 days = ₹3,000.
- Total = ₹6,600 + ₹3,000 = ₹9,600.
• (c) Comparison: Package A is cheaper. Difference = ₹9,600 - ₹8,400 = ₹1,200. Package A saves ₹1,200.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Package A = ₹8,400 1.0 M
Package B = ₹9,600 1.0 M
Package A saves ₹1,200 1.0 M
Q2 Excursion Expenses • Per-Student Share
A school excursion of 45 students incurs expenses:
• Bus rental: ₹14,500
• Entry tickets: ₹90 per student
• Packed meals: ₹150 per student
• Guide charges: ₹1,500
(a) Find the grand total expenditure for the trip.
(b) Calculate the contribution amount required from each student to cover all costs exactly.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Expense Calculations (45 Students):
• Bus rental = ₹14,500
• Entry tickets = 45 × ₹90 = ₹4,050
• Packed meals = 45 × ₹150 = ₹6,750
• Guide charges = ₹1,500
• (a) Grand Total: ₹14,500 + ₹4,050 + ₹6,750 + ₹1,500 = ₹26,800.
• (b) Per Student Share: ₹26,800 ÷ 45 = ₹595.55 ≈ ₹596 per student.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Itemized totals (Tickets ₹4,050, Meals ₹6,750) 1.0 M
Grand total = ₹26,800 1.0 M
Per student share = ₹596 1.0 M
Q3 Fuel Consumption & Cost
A family car consumes 1 litre of petrol for every 15 km traveled. They plan a road trip of 1,080 km.
(a) How many litres of petrol are required for the round trip?
(b) If petrol costs ₹96 per litre, what is their total expenditure on fuel?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations:
• (a) Petrol Needed: Total distance ÷ Mileage = 1,080 ÷ 15 = 72 litres.
• (b) Fuel Cost: 72 litres × ₹96 = ₹6,912.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Petrol needed = 72 L 1.5 M
Total fuel cost = ₹6,912 1.5 M

Q5 Unitary Method • Meal Charges
A highway restaurant charges ₹480 for 4 thalis.
(a) What is the cost of 1 thali?
(b) What will be the total bill for 9 thalis?
(c) How many thalis can be purchased for ₹1,200?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Unitary Method Steps:
• (a) Cost of 1 Thali: ₹480 ÷ 4 = ₹120.
• (b) Cost of 9 Thalis: 9 × ₹120 = ₹1,080.
• (c) Thalis for ₹1,200: ₹1,200 ÷ ₹120 = 10 thalis.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Cost of 1 thali = ₹120 1.0 M
Cost of 9 thalis = ₹1,080 1.0 M
10 thalis for ₹1,200 1.0 M
Q6 Time-Speed-Distance • Multi-Stop Journey
A luxury bus leaves Jaipur at 6:00 AM. It travels at 60 km/h for 3 hours, stops for 45 minutes for breakfast, and then travels the remaining 140 km at 70 km/h.
(a) What is the total distance of the trip?
(b) What was the travel time spent driving?
(c) At what time does the bus reach its destination?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Schedule:
• (a) Distance: First leg = 3 h × 60 km/h = 180 km. Second leg = 140 km. Total distance = 180 + 140 = 320 km.
• (b) Driving Time: First leg = 3 hours. Second leg = 140 ÷ 70 = 2 hours. Total driving time = 3 + 2 = 5 hours.
• (c) Arrival Time:
- Total elapsed time = 5 hours driving + 45 min breakfast = 5 hours 45 minutes.
- 6:00 AM + 5 h 45 min = 11:45 AM.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total distance = 320 km 1.0 M
Driving time = 5 hours 1.0 M
Arrival time = 11:45 AM 1.0 M

• (b) Final Total: ₹35,000 + ₹1,750 = ₹36,750.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
GST = ₹1,750 1.5 M
Final bill = ₹36,750 1.5 M

Q14 Multi-Family Travel Split
Three families rent a vacation villa for 4 days at ₹6,500 per day. They share the accommodation cost equally.
(a) Find the total rent of the villa for 4 days.
(b) What is the share of each family?
(c) If Family 1 checks out one day early (staying 3 days), how should the total bill be divided fairly among the 3 families based on room-days?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Working:
• (a) Total Rent: 4 days × ₹6,500 = ₹26,000.
• (b) Equal Share (All 4 days): ₹26,000 ÷ 3 = ₹8,666.67 per family.
• (c) Fair Room-Days Division:
- Total family-days = 3 + 4 + 4 = 11 family-days.
- Cost per family-day = ₹26,000 ÷ 11 = ₹2,363.64.
- Family 1 (3 days) pays: 3 × ₹2,363.64 = ₹7,091.
- Family 2 & 3 (4 days each) pay: 4 × ₹2,363.64 = ₹9,454.50 each.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total rent = ₹26,000 1.0 M
Equal share = ₹8,667 1.0 M
Weighted room-days split 1.0 M
Q15 Train Berth Allotment • Logic
In an Indian Railways sleeper coach, berths are numbered 1 to 72. Lower berths are 1, 4, 9, 12, 17, 20... (numbers leaving remainders 1 or 4 when divided by 8).
(a) What type of berth is Berth 25?
(b) What type of berth is Berth 32?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Berth Pattern in 8-berth Compartment:
• 1: Lower, 2: Middle, 3: Upper, 4: Lower, 5: Middle, 6: Upper, 7: Side Lower, 8: Side Upper.
• (a) Berth 25: 25 ÷ 8 gives remainder 1. Remainder 1 corresponds to a Lower Berth.
• (b) Berth 32: 32 ÷ 8 gives remainder 0 (exact multiple of 8). This corresponds to a Side Upper Berth (SU).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Berth pattern rule stated (modulo 8) 1.5 M
Berth 25 is Lower; Berth 32 is Side Upper 1.5 M
Q16 Fuel Tank Capacity • Kilometer Range
An SUV has a 65-litre fuel tank. Its highway mileage is 14 km/litre.
(a) What is the maximum driving range of the vehicle on a full tank?
(b) The driver sees a warning that 12 litres of petrol remain. Can the SUV reach a petrol station 180 km away without refueling?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Working:
• (a) Maximum Range: 65 L × 14 km/L = 910 km.
• (b) Remaining Range: 12 L × 14 km/L = 168 km.
- Since 168 km < 180 km, No, the SUV cannot reach the station 180 km away without refueling (it will run out of fuel 12 km before reaching).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Max range = 910 km 1.5 M
Remaining range = 168 km with negative conclusion 1.5 M

Q19 HOTS • Travel Riddle
A train leaves City A and City B at the same time, traveling toward each other along parallel tracks. Train 1 travels at 60 km/h and Train 2 travels at 80 km/h. If the cities are 420 km apart, after how many hours will the trains meet?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Relative Speed Concept:
1. Since the trains are moving toward each other, their combined speed is: 60 km/h + 80 km/h = 140 km/h.
2. Total distance to cover = 420 km.
3. Time taken to meet = Distance ÷ Relative Speed = 420 ÷ 140 = 3 hours.
4. Verification: In 3 hours, Train 1 covers 3 × 60 = 180 km; Train 2 covers 3 × 80 = 240 km. Total = 180 + 240 = 420 km. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Combined speed = 140 km/h 1.5 M
Meeting time = 3 hours with verification 1.5 M

Competency Question: Taxi Billing with Minimum Distance Threshold

A taxi operator charges a flat hire of ₹2,500 per day covering up to 250 km. Any extra distance above 250 km in a day is billed at ₹12 per km. On a 3-day tour, the odometer readings showed: Day 1: 240 km; Day 2: 290 km; Day 3: 275 km.

Analytical Task: Calculate the total billing for each of the 3 days and the overall bill for the trip.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
Day 1 (240 km): < 250 km ⇒ Flat rate = ₹2,500.
Day 2 (290 km): Extra = 40 km ⇒ ₹2,500 + (40 × 12) = ₹2,980.
Day 3 (275 km): Extra = 25 km ⇒ ₹2,500 + (25 × 12) = ₹2,800.
Grand Total: 2,500 + 2,980 + 2,800 = ₹8,280.
Rubric: 1M concept identification + 1M step execution + 1M conclusion
