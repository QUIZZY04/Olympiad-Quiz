Q1 Textbook Exercise
A hotel offers two package options for a 3-night stay:
• Package A: ₹3,200 per night inclusive of breakfast.
• Package B: ₹2,600 per night room only, plus ₹350 per day for breakfast per person.
For a couple (2 people) staying for 3 nights, which package is cheaper and by how much?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Package A (2 people, 3 nights): 3 × 3,200 = ₹9,600.

Q4 Textbook Exercise
Find three equivalent fractions for each of the following: (a) 2/5, (b) 3/7, (c) 4/9. Show your multiplication steps.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  • (a) 2/5:
    $\frac{2 \times 2}{5 \times 2} = \mathbf{\frac{4}{10}}$, $\frac{2 \times 3}{5 \times 3} = \mathbf{\frac{6}{15}}$, $\frac{2 \times 4}{5 \times 4} = \mathbf{\frac{8}{20}}$.
  • (b) 3/7:
    $\frac{3 \times 2}{7 \times 2} = \mathbf{\frac{6}{14}}$, $\frac{3 \times 3}{7 \times 3} = \mathbf{\frac{9}{21}}$, $\frac{3 \times 4}{7 \times 4} = \mathbf{\frac{12}{28}}$.
  • (c) 4/9:
    $\frac{4 \times 2}{9 \times 2} = \mathbf{\frac{8}{18}}$, $\frac{4 \times 3}{9 \times 3} = \mathbf{\frac{12}{27}}$, $\frac{4 \times 4}{9 \times 4} = \mathbf{\frac{16}{36}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Equivalent fractions for 2/5 1 Mark
Equivalent fractions for 3/7 1 Mark
Equivalent fractions for 4/9 1 Mark

Q6 National Flag Fractions • Symmetry
In the Indian National Flag (Tricolour):
(a) What fraction of the flag is saffron, white, and green?
(b) Is the white portion really 1/3 of the flag? Explain with reference to the Ashoka Chakra.
(c) How many spokes does the Chakra have, and what is the angle between two consecutive spokes?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Primary Fractions:
The flag is divided into 3 equal horizontal bands. Each band represents 1/3 of the total flag area (Saffron = 1/3, White = 1/3, Green = 1/3).

(b) White Portion Analysis:
Strictly speaking, the white band contains the blue Ashoka Chakra in the centre. Because the Chakra takes up space inside the white band, the pure white cloth area is slightly less than 1/3 of the flag.

(c) Ashoka Chakra Angles:
• Total spokes = 24 spokes.
• Angle between two consecutive spokes = 360° / 24 = 15°.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct 1/3 fractions stated 1.0 M
Logical explanation regarding Chakra area 1.0 M
Spoke count (24) and angle (15°) 1.0 M
Q7 Ramu's Vegetable Field • Fractions Division
Ramu's vegetable field has 9 equal parts. He grows potatoes on 2 parts, tomatoes on 3 parts, spinach on 1 part, and brinjals on 2 parts.
(a) Which vegetable occupies the largest part of the field? What fraction is it?
(b) What fraction of the field is used for growing potatoes and brinjals together?
(c) What fraction remains for chillies?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Fraction Calculations (Total = 9 parts):
• Tomatoes: 3/9 = 1/3
• Potatoes: 2/9
• Spinach: 1/9
• Brinjals: 2/9

(a) Largest Part: Tomatoes occupy the largest part, which is 3/9 or 1/3 of the field.

(b) Potatoes + Brinjals: 2/9 + 2/9 = 4/9 of the field.

(c) Remaining for Chillies: 9/9 - (3/9 + 2/9 + 1/9 + 2/9) = 9/9 - 8/9 = 1/9.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Largest part (Tomatoes, 3/9) 1.0 M
Combined fraction (4/9) 1.0 M
Remaining fraction (1/9) 1.0 M

Competency Question: Taxi Billing with Minimum Distance Threshold

A taxi operator charges a flat hire of ₹2,500 per day covering up to 250 km. Any extra distance above 250 km in a day is billed at ₹12 per km. On a 3-day tour, the odometer readings showed: Day 1: 240 km; Day 2: 290 km; Day 3: 275 km.

Analytical Task: Calculate the total billing for each of the 3 days and the overall bill for the trip.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
Day 1 (240 km): < 250 km ⇒ Flat rate = ₹2,500.
Day 2 (290 km): Extra = 40 km ⇒ ₹2,500 + (40 × 12) = ₹2,980.
Day 3 (275 km): Extra = 25 km ⇒ ₹2,500 + (25 × 12) = ₹2,800.
Grand Total: 2,500 + 2,980 + 2,800 = ₹8,280.
