Chapter 8: Mapping Your Way

नक्शा (इंडिया गेट, राजपथ, सेंट्रल हेक्सागन एवं नक्शे का पैमाना) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Reading and interpreting scaled architectural maps and ground plans.
  • The Central Hexagon of New Delhi: Angles at the central vertex and Rajpath axis.
  • Map Scale Ratios: e.g., 1 cm = 1 km or 1 cm = 2 km.
  • Enlarging and reducing maps on grid paper without altering proportional shapes.
  • Given Scale: $1\text{ cm} = 200\text{ km}$.

Q1 Textbook Exercise
Look at the map of New Delhi and Rajpath where the scale is 1 cm = 2 km:
(a) On the map, the distance between Rashtrapati Bhavan and India Gate is 4 cm. What is the actual ground distance in kilometres?
(b) The parade route on the map measures: Rajpath (4 cm) + Tilak Marg (3 cm) + Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg (2 cm) + Subhash Marg (2.5 cm). What is the total route distance on ground in kilometres?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

(a) Distance between Rashtrapati Bhavan and India Gate:

Map Distance = 4 cm; Scale = 1 cm = 2 km.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 4 × 2 = 8 km 1.5 Marks
Part (b): 11.5 cm and 23 km 2.5 Marks

Q3 Textbook Exercise
On a school tour map, the scale is given as: 1 cm on the map = 200 km on the ground. (a) If the distance between two cities on the map is 3.5 cm, what is the actual ground distance? (b) If two hill stations are 500 km apart on the ground, how far apart will they be on the map?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
  1. Given Scale: $1\text{ cm} = 200\text{ km}$.
  2. (a) Actual distance for 3.5 cm:
    $\text{Ground Distance} = 3.5 \times 200\text{ km} = \mathbf{700\text{ km}}$.
  3. (b) Map distance for 500 km:
    $\text{Map Distance} = \frac{500}{200} = \mathbf{2.5\text{ cm}}$.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 3.5 × 200 = 700 km 2 Marks
Part (b): 500 / 200 = 2.5 cm 2 Marks

Q5 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Mathematical Patterns
In 'Mapping Your Way', analyze the following arithmetic progression: The first term is 128 and each subsequent term increases by a fixed constant of 14. Find the next three terms (142, 156, 170) and determine the mathematical rule for the $n$-th term.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Investigation:
1. Given initial term $T_1 = \mathbf{128}$.
2. Common difference $d = \mathbf{14}$.
3. Term 2: 128 + 14 = 142.
4. Term 3: 142 + 14 = 156.
5. Term 4: 156 + 14 = 170.
6. General Rule: $T_n = 128 + (n - 1) \times 14$. Successive addition of 14 maintains uniform spacing on the number line.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate calculation of terms (142, 156, 170) 1.5 M
Valid statement of rule (+14) and progression formula 1.5 M

Q7 Measurement & Unit Conversion • Area, Perimeter & Volume
A rectangular courtyard described in 'Mapping Your Way' has length $L = 13\text{ m}$ and width $B = 5\text{ m}$.
(a) Find the perimeter of the boundary fencing.
(b) Calculate the total area enclosed.
(c) If 1 square metre of turfing costs ₹25, what is the total cost of carpeting the floor?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Geometric Steps:
• (a) Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (L + B) = 2 \times (13 + 5) = 2 \times 18 = \mathbf{36\text{ m}}$.
• (b) Area: $A = L \times B = 13 \times 5 = \mathbf{65\text{ sq m}}$.
• (c) Cost of Turfing: Total area × Rate = 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formula & calculation of Perimeter = 36 m 1.0 M
Formula & calculation of Area = 65 sq m 1.0 M
Cost computation: 65 × ₹25 = ₹1625 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning • Algebraic & Spatial Puzzles
HOTS Challenge from 'Mapping Your Way': A mystery number $N$ is multiplied by 4. When 16 is subtracted from the product, the final value obtained is 164. Find the mystery number using inverse operations and verify your answer step-by-step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Inverse Operations & Algebraic Proof:
1. Mathematical sentence: $(4 \times N) - 16 = 164$.
2. Undo subtraction by adding 16: 4 \times N = 164 + 16 = 180$.
3. Undo multiplication by dividing by 4: $N = \frac{180}{4} = \mathbf{45}$.
4. Verification: (45 × 4) - 16 = 180 - 16 = 164. Proof is verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Setting up inverse relation: 4N = 180 1.5 M
Correct mystery number N = 45 with full verification 1.5 M
