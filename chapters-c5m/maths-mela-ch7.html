Chapter 7: Shapes and Patterns

आकृतियाँ और पैटर्न (ज्यामितीय आकृतियाँ, घूर्णन एवं टाइलिंग) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • 2D geometric shapes: Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Pentagons, Hexagons and their properties.
  • Rotational patterns: Rotating a motif by 1/4 turn (90°), 1/2 turn (180°), or 1/6 turn (60°).
  • Tessellations (Tiling): Covering a flat surface completely with geometric tiles without any gaps or overlapping.
  • Number and shape sequences following progressive mathematical rules.
  • (a) 1, 4, 9, 16, 25: Rule: Square of consecutive natural numbers ($1^2, 2^2, 3^2, 4^2, 5^2$). Next terms: $6^2 = \mathbf{36}$, $7^2 = \mathbf{49}$.
  • (b) 3, 6, 12, 24: Rule: Multiply by 2. Next terms: $24 \times 2 = \mathbf{48}$, $48 \times 2 = \mathbf{96}$.
  • (c) 100, 95, 85, 70: Rule: Subtract consecutive multiples of 5 ($-5, -10, -15$). Next terms: $70 - 20 = \mathbf{50}$, $50 - 25 = \mathbf{25}$.

Q6 Magic Squares • 3x3 Grid Sum
Fill in the numbers 1 to 9 in a 3×3 grid such that the sum of each row, column, and diagonal is 15. What number must be placed in the center, and why?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Magic Square Solution (Sum = 15):
  8   1   6  → 15
  3   5   7  → 15
  4   9   2  → 15
  ↓   ↓   ↓
 15  15  15  (Diagonals: 8+5+2=15, 6+5+4=15)
2. Center Number Rationale:
The center cell participates in 4 lines (row, column, and two diagonals). The average of numbers from 1 to 9 is (1+2+...+9)/9 = 45/9 = 5. Therefore, 5 must be placed in the center cell for symmetry.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct 3x3 magic grid configuration 2.0 M
Valid mathematical reasoning for center 5 1.0 M

Competency Question: Heritage Haveli Flooring Tile Design

An architect designs a courtyard floor measuring 12 metres by 8 metres using rectangular tiles of size 40 cm by 20 cm.

Analytical Task: (a) What is the area of the courtyard in square centimetres?
(b) What is the area of one tile?
(c) How many tiles are needed to pave the entire floor without gaps?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Suggested Model Solution:
(a) Courtyard Area: 1,200 × 800 = 9,60,000 sq cm.
(b) Tile Area: 40 × 20 = 800 sq cm.
(c) Number of Tiles: 960,000/800 = 1,200 tiles.
