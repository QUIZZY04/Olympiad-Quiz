Chapter 25: Spicy Riddles

Indian Spices (Masale): Black Pepper, Turmeric, Clove, Cumin, Cardamom & Kerala's Spice Gardens | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Kerala: The historic 'Land of Spices' (Tejpatta, Chhoti Elaichi, Badi Elaichi, Kali Mirch).
  • Spice Riddles and medicinal properties:
  • 1. Red Chilli (Lal Mirch): Sharp, spicy, makes tongue burn, watering eyes.
  • 2. Turmeric (Haldi): Bright yellow, heals cuts and wounds, mixed in hot milk for coughs.
  • 3. Black Pepper (Kali Mirch): Tiny, round, black ball, sharp and pungent, ground coarse or fine.
  • 4. Cumin (Jeera): Slender brown seeds fried in oil/ghee to scent dal; aids digestion.
  • 5. Clove (Laung): Looks like a nail with a round crown; cures severe toothache.
  • 6. Cardamom (Elaichi): Green fragrant pods that sweeten kheer and freshen breath.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (13 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Botanical Remedies & Spices
Solve the riddles: (a) 'I look like a nail with a round head; put me on your aching tooth and your pain will flee.' Who am I? (b) 'I heal cuts, make food look yellow, and am mixed in milk when you cough.' Who am I? (c) Why was Kerala famous worldwide as the 'Land of Spices'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Riddle 1: Clove (Laung). Its oil contains eugenol, a natural anesthetic that numbs toothache instantly.

(b) Riddle 2: Turmeric (Haldi). Its active compound curcumin has powerful anti-inflammatory and antiseptic healing properties.

(c) Why Kerala is the 'Land of Spices':
Kerala's warm tropical monsoon climate and rich Western Ghat mountain soil are ideal for growing the world's most aromatic spices—especially black pepper (known as 'Black Gold'), green cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves. Arab, Portuguese, and Dutch traders sailed across oceans for centuries to trade in Kerala spices.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Solves Clove (Laung) with toothache benefit 1.0 M
Solves Turmeric (Haldi) with healing antiseptic benefit 1.0 M
Explains Kerala's historical 'Land of Spices' status 1.0 M
Q2 Spices of India • Medicinal Uses
Match the following Indian spices with their traditional medicinal benefits:
1. Clove (Laung)
2. Turmeric (Haldi)
3. Fennel (Saunf)
4. Black Pepper (Kaali Mirch)
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Spices and Their Medicinal Benefits:
1. Clove (Laung): Looks like a tiny nail with a crown; clove oil provides instant relief from acute toothache and gum pain.
2. Turmeric (Haldi): Bright yellow antiseptic root; applied on wounds to heal cuts and mixed with warm milk to cure body aches.
3. Fennel (Saunf): Green, sweet, and cooling seeds; chewed after meals to aid digestion and freshen breath.
4. Black Pepper (Kaali Mirch): Small, round, wrinkled black bead; sharp and pungent; cures sore throats and seasonal colds.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four correct descriptions linking spice to its medicinal action 4.0 M
Q3 Historical Geography • The Spice Coast
Why has Kerala historically been renowned as the 'Land of Spices'? Which spices grow abundantly there?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Kerala - Land of Spices:
The warm, moist climate and fertile slopes of the Western Ghats in Kerala provide the perfect environment for growing world-class spices like black pepper (black gold), cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, and ginger. For thousands of years, ancient Arab, Roman, and European traders sailed across oceans to trade for Kerala's aromatic spices.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Western Ghats microclimate and list of 4 key spices 2.0 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Garam Masala Ingredients

Grandmother grinds a fragrant powder called 'Garam Masala' to sprinkle over curries.

Analytical Task: Name four whole spices that are roasted and ground together to create Garam Masala.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Coriander seeds (Dhania)
2. Cumin seeds (Jeera)
3. Black Pepper (Kali Mirch)
4. Cinnamon (Dalchini) and Cloves (Laung).
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for identifying at least 3 correct spices; 1.5 Marks for explaining grinding for flavor.
