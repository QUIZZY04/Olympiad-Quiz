Chapter 1: Living Together

Family Structures, Joint & Nuclear Families, Mutual Responsibilities & Caring for Elders | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Diverse family structures in India: Joint families, nuclear families, single-parent homes, and extended family networks.
  • Sharing household chores equitably among all family members without gender stereotyping.
  • Caring for elders and differently-abled family members with patience, affection, and dignity.
  • Family traditions, cultural celebrations, and tracing family generations through family trees.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Concept Explanation • Family Dynamics
What is the difference between a nuclear family and a joint family? How does sharing daily household chores help family members?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Nuclear Family vs. Joint Family:
• Nuclear Family: Consists only of parents and their children living together in a household.
• Joint Family: An extended family where grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts, and cousins all live together under one roof, sharing a common kitchen and household resources.

2. Benefits of Sharing Household Chores:
When chores like cooking, cleaning, gardening, and washing dishes are distributed among all members: (a) No single person (especially mothers) gets overburdened, (b) Children learn practical life skills and self-reliance, and (c) It builds mutual respect and deep family bonding.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear contrast between nuclear and joint family 1.5 M
Three tangible benefits of shared domestic chores 1.5 M
Q2 Observation & Field Work • Family Tree
What is a 'family tree'? Distinguish between maternal and paternal relatives with two examples each.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Family Tree:
A family tree is a graphical diagram that illustrates the relationships between different generations of a family, starting from the oldest ancestors (grandparents or great-grandparents) down to the youngest children.

2. Paternal vs. Maternal Relatives:
• Paternal Relatives (from Father's side): Paternal Grandfather (Dada ji), Paternal Grandmother (Dadi ji), Father's brother (Chacha ji / Tau ji), Father's sister (Bua ji).
• Maternal Relatives (from Mother's side): Maternal Grandfather (Nana ji), Maternal Grandmother (Nani ji), Mother's brother (Mama ji), Mother's sister (Masi ji).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate definition of a family tree across generations 1.5 M
Correct differentiation and examples of paternal and maternal relatives 1.5 M
Q3 Think and Discuss • Caring for Elders
Why do elderly family members require special care and attention? List three practical ways children can support grandparents at home.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Why Elders Need Special Care:
As people grow old, their physical strength diminishes, vision and hearing weaken, joints become stiff, and they may suffer from chronic health conditions. They also feel lonely if not engaged in family conversations.

2. Ways Children Can Help:
• Physical Assistance: Fetching water, glasses, or medicines on time, and helping them walk safely without slippery obstacles.
• Emotional Companionship: Sitting with them, listening attentively to their life stories, and reading newspapers or books aloud to them.
• Digital Assistance: Helping them dial phone numbers or video call relatives on smartphones with patience and kindness.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Scientific and emotional reasons for elder vulnerability 1.5 M
Three realistic, actionable ways children can support elders 1.5 M
Q4 Life Skills & Values • Changing Families
What are the common reasons why families move from one place to another? How can a child adapt to a new neighbourhood?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Reasons for Family Relocation:
• Job Transfers & Employment: Parents moving to new cities for work opportunities or promotions.
• Higher Education: Shifting to cities with better schools, colleges, and training centres.
• Natural Calamities: Temporary or permanent displacement due to floods, earthquakes, or droughts.

2. Adapting to a New Neighbourhood:
A child can adapt smoothly by: introducing themselves politely to neighborhood children in the park, learning local customs and language, participating in community festivals, and keeping in touch with old friends while embracing new ones.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Valid reasons for migration/relocation 1.0 M
Positive psychological strategies for adapting to a new environment 1.0 M
Q5 Family Dynamics • Roles & Cooperation
In what ways do family members help and support each other in daily household chores and during illness?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Family Support & Cooperation:
1. Sharing Chores: Parents cook meals, wash clothes, and earn a livelihood, while children help by arranging study desks, watering plants, and tidying rooms.
2. Care During Illness: When someone falls sick, family members take turns preparing light nutritious meals, giving prescribed medicines on time, and providing comfort.
3. Emotional Bond: Celebrating festivals and overcoming difficulties together strengthens mutual love and emotional security.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sharing daily chores described 1 M
Care during illness explained 1 M
Emotional bonding and unity 1 M
Q6 Diverse Family Structures • Nuclear vs Joint
What is the key difference between a nuclear family and a joint family? Mention one advantage of each.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Comparison:
• Nuclear Family: Consists only of parents and their children living together. Advantage: Independent decision-making and peaceful privacy.
• Joint Family: Consists of grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts, and cousins living under the same roof. Advantage: Grandparents provide loving guidance, childcare support, and companionship.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear definition of both family structures 1.5 M
Valid advantages stated for both 1.5 M
Q7 Respecting Elders • Values
How can children show respect and care for elderly grandparents in their home?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Caring for Grandparents:
1. Spending quality time listening to their childhood stories and experiences.
2. Helping them find their spectacles, walking sticks, or fetching water and medicines.
3. Accompanying them for morning and evening walks and speaking with polite, patient words.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three practical ways to care for grandparents 3 M
Q8 Empathy • Pets as Family
Why do many families adopt pets (dogs, cats, birds)? What responsibilities come with adopting a pet?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Pet Adoption & Responsibilities:
• Why: Pets provide companionship, love, and loyalty, teaching children empathy and gentle care.
• Responsibilities: Providing clean drinking water, fresh balanced pet food, regular walks, clean living space, and timely veterinary vaccinations.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Emotional importance of pets 1 M
Key pet care responsibilities 1 M
Q9 Conflict Resolution • Harmony
When siblings or family members disagree on something (like watching TV or playing a game), how should they resolve the conflict peacefully?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Peaceful Resolution:
1. Take turns talking calmly without shouting or interrupting.
2. Listen to each other's point of view with patience.
3. Agree on a fair compromise, such as taking turns or choosing an activity both enjoy.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Calm communication emphasized 1 M
Fair compromise and mutual respect 1 M
Q10 Family Traditions • Cultural Heritage
What are family traditions, and how do they pass from one generation to the next?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Family Traditions:
Traditions are special customs, festival recipes, songs, or values practiced regularly by a family. They are passed down as children observe and participate with parents and grandparents during annual celebrations and religious ceremonies.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Definition of family traditions 1 M
Generational transmission explained 1 M
Q11 Inclusivity • Special Needs Support
How should family members support a child or relative who has a visual or hearing impairment?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Supportive Actions:
• Keep pathways clear of clutter and loose wires so they can move safely.
• Learn basic sign language or speak clearly facing the person.
• Encourage them with patience and treat them with equal dignity and love.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Physical safety precautions 1 M
Equal dignity and empathy 1 M
Q12 Daily Routine • Time Management
Why is having a structured daily family routine beneficial for growing children?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Benefits of Routine:
1. Instills discipline for waking up, studying, eating, and sleeping on time.
2. Ensures adequate rest (8-9 hours of sleep) for brain and body growth.
3. Reduces stress and chaos in the morning before school.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two valid health/discipline benefits 2 M
Q13 Environmental Responsibility • Green Homes
What are three eco-friendly habits a family can practice together at home to save electricity and water?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Eco-Friendly Family Habits:
1. Switch off lights, fans, and appliances when leaving a room.
2. Fix leaking taps immediately and use a bucket for bathing instead of a shower.
3. Segregate dry and wet kitchen waste into separate dustbins.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three practical eco-friendly home habits 3 M
Q14 Personal Reflection • Expressing Gratitude
Write a short thank-you note to one family member expressing appreciation for something special they did for you.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sample Thank-You Note:
Dear Grandma,
Thank you so much for reading bedtime stories to me and teaching me how to make paper boats during the rainy season. Your warm hugs and delicious carrot halwa always make me feel very happy. I love you!
— Your loving grandchild
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Warm, respectful, and sincere expression of gratitude 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Supporting Grandparents with Digital Technology

Grandfather wants to consult his eye doctor through a telemedicine video call but feels confused by the smartphone screen.

Analytical Task: How can a Class 4 student assist their grandfather respectfully without making him feel helpless?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The student should sit patiently beside grandfather, open the app, adjust the screen brightness and volume, and gently guide grandfather's finger to tap the call button. Explaining each step with kindness builds grandfather's confidence while showing deep filial gratitude.
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for patient step-by-step guidance; 1.5 Marks for showing filial respect and dignity.
