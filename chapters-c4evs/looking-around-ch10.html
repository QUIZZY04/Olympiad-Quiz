Chapter 10: Hu Tu Tu, Hu Tu Tu

The Sport of Kabaddi, Physical Stamina, Rules & The Inspiring Story of Karnam Malleswari | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • The game of Kabaddi: Holding breath while chanting 'Kabaddi-Kabaddi', tagging opponents, crossing the central midline safely.
  • Physical coordination: Sharp reflexes, lung stamina, leg sweeps, and team catching.
  • Olympic Inspiration: Karnam Malleswari, weightlifter from Andhra Pradesh, daughter of a police constable. Started lifting at age 12; won India's first Olympic bronze medal in weightlifting at Sydney 2000 (can lift 130 kg!).
  • Sisters Jwala, Leela, and Heera: Pioneering women who broke taboos to play and coach Kabaddi in Mumbai 50 years ago.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Sports Rules & Women in Sports
What are the essential rules of Kabaddi? Who is Karnam Malleswari, and what historic achievement did she accomplish for Indian sports?
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Essential Rules of Kabaddi:
• A raider crosses the center line into the opposing team's court while chanting 'Kabaddi-Kabaddi' continuously in a single breath without stopping.
• The raider tries to touch one or more defending players and rush back safely across the midline.
• If defenders pin the raider down until they lose their breath, the raider is declared 'Out'. If the raider touches the center line, all touched defenders are 'Out'.

2. Karnam Malleswari's Historic Achievement:
Karnam Malleswari is an internationally celebrated weightlifter from Andhra Pradesh. Daughter of a police constable, she began training at age 12. At the Sydney 2000 Olympic Games, she won a Bronze Medal, becoming the first Indian woman in history to win an Olympic medal. She has won 29 international medals and can lift up to 130 kg.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurately details raider chant, breath control, and tag-out rules 2.0 M
Highlights Karnam Malleswari as first Indian woman Olympic medalist 2.0 M
Q2 Sports Excellence • Karnam Malleswari
Who is Karnam Malleswari? Summarize her athletic achievements in Indian sports history.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Karnam Malleswari:
Karnam Malleswari is an iconic Indian weightlifter from Andhra Pradesh. She started weightlifting at the age of 12 under her coach's guidance.

Major Achievements:
• She became capable of lifting a massive weight of 130 kilograms.
• At the 2000 Sydney Olympic Games, she made history by becoming the first Indian woman ever to win an Olympic medal (Bronze in 69 kg category).
• She has won 29 international medals in world weightlifting championships, inspiring millions of Indian girls to take up competitive sports.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification as pioneer Olympic weightlifter from AP 1.5 M
Sydney 2000 Olympic bronze and 130 kg lifting record 1.5 M
Q3 Inspiring Women • The Kabaddi Sisters
Who were the three sisters Jwala, Leela, and Heera? How did they overcome conservative social norms to promote Kabaddi?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The Kabaddi Sisters (Jwala, Leela, Heera):
Living in Mumbai more than fifty years ago, these three sisters loved playing Kabaddi. In those times, society believed girls should not play aggressive games wearing shorts, fearing nobody would marry them.

Their Courage:
Supported by their open-minded mother and maternal uncles, they practiced determinedly, formed their own Kabaddi club, competed at national tournaments, and proved that women can excel in traditional contact sports with discipline and dignity.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Societal prejudices against women playing Kabaddi 1.5 M
Formation of club and national competitive success 1.5 M

How does 'Hu Tu Tu, Hu Tu Tu' illustrate the importance of community helpers, traditional skills, or regional diversity across India? Give two concrete examples.

Community Helpers & Regional Diversity:

Q7 Conservation Action • Water, Soil & Wildlife Protection
Suggest three practical conservation actions that a Class 4 student can implement at home and school inspired by the teachings of 'Hu Tu Tu, Hu Tu Tu'.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Three Student-Led Conservation Actions:
1. Water Conservation: Turn off taps while brushing teeth and reuse rinse-water from washed vegetables to water garden pots.
2. Waste Segregation: Separate organic wet kitchen peelings into compost bins while sending clean paper, cardboard, and plastics for recycling.
3. Protecting Green Cover: Plant a native sapling on birthdays and set up a bird bath on balconies during warm summer months.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Water saving technique explained 1.0 M
Waste segregation practice detailed 1.0 M
Flora/fauna protection action listed 1.0 M
Q8 Critical Thinking & Case Study • Environmental Decision Making
Analyze a real-world scenario from 'Hu Tu Tu, Hu Tu Tu': If a local natural waterbody or forest area is polluted by garbage, what steps should the local community take to restore it?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Community Restoration Action Plan:
1. Immediate Cleanup Drive: Organize student and resident volunteer drives equipped with gloves to safely collect floating plastics and litter.
2. Preventive Regulations: Install prominent awareness signboards prohibiting dumping, accompanied by secure covered dustbins.
3. Biological Rejuvenation: Plant native bank-stabilizing trees and reeds along water edges to prevent soil erosion and encourage aquatic life.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Immediate cleanup measures proposed 1.5 M
Long-term preventive community measures 1.5 M
Q9 Values & Empathy • Caring for All Living Beings
How can young citizens demonstrate empathy towards stray animals, birds, and elderly citizens in their daily lives, as highlighted in 'Hu Tu Tu, Hu Tu Tu'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Everyday Empathy in Practice:
1. Care for Living Creatures: Never throw stones or tease stray animals; keep a bowl of clean drinking water in shady spots.
2. Respect for Elders: Offer seats in public transport, speak politely, listen patiently to grandparents' stories, and assist them in carrying light groceries.
3. Core Principle: Compassion for all living beings builds a humane, peaceful, and responsible society.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Kindness towards animals and birds 1.5 M
Respectful support towards senior citizens 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Breaking Gender Stereotypes in Sports

Fifty years ago, parents stopped daughters from playing Kabaddi because 'girls would have to wear shorts and nobody would marry them'.

Analytical Task: How have champions like Karnam Malleswari and Mary Kom destroyed these orthodox beliefs?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Women athletes have demonstrated that sports talent and physical strength are not limited by gender. By winning Olympic and World Championship medals for India, they brought immense national pride, proving that when girls receive equal training and family support, they can conquer the highest podiums in world sports.
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for refuting gender orthodoxies; 1.5 Marks for national glory and equal opportunity.
