Class 5 IMO Syllabus & Exam Pattern

The Class 5 Math Olympiad test assesses students across four major sections. Our practice perfectly aligns with this syllabus.

  • Section 1
    Logical Reasoning

    Patterns, Analogy and Classification, Geometrical Shapes, Mirror and Water Images, Direction Sense Test, Coding-Decoding.

  • Section 2
    Mathematical Reasoning

    Large Numerals, Operations on Numbers, Fractions and Decimals, Measurement, Angles, Perimeter, Area and Volume, Data Handling.

  • Section 3
    Everyday Mathematics

    Word problems applying Mathematical Reasoning concepts to real-world scenarios.

  • Section 4
    Achievers Section

    Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions based on the syllabus. These questions carry 3 marks each.
