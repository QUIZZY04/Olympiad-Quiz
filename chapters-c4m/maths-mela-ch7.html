Q1 Tally Chart Interpretation
During a clean-up campaign, children recorded dry leaves baskets using tally marks: Team Red gathered '卌 卌 卌 卌 |', Team Blue gathered '卌 卌 卌 |||', and Team Green gathered '卌 卌 卌 卌 卌'. (a) How many baskets did each team gather? (b) Which team collected the most? (c) What is the total count of baskets?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Basket counts from tally marks:
• Team Red: 4 bundles of 5 + 1 = (4 × 5) + 1 = 21 baskets.
• Team Blue: 3 bundles of 5 + 3 = (3 × 5) + 3 = 18 baskets.
• Team Green: 5 bundles of 5 = (5 × 5) = 25 baskets.

(b) Maximum collection:
Team Green collected the most with 25 baskets.

(c) Total baskets collected:
Total = 21 + 18 + 25 = 64 baskets.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct counts for all three teams 1.5 M
Correct identification of winner and total sum 64 1.5 M
Q2 Weight & Community Cleanliness • Tally Arithmetic
In a village cleanliness drive, four teams collected wet waste: Team A collected 42 kg 500 g, Team B collected 38 kg 250 g, Team C collected 51 kg 750 g, and Team D collected 47 kg. What was the total weight of wet waste collected?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Column Addition of Weight:
  kg          g
  42        500
+ 38        250
+ 51        750
+ 47        000
-------------------
Sum of grams = 500 + 250 + 750 + 0 = 1,500 g = 1 kg 500 g.
Sum of kg = 42 + 38 + 51 + 47 + 1 (carry) = 179 kg.

Total wet waste collected = 179 kg 500 g.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct column alignment of kg and g 1.0 M
Gram sum carry-over (1,500 g = 1 kg 500 g) 1.0 M
Final sum: 179 kg 500 g 1.0 M
Q3 Capacity & Conservation • Water Math
A leaky public tap wastes 150 mL of clean drinking water every minute. How many litres of water are wasted if the tap is left leaking for 2 hours?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
1. Wastage in 1 hour (60 minutes):
150 mL × 60 = 9,000 mL.

2. Wastage in 2 hours:
9,000 mL × 2 = 18,000 mL.

3. Convert to Litres (1 L = 1,000 mL):
18,000 ÷ 1,000 = 18 Litres.

In 2 hours, 18 litres of precious drinking water are wasted.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Calculating minutes in 2 hours (120 mins) 1.0 M
Total mL calculation (18,000 mL) 1.0 M
Conversion to litres (18 L) 1.0 M
Q4 Practical Arithmetic • Garbage Sorting
A school canteen produced 65 kg of garbage in a week. If 3/5 of it was biodegradable food waste and the rest was recyclable plastic and paper, find the weight of recyclable waste.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Fraction & Weight Solution:
Total garbage = 65 kg

1. Weight of Biodegradable Food Waste:
3/5 of 65 kg = (65 ÷ 5) × 3 = 13 × 3 = 39 kg.

2. Weight of Recyclable Waste:
65 kg - 39 kg = 26 kg.
(Alternatively, 1 - 3/5 = 2/5; 2/5 of 65 = 26 kg).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Calculating food waste fraction (39 kg) 1.0 M
Deducting to find recyclable waste (26 kg) 1.0 M

Q7 Measurement & Unit Conversion

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'The Cleanest Village': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 246. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 246$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 246 + 15 = 261$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{261}{3} = \mathbf{87}$.
5. Verification: (87 × 3) - 15 = 261 - 15 = 246. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 246 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 87 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Recycling Waste Funds for Village Library

The village panchayat collected 120 kg of old newspapers and 45 kg of cardboard boxes. The scrap dealer buys newspapers at ₹14 per kg and cardboard at ₹10 per kg.

Analytical Task: How much total money did the village raise for their new reading room?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Working:
• Money from newspapers = 120 kg × ₹14 = ₹1,680.
• Money from cardboard = 45 kg × ₹10 = ₹450.
• Total funds raised = ₹1,680 + ₹450 = ₹2,130.
Rubric: 1 Mark for individual product calculations; 1 Mark for total ₹2,130.
