Chapter 11: Fun with Symmetry

Lines of Symmetry, Mirror Reflections, Folding Paper & Symmetrical Shapes | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • A line of symmetry divides a figure into two identical halves that overlap exactly when folded.
  • Vertical, horizontal, and diagonal lines of symmetry.
  • Recognizing symmetry in nature (butterflies, leaves), architecture (Taj Mahal), and geometrical shapes.
  • Completing symmetrical pictures across a dashed mirror line.

Q1 Lines of Symmetry in Plane Shapes
How many lines of symmetry do each of the following shapes have? (a) An equilateral triangle, (b) A square, (c) A rectangle, (d) A circle, (e) A scalene triangle.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Equilateral Triangle: 3 lines of symmetry (one from each vertex perpendicular to the opposite side).
(b) Square: 4 lines of symmetry (2 through midpoints of opposite sides + 2 along diagonals).
(c) Rectangle: 2 lines of symmetry (only through midpoints of opposite sides; diagonals are NOT lines of symmetry).
(d) Circle: Infinite lines of symmetry (any straight line passing through the centre is a line of symmetry).
(e) Scalene Triangle: 0 lines of symmetry (all sides and angles are unequal).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct counts for triangle, square, rectangle 1.5 M
Correct counts for circle (infinite) and scalene triangle (0) 1.5 M
Q2 Rotational Symmetry • Turns and Angles
Which of the following English capital letters look identical after a 1/2 turn (180° rotation)?
A, H, M, O, S, Z
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Half-Turn (180°) Invariance Analysis:
Rotate each letter upside down by 180°:
• A: Turns upside down (∀) - NO.
• H: Remains exactly H - YES.
• M: Turns into W - NO.
• O: Remains exactly O - YES.
• S: Remains exactly S - YES.
• Z: Remains exactly Z - YES.

The letters that look identical after a 1/2 turn are H, O, S, and Z.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correctly identifying all 4 letters (H, O, S, Z) 2.0 M
Accurately ruling out A and M 1.0 M
Q3 Geometric Lines of Symmetry
How many lines of symmetry do each of the following geometric shapes have?
(a) Equilateral triangle
(b) Square
(c) Circle
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Lines of Symmetry:
• (a) Equilateral Triangle: 3 lines of symmetry (each passing from a vertex perpendicular to the opposite side).
• (b) Square: 4 lines of symmetry (2 bisecting opposite sides horizontally and vertically, and 2 along the diagonals).
• (c) Circle: Infinite (countless) lines of symmetry (any straight line passing through the centre point is a line of symmetry).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Equilateral triangle: 3 1.0 M
Square: 4 1.0 M
Circle: Infinite 1.0 M
Q4 Mirror Halves • Reflectional Symmetry
Draw an imaginary mirror line through the digit '8' and explain why it has both horizontal and vertical symmetry.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Symmetry of Digit 8:
• Vertical Line of Symmetry: A vertical line dividing '8' through the middle produces two identical left and right halves that reflect each other perfectly.
• Horizontal Line of Symmetry: A horizontal line passing through the central intersection produces matching top and bottom loops.
Therefore, digit 8 possesses 2 lines of reflectional symmetry.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of vertical reflection 1.0 M
Explanation of horizontal reflection 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Fun with Symmetry': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 318. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 318$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 318 + 15 = 333$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{333}{3} = \mathbf{111}$.
5. Verification: (111 × 3) - 15 = 333 - 15 = 318. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 318 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 111 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Rangoli Mirror Pattern Creation

Pooja draws half of a diya pattern on folded paper. When she opens the fold, both sides are perfectly identical.

Analytical Task: What is the crease of the paper called? If she cuts a heart shape along the fold, how many lines of symmetry will the heart have?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Answers:
1. The crease is called the Line of Symmetry (or Axis of Reflection).
2. A symmetrical heart shape has exactly 1 vertical line of symmetry running down the center.
Rubric: 1 Mark for Line of Symmetry; 1 Mark for 1 vertical line.
