Chapter 12: How Heavy? How Light?

Pan Balance Weighing, Grams & Kilograms, Postal Tariffs & Weight Estimation | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Comparing weights using a beam balance scale.
  • 1 Kilogram (kg) = 1,000 Grams (g); 1/2 kg = 500 g; 1/4 kg = 250 g.
  • Indian Post Office parcel and letter tariff rates based on weight increments.
  • Estimating weights of heavy objects (elephant, tractor, sack of wheat) vs light objects (pencil, feather).

Q1 Postal Tariff Calculation
Look at the postal rates: A parcel weighing 50 grams or less costs ₹5. For every additional 50 grams or part thereof, it costs ₹3. Radha wants to send a book parcel weighing 225 grams to her friend. How much postal stamp value must she affix on the parcel?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step 1: Break down the weight of 225 grams into 50 g slabs:
• First 50 grams = 50 g (remaining = 225 - 50 = 175 g).
• Next 50 grams = 50 g (remaining = 125 g).
• Next 50 grams = 50 g (remaining = 75 g).
• Next 50 grams = 50 g (remaining = 25 g).
• Final 25 grams counts as an additional 50 g slab.
Total slabs = 1 basic slab + 4 additional slabs.

Step 2: Calculate cost:
• First 50 g = ₹5.
• 4 additional slabs of 50 g = 4 × ₹3 = ₹12.
• Total postal stamp value = ₹5 + ₹12 = ₹17.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct breakdown of 225 g into 5 slabs 1.5 M
Accurate calculation giving ₹17 stamp cost 1.5 M
Q2 Postal Mathematics • Calculating Postage
Look at the postal rates:
• Single letter (up to 20 g): ₹5.00
• For every additional 20 g: ₹2.00
Calculate the postage stamps required to send a heavy official letter weighing 75 grams.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Postal Calculation:
Total weight = 75 grams.

1. First 20 grams: Cost = ₹5.00.
2. Remaining weight: 75 g - 20 g = 55 grams.
• Next 20 g (21–40 g): + ₹2.00
• Next 20 g (41–60 g): + ₹2.00
• Remaining 15 g (part of next 20 g, 61–75 g): + ₹2.00
Total additional 20 g units = 3 units.
Additional cost = 3 × ₹2.00 = ₹6.00.

Total Postage Required:
₹5.00 + ₹6.00 = ₹11.00.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Base charge for first 20 g (₹5.00) 1.0 M
Identifying 3 additional units (₹6.00) 1.0 M
Total postage: ₹11.00 1.0 M

Q4 Weight Conversion • Balancing Scales
How many 250-gram packets of tea make 1 1/2 kilograms?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
1. Convert 1 1/2 kg into grams:
1 kg = 1,000 g
1/2 kg = 500 g
Total weight = 1,000 g + 500 g = 1,500 grams.

2. Number of 250 g packets:
1,500 ÷ 250 = 6 packets.

Exactly 6 packets of tea make 1 1/2 kg.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Conversion to 1,500 g 1.0 M
Division: 1500 ÷ 250 = 6 packets 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'How Heavy? How Light?': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 336. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 336$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 336 + 15 = 351$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{351}{3} = \mathbf{117}$.
5. Verification: (117 × 3) - 15 = 351 - 15 = 336. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 336 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 117 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Balancing Scales with Broken Stone Weights

A vendor had a single 4 kg stone weight. It broke into two pieces of 1 kg and 3 kg.

Analytical Task: How can the vendor weigh out exactly 2 kg of firewood using these two pieces on a two-pan balance?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Procedure:
• Place the 3 kg piece on the left pan.
• Place the 1 kg piece on the right pan.
• Add firewood to the right pan until both pans balance in equilibrium.
• Since Left Pan (3 kg) = Right Pan (1 kg + Firewood), the weight of firewood is 3 kg - 1 kg = 2 kg.
Rubric: 1.5 Marks for correct pan placement; 1.5 Marks for mathematical balance proof.
