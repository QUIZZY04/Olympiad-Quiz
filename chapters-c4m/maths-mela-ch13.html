Chapter 13: The Transport Museum

Ticket Booking, Travel Fares, Currency Operations (Rupees & Paise) & Timetables | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Financial arithmetic involving Indian Currency: Rupees (₹) and Paise (p); 1 Rupee = 100 Paise.
  • Reading and interpreting bus/train fare charts and ticket rates for adults and children.
  • Calculating change returned by cashiers during purchases.
  • Planning trip budgets and comparing transport choices.

Q1 Currency Operations & Change Calculation
A family of 2 adults and 3 children visits the Transport Museum. Ticket rates are ₹85 per adult and ₹40 per child. (a) What is the total ticket cost? (b) The father pays with a ₹500 currency note. How much change does the booking clerk return?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Total Ticket Cost:
• Cost for 2 adults = 2 × ₹85 = ₹170.
• Cost for 3 children = 3 × ₹40 = ₹120.
• Total cost = ₹170 + ₹120 = ₹290.

(b) Change Returned:
• Amount given = ₹500.
• Total bill = ₹290.
• Change = ₹500 - ₹290 = ₹210.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct adult and child ticket total (₹290) 1.5 M
Correct change calculation (₹210) 1.5 M

Q3 Unitary Method • Real-World Math
If 8 bus tickets from the city centre to the museum cost ₹360, how much will 15 tickets cost?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Unitary Method Solution:
1. Cost of 1 ticket:
₹360 ÷ 8 = ₹45 per ticket.

2. Cost of 15 tickets:
15 × ₹45 = ₹675.

The cost of 15 bus tickets is ₹675.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Unit cost calculation: ₹360 ÷ 8 = ₹45 1.0 M
Final cost calculation: 15 × ₹45 = ₹675 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'The Transport Museum': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 354. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 354$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 354 + 15 = 369$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{369}{3} = \mathbf{123}$.
5. Verification: (123 × 3) - 15 = 369 - 15 = 354. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 354 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 123 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Toy Train Ride Booking

The museum toy train operates every 20 minutes from 10:00 am. It accommodates 32 passengers per trip.

Analytical Task: How many trips will the train make between 10:00 am and 12:00 noon, and what is the maximum number of visitors it can carry?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Trip Calculation:
• Duration from 10:00 am to 12:00 noon = 2 hours = 120 minutes.
• Number of trips = 120 min ÷ 20 min = 6 trips (at 10:00, 10:20, 10:40, 11:00, 11:20, 11:40).
• Maximum visitors = 6 trips × 32 passengers = 192 passengers.
Rubric: 1 Mark for 6 trips; 1 Mark for 192 total passengers.
