Chapter 4: Tick-Tick-Tick

Clock Reading, Movement of Minute Hand, 12h vs 24h Formats & Expiry Date Verification | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Understanding clock mechanics: the minute hand moves 1 small mark (6°) in 1 minute, 5 marks (30°) from one number to the next.
  • Calculating the time taken by the minute hand to move from one digit to another.
  • Differentiating morning (am) and afternoon/evening (pm).
  • Reading manufacturing (MFG) and expiry dates on ghee tins, medicines, and butter packets.

Q1 Minute Hand Movement
How many minutes does it take the minute hand to move from: (a) 2 to 7, (b) 12 to 8, (c) 1 to 4, (d) 7 to 10?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Each number on the clock represents 5 minutes.
• (a) From 2 to 7: (7 - 2) = 5 numbers. 5 × 5 = 25 minutes.
• (b) From 12 to 8: 8 numbers forward. 8 × 5 = 40 minutes.
• (c) From 1 to 4: (4 - 1) = 3 numbers. 3 × 5 = 15 minutes.
• (d) From 7 to 10: (10 - 7) = 3 numbers. 3 × 5 = 15 minutes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct answers for (a) and (b) 1.5 M
Correct answers for (c) and (d) 1.5 M
Q2 Product Expiry Date Check
Amrit bought butter on 15 May 2026. It was written on the packet 'Best before 180 days from the date of packing'. The date of packing was 15/01/2026. Should Amrit eat this butter or not? Explain your calculation.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step 1: Calculate days from 15 January 2026 to 15 May 2026:
• January: 31 - 15 = 16 days remaining.
• February 2026 (non-leap year) = 28 days.
• March = 31 days.
• April = 30 days.
• May (up to 15th) = 15 days.
• Total days passed = 16 + 28 + 31 + 30 + 15 = 120 days.

Step 2: Compare with expiry limit:
• The packet is safe for 180 days.
• Since 120 days < 180 days, the butter has NOT expired.
• Conclusion: Yes, Amrit can safely eat the butter.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate monthly day count sum = 120 days 2.0 M
Valid comparison and safe consumption verdict 1.0 M
Q3 Developmental Timeline • Munia's Milestones
Munia was born on 5th March 2025. She cut her first tooth on 15th September 2025 and started walking on 2nd February 2026. (a) How old was she in completed months when she cut her first tooth? (b) How many months after birth did she start walking?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Timeline Milestones:
Date of Birth: 5th March 2025

1. (a) First Tooth (15th September 2025):
From March to September = 6 months (March 5 to Sept 5 = 6 months) + 10 days.
Munia was 6 months and 10 days old.

2. (b) Walking (2nd February 2026):
From March 2025 to January 2026 = 10 months. Adding up to Feb 2 = nearly 11 months old.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
First tooth age: 6 months 10 days 1.5 M
Walking milestone age: approx 11 months 1.5 M
Q4 Calendar Math • Number of Days in a Year
Is the year 2028 a leap year? How many days will the months of February and the entire year 2028 have?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Leap Year Test:
A year is a leap year if its last two digits are divisible by 4.
• 28 ÷ 4 = 7 (completely divisible, remainder 0).
Therefore, 2028 is a leap year.

Days Count:
• February 2028 will have 29 days.
• The entire year 2028 will have 366 days (instead of the normal 365 days).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Divisibility rule proving 2028 is a leap year 1.0 M
February has 29 days and total year has 366 days 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Tick-Tick-Tick': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 192. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 192$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 192 + 15 = 207$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{207}{3} = \mathbf{69}$.
5. Verification: (69 × 3) - 15 = 207 - 15 = 192. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 192 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 69 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Missing a Train Due to 24-Hour Time Format

Ranjit has a train ticket with departure time printed as '05:30'. He reaches the railway station at 5:00 in the evening on the same day.

Analytical Task: Did Ranjit catch his train or miss it? Explain why the railway ticket printed 05:30.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Ranjit missed his train.
Explanation: Indian Railways uses the 24-hour clock. '05:30' represents 5:30 am in the early morning. If the train were at 5:30 in the evening, the ticket would have printed '17:30'. Since Ranjit arrived at 5:00 pm (17:00), his train had already departed 11 hours and 30 minutes earlier.
Rubric: 1 Mark for missed train conclusion; 1 Mark for explaining 05:30 am vs 17:30 pm.
