Chapter 6: Measuring Length

Centimetres, Metres, Kilometres, Conversions & Perimeter of Shapes | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Metric units of length: millimetre (mm), centimetre (cm), metre (m), and kilometre (km).
  • Key conversion relationships: 1 m = 100 cm; 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 cm = 10 mm.
  • Measuring curved paths using a string and ruler.
  • Calculating perimeter (total boundary length) by summing the lengths of all outer sides.

Q1 Metric Unit Conversions
Convert the following: (a) 4 metres 65 centimetres into cm; (b) 7,500 metres into kilometres and metres; (c) 8 kilometres 40 metres into metres.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) 4 m 65 cm into cm:
• Since 1 m = 100 cm, 4 m = 4 × 100 = 400 cm.
• Total = 400 cm + 65 cm = 465 cm.

(b) 7,500 m into km and m:
• Since 1,000 m = 1 km, divide 7,500 by 1,000.
• 7,500 m = 7,000 m + 500 m = 7 km 500 m.

(c) 8 km 40 m into m:
• Since 1 km = 1,000 m, 8 km = 8 × 1,000 = 8,000 m.
• Total = 8,000 m + 40 m = 8,040 m (be careful not to write 8,400 m!).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct conversion for (a) 1.0 M
Correct conversion for (b) 1.0 M
Correct conversion for (c) with zero placeholder 1.0 M
Q2 Perimeter Word Problem
A rectangular playground is 35 metres long and 20 metres wide. An athlete runs around the boundary 4 times every morning. What is the total distance run by the athlete in metres and kilometres?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step 1: Calculate the perimeter of the playground:
• Formula: Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width)
• Perimeter = 2 × (35 m + 20 m) = 2 × 55 m = 110 metres.
(One full round = 110 m).

Step 2: Calculate distance for 4 rounds:
• Total distance = 4 × 110 m = 440 metres.

Step 3: Convert into kilometres:
• 440 m = 440 ÷ 1,000 = 0.44 km.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Perimeter calculation of 110 m 1.5 M
Total 4 rounds distance 440 m and km conversion 1.5 M
Q3 Geometry • Perimeter Calculation
A rectangular school playground is 45 metres long and 30 metres wide. (a) What is its perimeter? (b) If an athlete runs 3 complete rounds around it, what total distance does she run?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculation:
Length (l) = 45 m, Breadth (b) = 30 m

1. Perimeter of the Playground:
Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Breadth)
= 2 × (45 m + 30 m) = 2 × 75 m = 150 metres.

2. Total Distance for 3 Rounds:
Distance = 3 × Perimeter = 3 × 150 m = 450 metres.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Perimeter formula and correct answer (150 m) 1.5 M
Multiplication by 3 rounds (450 m) 1.5 M
Q4 Unit Conversions • Metres & Kilometres
Convert: (a) 4,250 metres into kilometres and metres, (b) 7 kilometres 85 metres into metres.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Conversions (1 km = 1,000 m):
• (a) 4,250 m:
4,250 ÷ 1,000 = 4 km and 250 m.

• (b) 7 km 85 m:
(7 × 1,000 m) + 85 m = 7,000 m + 85 m = 7,085 m.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 4 km 250 m 1.0 M
Part (b): 7,085 m 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Measuring Length': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 228. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 228$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 228 + 15 = 243$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{243}{3} = \mathbf{81}$.
5. Verification: (81 × 3) - 15 = 243 - 15 = 228. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 228 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 81 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Boundary Fencing Budget

The school management needs to put a wire fence around a square herb garden measuring 15 metres on each side. The fencing contractor charges ₹40 per metre of wire.

Analytical Task: Calculate: (a) The total length of wire needed for 1 round of fence, (b) The total cost of fencing.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
(a) Length of wire:
Perimeter of square garden = 4 × side = 4 × 15 m = 60 metres.
(b) Total Cost:
Cost = 60 m × ₹40/m = ₹2,400.
Rubric: 1 Mark for perimeter 60 m; 1 Mark for multiplying rate to get ₹2,400.
