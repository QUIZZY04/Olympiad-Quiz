Chapter 2: Long and Short

Measuring Heights, Distances, Conversions (cm, m, km), Sports Records & Marathon Math | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Comparing lengths visually and measuring accurately using a ruler and measuring tape.
  • Units of length: 1 m = 100 cm; 1 km = 1,000 m.
  • Analyzing world records and Indian records in high jump and long jump.
  • Calculating distances in round tracks and solving marathon running problems.

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Sports Records Comparison
Look at the athletic records: Javier S. holds the Men's World Record for High Jump at 2 m 45 cm. Chandrapal holds the Indian Record for High Jump at 2 m 17 cm. (a) How many more centimetres must Chandrapal jump to equal Javier's world record? (b) How many centimetres short of 2 and a half metres is Javier's jump?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Difference between World and Indian High Jump records:
• Javier S. (World Record) = 2 m 45 cm = 245 cm.
• Chandrapal (Indian Record) = 2 m 17 cm = 217 cm.
• Difference = 245 cm - 217 cm = 28 cm.
Chandrapal must jump 28 cm higher.

(b) Short of 2 and a half metres (2 m 50 cm):
• 2 and a half metres = 2 m 50 cm = 250 cm.
• Javier's jump = 245 cm.
• Difference = 250 cm - 245 cm = 5 cm.
Javier's jump is only 5 cm short of 2.5 metres.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct difference of 28 cm with conversion 1.5 M
Correct calculation showing 5 cm short of 2.5 m 1.5 M
Q2 Marathon & Stadium Track Math
A stadium running track is 400 metres long for one complete lap. People run a marathon race of 40 kilometres. How many complete rounds of the stadium track must a runner make to complete the 40 km marathon?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step 1: Convert marathon distance into metres:
• 1 kilometre = 1,000 metres.
• 40 km = 40 × 1,000 = 40,000 metres.

Step 2: Calculate number of rounds:
• Length of 1 round of stadium = 400 metres.
• Number of rounds = Total distance ÷ Track length
• Number of rounds = 40,000 ÷ 400 = 100 rounds.

A runner must complete exactly 100 rounds of the stadium track.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Unit conversion 40 km = 40,000 m 1.5 M
Division 40,000 ÷ 400 = 100 rounds 1.5 M

Q4 Marathon & Distance • Stadium Track
A doctor tells Devi Prasad to run 2 km every day to stay fit. He runs around a square field with each side 500 metres long. (a) How many rounds of this field must he take to complete 2 km? (b) If he instead runs on a 400-metre circular stadium track, how many rounds are needed?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution (Target = 2 km = 2,000 m):
1. (a) Square Field:
• Side = 500 m
• Perimeter of square = 4 × 500 m = 2,000 m.
• Number of rounds = 2,000 m ÷ 2,000 m = 1 round.

2. (b) 400-metre Stadium Track:
• Number of rounds = 2,000 m ÷ 400 m = 5 rounds.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Square field perimeter (2,000 m) = 1 round 1.5 M
Stadium track calculation: 2,000 ÷ 400 = 5 rounds 1.5 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Long and Short': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 156. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 156$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 156 + 15 = 171$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{171}{3} = \mathbf{57}$.
5. Verification: (57 × 3) - 15 = 171 - 15 = 156. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 156 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 57 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Daily Walking Distance Goal

Doctor advises grandfather to walk 2 kilometres every day to stay fit. Grandfather walks around a square park that measures 125 metres on each side.

Analytical Task: How many complete rounds of the park must grandfather take to achieve his 2 km goal?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Step 1: Perimeter of square park:
Perimeter = 4 × 125 m = 500 metres per round.
Step 2: Rounds for 2 km:
2 km = 2,000 metres.
Rounds required = 2,000 m ÷ 500 m = 4 rounds.
Grandfather must walk 4 complete rounds.
Rubric: 1 Mark for park perimeter 500 m; 1 Mark for 4 rounds.
