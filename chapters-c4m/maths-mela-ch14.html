Q1 Pictograph & Scale Interpretation
In a pictograph, the symbol 📚 represents 6 library books borrowed. Look at the data: Class 4A has 5 symbols; Class 4B has 4 symbols; Class 4C has 6 symbols; Class 4D has 3 and a half symbols. (a) How many books were borrowed by each class? (b) What is the total number of books borrowed?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Scale: 1 symbol 📚 = 6 books.
Half symbol = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 books.

(a) Books borrowed by each section:
• Class 4A: 5 × 6 = 30 books.
• Class 4B: 4 × 6 = 24 books.
• Class 4C: 6 × 6 = 36 books.
• Class 4D: (3 × 6) + 3 = 18 + 3 = 21 books.

(b) Total books borrowed:
Total = 30 + 24 + 36 + 21 = 111 books.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct calculation for all 4 sections with half-symbol logic 2.0 M
Accurate sum of 111 books 1.0 M

In a school library pictograph, one symbol 📖 represents 10 books. The records show:
• Science: 📖 📖 📖 📖

(a) How many Maths books are there? (b) How many more Storybooks are there than Science books?

1. (a) Number of Maths books: 60 books.
2. (b) Difference between Storybooks and Science:
80 - 40 = 40 more Storybooks.

Maths books count = 60 1.0 M
Difference = 40 books 1.0 M
Q3 Data Visualization • Chapati (Pie) Chart
A survey of 40 children about their favourite sport revealed: 20 like Cricket, 10 like Football, and 10 like Badminton. Represent this data as fractions on a circular Chapati Chart.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Circular Chart Representation (Total = 40 children):
• Cricket: 20 out of 40 = 20/40 = 1/2 (Half of the circle).
• Football: 10 out of 40 = 10/40 = 1/4 (One-fourth / quarter of the circle).
• Badminton: 10 out of 40 = 10/40 = 1/4 (One-fourth / quarter of the circle).

On the circular chart, draw a straight diameter dividing the circle into two halves; label one half 'Cricket (1/2)'. Divide the other half into two equal quarters; label one 'Football (1/4)' and the other 'Badminton (1/4)'.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Calculating correct fractional share (1/2, 1/4, 1/4) 1.5 M
Describing accurate geometric sector partition of circle 1.5 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Data Handling': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 372. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 372$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 372 + 15 = 387$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{387}{3} = \mathbf{129}$.
5. Verification: (129 × 3) - 15 = 387 - 15 = 372. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 372 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 129 with check 1.5 M
