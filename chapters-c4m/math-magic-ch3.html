Q1 Excursion Bus Seating Calculation
Total students going on the Bhopal trip from Class 1 to Class 5 are: Class 1: 33, Class 2: 32, Class 3: 42, Class 4: 50, Class 5: 53. (a) How many total children are going on the trip? (b) If the school hires 4 buses with 50 seats each, how many children will get seats? (c) How many children will be left without seats?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Total Children Going on the Trip:
   33 (Class 1)
+ 32 (Class 2)
+ 42 (Class 3)
+ 50 (Class 4)
+ 53 (Class 5)
-------
210 total children.

(b) Seating Capacity of 4 Buses:
• Each bus has 50 seats.
• 4 buses have 4 × 50 = 200 seats.
So, 200 children will get seats.

(c) Children Left Without Seats:
• 210 - 200 = 10 children will be left without seats.
(They will share seats with classmates).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct sum of 210 children 1.0 M
Correct calculation of 200 seats in 4 buses 1.0 M
Difference of 10 children without seats 1.0 M
Q2 Boat Ride Rates & Time Budgeting
At the Bhopal lake, ticket prices are: Double-Decker Boat: ₹30 (45 mins), Motor Boat: ₹25 (20 mins), Paddle Boat: ₹15 (30 mins), Oar Boat: ₹15 (45 mins). (a) Indra and Bhanu first took the motor boat and then the oar boat. How much total did they pay for both? (b) Which boat makes two trips in 1 hour?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Cost for Indra and Bhanu:
• Motor boat: Ticket = ₹25 per person. For 2 persons = 2 × ₹25 = ₹50.
• Oar boat: Ticket = ₹15 per person. For 2 persons = 2 × ₹15 = ₹30.
• Total amount paid = ₹50 + ₹30 = ₹80.

(b) Boat that makes 2 trips in 1 hour:
• 1 hour = 60 minutes.
• Time for 1 trip must be 60 ÷ 2 = 30 minutes.
• Looking at the table, the Paddle Boat has a trip duration of 30 minutes. Hence, the paddle boat makes two trips in 1 hour.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct total fare of ₹80 for 2 children 1.5 M
Correct identification of Paddle Boat with 30 min duration 1.5 M
Q3 Travel Budget • Boating Rates
At the Bhopal lake, a motorboat charges ₹25 per person for a 20-minute ride, and a paddle boat charges ₹15 per person for a 45-minute ride. A group of 4 children have ₹100 and 40 minutes time. Which boat ride can they take?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Comparison of Boating Options for 4 Children:
Available Budget = ₹100, Available Time = 40 minutes.

1. Motorboat Option:
• Total Cost = 4 × ₹25 = ₹100 (Within budget).
• Duration = 20 minutes (Well within 40 minutes).

2. Paddle Boat Option:
• Total Cost = 4 × ₹15 = ₹60 (Within budget).
• Duration = 45 minutes (Exceeds available 40 minutes).

Conclusion: They can only take the motorboat ride because the paddle boat exceeds their 40-minute time limit.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Budget calculation for both boats (₹100 vs ₹60) 1.5 M
Time limit comparison selecting motorboat (20 mins vs 45 mins) 1.5 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'A Trip to Bhopal': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 174. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 174$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 174 + 15 = 189$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{189}{3} = \mathbf{63}$.
5. Verification: (63 × 3) - 15 = 189 - 15 = 174. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 174 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 63 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Petrol Pump Refueling Time

Two buses stop at a petrol pump to refill diesel. Each bus needs 50 litres of diesel. 1 litre of diesel costs ₹45. The pump fills diesel at a rate of 10 litres per minute.

Analytical Task: What is the total bill for both buses, and how long does it take to fill both buses?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Step 1: Total diesel required:
2 buses × 50 litres = 100 litres.
Step 2: Total cost:
100 litres × ₹45/litre = ₹4,500.
Step 3: Total filling time:
100 litres ÷ 10 litres/min = 10 minutes.
Rubric: 1 Mark for cost ₹4,500; 1 Mark for time 10 minutes.
