Chapter 12: Ticking Clocks and Turning Calendar

12-Hour vs. 24-Hour Clocks, AM/PM, Elapsed Time & Calendar Logic | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Reading time to the exact minute on analog and digital clocks.
  • Converting between 12-hour (am/pm) and 24-hour railway time formats.
  • Calculating elapsed time (start time, end time, duration).
  • Understanding calendar months, leap years, manufacturing and expiry dates on packaged products.

Q1 Time Conversion & Calculation
Convert to 24-hour clock time: (a) 6:15 am, (b) 2:45 pm, (c) 11:20 pm, (d) 12:00 midnight. (e) If a train departs at 08:45 and arrives at 14:15, what is the journey duration?
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Part 1: Conversions to 24-Hour Time:
• (a) 6:15 am = 06:15 hours (am times before noon remain unchanged).
• (b) 2:45 pm = 2:45 + 12:00 = 14:45 hours.
• (c) 11:20 pm = 11:20 + 12:00 = 23:20 hours.
• (d) 12:00 midnight = 00:00 hours (or 24:00 hours).

Part 2: Train Journey Duration:
• Departure: 08:45 hours.
• From 08:45 to 09:00 = 15 minutes.
• From 09:00 to 14:00 = 5 hours.
• From 14:00 to 14:15 = 15 minutes.
• Total duration = 5 hours + 15 min + 15 min = 5 hours 30 minutes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct conversions for all 4 time formats 2.0 M
Accurate step-by-step elapsed time calculation (5h 30m) 2.0 M
Q2 Time Arithmetic • 24-Hour Railway Clock
Convert the following times between 12-hour clock (am/pm) and 24-hour railway clock:
(a) 07:45 am
(b) 03:20 pm
(c) 21:50 hours
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Time Conversions:
• (a) 07:45 am: Since it is am before noon, the time remains 07:45 hours.
• (b) 03:20 pm: Since it is pm after noon, add 12 hours: 3:20 + 12:00 = 15:20 hours.
• (c) 21:50 hours: Since it is greater than 12:00, subtract 12 hours: 21:50 - 12:00 = 09:50 pm.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) 07:45 hours 1.0 M
(b) 15:20 hours 1.0 M
(c) 09:50 pm 1.0 M
Q3 Elapsed Time • Journey Duration
A superfast train departs from New Delhi at 06:15 am and arrives in Amritsar on the same day at 01:45 pm. What is the total duration of the train journey?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Elapsed Time Calculation:
• Departure Time: 06:15 am
• Arrival Time: 01:45 pm

1. From 06:15 am to 12:15 pm (noon) = 6 hours.
2. From 12:15 pm to 01:15 pm = 1 hour.
3. From 01:15 pm to 01:45 pm = 30 minutes.

Total Journey Time = 6 hours + 1 hour + 30 minutes = 7 hours 30 minutes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Decomposing time to noon milestone 1.5 M
Accurate sum: 7 hours 30 minutes 1.5 M
Q4 Calendar Literacy • Expiry Dates
A packet of butter shows 'Manufacture Date: 15/04/2026' and 'Best before 180 days from packaging'. Can it be safely consumed on 15th October 2026? Show calculations.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Days Count Calculation:
• Days remaining in April (after 15th): 30 - 15 = 15 days.
• May: 31 days.
• June: 30 days.
• July: 31 days.
• August: 31 days.
• September: 30 days.
• Total days elapsed by Sept 30 = 15 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 31 + 30 = 168 days.

Remaining valid days for October = 180 - 168 = 12 days.
Hence, the product expires on 12th October 2026.
Therefore, on 15th October 2026 (183 days), the butter is expired and unsafe to consume.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate month-by-month day counting (168 days up to Sept 30) 1.5 M
Calculating expiry on Oct 12 and concluding unsafe on Oct 15 1.5 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Ticking Clocks and Turning Calendar': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 336. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 336$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 336 + 15 = 351$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{351}{3} = \mathbf{117}$.
5. Verification: (117 × 3) - 15 = 351 - 15 = 336. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 336 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 117 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Medicine Expiry Date Verification

A medicine bottle has manufacturing date 'MFG: 03/24' and states 'Best before 24 months from manufacture'.

Analytical Task: Can this medicine be safely consumed in February 2026? What is the last safe month?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Analysis:
• Manufacturing date = March 2024 (03/24).
• Adding 24 months (2 full years): March 2024 + 2 years = March 2026.
Conclusion:
Yes, it can be safely consumed in February 2026 because February 2026 falls before the expiry date of March 2026.
Rubric: 1 Mark for calculating expiry month (March 2026); 1 Mark for valid consumption conclusion.
