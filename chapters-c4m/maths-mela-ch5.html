Chapter 5: Sharing and Measuring

Equal Sharing, Division Concepts, Quotients, Remainders & Real-Life Grouping | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Division as equal sharing and repeated subtraction.
  • Terms of division: Dividend ÷ Divisor = Quotient with Remainder.
  • The division check rule: Dividend = (Divisor × Quotient) + Remainder, where Remainder < Divisor.
  • Solving real-world word problems involving distribution of seeds, sweets, and books.

Q1 Division with Remainder & Verification
A farmer packs 148 oranges equally into boxes that hold 6 oranges each. (a) How many full boxes can he pack? (b) How many oranges are left over? (c) Verify your answer using the division verification rule.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step 1: Perform the division:
We need to calculate 148 ÷ 6.
• Divide 14 by 6: 6 × 2 = 12. Remainder = 14 - 12 = 2.
• Bring down 8 to make 28.
• Divide 28 by 6: 6 × 4 = 24. Remainder = 28 - 24 = 4.
• Quotient = 24, Remainder = 4.

(a) The farmer can pack 24 full boxes.
(b) 4 oranges are left over.

(c) Verification:
• Formula: Dividend = (Divisor × Quotient) + Remainder
• RHS = (6 × 24) + 4 = 144 + 4 = 148.
• LHS = Dividend = 148. Verified successfully!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct division steps showing Quotient 24 & Remainder 4 1.5 M
Accurate verification formula and arithmetic proof 1.5 M
Q2 Real-Life Grouping Word Problem
In a school library, there are 215 storybooks. The librarian wants to place an equal number of books on 7 empty shelves. How many books will be placed on each shelf, and how many additional books are needed to fill the last shelf completely?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step 1: Divide books equally:
Calculate 215 ÷ 7.
• 7 × 30 = 210.
• 215 - 210 = 5 remainder.
• So, each shelf gets 30 books.
• 5 books remain on the last shelf.

Step 2: Additional books needed for full shelf:
• Each shelf needs 31 books if all shelves are equally full.
• Currently, 5 books are left.
• Additional books needed = 7 - 5 = 2 books.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct quotient 30 books per shelf 1.5 M
Correct calculation of 2 additional books needed 1.5 M
Q3 Fraction of a Collection • Word Problem
A fruit vendor has a basket of 36 mangoes. He sells 1/4 of them in the morning and 1/2 of them in the afternoon. (a) How many mangoes did he sell in total? (b) How many mangoes are left?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
Total mangoes = 36

1. Morning Sales:
1/4 of 36 = 36 ÷ 4 = 9 mangoes.

2. Afternoon Sales:
1/2 of 36 = 36 ÷ 2 = 18 mangoes.

3. Total Sold:
9 + 18 = 27 mangoes.

4. Mangoes Left:
36 - 27 = 9 mangoes (which is 1/4 of the basket).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Morning calculation (9) and afternoon (18) 1.5 M
Total sold (27) and remaining mangoes (9) 1.5 M
Q4 Equivalent Fractions • Shading Models
Show that 2/4 and 4/8 are equivalent to 1/2 using a rectangular strip model.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Demonstration of Equivalent Fractions:
Take three identical strips of paper of length 12 cm:
1. Strip 1 (1/2): Divide into 2 equal parts of 6 cm each. Shade 1 part. Shaded length = 6 cm.
2. Strip 2 (2/4): Divide into 4 equal parts of 3 cm each. Shade 2 parts. Shaded length = 2 × 3 cm = 6 cm.
3. Strip 3 (4/8): Divide into 8 equal parts of 1.5 cm each. Shade 4 parts. Shaded length = 4 × 1.5 cm = 6 cm.

Since all three shaded portions cover the exact same physical length of 6 cm, 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8. They represent the exact same fraction of the whole.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Equal partition measurement of strips 1.0 M
Equal shaded length proving equivalence 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Sharing and Measuring': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 210. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 210$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 210 + 15 = 225$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{225}{3} = \mathbf{75}$.
5. Verification: (75 × 3) - 15 = 225 - 15 = 210. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 210 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 75 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Van Seating Arrangement

A school organizes an excursion for 58 students. Each school cab can carry a maximum of 8 students safely.

Analytical Task: How many cabs must be booked so that EVERY student gets a seat?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Answer: 8 cabs must be booked.
Explanation: 58 ÷ 8 = 7 with a remainder of 2 students.
7 cabs can only seat 7 × 8 = 56 students. The remaining 2 students cannot be left behind, so 1 more cab must be hired.
Total cabs required = 7 + 1 = 8 cabs.
Rubric: 1 Mark for division quotient 7 with remainder 2; 1 Mark for practical round-up to 8 cabs.
