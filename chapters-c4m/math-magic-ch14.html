Q1 Chapati Chart Interpretation
In a class of 36 students, a Chapati Chart shows their favorite seasons: 1/2 of the students like Winter, 1/4 like Rainy season, and 1/4 like Summer. (a) How many students like Winter? (b) How many students like Summer? (c) How many more students like Winter than Summer?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Total students in class = 36.

• (a) Students who like Winter:
1/2 of 36 = 36 ÷ 2 = 18 students.

• (b) Students who like Summer:
1/4 of 36 = 36 ÷ 4 = 9 students.

• (c) Difference:
18 - 9 = 9 more students like Winter than Summer.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Winter = 18 students 1.0 M
Summer = 9 students 1.0 M
Difference = 9 students 1.0 M
Q2 Smart Charts • Attendance Analysis
Look at the primary school attendance record for a rainy day:
• Class 1: Total 30, Absent 4
• Class 2: Total 28, Absent 3
• Class 3: Total 32, Absent 5
• Class 4: Total 35, Absent 2
• Class 5: Total 25, Absent 1
(a) What was the total number of students enrolled in the school? (b) How many total students were present on that day?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Data Analysis:
1. (a) Total Students Enrolled:
30 + 28 + 32 + 35 + 25 = 150 students.

2. Total Students Absent:
4 + 3 + 5 + 2 + 1 = 15 students.

3. (b) Total Students Present:
150 (Total) - 15 (Absent) = 135 students.

In total, 135 students were present on that rainy day.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total enrolled = 150 1.0 M
Total absent = 15 1.0 M
Total present = 135 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Smart Charts': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 372. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 372$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 372 + 15 = 387$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{387}{3} = \mathbf{129}$.
5. Verification: (129 × 3) - 15 = 387 - 15 = 372. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 372 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 129 with check 1.5 M

Evaluation & Model Answer:
Counts:
• Cycle-rickshaws: 15
• Bicycles: 25
• Buses: 7
• Cars: 12
• Most seen vehicle: Bicycles (25).
• Total vehicles: 15 + 25 + 7 + 12 = 59 vehicles.
