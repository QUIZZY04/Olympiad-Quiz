Q1 Multiplication Strategy & Break-up
Show how to construct the multiplication table of 8 using the tables of 5 and 3. Write out the values for 8 × 1 up to 8 × 6.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
By the distributive property of multiplication:
8 × n = (5 + 3) × n = (5 × n) + (3 × n).

• 8 × 1 = (5 × 1) + (3 × 1) = 5 + 3 = 8
• 8 × 2 = (5 × 2) + (3 × 2) = 10 + 6 = 16
• 8 × 3 = (5 × 3) + (3 × 3) = 15 + 9 = 24
• 8 × 4 = (5 × 4) + (3 × 4) = 20 + 12 = 32
• 8 × 5 = (5 × 5) + (3 × 5) = 25 + 15 = 40
• 8 × 6 = (5 × 6) + (3 × 6) = 30 + 18 = 48
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explains distributive break-up (5 + 3) 1.0 M
Correct step-by-step table construction 2.0 M
Q2 Two-Digit Multiplication Word Problem
A cinema hall has 28 rows of seats, and each row has 15 seats. During a premiere show, all seats are occupied except 12 seats. How many people are watching the movie?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step 1: Calculate total seating capacity:
Total seats = 28 rows × 15 seats/row.
• Using break-up: 28 × 15 = 28 × (10 + 5) = (28 × 10) + (28 × 5)
• 28 × 10 = 280
• 28 × 5 = 140
• Total seats = 280 + 140 = 420 seats.

Step 2: Subtract empty seats:
• Empty seats = 12
• People watching = 420 - 12 = 408 people.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate multiplication 28 × 15 = 420 2.0 M
Correct subtraction giving 408 people 1.0 M

Q4 Mental Math • Multiples of 10 and 100
Calculate mentally using place value patterns:
(a) 70 × 60
(b) 125 × 40
(c) 48 × 25
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mental Calculation Strategies:
• (a) 70 × 60:
Multiply the non-zero digits: 7 × 6 = 42, then attach two zeroes: 4,200.

• (b) 125 × 40:
Think of (125 × 4) × 10 = 500 × 10 = 5,000.

• (c) 48 × 25:
Since 25 = 100 ÷ 4, we divide 48 by 4 and multiply by 100: (48 ÷ 4) × 100 = 12 × 100 = 1,200.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) 4,200 1.0 M
(b) 5,000 1.0 M
(c) 1,200 1.0 M

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Equal Groups': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 282. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 282$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 282 + 15 = 297$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{297}{3} = \mathbf{99}$.
5. Verification: (99 × 3) - 15 = 297 - 15 = 282. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 282 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 99 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Tree Plantation Drive Rows

The Forest Department plants 36 saplings in each row across 25 parallel strips. The cost of protecting each sapling with a bamboo guard is ₹50.

Analytical Task: How many total saplings are planted, and what is the total cost of bamboo guards for all saplings?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Step 1: Total saplings:
36 × 25 = 36 × (100 ÷ 4) = 3,600 ÷ 4 = 900 saplings.
Step 2: Total cost:
900 × ₹50 = ₹45,000.
Rubric: 1 Mark for 900 saplings; 1 Mark for ₹45,000.
