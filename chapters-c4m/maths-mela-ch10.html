Q1 Multi-Digit Addition & Subtraction
In Corbett National Park, the wildlife census recorded 1,245 elephants, 680 leopards, and 315 tigers. (a) What is the total population of these three animals? (b) How many more elephants are there than tigers and leopards combined?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Total Population:
   1,245
+   680
+   315
-------
2,240 total animals.

(b) Comparison with combined tigers and leopards:
• Tigers + Leopards = 315 + 680 = 995 animals.
• Difference from elephants = 1,245 - 995 = 250.
• There are 250 more elephants than tigers and leopards combined.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct addition of 2,240 with regrouping 1.5 M
Correct subtraction giving 250 difference 1.5 M
Q2 Long Division • Quotient and Remainder
Divide 487 by 6. Show the dividend, divisor, quotient, and remainder. Verify your answer using the division verification formula.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Long Division:
487 ÷ 6
• 48 ÷ 6 = 8 (6 × 8 = 48), remainder 0.
• Bring down 7.
• 7 ÷ 6 = 1 (6 × 1 = 6), remainder 1.

Components:
• Dividend: 487
• Divisor: 6
• Quotient: 81
• Remainder: 1

Verification Formula:
Dividend = (Divisor × Quotient) + Remainder
= (6 × 81) + 1 = 486 + 1 = 487 (Verified).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct division step yielding Q = 81, R = 1 1.5 M
Identification of components and successful verification 1.5 M
Q3 Real-Life Division • Safari Jeeps
A national park has 125 tourists waiting for a tiger safari. Each safari jeep can seat a maximum of 6 tourists. (a) How many jeeps will be completely full? (b) How many total jeeps are needed so that no tourist is left behind?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Word Problem:
Total tourists = 125, Capacity per jeep = 6

1. Division:
125 ÷ 6 = 20 with a remainder of 5.

2. Answers:
• (a) Completely Full Jeeps: Exactly 20 jeeps will be completely full (20 × 6 = 120 tourists).
• (b) Total Jeeps Needed: The remaining 5 tourists also need to go on the safari. Therefore, 1 additional jeep is required.
Total jeeps needed = 20 + 1 = 21 jeeps.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Division 125 ÷ 6 = 20 R 5 1.5 M
Interpreting remainder: 20 full jeeps and 21 total jeeps needed 1.5 M

(a) Convert 6 meters into centimeters.
(b) Express 645 cm in meters and centimeters.
(c) Which is longer: 7 m or 645 cm? By how much?

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'Elephants, Tigers, and Leopards': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 300. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 300$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 300 + 15 = 315$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{315}{3} = \mathbf{105}$.
5. Verification: (105 × 3) - 15 = 315 - 15 = 300. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 300 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 105 with check 1.5 M

Current Population:
