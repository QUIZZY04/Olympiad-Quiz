Chapter 5: The Way The World Looks

Gappu's Balloon Journey, Aerial Maps, Matching Top Views & Perspective Drawing | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Perspective changes with distance: objects look big and wide at the near end, but small and narrow at the distant end.
  • Gappu's view from a gas balloon: seeing the top of the bed, fan blades, chairs, and park benches.
  • Matching top view (bird's-eye view) with ground view for bowls, shoes, cars, and railway lines.
  • Navigating classroom floor maps and finding doors, windows, and blackboards.

Q1 Perspective Illusion Explanation
Why do the two sides of a railway track or road look wide and far apart near your feet, but appear to meet at a single point in the far distance?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Scientific & Geometric Reason:
This phenomenon is called Perspective Convergence.
• Objects or distances that are close to our eyes subtend a larger visual angle, so they appear wider and larger.
• As objects move farther away, their visual angle shrinks, so they appear smaller and narrower.
• Although the two rails of a train track are strictly parallel and always equal distance apart on the ground, our eye's perspective causes them to appear to converge toward a 'vanishing point' on the horizon.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mentions perspective / visual angle effect 1.5 M
Explains parallel lines appearing to converge at horizon 1.5 M
Q2 Perspective Views • Everyday Objects
Draw or describe: (a) The top view of a teacup, (b) The side view of a pressure cooker, (c) The top view of a railway track.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Perspective Projections:
• (a) Top View of a Teacup: Shows two concentric circles (the inner circle representing the cup rim and the outer circle representing the saucer) with a small oval loop on one side representing the handle.
• (b) Side View of a Pressure Cooker: Shows the cylindrical metallic body, the side handle, the lid clamp, and the safety weight (whistle) sitting on the top vent pipe.
• (c) Top View of a Railway Track: Shows two parallel vertical lines with horizontal wooden or concrete sleepers spaced evenly across them like a ladder.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate geometric description of teacup top view 1.0 M
Accurate side view of cooker 1.0 M
Accurate top view of railway track 1.0 M
Q3 Grid Map Navigation • Directions
On a neighbourhood map, Neha starts at the Park facing East. She walks 50 metres straight, turns 90° right, walks 30 metres, and enters the Library. (a) In which direction did she walk after turning? (b) In which direction is the Library from the Park?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Spatial Navigation:
1. Direction after turning:
Starting facing East, turning 90° to the right means she is now facing South. She walked 30 metres South.

2. Position of Library relative to Park:
The Library is 50 m East and 30 m South of the Park. Therefore, the Library is located to the South-East of the Park.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Right turn from East = South 1.5 M
Combined position = South-East 1.5 M
Q4 Isometric 3D Blocks • Visual Reasoning
A boy looks at a dice. The sum of opposite faces of a standard dice is always 7. If the top face shows 3 and the front face shows 2, what numbers are on: (a) the bottom face, (b) the back face?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Opposite Face Rule (Sum = 7):
• (a) Bottom Face: Opposite to the top face (3).
Bottom = 7 - 3 = 4.

• (b) Back Face: Opposite to the front face (2).
Back = 7 - 2 = 5.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bottom face = 4 1.0 M
Back face = 5 1.0 M

(a) Convert 6 meters into centimeters.

(c) Which is longer: 7 m or 645 cm? By how much?

Q9 HOTS & Mathematical Reasoning Puzzle
Higher-Order Thinking Puzzle from 'The Way The World Looks': I am thinking of a 2-digit number. When I multiply it by 3 and then subtract 15, the result is 210. What is my mystery number? Show full algebraic proof.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mathematical Reasoning & Working:
1. Let the mystery number be $N$.
2. Equation: $(3 \times N) - 15 = 210$.
3. Add 15 to both sides: $3 \times N = 210 + 15 = 225$.
4. Divide by 3: $N = \frac{225}{3} = \mathbf{75}$.
5. Verification: (75 × 3) - 15 = 225 - 15 = 210. Verified!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Forming equation: (3 × N) - 15 = 210 1.5 M
Solving correctly: N = 75 with check 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Classroom Blackboard Location Map

In a classroom top-view plan, the teacher's table is at (3, 5), the entrance door is at (1, 1), and student benches are arranged in 4 columns from X = 2 to 5.

Analytical Task: If a student enters through the door and walks straight North along the left aisle, which direction should she turn to face the teacher's table?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
She should walk North up to Y = 5, and then turn Right (East) to face the teacher's table at (3, 5).
Rubric: 1 Mark for North direction; 1 Mark for Right / East turn.
