The Generation Gap: Emotional Alienation Under One Roof

NCERT Exercises: Think It Out & Thematic Analysis
Question 1
Think It Out
Does the poem talk of an exclusively personal experience or is it fairly universal?
[3]
✅ CBSE Step-by-Step English Core Solution

The poem does not narrate an exclusively personal experience; rather, it articulates a fairly universal human predicament.

Reasons for Universality:

  • The Generation Gap: The emotional estrangement, inability to communicate, and conflict of values between aging parents and grown-up children is a widespread phenomenon across families and cultures worldwide.
  • Parental Incomprehension: Parents frequently expect their offspring to mirror their own beliefs, design, and choices. When children grow into autonomous individuals with distinct worldviews, parents feel bewildered and isolated.
  • Universal Yearning for Reconciliation: The father's desperate struggle to reach out, forgive, and bridge the gulf with extended "empty hands" mirrors the silent longing of countless parents across human society.

Question 2
Think It Out
How is the father’s helplessness brought out in the poem?
[3]
✅ CBSE Step-by-Step English Core Solution

Elizabeth Jennings poignantly portrays the father’s profound helplessness and emotional grief:

  • Strangers Under One Roof: Despite having lived in the same house for years, the father confesses with profound agony: “I do not understand this child... I know nothing of him.” They speak like strangers with no sign of understanding in the air.
  • Loss of Influence: He acknowledges that while the child is physically “built to my design,” what his son loves in his heart, the father cannot share or comprehend.
  • Inability to Prevent Estrangement: He longs to welcome his son back like the biblical "prodigal son" and forgive him unconditionally rather than see him build a separate, alien world. Yet, his inability to articulate this love results in an icy, suffocating silence.
  • The Empty Hands: In the final heartbreaking image, both father and son stand with “empty hands,” longing for something to forgive, yet paralyzed by pride and misunderstanding.

Question 3
Think It Out
Identify the phrases and lines that indicate the distance between father and son.
[3]
✅ CBSE Step-by-Step English Core Solution

The emotional and physical chasm between father and son is articulated through key phrases:

  • “I do not understand this child” — Highlights the complete failure of parental comprehension.
  • “I know / Nothing of him” — Highlights total emotional detachment.
  • “We speak like strangers” — Highlights the loss of familial intimacy.
  • “There’s no sign of understanding in the air” — Conveys an atmosphere devoid of empathy.
  • “Yet what he loves I cannot share” — Marks the divergence in tastes, values, and lifestyle.
  • “Silence surrounds us” — Symbolizes the oppressive, suffocating absence of communication.
  • “Each puts out an empty hand, / Longing for something to forgive” — Depicts unfulfilled emotional yearning blocked by pride.

Question 4
Think It Out
Does the poem have a consistent rhyme scheme? What is the structural significance?
[3]
✅ CBSE Step-by-Step English Core Solution

Rhyme Scheme Analysis:

The poem does not follow a completely consistent or uniform rhyme scheme across all stanzas:

  • The early stanzas loosely follow an ab ba ba or alternating end-rhyme pattern (e.g., child/build, year/share, air/there).
  • As the emotional tension mounts in later stanzas, the rhyme structure becomes irregular, shifting to abc aba and abbcb schemes with near rhymes and half-rhymes.

Structural Significance:

This irregular, slightly fractured rhyme scheme is intentional. It artistically mirrors the fractured, disrupted relationship between the father and the son. The lack of strict metric harmony echoes the discord, lack of rhythm, and broken communication in their home.
