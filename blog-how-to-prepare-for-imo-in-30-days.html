How to Prepare for IMO in 30 Days: A Step-by-Step Guide

📅 March 05, 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ Olympiad Portal Team

The International Mathematics Olympiad (IMO) conducted by SOF is one of the most prestigious competitive exams for school students. Scoring a top rank requires more than just knowing your school syllabus—it requires speed, accuracy, and advanced logical reasoning.

If you only have 30 days left, don't panic! Here is a focused, high-yield 4-week strategy designed to maximize your percentile.

The key to short-term preparation is eliminating distractions. You must shift from reading textbooks passively to actively solving problems and identifying your specific weak zones.

Week 1: Concept Consolidation & Chapterwise Assessment

Your first week should not be spent taking full mock tests. Instead, focus on understanding exactly where you stand.

  • Day 1-2: Download the exact syllabus for your class. Cross-check it against your school textbook.
  • Day 3-5: Use the Chapterwise Free Tests on our portal. Take a quick quiz on every major topic (e.g., Algebra, Geometry, Fractions).
  • Day 6-7: Identify the 3 weakest chapters based on your analytics. Spend the weekend reviewing formulas and watching tutorial videos for those specific chapters.

Maintain a formula cheat sheet during this week. Write down important identities, mensuration formulas, and conversion units in a single notebook for rapid revision later.

Pro Tip: Logical Reasoning makes up roughly 20% of the IMO paper. Do not ignore it! Allocate 30 minutes daily just for reasoning puzzles.

Week 2: Focus on the Achievers Section (HOTS)

The Achievers Section contains High-Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions. These carry 2 to 3 marks each and are the ultimate rank deciders.

Instead of direct formula application, these questions require multi-step logic. During this week, spend time solving complex word problems. Learn to break down a long paragraph into mathematical equations.

When you solve HOTS questions, don't just look at the final answer. Analyze the step-by-step solution. Understanding the methodology is much more important than just getting the right answer.

Week 3: Time Management & Full Mock Tests

Now it's time to build your stamina. The IMO is a strict 60-minute exam.

  • Attempt at least 3 full-length IMO Mock Tests this week.
  • Simulate exam conditions: No breaks, no calculators, and a strict 60-minute timer.
  • Review your mistakes meticulously. Did you run out of time? Or did you make silly calculation errors?

This is where you practice the "Skip and Return" strategy. If a question takes more than 90 seconds to solve, skip it. Secure the easy marks first to build your confidence, then return to the tricky questions at the end.

Week 4: Real-time Live Arenas & Confidence Building

In the final week, do not learn any new concepts. Focus strictly on revision and performing under pressure.

Join a Live Quiz Arena over the weekend. Competing in real-time on a national leaderboard mimics the psychological pressure of the actual exam hall, effectively killing exam anxiety.

Spend your last 2 days purely reviewing your mistake journal and formula cheat sheet. Ensure you get 8 hours of sleep before the big day.

Conclusion

Consistency is key. 30 days of focused, data-driven practice using free mock tests and detailed analytics will guarantee a massive leap in your final IMO score. Best of luck!

5. Managing Exam Stress and Diet

An often-ignored aspect of the 30-day preparation sprint is the physical and mental well-being of the student. Mathematical problem solving requires immense cognitive energy, and mental fog is a math student's worst enemy. As the exam date approaches, anxiety levels naturally spike, which can severely degrade performance and lead to silly arithmetic errors. To combat this, ensure you are staying optimally hydrated and maintaining a light, brain-boosting diet rich in omega-3s and complex carbohydrates. Avoid heavy, sugar-laden meals before your study sessions or mock tests, as they cause energy crashes. Furthermore, incorporate short, deep-breathing exercises or a brisk 15-minute walk into your daily routine to lower cortisol levels and keep your mind sharp. A calm, well-rested brain processes numbers significantly faster and is much more adept at recognizing the traps hidden in the Achievers Section. Prioritize getting a full 8 hours of sleep, especially during the final week; sleep is when your brain consolidates all the formulas and patterns you've learned into long-term memory.
