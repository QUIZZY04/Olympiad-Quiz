The JEE Advanced Exam Pattern Simulation

Unlike JEE Main, JEE Advanced is notorious for changing its pattern every year. Our platform is equipped to handle complex dynamic testing formats.

  • Multi-Correct
    One or More Than One Correct Options

    Questions where multiple choices are right. Our engine supports partial marking (+1 for each correct option chosen, provided no incorrect option is selected) and negative marking (-2 for incorrect answers).

  • Integer/Numerical
    Numerical Value Answers

    Non-MCQ questions requiring precise numerical inputs, often calculated to two decimal places. Perfect for testing accuracy without the crutch of given options.

  • Matrix Match & Paragraph
    Comprehension Based Questions

    Test your ability to extract data from a long passage and answer a series of interrelated complex questions.
