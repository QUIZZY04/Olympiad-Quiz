Question 3
The sun rises two hours earlier in Arunachal Pradesh as compared to Gujarat in the west but the watches show the same time. How does this happen?
3 Marks (50-60 words)
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

This occurs due to India's vast longitudinal extent of nearly 30° (from 68°7' E in Gujarat to 97°25' E in Arunachal Pradesh):

  • The Earth rotates 360° on its axis in 24 hours, meaning it takes 4 minutes to rotate 1° of longitude (360° / 24 hours = 15° per hour, or 1° = 4 minutes).
  • The longitudinal difference between Arunachal Pradesh and Gujarat is approximately 30°:
    Time difference = 30° × 4 minutes = 120 minutes = 2 hours.
  • To prevent total administrative confusion, rail scheduling chaos, and commercial disorder that would arise from different local times, the local time along the Standard Meridian of India (82°30' E) passing through Mirzapur (Uttar Pradesh) is adopted as the Indian Standard Time (IST) for the entire country. Hence, all wristwatches across India display the exact same time.

Question 4
The central location of India at the head of the Indian Ocean is considered of great significance. Why?
4 Marks (60-80 words)
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

India occupies an extraordinarily strategic and pivotal geographic position at the head of the Indian Ocean:

  1. Trans-Indian Ocean Maritime Highway: India connects the maritime trade routes of industrialized European nations in the West with the resource-rich, emerging economies of East and Southeast Asia.
  2. Deccan Peninsular Projection: The triangular Deccan Peninsula projects deep into the Indian Ocean, giving India an immense coastline of 7,516.6 km. This allows India to maintain close maritime, diplomatic, and commercial contact with West Asia, Africa, and Europe from the western coast, and with Southeast and East Asia from the eastern coast.
  3. Maritime Hegemony & Nomenclature: No other country in the world possesses so vast a coastline on the Indian Ocean as India does. It is this pre-eminent maritime position that justifies naming an entire global ocean—the Indian Ocean—after our country.
  4. Historic Diffusion of Culture and Trade: For millennia, ocean routes and Himalayan mountain passes facilitated the export of Indian spices, fine muslins, mathematics (decimal system and zero), and sacred epics (Upanishads, Ramayana, Panchatantra) to the world.

Latitudinal Extent & Day-Night Variation
Why is the difference between the durations of day and night hardly felt at Kanyakumari, but not so in Kashmir?

This phenomenon is directly governed by latitudinal location relative to the Equator. Kanyakumari is situated at approximately 8°4' N latitude, very close to the Equator where the sun's rays fall almost vertically throughout the year; consequently, day and night durations remain almost equal (difference of barely 45 minutes).

In contrast, Kashmir is situated far north at 37°6' N latitude. In temperate latitudes, the slant of solar rays changes drastically between winter and summer solstices, causing the day-night duration difference to vary by as much as 4 to 5 hours between seasons.

Map & Geopolitical Competency
State the significance of the 82°30' E meridian being chosen as India's Standard Meridian instead of any other longitude.

The Standard Meridian of a nation is conventionally chosen as a multiple of 7°30' (which corresponds to exactly 30 minutes of time difference), as per international maritime and temporal conventions. 82°30' E is an exact multiple of 7°30' (7°30' × 11 = 82°30').

Furthermore, it passes almost precisely through the central longitude of India near Mirzapur (UP), equidistant from Gujarat (68°7' E) and Arunachal Pradesh (97°25' E), making it the most balanced, scientifically objective, and geographically representative meridian for Indian Standard Time (UTC + 05:30).
