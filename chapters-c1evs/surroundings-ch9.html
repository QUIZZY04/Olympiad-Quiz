Chapter 9: Keeping Our Earth Green & Clean

Waste Segregation, Blue & Green Dustbins, No Plastic & Planting | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of 'Keeping Our Earth Green & Clean': Exploring human community networks and societal roles.
  • Fostering civic responsibility, mutual respect for community helpers, and safety rules.
  • Developing environmental stewardship and neighborhood awareness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Clean Earth • The 3 R's
What are the three magical R's for protecting our environment? Explain each briefly.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. REDUCE: Use fewer things and avoid creating unnecessary waste.
2. REUSE: Use things again instead of throwing them away (e.g., using glass jars to store pulses).
3. RECYCLE: Turn used paper, plastic, and metal waste into new useful products.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Reduce explained 1 M
Reuse explained 1 M
Recycle explained 1 M
Q2 Waste Segregation • Green vs Blue Dustbins
What kind of garbage goes into:
(a) The GREEN Dustbin?
(b) The BLUE Dustbin?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• GREEN Dustbin (Wet / Biodegradable Waste): Fruit and vegetable peels, leftover food, tea leaves, fallen leaves.
• BLUE Dustbin (Dry / Recyclable Waste): Paper, plastic bottles, glass bottles, metal cans, cardboard boxes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Green bin = wet/food waste 1.5 M
Blue bin = dry/recyclable waste 1.5 M
Q3 Plastic Pollution • Say No to Single-Use Plastic
Why are single-use plastic bags harmful to our soil, water, and stray animals?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Plastic never decomposes, blocks city drains causing floods, and chokes cows and birds when they accidentally swallow plastic bags.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Does not decompose / chokes drains 1 M
Harmful/fatal to animals 1 M
Q4 Cloth Bags • Eco Alternative
What should you always carry in your pocket when going to the market with your parents?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A reusable cloth bag (or jute bag) instead of asking for plastic carry bags.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Cloth or jute bag 1 M
Q5 Composting • Kitchen Magic
What happens when wet kitchen food peels and dry garden leaves are buried in a compost pit?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
They decompose into rich, dark organic compost (manure), which makes garden plants grow healthy and strong without chemicals.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Turns into organic compost / manure 2 M
Q6 Fill in the Blanks • Clean Earth
Fill in the blanks:
(a) We must always throw trash into the _______ (dustbin / street).
(b) Trees make our air fresh and _______ (clean / polluted).
(c) We celebrate Earth Day on _______ (22nd April / 1st January).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) dustbin
(b) clean
(c) 22nd April
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
dustbin 1 M
clean 1 M
22nd April 1 M
Q7 True or False • Ecology
State True or False:
(a) Burning dry leaves and plastic on the roadside creates toxic smoke that hurts our lungs.
(b) Turning off lights and fans when leaving an empty room saves electricity.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) True. Smoke pollutes air and harms lungs.
(b) True. Conserving electricity protects the planet.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) True 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q8 Saving Paper • Saving Trees
Why does saving paper directly help save trees in forests?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Because paper is manufactured from wood pulp cut from trees. Using both sides of paper saves trees from being chopped down.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Paper comes from tree wood pulp 1 M
Saving paper prevents tree cutting 1 M
Q9 Littering • Public Cleanliness
If there is no dustbin nearby while eating a banana on the street, what should you do?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Hold the peel in a napkin or bag and carry it until you find a proper dustbin. Never litter on the pavement.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Carry until finding dustbin, never litter 1 M
Q10 Matching • Waste Items & Bins
Match each waste item with the right bin:
1. Banana peel     (A) Blue Dustbin (Dry)
2. Plastic water bottle   (B) Green Dustbin (Wet)
3. Old newspaper     (C) Blue Dustbin (Dry)
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• 1. Banana peel → (B) Green Dustbin
• 2. Plastic bottle → (A) Blue Dustbin
• 3. Old newspaper → (C) Blue Dustbin
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 bin matches correct 2 M
Q11 Clean Water Bodies • River Care
Why should factories and people never dump sewage or chemical waste into rivers?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
It poisons fish and river life and makes river water undrinkable and toxic for humans and crops.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Kills aquatic life and poisons drinking water 2 M
Q12 Odd One Out • Waste
Find the odd one out: [Apple core, Orange peel, Plastic wrapper, Tea leaves].
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Plastic wrapper is non-biodegradable dry waste, while the others are natural biodegradable wet kitchen waste.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Plastic wrapper with reason 1 M
Q13 Planting Campaign • Vanamahotsava
What is the annual Indian tree-planting festival celebrated in July called?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Van Mahotsav (Forest Festival).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Van Mahotsav 1 M
Q14 Clean School Campus • Swachh Bharat
How can students participate in keeping their school campus clean?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
By picking up stray wrappers, keeping classrooms tidy, not scribbling on walls, and sorting cafeteria waste.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Classroom cleanliness and picking wrappers 2 M
Q15 Think and Share • Electricity Conservation
Why should we turn off the tap and switch off lights during daytime?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
To conserve natural resources and reduce energy consumption.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Conserves natural resources 1 M
Q16 Environmental Pledge
Recite the Earth Day pledge.
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
'I promise to protect nature, plant trees, save water, avoid plastic, and keep my Earth green, clean, and beautiful!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Earth Day pledge 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Keeping Our Earth Green & Clean Civic Context Study

Every child is an important member of a vibrant neighborhood community. In 'Keeping Our Earth Green & Clean', learners explore how mutual support, civic awareness, and safety create a peaceful environment.

Analytical Task: How does understanding 'Keeping Our Earth Green & Clean' help us become caring and responsible citizens?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It helps us appreciate the work of others, follow community rules, protect public places, and ensure the safety and happiness of everyone around us.
Rubric: 1.5 M for community understanding + 1.5 M for civic action
