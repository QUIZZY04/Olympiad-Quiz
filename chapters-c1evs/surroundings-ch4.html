Chapter 4: Travel & Transport

Land, Water & Air Vehicles, Wheels, Safety on Roads & Eco-Travel | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of 'Travel & Transport': Exploring human community networks and societal roles.
  • Fostering civic responsibility, mutual respect for community helpers, and safety rules.
  • Developing environmental stewardship and neighborhood awareness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Modes of Transport • Three Types
Name the three main modes of transport and give two examples of vehicles for each.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Land Transport: Bicycle, scooter, car, bus, train.
2. Water Transport: Boat, ship, steamer, ferry.
3. Air Transport: Aeroplane, helicopter, rocket.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Land transport examples 1 M
Water transport examples 1 M
Air transport examples 1 M
Q2 Fastest Means of Transport
Which is the fastest mode of travel in the world?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Air Transport (Aeroplane) is the fastest means of transport.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Aeroplane / Air transport 1 M
Q3 Land Transport • Rails
Which vehicle carries hundreds of passengers and heavy goods across long distances on iron tracks?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A Train (Railways).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Train 1 M
Q4 Eco-friendly Vehicles • No Pollution
Name two means of transport that do NOT run on petrol or diesel and cause zero smoke pollution.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Bicycle (pedal cycle) and E-rickshaw / Electric cycle (also walking).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bicycle and electric vehicle 2 M
Q5 Special Vehicles • Emergency
Name the vehicle used by:
(a) Doctors to carry critically sick patients → _______
(b) Firefighters to spray water on big fires → _______
(c) Police officers on road patrol → _______
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Ambulance
(b) Fire Engine
(c) Police PCR Van / Patrol Car
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Ambulance 1 M
Fire engine 1 M
Police patrol car 1 M
Q6 Matching • Vehicles & Wheels
Match each vehicle with its number of wheels:
1. Bicycle      (A) 4 wheels
2. Auto-rickshaw   (B) 2 wheels
3. Motor Car     (C) 3 wheels
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• 1. Bicycle → (B) 2 wheels
• 2. Auto-rickshaw → (C) 3 wheels
• 3. Motor Car → (A) 4 wheels
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 wheel counts correct 2 M
Q7 Fill in the Blanks • Vehicles
Fill in the blanks:
(a) A ship sails on deep _______ (water / railway track).
(b) A helicopter lands on a _______ (helipad / bus stop).
(c) We must always wear a _______ (helmet / cap) while riding a two-wheeler.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) water
(b) helipad
(c) helmet
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
water 1 M
helipad 1 M
helmet 1 M
Q8 Animal Transport • Desert and Mountains
Which animal is called the 'Ship of the Desert'? Which animal carries loads in steep hills?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• Camel is the Ship of the Desert.
• Pony / Mule / Donkey carries loads in steep mountains.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Camel 1 M
Pony / mule 1 M
Q9 True or False • Transport Facts
State True or False:
(a) Trains run on concrete highways just like scooters.
(b) Fastening a car seatbelt keeps passengers safe in sudden stops.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. Trains run on railway tracks.
(b) True. Seatbelts save lives.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q10 Public Transport • Mass Transit
What is an underground or elevated electric passenger train inside large cities called?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The Metro Train (Metro Rail).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Metro train 1 M
Q11 Water Transport • Harbour
Where do big cargo and passenger ships load and unload at the seashore?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
At a Port (or Harbour).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Port / harbour 1 M
Q12 Pilots and Captains • Drivers
Who drives:
(a) An aeroplane? → _______
(b) A ship? → _______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Aeroplane → Pilot
(b) Ship → Captain
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pilot 1 M
Captain 1 M
Q13 Road Safety • Seatbelt and Helmet
Why is it mandatory to wear a helmet on a motorcycle?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
To protect our head and brain from severe injury in case of a road accident.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Protects head from injury 1 M
Q14 Odd One Out • Transport
Find the odd one out: [Bus, Car, Helicopter, Scooter].
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Helicopter is an air transport vehicle, while Bus, Car, and Scooter are land transport vehicles.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Helicopter with reason 1 M
Q15 Carpooling • Saving Fuel
How does traveling together in school buses or carpools help the environment?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
It reduces the number of cars on the road, which saves petrol and decreases smoke and traffic jams.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Saves fuel and reduces pollution 1 M
Q16 Space Vehicle • Rocket
What vehicle is used by astronauts to travel outside the Earth into outer space?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A Rocket (Spacecraft).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rocket / spacecraft 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Travel & Transport Civic Context Study

Every child is an important member of a vibrant neighborhood community. In 'Travel & Transport', learners explore how mutual support, civic awareness, and safety create a peaceful environment.

Analytical Task: How does understanding 'Travel & Transport' help us become caring and responsible citizens?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It helps us appreciate the work of others, follow community rules, protect public places, and ensure the safety and happiness of everyone around us.
Rubric: 1.5 M for community understanding + 1.5 M for civic action
