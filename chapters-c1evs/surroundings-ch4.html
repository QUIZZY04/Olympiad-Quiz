Chapter 4: Travel & Transport

Land, Water & Air Vehicles, Wheels, Safety on Roads & Eco-Travel | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of 'Travel & Transport': Exploring human community networks and societal roles.
  • Fostering civic responsibility, mutual respect for community helpers, and safety rules.
  • Developing environmental stewardship and neighborhood awareness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (6 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Community Exploration • Roles
What is the main role or purpose of 'Travel & Transport' in our daily lives?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• 'Travel & Transport' focuses on Land, Water & Air Vehicles, Wheels, Safety on Roads & Eco-Travel.
• It teaches children how different people and places in our locality help us live smoothly and safely.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Role identified 1 M
Locality impact 1 M
Q2 Helper Recognition • Civic Duties
Name two community helpers or public places associated with 'Travel & Transport' and what they do.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Doctors and Nurses: Treat sick people in hospitals.
2. Teachers: Help children learn knowledge and good values in schools.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two helpers/places with roles 2 M
Q3 Safety and Etiquette
What key safety rule or etiquette must we practice regarding 'Travel & Transport'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Always show politeness, respect others' time, wait patiently in queues, and never damage public property.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Politeness and public safety rules 2 M
Q4 Field Observation • Neighborhood
When you walk through your neighbourhood, what important things should you observe?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
We should observe cleanliness of streets, zebra crossings, dustbins, road signs, and helpful community members at work.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Observational items listed 2 M
Q5 Values and Social Harmony
How do festivals and celebrations bring people of different communities together?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Festivals teach us to share sweets, exchange greetings, respect all cultures, and celebrate together with joy and harmony.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sharing and cultural harmony 2 M
Q6 Responsible Citizen Action
What should you do if you see litter lying on the park playground?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Pick it up carefully and throw it into the nearest dustbin to keep the park clean and green.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Throw in dustbin 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Travel & Transport Civic Context Study

Every child is an important member of a vibrant neighborhood community. In 'Travel & Transport', learners explore how mutual support, civic awareness, and safety create a peaceful environment.

Analytical Task: How does understanding 'Travel & Transport' help us become caring and responsible citizens?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It helps us appreciate the work of others, follow community rules, protect public places, and ensure the safety and happiness of everyone around us.
Rubric: 1.5 M for community understanding + 1.5 M for civic action
