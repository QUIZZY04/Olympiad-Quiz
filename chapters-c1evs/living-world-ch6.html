Chapter 6: My School & Friends

Classroom Ethics, Teachers, Playground Sharing & School Activities | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Core Theme of 'My School & Friends': Understanding natural and bodily phenomena.
  • Developing keen observational skills, scientific inquiry, and environmental empathy.
  • Practical health, hygiene, and ecological awareness aligned with NEP 2020.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 School Places • Tour of Campus
Name four important places inside your school campus and what children do there.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Classroom: Where students sit and learn lessons from teachers.
2. Library: Where we read storybooks silently.
3. Playground: Where we play sports and run during games period.
4. Assembly Hall: Where morning prayer and announcements take place.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four places named 1.5 M
Functions described correctly 1.5 M
Q2 School Helpers • The Staff
Who is the head of the entire school? What does a teacher do?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• The Principal is the head of the school.
• A Teacher teaches us reading, writing, good manners, and guides us.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Principal is head 1 M
Teacher's role explained 1 M
Q3 Other Helpers • Security & Cleaning
Name two non-teaching staff members who help keep our school safe and clean.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Security Guard: Keeps the school gate safe and monitors visitors.
2. School Cleaner / Sweeper: Keeps classrooms, corridors, and toilets neat and clean.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Security guard 1 M
Cleaner / sweeper 1 M
Q4 Classroom Etiquette • Good Manners
Write two golden rules of good behavior inside the classroom.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Raise your hand before speaking or answering a question.
2. Listen quietly when the teacher or a classmate is speaking (do not shout).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two classroom rules 2 M
Q5 Friendship & Sharing • Social Skills
How can you be a good friend to your classmates in school?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
By sharing crayons or erasers, playing together nicely, helping a friend who is hurt, and never bullying anyone.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sharing, helping, playing kindly 2 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

My School & Friends Environmental Case Study

In 'My School & Friends', Grade 1 learners explore their natural environment and daily interactions, observing cause and effect in the world around them.

Analytical Task: What essential ecological or health habit does 'My School & Friends' teach us to practice every day?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It teaches us to observe nature carefully, practice daily personal hygiene, and protect all living beings with kindness and respect.
Rubric: 1.5 M for habit identification + 1.5 M for environmental rationale
