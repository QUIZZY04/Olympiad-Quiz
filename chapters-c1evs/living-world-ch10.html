Chapter 10: Keeping Safe & Good Manners

Home Safety, Road Rules, Zebra Crossing & Polite Magic Words | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Core Theme of 'Keeping Safe & Good Manners': Understanding natural and bodily phenomena.
  • Developing keen observational skills, scientific inquiry, and environmental empathy.
  • Practical health, hygiene, and ecological awareness aligned with NEP 2020.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Safety on Road • Traffic Lights
What do the three colours of traffic lights tell drivers and pedestrians?
(a) Red Light → _______
(b) Yellow Light → _______
(c) Green Light → _______
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) RED: STOP immediately.
(b) YELLOW: WAIT / GET READY.
(c) GREEN: GO ahead safely.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Red = Stop 1 M
Yellow = Wait 1 M
Green = Go 1 M
Q2 Crossing the Road • Zebra Crossing
Where should pedestrians always cross a busy road? What are the safety steps?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
We should always cross at the Zebra Crossing.
Steps: Look to your right, look to your left, look to your right again, and cross only when the road is clear.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Zebra crossing 1 M
Look right, left, right 1 M
Q3 Safety at Home • Dangerous Objects
Name three things at home that children should NOT touch or play with.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Sharp objects: Knives, scissors, blades, and needles.
2. Electricity: Wet hands on electric switches, open sockets, and live wires.
3. Fire: Matchboxes, gas stoves, and burning candle flames.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sharp objects 1 M
Electrical sockets/switches 1 M
Fire/matches/stove 1 M
Q4 Safety in the Bus • Commuting Rules
Write two important safety rules to follow while traveling in a school bus.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Never put your head, hand, or arm out of a moving bus window.
2. Always board or alight the bus in a calm queue only when the bus has stopped completely.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Never put body parts out of window 1 M
Stand in queue / wait for complete stop 1 M
Q5 Safety on the Playground • Play Fair
What should you do while waiting for your turn on the slide or swing?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
We must wait patiently for our turn in a queue and never push or shove others.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Wait in turn, never push 1 M
Q6 Good Manners • Magic Words
Name the four golden magic words of politeness and when to use them.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. 'Please': When requesting something.
2. 'Thank You': When receiving help or a gift.
3. 'Sorry': When we make a mistake or hurt someone by accident.
4. 'Excuse Me': When getting someone's attention or sneezing.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four magic words explained 2 M
Q7 Fill in the Blanks • Safety & Manners
Fill in the blanks:
(a) Always walk on the _______ (footpath / middle of the road).
(b) Never take toffees or gifts from a _______ (stranger / parent).
(c) Greet elders with a pleasant _______ (smile / frown).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) footpath
(b) stranger
(c) smile
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
footpath 1 M
stranger 1 M
smile 1 M
Q8 True or False • Safety Knowledge
State True or False:
(a) Running across a busy road while playing with a ball is very safe.
(b) It is good manners to say 'Good Morning' to teachers and elders.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. It causes dangerous road accidents.
(b) True. Wishing elders shows respect and politeness.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q9 First Aid • Minor Cuts
What is a First Aid box? Name two items kept in a school first-aid kit.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A first-aid box provides immediate medical care before a doctor arrives.
Items kept: Antiseptic lotion (Dettol/Savlon), band-aids, cotton gauze, and bandage.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
First aid definition 1 M
Two items named 1 M
Q10 Swimming Safety • Water Caution
Why must young children always swim with a rubber float tube and in adult presence?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
To prevent accidental drowning and stay afloat safely in deep water.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Prevents drowning / ensures safety 1 M
Q11 Medicine Safety • Poison Prevention
Why should children NEVER take pills or medicines by themselves without parents?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Because taking the wrong medicine or wrong dose can cause serious poisoning and illness.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Risk of poisoning and illness 1 M
Q12 Emergency Helpline Numbers
What emergency phone number in India is dialed for:
(a) Police Assistance → _______
(b) Ambulance / Medical Emergency → _______
(c) Fire Brigade → _______
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Police: 112 (or 100)
(b) Ambulance: 102 (or 108)
(c) Fire Brigade: 101
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Police 112/100 1 M
Ambulance 102/108 1 M
Fire 101 1 M
Q13 Stranger Danger • Personal Safety
What should you do if an unknown stranger asks you to get into their car?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Say 'NO' loudly, run away to a trusted teacher or parent, and shout for help.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Say NO, run away, inform trusted adult 1 M
Q14 Good Touch and Bad Touch Awareness
If someone's touch makes you feel uncomfortable or scared, what should you do?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Shout 'NO!', move away immediately, and tell your parents or teacher right away without keeping it a secret.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Say NO and move away 1 M
Tell parents/teacher immediately 1 M
Q15 Odd One Out • Safety Objects
Which object is safe for a child to play with? [Scissors, Matchstick, Soft Teddy Bear, Blade].
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Soft Teddy Bear is safe to play with, while the others are dangerous sharp/fire objects.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Teddy bear identified 1 M
Q16 Safety Motto • Life Golden Rule
Complete the golden safety motto: 'Safety First, ...'
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
'Safety First, Danger Never!' (or 'Precaution is better than cure!')
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Safety slogan 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Keeping Safe & Good Manners Environmental Case Study

In 'Keeping Safe & Good Manners', Grade 1 learners explore their natural environment and daily interactions, observing cause and effect in the world around them.

Analytical Task: What essential ecological or health habit does 'Keeping Safe & Good Manners' teach us to practice every day?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It teaches us to observe nature carefully, practice daily personal hygiene, and protect all living beings with kindness and respect.
Rubric: 1.5 M for habit identification + 1.5 M for environmental rationale
