Chapter 7: Plants Around Us

Big Trees, Small Shrubs, Herbs, Green Leaves & Flower Care | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Core Theme of 'Plants Around Us': Understanding natural and bodily phenomena.
  • Developing keen observational skills, scientific inquiry, and environmental empathy.
  • Practical health, hygiene, and ecological awareness aligned with NEP 2020.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Plant Types • Classification
Name five different kinds of plants found in nature and give one example of each.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Trees: Big, tall plants with thick woody trunks (e.g., Banyan, Neem, Mango).
2. Shrubs: Medium-sized bushy plants (e.g., Rose, Hibiscus).
3. Herbs: Small plants with soft green stems (e.g., Mint, Coriander, Tulsi).
4. Climbers: Weak-stemmed plants that climb on supports (e.g., Money plant, Pea plant).
5. Creepers: Weak plants that crawl along the ground (e.g., Watermelon, Pumpkin).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 5 plant types named 2 M
Valid examples for each 1 M
Q2 Parts of a Plant • Structure
Name the five main parts of a plant and state what the root and leaf do.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• Parts: Roots, Stem, Leaves, Flowers, Fruits (and Seeds).
• Roots: Fix the plant in the soil and absorb water and minerals.
• Leaves: Prepare food for the plant using sunlight (kitchen of the plant).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Five plant parts 1 M
Root function 1 M
Leaf function 1 M
Q3 Plant Needs • Growth Requirements
What four essential things does a plant need to grow healthy and green?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Plants need: 1. Sunlight, 2. Water, 3. Air (carbon dioxide), 4. Soil (nutrients).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sunlight, water, air, soil 2 M
Q4 Uses of Plants • Gifts of Nature
Give four important gifts that plants give to humans and animals.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Food: Fruits, vegetables, grains, nuts, and spices.
2. Oxygen: Fresh, clean air to breathe.
3. Shade & Shelter: Cool shade for travelers and homes for birds.
4. Wood & Medicines: Timber for furniture and herbal medicines.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four uses of plants listed 2 M
Q5 Trees • Woody Giants
What is the hard, woody stem of a big tree called?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The thick, brown, woody stem of a tree is called a Trunk.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Trunk 1 M
Q6 Fill in the Blanks • Plant Life
Fill in the blanks:
(a) A seed sprouts into a baby plant called a _______ (seedling / flower).
(b) The _______ (leaf / root) grows underneath the ground.
(c) A watermelon plant is a _______ (creeper / tree) because its fruits are heavy.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) seedling
(b) root
(c) creeper

Q8 True or False • Botany Basics
State True or False:
(a) Plants can grow inside a dark cupboard without any sunlight or water.
(b) Trees take in carbon dioxide and release fresh oxygen for us to breathe.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. Plants need light and water.
(b) True. Photosynthesis gives us oxygen.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M

Why do cactus and rose plants have thorns?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Q11 Caring for Plants • Green Thumb
How can you take care of house plants in your balcony or garden?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
We should water them regularly, ensure they get sunlight, and never pluck their leaves or flowers needlessly.

Plants Around Us Environmental Case Study

Analytical Task: What essential ecological or health habit does 'Plants Around Us' teach us to practice every day?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It teaches us to observe nature carefully, practice daily personal hygiene, and protect all living beings with kindness and respect.
