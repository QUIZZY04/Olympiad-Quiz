Chapter 2: My Family & Home

Family Members, Types of Families, Sharing Chores & Home Rooms | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Core Theme of 'My Family & Home': Understanding natural and bodily phenomena.
  • Developing keen observational skills, scientific inquiry, and environmental empathy.
  • Practical health, hygiene, and ecological awareness aligned with NEP 2020.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Family Types • Nuclear vs Joint
What is the difference between a small nuclear family and a large joint family?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• Nuclear family: Parents and their one or two children live together.
• Joint family: Grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts, cousins, and children all live together in the same house.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Nuclear family definition 1 M
Joint family definition 1 M
Q2 Family Relations • Terms
What do you call:
(a) Your father's father?
(b) Your mother's mother?
(c) Your uncle's children?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Father's father: Grandfather (Dada ji)
(b) Mother's mother: Grandmother (Nani ji)
(c) Uncle's children: Cousins
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Grandfather 1 M
Grandmother 1 M
Cousins 1 M
Q3 Rooms in a House • Uses
Name three different rooms in a house and state what we do in each room.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Kitchen: Where food is cooked.
2. Bedroom: Where we rest and sleep.
3. Living Room (Drawing Room): Where we sit together and welcome guests.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three rooms named 1.5 M
Their uses explained 1.5 M
Q4 Helping at Home • Sharing Work
Write two simple chores you can do to help your parents at home.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. Keeping our toys, books, and shoes neatly in their proper places.
2. Watering potted plants and helping clear the dining table.

Two helpful chores listed 2 M
Q5 Types of Houses • Materials
What is a 'Pucca House' made of? What is a 'Kutcha House' made of?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• Pucca House: Made of strong bricks, cement, iron rods, and stone.
• Kutcha House: Made of mud, thatch, bamboo, and dry straw.

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

To leave outside mud, dirt, and dust at the doorway and keep the floors inside clean.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

My Family & Home Environmental Case Study

In 'My Family & Home', Grade 1 learners explore their natural environment and daily interactions, observing cause and effect in the world around them.

Analytical Task: What essential ecological or health habit does 'My Family & Home' teach us to practice every day?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It teaches us to observe nature carefully, practice daily personal hygiene, and protect all living beings with kindness and respect.
Rubric: 1.5 M for habit identification + 1.5 M for environmental rationale
