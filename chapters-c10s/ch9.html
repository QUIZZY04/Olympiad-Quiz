In-Text Page 149 • Q1
Define the principal focus of a concave mirror.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Definition:

The principal focus of a concave mirror is a fixed point on its principal axis at which all incident light rays traveling parallel and close to the principal axis actually converge and intersect after undergoing reflection from the mirror surface.

It is denoted by the capital letter $F$, and lies in front of the reflecting surface at a distance equal to half the radius of curvature ($f = R/2$).

[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Calculation:

The relationship between the radius of curvature ($R$) and focal length ($f$) of a spherical mirror is given by:

$$f = \frac{R}{2}$$

$$f = \frac{20\text{ cm}}{2} = \mathbf{10\text{ cm}}$$

Therefore, the focal length of the spherical mirror is $10\text{ cm}$.

In-Text Page 161 • Q1
A ray of light travelling in air enters obliquely into water. Does the light ray bend towards the normal or away from the normal? Why?
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Refraction Analysis:

The ray of light will bend towards the normal.

Reason: Water is an optically denser medium compared to air (refractive index of water $n_w \approx 1.33$ vs air $n_a \approx 1.00$). When light passes obliquely from an optically rarer medium (air) into an optically denser medium (water), its speed slows down ($v = c/n$). According to Snell's law of refraction ($\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{n_2}{n_1} > 1 \implies \angle r < \angle i$), the light ray bends towards the normal line at the interface.

Light bends towards the normal1 Mark

In-Text Page 161 • Q2
Light enters from air to glass having refractive index 1.50. What is the speed of light in the glass? The speed of light in vacuum is $3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Calculation:

The absolute refractive index of a medium is defined as:

$$n = \frac{c}{v}$$

Where $c = 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ and $n = 1.50$.

$$v = \frac{c}{n} = \frac{3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}{1.50} = \mathbf{2 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}$$

The speed of light in glass is $2 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$.

Physical Meaning:

NCERT Exercise • Q10
An object 5 cm in length is held 25 cm away from a converging lens of focal length 10 cm. Draw the ray diagram and find the position, size and the nature of the image formed.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Given Data:
    • Object height, $h_1 = +5\text{ cm}$
    • Object distance, $u = -25\text{ cm}$
    • Focal length of converging lens, $f = +10\text{ cm}$
  2. Lens Formula: $$\frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f} \implies \frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} + \frac{1}{u}$$ $$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{-25} = \frac{5 - 2}{50} = \frac{3}{50}$$ $$v = \frac{50}{3} = \mathbf{+16.67\text{ cm}}$$ The image is formed at a distance of $16.67\text{ cm}$ on the other side of the lens.
  3. Size and Nature of Image: $$m = \frac{h_2}{h_1} = \frac{v}{u} \implies h_2 = h_1 \left(\frac{v}{u}\right)$$ $$h_2 = 5 \times \left(\frac{50/3}{-25}\right) = 5 \times \left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) = -\frac{10}{3} = \mathbf{-3.33\text{ cm}}$$
  4. Conclusion: The negative sign indicates that the image is Real and Inverted, diminished in size ($3.33\text{ cm}$ tall).

Case Study Question • Lens Combination & Optical Instruments
An optician places two thin lenses in contact: Lens 1 of focal length $+20\text{ cm}$ and Lens 2 of focal length $-50\text{ cm}$. (i) Calculate the net power of the combination. (ii) What is the effective focal length of the combination? (iii) Does the combination behave as a converging or diverging system?

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. (i) Net Power: $$P_1 = \frac{1}{+0.20\text{ m}} = +5\text{ D}, \quad P_2 = \frac{1}{-0.50\text{ m}} = -2\text{ D}$$ $$P_{\text{net}} = P_1 + P_2 = +5\text{ D} - 2\text{ D} = \mathbf{+3\text{ D}}$$
  2. (ii) Effective Focal Length: $$F = \frac{1}{P_{\text{net}}} = \frac{1}{+3}\text{ m} = +0.333\text{ m} = \mathbf{+33.3\text{ cm}}$$
  3. (iii) System Behavior: Because the net power ($+3\text{ D}$) and effective focal length ($+33.3\text{ cm}$) are positive, the combination behaves as a Converging lens system.
