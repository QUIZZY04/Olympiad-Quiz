Class 4 Reasoning Syllabus & Exam Pattern

The Class 4 Logical Reasoning Olympiad assesses students' mental aptitude and out-of-the-box thinking across three major sections.

  • Section 1
    Verbal Reasoning

    Patterns, Alphabet Test, Coding-Decoding, Ranking Test, Analogy and Classification, Direction Sense Test.

  • Section 2
    Non-Verbal Reasoning

    Geometrical Shapes, Mirror Images, Spatial Understanding, Grouping of Figures, Figure Matrix.

  • Section 3
    Achievers Section

    Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions based on the reasoning syllabus. These complex puzzles carry more marks.

Why Logical Reasoning is Crucial in Class 4

Class 4 is an excellent stage to introduce students to structured analytical thinking. Participating in the Class 4 Reasoning Olympiad builds a robust cognitive foundation that helps in all other subjects, especially Mathematics and Science.

By practicing our Reasoning Class 4 mock tests, students develop a deep understanding of spatial visualization and logic sequences, moving away from rote memorization. Developing mental aptitude at this age trains young minds for future competitive exams like JEE, NEET, and standard aptitude tests.
