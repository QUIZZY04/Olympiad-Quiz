Class 12 JEE Main Core Syllabus Highlights

Class 12 constitutes over 50% of the JEE Main paper. Mastering these advanced topics is essential to secure a 99+ percentile.

  • Physics
    Electromagnetism & Modern Physics

    Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Current, EMI & AC, Electromagnetic Waves, Optics, Dual Nature of Matter, Atoms and Nuclei, Electronic Devices.

  • Chemistry
    Inorganic & Advanced Organic

    Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Coordination Compounds, p-block, d-block, f-block elements, Haloalkanes, Alcohols, Aldehydes, Ketones, Amines, Biomolecules.

  • Mathematics
    Calculus & Vectors

    Matrices and Determinants, Limits, Continuity and Differentiability, Integral Calculus, Differential Equations, Vector Algebra, 3D Geometry, Probability.
