Fractions Questions for Class 6

Important sample questions to help you master fraction simplification and word problems for the IMO.

Q1. A tank is 3/4 full of water. If 15 liters of water is drawn from it, it becomes 1/2 full. What is the total capacity of the tank?

A) 30 liters
B) 45 liters
C) 60 liters
D) 75 liters

Correct Answer: C

Step-by-step Solution: The fraction of water drawn out is 3/4 - 1/2 = 3/4 - 2/4 = 1/4 of the tank. Since 1/4 of the tank equals 15 liters, the total capacity is 15 × 4 = 60 liters.

Q2. Which of the following fractions is equivalent to 5/8?

A) 15/20
B) 25/40
C) 10/18
D) 35/48

Correct Answer: B

Step-by-step Solution: Multiply both numerator and denominator of 5/8 by 5: (5 × 5)/(8 × 5) = 25/40. Simplifying 25/40 by dividing by 5 returns 5/8.

Q3. Arrange the fractions 2/3, 5/6, and 7/12 in ascending order.

A) 2/3 < 5/6 < 7/12
B) 7/12 < 2/3 < 5/6
C) 5/6 < 2/3 < 7/12
D) 7/12 < 5/6 < 2/3

Correct Answer: B

Step-by-step Solution: Convert all fractions to a common denominator of 12 (the LCM of 3, 6, and 12): 2/3 = 8/12, 5/6 = 10/12, and 7/12 = 7/12. Comparing numerators gives 7/12 < 8/12 < 10/12, so 7/12 < 2/3 < 5/6.

Q4. What is the simplified value of 2 1/3 + 1 3/4?

A) 3 4/7
B) 4 1/12
C) 3 11/12
D) 4 5/12

Correct Answer: B

Step-by-step Solution: Add the whole numbers (2 + 1 = 3) and the fractional parts (1/3 + 3/4 = 4/12 + 9/12 = 13/12 = 1 1/12). Total = 3 + 1 1/12 = 4 1/12.

Q5. A ribbon of length 5 1/4 meters is cut into 7 equal pieces. What is the length of each piece?

A) 3/4 m
B) 4/7 m
C) 7/8 m
D) 1/2 m

Correct Answer: A

Step-by-step Solution: Convert 5 1/4 to an improper fraction: 21/4. Divide by 7: (21/4) ÷ 7 = (21/4) × (1/7) = 3/4 meter.

Fractions Tips for Class 6 Maths Olympiad

To solve Class 6 fraction problems quickly in IMO exams, always convert mixed numbers into improper fractions before multiplying or dividing, use LCM to compare unlike fractions, and cross-check word problems by verifying what fraction represents the whole.
