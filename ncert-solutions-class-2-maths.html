Q1 Look at the starfish on the sand. There are 4 groups with 2 starfish in each group. How many starfish are there in total? Textbook Exercise • Group Counting 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Number of groups = 4
• Starfish in each group = 2
• Total starfish = 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8 starfish (or 4 × 2 = 8).

Q2 Rohit has 11 flip-flops (slippers). Can he make complete pairs without any slipper left over? Explain your answer. Visual Estimation • Pairs 3 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Answer:
• A pair requires 2 items.
• 11 = 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1.
• Rohit makes 5 complete pairs and 1 slipper is left over. Therefore, 11 is an odd number and cannot form complete pairs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Division into pairs (5 pairs) 1.5 M
Identification of 1 remainder / odd number 1.5 M

1. White shells = 15
2. Brown shells = 9
3. Total shells = 15 + 9 = 24 shells.
4. Comparison: White shells (15) are more than Brown shells (9).
5. Difference = 15 - 9 = 6 more white shells.

Correct deduction 1 M

• (b) Eraser: Slides (it has flat faces).
• (c) Coin: Both rolls and slides (rolls on circular edge, slides on flat faces).

Q6 Why are the wheels of cars, buses, and bicycles made round (circular) and not square or triangular? Let Us Think • Wheels of Vehicles 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Answer:
• Round wheels have a smooth curved surface with no sharp corners, allowing them to roll effortlessly with minimal friction.
• Square or triangular wheels have corners that would get stuck on the ground and bump violently, making vehicles unable to roll smoothly.
