About Major Olympiads

Our platform helps you prepare for the most prestigious Olympiad examinations conducted in India and worldwide.

Science Olympiad Foundation (SOF)

SOF is one of the largest organizers of Olympiad exams. Our content is tailored for their key tests, including the National Science Olympiad (NSO), International Mathematics Olympiad (IMO), and International English Olympiad (IEO). These exams are known for their application-based questions that test conceptual understanding.

SilverZone Foundation

SilverZone Olympiads are popular for their comprehensive assessment across various subjects. We provide practice material relevant to the International Olympiad of Mathematics (iOM) and International Olympiad of Science (iOS), helping students get familiar with their question patterns and syllabus.

CREST Olympiads

CREST (Centre for Research and Exams in Science and Technology) conducts online, proctored Olympiads that focus on practical and logical thinking. Our platform is an excellent resource for preparing for the CREST Mathematics Olympiad (CMO) and CREST Science Olympiad (CSO).
