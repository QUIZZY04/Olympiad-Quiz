About OlympiadQuiz.org

OlympiadQuiz.org is a dedicated educational platform designed to help students from Class 1 to Class 10 master their academic concepts through interactive testing. We provide high-quality practice sets for Math Olympiads (IMO), Science Olympiads (NSO), and General Knowledge.

Our quizzes are structured to follow the school curriculum while adding a layer of critical thinking required for competitive exams. With our real-time dashboard, students can track their accuracy, speed, and progress across different subjects, making us the perfect companion for school foundation prep.
