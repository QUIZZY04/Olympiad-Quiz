NSO Preparation Guide for Class 1 to 10

The National Science Olympiad (NSO) evaluates a student's scientific temperament and logical ability. To excel, students need to move beyond rote learning and understand the practical application of scientific laws. Here is how we support your journey:

Primary Level (Class 1-4)

Focus on basic observation of plants, animals, human body, and the environment. Our mock tests use clear diagrams to help young learners identify parts and processes easily.

Middle & Secondary (Class 5-10)

In-depth coverage of Physics (Motion, Light, Electricity), Chemistry (Matter, Atoms, Chemical Reactions), and Biology (Cells, Tissues, Life Processes). We include experiment-based questions common in SOF second-level exams.
