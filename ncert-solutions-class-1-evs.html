Chapter 1: About Me & My Wonderful Body

📖 NCERT Textbook Exercise Solutions & Step-by-Step Working (6 Solved Questions)
Q1 What is the most important concept explained in 'About Me & My Wonderful Body'? Concept Check • Observation 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Step-by-Step Answer:
• The chapter highlights Body Parts, Five Sense Organs, Growing Up & Unique Traits.
• It teaches children to understand their surroundings and make healthy, responsible choices.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Concept identified 1 M
Explanation of significance 1 M
Q2 Give two real-life examples related to 'About Me & My Wonderful Body' that you see around you every day. Think and Answer • Practical Science 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Answer:
1. Practicing daily cleanliness and healthy routines at home.
2. Observing plants, animals, and weather changes during our walks outside.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two authentic examples given 2 M
Q3 Why is it important to follow good habits in connection with 'About Me & My Wonderful Body'? Good Habits and Safety 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Answer:

Environmental cleanliness 1 M

Q5 How can you show kindness and responsibility towards our living world? Life Values and Care 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:

Kindness and responsibility explained 2 M
Q6 State True or False: Taking care of our body and environment makes us healthy and cheerful. True or False • Quick Check 1 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study (CBQ)

About Me & My Wonderful Body Environmental Case Study

Real-World Context: In 'About Me & My Wonderful Body', Grade 1 learners explore their natural environment and daily interactions, observing cause and effect in the world around them.

Analytical Task: What essential ecological or health habit does 'About Me & My Wonderful Body' teach us to practice every day?

It teaches us to observe nature carefully, practice daily personal hygiene, and protect all living beings with kindness and respect.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1.5 M for habit identification + 1.5 M for environmental rationale

Class 1 Environmental Studies comprehensively covers foundational environmental literacy across 20 chapters: Our Living World & Nature (10 chapters) and Surroundings & Community (10 chapters).

❓ Are environmental protection and hygiene practices included?

Yes, solutions include healthy eating habits, clean water, personal hygiene, plant care, animal compassion, and traffic safety.

❓ Do solutions provide observation activities for young learners?

Yes, inquiry-based observational activities, nature journaling prompts, and sensory exploration tasks are woven throughout the solutions.
