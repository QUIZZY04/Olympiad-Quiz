📖 NCERT Textbook Exercise Solutions & Step-by-Step Working (16 Solved Questions)
Q1 Name the five sense organs of the human body and state what each one helps us do. Parts of My Body • Identification 3 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Model Answer:
1. Eyes: Help us to see colors, shapes, and pictures.
2. Ears: Help us to hear sounds, music, and voices.
3. Nose: Helps us to smell fragrances and breathe.
4. Tongue: Helps us to taste sweet, salty, sour, and bitter food.
5. Skin: Helps us to feel hot, cold, soft, hard, and rough textures.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Names all 5 sense organs 2 M
Explains their functions correctly 1 M
Q2 Write two actions we do using our hands, and two actions we do using our legs. Body Actions • Hands and Legs 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Model Answer:
• Using Hands: Writing, holding, eating, clapping.
• Using Legs: Walking, running, jumping, kicking a ball.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two hand actions 1 M
Two leg actions 1 M
Q3 How do you know that you are growing taller and bigger every year? Growing Up • Changes in Body 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Model Answer:
We know we are growing because our old clothes and shoes become too small and tight, and our height increases when measured on a growth chart.
