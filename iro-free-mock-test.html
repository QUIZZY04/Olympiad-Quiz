Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What topics are covered in the Logical Reasoning Olympiad?

Our reasoning mock tests cover Verbal Reasoning (Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Number Series, Ranking) and Non-Verbal Reasoning (Mirror Images, Paper Folding, Spatial Patterns).

Are reasoning questions included in IMO and NSO exams too?

Yes! The first section of almost all major SOF exams (IMO, NSO, NCO) consists of Logical Reasoning questions. Mastering this section gives you a massive advantage and guarantees easy marks.

Is this reasoning practice helpful for NTSE?

Definitely. The Mental Ability Test (MAT) section of the NTSE relies on the exact same logical reasoning frameworks. Practicing IRO papers from Class 7 & 8 builds a strong foundation for NTSE.
