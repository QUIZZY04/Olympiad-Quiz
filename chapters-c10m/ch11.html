Question 1
Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius $6\text{ cm}$ if angle of the sector is $60^\circ$.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Figure: Sector of Circle with Central Angle θ and Segment Formed by Chord AB

Step-by-step Detailed Solution:

  1. Given Parameters:
    • Radius of the circle $r = 6\text{ cm}$
    • Sector angle $\theta = 60^\circ$
  2. Formula:

    The area of a sector of a circle of radius $r$ and sector angle $\theta$ is given by:

    $$\text{Area of Sector} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$$
  3. Substitution and Calculation: $$\text{Area} = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \frac{22}{7} \times (6)^2 = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 36$$ $$= \frac{22 \times 6}{7} = \frac{132}{7}\text{ cm}^2 = 18.86\text{ cm}^2$$
  4. Conclusion: The area of the sector is $\frac{132}{7}\text{ cm}^2$ (or $18.86\text{ cm}^2$).

Question 2
Find the area of a quadrant of a circle whose circumference is $22\text{ cm}$.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Given: Circumference of circle $C = 22\text{ cm}$.

    Circumference formula: $2\pi r = 22$

    $$2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times r = 22 \implies r = \frac{22 \times 7}{2 \times 22} = \frac{7}{2} = 3.5\text{ cm}$$
  2. Area of Quadrant:

    A quadrant is a sector with central angle $\theta = 90^\circ$ (one-fourth of a circle):

    $$\text{Area of Quadrant} = \frac{1}{4} \pi r^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{22}{7} \times \left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^2$$ $$= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{49}{4} = \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{154}{4} = \frac{77}{8}\text{ cm}^2 = 9.625\text{ cm}^2$$
  3. Conclusion: The area of the quadrant is $\frac{77}{8}\text{ cm}^2$ ($9.625\text{ cm}^2$).

Question 3
The length of the minute hand of a clock is $14\text{ cm}$. Find the area swept by the minute hand in 5 minutes.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Understanding Clock Geometry:
    • In 60 minutes, the minute hand completes one full circle of $360^\circ$.
    • Therefore, angle swept by minute hand in 1 minute $= \frac{360^\circ}{60} = 6^\circ$.
    • Angle swept in 5 minutes $\theta = 5 \times 6^\circ = 30^\circ$.
    • Length of minute hand acts as radius $r = 14\text{ cm}$.
  2. Calculating Area Swept: $$\text{Area} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2 = \frac{30^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \frac{22}{7} \times (14)^2$$ $$= \frac{1}{12} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 196 = \frac{1}{12} \times 22 \times 28 = \frac{616}{12} = \frac{154}{3}\text{ cm}^2$$
  3. Conclusion: The area swept by the minute hand in 5 minutes is $\frac{154}{3}\text{ cm}^2$ (or $51.33\text{ cm}^2$).

Question 4
A chord of a circle of radius $10\text{ cm}$ subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding:
(i) minor segment
(ii) major sector. (Use $\pi = 3.14$)
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Detailed Solution:

  1. Given Parameters:
    • Radius $r = 10\text{ cm}$
    • Central angle subtended by chord $\theta = 90^\circ$
  2. (i) Area of Minor Segment:

    $\text{Area of Minor Segment} = \text{Area of Minor Sector} - \text{Area of } \Delta OAB$

    $$\text{Area of Sector} = \frac{90^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times 3.14 \times (10)^2 = \frac{314}{4} = 78.5\text{ cm}^2$$

    In right-angled triangle $\Delta OAB$ with $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$:

    $$\text{Area}(\Delta OAB) = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times r \times r = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 10 = 50\text{ cm}^2$$ $$\text{Area of Minor Segment} = 78.5 - 50 = 28.5\text{ cm}^2$$
  3. (ii) Area of Major Sector:

    Central angle for major sector $\theta_{\text{major}} = 360^\circ - 90^\circ = 270^\circ$.

    $$\text{Area of Major Sector} = \frac{270^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2 = \frac{3}{4} \times 3.14 \times 100 = \frac{3 \times 314}{4} = 3 \times 78.5 = 235.5\text{ cm}^2$$

    Alternative: Area of circle - Area of minor sector $= 314 - 78.5 = 235.5\text{ cm}^2$.

  4. Conclusion:
    (i) Minor segment area = $28.5\text{ cm}^2$
    (ii) Major sector area = $235.5\text{ cm}^2$

Question 5
In a circle of radius $21\text{ cm}$, an arc subtends an angle of $60^\circ$ at the centre. Find:
(i) the length of the arc
(ii) area of the sector formed by the arc
(iii) area of the segment formed by the corresponding chord. (Take $\pi = 22/7$)
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Given: $r = 21\text{ cm}, \theta = 60^\circ$.
  2. (i) Length of Arc: $$l = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 21 = \frac{1}{6} \times 2 \times 22 \times 3 = 22\text{ cm}$$
  3. (ii) Area of Sector: $$\text{Area} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2 = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 21 \times 21 = \frac{1}{6} \times 22 \times 3 \times 21 = 11 \times 21 = 231\text{ cm}^2$$
  4. (iii) Area of Segment:

    Since $\angle AOB = 60^\circ$ and $OA = OB = 21\text{ cm}$, $\Delta OAB$ is an equilateral triangle ($\angle A = \angle B = 60^\circ$).

    $$\text{Area of Equilateral } \Delta OAB = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} (\text{side})^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times (21)^2 = \frac{441\sqrt{3}}{4}\text{ cm}^2$$ $$\text{Area of Segment} = \text{Area of Sector} - \text{Area}(\Delta OAB) = \left(231 - \frac{441\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)\text{ cm}^2 \approx 40.05\text{ cm}^2$$
  5. Answer: (i) $22\text{ cm}$, (ii) $231\text{ cm}^2$, (iii) $\left(231 - \frac{441\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)\text{ cm}^2$.

Question 6
A chord of a circle of radius $15\text{ cm}$ subtends an angle of $60^\circ$ at the centre. Find the areas of the corresponding minor and major segments of the circle. (Use $\pi = 3.14$ and $\sqrt{3} = 1.732$)
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Given: $r = 15\text{ cm}, \theta = 60^\circ, \pi = 3.14, \sqrt{3} = 1.732$.
  2. Area of Sector: $$\text{Area of Sector} = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 3.14 \times (15)^2 = \frac{1}{6} \times 3.14 \times 225 = 117.75\text{ cm}^2$$
  3. Area of Equilateral $\Delta OAB$: $$\text{Area}(\Delta OAB) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} r^2 = \frac{1.732}{4} \times 225 = 0.433 \times 225 = 97.3125\text{ cm}^2$$
  4. Area of Minor Segment: $$\text{Area} = 117.75 - 97.3125 = 20.4375\text{ cm}^2$$
  5. Area of Major Segment:

    $\text{Area of Circle} = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 225 = 706.5\text{ cm}^2$.

    $$\text{Area of Major Segment} = \text{Area of Circle} - \text{Area of Minor Segment}$$ $$= 706.5 - 20.4375 = 686.0625\text{ cm}^2$$
  6. Conclusion:
    Minor segment area = $20.44\text{ cm}^2$
    Major segment area = $686.06\text{ cm}^2$

Question 7 & 8
(i) A chord of a circle of radius $12\text{ cm}$ subtends an angle of $120^\circ$ at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding segment. ($\pi = 3.14, \sqrt{3} = 1.73$)
(ii) A horse is tied to a peg at one corner of a $15\text{ m}$ square grass field by a $5\text{ m}$ long rope. Find (a) grazing area, (b) increase in grazing area if rope were $10\text{ m}$ long.
[5 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solutions:

  1. Solution to Question 7:
    • $r = 12\text{ cm}, \theta = 120^\circ$.
    • $\text{Area of Sector} = \frac{120^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 3.14 \times (12)^2 = \frac{1}{3} \times 3.14 \times 144 = 48 \times 3.14 = 150.72\text{ cm}^2$.
    • Draw $OD \perp AB$. In $\Delta ODA$, $\angle AOD = 60^\circ \implies AD = r \sin 60^\circ = 12 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 6\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}$.
      $AB = 2AD = 12\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}$. Height $OD = r \cos 60^\circ = 12 \times \frac{1}{2} = 6\text{ cm}$.
    • $\text{Area}(\Delta OAB) = \frac{1}{2} \times AB \times OD = \frac{1}{2} \times 12\sqrt{3} \times 6 = 36\sqrt{3} = 36 \times 1.73 = 62.28\text{ cm}^2$.
    • $\text{Area of Segment} = 150.72 - 62.28 = 88.44\text{ cm}^2$.
  2. Solution to Question 8:
    • Peg is at corner of square field, so sector angle $\theta = 90^\circ$.
    • (a) With rope $r_1 = 5\text{ m}$: $$\text{Area}_1 = \frac{1}{4} \pi r_1^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times 3.14 \times 25 = \frac{78.5}{4} = 19.625\text{ m}^2$$
    • (b) With rope $r_2 = 10\text{ m}$: $$\text{Area}_2 = \frac{1}{4} \pi r_2^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times 3.14 \times 100 = \frac{314}{4} = 78.5\text{ m}^2$$ $$\text{Increase in grazing area} = 78.5 - 19.625 = 58.875\text{ m}^2$$

Step-by-step Solutions:

    • Length of 5 diameters $= 5 \times 35 = 175\text{ mm}$.
    • (i) Total wire length $= 110 + 175 = 285\text{ mm}$.
    • (ii) Area of each of 10 equal sectors $= \frac{1}{10} \times \pi r^2 = \frac{1}{10} \times \frac{22}{7} \times \left(\frac{35}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{385}{4}\text{ mm}^2 = 96.25\text{ mm}^2$.
  2. Q10: Umbrella Ribs:

    8 ribs form 8 equal sectors: Sector area $= \frac{1}{8} \times \frac{22}{7} \times (45)^2 = \frac{22275}{28}\text{ cm}^2 = 795.54\text{ cm}^2$.

  3. Q11: Car Wipers: $$\text{Area} = 2 \times \left[\frac{115^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \frac{22}{7} \times (25)^2\right] = 2 \times \frac{23}{72} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 625 = \frac{158125}{126}\text{ cm}^2 = 1254.96\text{ cm}^2$$
  4. Q12: Lighthouse Warning: $$\text{Area} = \frac{80^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 3.14 \times (16.5)^2 = \frac{2}{9} \times 3.14 \times 272.25 = 189.97\text{ km}^2$$
  5. Q13: Round Table Cover:

    6 equal designs $\implies \theta = \frac{360^\circ}{6} = 60^\circ$ each. Triangle is equilateral.

    $$\text{Area of 1 sector} = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 28^2 = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 784 = 410.67\text{ cm}^2$$ $$\text{Area of 1 triangle} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 28^2 = \frac{1.7}{4} \times 784 = 333.2\text{ cm}^2$$ $$\text{Area of 1 design} = 410.67 - 333.2 = 77.47\text{ cm}^2 \implies \text{Total area (6 designs)} = 6 \times 77.47 = 464.8\text{ cm}^2$$ $$\text{Total Cost} = 464.8 \times 0.35 = ₹162.68$$
  6. Q14: Sector Formula MCQ: $$\text{Area} = \frac{p}{360^\circ} \times \pi R^2 = \frac{p}{720^\circ} \times 2\pi R^2 \quad \text{(Option D)}$$

Case-Study Competency Question
A mechanized agricultural farm uses a central pivot rotary sprinkler that sprays water in a circular sector of radius $28\text{ m}$. The sprinkler sweeps an angle of $135^\circ$ across the crop field.
(i) Calculate the area of the crop field irrigated in one complete sweep.
(ii) Find the length of the outer boundary watered during the sweep.
(iii) If the water flow is expanded to $35\text{ m}$ radius with the same angle, find the increase in irrigated area.

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. (i) Irrigated Sector Area:
    $$\text{Area} = \frac{135^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 28^2 = \frac{3}{8} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 784 = \frac{3}{8} \times 2464 = 924\text{ m}^2.$$
  2. (ii) Length of outer arc boundary:
    $$l = \frac{135^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 28 = \frac{3}{8} \times 176 = 66\text{ m}.$$
  3. (iii) Expanded area with $R = 35\text{ m}$:
    $$\text{Area}_2 = \frac{3}{8} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 35^2 = \frac{3}{8} \times 3850 = 1443.75\text{ m}^2.$$
    $$\text{Increase} = 1443.75 - 924 = 519.75\text{ m}^2.$$
Assertion & Reason HOTS Question
Assertion (A): The area of a sector of a circle of radius $7\text{ cm}$ with central angle $90^\circ$ is $38.5\text{ cm}^2$.
Reason (R): The perimeter of a sector of angle $\theta$ and radius $r$ is given by $\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r$.

CBSE Board Solution:

Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.

Explanation: Assertion (A) evaluates: $\frac{90}{360} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = \frac{1}{4} \times 154 = 38.5\text{ cm}^2$, which is true. However, Reason (R) defines only the arc length, whereas the total perimeter of a sector includes the two bounding radii: $\text{Perimeter} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r + 2r$. Therefore Reason (R) is false.
