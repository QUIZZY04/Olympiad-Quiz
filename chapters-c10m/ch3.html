NCERT Exercise 3.1: Graphical Interpretation & Consistency Criteria
Question 1
On comparing the ratios $\frac{a_1}{a_2}, \frac{b_1}{b_2}$ and $\frac{c_1}{c_2}$, find out whether the lines representing the following pairs of linear equations intersect at a point, are parallel or coincident: (i) $5x - 4y + 8 = 0; 7x + 6y - 9 = 0$, (ii) $9x + 3y + 12 = 0; 18x + 6y + 24 = 0$, (iii) $6x - 3y + 10 = 0; 2x - y + 9 = 0$.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Consistency and Graphical Ratio Rules:

For $a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$ and $a_2x + b_2y + c_2 = 0$:

  • $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2} \implies$ Intersecting lines at exactly one point (Unique solution, Consistent).
  • $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2} \implies$ Coincident lines (Infinitely many solutions, Consistent and dependent).
  • $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2} \implies$ Parallel lines (No solution, Inconsistent).

(i) $5x - 4y + 8 = 0$ and $7x + 6y - 9 = 0$:

$$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{5}{7}, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{-4}{6} = -\frac{2}{3}$$

Since $\frac{5}{7} \neq -\frac{2}{3}$, $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$. The lines intersect at a point.

(ii) $9x + 3y + 12 = 0$ and $18x + 6y + 24 = 0$:

$$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{9}{18} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{12}{24} = \frac{1}{2}$$

Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{1}{2}$, the lines are coincident.

(iii) $6x - 3y + 10 = 0$ and $2x - y + 9 = 0$:

$$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{-3}{-1} = 3, \quad \frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{10}{9}$$

Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2}$, the lines are parallel.

NCERT Exercise 3.2: Algebraic Methods (Substitution & Elimination)
Question 2
Solve the following pairs of linear equations by the substitution method: (i) $x + y = 14; x - y = 4$, (ii) $s - t = 3; \frac{s}{3} + \frac{t}{2} = 6$.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

(i) Solve $x + y = 14$ and $x - y = 4$ by Substitution:

  1. From equation (2): $x = y + 4$.
  2. Substitute into equation (1):
    $$(y + 4) + y = 14 \implies 2y + 4 = 14 \implies 2y = 10 \implies y = 5$$
  3. Then $x = 5 + 4 = 9$.
  4. Solution: $x = 9, y = 5$.

(ii) Solve $s - t = 3$ and $\frac{s}{3} + \frac{t}{2} = 6$:

  1. From first equation: $s = t + 3$.
  2. Multiply second equation by 6 to clear fractions:
    $$2s + 3t = 36$$
  3. Substitute $s = t + 3$:
    $$2(t + 3) + 3t = 36 \implies 2t + 6 + 3t = 36 \implies 5t = 30 \implies t = 6$$
  4. Then $s = 6 + 3 = 9$.
  5. Solution: $s = 9, t = 6$.

Question 3
Solve $2x + 3y = 11$ and $2x - 4y = -24$ and hence find the value of '$m$' for which $y = mx + 3$.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Solve the System of Equations

$$2x + 3y = 11 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$ $$2x - 4y = -24 \quad \text{--- (2)}$$

Subtract equation (2) from equation (1):

$$(2x + 3y) - (2x - 4y) = 11 - (-24)$$ $$7y = 35 \implies y = 5$$

Substitute $y = 5$ into equation (1):

$$2x + 3(5) = 11 \implies 2x + 15 = 11 \implies 2x = -4 \implies x = -2$$

Step 2: Find the value of $m$

We are given that $y = mx + 3$. Substituting $x = -2$ and $y = 5$:

$$5 = m(-2) + 3$$ $$5 - 3 = -2m \implies 2 = -2m \implies m = -1$$

Final Answer: $x = -2$, $y = 5$, and $m = -1$.

Question 4
Solve the following pairs of linear equations by the elimination method: (i) $3x + 4y = 10; 2x - 2y = 2$, (ii) $\frac{x}{2} + \frac{2y}{3} = -1; x - \frac{y}{3} = 3$.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

(i) Solve $3x + 4y = 10$ and $2x - 2y = 2$ by Elimination:

  1. Multiply second equation by 2:
    $$4x - 4y = 4 \quad \text{--- (3)}$$
  2. Add equation (1) and equation (3):
    $$(3x + 4y) + (4x - 4y) = 10 + 4 \implies 7x = 14 \implies x = 2$$
  3. Substitute $x = 2$ into $2x - 2y = 2$:
    $$2(2) - 2y = 2 \implies 4 - 2y = 2 \implies 2y = 2 \implies y = 1$$
  4. Solution: $x = 2, y = 1$.

(ii) Solve $\frac{x}{2} + \frac{2y}{3} = -1$ and $x - \frac{y}{3} = 3$:

  1. Multiply equation (1) by 6: $3x + 4y = -6$.
  2. Multiply equation (2) by 3: $3x - y = 9$.
  3. Subtract equation (2') from equation (1'):
    $$(3x + 4y) - (3x - y) = -6 - 9 \implies 5y = -15 \implies y = -3$$
  4. Substitute $y = -3$ into $3x - (-3) = 9 \implies 3x + 3 = 9 \implies 3x = 6 \implies x = 2$.
  5. Solution: $x = 2, y = -3$.

Question 5
Five years ago, Nuri was thrice as old as Sonu. Ten years later, Nuri will be twice as old as Sonu. How old are Nuri and Sonu?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Define Variables

Let Nuri's present age be $x\text{ years}$ and Sonu's present age be $y\text{ years}$.

Step 2: Condition 1 (Five years ago)

  • Nuri's age $= x - 5$, Sonu's age $= y - 5$.
  • Condition: $x - 5 = 3(y - 5) \implies x - 5 = 3y - 15 \implies x - 3y = -10 \quad \text{--- (1)}$

Step 3: Condition 2 (Ten years later)

  • Nuri's age $= x + 10$, Sonu's age $= y + 10$.
  • Condition: $x + 10 = 2(y + 10) \implies x + 10 = 2y + 20 \implies x - 2y = 10 \quad \text{--- (2)}$

Step 4: Solve the System

Subtract equation (1) from equation (2):

$$(x - 2y) - (x - 3y) = 10 - (-10)$$ $$y = 20$$

Substitute $y = 20$ into equation (2):

$$x - 2(20) = 10 \implies x - 40 = 10 \implies x = 50$$

Final Answer: Nuri's present age is 50 years and Sonu's present age is 20 years.

Question 6
The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Also, nine times this number is twice the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits. Find the number.
[5 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Mathematical Representation of a Two-Digit Number

Let the digit in the tens place be $x$ and the digit in the units place be $y$.

  • Original number $= 10x + y$.
  • Number formed by reversing the digits $= 10y + x$.

Step 2: Set Up Algebraic Equations

  1. Sum of the digits is 9:
    $$x + y = 9 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$
  2. Nine times original number is twice the reversed number:
    $$9(10x + y) = 2(10y + x)$$ $$90x + 9y = 20y + 2x$$ $$90x - 2x + 9y - 20y = 0$$ $$88x - 11y = 0$$ Divide by 11:
    $$8x - y = 0 \implies y = 8x \quad \text{--- (2)}$$

Step 3: Solve the Linear System

Substitute $y = 8x$ from equation (2) into equation (1):

$$x + 8x = 9 \implies 9x = 9 \implies x = 1$$

Substitute $x = 1$ into equation (2):

$$y = 8(1) = 8$$

Step 4: Find the Number & Verify

  • Original number $= 10(1) + 8 = 18$.
  • Verification: Sum of digits $= 1 + 8 = 9$. Reversed number $= 81$.
    $9 \times 18 = 162$, and $2 \times 81 = 162$. Both conditions verified.

Final Answer: The number is 18.

Question 7
If we add 1 to the numerator and subtract 1 from the denominator, a fraction reduces to 1. It becomes $\frac{1}{2}$ if we only add 1 to the denominator. What is the fraction?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Variable Definition

Let the numerator of the fraction be $x$ and the denominator be $y$ ($y \neq 0$). The fraction is $\frac{x}{y}$.

Step 2: Formulating Equations

  1. Condition 1: Adding 1 to numerator and subtracting 1 from denominator reduces fraction to 1:
    $$\frac{x + 1}{y - 1} = 1 \implies x + 1 = y - 1 \implies x - y = -2 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$
  2. Condition 2: Adding 1 only to denominator makes fraction $\frac{1}{2}$:
    $$\frac{x}{y + 1} = \frac{1}{2} \implies 2x = y + 1 \implies 2x - y = 1 \quad \text{--- (2)}$$

Step 3: Solving the Equations

Subtract equation (1) from equation (2):

$$(2x - y) - (x - y) = 1 - (-2) \implies x = 3$$

Substitute $x = 3$ into equation (1):

$$3 - y = -2 \implies y = 5$$

Final Answer: The required fraction is $\frac{3}{5}$.

Graphical Intersection: The two straight lines intersect at $(42, 12)$. Therefore, Aftab's present age is 42 years and his daughter's present age is 12 years.

NCERT Exercise 3.2 • Q5
Half the perimeter of a rectangular garden, whose length is 4 m more than its width, is 36 m. Find the dimensions of the garden.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Let the width (breadth) of the garden be $x$ metres and length be $y$ metres.
  2. According to the question: $y = x + 4 \implies y - x = 4 \quad \text{--- (1)}$
  3. Perimeter of rectangle $= 2(x + y)$. Half perimeter $= x + y = 36 \quad \text{--- (2)}$.
  4. Adding equations (1) and (2): $(y - x) + (x + y) = 4 + 36 \implies 2y = 40 \implies y = 20\text{ m}$
  5. Substituting $y = 20$ in (1): $x = y - 4 = 20 - 4 = 16\text{ m}$

Answer: The length of the garden is $20\text{ metres}$ and its width is $16\text{ metres}$.

NCERT Exercise 3.3 • Q3(iii)
The coach of a cricket team buys 7 bats and 6 balls for ₹ 3800. Later, she buys 3 bats and 5 balls for ₹ 1750. Find the cost of each bat and each ball.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Let cost of one bat be $₹\ x$ and cost of one ball be $₹\ y$.
  2. From the given conditions: $7x + 6y = 3800 \quad \text{--- (1)}$ $3x + 5y = 1750 \quad \text{--- (2)}$
  3. From equation (2), express $x$ in terms of $y$: $3x = 1750 - 5y \implies x = \frac{1750 - 5y}{3} \quad \text{--- (3)}$
  4. Substitute (3) into equation (1): $7\left(\frac{1750 - 5y}{3}\right) + 6y = 3800$ $12250 - 35y + 18y = 11400 \implies -17y = 11400 - 12250 = -850$ $y = \frac{-850}{-17} = 50$
  5. Substitute $y = 50$ into (3): $x = \frac{1750 - 5(50)}{3} = \frac{1750 - 250}{3} = \frac{1500}{3} = 500$

Answer: Cost of each bat is ₹ 500 and cost of each ball is ₹ 50.

The taxi charges in a city consist of a fixed charge together with the charge for the distance covered. NCERT Exercise 3.2 • Q2 & Q3
On comparing the ratios $\frac{a_1}{a_2}, \frac{b_1}{b_2}$ and $\frac{c_1}{c_2}$, find out whether the lines representing the following pairs of linear equations intersect at a point, are parallel or coincident: (i) $5x - 4y + 8 = 0, 7x + 6y - 9 = 0$; (ii) $9x + 3y + 12 = 0, 18x + 6y + 24 = 0$; (iii) $6x - 3y + 10 = 0, 2x - y + 9 = 0$.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

(i) $5x - 4y + 8 = 0$ and $7x + 6y - 9 = 0$:
$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{5}{7}, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{-4}{6} = -\frac{2}{3}$ Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$, the lines intersect at a point (unique solution, consistent).

(ii) $9x + 3y + 12 = 0$ and $18x + 6y + 24 = 0$:
$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{9}{18} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{12}{24} = \frac{1}{2}$ Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}$, the lines are coincident (infinitely many solutions, consistent).

(iii) $6x - 3y + 10 = 0$ and $2x - y + 9 = 0$:
$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{-3}{-1} = 3, \quad \frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{10}{9}$ Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2}$, the lines are parallel (no solution, inconsistent).

Solve $2x + 3y = 11$ and $2x - 4y = -24$ and hence find the value of 'm' for which $y = mx + 3$.

✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Given system: $2x + 3y = 11 \quad \text{--- (1)}$ $2x - 4y = -24 \quad \text{--- (2)}$

Answer: $x = -2, y = 5$, and $\mathbf{m = -1}$.

Case Study Question • Commercial Billing
The taxi charges in a city consist of a fixed charge together with the charge for the distance covered. For a distance of 10 km, the charge paid is Rs 105 and for a journey of 15 km, the charge paid is Rs 155. (i) Formulate linear equations for this situation. (ii) What are the fixed charges and the charge per km? (iii) How much does a person have to pay for travelling a distance of 25 km?

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. (i) Formulation:
    Let fixed charge $= \text{Rs } x$ and charge per km $= \text{Rs } y$.
    For 10 km: $x + 10y = 105 \quad \text{--- (1)}$
    For 15 km: $x + 15y = 155 \quad \text{--- (2)}$
  2. (ii) Solving for rates:
    Subtract (1) from (2): $5y = 50 \implies y = 10$.
    Substitute $y = 10$: $x + 10(10) = 105 \implies x = 5$.
    Fixed charge $= \text{Rs } 5$, Charge per km $= \text{Rs } 10/\text{km}$.
  3. (iii) Cost for 25 km:
    $\text{Total} = x + 25y = 5 + 25(10) = 5 + 250 = \text{Rs } 255$.
