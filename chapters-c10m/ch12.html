NCERT Exercise 12.1: Surface Areas of Combinations of Solids
Question 1
2 cubes each of volume $64\text{ cm}^3$ are joined end to end. Find the surface area of the resulting cuboid.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Finding Edge of Cube:

    Let edge of each cube be $a$.

    $$V = a^3 = 64\text{ cm}^3 \implies a = \sqrt[3]{64} = 4\text{ cm}$$
  2. Dimensions of Resulting Cuboid:

    When two cubes are joined end to end along one face:

    • Length $l = 4 + 4 = 8\text{ cm}$
    • Breadth $b = 4\text{ cm}$
    • Height $h = 4\text{ cm}$
  3. Total Surface Area of Cuboid: $$\text{TSA} = 2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(8 \times 4 + 4 \times 4 + 4 \times 8)$$ $$= 2(32 + 16 + 32) = 2(80) = 160\text{ cm}^2$$
  4. Conclusion: The surface area of the resulting cuboid is $160\text{ cm}^2$.

Question 2
A vessel is in the form of a hollow hemisphere mounted by a hollow cylinder. The diameter of the hemisphere is $14\text{ cm}$ and the total height of the vessel is $13\text{ cm}$. Find the inner surface area of the vessel.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Dimensions:
    • Diameter $d = 14\text{ cm} \implies$ Radius $r = \frac{14}{2} = 7\text{ cm}$.
    • The radius applies to both the hemispherical bowl and cylindrical mount.
    • Depth of hemisphere $= r = 7\text{ cm}$.
    • Height of cylinder $h = \text{Total height} - \text{hemisphere radius} = 13 - 7 = 6\text{ cm}$.
  2. Inner Surface Area of Vessel: $$\text{Inner Area} = \text{CSA of Cylinder} + \text{CSA of Hemisphere}$$ $$= 2\pi rh + 2\pi r^2 = 2\pi r (h + r)$$ $$= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times (6 + 7) = 44 \times 13 = 572\text{ cm}^2$$
  3. Conclusion: The inner surface area of the vessel is $572\text{ cm}^2$.

Question 3
A toy is in the form of a cone of radius $3.5\text{ cm}$ mounted on a hemisphere of same radius. The total height of the toy is $15.5\text{ cm}$. Find the total surface area of the toy.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Dimensions:
    • Radius of cone and hemisphere $r = 3.5\text{ cm} = \frac{7}{2}\text{ cm}$.
    • Height of cone $h = 15.5 - 3.5 = 12\text{ cm}$.
  2. Finding Slant Height ($l$) of Conical Part: $$l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{(3.5)^2 + (12)^2} = \sqrt{12.25 + 144} = \sqrt{156.25} = 12.5\text{ cm}$$
  3. Total Surface Area of Toy:

    Since the circular base is joined internally, the exposed surface consists of the conical curved surface and the hemispherical surface:

    $$\text{TSA} = \text{CSA of Cone} + \text{CSA of Hemisphere} = \pi r l + 2\pi r^2 = \pi r (l + 2r)$$ $$= \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{7}{2} \times (12.5 + 2 \times 3.5) = 11 \times (12.5 + 7) = 11 \times 19.5 = 214.5\text{ cm}^2$$
  4. Conclusion: The total surface area of the toy is $214.5\text{ cm}^2$.

Question 4 & 5
(i) A cubical block of side $7\text{ cm}$ is surmounted by a hemisphere. What is the greatest diameter the hemisphere can have? Find the surface area of the solid.
(ii) A hemispherical depression is cut out from one face of a cubical wooden block of edge $l$ such that diameter of hemisphere is equal to the edge. Determine surface area of remaining solid.
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solutions:

  1. Part (i): Cubical block surmounted by hemisphere:
    • Greatest diameter of hemisphere equals edge of cube $= 7\text{ cm}$. Radius $r = \frac{7}{2} = 3.5\text{ cm}$.
    • $\text{Surface Area} = \text{TSA of Cube} - \text{Base area of hemisphere} + \text{CSA of hemisphere}$ $$= 6a^2 - \pi r^2 + 2\pi r^2 = 6a^2 + \pi r^2$$ $$= 6(7)^2 + \frac{22}{7} \times \left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^2 = 6(49) + \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{49}{4} = 294 + 38.5 = 332.5\text{ cm}^2$$
  2. Part (ii): Hemispherical depression cut out:
    • Edge of cube $= l$. Diameter $= l \implies$ Radius $r = \frac{l}{2}$.
    • $\text{Surface Area} = \text{TSA of Cube} - \text{Base circle area} + \text{Inner curved area of depression}$ $$= 6l^2 - \pi r^2 + 2\pi r^2 = 6l^2 + \pi r^2$$ $$= 6l^2 + \pi \left(\frac{l}{2}\right)^2 = 6l^2 + \frac{\pi l^2}{4} = \frac{l^2}{4} (24 + \pi)\text{ sq. units}$$

Question 6 to 9
Key composite solid problems:
6. Medicine capsule ($L = 14\text{ mm}, d = 5\text{ mm}$): surface area.
7. Tent (cylinder $h = 2.1\text{ m}, d = 4\text{ m}$ surmounted by cone slant height $2.8\text{ m}$): canvas area & cost at ₹500/m².
8. Solid cylinder ($h = 2.4\text{ cm}, d = 1.4\text{ cm}$) with conical cavity hollowed out: remaining total surface area.
9. Wooden cylinder ($h = 10\text{ cm}, r = 3.5\text{ cm}$) scooped with hemispheres: total surface area.
[5 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solutions:

  1. Q6: Capsule Surface Area:

    Radius $r = 2.5\text{ mm}$. Length of cylinder $= 14 - 2(2.5) = 9\text{ mm}$.

    $$\text{TSA} = 2\pi rh + 4\pi r^2 = 2\pi r(h + 2r) = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 2.5 \times (9 + 5) = \frac{110}{7} \times 14 = 220\text{ mm}^2$$
  2. Q7: Tent Canvas:

    Radius $r = 2\text{ m}$. Height $h = 2.1\text{ m}$, slant height $l = 2.8\text{ m}$.

    $$\text{Canvas Area} = \text{CSA of Cylinder} + \text{CSA of Cone} = 2\pi rh + \pi r l = \pi r (2h + l)$$ $$= \frac{22}{7} \times 2 \times (2(2.1) + 2.8) = \frac{44}{7} \times (4.2 + 2.8) = \frac{44}{7} \times 7 = 44\text{ m}^2$$ $$\text{Total Cost} = 44 \times 500 = ₹22,000$$
  3. Q8: Conical Cavity Hollowed Solid:

    $r = 0.7\text{ cm}, h = 2.4\text{ cm}$. Slant height $l = \sqrt{0.7^2 + 2.4^2} = \sqrt{0.49 + 5.76} = \sqrt{6.25} = 2.5\text{ cm}$.

    $$\text{TSA} = \text{CSA of Cylinder} + \text{Base Area of Cylinder} + \text{CSA of Cone}$$ $$= 2\pi rh + \pi r^2 + \pi rl = \pi r(2h + r + l)$$ $$= \frac{22}{7} \times 0.7 \times (2(2.4) + 0.7 + 2.5) = 2.2 \times (4.8 + 3.2) = 2.2 \times 8 = 17.6\text{ cm}^2 \approx 18\text{ cm}^2$$
  4. Q9: Scooped Out Wooden Cylinder: $$\text{TSA} = \text{CSA of Cylinder} + 2 \times (\text{CSA of Hemisphere}) = 2\pi rh + 4\pi r^2 = 2\pi r(h + 2r)$$ $$= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times (10 + 2(3.5)) = 22 \times (10 + 7) = 22 \times 17 = 374\text{ cm}^2$$

NCERT Exercise 12.2: Volumes of Combinations of Solids
Question 1 & 2
(i) A solid is in the shape of a cone standing on a hemisphere with both their radii being equal to $1\text{ cm}$ and the height of the cone is equal to its radius. Find the volume of the solid in terms of $\pi$.
(ii) Rachel, an engineering student, made a model shaped like a cylinder with two cones attached at its two ends. The diameter of the model is $3\text{ cm}$ and its length is $12\text{ cm}$. If each cone has a height of $2\text{ cm}$, find the volume of air contained in the model.
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Solutions:

  1. Part (i): Solid volume in terms of $\pi$:
    • Radius $r = 1\text{ cm}$, cone height $h = 1\text{ cm}$.
    • $\text{Volume} = \text{Volume of Cone} + \text{Volume of Hemisphere}$ $$= \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h + \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{1}{3}\pi (1)^2(1) + \frac{2}{3}\pi (1)^3 = \frac{\pi}{3} + \frac{2\pi}{3} = \frac{3\pi}{3} = \pi\text{ cm}^3$$
    • Volume of the solid is $\pi\text{ cm}^3$.
  2. Part (ii): Model Volume:
    • Diameter $= 3\text{ cm} \implies r = 1.5\text{ cm} = \frac{3}{2}\text{ cm}$.
    • Height of each cone $h_c = 2\text{ cm}$.
    • Height of cylinder $h = 12 - 2(2) = 12 - 4 = 8\text{ cm}$.
    • $\text{Total Volume} = \text{Volume of Cylinder} + 2 \times \text{Volume of Cone}$ $$= \pi r^2 h + 2 \times \left(\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h_c\right) = \pi r^2 \left(h + \frac{2}{3}h_c\right)$$ $$= \frac{22}{7} \times \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 \times \left(8 + \frac{4}{3}\right) = \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{9}{4} \times \frac{28}{3} = \frac{22 \times 9 \times 28}{7 \times 4 \times 3} = 22 \times 3 = 66\text{ cm}^3$$
    • Volume of air contained is $66\text{ cm}^3$.

Question 3 to 5
3. A gulab jamun contains sugar syrup up to about $30\%$ of its volume. Find approximately how much syrup would be found in 45 gulab jamuns, each shaped like a cylinder with two hemispherical ends with length $5\text{ cm}$ and diameter $2.8\text{ cm}$.
4. Pen stand with 4 conical depressions ($15 \times 10 \times 3.5\text{ cm}$, cone $r = 0.5\text{ cm}, h = 1.4\text{ cm}$): volume of wood.
5. Inverted cone vessel ($r = 5\text{ cm}, h = 8\text{ cm}$) filled with water. Spherical lead shots ($r = 0.5\text{ cm}$) dropped in; $1/4$ water flows out. Find number of lead shots.
[5 Marks]
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step-by-step Detailed Solutions:

  1. Q3: Gulab Jamun Syrup:
    • Radius $r = 1.4\text{ cm}$. Cylindrical height $h = 5 - 2(1.4) = 5 - 2.8 = 2.2\text{ cm}$.
    • $\text{Volume of 1 gulab jamun} = \pi r^2 h + \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \pi r^2 \left(h + \frac{4}{3}r\right)$ $$= \frac{22}{7} \times (1.4)^2 \times \left(2.2 + \frac{4}{3}(1.4)\right) = \frac{22}{7} \times 1.96 \times \left(2.2 + 1.87\right) = 6.16 \times 4.07 = 25.07\text{ cm}^3$$
    • Volume of 45 gulab jamuns $= 45 \times 25.07 = 1128.15\text{ cm}^3$.
    • Quantity of syrup ($30\%$) $= 0.30 \times 1128.15 \approx 338\text{ cm}^3$.
  2. Q4: Pen Stand Wood Volume:
    • Cuboid volume $= 15 \times 10 \times 3.5 = 525\text{ cm}^3$.
    • Volume of 4 conical depressions $= 4 \times \left(\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 0.5^2 \times 1.4\right) = 4 \times \left(\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 0.25 \times 1.4\right) = 4 \times 0.3667 = 1.47\text{ cm}^3$.
    • $\text{Remaining Volume} = 525 - 1.47 = 523.53\text{ cm}^3$.
  3. Q5: Number of Lead Shots:
    • Volume of cone $= \frac{1}{3}\pi R^2 H = \frac{1}{3}\pi (5)^2(8) = \frac{200\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.
    • Water overflowed $= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{200\pi}{3} = \frac{50\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.
    • Volume of each lead sphere $= \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (0.5)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (0.125) = \frac{0.5\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.
    • $$\text{Number of lead shots} = \frac{\text{Water displaced}}{\text{Volume of one shot}} = \frac{\frac{50\pi}{3}}{\frac{0.5\pi}{3}} = \frac{50}{0.5} = 100$$

Case-Study Competency Question
An architectural grain storage facility consists of a cylindrical base of diameter $12\text{ m}$ and height $15\text{ m}$, topped by a conical roof of slant height $10\text{ m}$.
(i) Find the height of the conical roof.
(ii) Calculate the total storage volume capacity of the silo. (Take $\pi = 3.14$)
(iii) Determine the cost of painting the external surface area at ₹40 per $\text{m}^2$.

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. (i) Radius $r = 6\text{ m}$. Conical height $h_c = \sqrt{l^2 - r^2} = \sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = \sqrt{64} = 8\text{ m}$.
  2. (ii) Storage Capacity:
    $$V = \pi r^2 h + \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h_c = \pi r^2 \left(h + \frac{h_c}{3}\right) = 3.14 \times 36 \times \left(15 + \frac{8}{3}\right) = 113.04 \times 17.67 = 1997.4\text{ m}^3.$$
  3. (iii) External Surface Area (excluding base on ground):
    $$\text{Area} = 2\pi rh + \pi rl = \pi r (2h + l) = 3.14 \times 6 \times (30 + 10) = 18.84 \times 40 = 753.6\text{ m}^2.$$
    $$\text{Cost} = 753.6 \times 40 = ₹30,144.$$
Assertion & Reason HOTS Question
Assertion (A): When a metallic solid sphere is melted and recast into small cones, the total volume of all the cones equals the volume of the sphere.
Reason (R): During reshaping or recasting from one solid to another, the total mass and volume remain conserved.

CBSE Board Solution:

Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).

Explanation: The fundamental principle of conversion of solids states that matter is neither created nor destroyed during melting and recasting without loss of material; hence Volume(Sphere) = $\sum$ Volume(Cones).
