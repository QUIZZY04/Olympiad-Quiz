Q1
State and prove Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales' Theorem).
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Theorem 6.1 (Basic Proportionality Theorem / Thales' Theorem):

Statement: If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

Proof:

  1. Given: A triangle $ABC$ in which a line parallel to side $BC$ intersects other two sides $AB$ and $AC$ at $D$ and $E$ respectively (i.e., $DE \parallel BC$).
  2. To Prove: $$\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$$
  3. Construction:
    • Join $B$ to $E$ and $C$ to $D$ (Draw line segments $BE$ and $CD$).
    • Draw $DM \perp AC$ and $EN \perp AB$.
  4. Proof Steps:
    • Area of a triangle = $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
    • $\text{ar}(\Delta ADE) = \frac{1}{2} \times AD \times EN$
    • $\text{ar}(\Delta BDE) = \frac{1}{2} \times DB \times EN$
    • Therefore, dividing the two areas:
      $$\frac{\text{ar}(\Delta ADE)}{\text{ar}(\Delta BDE)} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} \times AD \times EN}{\frac{1}{2} \times DB \times EN} = \frac{AD}{DB} \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$$
    • Similarly, taking $AE$ and $EC$ as bases with height $DM$:
      $\text{ar}(\Delta ADE) = \frac{1}{2} \times AE \times DM$
      $\text{ar}(\Delta DEC) = \frac{1}{2} \times EC \times DM$
      $$\frac{\text{ar}(\Delta ADE)}{\text{ar}(\Delta DEC)} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} \times AE \times DM}{\frac{1}{2} \times EC \times DM} = \frac{AE}{EC} \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$$
    • Note that $\Delta BDE$ and $\Delta DEC$ are on the same base $DE$ and between the same parallel lines $DE$ and $BC$.
      Geometric Theorem: Triangles on the same base and between the same parallels are equal in area.
      $$\text{ar}(\Delta BDE) = \text{ar}(\Delta DEC) \quad \text{--- (Equation 3)}$$
    • From Equations (1), (2), and (3), the left-hand sides are equal. Therefore, equating right-hand sides:
      $$\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$$

Hence Proved.

Q2
In triangle ABC, DE || AC and DF || AE. Prove that BF/FE = BE/EC.
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Geometric Rider on Basic Proportionality Theorem:

Given: In $\Delta ABC$, $DE \parallel AC$ and $DF \parallel AE$.

To Prove: $\frac{BF}{FE} = \frac{BE}{EC}$.

Step-by-step Proof:

  1. In $\Delta ABE$:
    We are given that $DF \parallel AE$.
    Applying the Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT) to $\Delta ABE$ with line $DF \parallel AE$:
    $$\frac{BD}{DA} = \frac{BF}{FE} \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$$
  2. In $\Delta ABC$:
    We are given that $DE \parallel AC$.
    Applying the Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT) to $\Delta ABC$ with line $DE \parallel AC$:
    $$\frac{BD}{DA} = \frac{BE}{EC} \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$$
  3. From Equation (1) and Equation (2):
    Both $\frac{BF}{FE}$ and $\frac{BE}{EC}$ are equal to the common ratio $\frac{BD}{DA}$.
    Equating the two expressions:
    $$\frac{BF}{FE} = \frac{BE}{EC}$$

Hence Proved.

Q3
A vertical pole of length 6 m casts a shadow 4 m long on the ground and at the same time a tower casts a shadow 28 m long. Find the height of the tower.
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Board Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Given Information and Mathematical Diagram Model

  • Let $AB$ be the vertical pole of height $6\text{ m}$.
  • Let $BC$ be the shadow cast by the pole on the level ground = $4\text{ m}$.
  • Let $PQ$ be the vertical tower of height $h\text{ m}$.
  • Let $QR$ be the shadow cast by the tower on the ground at the same time = $28\text{ m}$.

Step 2: Proving Similarity of Triangles

Consider $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta PQR$:

  1. $\angle B = \angle Q = 90^\circ$ (Both the pole and the tower stand vertically upright to the horizontal ground).
  2. $\angle C = \angle R$ (Angular elevation of the Sun is the same for all objects in the vicinity at the same time).
  3. Therefore, by AA Similarity Criterion:
    $$\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$$

Step 3: Equating Corresponding Sides

Since corresponding sides of similar triangles are proportional:

$$\frac{AB}{PQ} = \frac{BC}{QR}$$ $$\frac{6}{h} = \frac{4}{28}$$ $$\frac{6}{h} = \frac{1}{7}$$ $$h = 6 \times 7 = 42\text{ metres}$$

Conclusion: The height of the tower is 42 metres.

Competency Real-Life Pythagoras Problem
Question: A ladder 10 m long reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Find the distance of the foot of the ladder from the base of the wall.

Step-by-step Solution:

  1. Let $AB$ be the wall with window at $A$, so $AB = 8 ext{ m}$.
  2. Let $AC$ be the ladder resting against the window, so $AC = 10 ext{ m}$.
  3. Let $BC = x ext{ m}$ be the distance of the foot of the ladder from the base of the wall.
  4. The wall is perpendicular to the ground, so $ngle ABC = 90^\circ$. In right-angled triangle $\Delta ABC$, by Pythagoras Theorem:
    $$AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2$$
    $$10^2 = 8^2 + x^2$$
    $$100 = 64 + x^2 \implies x^2 = 100 - 64 = 36$$
    $$x = \sqrt{36} = 6 ext{ m}$$
  5. Conclusion: The distance of the foot of the ladder from the base of the wall is 6 metres.
Assertion & Reason Assessment
Assertion (A): All congruent figures are similar, but all similar figures need not be congruent.
Reason (R): Two polygons of the same number of sides are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio.

Evaluate whether Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are correct and if (R) explains (A).

CBSE Board Solution:

Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).

Explanation: Congruent figures have the exact same shape and same size (ratio of corresponding sides = 1), satisfying all similarity conditions. Similar figures have the same shape, but their sizes can differ (ratio of sides can be any positive constant $k eq 1$), so they need not be congruent.
