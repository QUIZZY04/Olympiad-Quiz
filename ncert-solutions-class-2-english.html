🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study (CBQ)

The Little Red Hen's Harvest Dilemma

Real-World Context: The Little Red Hen found some golden grains of wheat. She asked the dog, the cat, and the duck: 'Who will help me plant these seeds?' All three replied: 'Not I!' The hen planted, watered, cut the wheat, and baked delicious hot bread with her chicks all by herself. When the aroma spread, the dog, cat, and duck ran over and said: 'We will help you eat it!'

Analytical Task: Did the friends help the hen at any stage of making bread? What did the hen reply when they wanted to eat the bread? What is the moral lesson?
