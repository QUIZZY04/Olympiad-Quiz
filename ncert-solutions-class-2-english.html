The Mystery Box Game

Real-World Context: In the classroom, Rahul had a blindfold over his eyes. The teacher handed him three objects: a fresh rose, a fluffy cotton ball, and a cold metal spoon. Without looking, Rahul identified all three correctly within seconds.

Analytical Task: Which senses did Rahul use to identify the rose, cotton ball, and spoon? What does this activity teach us?

1. Rahul used his sense of smell (fragrance of the rose) and his sense of touch (soft cotton, cold hard metal spoon).
2. This activity teaches us that our other senses (touch, smell, hearing) are powerful tools that help us perceive the world even when we cannot see.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study (CBQ)

The Winter Grasshopper and Ant Contrast

Real-World Context: All through the warm sunny summer, the grasshopper did nothing but play his fiddle, sing merry songs, and lounge on soft green clover. Whenever he saw little ants sweating under heavy kernels of golden corn, he laughed: 'Foolish ants! Why work so hard in the sunshine? Life is meant for fun!'. But when freezing winter arrived, thick snow buried the fields. The grasshopper had not a single crumb or fly to eat, while the ants stayed warm in their underground tunnels with piles of sweet grain.

Analytical Task: How did the grasshopper spend his summer? What were the ants doing? Why did the grasshopper suffer in winter?

1. The grasshopper spent his summer singing, dancing, and mocking the ants without doing any work.
2. The ants were working tirelessly, collecting and storing corn and wheat grains for the cold winter ahead.
3. The grasshopper suffered in winter because he had saved nothing, and thick snow covered all natural food sources, leaving him starving and freezing.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1 M for grasshopper's summer laziness + 1 M for ants' hard work + 1 M for winter consequence
