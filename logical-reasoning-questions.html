Logical Reasoning Questions

Secure an easy 20% of your Olympiad marks by practicing essential mental aptitude and logic patterns.

Q1. If in a certain code, 'APPLE' is written as 'BQQMF', how is 'MANGO' written?

A) NBOHP
B) NCOHP
C) NZOHP
D) NBPGP

Correct Answer: A

Step-by-step Solution: Each letter in 'APPLE' is shifted forward by +1 in the English alphabet (A→B, P→Q, L→M, E→F). Applying the same +1 shift to 'MANGO' gives M→N, A→B, N→O, G→H, O→P, which forms NBOHP.

Q2. Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "I have no brother or sister but that man's father is my father's son." Whose photograph was it?

A) His own
B) His son's
C) His father's
D) His nephew's

Correct Answer: B

Step-by-step Solution: Since the speaker has no siblings, "my father's son" refers to the speaker himself. So "that man's father is myself" — meaning the photograph shows his son.

Q3. Find the missing number in the series: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?

A) 46
B) 48
C) 50
D) 52

Correct Answer: B

Step-by-step Solution: Look at the differences between consecutive terms: 8 - 3 = 5, 15 - 8 = 7, 24 - 15 = 9, 35 - 24 = 11. The differences increase by 2 each step, so the next difference is 13: 35 + 13 = 48. (Alternatively, each term is n² - 1 starting from n = 2: 7² - 1 = 48.)

Q4. Rohan walks 10 m North, turns right and walks 6 m, then turns right again and walks 10 m. How far and in which direction is he from the starting point?

A) 6 m East
B) 6 m West
C) 10 m East
D) 16 m North

Correct Answer: A

Step-by-step Solution: Walking 10 m North and later 10 m South (after two right turns) cancels the North-South displacement. Only the 6 m eastward walk remains, so Rohan is 6 m East of his starting point.

Q5. In a row of 30 students, Aman is 12th from the left end. What is his rank from the right end?

A) 18th
B) 19th
C) 20th
D) 21st

Correct Answer: B

Step-by-step Solution: Use the ranking formula Rank from Right = Total - Rank from Left + 1. Substituting the values: 30 - 12 + 1 = 19th from the right end.

Strategy for Olympiad Logical Reasoning Sections

Logical Reasoning makes up 15–25% of SOF IMO, NSO, and IEO papers. Write down alphabet position pairs (A=1 to Z=26) on rough paper at the start of the exam, draw family-tree or direction diagrams rather than solving mentally, and verify ranking formulas with the +1 adjustment.
