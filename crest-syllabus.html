Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is there negative marking in these Olympiads?

Generally, preliminary levels of Olympiads like CREST do not have negative marking for Class 1 to 10. However, always verify with the official guidelines for specific advanced levels.

What is the Achievers Section?

The Achievers Section contains Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions. These are more complex and carry a higher weightage (usually 2 marks per question in CREST), making them crucial for securing top ranks.
