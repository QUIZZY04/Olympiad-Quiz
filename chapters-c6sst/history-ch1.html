Chapter 1: What, Where, How and When?

NCERT Our Pasts - I (Class 6) — Finding out about the past: River valleys (Narmada, Indus, Ganga), Sulaiman and Kirthar hills, Garo hills, Inscriptions, Manuscripts on palm leaf and birch bark, Archaeology, BCE and CE notation. | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Early settlement along river valleys: Narmada (hunter-gatherers), Indus & tributaries (earliest cities), Ganga & Son (Magadha kingdom).
  • Agriculture origins: Sulaiman and Kirthar hills (wheat and barley ~8000 years ago), Garo hills, Vindhyas (rice).
  • Sources of History: Manuscripts (handwritten on palm leaf/birch bark) and Inscriptions (engraved on stone or metal).
  • Archaeology: Study of material remains (tools, weapons, pots, coins, bones).
  • Dating: BCE (Before Common Era) and CE (Common Era).
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Match the following: (a) Narmada Valley (b) Magadha (c) Garo hills (d) Indus and its tributaries (e) Ganga valley.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Historical Geographies:
• (a) Narmada Valley: Hunting and gathering.
• (b) Magadha: The first big kingdom.
• (c) Garo hills: Early agriculture.
• (d) Indus and its tributaries: The first cities (~4700 years ago).
• (e) Ganga valley: Cities about 2500 years ago.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Correct matches for (a), (b), (c)1.5 Marks
Correct matches for (d) and (e)1.5 Marks
Q2
List one major difference between manuscripts and inscriptions. Why did ordinary people not leave written records?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Primary Historical Sources:
• Manuscripts vs Inscriptions: Manuscripts were handwritten on delicate, perishable organic materials like palm leaves or the bark of birch trees (Bhurja). Inscriptions were chiselled onto relatively indestructible, hard surfaces such as rock faces, stone pillars, or metal plates.
• Absence of Ordinary Records: Ordinary people (hunters, farmers, herders) did not keep records because most were illiterate, had no access to scribes, and were preoccupied with daily subsistence, while kings commissioned official panegyrics to celebrate their military victories and decrees.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Contrast of writing material durability1.5 Marks
Sociological explanation for ordinary citizens1.5 Marks
CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Archaeological Epigraphy: Rosetta Stone Decipherment:
Scholars decipher ancient Egyptian hieroglyphics using the Rosetta Stone inscribed in three scripts. (a) What is decipherment in epigraphy? (b) Why are bilingual inscriptions indispensable for unlocking forgotten scripts?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Decipherment: The scholarly process of identifying phonetic values and grammatical meanings of unknown ancient glyphs by systematic comparison with known languages.
(b) Indispensability: A bilingual text provides a linguistic key: by matching royal names (cartouches like Ptolemy and Cleopatra) across Greek and Egyptian scripts, researchers can cross-correlate sounds to symbols.
