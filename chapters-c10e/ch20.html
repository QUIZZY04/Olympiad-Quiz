Chapter 1: A Triumph of Surgery

James Herriot — Pampered Affection vs Sensible Care — CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27

I. Textbook Exercise Solutions & Important Questions — CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27
Q1
Why is Mrs Pumphrey worried about Tricki? What symptoms does Tricki exhibit?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Mrs Pumphrey is frantic with worry because her pampered little dog Tricki has become listless, bloated, and utterly inactive. He looks like a bloated sausage with a leg at each corner; his eyes are bloodshot and rheumy, his tongue lolls from his jaws, and he refuses to eat even his favorite delicacies, having frequent bouts of vomiting while lying down panting on a rug.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Physical appearance: Bloated sausage, bloodshot and rheumy eyes1½ Marks
Behavioral symptoms: Complete listlessness, refusing food, vomiting, panting1½ Marks
Q2
What mistakes did Mrs Pumphrey make in feeding Tricki? Did she follow Dr. Herriot's advice?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Assuming Tricki was suffering from malnutrition, Mrs Pumphrey overindulged him by feeding him extra malt, cod-liver oil, and bowls of Horlicks between meals, along with cream cakes and chocolates. She completely ignored Dr. Herriot's strict warnings to cut down on sweets, give him regular exercise, and keep him on a strict diet, thereby causing his severe illness through excessive pampering.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Overfeeding: Malt, cod-liver oil, Horlicks, cream cakes, chocolates1½ Marks
Failure to follow doctor's advice on strict diet and exercise1½ Marks
Q3
How does Dr. Herriot treat Tricki at his surgery? Did he administer any medicine or surgery?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Dr. Herriot administered zero medication and performed no surgery whatsoever. His treatment consisted of a strictly sensible natural regimen:

  • For the first two days, Tricki was given no food at all, only plenty of fresh water.
  • On the third day, Tricki showed interest in his surroundings and whined at the door.
  • He was introduced into the pack of surgery dogs where he jostled for food, ran across the yard, and wrestled, rapidly shedding fat and regaining muscular health.
CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Zero medicines and zero surgery administered1 Mark
Fasting on water for two days to detoxify digestive system1 Mark
Vigorous natural exercise playing with other dogs in the pack1 Mark
Q4
Why was Dr. Herriot tempted to keep Tricki on as a permanent guest at the surgery?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Dr. Herriot and his staff were tempted to retain Tricki because Mrs Pumphrey sent lavish supplies of fresh eggs (two dozen at a time), bottles of fine wine to enrich Tricki's blood, and bottles of brandy. The doctor and his assistants enjoyed royal breakfasts with eggs, leisurely lunches with wine, and luxurious evenings by the fire with brandy, all at Mrs Pumphrey's expense!

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Mrs Pumphrey sent lavish food items: 2 dozen fresh eggs, wine, and brandy1 Mark
Doctor and staff enjoyed luxurious meals every day1 Mark
Q5
Why does Mrs Pumphrey proclaim Tricki's recovery as 'a triumph of surgery'? Contrast Mrs Pumphrey's suffocating indulgence with Dr. Herriot's pragmatic common sense.
6 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Mrs Pumphrey's Exclamation:
When Mrs Pumphrey arrives to collect Tricki after a fortnight, she is astonished to see him transformed into a lithe, hard-muscled, energetic animal who leaps into her lap licking her face. Overcome with emotional tears, she proclaims: 'Oh, Mr Herriot, how can I ever thank you? This is a triumph of surgery!' In her aristocratic innocence, she assumes an extraordinary surgical marvel cured him.

2. Contrast in Attitudes:

  • Mrs Pumphrey (Blind Affection): Represents toxic overindulgence where love is equated with material luxury and overfeeding. Her inability to discipline Tricki nearly killed him through obesity and lethargy.
  • Dr. Herriot (Pragmatic Professionalism): Represents medical integrity, common sense, and psychological insight. He recognized that Tricki's only ailment was overfeeding and lack of exercise. By enforcing natural fasting and socialization rather than prescribing unnecessary drugs, he cured the dog painlessly.
CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Mrs Pumphrey's reaction: Believed a miraculous surgical intervention cured him2 Marks
Characterization of Mrs Pumphrey: Smothering love causing physical harm2 Marks
Characterization of Dr. Herriot: Professional pragmatism, common sense medicine2 Marks
Q6
How did the pack of hospital dogs at Dr. Herriot's surgery initially receive Tricki, and how did Tricki become an accepted member of the gang?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

When Tricki arrived at the surgery, the energetic pack rushed around him. Tricki looked down with dull eyes and lay motionless on the rug. After sniffing around him for a few seconds, the dogs decided he was an 'uninteresting object' and ignored him. However, by the third day, after fasting on plenty of water and recovering his energy, Tricki began to whimpering at the door, joined the bowl-licking scrambles, and was enthusiastically accepted into the rough-and-tumble gang, hunting rats in the hen-house by night like a wild, happy canine.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Initial indifference: Sniffed and dismissed as 'uninteresting object'1½ Marks
Assimilation: Joining bowl runs, hunting rats, playing with the pack1½ Marks
🎯 Competency-Based & Value-Based Questions — Unit 20CBSE 2026-27
Value-Based Application
Analysis: How is 'A Triumph of Surgery' a timeless warning to parents who overindulge their children with junk food and luxury without physical activity?

The story is an allegory for modern parental overindulgence. Wealthy parents often shower children with excessive fast food, gadgets, and sedentary luxury under the mistaken belief of providing affection. Like Tricki, children subjected to undisciplined pampering develop health complications and lethargy. True love involves setting healthy boundaries, teaching moderation, and ensuring physical activity rather than gratifying every fleeting desire.
