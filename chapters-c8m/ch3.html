Chapter 3: A Story of Numbers

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 1) — Evolution of Counting, Tally Marks, Ancient Numeral Systems, Place Value & Base Conversions

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Evolution of Numbers: Early humans tallied marks. Additive systems (Egyptian, Roman) gave way to the Indian decimal place-value system.
  • Indian Decimal System: The concept of zero (Shunya) as both placeholder and number allowed infinite values with only 10 symbols (0–9).
  • Base Systems: A base-b system uses digits 0 to b − 1. A number (dₖ...d₀)ᵦ = dₖ·bᵏ + ... + d₀·b⁰.

Q1
Convert decimal 45 into: (a) Binary (Base 2) (b) Quinary (Base 5) (c) Octal (Base 8).
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Binary: 45 ÷ 2 repeatedly gives remainders 1, 0, 1, 1, 0, 1. Read bottom to top: 101101₂.
(b) Quinary (Base 5): 45 ÷ 5 = 9 R 0; 9 ÷ 5 = 1 R 4; 1 ÷ 5 = 0 R 1 ⟹ 140₅.
(c) Octal (Base 8): 45 ÷ 8 = 5 R 5; 5 ÷ 8 = 0 R 5 ⟹ 55₈.
Suggested Marks Distribution
Binary 101101₂1 Mark
Base 5: 140₅1 Mark
Base 8: 55₈1 Mark

Q3
If 21y5 is a multiple of 9, where y is a single digit, what is the value of y?
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is a multiple of 9.
Sum of digits of 21y5 = 2 + 1 + y + 5 = 8 + y.
Since y is a digit (0 to 9), 8 + y must equal 9 or 18. If 8 + y = 9 ⟹ y = 1. (8 + y = 18 gives y = 10, not a single digit).
Suggested Marks Distribution
Stating divisibility rule for 91 Mark
Solving 8 + y = 9 to get y = 11 Mark
Q4
If 31z5 is a multiple of 3, where z is a digit, what might be the values of z?
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.
Sum of digits = 3 + 1 + z + 5 = 9 + z.
Since 9 is divisible by 3, z must be a single-digit multiple of 3. Therefore, possible values of z are 0, 3, 6, or 9.
Suggested Marks Distribution
Sum of digits expression 9 + z1 Mark
Listing all 4 valid digit solutions: 0, 3, 6, 91 Mark
Q5
Check the divisibility of 21436587 by 9 and by 11 using divisibility rules without actual long division.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Divisibility by 9: Sum of digits = 2 + 1 + 4 + 3 + 6 + 5 + 8 + 7 = 36. Since 36 is divisible by 9 (36 ÷ 9 = 4), 21436587 is divisible by 9.
Divisibility by 11: Sum of digits in odd places (from right) = 7 + 5 + 3 + 1 = 16. Sum of digits in even places (from right) = 8 + 6 + 4 + 2 = 20.
Difference = 20 − 16 = 4. Since 4 is neither 0 nor a multiple of 11, the number is not divisible by 11.
Suggested Marks Distribution
Sum of digits = 36; concluding divisible by 91.5 Marks
Alternating sum test difference = 4; concluding not divisible by 111.5 Marks
Q6
Find the values of letters in the addition cryptarithm:
  4 A
+ 9 8
------
C B 3
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
In units column: A + 8 produces 3. Since A is a digit, A + 8 = 13 ⟹ A = 13 − 8 = 5 with a carry-over of 1.
In tens column: 1 (carry) + 4 + 9 = 14. This gives B = 4 and carry-over of 1 to hundreds column, giving C = 1.
Verification: 45 + 98 = 143. Thus, A = 5, B = 4, C = 1.
Suggested Marks Distribution
Units column deduction: A = 51 Mark
Tens and hundreds column deduction: B = 4, C = 11 Mark
Complete check 45 + 98 = 1431 Mark
