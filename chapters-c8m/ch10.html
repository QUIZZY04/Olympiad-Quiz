📌 Laws of Exponents & Standard Form
  • Laws of Exponents (for non-zero integers a, b and integers m, n):
    • aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ (Product rule)
    • aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ (Quotient rule)
    • (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ (Power of a power)
    • aᵐ × bᵐ = (ab)ᵐ (Power of a product)
    • aᵐ/bᵐ = (a/b)ᵐ (Power of a quotient)
    • a⁰ = 1 (Zero exponent)
    • a⁻ᵐ = 1/aᵐ and (a/b)⁻ᵐ = (b/a)ᵐ (Negative exponent)
  • Standard Scientific Notation: A number expressed in the form k × 10ⁿ, where 1.0 ≤ k < 10.0 and n ∈ ℤ.

Evaluate:
(i) 3⁻²    (ii) (−4)⁻²    (iii) (1/2)⁻⁵
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

Formula: a⁻ᵐ = 1/aᵐ.

(i) 3⁻² = 1/3² = 1/9.
(ii) (−4)⁻² = 1/(−4)² = 1/16 = 1/16.
(iii) (1/2)⁻⁵ = (2/1)⁵ = 2⁵ = 32.

Q2
Simplify and express the result in power notation with positive exponent:
(i) (−4)⁵ ÷ (−4)⁸
(ii) (1/2³)²
(iii) (−3)⁴ × (5/3)⁴
(iv) (3⁻⁷ ÷ 3⁻¹⁰) × 3⁻⁵
(v) 2⁻³ × (−7)⁻³
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) (−4)⁵ ÷ (−4)⁸ = (−4)⁵⁻⁸ = (−4)⁻³ = 1/(−4)³.
(ii) (1/2³)² = 1²/(2³)² = 1/2⁶.
(iii) (−3)⁴ × 5⁴/3⁴ = 3⁴ × 5⁴/3⁴ = 5⁴.
(iv) (3⁻⁷ ÷ 3⁻¹⁰) × 3⁻⁵ = 3⁻⁷⁻⁽⁻¹⁰⁾ × 3⁻⁵ = 3³ × 3⁻⁵ = 3³⁻⁵ = 3⁻² = 1/3².
(v) 2⁻³ × (−7)⁻³ = [2 × (−7)]⁻³ = (−14)⁻³ = 1/(−14)³.

Q5
Simplify:
(i) (25 × t⁻⁴)/(5⁻³ × 10 × t⁻⁸) (t ≠ 0)
(ii) (3⁻⁵ × 10⁻⁵ × 125)/(5⁻⁷ × 6⁻⁵)
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Write numbers in powers of prime 5:
= (5² × t⁻⁴)/(5⁻³ × (2 × 5) × t⁻⁸) = (5² × t⁻⁴)/(5⁻³⁺¹ × 2 × t⁻⁸) = (5² × t⁻⁴)/(5⁻² × 2 × t⁻⁸)
= (5²⁻⁽⁻²⁾ × t⁻⁴⁻⁽⁻⁸⁾)/2 = 5⁴ t⁴/2 = 625t⁴/2.
(ii) Factorize composite numbers into primes (10 = 2 × 5, 6 = 2 × 3, 125 = 5³):
= (3⁻⁵ × (2 × 5)⁻⁵ × 5³)/(5⁻⁷ × (2 × 3)⁻⁵) = (3⁻⁵ × 2⁻⁵ × 5⁻⁵ × 5³)/(5⁻⁷ × 2⁻⁵ × 3⁻⁵)
Cancel identical factors 3⁻⁵ and 2⁻⁵:
= 5⁻⁵⁺³/5⁻⁷ = 5⁻²/5⁻⁷ = 5⁻²⁻⁽⁻⁷⁾ = 5⁵ = 3125.

Q7
In a stack there are 5 books each of thickness 20 mm and 5 paper sheets each of thickness 0.016 mm. What is the total thickness of the stack? Express in standard form.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Thickness of 5 books = 5 × 20 mm = 100 mm.
Thickness of 5 paper sheets = 5 × 0.016 mm = 0.08 mm.
Total thickness = 100 + 0.08 = 100.08 mm.
Convert to standard scientific form:
100.08 = 1.0008 × 10² mm.

Case Study: Astronomy & Distance in Space
Scenario: The distance between the Sun and Earth is approximately 1.496 × 10¹¹ metres, and the distance between Earth and the Moon is 3.84 × 10⁸ metres. During a solar eclipse, the Moon comes directly between the Earth and the Sun. Find the distance between the Sun and the Moon in scientific form.

Distance = Distance(Sun-Earth) − Distance(Earth-Moon):
= 1.496 × 10¹¹ − 3.84 × 10⁸

Convert to common power of 10 (10¹¹):
3.84 × 10⁸ = 0.00384 × 10¹¹.

= (1.496 − 0.00384) × 10¹¹ = 1.49216 × 10¹¹ metres.

⚡ Power Comparison (HOTS)
Which is larger: 2³⁰⁰ or 3²⁰⁰? Justify using laws of exponents without evaluating the huge numbers.

Find the HCF of the exponents 300 and 200: HCF(300, 200) = 100.

Express both with common outer exponent 100:
2³⁰⁰ = (2³)¹⁰⁰ = 8¹⁰⁰
3²⁰⁰ = (3²)¹⁰⁰ = 9¹⁰⁰

Since 9 > 8, it follows that 9¹⁰⁰ > 8¹⁰⁰.
Therefore, 3²⁰⁰ is larger.
