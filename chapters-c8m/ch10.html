Chapter 10: Proportional Reasoning - 2

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Inverse Proportion, Compound Rates, Speed-Time Relationships & Work Problems

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Inverse Proportion: Two quantities x and y vary inversely if their product is constant: x · y = k. When x increases, y decreases proportionally: x₁ y₁ = x₂ y₂.
  • Work and Time: If a person completes a job in d days, their work rate per day is 1/d. More workers require inversely fewer days.
  • Speed and Time: For a fixed distance, speed and time are inversely proportional: Speed × Time = Distance.
OlympiadQuiz Chapter Practice Questions & Concept Applications
Q1
A team of 15 workers can construct a boundary wall in 48 hours. How many workers would be needed to complete the same job in 30 hours?
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Workers (w) and time (t) are in inverse proportion: w₁ × t₁ = w₂ × t₂.
15 × 48 = w₂ × 30 ⟹ 720 = 30 w₂ ⟹ w₂ = 720 / 30 = 24 workers.
Answer: 24 workers are needed.

Q2
A car traveling at 60 km/h takes 2 hours to reach its destination. How fast must it travel to reach the destination in 1.5 hours (1 hour 30 min)?
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Speed × Time = Distance (constant) ⟹ s₁ × t₁ = s₂ × t₂.
60 × 2 = s₂ × 1.5 ⟹ 120 = 1.5 s₂ ⟹ s₂ = 120 / 1.5 = 80 km/h.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Hostel Ration Supplies: A school hostel has food provisions sufficient for 120 students for 25 days. If 30 new students join the hostel, how many days will the provisions last?
Students and days are inversely proportional: S₁ × D₁ = S₂ × D₂.
Total students S₂ = 120 + 30 = 150.
120 × 25 = 150 × D₂ ⟹ 3000 = 150 D₂ ⟹ D₂ = 3000 / 150 = 20 days.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): The graph of inverse proportion y = k/x is a rectangular hyperbola that never touches the axes.
Reason (R): Neither x nor y can be zero because their product xy = k is a non-zero constant.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
