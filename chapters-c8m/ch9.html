Chapter 9: The Baudhāyana - Pythagoras Theorem

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Baudhāyana Shulba Sutra Theorem, Geometric Dissections, Hypotenuse Properties & Applications

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Historical Context: Baudhāyana in the Shulba Sutras (c. 800 BCE) stated: "The diagonal of a rectangle produces both areas which its length and breadth produce separately." This is the geometric equivalent of Pythagoras' theorem.
  • Theorem Statement: In any right-angled triangle, the area of the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the areas of the squares on the other two legs: c² = a² + b².
  • Geometric Dissection Proofs: Four identical right triangles of legs a and b arranged inside a square of side (a + b) leave an inner square of side c: (a + b)² = 4(½ ab) + c² ⟹ c² = a² + b².

Q1
A ladder of length 15 m reaches a window 12 m above the ground when placed against a wall. How far is the foot of the ladder from the wall?
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
In right triangle: Hypotenuse (ladder) c = 15 m, Vertical height a = 12 m, Distance from wall = b.
By Baudhāyana-Pythagoras theorem: a² + b² = c² ⟹ 12² + b² = 15²
144 + b² = 225 ⟹ b² = 225 − 144 = 81 ⟹ b = √81 = 9 m.
Answer: The foot of the ladder is 9 meters from the wall.

Shortest distance d = √(24² + 10²) = √(576 + 100) = √676 = 26 meters.

Q3
The hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle is 25 cm and one of the other sides is 7 cm. Find the third side and the area of the triangle.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
By Baudhāyana-Pythagoras theorem: Hypotenuse² = Base² + Altitude².
25² = 7² + b² ⟹ 625 = 49 + b² ⟹ b² = 625 − 49 = 576.
Third side b = √576 = 24 cm.
Area of triangle = (1/2) × base × height = (1/2) × 7 × 24 = 84 cm².

Q4
A 13 m long ladder reaches a window of a building 12 m above the ground. Determine the distance of the foot of the ladder from the base of the wall.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
The ladder, wall, and ground form a right-angled triangle with hypotenuse = 13 m and height = 12 m.
Distance d² + 12² = 13² ⟹ d² + 144 = 169 ⟹ d² = 25.
Distance d = √25 = 5 meters.

Q5
A tree is broken at a height of 5 m from the ground and its top touches the ground at a distance of 12 m from the base of the tree. Find the original total height of the tree.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
The standing trunk (5 m), ground distance (12 m), and broken top form a right triangle.
Broken part length² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169.
Broken top length = √169 = 13 m.
Original height of tree = standing trunk + broken part = 5 m + 13 m = 18 meters.

Q6
Two vertical poles of heights 6 m and 11 m stand on level ground. If the distance between their feet is 12 m, find the distance between their tops.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Difference in pole heights = 11 m − 6 m = 5 m.
Horizontal distance between tops = distance between feet = 12 m.
Distance between tops d forms the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 5 m and 12 m.
d² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 ⟹ d = √169 = 13 meters.

Q7
The sides of a triangle are 15 cm, 20 cm, and 25 cm. Verify whether it forms a right-angled triangle using the Baudhāyana-Pythagoras converse.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Check if sum of squares of two smaller sides equals square of the longest side.
15² + 20² = 225 + 400 = 625.
Longest side² = 25² = 625.
Since 15² + 20² = 25² (625 = 625), by the converse of Baudhāyana-Pythagoras theorem, this is a right-angled triangle.

Q8
In right triangle ABC right-angled at B, AB = 6 cm and BC = 8 cm. Find hypotenuse AC. Also find the length of the perpendicular altitude BD drawn from B onto AC.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
By Baudhāyana theorem: AC² = AB² + BC² = 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100 ⟹ AC = 10 cm.
Area of △ABC = (1/2) × AB × BC = (1/2) × 6 × 8 = 24 cm².
Also Area = (1/2) × AC × BD ⟹ 24 = (1/2) × 10 × BD ⟹ 5 × BD = 24.
BD = 24 / 5 = 4.8 cm.

Diagonal Bracing in Construction: A rectangular gate of width 2.4 meters and height 1.8 meters needs a diagonal metal support strut to prevent sagging.
(a) Find the required length of the diagonal strut.
(b) If metal struts cost ₹450 per meter, find the cost of the strut.
(a) Diagonal = √(2.4² + 1.8²) = √(5.76 + 3.24) = √9.0 = 3.0 meters.
(b) Cost = 3.0 × ₹450 = ₹1,350.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): A triangle with sides 7 cm, 24 cm, and 25 cm is a right-angled triangle.
Reason (R): By the converse of Baudhāyana-Pythagoras theorem, if a² + b² = c², the angle opposite to side c is 90°.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
Explanation: 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25².
