Chapter 9: The Baudhāyana - Pythagoras Theorem

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Baudhāyana Shulba Sutra Theorem, Geometric Dissections, Hypotenuse Properties & Applications

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Historical Context: Baudhāyana in the Shulba Sutras (c. 800 BCE) stated: "The diagonal of a rectangle produces both areas which its length and breadth produce separately." This is the geometric equivalent of Pythagoras' theorem.
  • Theorem Statement: In any right-angled triangle, the area of the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the areas of the squares on the other two legs: c² = a² + b².
  • Geometric Dissection Proofs: Four identical right triangles of legs a and b arranged inside a square of side (a + b) leave an inner square of side c: (a + b)² = 4(½ ab) + c² ⟹ c² = a² + b².
OlympiadQuiz Chapter Practice Questions & Concept Applications
Q1
A ladder of length 15 m reaches a window 12 m above the ground when placed against a wall. How far is the foot of the ladder from the wall?
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
In right triangle: Hypotenuse (ladder) c = 15 m, Vertical height a = 12 m, Distance from wall = b.
By Baudhāyana-Pythagoras theorem: a² + b² = c² ⟹ 12² + b² = 15²
144 + b² = 225 ⟹ b² = 225 − 144 = 81 ⟹ b = √81 = 9 m.
Answer: The foot of the ladder is 9 meters from the wall.

Q2
A person walks 24 meters Due East and then 10 meters Due North. What is the shortest distance from the starting point?
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
East and North directions form a right angle (90°).
Shortest distance d = √(24² + 10²) = √(576 + 100) = √676 = 26 meters.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Diagonal Bracing in Construction: A rectangular gate of width 2.4 meters and height 1.8 meters needs a diagonal metal support strut to prevent sagging.
(a) Find the required length of the diagonal strut.
(b) If metal struts cost ₹450 per meter, find the cost of the strut.
(a) Diagonal = √(2.4² + 1.8²) = √(5.76 + 3.24) = √9.0 = 3.0 meters.
(b) Cost = 3.0 × ₹450 = ₹1,350.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): A triangle with sides 7 cm, 24 cm, and 25 cm is a right-angled triangle.
Reason (R): By the converse of Baudhāyana-Pythagoras theorem, if a² + b² = c², the angle opposite to side c is 90°.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
Explanation: 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25².
