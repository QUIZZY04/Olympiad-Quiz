Chapter 2: Power Play

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 1) — Exponents, Paper-Folding Doubling Model, Laws of Indices, Scientific Notation & Unit Digit Cyclicity

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Powers and Exponents: In aⁿ, a is the base and n is the exponent, representing repeated multiplication.
  • Paper-Folding Model: Repeatedly folding a sheet doubles its layers: 1 fold = 2¹ = 2; 2 folds = 2² = 4; n folds = 2ⁿ layers. Exponential growth rapidly surpasses linear increments.
  • Laws of Indices: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ, aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ, (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ, a⁰ = 1, a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ.
  • Scientific Notation: Writing numbers as k × 10ᵐ where 1 ≤ k < 10.
  • Cyclicity of Digits: Powers of 2, 3, 7, 8 have cyclicity 4; powers of 4 and 9 have cyclicity 2.
OlympiadQuiz Chapter Practice Questions & Concept Applications
Q1
A sheet of paper of thickness 0.1 mm is folded in half repeatedly.
(a) Write an exponential expression for the thickness after n folds.
(b) Calculate the thickness (in mm and cm) after 10 folds.
(c) How many folds are required for thickness to exceed 1 meter (1000 mm)?
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Formula: After n folds, layers = 2ⁿ. Thickness = T(n) = 0.1 × 2ⁿ mm.
(b) For n = 10: 2¹⁰ = 1024. Thickness = 0.1 × 1024 = 102.4 mm = 10.24 cm.
(c) Exceeding 1 meter: 0.1 × 2ⁿ > 1000 ⟹ 2ⁿ > 10,000. Since 2¹³ = 8192 and 2¹⁴ = 16384, at least 14 folds are required.
Suggested Marks Distribution

Q2
Simplify using laws of exponents:
(a) (2⁻³ × 5⁻³) ÷ 10⁻⁵
(b) [(1/3)⁻² − (1/2)⁻³] ÷ (1/4)⁻²
(c) (3⁻⁷ ÷ 3⁻¹⁰) × 3⁻⁵
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(a): 2⁻³ × 5⁻³ = (2 × 5)⁻³ = 10⁻³. 10⁻³ ÷ 10⁻⁵ = 10⁻³ ⁺ ⁵ = 10² = 100.
(b): (1/3)⁻² = 9, (1/2)⁻³ = 8. (9 − 8) = 1. Divisor: (1/4)⁻² = 16. Result = 1 ÷ 16 = 1/16.
(c): 3⁻⁷ ÷ 3⁻¹⁰ = 3⁻⁷ ⁺ ¹⁰ = 3³. 3³ × 3⁻⁵ = 3⁻² = 1/9.
Suggested Marks Distribution
Part (a) = 1001 Mark
Part (b) = 1/161 Mark
Part (c) = 1/91 Mark
Q3
Find the unit digit of: (a) 3⁶⁵ (b) 7⁸² (c) 2¹⁰⁰
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(a) 3⁶⁵: Cyclicity 4. 65 ÷ 4 = 16 R 1. Unit digit = 3¹ = 3.
(b) 7⁸²: Cyclicity 4. 82 ÷ 4 = 20 R 2. Unit digit = 7² = 49 ⟹ 9.
(c) 2¹⁰⁰: Cyclicity 4. 100 ÷ 4 = 25 R 0 (4th position). Unit digit = 2⁴ = 16 ⟹ 6.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Digital Data Storage Growth (Powers of 2): 1 KB = 2¹⁰ bytes, 1 MB = 2²⁰ bytes, 1 GB = 2³⁰ bytes.
(a) Express 1 GB in bytes as a power of 2.
(b) How many KB are in a 4 GB flash drive?
(c) If an image takes 2¹⁶ bytes, how many fit in 1 MB?
(a) 1 GB = 2³⁰ bytes.
(b) 4 GB = 2² × 2³⁰ = 2³² bytes. In KB: 2³² / 2¹⁰ = 2²² = 4,194,304 KB.
(c) 1 MB / 2¹⁶ = 2²⁰ / 2¹⁶ = 2⁴ = 16 images.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): (a/b)⁻ᵐ × (b/a)⁻ᵐ = 1 for all non-zero a, b and integer m.
Reason (R): (a/b)⁻ᵐ = (b/a)ᵐ, and (b/a)ᵐ × (b/a)⁻ᵐ = (b/a)⁰ = 1.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
