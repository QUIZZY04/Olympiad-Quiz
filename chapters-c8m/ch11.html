Chapter 11: Direct and Inverse Proportions

NCERT Exercises 11.1 & 11.2 — Complete Solutions as per CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27

📌 Key Mathematical Definitions & Formulas
  • Direct Proportion: Two quantities x and y vary directly if the ratio x/y = k (a constant). x₁/y₁ = x₂/y₂. Characteristic: When x increases, y increases at the same rate.
  • Inverse Proportion: Two quantities x and y vary inversely if their product x × y = k (a constant). x₁ y₁ = x₂ y₂. Characteristic: When x increases, y decreases proportionally so that their product remains constant.
NCERT Exercise 11.1
Q1
Following are the car parking charges near a railway station up to:
4 hours: ₹ 60    8 hours: ₹ 100    12 hours: ₹ 140    24 hours: ₹ 180.
Check if the parking charges are in direct proportion to the parking time.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

For direct proportion, the ratio C/T (Charges/Time) must remain constant.

C₁/T₁ = 60/4 = 15
C₂/T₂ = 100/8 = 12.5
C₃/T₃ = 140/12 = 11.67
C₄/T₄ = 180/24 = 7.5

Since C₁/T₁ ≠ C₂/T₂ ≠ C₃/T₃ ≠ C₄/T₄, the parking charges are NOT in direct proportion to the parking time.

Q2
A mixture of paint is prepared by mixing 1 part of red pigments with 8 parts of base. In the table, find the parts of base that need to be added for: 4, 7, 12, and 20 parts of red pigment.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

Let parts of red pigment be x and base be y. Ratio x/y = 1/8 ⇒ y = 8x.

− For x = 4: y₁ = 4 × 8 = 32.
− For x = 7: y₂ = 7 × 8 = 56.
− For x = 12: y₃ = 12 × 8 = 96.
− For x = 20: y₄ = 20 × 8 = 160.

Q3
A machine in a soft drink factory fills 840 bottles in 6 hours. How many bottles will it fill in 5 hours?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Number of bottles and time are in direct proportion:
x₁/y₁ = x₂/y₂ ⇒ 840/6 = x₂/5
x₂ = (840 × 5)/6 = 140 × 5 = 700 bottles.

Final Answer: The machine fills 700 bottles in 5 hours.

Q4
A loaded truck travels 14 km in 25 minutes. If the speed remains the same, how far can it travel in 5 hours?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Convert 5 hours into minutes: 5 × 60 = 300 minutes.
Distance and time are in direct proportion at constant speed.
14/25 = x/300
x = (14 × 300)/25 = 14 × 12 = 168 km.

Final Answer: The truck travels 168 km in 5 hours.

NCERT Exercise 11.2
Q5
Which of the following are in inverse proportion?
(i) The number of workers on a job and the time to complete the job.
(ii) The time taken for a journey and the distance travelled at a uniform speed.
(iii) Area of cultivated land and the crop harvested.
(iv) The time taken for a fixed journey and the speed of the vehicle.
(v) The population of a country and the area of land per person.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Inverse proportion — More workers require less time to complete the same job.
(ii) Direct proportion — More time spent travelling means more distance covered at constant speed.
(iii) Direct proportion — More cultivated land yields a larger crop harvest.
(iv) Inverse proportion — Higher speed reduces the time needed for a fixed journey distance.
(v) Inverse proportion — As population increases in a fixed territory, the area of land per person decreases.

Statements in inverse proportion: (i), (iv), and (v).

Q6
If a box of sweets is divided among 24 children, they will get 5 sweets each. How many would each get, if the number of the children is reduced by 4?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Total number of sweets is constant: x₁ y₁ = 24 × 5 = 120 sweets.
Number of children is reduced by 4: x₂ = 24 − 4 = 20 children.
Since number of children and sweets per child are inversely proportional:
x₁ y₁ = x₂ y₂ ⇒ 120 = 20 × y₂
y₂ = 120/20 = 6 sweets.

Final Answer: Each child would get 6 sweets.

Q7
A car takes 2 hours to reach a destination by travelling at the speed of 60 km/h. How long will it take when the car travels at the speed of 80 km/h?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Speed and time are in inverse proportion for a fixed distance:
s₁ × t₁ = s₂ × t₂
60 × 2 = 80 × t₂
t₂ = 120/80 = 3/2 hours = 1 1/2 hours (or 1 hour 30 minutes).

Q8
A school has 8 periods a day each of 45 minutes duration. How long would each period be, if the school has 9 periods a day, assuming the number of school hours to be the same?
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Total instructional time is constant: 8 × 45 = 360 minutes.
Number of periods and duration of each period are inversely proportional.
Let duration of each of the 9 periods be x minutes:
9 × x = 360 ⇒ x = 360/9 = 40 minutes.

Final Answer: Each period would be 40 minutes long.

Case Study: Disaster Relief Supply Allocation
Scenario: A humanitarian relief camp has enough grain to sustain 150 refugees for 40 days. After 10 days, 50 more refugees join the camp. For how many more days will the remaining food last?

After 10 days, the food would last for the original 150 refugees for: 40 − 10 = 30 days.
Total remaining refugee-days of food = 150 × 30 = 4500.

New total number of refugees = 150 + 50 = 200 refugees.
Number of people and days are inversely proportional:
Remaining days = 4500/200 = 22.5 days (or 22 days and 12 hours).

⚡ Gear Ratio Mechanics (HOTS)
In a bicycle, the front pedal chainring has 48 teeth and the rear wheel sprocket has 16 teeth. If the cyclist pedals at 60 RPM, what is the rotational speed of the rear wheel? Identify the type of proportion.

Number of teeth T and Rotational Speed N are inversely proportional (T₁ N₁ = T₂ N₂).
48 × 60 = 16 × N₂
N₂ = (48 × 60)/16 = 3 × 60 = 180 RPM.
The rear wheel rotates three times faster than the pedal.
