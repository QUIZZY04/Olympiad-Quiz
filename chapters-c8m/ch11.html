Chapter 11: Exploring Some Geometric Themes

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Symmetry, Geometric Constructions, Polyhedra & Euler's Formula for 3D Solids

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Line & Rotational Symmetry: A figure has rotational symmetry of order n if it coincides with itself n times in a full 360° turn. Angle of rotation = 360° / n.
  • Polyhedra: Solid shapes bounded by flat polygonal faces, straight edges, and vertices. Convex polyhedra satisfy Euler's Formula: Faces (F) + Vertices (V) − Edges (E) = 2.
  • Platonic Solids: Exactly five regular convex polyhedra exist: Tetrahedron (4 faces), Cube (6), Octahedron (8), Dodecahedron (12), Icosahedron (20).

Q1
Verify Euler's formula (F + V − E = 2) for:
(a) A triangular prism
(b) A square pyramid
(c) A cuboid
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Triangular Prism: Faces F = 5 (2 triangles + 3 rectangles), Vertices V = 6, Edges E = 9.
F + V − E = 5 + 6 − 9 = 2 ✓.
(b) Square Pyramid: Faces F = 5 (1 square + 4 triangles), Vertices V = 5, Edges E = 8.
F + V − E = 5 + 5 − 8 = 2 ✓.
(c) Cuboid: Faces F = 6, Vertices V = 8, Edges E = 12.
F + V − E = 6 + 8 − 12 = 2 ✓.

Q2
A polyhedron has 20 vertices and 30 edges. Find the number of faces it has.
2 Marks

By Euler's formula: F + V − E = 2.
F + 20 − 30 = 2 ⟹ F − 10 = 2 ⟹ F = 12 faces (This is a dodecahedron).

Q4
Can a polyhedron have 10 faces, 20 edges, and 15 vertices? Justify your answer using Euler's formula.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Given: F = 10, E = 20, V = 15.
Test Euler's formula: F + V − E = 10 + 15 − 20 = 25 − 20 = 5.
Since 5 ≠ 2, such a polyhedron cannot exist.

Q7
How are prisms and cylinders alike? How are pyramids and cones alike?
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
A prism becomes a cylinder as the number of sides of its base polygon increases indefinitely to form a smooth circular boundary.
A pyramid becomes a cone as the number of sides of its base polygon increases indefinitely to form a circular base meeting at an apex.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Geodesic Domes & Polyhedra: A soccer ball (truncated icosahedron) is constructed using 12 regular pentagons and 20 regular hexagons.
(a) Find the total number of faces F.
(b) If each vertex connects 3 faces, verify Euler's formula.
(a) F = 12 + 20 = 32 faces.
(b) Total sides = 12(5) + 20(6) = 60 + 120 = 180. Each edge is shared by 2 faces ⟹ E = 180 / 2 = 90 edges. Vertices V = 180 / 3 = 60 vertices.
F + V − E = 32 + 60 − 90 = 2 (Euler's formula verified).
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): No polyhedron can have 10 faces, 20 edges, and 15 vertices.
Reason (R): Any convex polyhedron must strictly satisfy Euler's formula F + V − E = 2.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
Explanation: F + V − E = 10 + 15 − 20 = 5 ≠ 2. Such a polyhedron is topologically impossible.
