Chapter 5: Number Play

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 1) — Generalized Form, Algebraic Proofs of Divisibility Rules, Digit Reversals & Cryptarithms

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Generalized Form: A 2-digit number is 10a + b; a 3-digit number is 100a + 10b + c.
  • Digit Reversals: Sum of a number and its reverse: (10a+b)+(10b+a) = 11(a+b) (multiple of 11). Difference: (10a+b)−(10b+a) = 9(a−b) (multiple of 9).
  • Divisibility Proofs: 100a + 10b + c = 9(11a+b) + (a+b+c) proves divisibility by 9 depends only on the digit sum.

Q1
Prove algebraically that the difference between any 3-digit number and the number with reversed digits is always divisible by 99.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Let N = 100a + 10b + c, and reverse N′ = 100c + 10b + a.
N − N′ = (100a + 10b + c) − (100c + 10b + a) = 99a − 99c = 99(a − c).
Since (a − c) is an integer, the difference is always a multiple of 99.

Q3
Explain why any two-digit number "ab" and its reverse "ba" when added together are always divisible by 11.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
In generalized place value form: ab = 10a + b and ba = 10b + a.
Sum = (10a + b) + (10b + a) = 11a + 11b = 11(a + b).
Since 11 is a factor of the sum, the sum is always divisible by 11, and the quotient is the sum of the digits (a + b).

Q4
Construct a 3 × 3 magic square using natural numbers from 1 to 9 such that the sum along every row, column, and diagonal is 15.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Sum of 1 to 9 = (9 × 10)/2 = 45. In 3 rows, magic constant M = 45 / 3 = 15.
The central number must be the median: 15 / 3 = 5.
Arrangement (Lo Shu square):
Row 1: 8, 1, 6 (sum = 15)
Row 2: 3, 5, 7 (sum = 15)
Row 3: 4, 9, 2 (sum = 15).
Verification: Columns: 8+3+4=15; 1+5+9=15; 6+7+2=15. Diagonals: 8+5+2=15; 6+5+4=15.
Suggested Marks Distribution
Deducing central value = 5 and magic constant = 151 Mark

Q5
Find the digital root (beejank) of: (i) 87654 (ii) 999999, and explain how digital roots verify the addition: 347 + 586 = 933.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(i) 87654: 8 + 7 + 6 + 5 + 4 = 30 ⟹ 3 + 0 = 3.
(ii) 999999: Sum of digits = 54 ⟹ 5 + 4 = 9.
Verification of 347 + 586 = 933:
Beejank of 347 = 3+4+7 = 14 ⟹ 5.
Beejank of 586 = 5+8+6 = 19 ⟹ 1+0 = 1.
Sum of beejanks = 5 + 1 = 6.
Beejank of answer 933 = 9+3+3 = 15 ⟹ 1+5 = 6.
Since 6 = 6, the calculation is verified.

Finding digital roots 3 and 91 Mark
Calculating addends digital roots: 5 and 1 -> 61 Mark
Matching answer digital root 61 Mark
Q6
Write down the general form of a 3-digit number "abc" and show that (abc − cba) is always a multiple of 99.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
General form: abc = 100a + 10b + c, and reversed cba = 100c + 10b + a.
Difference = (100a + 10b + c) − (100c + 10b + a) = (100a − a) + (10b − 10b) + (c − 100c) = 99a − 99c = 99(a − c).
Since 99 is a factor, the difference is always divisible by 99.

Simplifying to 99(a − c) and concluding divisibility1 Mark

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Checksum Digit Verification: Test whether 56784 is divisible by 11 using the alternating digit sum test.
Odd places from right: 4 + 7 + 5 = 16. Even places: 8 + 6 = 14. Difference = 16 − 14 = 2. Since 2 is not divisible by 11, 56784 is not divisible by 11.

Assertion (A): A number is divisible by 3 if its digit sum is divisible by 3.
Reason (R): 10ⁿ = (99...9) + 1, so every power of 10 leaves remainder 1 on division by 3.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
