📌 Key Coordinate Geometry & Graphing Concepts
  • Types of Graphs:
    • Line Graph: Displays data that changes continuously over periods of time.
    • Linear Graph: A line graph that consists of a single unbroken straight line.
  • Cartesian Coordinates:
    • Horizontal line is the x-axis; vertical line is the y-axis. Their intersection is the Origin (0, 0).
    • Any point is represented by an ordered pair (x, y), where x is the x-coordinate (abscissa) and y is the y-coordinate (ordinate).
    • Points on the x-axis have y = 0, of the form (a, 0).
    • Points on the y-axis have x = 0, of the form (0, b).
  • Variables in Graphing:
    • Independent Variable: Plotted along the horizontal x-axis (e.g. Time, Deposit, Quantity).
    • Dependent Variable: Value changes in response to the independent variable, plotted on the vertical y-axis (e.g. Temperature, Distance, Interest).
NCERT Exercise 13.1
Q1
The following graph shows the temperature of a patient in a hospital, recorded every hour:
(9 am: 35.5°C, 10 am: 36°C, 11 am: 39°C, 12 noon: 38.5°C, 1 pm: 36.5°C, 2 pm: 36.5°C, 3 pm: 37°C)
(a) What was the patient's temperature at 1 p.m.?
(b) When was the patient's temperature 38.5°C?
(c) The patient's temperature was the same at two times during the period. What were these two times?
(d) What was the temperature at 1.30 p.m.? How did you arrive at your answer?
(e) During which periods did the patients' temperature show an upward trend?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) At 1 p.m., the patient's temperature was 36.5°C.
(b) The patient's temperature was 38.5°C at 12 noon.
(c) The temperature was the same (36.5°C) at 1 p.m. and 2 p.m.
(d) The point between 1 p.m. and 2 p.m. is horizontal at 36.5°C. Thus, the temperature at 1.30 p.m. was 36.5°C.
(e) Upward trends occurred during: 9 a.m. to 10 a.m., 10 a.m. to 11 a.m., and 2 p.m. to 3 p.m.

Q2
A courier-person cycles from a town to a neighbouring suburban area to deliver a parcel to a merchant. His distance from the town at different times is shown by the following graph:
(8 am: 0 km, 9 am: 10 km, 10 am: 16 km, 10:30 am: 16 km, 11:30 am: 22 km)
(a) What is the scale taken for the time axis?
(b) How much time did the person take for the travel?
(c) How far is the place of the merchant from the town?
(d) Did the person stop on his way? Explain.
(e) During which period did he ride fastest?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) Scale: 4 grid units = 1 hour (so each small unit represents 15 minutes).
(b) Total travel time: From 8:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. = 3 1/2 hours (or 3 hours 30 minutes).
(c) Distance of merchant: The maximum distance at destination = 22 km.
(d) Did he stop? Yes, he stopped from 10:00 a.m. to 10:30 a.m., as indicated by the horizontal line segment where distance remained constant at 16 km.
(e) Fastest riding period: Between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 a.m., covering 10 km in 1 hour (Speed = 10 km/h, the steepest segment).

Q3
Can there be a time-temperature graph as follows? Justify your answer:
(i) Temperature increasing linearly with time
(ii) Temperature decreasing linearly with time
(iii) Temperature constant over time (horizontal line)
(iv) Vertical line parallel to temperature axis
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Yes — Temperature can steadily rise as daytime progresses.
(ii) Yes — Temperature can steadily drop during the evening/night.
(iii) Yes — Temperature can remain constant over an interval (isothermal conditions).
(iv) No — A vertical line means infinite different temperatures existing at the exact same instant of time, which is physically impossible.

Q4
Draw a graph for the following:
(a) Cost of apples: (Number of apples: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 vs Cost in ₹: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25). Is it a linear graph?
(b) Distance travelled by a car: (Time: 6 am, 7 am, 8 am, 9 am vs Dist in km: 40, 80, 120, 160). (i) How much distance did the car cover during the period 7.30 a.m. to 8 a.m.? (ii) What was the time when the car had covered a distance of 100 km since its start?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(a) Cost of Apples:
Points: (1, 5), (2, 10), (3, 15), (4, 20), (5, 25).
Plotting these on graph paper, all points lie on a single unbroken straight line passing through origin (0, 0).
Yes, it is a linear graph.
(b) Distance Travelled by Car:
Points: (6 am, 40), (7 am, 80), (8 am, 120), (9 am, 160). Speed = 40 km/h constant.
(i) Between 7.30 a.m. (distance 100 km) and 8 a.m. (distance 120 km):
Distance covered = 120 − 100 = 20 km.
(ii) Distance of 100 km corresponds to time = 7:30 a.m.

Q5
Draw a graph for:
(i) Side of square (in cm): 2, 3, 3.5, 5, 6 vs Perimeter (in cm): 8, 12, 14, 20, 24. Is it a linear graph?
(ii) Side of square (in cm): 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 vs Area (in cm²): 4, 9, 16, 25, 36. Is it a linear graph?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Side vs Perimeter (P = 4s):
The ratio P/s = 4 is constant. All points (2, 8), (3, 12), (3.5, 14), (5, 20), (6, 24) lie along a straight line.
Yes, it is a linear graph.
(ii) Side vs Area (A = s²):
The ratio A/s = s is not constant (it increases as side increases). Joining the points (2, 4), (3, 9), (4, 16), (5, 25), (6, 36) yields a curve (parabola), not a straight line.
No, it is NOT a linear graph.

Case Study: Telecommunication Data Plan
Scenario: A cellular service provider charges a base monthly fee of ₹ 150 plus ₹ 2 per gigabyte (GB) of high-speed data consumed. (i) Write the linear equation relating total cost C and data x in GB. (ii) What are the coordinates of the graph where it crosses the vertical axis? (iii) Find the bill amount for consuming 45 GB of data.

(i) Linear Equation:
C = 2x + 150.

(ii) Vertical Intercept (y-intercept):
When data x = 0, C = 150. Point coordinates = (0, 150).

(iii) Bill for 45 GB:
C = 2(45) + 150 = 90 + 150 = ₹ 240.

⚡ Collinearity Verification (HOTS)
Points A(2, 3), B(4, 5), and C(6, 7) are plotted on a graph. Verify algebraically and graphically whether these three points lie on the same straight line (are collinear).

Slope between A and B = (5 − 3)/(4 − 2) = 2/2 = 1.
Slope between B and C = (7 − 5)/(6 − 4) = 2/2 = 1.

Since the slopes are equal and point B is common to both segments, the points A, B, and C are strictly collinear and lie on the line y = x + 1.
