NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Linear Equations in One Variable, Balance Model, Transposition & Word Problem Modeling

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Linear Equation in One Variable: An algebraic equation of the form ax + b = 0 (where a ≠ 0). Highest power of variable is 1.
  • Balance Model: An equation is like a balanced weighing scale: performing identical operations (adding, subtracting, multiplying, dividing by non-zero) on both sides preserves balance.
  • Transposition: Shifting a term across the equality sign changes its sign (+ to −, × to ÷).

Q1
Solve the equation: 5x + 9 = 5 + 3x, and verify the solution.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Transpose variable terms to LHS and constants to RHS:
5x − 3x = 5 − 9 ⟹ 2x = −4 ⟹ x = −2.
Verification:
LHS = 5(−2) + 9 = −10 + 9 = −1.
RHS = 5 + 3(−2) = 5 − 6 = −1.
LHS = RHS. Verified ✓.

Q3
The sum of three consecutive multiples of 8 is 888. Find the multiples.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Let the three consecutive multiples of 8 be 8x, 8(x + 1), and 8(x + 2).
Sum = 8x + 8x + 8 + 8x + 16 = 888 ⟹ 24x + 24 = 888
24x = 864 ⟹ x = 864 / 24 = 36.
Multiples: 8(36) = 288, 8(37) = 296, 8(38) = 304.
Check: 288 + 296 + 304 = 888.

Q9

Q10
Solve the linear equation: (3t − 2)/4 − (2t + 3)/3 = 2/3 − t.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
LCM of denominators (4, 3) = 12. Multiply both sides by 12:
12 × [(3t − 2)/4] − 12 × [(2t + 3)/3] = 12 × (2/3) − 12 × t.
3(3t − 2) − 4(2t + 3) = 4(2) − 12t.
9t − 6 − 8t − 12 = 8 − 12t ⟹ t − 18 = 8 − 12t.
t + 12t = 8 + 18 ⟹ 13t = 26 ⟹ t = 2.
Suggested Marks Distribution

Expanding and combining like terms: 13t = 261 Mark
Evaluating t = 21 Mark

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Age Problem Puzzle: A father is 3 times as old as his son. Fifteen years ago, the father was 9 times as old as his son.
(a) Set up a linear equation in one variable.
(b) Find their present ages.
(a) Let son's present age = x. Father's age = 3x. 15 years ago: (3x − 15) = 9(x − 15).
(b) 3x − 15 = 9x − 135 ⟹ 6x = 120 ⟹ x = 20. Son is 20 years, Father is 60 years.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): The equation 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6 is an identity with infinitely many solutions.
Reason (R): Expanding LHS yields 2x + 6 = 2x + 6, which is true for all real numbers x.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
