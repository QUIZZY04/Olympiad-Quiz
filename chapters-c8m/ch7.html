Chapter 7: Proportional Reasoning - 1

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 1) — Ratios, Unitary Method, Direct Proportion, Map Scales & Representative Fractions

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Direct Proportion: Two quantities x and y are directly proportional if their ratio remains constant: x / y = k (constant). If x increases, y increases in the same proportion.
  • Scale Drawings & Representative Fraction (RF): Ratio of distance on map to actual ground distance: RF = Map Distance / Actual Distance (both in identical units).
  • Unitary Method: Finding the value of one unit first, then multiplying to find the required quantity.
OlympiadQuiz Chapter Practice Questions & Concept Applications
Q1
A car consumes 4 litres of petrol to cover 60 km.
(a) How much petrol is needed to cover 180 km?
(b) How far can the car travel on 15 litres of petrol?
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Distance and petrol are in direct proportion: x / y = constant.
(a) Petrol for 180 km: 4 / 60 = P / 180 ⟹ P = (4 × 180) / 60 = 12 litres.
(b) Distance for 15 litres: 60 / 4 = D / 15 ⟹ D = 15 × 15 = 225 km.

Q2
A map has a scale of 1 : 250,000 (RF = 1/250000). Two towns are 4 cm apart on the map. Find the actual distance between them in kilometers.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Actual distance = 4 cm × 250,000 = 1,000,000 cm.
Converting to km: 100 cm = 1 m, 1000 m = 1 km ⟹ divide by 100,000.
Actual distance = 1,000,000 / 100,000 = 10 km.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Architectural Blueprint Scaling: A building of height 45 meters is drawn with height 9 cm on an architectural blueprint.
(a) Find the Representative Fraction (RF) of the drawing.
(b) If a doorway is 2 meters high in reality, what is its height on the blueprint?
(a) RF = 9 cm / 4500 cm = 1 : 500 (or 1/500).
(b) Blueprint height = 200 cm / 500 = 0.4 cm = 4 mm.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): If y is directly proportional to x, then the graph of y versus x is a straight line passing through the origin (0, 0).
Reason (R): Direct proportion satisfies the linear equation y = kx with y-intercept equal to 0.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
