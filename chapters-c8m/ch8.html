Chapter 8: Fractions in Disguise

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Fractions as Percentages, Recurring Decimals, Conversions & Financial Mathematics

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Fractions as Decimals & Percentages: A fraction p/q can be expressed as a decimal by division, or as a percentage by multiplying by 100%: 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%, 1/8 = 0.125 = 12.5%.
  • Terminating vs Recurring Decimals: A fraction in lowest terms terminates if denominator has only prime factors 2 and 5. Otherwise it produces a periodic recurring decimal (e.g. 1/3 = 0.3̄).
  • Percentage Change: Percentage Change = (Change / Original Value) × 100%.

Q1
Express the recurring decimal 0.47̄ (i.e. 0.4777...) as a fraction in simplest p/q form.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Let x = 0.4777... (1)
Multiply by 10: 10x = 4.777... (2)
Multiply by 100: 100x = 47.777... (3)
Subtract (2) from (3): 100x − 10x = 47.777... − 4.777... ⟹ 90x = 43 ⟹ x = 43/90.

Q6
A VCR and TV were bought for ₹8,000 each. The shopkeeper made a loss of 4% on the VCR and a profit of 8% on the TV. Find the overall gain or loss percent on the whole transaction.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Cost Price of each = ₹8,000. Total CP = ₹8,000 + ₹8,000 = ₹16,000.
Loss on VCR = 4% of 8,000 = ₹320 ⟹ SP of VCR = 8,000 − 320 = ₹7,680.
Profit on TV = 8% of 8,000 = ₹640 ⟹ SP of TV = 8,000 + 640 = ₹8,640.
Total SP = ₹7,680 + ₹8,640 = ₹16,320.
Since SP > CP, there is an overall profit = ₹16,320 − ₹16,000 = ₹320. Profit % = (320 / 16,000) × 100 = 2% gain.
Suggested Marks Distribution

Total CP = ₹16000 and Total SP = ₹163201 Mark
Net profit = ₹320 and gain % = 2%1 Mark

Q8
Calculate the total amount and compound interest on ₹10,800 for 3 years at 12½% per annum compounded annually.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Principal P = ₹10,800, Rate R = 12.5% = 25/2 %, Time n = 3 years.
A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ = 10800 × (1 + 25/200)³ = 10800 × (9/8)³.
A = 10800 × (729 / 512) = ₹15,377.34.
Compound Interest CI = A − P = ₹15,377.34 − ₹10,800 = ₹4,577.34.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Compound Retail Discounts: A store offers successive discounts of 20% and 10% on a jacket marked at ₹2,000.
(a) Find the final price paid by the customer.
(b) Is the effective discount equal to 30%? Find the single equivalent discount percentage.
(a) After 20% discount: 2000 − 400 = ₹1,600. After additional 10%: 1600 − 160 = ₹1,440.
(b) Total discount = 2000 − 1440 = ₹560. Equivalent single discount = (560/2000) × 100 = 28% (not 30%).
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): 1/7 produces a repeating block of at most 6 digits.
Reason (R): The possible non-zero remainders on division by 7 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, which must repeat in at most 7 − 1 = 6 steps.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
