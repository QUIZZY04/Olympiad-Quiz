Chapter 4: Quadrilaterals

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 1) — Properties of Four-Sided Figures, Angle Sum Property, Parallelograms, Rhombuses & Tiling

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Angle Sum: Sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral is always 360°.
  • Parallelograms: Opposite sides and angles are equal; consecutive angles are supplementary; diagonals bisect each other.
  • Rhombus & Rectangle: A rhombus has perpendicular bisecting diagonals. A rectangle has equal bisecting diagonals. A square has both.
  • Tiling: Any quadrilateral can tile the plane because 4 angles around each vertex sum to 360°.

Q2
In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = (3x − 20)° and ∠C = (x + 40)°. Find x and all four angles.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Opposite angles equal: 3x − 20 = x + 40 ⟹ 2x = 60 ⟹ x = 30.
∠A = ∠C = 3(30) − 20 = 70°.
Consecutive angles supplementary: ∠B = ∠D = 180° − 70° = 110°.
Angles: 70°, 110°, 70°, 110°.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Tiling in Floor Paving: Four tiles with angles 60°, 100°, 120°, and 80° meet at a vertex.
(a) Show that they meet seamlessly without gaps.
(b) Why can any quadrilateral tile the plane?
(a) Sum = 60° + 100° + 120° + 80° = 360°, matching the full turn around a point.
(b) Interior angle sum of any 4-gon is always 360°, guaranteeing complete vertex coverage.

Assertion (A): Every square is a rhombus and a rectangle, but a rectangle is not necessarily a square.
Reason (R): A square combines equal sides (rhombus) with 90° angles (rectangle).
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
