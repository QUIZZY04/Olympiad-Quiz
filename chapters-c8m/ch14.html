Chapter 14: Area

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Measurement of Enclosed Space, Trapeziums, Rhombuses, General Polygons & Decompositions

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Area of Trapezium: Area = ½ × (Sum of Parallel Sides) × Height = ½(a + b)h.
  • Area of Rhombus: Area = ½ × d₁ × d₂ (half the product of its diagonals).
  • Area of General Quadrilateral: Split into two triangles along a diagonal d with offset heights h₁ and h₂: Area = ½ d (h₁ + h₂).
  • Decomposition Method: Any irregular polygon can be divided into non-overlapping triangles and trapeziums to calculate its total area.

The parallel sides of a trapezium are 12 cm and 20 cm, and the perpendicular distance between them is 8 cm. Find its area.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution

Q2
The area of a trapezium is 34 cm² and one parallel side is 10 cm. If its height is 4 cm, find the length of the other parallel side.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Area = ½(a + b)h ⟹ 34 = ½(10 + b) × 4 ⟹ 34 = 2(10 + b)
17 = 10 + b ⟹ b = 17 − 10 = 7 cm.
Answer: The other parallel side is 7 cm.

Q4
The floor of a building consists of 3000 rhombus-shaped tiles. The diagonals of each tile are 45 cm and 30 cm. Find the total cost of polishing the floor, if the cost per m² is ₹4.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Area of 1 tile = (1/2) × d₁ × d₂ = (1/2) × 45 × 30 = 675 cm².
Total area of 3000 tiles = 3000 × 675 = 2,025,000 cm².
Convert to m²: 1 m² = 10,000 cm² ⟹ Total area = 2,025,000 / 10,000 = 202.5 m².
Cost of polishing = 202.5 m² × ₹4/m² = ₹810.

Q5
Mohan wants to buy a trapezium-shaped field. Its side along the river is parallel to and twice the side along the road. The perpendicular distance between the two parallel sides is 100 m and the area of the field is 10,500 m². Find the length of the side along the river.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Let the side along the road be x meters. Then the side along the river is 2x meters.
Area of trapezium = (1/2) × (sum of parallel sides) × height.
10500 = (1/2) × (x + 2x) × 100 = (1/2) × 3x × 100 = 150x.
x = 10500 / 150 = 70 m (side along the road).
Side along the river = 2x = 2 × 70 = 140 meters.
Suggested Marks Distribution

Q6
An aquarium is in the form of a cuboid whose external measures are 80 cm × 30 cm × 40 cm. The base, side faces and back face are to be covered with a coloured paper. Find the area of the paper needed.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Dimensions: Length l = 80 cm, breadth b = 30 cm, height h = 40 cm.
Area of base = l × b = 80 × 30 = 2400 cm².
Area of 2 side faces = 2 × (b × h) = 2 × (30 × 40) = 2400 cm².
Area of back face = l × h = 80 × 40 = 3200 cm².
Total paper required = 2400 + 2400 + 3200 = 8,000 cm².

Q7
A suitcase with measures 80 cm × 48 cm × 24 cm is to be covered with a tarpaulin cloth. How many metres of tarpaulin of width 96 cm is required to cover 100 such suitcases?
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Total surface area of 1 suitcase = 2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(80×48 + 48×24 + 24×80) = 2(3840 + 1152 + 1920) = 2(6912) = 13,824 cm².
Total area for 100 suitcases = 100 × 13,824 = 1,382,400 cm².
Width of tarpaulin = 96 cm. Length of cloth required = Total area / width = 1,382,400 / 96 = 14,400 cm.
Convert to metres: 14,400 cm / 100 = 144 metres.

Q9
A cylindrical milk tank has radius 1.5 m and height (length) 7 m. Find the quantity of milk in litres that can be stored in the tank. (Use π = 22/7 and 1 m³ = 1000 litres).
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Volume of cylinder V = πr²h.
V = (22 / 7) × (1.5)² × 7 = 22 × 2.25 = 49.5 m³.
Convert to litres: Quantity of milk = 49.5 × 1000 = 49,500 litres.

Q10
A road roller takes 750 complete revolutions to move once over to level a road. Find the area of the road if the diameter of the road roller is 84 cm and its width (length) is 1 m.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Diameter d = 84 cm ⟹ radius r = 42 cm = 0.42 m. Width (height) h = 1 m.
Curved surface area (CSA) in 1 revolution = 2πrh = 2 × (22 / 7) × 0.42 × 1 = 2 × 22 × 0.06 = 2.64 m².
Area leveled in 750 revolutions = 750 × 2.64 m² = 1,980 m².

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Surveying an Irregular Field by Triangulation: A surveyor plots a field by drawing a baseline AD = 100 m. Triangles are formed on either side with perpendicular offsets: to point B (height 30 m at distance 40 m from A) and to point C (height 40 m at distance 60 m from A).
(a) Explain how decomposition simplifies measuring irregular plots.
(b) Calculate the total field area.
(a) Dividing the land into right triangles and trapeziums allows simple standard formulas to measure complex boundaries.
(b) Total Area = Area(ΔABD) + Area(ΔACD) = ½(100 × 30) + ½(100 × 40) = 1500 + 2000 = 3,500 m².
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): The area of a rhombus is equal to half the product of its diagonals.
Reason (R): The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other perpendicularly, splitting it into four congruent right triangles.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
Explanation: 4 × (½ × (d₁/2) × (d₂/2)) = 4 × (d₁d₂ / 8) = ½ d₁ d₂.
