📌 Key Factorisation Techniques & Identities
  • Factorisation: Writing an algebraic expression as the product of its irreducible factors.
  • Method 1 — Common Factors: Extract the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of terms: ax + ay = a(x + y).
  • Method 2 — Regrouping Terms: Rearrange terms into groups having common factors: x² + xy + 8x + 8y = x(x + y) + 8(x + y) = (x + y)(x + 8).
  • Method 3 — Using Algebraic Identities:
    • a² + 2ab + b² = (a + b)²
    • a² − 2ab + b² = (a − b)²
    • a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
  • Method 4 — Splitting the Middle Term: For quadratic trinomial x² + (a + b)x + ab = (x + a)(x + b).
  • Division of Polynomials: Factorise the dividend polynomial completely and cancel out common linear/monomial factors with the divisor.
NCERT Exercise 12.1
Q1
Find the common factors of the given terms:
(i) 12x, 36    (ii) 2y, 22xy    (iii) 14pq, 28p²q²
(iv) 2x, 3x², 4    (v) 6abc, 24ab², 12a²b    (vi) 16x³, -4x², 32x
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 12x = 2 × 2 × 3 × x; 36 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3.
Common factors = 2 × 2 × 3 = 12.
(ii) 2y = 2 × y; 22xy = 2 × 11 × x × y.
Common factors = 2 × y = 2y.
(iii) 14pq = 2 × 7 × p × q; 28p²q² = 2 × 2 × 7 × p² × q².
Common factors = 2 × 7 × p × q = 14pq.
(iv) 2x = 2 × x; 3x² = 3 × x²; 4 = 2 × 2.
Common factor = 1 (no variable or prime factor common to all three).
(v) 6abc = 2 × 3 × a × b × c; 24ab² = 2³ × 3 × a × b²; 12a²b = 2² × 3 × a² × b.
Common factor = 2 × 3 × a × b = 6ab.
(vi) 16x³ = 2⁴ × x³; -4x² = (−1) × 2² × x²; 32x = 2⁵ × x.
Common factor = 2² × x = 4x.

Q2
Factorise by regrouping:
(i) x² + xy + 8x + 8y
(ii) 15xy − 6x + 5y − 2
(iii) ax + bx − ay − by
(iv) 15pq + 15 + 9q + 25p
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Group first two and last two terms:
= x(x + y) + 8(x + y) = (x + y)(x + 8).
(ii) Group (15xy − 6x) + (5y − 2):
= 3x(5y − 2) + 1(5y − 2) = (5y − 2)(3x + 1).
(iii) Group (ax + bx) − (ay + by):
= x(a + b) − y(a + b) = (a + b)(x − y).
(iv) Rearrange terms: 15pq + 25p + 9q + 15:
= 5p(3q + 5) + 3(3q + 5) = (3q + 5)(5p + 3).

Q4
Factorise using difference of squares identity a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b):
(i) 4p² − 9q²
(ii) 63a² − 112b²
(iii) 49x² − 36
(iv) 16x⁵ − 144x³
(v) (x² − 2xy + y²) − z²
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) (2p)² − (3q)² = (2p − 3q)(2p + 3q).
(ii) Take out common factor 7:
= 7(9a² − 16b²) = 7[(3a)² − (4b)²] = 7(3a − 4b)(3a + 4b).
(iii) (7x)² − 6² = (7x − 6)(7x + 6).
(iv) Take out common factor 16x³:
= 16x³(x² − 9) = 16x³(x² − 3²) = 16x³(x − 3)(x + 3).
(v) Recognize (x² − 2xy + y²) = (x − y)²:
= (x − y)² − z² = (x − y − z)(x − y + z).

Q5
Factorise the expressions by splitting the middle term:
(i) p² + 6p + 8
(ii) q² − 10q + 21
(iii) p² + 6p − 16
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) p² + 6p + 8:
Find two numbers whose product is 8 and sum is 6: numbers are 4 and 2.
= p² + 4p + 2p + 8 = p(p + 4) + 2(p + 4) = (p + 4)(p + 2).
(ii) q² − 10q + 21:
Find two numbers whose product is 21 and sum is -10: numbers are -7 and -3.
= q² − 7q − 3q + 21 = q(q − 7) − 3(q − 7) = (q − 7)(q − 3).
(iii) p² + 6p − 16:
Find two numbers whose product is -16 and sum is 6: numbers are 8 and -2.
= p² + 8p − 2p − 16 = p(p + 8) − 2(p + 8) = (p + 8)(p − 2).

NCERT Exercise 12.3
Q6
Divide as directed:
(i) 28x⁴ ÷ 56x
(ii) (5x² − 6x) ÷ 3x
(iii) 10y(6y + 21) ÷ 5(2y + 7)
(iv) (y² + 7y + 10) ÷ (y + 5)
(v) (m² − 14m − 32) ÷ (m + 2)
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) 28x⁴/56x = 28/56 × x⁴/x = 1/2x⁴⁻¹ = x³/2.
(ii) (5x² − 6x)/3x = (x(5x − 6))/3x = (5x − 6)/3 (or 5/3x − 2).
(iii) (10y × 3(2y + 7))/(5(2y + 7)) = (30y(2y + 7))/(5(2y + 7)) = 6y.
(iv) Factorise numerator: y² + 7y + 10 = (y + 5)(y + 2).
(y + 5)(y + 2)/(y + 5) = y + 2.
(v) Factorise numerator: m² − 14m − 32 = (m − 16)(m + 2).
(m − 16)(m + 2)/(m + 2) = m − 16.

Case Study: Land Area Division
Scenario: A developer owns a rectangular commercial plot of total area (2x² + 7x + 3) m². The length of the plot is known to be (2x + 1) m. (i) Find the algebraic expression for the breadth of the plot. (ii) If x = 10 m, calculate the perimeter of the plot.

(i) Breadth of plot:
Breadth = Area/Length = (2x² + 7x + 3)/(2x + 1).
Factorise numerator: 2x² + 6x + x + 3 = 2x(x + 3) + 1(x + 3) = (2x + 1)(x + 3).
Breadth = (2x + 1)(x + 3)/(2x + 1) = (x + 3) m.

(ii) Perimeter for x = 10 m:
Length = 2(10) + 1 = 21 m.
Breadth = 10 + 3 = 13 m.
Perimeter = 2(length + breadth) = 2(21 + 13) = 2(34) = 68 m.

⚡ Repeated Factorisation Challenge (HOTS)
Factorise completely: x⁸ − y⁸ into its irreducible factors.

Apply identity a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b) repeatedly:
x⁸ − y⁸ = (x⁴)² − (y⁴)² = (x⁴ − y⁴)(x⁴ + y⁴).
Factorise (x⁴ − y⁴) = (x² − y²)(x² + y²) = (x − y)(x + y)(x² + y²).

Final Answer: (x − y)(x + y)(x² + y²)(x⁴ + y⁴).
