NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Coordinate Geometry, Ordered Pairs, Plotting Points, Reading Graphs & Linear Trends

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Cartesian Plane: Two perpendicular axes: horizontal x-axis and vertical y-axis meeting at origin O(0, 0).
  • Coordinates: A point P(x, y) has abscissa (x-coordinate, distance from y-axis) and ordinate (y-coordinate, distance from x-axis).
  • Linear Graphs: Points satisfying a linear equation lie on a straight line. Line graphs display trends across time.

Q1
State the quadrant or axis for each point: (i) (3, 5) (ii) (−2, 4) (iii) (−4, −3) (iv) (5, −2) (v) (0, −6) (vi) (4, 0).
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(i) (3, 5): Both positive ⟹ Quadrant I.
(ii) (−2, 4): x negative, y positive ⟹ Quadrant II.
(iii) (−4, −3): Both negative ⟹ Quadrant III.
(iv) (5, −2): x positive, y negative ⟹ Quadrant IV.
(v) (0, −6): x = 0 ⟹ negative y-axis.
(vi) (4, 0): y = 0 ⟹ positive x-axis.

Q2
The perimeter of an equilateral triangle of side s is P = 3s. Draw up a table of values for s = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and state whether the graph of P versus s is a straight line.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Table of values: (1, 3), (2, 6), (3, 9), (4, 12), (5, 15).
Notice ratio P/s = 3 (constant rate of change).
Plotting points and connecting them yields a continuous ray starting from origin (0, 0).
Conclusion: Yes, it is a straight line passing through the origin.

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Fitness Tracking Distance-Time Graph: A runner jogs at constant speed. In 10 min they cover 1.5 km, in 20 min 3 km, in 30 min 4.5 km.
(a) Find the speed in km/h.
(b) Predict distance after 50 minutes.
(a) Speed = 1.5 km / (10/60 h) = 1.5 × 6 = 9 km/h.
(b) In 50 min: Distance = 9 × (50/60) = 7.5 km.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): The equation y = 2x + 1 represents a straight line that does not pass through the origin.
Reason (R): When x = 0, y = 1 ≠ 0, so the line has a y-intercept at (0, 1).
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
