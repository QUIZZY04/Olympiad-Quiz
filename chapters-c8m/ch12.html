Chapter 12: Tales by Dots and Lines

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Part 2) — Coordinate Geometry, Ordered Pairs, Plotting Points, Reading Graphs & Linear Trends

📌 Key Concepts & Mathematical Foundations
  • Cartesian Plane: Two perpendicular axes: horizontal x-axis and vertical y-axis meeting at origin O(0, 0).
  • Coordinates: A point P(x, y) has abscissa (x-coordinate, distance from y-axis) and ordinate (y-coordinate, distance from x-axis).
  • Linear Graphs: Points satisfying a linear equation lie on a straight line. Line graphs display trends across time.

Q1
State the quadrant or axis for each point: (i) (3, 5) (ii) (−2, 4) (iii) (−4, −3) (iv) (5, −2) (v) (0, −6) (vi) (4, 0).
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(i) (3, 5): Both positive ⟹ Quadrant I.
(ii) (−2, 4): x negative, y positive ⟹ Quadrant II.
(iii) (−4, −3): Both negative ⟹ Quadrant III.
(iv) (5, −2): x positive, y negative ⟹ Quadrant IV.
(v) (0, −6): x = 0 ⟹ negative y-axis.
(vi) (4, 0): y = 0 ⟹ positive x-axis.

Q2
The perimeter of an equilateral triangle of side s is P = 3s. Draw up a table of values for s = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and state whether the graph of P versus s is a straight line.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Table of values: (1, 3), (2, 6), (3, 9), (4, 12), (5, 15).
Notice ratio P/s = 3 (constant rate of change).
Plotting points and connecting them yields a continuous ray starting from origin (0, 0).
Conclusion: Yes, it is a straight line passing through the origin.

Q3
Plot points A(4, 0), B(4, 2), C(4, 6), and D(4, 2.5) on a Cartesian grid. Do all these points lie on a single straight line? State the line's equation.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Notice that the x-coordinate of all four points is identical: x = 4.
All points lie on a straight vertical line parallel to the y-axis, located 4 units to the right of the origin.
Yes, they lie on the straight line whose equation is x = 4.

Q4
Draw the line passing through (2, 3) and (3, 2). Find the coordinates of the points at which this line intersects the x-axis and the y-axis.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Notice that for both points, the sum of coordinates is constant: 2 + 3 = 5 and 3 + 2 = 5.
The equation of this line is x + y = 5.
At x-axis (where y = 0): x + 0 = 5 ⟹ x = 5. Coordinates = (5, 0).
At y-axis (where x = 0): 0 + y = 5 ⟹ y = 5. Coordinates = (0, 5).

Q6
A bank offers 10% Simple Interest per annum on deposits. For deposits of ₹1000, ₹2000, ₹3000, ₹4000, and ₹5000:
(i) Tabulate interest for 1 year.
(ii) Does the graph pass through the origin?
(iii) Find the annual interest on a deposit of ₹2500 using the graph.
3 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(i) SI = (P × 10 × 1) / 100 = 0.1P. Deposits vs Interest: (1000, 100), (2000, 200), (3000, 300), (4000, 400), (5000, 500).
(ii) For P = 0, SI = 0. Yes, the linear graph passes through the origin (0, 0).
(iii) For deposit P = ₹2500: Reading from the graph line halfway between 2000 and 3000 gives Interest = ₹250.

Q7
A courier rider travels from a town to a suburb. Distance from town: 8 am: 0 km; 9 am: 10 km; 10 am: 16 km; 10:30 am: 16 km; 11:30 am: 22 km.
(i) How much total time did the journey take?
(ii) How far is the delivery location from town?
(iii) Did the rider stop on the way? Explain.
3 Marks

(ii) Distance of delivery location = 22 km.
(iii) Yes, the rider stopped between 10:00 am and 10:30 am because the distance remained constant at 16 km for 30 minutes (horizontal line segment on distance-time graph).

Q8
The cost of petrol is ₹70 per litre. Tabulate the cost for 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 litres. Does the relation represent direct proportion? Explain.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Table: 1 L ⟹ ₹70; 2 L ⟹ ₹140; 3 L ⟹ ₹210; 4 L ⟹ ₹280; 5 L ⟹ ₹350.
Cost / Litres = 70 / 1 = 140 / 2 = 210 / 3 = 70 (constant).
Yes, it represents direct proportion because the ratio Cost/Volume is constant and the graph is a straight line passing through (0, 0).

📝 Case Study & Conceptual Modeling
Fitness Tracking Distance-Time Graph: A runner jogs at constant speed. In 10 min they cover 1.5 km, in 20 min 3 km, in 30 min 4.5 km.
(a) Find the speed in km/h.
(b) Predict distance after 50 minutes.
(a) Speed = 1.5 km / (10/60 h) = 1.5 × 6 = 9 km/h.
(b) In 50 min: Distance = 9 × (50/60) = 7.5 km.
🧠 HOTS Practice Questions (Assertion-Reasoning)
Assertion (A): The equation y = 2x + 1 represents a straight line that does not pass through the origin.
Reason (R): When x = 0, y = 1 ≠ 0, so the line has a y-intercept at (0, 1).
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
