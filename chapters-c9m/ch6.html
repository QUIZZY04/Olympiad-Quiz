Exercise 6.1
Q1
In the figure, lines AB and CD intersect at O. If ∠AOC + ∠BOE = 70° and ∠BOD = 40°, find ∠BOE and reflex ∠COE.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given:
• Lines AB and CD intersect at point O.
• ∠AOC + ∠BOE = 70°
• ∠BOD = 40°
Step 1: Find ∠AOC:
Since lines AB and CD intersect at O, vertically opposite angles are equal:
∠AOC = ∠BOD = 40°
Step 2: Find ∠BOE:
Given ∠AOC + ∠BOE = 70°:
40° + ∠BOE = 70° ⟹ ∠BOE = 70° - 40° = 30°.
Step 3: Find reflex ∠COE:
AOB is a straight line. The sum of angles forming a linear straight angle is 180°:
∠AOC + ∠COE + ∠BOE = 180°
(∠AOC + ∠BOE) + ∠COE = 180°
70° + ∠COE = 180° ⟹ ∠COE = 180° - 70° = 110°.

By definition, reflex angle = 360° - ∠COE:
Reflex ∠COE = 360° - 110° = 250°.

Final Answer: ∠BOE = 30° and Reflex ∠COE = 250°.

Q2
In the figure, lines XY and MN intersect at O. If ∠POY = 90° and a : b = 2 : 3, find c.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given:
• Lines XY and MN intersect at O.
• Ray OP stands on line XY, with ∠POY = 90°.
• a : b = 2 : 3 where a = ∠POM and b = ∠XOM.
• c = ∠XON.
Step 1: Find a and b:
Since XOY is a straight line, the sum of adjacent angles is 180°:
∠POX + ∠POY = 180° ⟹ ∠POX + 90° = 180° ⟹ ∠POX = 90°.
Also, ray OM lies between OP and OX, so a + b = ∠POX = 90°.
Given a : b = 2 : 3, let a = 2x and b = 3x:
2x + 3x = 90° ⟹ 5x = 90° ⟹ x = 18°.
Therefore:
a = 2 × 18° = 36°
b = 3 × 18° = 54°.
Step 2: Find c:
MN is a straight line and ray OX stands on it. By Linear Pair Axiom:
∠XOM + ∠XON = 180°
b + c = 180°
54° + c = 180° ⟹ c = 180° - 54° = 126°.

Final Answer: The value of c = 126°.

Q3
In the figure, ∠PQR = ∠PRQ, then prove that ∠PQS = ∠PRT.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: In triangle PQR, ∠PQR = ∠PRQ.
S-Q-R-T is a straight line.
To Prove: ∠PQS = ∠PRT.
Proof:
1. Ray QP stands on line ST. By Linear Pair Axiom:
∠PQS + ∠PQR = 180°    --- (1)

2. Similarly, ray RP stands on line ST. By Linear Pair Axiom:
∠PRT + ∠PRQ = 180°    --- (2)

3. From equations (1) and (2), both sums equal 180°:
∠PQS + ∠PQR = ∠PRT + ∠PRQ    --- (3)

4. But it is given that ∠PQR = ∠PRQ.
Subtracting ∠PQR (or ∠PRQ) from both sides of (3):
∠PQS = ∠PRT. Hence Proved.

Q4
In the figure, if x + y = w + z, then prove that AOB is a line.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Rays OA, OB, OC, OD meet at point O such that x + y = w + z.
x = ∠BOC, y = ∠AOC, w = ∠BOD, z = ∠AOD.
To Prove: AOB is a straight line.
Proof:
The sum of all angles around a single point O in a complete rotation is 360°:
∠BOC + ∠AOC + ∠BOD + ∠AOD = 360°
(x + y) + (w + z) = 360°    --- (1)

Given that w + z = x + y. Substituting this in (1):
(x + y) + (x + y) = 360°
2(x + y) = 360° ⟹ x + y = 360° / 2 = 180°.

Now, ∠BOC + ∠AOC = x + y = 180°.
By the Converse of Linear Pair Axiom: If the sum of two adjacent angles is 180°, then their non-common arms form a straight line.
Here, non-common arms OA and OB form a line.
Hence, AOB is a straight line.

Exercise 6.2 (Parallel Lines and Transversals)
Q1
In the figure, find the values of x and y and then show that AB || CD (where line intersects transversal making angle 50° with x on straight line, and opposite angle 130° at y).
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Find x:
The transversal line is a straight line. The angles 50° and x form a linear pair:
50° + x = 180° ⟹ x = 180° - 50° = 130°.
Step 2: Find y:
The transversal line and line CD intersect, forming vertically opposite angles:
y = 130° (Vertically opposite angles).
Step 3: Prove AB || CD:
From above calculations:
x = 130° and y = 130° ⟹ x = y.
Notice that angles x and y are a pair of alternate interior angles formed by the transversal with lines AB and CD.
By the Converse of Alternate Interior Angles Theorem: If a transversal intersects two lines such that a pair of alternate interior angles are equal, then the two lines are parallel.
Therefore, AB || CD.

x = z (Alternate interior angles)    --- (1)

x + y = 180°.

Therefore:
z = 7 × 18° = 126°.
Since x = z: x = 126°.

Final Answer: x = 126°.

Q6
In the figure, two mirrors PQ and RS are placed parallel to each other. An incident ray AB strikes the mirror PQ at B, the reflected ray moves along the path BC and strikes the mirror RS at C and again reflects back along CD. Prove that AB || CD.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Plane mirrors PQ || RS. Ray AB is incident on PQ at B, reflected as BC, incident on RS at C, reflected as CD.
To Prove: AB || CD.
Construction:
Draw normal BL ⊥ PQ at B, and normal CM ⊥ RS at C.
Let ∠ABL = ∠1, ∠LBC = ∠2, ∠BCM = ∠3, ∠MCD = ∠4.
Proof:
1. By Laws of Reflection: Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection.
At mirror PQ: ∠1 = ∠2 ⟹ ∠ABC = ∠1 + ∠2 = 2∠2    --- (1)
At mirror RS: ∠3 = ∠4 ⟹ ∠BCD = ∠3 + ∠4 = 2∠3    --- (2)

2. Since PQ || RS and BL ⊥ PQ, CM ⊥ RS, normals to parallel lines are themselves parallel:
BL || CM.

3. Now, BL || CM and transversal BC intersects them:
∠2 = ∠3 (Alternate interior angles)    --- (3)

4. Multiplying both sides of (3) by 2:
2∠2 = 2∠3
From (1) and (2):
∠ABC = ∠BCD.

5. Angles ∠ABC and ∠BCD are alternate interior angles formed by transversal BC with lines AB and CD.
By the Converse of Alternate Interior Angles Theorem:
AB || CD. Hence Proved.

Q1
In the figure, sides QP and RQ of ΔPQR are produced to points S and T respectively. If ∠SPR = 135° and ∠PQT = 110°, find ∠PRQ.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Find ∠QPR:
SQP is a straight line. By Linear Pair Axiom:
∠SPR + ∠QPR = 180°
135° + ∠QPR = 180° ⟹ ∠QPR = 180° - 135° = 45°.
Step 2: Find ∠PQR:
TQR is a straight line. By Linear Pair Axiom:
∠PQT + ∠PQR = 180°
110° + ∠PQR = 180° ⟹ ∠PQR = 180° - 110° = 70°.
Step 3: Find ∠PRQ:
By the Angle Sum Property of a Triangle, the sum of three interior angles of ΔPQR is 180°:
∠QPR + ∠PQR + ∠PRQ = 180°
45° + 70° + ∠PRQ = 180°
115° + ∠PRQ = 180° ⟹ ∠PRQ = 180° - 115° = 65°.

Final Answer: ∠PRQ = 65°.

Q6
In the figure, the side QR of ΔPQR is produced to a point S. If the bisectors of ∠PQR and ∠PRS meet at point T, then prove that ∠QTR = 1/2 ∠QPR.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Side QR of ΔPQR is produced to S. Ray QT bisects ∠PQR and ray RT bisects ∠PRS.
To Prove: ∠QTR = 1/2 ∠QPR.
Proof:
1. In ΔPQR, ∠PRS is an exterior angle. By Exterior Angle Theorem:
∠PRS = ∠PQR + ∠QPR    --- (1)

2. Dividing (1) by 2:
1/2 ∠PRS = 1/2 ∠PQR + 1/2 ∠QPR    --- (2)

3. Now, in ΔTQR, ∠TRS is an exterior angle. By Exterior Angle Theorem:
∠TRS = ∠TQR + ∠QTR    --- (3)

4. Since RT bisects ∠PRS and QT bisects ∠PQR:
∠TRS = 1/2 ∠PRS and ∠TQR = 1/2 ∠PQR.

5. Substituting these into (3):
1/2 ∠PRS = 1/2 ∠PQR + ∠QTR    --- (4)

6. Comparing equation (2) and equation (4):
1/2 ∠PQR + 1/2 ∠QPR = 1/2 ∠PQR + ∠QTR
Subtracting 1/2 ∠PQR from both sides:
∠QTR = 1/2 ∠QPR. Hence Proved.

📝 Case Study Question
Structural Truss Alignment: A steel roof truss consists of two inclined rafters AB and AC meeting at apex A at 70°. Steel ties connect the rafters such that DE || BC.
(a) If ∠ABC = 55°, find ∠ACB.
(b) What is the measure of corresponding angle ∠ADE?
(c) What is the sum of consecutive interior angles ∠BDE and ∠DBC?
(a) In ΔABC, ∠ACB = 180° - (70° + 55°) = 180° - 125° = 55°.
(b) Since DE || BC and AB is transversal, ∠ADE = ∠ABC = 55° (corresponding angles).
(c) Consecutive interior angles on the same side of a transversal are supplementary, so ∠BDE + ∠DBC = 180°.
🧠 HOTS
HOTS: If the bisectors of pair of alternate interior angles formed by a transversal with two lines are parallel, prove that the two lines are parallel.
Let transversal t intersect lines l and m. Let alternate interior angles be ∠1 and ∠2.
Their bisectors are parallel, so the halves of the angles are alternate interior angles for the bisectors: 1/2 ∠1 = 1/2 ∠2.
Multiplying by 2: ∠1 = ∠2.
Since alternate interior angles are equal, by the converse theorem, lines l and m are parallel.
