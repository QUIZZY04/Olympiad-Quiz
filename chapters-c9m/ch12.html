Q1
A survey conducted by an organisation for the cause of illness and death among women between the ages 15-44 (in years) worldwide, found the following figures (in %):
CausesFatality Rate (%)
1. Reproductive health conditions31.8
2. Neuropsychiatric conditions25.4
3. Injuries12.4
4. Cardiovascular conditions4.3
5. Respiratory conditions4.1
6. Other causes22.0

(i) Represent the information given above graphically.
(ii) Which condition is the major cause of women’s ill health and death worldwide?
(iii) Try to find out, with the help of your teacher, any two factors which play a major role in the cause in (ii) above being the major cause.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Graphical Representation:
Since the data categories are qualitative causes of illness, a Bar Graph is the most suitable representation:
• On the horizontal axis (x-axis), plot the six causes of fatality with equal bar widths and equal spacing between bars.
• On the vertical axis (y-axis), represent the fatality rate (in %) using a scale: 1 unit length = 5% (ranging from 0% to 35%).
• Heights of the rectangular bars: Reproductive health (31.8), Neuropsychiatric (25.4), Injuries (12.4), Cardiovascular (4.3), Respiratory (4.1), Other causes (22.0).
(ii) Major Cause:
Looking at the bar heights, the highest bar corresponds to Reproductive health conditions (31.8%).
Hence, reproductive health conditions are the major cause of women's ill health and death worldwide.
(iii) Two Factors Contributing to Major Cause:
Two major socio-medical factors playing a critical role are:
1. Lack of adequate healthcare infrastructure and medical knowledge: Poor access to prenatal and postnatal care, skilled birth attendants, and clean maternity facilities in rural or developing regions.
2. Nutritional deficiency and lack of awareness: High prevalence of anaemia, malnutrition, and social taboos surrounding reproductive hygiene and maternal health.

Q4
The length of 40 leaves of a plant are measured correct to one millimetre, and the obtained data is represented in the following table:
Length (in mm)Number of leaves
118 - 1263
127 - 1355
136 - 1449
145 - 15312
154 - 1625
163 - 1714
172 - 1802

(i) Draw a histogram to represent the given data.
(ii) Is there any other suitable graphical representation for the same data?
(iii) Is it correct to conclude that the maximum number of leaves are 153 mm long? Why?
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Making Class Intervals Continuous:
The given classes are inclusive/discrete (gap between upper limit 126 and lower limit 127 is 127 - 126 = 1).
Adjustment factor = 1 / 2 = 0.5.
Subtract 0.5 from lower limits and add 0.5 to upper limits:
• 117.5 - 126.5: 3 leaves
• 126.5 - 135.5: 5 leaves
• 135.5 - 144.5: 9 leaves
• 144.5 - 153.5: 12 leaves
• 153.5 - 162.5: 5 leaves
• 162.5 - 171.5: 4 leaves
• 171.5 - 180.5: 2 leaves

Histogram Construction:
• On horizontal axis: Mark continuous class boundaries from 117.5 to 180.5 (use a kink between 0 and 117.5).
• On vertical axis: Number of leaves with scale 1 cm = 2 leaves.
• Draw adjacent rectangles over each class interval without any gaps between them.
(ii) Other suitable graphical representation:
Yes, a Frequency Polygon can also be suitably used to represent this grouped continuous data.
(iii) Is it correct to conclude maximum leaves are 153 mm long?
No, it is incorrect.
Reason: The class interval 145 - 153 (or 144.5 - 153.5) has the maximum frequency of 12 leaves. This means 12 leaves have lengths anywhere between 144.5 mm and 153.5 mm, distributed throughout this range. We cannot claim that all or even a majority of these 12 leaves have the exact length of 153 mm.

The following table gives the life times of 400 neon lamps:

700 - 80074
800 - 90062
900 - 100048

(i) Represent the given information with the help of a histogram.
(ii) How many lamps have a life time of more than 700 hours?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
(i) Histogram Construction:
• Class intervals are continuous (class width = 100 hours each).
• On horizontal axis: Lifetime (in hours), using a kink from 0 to 300, then marking intervals 300, 400, 500, ..., 1000.
• On vertical axis: Number of lamps (frequency) with scale 1 cm = 10 lamps (0 to 90).

Sum of frequencies of class intervals greater than 700:
• Class 700 - 800: 74 lamps

Total = 74 + 62 + 48 = 184 lamps.

Final Answer: 184 lamps have a life time of more than 700 hours.

Q8
A random survey of the number of children of various age groups playing in a park was found as follows:
Age (in years)Number of children
1 - 25
2 - 33
3 - 56
5 - 712
7 - 109
10 - 1510
15 - 174

Draw a histogram to represent the data.
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Key Principle for Histograms with Varying Base Widths:
In a histogram, the area of a rectangle is proportional to the frequency. When class widths vary, we must calculate Adjusted Frequency (Length of Rectangle):
Length of rectangle = (Minimum class width / Class width) × Frequency
Here, minimum class width = 1.
Calculation of Adjusted Frequencies:
• 1 - 2: Width = 1 ⟹ Adjusted Frequency = (1 / 1) × 5 = 5
• 2 - 3: Width = 1 ⟹ Adjusted Frequency = (1 / 1) × 3 = 3
• 3 - 5: Width = 2 ⟹ Adjusted Frequency = (1 / 2) × 6 = 3
• 5 - 7: Width = 2 ⟹ Adjusted Frequency = (1 / 2) × 12 = 6
• 7 - 10: Width = 3 ⟹ Adjusted Frequency = (1 / 3) × 9 = 3
• 10 - 15: Width = 5 ⟹ Adjusted Frequency = (1 / 5) × 10 = 2
• 15 - 17: Width = 2 ⟹ Adjusted Frequency = (1 / 2) × 4 = 2
Histogram Construction:
• On horizontal axis: Age (in years) from 1 to 17.
• On vertical axis: Proportion of children per 1-year interval (Adjusted Frequency).
• Rectangles are drawn with bases corresponding to the class intervals and heights equal to adjusted frequencies: 5, 3, 3, 6, 3, 2, 2.

📝 Case Study Question
Air Quality Index (AQI) Monitoring: An environmental agency monitors the AQI of an industrial city over 30 days. The data is grouped as: 50-100 (4 days), 100-150 (8 days), 150-200 (10 days), 200-250 (5 days), 250-300 (3 days).
(a) What is the class mark of the modal class interval?
(b) On how many days was the AQI 150 or more?
(c) Which graphical representation is best to compare AQI across two different seasons?
(a) The class with maximum frequency is 150-200 (10 days). Class mark = (150 + 200) / 2 = 175.
(b) Days with AQI ≥ 150: 10 + 5 + 3 = 18 days.
(c) Frequency Polygons drawn on the same coordinate axes are ideal for comparative analysis between two different data series.
🧠 HOTS
HOTS: Explain why the area of a histogram rectangle represents frequency rather than simply its height when class widths are unequal.
When class intervals have unequal widths, a wider interval naturally collects more data points simply because it covers a larger span. If heights alone were used, wider intervals would visually distort the distribution by exaggerating their importance.
By making Area = Width × Height ∝ Frequency, the height represents the frequency density (frequency per unit width), ensuring that visual weight directly reflects true data concentration.
