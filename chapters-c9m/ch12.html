Exercise Set 12.1 — Properties of Parallelograms
Q1
Prove that opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal.
4 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution

Given: ABCD is a parallelogram (AB ∥ CD and AD ∥ BC). To prove: AB = CD and AD = BC.

Construction: Draw diagonal AC.
In △ABC and △CDA:
∠BAC = ∠DCA (alternate interior angles, AB∥CD)
∠BCA = ∠DAC (alternate interior angles, AD∥BC)
AC = AC (common)
By ASA congruence: △ABC ≅ △CDA
By CPCT: AB = CD and BC = AD. □

Exercise Set 12.2 — Midpoint Theorem and Varignon’s Theorem
Q1
State and prove the Midpoint Theorem for a triangle.
5 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution

Statement: The line segment joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and equal to half of it.

Given: In △ABC, M and N are midpoints of AB and AC. To prove: MN ∥ BC and MN = BC/2.

Construction: Extend MN to point D such that MN = ND. Join CD.
In △AMN and △CDN:
AN = NC (N is midpoint of AC)
MN = ND (by construction)
∠ANM = ∠CND (vertically opposite)
By SAS: △AMN ≅ △CDN → AM = CD and CD ∥ AB
Also AM = MB → CD = MB and CD ∥ MB. So MBCD is a parallelogram → MN ∥ BC.
MD = BC (opposite sides of parallelogram); MN = MD/2 → MN = BC/2 □

Q3
Show that the diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular to each other.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Let ABCD be a rhombus with diagonals AC and BD intersecting at O.
Since a rhombus is a parallelogram, its diagonals bisect each other: OA = OC and OB = OD.
In ΔAOD and ΔCOD: OA = OC, OD = OD (common), AD = CD (sides of rhombus are equal).
Therefore, ΔAOD ≅ ΔCOD by SSS congruence.
∠AOD = ∠COD (by CPCT).
Since AC is a line, ∠AOD + ∠COD = 180° → 2∠AOD = 180° → ∠AOD = 90°. Hence proved.
Suggested Marks Distribution
Congruence of adjacent triangles using SSS1 Mark
Linear pair leading to 90 degrees1 Mark
Q2
ABC is a triangle right-angled at C. A line through the midpoint M of hypotenuse AB and parallel to BC intersects AC at D. Show that: (i) D is the midpoint of AC, (ii) MD ⊥ AC.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
(i) In ΔABC, M is the midpoint of AB and MD || BC.
By the Converse of Midpoint Theorem, a line drawn through the midpoint of one side of a triangle parallel to another side bisects the third side. Therefore, D is the midpoint of AC.
(ii) Since MD || BC and AC is transversal: ∠ADM = ∠C (corresponding angles).
Since ∠C = 90°, ∠ADM = 90° → MD ⊥ AC.

Q3
State Varignon's Theorem regarding the midpoints of sides of any quadrilateral.
2 Marks
✓ Suggested Step-by-Step Solution
Varignon's Theorem:
The figure formed by connecting the consecutive midpoints of the sides of any arbitrary quadrilateral is always a parallelogram.
Furthermore, the area of the Varignon parallelogram is equal to half the area of the original quadrilateral.

📝 Case Study & Real-Life Modeling
Civil Engineering & Structural Midpoints (Varignon's Theorem): Four structural columns A, B, C, D form an arbitrary non-planar quadrilateral ABCD on a construction site. Surveyors mark the midpoints of the four sides: P on AB, Q on BC, R on CD, and S on DA.
(a) State Varignon's Theorem concerning the quadrilateral PQRS formed by joining the midpoints.
(b) Prove that PQRS is always a parallelogram using the Midpoint Theorem on ΔABC and ΔADC.
(c) If the diagonals AC = 18 m and BD = 24 m intersect at right angles, what special shape does PQRS become?
(a) Varignon's Theorem: The figure formed by connecting the midpoints of the sides of any quadrilateral is always a parallelogram.
(b) In ΔABC, P and Q are midpoints of AB and BC. By the Midpoint Theorem: PQ || AC and PQ = ½ AC. In ΔADC, S and R are midpoints of AD and CD. By Midpoint Theorem: SR || AC and SR = ½ AC. Hence, PQ || SR and PQ = SR. Since one pair of opposite sides is equal and parallel, PQRS is a parallelogram.
(c) When diagonals AC ⊥ BD, the adjacent sides PQ (parallel to AC) and QR (parallel to BD) are perpendicular. A parallelogram with a right angle is a rectangle.
🧠 HOTS & Assertion-Reasoning
Assertion (A): The diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length and bisect each other.
Reason (R): A rectangle is a parallelogram with all four interior angles equal to 90°.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation. (b) Both A and R are true, but R is not correct explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.
Explanation: Since it is a parallelogram, its diagonals bisect each other. Considering right triangles with common base and equal heights, hypotenuses (the diagonals) are congruent by SAS.
