Q1
The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 3 : 5 : 9 : 13. Find all the angles of the quadrilateral.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Ratio of four angles = 3 : 5 : 9 : 13.
Let the common ratio multiplier be x.
Then the angles are: 3x, 5x, 9x, 13x.
Step 1: Angle Sum Property of a Quadrilateral:
The sum of all four interior angles of a quadrilateral is 360°:
3x + 5x + 9x + 13x = 360°
30x = 360° ⟹ x = 360° / 30 = 12°.
Step 2: Calculate each angle:
• First angle = 3x = 3 × 12° = 36°
• Second angle = 5x = 5 × 12° = 60°
• Third angle = 9x = 9 × 12° = 108°
• Fourth angle = 13x = 13 × 12° = 156°

Check: 36° + 60° + 108° + 156° = 360° (Verified).

Final Answer: The angles of the quadrilateral are 36°, 60°, 108°, and 156°.

Q2
If the diagonals of a parallelogram are equal, then show that it is a rectangle.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Parallelogram ABCD in which diagonals are equal: AC = BD.
To Prove: ABCD is a rectangle (i.e. one of its interior angles is 90°).
Proof:
In ΔABC and ΔDCB:
1. AB = DC (Opposite sides of parallelogram ABCD are equal)
2. BC = CB (Common base)
3. AC = DB (Given, diagonals are equal)

By SSS Congruence Rule:
ΔABC ≅ ΔDCB.

By CPCT:
∠ABC = ∠DCB    --- (1)

Now, since ABCD is a parallelogram, AB || DC and BC is a transversal.
Consecutive interior angles on the same side of transversal are supplementary:
∠ABC + ∠DCB = 180°
From (1), substituting ∠DCB = ∠ABC:
∠ABC + ∠ABC = 180° ⟹ 2∠ABC = 180° ⟹ ∠ABC = 90°.

Conclusion: A parallelogram with one right angle is a rectangle. Hence, ABCD is a rectangle. Hence Proved.

Q3
Show that if the diagonals of a quadrilateral bisect each other at right angles, then it is a rhombus.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Quadrilateral ABCD whose diagonals AC and BD intersect at O such that:
• OA = OC and OB = OD (Diagonals bisect each other)
• ∠AOB = ∠BOC = ∠COD = ∠DOA = 90° (Bisect at right angles).
To Prove: ABCD is a rhombus (all 4 sides are equal).
Proof:
In ΔAOD and ΔCOD:
1. OA = OC (Given, O is mid-point of AC)
2. ∠AOD = ∠COD = 90° (Given perpendicular diagonals)
3. OD = OD (Common side)

By SAS Congruence Rule:
ΔAOD ≅ ΔCOD.
By CPCT: AD = CD    --- (1)

Similarly, by considering adjacent pairs of triangles:
• ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOB ⟹ AB = BC
• ΔCOB ≅ ΔCOD ⟹ BC = CD

Combining all equalities:
AB = BC = CD = DA.

Conclusion: A quadrilateral with all four sides equal is a rhombus. Hence, ABCD is a rhombus. Hence Proved.

Q4
Show that the diagonals of a square are equal and bisect each other at right angles.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Let ABCD be a square. Diagonals AC and BD intersect at O.
All sides are equal: AB = BC = CD = DA and all angles are 90°: ∠A = ∠B = ∠C = ∠D = 90°.
To Prove: (i) AC = BD, (ii) OA = OC, OB = OD, (iii) AC ⊥ BD (∠AOB = 90°).
Part (i) Prove Diagonals are Equal (AC = BD):
In ΔABC and ΔBAD:
• AB = BA (Common side)
• ∠ABC = ∠BAD = 90° (Angles of square)
• BC = AD (Sides of square)
By SAS rule: ΔABC ≅ ΔBAD ⟹ AC = BD (CPCT).
Part (ii) Prove Diagonals Bisect Each Other:
Since every square is a parallelogram, and the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other:
OA = OC and OB = OD.
Part (iii) Prove Diagonals are Perpendicular (AC ⊥ BD):
In ΔAOB and ΔCOB:
• OA = OC (Proven in part ii)
• AB = CB (Sides of square)
• OB = OB (Common side)
By SSS rule: ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOB ⟹ ∠AOB = ∠COB (CPCT).
Since AOC is a straight line: ∠AOB + ∠COB = 180° ⟹ 2∠AOB = 180° ⟹ ∠AOB = 90°.
Therefore, AC ⊥ BD. Hence Proved.

Q5
Show that if the diagonals of a quadrilateral are equal and bisect each other at right angles, then it is a square.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Quadrilateral ABCD where diagonals AC and BD intersect at O with:
• AC = BD (Equal diagonals)
• OA = OC, OB = OD (Diagonals bisect each other)
• AC ⊥ BD (∠AOB = 90°).
To Prove: ABCD is a square.
Step 1: Prove ABCD is a Parallelogram and Rhombus:
Since diagonals AC and BD bisect each other, ABCD is a parallelogram.
In ΔAOB and ΔCOB:
OA = OC, ∠AOB = ∠COB = 90°, OB = OB (Common).
By SAS rule: ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOB ⟹ AB = BC.
Since adjacent sides are equal in parallelogram ABCD, AB = BC = CD = DA. So ABCD is a rhombus.
Step 2: Prove each angle is 90°:
In ΔABC and ΔBAD:
• AB = BA (Common)
• BC = AD (Sides of rhombus)
• AC = BD (Given equal diagonals)
By SSS rule: ΔABC ≅ ΔBAD ⟹ ∠ABC = ∠BAD (by CPCT).

Since AD || BC (opposite sides of parallelogram) and AB is transversal:
∠BAD + ∠ABC = 180° (Consecutive interior angles)
2∠ABC = 180° ⟹ ∠ABC = 90°.

Conclusion: ABCD is a rhombus with an angle of 90°, which makes it a square. Hence Proved.

Q6
Diagonal AC of a parallelogram ABCD bisects ∠A. Show that:
(i) it bisects ∠C also,
(ii) ABCD is a rhombus.

✅ CBSE Standard Answer

(i) To prove AC bisects ∠C:
Since ABCD is a parallelogram:
• AB || CD and AC is transversal ⟹ ∠BAC = ∠DCA (Alternate interior angles)  --- (2)
• AD || BC and AC is transversal ⟹ ∠DAC = ∠BCA (Alternate interior angles)  --- (3)

From (1), ∠DAC = ∠BAC. Comparing with (2) and (3):
∠DCA = ∠BCA.
Therefore, diagonal AC bisects ∠C also. Hence Proved (i).
(ii) To prove ABCD is a rhombus:
From (1) and (2): ∠DAC = ∠DCA.
In ΔADC, sides opposite to equal angles are equal:
AD = CD.
Since ABCD is a parallelogram with adjacent sides equal (AD = CD):
AB = BC = CD = DA.
Therefore, ABCD is a rhombus. Hence Proved (ii).

Q7
ABCD is a rhombus. Show that diagonal AC bisects ∠A as well as ∠C and diagonal BD bisects ∠B as well as ∠D.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: ABCD is a rhombus ⟹ AB = BC = CD = DA and AB || CD, AD || BC.
Proof for Diagonal AC:
In ΔABC: AB = BC ⟹ ∠BAC = ∠BCA (Angles opposite equal sides)  --- (1)
Since AB || CD: ∠BAC = ∠DCA (Alternate interior angles)  --- (2)
From (1) and (2): ∠BCA = ∠DCA ⟹ AC bisects ∠C.

Similarly, since AD || BC: ∠DAC = ∠BCA. With (1): ∠DAC = ∠BAC ⟹ AC bisects ∠A.
Proof for Diagonal BD:
In ΔABD: AB = AD ⟹ ∠ABD = ∠ADB.
By alternate interior angles with parallel lines AB || CD and AD || BC:
∠ABD = ∠CDB and ∠ADB = ∠CBD.
Therefore, ∠ABD = ∠CBD (BD bisects ∠B) and ∠ADB = ∠CDB (BD bisects ∠D). Hence Proved.

Q8
ABCD is a rectangle in which diagonal AC bisects ∠A as well as ∠C. Show that:
(i) ABCD is a square
(ii) diagonal BD bisects ∠B as well as ∠D.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Rectangle ABCD (∠A = ∠B = ∠C = ∠D = 90°) where diagonal AC bisects ∠A and ∠C.
(i) To prove ABCD is a square:
Since AC bisects ∠A (= 90°):
∠DAC = ∠BAC = 90° / 2 = 45°  --- (1)
Since AC bisects ∠C (= 90°):
∠DCA = ∠BCA = 90° / 2 = 45°  --- (2)

In ΔADC, from (1) and (2):
∠DAC = ∠DCA = 45°.
Sides opposite to equal angles are equal:
AD = CD.
A rectangle with adjacent sides equal (AD = CD) has all four sides equal: AB = BC = CD = DA.
Hence, ABCD is a square. Hence Proved (i).
(ii) To prove diagonal BD bisects ∠B as well as ∠D:
Since ABCD is proven to be a square, AB = AD and CB = CD.
In ΔABD, AB = AD ⟹ ∠ABD = ∠ADB.
Since ∠A = 90°, ∠ABD = ∠ADB = (180° - 90°)/2 = 45°.
Therefore, ∠CBD = ∠B - ∠ABD = 90° - 45° = 45°.
So ∠ABD = ∠CBD = 45° (BD bisects ∠B), and ∠ADB = ∠CDB = 45° (BD bisects ∠D). Hence Proved (ii).

Exercise 8.2 (The Mid-Point Theorem)
Q1
ABCD is a quadrilateral in which P, Q, R and S are mid-points of the sides AB, BC, CD and DA. AC is a diagonal. Show that:
(i) SR || AC and SR = 1/2 AC
(ii) PQ = SR
(iii) PQRS is a parallelogram.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Mid-Point Theorem Statement: The line segment joining the mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and is half of it.
(i) In ΔACD:
S is mid-point of AD and R is mid-point of CD.
By Mid-Point Theorem:
SR || AC and SR = 1/2 AC    --- (1). Hence Proved (i).
(ii) In ΔABC:
P is mid-point of AB and Q is mid-point of BC.
By Mid-Point Theorem:
PQ || AC and PQ = 1/2 AC    --- (2).
From (1) and (2):
Both SR and PQ are equal to 1/2 AC.
Therefore, PQ = SR. Hence Proved (ii).
(iii) To prove PQRS is a parallelogram:
From (1) and (2):
SR || AC and PQ || AC ⟹ PQ || SR.
Also, PQ = SR.
Since one pair of opposite sides (PQ and SR) of quadrilateral PQRS is equal and parallel:
PQRS is a parallelogram. Hence Proved (iii).

Q2
ABCD is a rhombus and P, Q, R and S are the mid-points of the sides AB, BC, CD and DA respectively. Show that the quadrilateral PQRS is a rectangle.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: ABCD is a rhombus. Diagonals AC and BD intersect at right angles (AC ⊥ BD).
P, Q, R, S are mid-points of AB, BC, CD, DA respectively.
To Prove: PQRS is a rectangle.
Proof:
1. From Q1, joining mid-points of any quadrilateral gives a parallelogram:
In ΔABC: PQ || AC and PQ = 1/2 AC.
In ΔADC: SR || AC and SR = 1/2 AC.
Therefore, PQ || SR and PQ = SR ⟹ PQRS is a parallelogram.

2. In ΔABD, by Mid-Point Theorem:
SP || BD.

3. Now, PQ || AC and SP || BD.
Since diagonals of rhombus ABCD intersect at right angles: AC ⊥ BD.
Since PQ || AC and SP || BD, the angle between PQ and SP must be equal to the angle between AC and BD.
Therefore, ∠SPQ = 90°.

Conclusion: A parallelogram with one right angle is a rectangle. Hence, PQRS is a rectangle. Hence Proved.

(i) D is the mid-point of AC
(ii) MD ⊥ AC
(iii) CM = MA = 1/2 AB
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: ΔABC right-angled at C (∠ACB = 90°). M is mid-point of AB. Line through M parallel to BC intersects AC at D (MD || BC).
(i) To prove D is mid-point of AC:
In ΔABC, M is mid-point of AB and MD || BC.
By the Converse of Mid-Point Theorem: The line drawn through the mid-point of one side of a triangle parallel to another side bisects the third side.
Therefore, D is the mid-point of AC. Hence Proved (i).
(ii) To prove MD ⊥ AC:
Since MD || BC and AC is a transversal, corresponding angles are equal:
∠ADM = ∠ACB.
Given ∠ACB = 90° ⟹ ∠ADM = 90°.
Therefore, MD ⊥ AC. Hence Proved (ii).
(iii) To prove CM = MA = 1/2 AB:
Join C to M. In ΔADM and ΔCDM:
1. AD = CD (D is mid-point of AC from (i))
2. ∠ADM = ∠CDM = 90° (From (ii))
3. MD = MD (Common side)
By SAS Congruence Rule: ΔADM ≅ ΔCDM.
By CPCT: MA = MC (or CM = MA).
Since M is mid-point of hypotenuse AB, MA = 1/2 AB.
Therefore, CM = MA = 1/2 AB. Hence Proved (iii).

📝 Case Study Question
Kite Manufacturing & Symmetry: A kite maker cuts a diamond quadrilateral ABCD where diagonals AC and BD intersect at O. AC is 16 cm and BD is 12 cm. Midpoints of the four edges are joined to form an inner logo border PQRS.
(a) What type of geometric figure is the logo border PQRS?
(b) Find the perimeter of PQRS.
(c) Find the area of PQRS.
(a) By Mid-Point theorem, PQRS is a parallelogram (if ABCD is a rhombus, PQRS is a rectangle).
(b) Sides of PQRS: PQ = SR = 1/2 AC = 8 cm, and SP = QR = 1/2 BD = 6 cm.
Perimeter = 2(8 + 6) = 28 cm.
(c) Area = Length × Breadth = 8 × 6 = 48 cm².
🧠 HOTS
HOTS: In a parallelogram ABCD, the bisectors of consecutive angles ∠A and ∠B intersect at P. Prove that ∠APB = 90°.
In parallelogram ABCD, consecutive interior angles are supplementary: ∠A + ∠B = 180°.
Dividing by 2: 1/2 ∠A + 1/2 ∠B = 90°.
Since AP and BP bisect ∠A and ∠B: ∠PAB + ∠PBA = 90°.
In ΔPAB, by angle sum property: ∠APB = 180° - (∠PAB + ∠PBA) = 180° - 90° = 90°.
