Exercise 10.1
Q1
A traffic signal board, indicating 'SCHOOL AHEAD', is an equilateral triangle with side 'a'. Find the area of the signal board, using Heron's formula. If its perimeter is 180 cm, what will be the area of the signal board?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Part A: Derive Area Formula using Heron's Formula:
For an equilateral triangle with side a:
Three sides are a, b = a, c = a.
Semi-perimeter s = (a + a + a) / 2 = 3a / 2.

By Heron's Formula: Area = √[s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)]
s - a = 3a/2 - a = a/2
s - b = a/2
s - c = a/2

Area = √[(3a/2) × (a/2) × (a/2) × (a/2)] = √[3a⁴ / 16] = (√3 / 4) a².
Part B: When Perimeter is 180 cm:
Perimeter 2s = 3a = 180 cm ⟹ a = 180 / 3 = 60 cm.
Semi-perimeter s = 180 / 2 = 90 cm.

Substituting a = 60 into the derived formula:
Area = (√3 / 4) × (60)² = (√3 / 4) × 3600 = 900√3 cm².

Final Answer: Derived formula is (√3/4) a² and the area of the signal board is 900√3 cm² (≈ 1558.85 cm²).

Q2
The triangular side walls of a flyover have been used for advertisements. The sides of the walls are 122 m, 22 m and 120 m. The advertisements yield an earning of ₹ 5000 per m² per year. A company hired one of its walls for 3 months. How much rent did it pay?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Calculate Area using Heron's Formula:
Sides of triangular wall: a = 122 m, b = 22 m, c = 120 m.
Semi-perimeter s = (122 + 22 + 120) / 2 = 264 / 2 = 132 m.

Differences:
s - a = 132 - 122 = 10 m
s - b = 132 - 22 = 110 m
s - c = 132 - 120 = 12 m

Area = √[s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)] = √[132 × 10 × 110 × 12]
Factorising:
132 = 11 × 12, 110 = 11 × 10.
Area = √[(11 × 12) × 10 × (11 × 10) × 12] = √[11² × 12² × 10²] = 11 × 12 × 10 = 1320 m².
Step 2: Calculate Rent for 3 Months:
Annual rate per m² = ₹ 5000 / year.
Annual rent for 1320 m² = 1320 × 5000 = ₹ 66,00,000.
Rent for 3 months (3/12 year = 1/4 year):
Rent = 1320 × 5000 × (3 / 12) = 1320 × 5000 × (1 / 4) = 330 × 5000 = ₹ 16,50,000.

Final Answer: The company paid ₹ 16,50,000 as rent.

Q3
There is a slide in a park. One of its side walls has been painted in some colour with a message "KEEP THE PARK GREEN AND CLEAN". If the sides of the wall are 15 m, 11 m and 6 m, find the area painted in colour.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Sides of triangular wall are a = 15 m, b = 11 m, c = 6 m.
Step 1: Calculate semi-perimeter:
s = (a + b + c) / 2 = (15 + 11 + 6) / 2 = 32 / 2 = 16 m.
Step 2: Calculate factors:
s - a = 16 - 15 = 1 m
s - b = 16 - 11 = 5 m
s - c = 16 - 6 = 10 m
Step 3: Apply Heron's Formula:
Area = √[s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)] = √[16 × 1 × 5 × 10]
= √[16 × 5 × (5 × 2)] = √[16 × 25 × 2] = 4 × 5 × √2 = 20√2 m².

Final Answer: The area painted in colour is 20√2 m² (≈ 28.28 m²).

Q4
Find the area of a triangle two sides of which are 18 cm and 10 cm and the perimeter is 42 cm.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given:
• Perimeter P = 42 cm ⟹ s = 42 / 2 = 21 cm.
• Side a = 18 cm, side b = 10 cm.
Step 1: Find the third side c:
a + b + c = 42
18 + 10 + c = 42 ⟹ 28 + c = 42 ⟹ c = 42 - 28 = 14 cm.
Step 2: Calculate differences:
s - a = 21 - 18 = 3 cm
s - b = 21 - 10 = 11 cm
s - c = 21 - 14 = 7 cm
Step 3: Apply Heron's Formula:
Area = √[s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)] = √[21 × 3 × 11 × 7]
Notice that 21 = 3 × 7:
Area = √[(3 × 7) × 3 × 11 × 7] = √[3² × 7² × 11] = 3 × 7 × √11 = 21√11 cm².

Final Answer: The area of the triangle is 21√11 cm² (≈ 69.65 cm²).

Q5
Sides of a triangle are in the ratio of 12 : 17 : 25 and its perimeter is 540 cm. Find its area.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Find individual sides:
Ratio of sides = 12 : 17 : 25. Let sides be 12x, 17x, 25x.
Perimeter: 12x + 17x + 25x = 540 cm
54x = 540 ⟹ x = 10.

Therefore, the side lengths are:
a = 12 × 10 = 120 cm
b = 17 × 10 = 170 cm
c = 25 × 10 = 250 cm.
Step 2: Semi-perimeter and differences:
s = 540 / 2 = 270 cm.
s - a = 270 - 120 = 150 cm
s - b = 270 - 170 = 100 cm
s - c = 270 - 250 = 20 cm
Step 3: Apply Heron's Formula:
Area = √[270 × 150 × 100 × 20]
Factorising:
= √[(27 × 10) × (15 × 10) × 100 × (2 × 10)]
= √[(3³ × 10) × (3 × 5 × 10) × 10² × (2 × 10)]
= √[3⁴ × (5 × 2) × 10⁴] = √[81 × 10 × 10⁴] = √[81 × 10⁶] = 9 × 10³ = 9000 cm².

Final Answer: The area of the triangle is 9000 cm².

Q6
An isosceles triangle has perimeter 30 cm and each of the equal sides is 12 cm. Find the area of the triangle.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given:
• Perimeter P = 30 cm ⟹ s = 30 / 2 = 15 cm.
• Equal sides: a = 12 cm, b = 12 cm.
Step 1: Find the third side c:
a + b + c = 30 ⟹ 12 + 12 + c = 30 ⟹ 24 + c = 30 ⟹ c = 6 cm.
Step 2: Calculate differences:
s - a = 15 - 12 = 3 cm
s - b = 15 - 12 = 3 cm
s - c = 15 - 6 = 9 cm
Step 3: Apply Heron's Formula:
Area = √[s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)] = √[15 × 3 × 3 × 9]
= √[15 × 3² × 3²] = 3 × 3 × √15 = 9√15 cm².

Final Answer: The area of the isosceles triangle is 9√15 cm² (≈ 34.86 cm²).

Q1
A park, in the shape of a quadrilateral ABCD, has ∠C = 90°, AB = 9 m, BC = 12 m, CD = 5 m and AD = 8 m. How much area does it occupy?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Construction: Join BD, dividing quadrilateral ABCD into two triangles: right-angled ΔBCD and general ΔABD.
Step 1: Area of right-angled ΔBCD:
In right ΔBCD with ∠C = 90°:
Area(ΔBCD) = 1/2 × Base × Height = 1/2 × 12 × 5 = 30 m².

By Pythagoras theorem in ΔBCD:
BD = √(BC² + CD²) = √(12² + 5²) = √(144 + 25) = √169 = 13 m.
Step 2: Area of ΔABD using Heron's Formula:
Sides of ΔABD: a = 9 m, b = 8 m, c = 13 m.
Semi-perimeter s = (9 + 8 + 13) / 2 = 30 / 2 = 15 m.
s - a = 15 - 9 = 6 m
s - b = 15 - 8 = 7 m
s - c = 15 - 13 = 2 m

Area(ΔABD) = √[15 × 6 × 7 × 2] = √[1260] = 6√35 ≈ 6 × 5.916 = 35.5 m² (approx).
Step 3: Total Area of quadrilateral ABCD:
Total Area = Area(ΔBCD) + Area(ΔABD) = 30 + 35.5 = 65.5 m² (approx).

Final Answer: The park occupies approximately 65.5 m².

Q2
Find the area of a quadrilateral ABCD in which AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm, CD = 4 cm, DA = 5 cm and AC = 5 cm.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Quadrilateral ABCD divided by diagonal AC = 5 cm into ΔABC and ΔACD.
Step 1: Area of ΔABC:
Sides are AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm, AC = 5 cm.
Notice that: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5².
By Converse of Pythagoras Theorem, ΔABC is right-angled at B (∠B = 90°).
Area(ΔABC) = 1/2 × AB × BC = 1/2 × 3 × 4 = 6 cm².
Step 2: Area of ΔACD using Heron's Formula:
Sides are AC = 5 cm, CD = 4 cm, DA = 5 cm (Isosceles triangle).
Semi-perimeter s = (5 + 4 + 5) / 2 = 14 / 2 = 7 cm.
s - a = 7 - 5 = 2 cm
s - b = 7 - 4 = 3 cm
s - c = 7 - 5 = 2 cm

Area(ΔACD) = √[7 × 2 × 3 × 2] = √[84] = 2√21 ≈ 2 × 4.583 = 9.17 cm² (approx).
Step 3: Total Area of ABCD:
Total Area = Area(ΔABC) + Area(ΔACD) = 6 + 9.17 = 15.2 cm² (approx).

Final Answer: The area of quadrilateral ABCD is (6 + 2√21) cm² ≈ 15.2 cm².

Q3
A triangle and a parallelogram have the same base and the same area. If the sides of the triangle are 26 cm, 28 cm and 30 cm, and the parallelogram stands on the base 28 cm, find the height of the parallelogram.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Find Area of Triangle using Heron's Formula:
Sides: a = 26 cm, b = 28 cm, c = 30 cm.
Semi-perimeter s = (26 + 28 + 30) / 2 = 84 / 2 = 42 cm.

Differences:
s - a = 42 - 26 = 16 cm
s - b = 42 - 28 = 14 cm
s - c = 42 - 30 = 12 cm

Area = √[42 × 16 × 14 × 12] = √[(14 × 3) × 16 × 14 × (4 × 3)] = √[14² × 3² × 4² × 2²] = 14 × 3 × 4 × 2 = 336 cm².
Step 2: Find Height of Parallelogram:
Given: Area(Parallelogram) = Area(Triangle) = 336 cm².
Formula: Area = Base × Height.
Base = 28 cm.
28 × Height = 336 ⟹ Height = 336 / 28 = 12 cm.

Final Answer: The height of the parallelogram is 12 cm.

A rhombus shaped field has green grass for 18 cows to graze. If each side of the rhombus is 30 m and its longer diagonal is 48 m, how much area of grass field will each cow be getting?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Step 1: Area of one half triangle:
The longer diagonal 48 m divides the rhombus into two congruent triangles.
Each triangle has sides: a = 30 m, b = 30 m, c = 48 m.
Semi-perimeter s = (30 + 30 + 48) / 2 = 108 / 2 = 54 m.
s - a = 54 - 30 = 24 m
s - b = 54 - 30 = 24 m
s - c = 54 - 48 = 6 m

Area(one triangle) = √[54 × 24 × 24 × 6] = √[(9 × 6) × 24² × 6] = √[3² × 6² × 24²] = 3 × 6 × 24 = 432 m².
Step 2: Total Area of Rhombus:
Total Area = 2 × 432 = 864 m².
Step 3: Area per cow:
Total cows = 18.
Area per cow = 864 / 18 = 48 m².

Final Answer: Each cow will get 48 m² of grass field.

Step 2: Area of each colour:
Each colour has 5 triangular pieces:
Cloth of each colour = 5 × 200√6 = 1000√6 cm².

Final Answer: 1000√6 cm² (≈ 2449.49 cm²) of cloth of each colour is required.

s - a = 36 - 9 = 27 cm
s - b = 36 - 28 = 8 cm

Step 2: Total Area of 16 tiles:
Total Area = 16 × 36√6 = 576√6 ≈ 576 × 2.45 = 1411.2 cm².
Step 3: Cost of Polishing:
Cost = 1411.2 × ₹ 0.50 = ₹ 705.60.

Final Answer: Total cost of polishing is ₹ 705.60 (approx).

Q7
A field is in the shape of a trapezium whose parallel sides are 25 m and 10 m. The non-parallel sides are 14 m and 13 m. Find the area of the field.
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given: Trapezium ABCD with parallel sides AB = 25 m and CD = 10 m, non-parallel sides AD = 13 m and BC = 14 m.
Construction: Through C, draw CE || DA meeting AB at E.
Step 1: Analyze parallelogram AECD and triangle ΔCEB:
In quadrilateral AECD:
• CD || AE and CE || DA ⟹ AECD is a parallelogram.
• Therefore, AE = CD = 10 m and CE = DA = 13 m.

Now in ΔCEB:
• Side EB = AB - AE = 25 - 10 = 15 m.
• Side CE = 13 m.
• Side BC = 14 m.
Step 2: Area of ΔCEB using Heron's Formula:
s = (15 + 13 + 14) / 2 = 42 / 2 = 21 m.
s - 15 = 6 m, s - 13 = 8 m, s - 14 = 7 m.
Area(ΔCEB) = √[21 × 6 × 8 × 7] = √[(7 × 3) × (3 × 2) × 8 × 7] = √[7² × 3² × 16] = 7 × 3 × 4 = 84 m².
Step 3: Height of trapezium (h):
Area of ΔCEB = 1/2 × Base(EB) × Height(h)
84 = 1/2 × 15 × h ⟹ h = (84 × 2) / 15 = 168 / 15 = 11.2 m.
Step 4: Area of Trapezium ABCD:
Area = 1/2 × (Sum of parallel sides) × h = 1/2 × (25 + 10) × 11.2 = 1/2 × 35 × 11.2 = 35 × 5.6 = 196 m².

Final Answer: The area of the field is 196 m².

Landscape Gardening Plot: A landscape architect divides a triangular patch of ornamental flowers with perimeter 60 m into two sections by constructing a low fence along a median. The sides of the triangular patch are 20 m, 24 m, and 16 m.
(a) Find the semi-perimeter of the plot.
(b) Calculate the total area of the triangular patch using Heron's formula.
(c) What is the area of each flower section divided by the median?
(a) Semi-perimeter s = 60 / 2 = 30 m.
(b) Differences: s - a = 10, s - b = 6, s - c = 14.
Area = √[30 × 10 × 6 × 14] = √[25200] = 60√7 m² (≈ 158.75 m²).
(c) A median of a triangle divides it into two triangles of equal area. Hence each section has area 1/2 × 60√7 = 30√7 m² (≈ 79.37 m²).
🧠 HOTS
HOTS: If each side of a triangle is doubled, find the percentage increase in its area.
Let original sides be a, b, c with semi-perimeter s and original area Δ = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)].
New sides are 2a, 2b, 2c with new semi-perimeter s' = 2s.
New differences: s' - 2a = 2(s-a), s' - 2b = 2(s-b), s' - 2c = 2(s-c).
New Area Δ' = √[2s × 2(s-a) × 2(s-b) × 2(s-c)] = √[16 s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)] = 4Δ.
Increase in area = Δ' - Δ = 4Δ - Δ = 3Δ.
Percentage increase = (3Δ / Δ) × 100% = 300%.
