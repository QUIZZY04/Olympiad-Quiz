📝 Case Study & Real-Life Modeling
Smart Delivery Drone Navigation (Cartesian Coordinates): A delivery hub is located at the origin O(0, 0). Drone Alpha delivers packages to customer A at (4, 3) and then directly to customer B at (-2, 3), before returning to the hub.
(a) In which quadrant is customer A located?
(b) Calculate the straight-line distance from customer A(4, 3) to customer B(-2, 3).
(c) Find the total round-trip distance traveled by Drone Alpha.
(a) Customer A(4, 3) has both coordinates positive, so it lies in Quadrant I.
(b) Since both points have the same y-coordinate (y = 3), distance AB = |x₂ - x₁| = |(-2) - 4| = 6 units (or by distance formula: √((-2-4)² + (3-3)²) = √36 = 6).
(c) OA = √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 units. OB = √((-2)² + 3²) = √(4 + 9) = √13 ≈ 3.61 units. Total round-trip distance = OA + AB + BO = 5 + 6 + √13 = 11 + √13 units (≈ 14.61 units).
🧠 HOTS & Assertion-Reasoning
Assertion (A): The distance between the points P(-3, 0) and Q(5, 0) on the x-axis is 8 units.
Reason (R): The distance between two points (x₁, 0) and (x₂, 0) on the x-axis is given by |x₂ - x₁|.
Choose: (a) Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation of A. (b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation. (c) A is true, R is false. (d) A is false, R is true.
Correct Choice: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
Explanation: Distance PQ = |5 - (-3)| = |5 + 3| = 8 units. Reason (R) states the universal formula for points lying on the horizontal axis, which directly explains (A).
