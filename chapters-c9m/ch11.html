Exercise 11.1 (Surface Area of a Right Circular Cone)
Q1
Diameter of the base of a cone is 10.5 cm and its slant height is 10 cm. Find its curved surface area.
2 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Given:
• Diameter d = 10.5 cm ⟹ Radius r = 10.5 / 2 = 5.25 cm (or 21/4 cm).
• Slant height l = 10 cm.
Formula & Calculation:
Curved Surface Area (CSA) of cone = πrl
CSA = (22/7) × (21/4) × 10 = (22 × 3 × 10) / 4 = 660 / 4 = 165 cm².

Final Answer: The curved surface area of the cone is 165 cm².

(i) radius of the base
(ii) total surface area of the cone.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer

(ii) Find Total Surface Area (TSA):

Final Answer: (i) 4πr², (ii) 4πr², (iii) 1 : 1.

Q9
Twenty seven solid iron spheres, each of radius r and surface area S are melted to form a sphere with surface area S'. Find the:
(i) radius r' of the new sphere
(ii) ratio of S and S'.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer
Conservation of Volume:
Volume of 27 small spheres = Volume of 1 large sphere
27 × (4/3 π r³) = 4/3 π (r')³
Cancelling 4/3 π from both sides:
(r')³ = 27r³ = (3r)³ ⟹ r' = 3r.
(i) Radius of the new sphere r' = 3r.
(ii) Ratio of S and S':
Surface area of small sphere S = 4πr².
Surface area of new sphere S' = 4π(r')² = 4π(3r)² = 4π(9r²) = 9(4πr²) = 9S.
Therefore:
S / S' = S / 9S = 1 / 9 = 1 : 9.

Final Answer: (i) r' = 3r; (ii) Ratio S : S' = 1 : 9.
