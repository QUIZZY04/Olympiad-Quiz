H1

Three Questions

Honeycomb (Prose) • Author: Leo Tolstoy — A king searches for the answers to the three most vital questions in life

Chapter Summary & Core Theme
A certain king believed that he would never fail if he knew the right time to begin everything, the right people to listen to, and the most important thing to do. He proclaimed a great reward, but the answers from wise men were diverse and contradictory. Disappointed, the king visited a wise hermit living in a forest. While the king helped the frail hermit dig flower beds, an injured, bleeding man ran towards them. The king washed and bandaged his severe stomach wound, saving his life. The man revealed he was the king's sworn enemy who came to assassinate him, but was wounded by the king's bodyguard. Overwhelmed by the king's mercy, the enemy asked for forgiveness and pledged loyalty. The hermit then revealed the answers: The most important time is NOW (the present moment); the most important person is the one you are currently with; and the most important deed is to do good to that person.
🎯 Central Theme: The importance of living in the present moment, listening to conscience, and performing acts of benevolence to humanity.

Why did the king want to know answers to three questions?
2 Marks
Detailed Solution

The king wanted to know the answers to the three questions because he believed that if he always knew:

1. What was the right time to begin everything,
2. Who were the right people to listen to (and whom to avoid), and
3. What was the most important thing for him to do,
he would never fail in any undertaking or governance in his life.

Comprehension Check — Page 14 (Q1)
Who was the bearded man who came running out of the woods? Why did he ask for the king's forgiveness?
3 Marks
Detailed Solution
1. Identity of the Bearded Man: The bearded man was the king's sworn enemy. The king had previously executed his brother and seized his ancestral property, so the man had resolved to ambush and assassinate the king on his return journey from the hermit's hut.
2. Reason for Asking Forgiveness: When the king's bodyguards recognized and wounded him, he escaped to the hermit's hut where he would have bled to death. The king, unaware of his identity, tirelessly washed and bandaged his severe wound and gave him fresh water, saving his life. Overwhelmed with remorse that the man he came to kill had saved his life, he asked for the king's forgiveness and promised lifelong faithful servitude.

Working with the Text — Q1
Why was the king advised to go to magicians?
2 Marks
Detailed Solution
In reply to the first question (the right time for every action), some wise men argued that to decide the right time for doing something, one must look into the future. Since only magicians possess the supernatural power to look into the future, they advised the king that he would have to consult magicians to know the right time for every action.

Need to know the future to decide the right time 1 Mark

Working with the Text — Q2
In answer to the second question, whose advice did the people say would be important to the king?
2 Marks
Detailed Solution
In response to the second question (who were the right people to listen to), the wise men gave varying opinions:
  • Some said the king's councillors were the most necessary advisors.
  • Others suggested the priests (clergy) were the most important.
  • A few recommended doctors for maintaining life and health.
  • Yet others claimed that his soldiers (warriors) were the most essential for defense and conquest.

Working with the Text — Q4
Did the wise men win the reward? If not, why not?
2 Marks
Detailed Solution
No, none of the wise men won the reward.
Reason: Their answers were so diverse, contradictory, and conflicting that the king could not agree with any of them. Since no answer proved convincing or satisfying to his intellect, the king gave the reward to no one.

Working with the Text — Q5
How did the king and the hermit help the wounded man?
3 Marks
Detailed Solution
1. The king unfastened the wounded man's clothing and discovered a large bleeding wound in his stomach.
2. The king washed the wound thoroughly and covered it with his own handkerchief and a towel provided by the hermit.
3. When blood continued to flow profusely, the king re-dressed the wound repeatedly until the bleeding finally ceased.
4. The king then brought fresh drinking water to revive the parched man.
5. As darkness fell and the air turned cool, the king and the hermit carried the wounded man inside the hut and laid him comfortably on the bed to rest safely overnight.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Washing and re-dressing the stomach wound with handkerchief/towel 1.5 Marks

Working with the Text — Q6
What were the hermit's answers to the three questions? Write each answer separately. Which answer do you like most, and why?
5 Marks
Detailed Solution
The Hermit's Answers:
1. The Right Time: The most important time is NOW (the present moment). It is the only time over which human beings possess any actual control or power to act.
2. The Right Person: The most important person is the person you are with at any particular moment, for no one knows whether they will ever meet anyone else in the future.
3. The Most Important Thing to Do: The most important business is to do good to the person you are with, because humanity was sent into this earthly life for that sole purpose.
Personal Preference & Justification:
I like the answer regarding the present time (NOW) the most. People often waste precious life regretting the unchangeable past or worrying anxiously about the uncertain future. Focusing entirely on the present moment empowers us to act ethically, solve problems immediately, and live meaningful, productive lives.

💡 Competency-Based & HOTS Questions (CBSE 2026-27 Pattern) Analytical Thinking
Competency-Based Case Study: Practical Ethics
In modern corporate leadership and daily student life, how does Leo Tolstoy's philosophy of 'The Three Questions' help reduce anxiety and build empathetic relationships?

Tolstoy's philosophy provides two transformative life principles:

  1. Mindfulness over Multitasking: By realizing that the only real time is 'NOW', students avoid cognitive overload and procrastination. They focus deeply on the task at hand rather than future exam anxieties.
  2. Empathetic Human Relations: Treating the person standing in front of you as the most important individual fosters active listening, kindness, and deep mutual respect in families, friendships, and workplaces.
HOTS / Value-Based Question
How did the king turn an assassination attempt into an enduring lifelong alliance of friendship?

The king exemplified the transformative power of forgiveness and compassion. Instead of executing his enemy when he discovered his identity, the king treated his wounds with tenderness, promised his royal physicians would care for him, and restored his confiscated property. Compassion disarmed hatred, proving that love conquers enmity far more permanently than military force.
