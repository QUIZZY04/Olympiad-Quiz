IMO Syllabus Coverage

Our practice material is meticulously designed to cover the entire spectrum of the Mathematics Olympiad syllabus for Class 1 through Class 10. We ensure that students are well-versed in both foundational concepts and advanced application-based problems. Our test series covers all major sections:

  • Logical Reasoning: Pattern identification, series completion, coding-decoding, and spatial recognition.
  • Mathematical Reasoning: Number systems, fractions, decimals, algebra, geometry, and mensuration.
  • Everyday Mathematics: Practical application of math in daily life scenarios, including money, time, and data handling.
  • Achievers Section: High-order thinking skills (HOTS) questions that carry higher weightage and determine top ranks in SOF IMO.

IMO Preparation Guide for Class 1 to 10

Mathematics Olympiads require a different approach than school exams. Here is how we help students at different levels:

Primary Level (Class 1-4)

Focus on Number Sense, Basic Geometry, and Pictographs. Our tests use visual aids to help younger students grasp logical reasoning patterns.

Middle & Secondary (Class 5-10)

Advanced topics like Algebra, Ratio-Proportion, and Data Handling. We include "Achievers Section" questions that mimic the high-difficulty level of SOF second-level exams.
