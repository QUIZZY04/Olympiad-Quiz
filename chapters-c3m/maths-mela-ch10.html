Chapter 10: Fun at Class Party!

Repeated Addition, Arrays, Multiplication Tables (2, 3, 4, 5, 10) & Party Budgets | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Multiplication as repeated addition of equal groups.
  • Arranging objects in rows and columns: understanding arrays (e.g., 4 rows of 5).
  • Building multiplication tables for 2, 3, 4, 5, and 10 using patterns.
  • Calculating costs for party food items using unit rates.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Array Model • Multiplication Sentence
In the classroom party, chairs are placed in 5 rows with 8 chairs in each row. (a) Write the repeated addition fact. (b) Write the multiplication fact. (c) How many total chairs are there?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• Number of rows = 5
• Chairs in each row = 8
(a) Repeated Addition: 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 40.
(b) Multiplication Fact: 5 × 8 = 40.
(c) Total Chairs: 40 chairs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct repeated addition statement 1.0 M
Correct multiplication statement 1.0 M
Correct total chair count 1.0 M
Q2 Multiplication Tables • Patterns
Look at the 5 times table: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30... (a) What pattern do you notice in the ones digit? (b) Can any number ending in 7 be in the 5 times table? Why or why not?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Analysis:
(a) Ones Digit Pattern: The ones digit alternates continuously between 5 and 0.
(b) Ending in 7: No, because every multiple of 5 must end in either 0 or 5. A number ending in 7 will always leave a remainder of 2 when divided by 5.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of alternating 5 and 0 pattern 1.0 M
Correct justification regarding remainder 2 1.0 M
Q3 Unit Costing • Real-Life Math
For a class party, the teacher bought: 6 packs of juice at ₹10 each, 4 packets of paper cups at ₹15 each, and 5 bags of popcorn at ₹20 each. Calculate the total expenditure.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-step Calculation:
1. Cost of juice = 6 × ₹10 = ₹60
2. Cost of paper cups = 4 × ₹15 = ₹60
3. Cost of popcorn = 5 × ₹20 = ₹100
4. Total Expenditure = ₹60 + ₹60 + ₹100 = ₹220.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct multiplication for each item type 1.5 M
Correct summation of total bill (₹220) 1.5 M
Q4 Drink Sharing • Capacity Math
For the class party, the teacher bought two 2-litre bottles of mango juice.
(a) How many total litres of mango juice were bought?
(b) Convert the total litres into millilitres (mL).
(c) If each paper cup holds 200 mL, how many cups of juice can be served in total?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Total Litres:
• 2 bottles × 2 litres = 4 litres.

(b) Conversion to mL:
• 1 litre = 1000 mL.
• 4 litres = 4 × 1000 = 4000 mL.

(c) Cups Served:
• Capacity per cup = 200 mL.
• Total cups = 4000 ÷ 200 = 20 cups.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct total litres (4 L) 1.0 M
Correct conversion (4000 mL) 1.0 M
Correct cups division (20 cups) 1.0 M
Q5 Party Schedule • Duration Calculation
The Class 3 party began at 10:45 AM and ended at 1:15 PM.
(a) How many hours and minutes did the party last?
(b) If musical chairs took 40 minutes and quiz took 30 minutes, how much time was left for eating snacks and dancing?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Time Calculation:
(a) Total Duration:
• From 10:45 AM to 11:00 AM = 15 minutes.
• From 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM = 2 hours.
• From 1:00 PM to 1:15 PM = 15 minutes.
• Total = 2 hours + 15 min + 15 min = 2 hours 30 minutes (or 150 minutes).

(b) Remaining Time:
• Games time = 40 min + 30 min = 70 minutes.
• Remaining time = 150 min - 70 min = 80 minutes (or 1 hour 20 minutes).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct total duration (2 hours 30 minutes) 1.5 M
Correct remaining time (80 minutes / 1 hr 20 min) 1.5 M
Q6 Snack Budget & Cash Memo
Prepare a bill for the following party purchases:
• 25 samosas at ₹8 each
• 30 bananas at ₹4 each
• 2 packets of paper plates at ₹25 per packet
Calculate the total bill and the change from a ₹500 note.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Bill:
• Samosas: 25 × ₹8 = ₹200.
• Bananas: 30 × ₹4 = ₹120.
• Plates: 2 × ₹25 = ₹50.
• Total Bill: ₹200 + ₹120 + ₹50 = ₹370.

Change from ₹500:
• Change = ₹500 - ₹370 = ₹130.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct itemised multiplication and sum (₹370) 2.0 M
Correct change subtraction (₹130) 1.0 M

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Fun at Class Party!: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 160. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 160.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 150.
4. Divide by 2: N = 75.
5. Verification: Double of 75 is 150, plus 10 gives 160. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 160 1.5 M
Final number N = 75 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Party Gift Return Bags

The class teacher prepares 25 return gift pouches for students. In each pouch, she places 3 crayons and 2 stickers.

Analytical Task: How many crayons and how many stickers will she need in total? What is the total count of all gift items?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total crayons needed = 25 × 3 = 75 crayons.
2. Total stickers needed = 25 × 2 = 50 stickers.
3. Total gift items = 75 + 50 = 125 items (or 25 × 5 = 125 items).
Rubric: 1M crayons multiplication + 1M stickers multiplication + 1M total sum.
