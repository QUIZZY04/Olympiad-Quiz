Chapter 4: Long and Short

Length Measurement, Centimetres (cm) & Metres (m), Ruler Usage & Height Charts | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Measuring length using non-standard units (fingers, hand spans) vs standard units.
  • Using a 15 cm ruler correctly: starting from 0, not from 1.
  • Understanding standard units: Centimetre (cm), Metre (m), and Kilometre (km).
  • 1 Metre = 100 Centimetres (1 m = 100 cm); 1 Kilometre = 1000 Metres.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (26 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Unit Selection • cm, m, or km
Which unit (cm, m, or km) is best suited to measure: (a) The length of a safety pin, (b) The height of a coconut tree, (c) The distance between Delhi and Agra, (d) The length of a 500-rupee currency note?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Appropriate Units:
• (a) Safety pin: Centimetres (cm) (small object)
• (b) Coconut tree: Metres (m) (tall height)
• (c) Distance between Delhi and Agra: Kilometres (km) (intercity travel)
• (d) Currency note: Centimetres (cm) (approx 15 cm).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct units for small objects (cm) 1.0 M
Correct units for tree (m) and intercity distance (km) 1.0 M
Q2 Ruler Measurement Error Analysis
Rohan broke his ruler. The scale now starts from 3 cm instead of 0. If he places a wooden crayon with its left tip at 3 cm and the right tip reaches 11 cm, what is the actual length of the crayon?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Starting point = 3 cm
• Ending point = 11 cm
• Actual length = Ending point - Starting point = 11 cm - 3 cm = 8 cm.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Recognizing subtraction of starting offset 1.0 M
Correct calculation (11 - 3 = 8 cm) 1.0 M
Q3 Height Chart Comparison • Word Problem
In Class 3, Amit's height is 1 metre 25 centimetres. Sanya is 18 cm taller than Amit. What is Sanya's height in metres and centimetres?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• Amit's height = 1 m 25 cm
• Sanya's additional height = 18 cm
• Sanya's height = 1 m + (25 cm + 18 cm)
• 25 + 18 = 43 cm
• Sanya's total height = 1 metre 43 centimetres (1 m 43 cm).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct addition of centimetre values (25 + 18 = 43) 1.0 M
Clear final representation in m and cm 1.0 M

Q5 Lali's Rope Measurement • Non-Standard Units
In the story, a farmer comes to Lali to buy 7 arms of rope. Lali measures the rope with her small arm.
(a) Why was the rope short when the farmer measured it with his own arm?
(b) What should the farmer do to get the right length using non-standard units?
(c) Why do we use standard rulers instead of arm-spans?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
(a) Reason:
• The farmer is an adult and has a much longer arm than young Lali. 7 arms of Lali's length is shorter than 7 arms of the farmer's length.

(b) Solution with Non-Standard Unit:
• The farmer should measure the rope using his own arm, or measure 7 times his own arm-span.

(c) Need for Standard Rulers:
• Standard units like metres and centimetres do not change from person to person. A 1-metre ruler is exactly the same length worldwide, ensuring fairness in trade.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of arm length difference between adult and child 1.0 M
Correct solution for farmer measuring himself 1.0 M
Need for universal standard units (m, cm) 1.0 M

Q7 Unit Conversions • Metres and Centimetres
Convert the following measurements:
(a) 4 m into centimetres.
(b) 2 m 75 cm into centimetres.
(c) 350 cm into metres and centimetres.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Conversions (1 m = 100 cm):
• (a) 4 m: 4 × 100 = 400 cm.
• (b) 2 m 75 cm: (2 × 100) + 75 = 200 + 75 = 275 cm.
• (c) 350 cm: 300 cm + 50 cm = 3 m 50 cm.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct answer for (a): 400 cm 1.0 M
Correct answer for (b): 275 cm 1.0 M
Correct answer for (c): 3 m 50 cm 1.0 M

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Long and Short: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 100. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 100.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 90.
4. Divide by 2: N = 45.
5. Verification: Double of 45 is 90, plus 10 gives 100. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 100 1.5 M
Final number N = 45 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Inter-School Long Jump Competition

In the primary sports day long jump final, Danish jumped 2 metres 80 centimetres. Gurjeet jumped 15 cm less than Danish, while Rehana jumped 35 cm more than Danish.

Analytical Task: How far did Gurjeet jump? How far did Rehana jump? Did Rehana cross the 3-metre mark?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Danish = 2 m 80 cm.
2. Gurjeet = 2 m 80 cm - 15 cm = 2 metres 65 centimetres.
3. Rehana = 2 m 80 cm + 35 cm = 2 m + 115 cm = 2 m + 1 m 15 cm = 3 metres 15 centimetres.
4. Since 3 m 15 cm > 3 m, yes, Rehana crossed the 3-metre mark.
Rubric: 1M Gurjeet calculation + 1M Rehana calculation + 1M 3m threshold check.
