Chapter 14: Rupees and Paise

Indian Currency, Converting Rupees to Paise, Self-Service Store Bills & Cash Receipts | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Indian currency: coins (₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10, ₹20) and notes (₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500).
  • 1 Rupee = 100 Paise (₹1 = 100 p).
  • Writing amounts in rupees and paise with decimal points (e.g., ₹12.50).
  • Preparing itemized shopping bills and checking total amounts and change.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Rupees & Paise Conversion • Arithmetic
Convert: (a) ₹7 into paise, (b) 850 paise into rupees and paise, (c) ₹12.75 into paise.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Conversions (1 Rupee = 100 Paise):
• (a) ₹7: 7 × 100 p = 700 paise.
• (b) 850 paise: 800 p + 50 p = 8 rupees and 50 paise (₹8.50).
• (c) ₹12.75: (12 × 100) + 75 = 1200 + 75 = 1,275 paise.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct conversion for ₹7 (700 p) 1.0 M
Correct conversion for 850 p (₹8.50) 1.0 M
Correct conversion for ₹12.75 (1275 p) 1.0 M

Prepare a cash memo for the following purchases: 1 notebook for ₹24.50, 2 pencils at ₹6.00 each, and 1 sharpener for ₹4.50. What is the total bill amount?

Cash Memo:

Correct total addition (₹41.00) 1.5 M
Q3 Railway Ticket Booking • Money Word Problem
A railway ticket from Station A to Station B costs ₹62.50. (a) What is the total fare for two such tickets? (b) If you hand over a ₹200 note to the ticket booking clerk, how much balance will you receive back?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-step Solution:
(a) Total Ticket Fare:
2 × ₹62.50 = ₹(62.50 + 62.50) = ₹125.00.

(b) Balance Received Back:
Amount given = ₹200.00
Total fare = ₹125.00
Change = ₹200.00 - ₹125.00 = ₹75.00.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct calculation of 2 tickets fare (₹125) 1.5 M
Correct subtraction for balance change (₹75) 1.5 M
Q4 Self-Made Cash Memo • Toy Purchases
Prepare a cash memo for the following shopping trip:
• 1 toy car for ₹15.50
• 3 pencils at ₹2.50 each
• 2 toffees at ₹0.50 each
(a) Find the total amount of the bill.
(b) If you give a ₹50 note, how much change should you get back?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Itemized Cash Memo:
• 1 Toy Car: ₹15.50.
• 3 Pencils: 3 × ₹2.50 = ₹7.50.
• 2 Toffees: 2 × ₹0.50 = ₹1.00.
• Total Bill: ₹15.50 + ₹7.50 + ₹1.00 = ₹24.00.

Change from ₹50:
• Change = ₹50.00 - ₹24.00 = ₹26.00.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct itemised sub-totals and sum (₹24.00) 2.0 M
Correct change subtraction (₹26.00) 1.0 M
Q5 Train Ticket Fare Table • Route Math
Study the railway fare chart from New Jalpaiguri:
• To Siliguri (10 km): ₹12.50
• To Malbazar (45 km): ₹28.00
• To Alipurduar (120 km): ₹62.50
(a) What is the ticket fare for two persons traveling to Malbazar?
(b) How much more expensive is a ticket to Alipurduar than to Siliguri?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Railway Fare Calculations:
(a) Two Tickets to Malbazar:
• 2 × ₹28.00 = ₹56.00.

(b) Price Difference:
• Alipurduar fare = ₹62.50.
• Siliguri fare = ₹12.50.
• Difference = ₹62.50 - ₹12.50 = ₹50.00.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct multiplication for 2 tickets (₹56.00) 1.5 M
Correct subtraction for difference (₹50.00) 1.5 M
Q6 Coin Rubbing & Currency Recognition
In the textbook activity, children place a coin under paper and rub a pencil over it:
(a) What design appears when you rub a ₹5 coin?
(b) How many 50-paise coins make ₹1?
(c) How many 25-paise coins make ₹1?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Coin Mathematics:
• (a) Coin Rubbing: The impression shows the Lion Capital of Ashoka (emblem), the numeral '5', and the rupee symbol (₹).
• (b) 50-Paise Coins in ₹1: 100 paise ÷ 50 paise = 2 coins.
• (c) 25-Paise Coins in ₹1: 100 paise ÷ 25 paise = 4 coins.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate visual impression description 1.0 M
Correct count of 50-paise coins (2) 1.0 M
Correct count of 25-paise coins (4) 1.0 M
Q7 Money Addition with Paise • Regrouping
Solve the following money additions:
(a) ₹45.50 + ₹23.75
(b) ₹120.25 + ₹85.80
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Money Addition:
• (a) ₹45.50 + ₹23.75:
- Paise: 50 + 75 = 125 paise = ₹1 and 25 paise.
- Rupees: 45 + 23 + 1 (carry) = 69.
- Total = ₹69.25.

• (b) ₹120.25 + ₹85.80:
- Paise: 25 + 80 = 105 paise = ₹1 and 05 paise.
- Rupees: 120 + 85 + 1 (carry) = 206.
- Total = ₹206.05.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct addition with paise regrouping for (a): ₹69.25 1.5 M
Correct addition with paise regrouping for (b): ₹206.05 1.5 M
Q8 Textbook Exercise • Real-Life Math
In Chapter 14 ('Rupees and Paise'), a student solves an NCERT word problem: If 1 kg of apples costs ₹60 and 1 kg of oranges costs ₹40, how much do 2 kg of apples and 3 kg of oranges cost altogether?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Cost of 2 kg apples = 2 × ₹60 = ₹120.
2. Cost of 3 kg oranges = 3 × ₹40 = ₹120.
3. Total cost = ₹120 + ₹120 = ₹240.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Apples = ₹120 1 M
Oranges = ₹120 1 M
Total = ₹240 1 M
Q9 In-Text Activity • Hands-on Investigation
In 'Rupees and Paise', how do you measure and compare quantities accurately? Explain with an example using standard units.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Measurement Explanation:
• Standard units provide consistent and reliable measurements across the world.
• For length, we use metres (m) and centimetres (cm).
• For weight, we use kilograms (kg) and grams (g).
• For liquids, we use litres (L) and millilitres (mL).
• Unlike non-standard units (such as footsteps or handspans), standard units give the exact same measurement no matter who measures.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Standard units defined 1.5 M
Examples for length, weight, liquid 1.5 M
Q10 Practice Time • Two-Step Word Problem
Suresh bought a school bag for ₹175 and a pencil box for ₹45. He gave a ₹500 note to the shopkeeper. How much change did he receive?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Total money spent = ₹175 + ₹45 = ₹220.
2. Amount paid = ₹500.
3. Change returned = ₹500 - ₹220 = ₹280.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total spent = ₹220 1.5 M
Change = ₹280 1.5 M

Next terms are 80, 160.

Q14 Time & Calendar • Date Calculation
Rohan's summer vacation starts on 15th May and ends on 30th June. How many total days of vacation does Rohan get? (Remember: May has 31 days).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Days Count:
• Days in May: From 15th to 31st = (31 - 15) + 1 = 17 days.
• Days in June: Full 30 days = 30 days.
• Total vacation days = 17 + 30 = 47 days.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
May days = 17 1.5 M
June days = 30; Total = 47 days 1.5 M
Q15 Equal Sharing • Division Problem
Mother bakes 36 cookies and wants to distribute them equally among 4 children. How many cookies does each child receive? Write the division sentence.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Equal Sharing:
• Total cookies = 36.
• Number of children = 4.
• Cookies per child = 36 ÷ 4 = 9 cookies.
• Division sentence: 36 ÷ 4 = 9.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
36 ÷ 4 = 9 cookies 2 M

Q17 Money • Making a Change
A toy train costs ₹85. You pay with a ₹100 note. The shopkeeper gives you change in 5-rupee coins only. How many 5-rupee coins do you receive?

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Tuck Shop Purchasing

Kabir buys a sandwich for ₹32.50, an apple juice bottle for ₹25.00, and a fruit bun for ₹15.50 at the school canteen. He pays with a ₹100 note.

Analytical Task: What is his total canteen bill? How much change should the canteen cashier return to him?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total bill = ₹32.50 + ₹25.00 + ₹15.50 = ₹(32.50 + 15.50) + ₹25.00 = ₹48.00 + ₹25.00 = ₹73.00.
2. Amount paid = ₹100.00.
3. Change to receive = ₹100.00 - ₹73.00 = ₹27.00.
Rubric: 1.5M addition of rupees and paise + 1.5M change subtraction from ₹100.
