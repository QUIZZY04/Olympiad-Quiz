Chapter 9: How Many Times?

Repeated Addition, Multiplication Tables, Stick Multiplication & Word Problems | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Multiplication as 'times' or repeated addition: 4 times 3 = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12.
  • Drawing stick patterns to understand multiplication intersections.
  • Multiplication properties: multiplying by 0 gives 0, multiplying by 1 gives the same number.
  • Solving 2-digit by 1-digit multiplication problems.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (32 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Stick Crossing Multiplication • Concept
Using the stick crossing method: (a) How many sticks crossing in each direction would you draw for 4 × 5? (b) How many crossing points (dots) will you count?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• (a) Sticks: Draw 4 horizontal sticks and 5 vertical sticks crossing them.
• (b) Crossing Points: Each of the 4 horizontal sticks crosses all 5 vertical sticks. Total crossing points = 4 × 5 = 20 dots.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct number of horizontal and vertical sticks 1.0 M
Correct product calculation (20 dots) 1.0 M

Q3 2-Digit Multiplication • Box Method
Solve 23 × 4 using the split/box multiplication method. Show the breakdown into tens and ones.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-step Box Method:
1. Split 23 into 20 + 3.
2. Multiply each part by 4:
• 20 × 4 = 80
• 3 × 4 = 12
3. Add the two partial products:
• 80 + 12 = 92.
Therefore, 23 × 4 = 92.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct splitting of 23 into 20 and 3 1.0 M
Correct partial products (80 and 12) 1.0 M
Correct final sum (92) 1.0 M

Q5 Multiplication with Sticks • Vedic Method
Mithu had some matchsticks. She arranged them: 2 vertical sticks crossed by 3 horizontal sticks.
(a) How many crossing intersection points are formed?
(b) How can you find 4 × 5 using sticks?
(c) What is 4 × 5?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Stick Multiplication Method:
(a) 2 by 3 Sticks:
• 2 vertical sticks crossed by 3 horizontal sticks create 2 × 3 = 6 intersection points.

(b) Method for 4 × 5:
• Place 4 vertical sticks parallel to each other.
• Cross them with 5 horizontal sticks.
• Count all the points where the sticks cross each other.

(c) Result:
• 4 × 5 = 20 intersection points.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct intersection points for 2x3 (6 points) 1.0 M
Clear procedural explanation of stick arrangement 1.0 M
Correct product for 4 × 5 = 20 1.0 M
Q6 Tarva Frog Jumps • Step Multiplication
Tarva the frog jumps 3 steps at a time along a number line.
(a) Write the numbers Tarva lands on during his first 6 jumps.
(b) Which jump will land Tarva on number 27?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Multiplication by 3:
(a) First 6 jumps:
• 1 jump: 1 × 3 = 3
• 2 jumps: 2 × 3 = 6
• 3 jumps: 3 × 3 = 9
• 4 jumps: 4 × 3 = 12
• 5 jumps: 5 × 3 = 15
• 6 jumps: 6 × 3 = 18.

(b) Jump to reach 27:
• 27 ÷ 3 = 9th jump.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct first 6 multiples of 3 (3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18) 1.5 M
Correct division to find 9th jump 1.5 M
Q7 Word Problem • Classroom Desks

(a) How many desks are in the classroom in total?
(b) If 2 children sit at every desk, how many children can sit in the classroom?

(b) Total Seating Capacity:

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from How Many Times?: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 150. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 150.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 140.
4. Divide by 2: N = 70.
5. Verification: Double of 70 is 140, plus 10 gives 150. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 150 1.5 M
Final number N = 70 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Garden Flowerbed Rows

A municipal botanical garden has 14 flowerbeds. Each flowerbed is planted with 6 marigold plants.

Analytical Task: How many marigold plants are in the garden? If 5 plants dry up, how many healthy plants remain?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total marigold plants = 14 × 6 = (10 × 6) + (4 × 6) = 60 + 24 = 84 plants.
2. Healthy plants remaining = 84 - 5 = 79 healthy plants.
Rubric: 1.5M multiplication of 14 × 6 + 1.5M subtraction of 5 dried plants.
