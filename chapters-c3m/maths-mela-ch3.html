Chapter 3: Double Century

3-Digit Numbers, Place Value up to 200, Century & Number Line Jumps | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Understanding 'Century' (100) and 'Double Century' (200).
  • Counting beyond 100 up to 200 in 1s, 2s, 5s, and 10s.
  • Cricket scores and running between wickets: practical applications of centuries.
  • Number line representation and skip counting forward and backward.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (24 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Cricket Match Math • Centuries
In an ODI cricket match, Dhoni was batting on 93. A six was hit on the next ball. (a) Did he complete his century? (b) How many runs did he score in total?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• Initial score = 93 runs
• Runs from six = 6 runs
• Total score = 93 + 6 = 99 runs.
• (a) A century is exactly 100 runs. Since 99 is 1 less than 100, he did not complete his century (missed by 1 run).
• (b) Total score = 99 runs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct addition (93 + 6 = 99) 1.5 M
Accurate century determination (100 runs needed) 1.5 M

Q3 Place Value Exploration • Expanded Form
Write the number name and expanded form for: (1) 147, (2) 199, (3) 200.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Table of Values:

• 199: One hundred ninety-nine = 100 + 90 + 9 (1 Hundred, 9 Tens, 9 Ones)

Correct number names 1.5 M

Q4 Cricket Scoreboard Analysis • Centuries
Study the scores of four batsmen in an inter-school match:
• A.S. Kapil: 162 runs
• C.K. Nayudu: 128 runs
• P.K. Roy: 93 runs
• V.V.S. Laxman: 197 runs
(a) Who scored the highest runs, and who scored the lowest?
(b) How many more runs did V.V.S. Laxman need to complete a double century (200 runs)?
(c) How many more runs did P.K. Roy need to complete a century (100 runs)?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Highest and Lowest:
• Highest scorer: V.V.S. Laxman (197 runs)
• Lowest scorer: P.K. Roy (93 runs)

(b) Runs to Double Century:
• Target = 200 runs
• V.V.S. scored = 197 runs
• Needed = 200 - 197 = 3 runs.

(c) Runs to Century:
• Target = 100 runs
• P.K. scored = 93 runs
• Needed = 100 - 93 = 7 runs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct highest (197) and lowest (93) identified 1.0 M
Correct calculation for double century (200 - 197 = 3 runs) 1.0 M
Correct calculation for century (100 - 93 = 7 runs) 1.0 M
Q5 Number Line Jumps • Stepping Stones
In a stepping stone game on a 3-digit number track:
(a) A grasshopper starts at 110 and takes jumps of 10 steps forward. Write the next 4 numbers it lands on.
(b) A frog starts at 200 and takes jumps of 5 steps backward. Write the next 4 numbers it lands on.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
(a) Grasshopper's forward jumps (+10):
• 110 + 10 = 120
• 120 + 10 = 130
• 130 + 10 = 140
• 140 + 10 = 150.

(b) Frog's backward jumps (-5):
• 200 - 5 = 195
• 195 - 5 = 190
• 190 - 5 = 185
• 185 - 5 = 180.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct forward sequence: 120, 130, 140, 150 1.5 M
Correct backward sequence: 195, 190, 185, 180 1.5 M

Q7 Cricket Target Chase • Word Problem
In a 20-over match, Team Blue scored 182 runs. Team Green is batting and has scored 139 runs so far.
(a) What is the target score Team Green needs to win the match?
(b) How many more runs does Team Green need to reach the winning target?
(c) If they hit 3 sixes and 2 fours in the next over, how many runs will they score?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Winning Target:
• Team Blue scored = 182 runs.
• Target to win = 182 + 1 = 183 runs.

(b) Runs Needed:
• Target = 183 runs.
• Current score = 139 runs.
• Runs needed = 183 - 139 = 44 runs.

(c) Next Over Runs:
• 3 sixes = 3 × 6 = 18 runs.
• 2 fours = 2 × 4 = 8 runs.
• Total = 18 + 8 = 26 runs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct winning target (183 runs) 1.0 M
Correct subtraction (183 - 139 = 44 runs) 1.0 M
Correct boundary calculation (18 + 8 = 26 runs) 1.0 M
Q8 Cricket Match • Dhoni's Century
Dhoni is batting on 96 runs. How many runs does he need to complete his century? If he hits a 6 on the next ball, how many runs does he score in total, and did he complete his century?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations:
• Century = 100 runs.
• Runs needed = 100 - 96 = 4 runs.
• If he hits a six: 96 + 6 = 102 runs.
• Yes! 102 is greater than 100, so he successfully completed his century.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
4 runs needed for century 1.0 M
96 + 6 = 102 runs 1.0 M
Confirmed completed century 1.0 M

Q10 Double Century Math • 200 Runs
A double century equals 200 runs. Rohit Sharma is batting on 178 runs.
(a) How many more runs does he need to complete a double century?
(b) If he scores 3 boundaries (fours) and 2 sixes, does he reach the double century?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• (a) Runs needed: 200 - 178 = 22 runs.
• (b) Runs from boundaries & sixes:
- 3 fours = 3 × 4 = 12 runs.
- 2 sixes = 2 × 6 = 12 runs.
- Total scored = 12 + 12 = 24 runs.
- New score = 178 + 24 = 202 runs.
• Yes, 202 ≥ 200, so he reaches his double century!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
22 runs needed 1.0 M
Boundaries total = 24 runs 1.0 M
202 runs reached (double century completed) 1.0 M

In a cricket stadium, Section A has 115 fans, Section B has 95 fans, and Section C has 80 fans. What is the total number of fans in these three sections?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Addition:

    95
+  80
-----

Q23 HOTS • Double Century Riddle
I am exactly in the middle between 100 and 200. What number am I? What must be added to me to make a double century?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solving the Riddle:
• Halfway between 100 and 200: (100 + 200) ÷ 2 = 300 ÷ 2 = 150.
• Runs needed to make a double century (200): 200 - 150 = 50.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Midpoint = 150 1.0 M
Added to reach 200 = 50 1.0 M
