Chapter 8: Who Is Heavier?

Mass / Weight (kg & g), Pan Balance, Pumpkin Prize & Grocery Packing | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Understanding mass and weight: heavier vs lighter.
  • Standard units: Kilogram (kg) and Gram (g).
  • 1 Kilogram = 1000 Grams (1 kg = 1000 g); 1/2 kg = 500 g.
  • Balancing pans: when both pans are level, weights are equal.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (22 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Weight Unit Selection • Real-World Items
State whether you would measure the weight of the following in 'Kilograms (kg)' or 'Grams (g)': (a) A sack of wheat, (b) A gold ring, (c) A geometry compass, (d) A bag of cement.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Appropriate Units:
• (a) Sack of wheat: Kilograms (kg) (heavy staple, e.g., 25 kg)
• (b) Gold ring: Grams (g) (light jewellery, e.g., 5-8 g)
• (c) Geometry compass: Grams (g) (light tool, e.g., 50 g)
• (d) Bag of cement: Kilograms (kg) (heavy industrial bag, 50 kg).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct units for wheat and cement (kg) 1.0 M
Correct units for ring and compass (g) 1.0 M
Q2 Balance Calculations • Grams & Kilograms
A grocery shopkeeper has to weigh 2 kg of sugar. He has only 500 g weights. (a) How many 500 g weights must he put on the weight pan? (b) How many grams are in 2 kg?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• (b) Conversion: 1 kg = 1000 g, therefore 2 kg = 2 × 1000 g = 2,000 grams.
• (a) Weights needed: 2,000 g ÷ 500 g = 4 weights of 500 g.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct conversion to 2000 g 1.0 M
Correct number of 500 g weights (4) 1.0 M
Q3 Equal Weight Comparison • Volume vs Mass
Which is heavier: 1 kg of iron or 1 kg of cotton wool? Explain why many people mistakenly think iron is heavier.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Explanation:
• Answer: Both are equally heavy. Both weigh exactly 1 kilogram (1 kg = 1 kg).
• Reason for Common Mistake: Iron is dense and occupies a very small volume, whereas 1 kg of cotton is fluffy and occupies a huge bag. People confuse larger size or density with weight, but on a balance scale, both balance each other perfectly.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear assertion that both weigh the same (1 kg) 1.5 M
Scientific explanation distinguishing volume/density from mass 1.5 M
Q4 Gur and Groundnuts • Volume vs Weight
Shabnam bought 1 kg of jaggery (gur) and 1 kg of groundnuts in the market.
(a) Which is heavier: 1 kg of jaggery or 1 kg of groundnuts?
(b) Why does the bag of groundnuts look so much bigger than the bag of jaggery?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Physics & Math:
(a) Weight Comparison:
• Both weigh exactly the same (1 kg = 1 kg). Neither is heavier.

(b) Volume Difference:
• Groundnuts are light and hollow with air spaces inside their shells (low density), so 1 kg takes up a large space (volume).
• Jaggery is dense and solid (high density), so 1 kg occupies a small, compact volume.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct conclusion that both weigh equal (1 kg) 1.5 M
Scientific explanation of density and volume differences 1.5 M
Q5 Double Your Weight • Kunjamma's Parents Tradition
At Kunjamma's birth, her parents distributed sweets equal to double her weight. Kunjamma weighed 3 kg at birth.
(a) How many kilograms of sweets did they distribute at her birth?
(b) On her 4th birthday, Kunjamma weighed 15 kg. How many kilograms of sweets were distributed then?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calculations:
(a) Sweets at Birth:
• Weight at birth = 3 kg.
• Double her weight = 2 × 3 kg = 6 kg of sweets.

(b) Sweets on 4th Birthday:
• Weight on 4th birthday = 15 kg.
• Double her weight = 2 × 15 kg = 30 kg of sweets.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct calculation for birth (6 kg) 1.5 M
Correct calculation for 4th birthday (30 kg) 1.5 M

Q7 Grocery Package Weights • Finding Total Weight
Ramesh bought from the supermarket:
• 2 kg of rice
• 500 g of dal
• 250 g of tea leaves
• 250 g of almonds
(a) Find the total weight of items bought in kilograms and grams.
(b) Is the total weight more than 3 kg or less than 3 kg?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Addition:
• Rice = 2 kg 000 g.
• Dal = 500 g.
• Tea leaves = 250 g.
• Almonds = 250 g.
• Grams total: 500 + 250 + 250 = 1000 g = 1 kg.
• Kilograms total: 2 kg + 1 kg = 3 kg 000 g (exactly 3 kg).

(b) Comparison:
• The total weight is exactly 3 kg (neither more nor less).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct conversion and addition (3 kg 000 g) 2.0 M
Correct equality comparison 1.0 M

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Who Is Heavier?: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 140. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 140.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 130.
4. Divide by 2: N = 65.
5. Verification: Double of 65 is 130, plus 10 gives 140. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 140 1.5 M
Final number N = 65 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Sports Equipment Delivery

The sports department received a parcel containing 4 footballs (each weighing 400 g) and 2 cricket bats (each weighing 1 kg 200 g).

Analytical Task: What is the total weight of all the footballs? What is the combined total weight of all sports equipment in kilograms and grams?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total footballs weight = 4 × 400 g = 1,600 g = 1 kg 600 g.
2. Total cricket bats weight = 2 × (1 kg 200 g) = 2 kg 400 g.
3. Combined weight = (1 kg 600 g) + (2 kg 400 g) = 3 kg + 1000 g = 3 kg + 1 kg = 4 kilograms (4 kg).
Rubric: 1M footballs multiplication + 1M bats multiplication + 1M combined weight in kg.
