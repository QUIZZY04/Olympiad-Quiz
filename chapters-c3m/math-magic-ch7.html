Q1 Time Duration Estimation • Categorization
Classify each activity into 'Seconds', 'Minutes', 'Hours', or 'Days': (a) To blink your eyes, (b) To knit a woollen sweater, (c) To boil a cup of milk, (d) For curd to set from warm milk.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classifications:
• (a) Blink your eyes: Seconds (fraction of a second)
• (b) Knit a sweater: Days (usually several days or weeks)
• (c) Boil a cup of milk: Minutes (approx 3 to 5 minutes)
• (d) For curd to set: Hours (approx 6 to 8 hours).

Correct duration category for all 4 activities 2.0 M

Q4 Ultat-Pulta Story • Absurd Time Units Correction
Correct the ridiculous time words in this funny story:
'As the sun rises in the evening, Sumana wakes up. She washes her face quickly in 2 hours and runs out to the pond. She takes 10 months to reach the pond and catches a fish in 2 days.'
Rewrite the story with sensible time units (seconds, minutes, morning, hours).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Corrected Sensible Story:
• 'As the sun rises in the morning (not evening), Sumana wakes up. She washes her face quickly in 2 minutes (not 2 hours) and runs out to the pond. She takes 10 minutes (not 10 months) to reach the pond and catches a fish in 2 minutes/seconds (not 2 days).'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Corrected morning for sunrise 1.0 M
Corrected minutes for washing face and walking 1.0 M
Sensible duration for catching fish 1.0 M
Q5 Calendar Magic • 3x3 Magic Square Sum
On any standard monthly calendar page, draw a 3x3 square covering 9 dates.
(a) If the date in the exact center of the 3x3 box is 14, what is the sum of all 9 dates in the box?
(b) Explain the quick trick to find this sum in 2 seconds.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calendar Magic:
(a) Sum of 9 Dates:
• Quick Magic Formula: Center Number × 9.
• Center number = 14.
• Sum = 14 × 9 = 126.

(b) The Trick Explained:
• On a calendar, the numbers symmetrically surround the center number (e.g., 14-8 and 14+8, 14-7 and 14+7, 14-1 and 14+1).
• The average of all 9 numbers is exactly the center number. Therefore, simply multiplying the center date by 9 gives the exact total of all 9 dates instantly!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct total sum (126) 1.5 M
Clear mathematical explanation of multiplying center date by 9 1.5 M

In Time Goes on..., examine the practical pattern: If a student observes 29 units on Day 1 and increases by 9 units each day for 3 days, what is the sequence of values? Explain the mathematical rule applied.
[3 Marks]

Step-by-Step Working:
1. Day 1 value = 29.
2. Day 2 value = 29 + 9 = 38.
3. Day 3 value = 38 + 9 = 47.
4. Rule: Arithmetic progression with common difference +9 units per day.

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Time Goes on...: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 130. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 130.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 120.
4. Divide by 2: N = 60.
5. Verification: Double of 60 is 120, plus 10 gives 130. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 130 1.5 M
Final number N = 60 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Family Mango Tree Planting Record

Grandfather planted a mango sapling in the family orchard in July 2016. The tree bore its first batch of mangoes in July 2021.

Analytical Task: How many years did the sapling take to bear fruit? If it is now July 2026, for how many years has the tree been bearing fruit?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Years to first fruit = 2021 - 2016 = 5 years.
2. Years bearing fruit up to 2026 = 2026 - 2021 = 5 years (or 6 harvest seasons including 2021).
Rubric: 1.5M years to first fruit + 1.5M active fruiting years.
