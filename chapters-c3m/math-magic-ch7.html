Chapter 7: Time Goes on...

Seconds, Minutes, Hours, Days, Months, Years, Timeline & Age Problems | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Categorizing events by duration: taking seconds, minutes, hours, days, months, or years.
  • Reading dates and understanding different date notations (DD/MM/YYYY).
  • Building a personal life timeline: chronological ordering of milestone events.
  • Calculating age difference between family members.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Time Duration Estimation • Categorization
Classify each activity into 'Seconds', 'Minutes', 'Hours', or 'Days': (a) To blink your eyes, (b) To knit a woollen sweater, (c) To boil a cup of milk, (d) For curd to set from warm milk.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classifications:
• (a) Blink your eyes: Seconds (fraction of a second)
• (b) Knit a sweater: Days (usually several days or weeks)
• (c) Boil a cup of milk: Minutes (approx 3 to 5 minutes)
• (d) For curd to set: Hours (approx 6 to 8 hours).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct duration category for all 4 activities 2.0 M
Q2 Birth Certificate Analysis • Date Calculations
Look at Bincy's birth certificate: Date of birth: 02/05/2018 (2 May 2018). (a) How old was Bincy on 2 May 2026? (b) In which year did she celebrate her 5th birthday?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Analysis:
• (a) Age on 2 May 2026:
2026 - 2018 = 8 years old.
• (b) 5th Birthday Year:
2018 + 5 = 2023 (on 2 May 2023).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct subtraction for age (8 years) 1.0 M
Correct year calculation for 5th birthday (2023) 1.0 M

Rohan's mother is 34 years old. His grandmother is 62 years old. (a) What is the age difference between Rohan's mother and grandmother? (b) If Rohan is 8 years old, by how many years is his mother older than him?

62 - 34 = 28 years.
• (b) Mother & Rohan:
34 - 8 = 26 years.

Correct subtraction for mother & Rohan (26 years) 1.0 M
Q4 Ultat-Pulta Story • Absurd Time Units Correction
Correct the ridiculous time words in this funny story:
'As the sun rises in the evening, Sumana wakes up. She washes her face quickly in 2 hours and runs out to the pond. She takes 10 months to reach the pond and catches a fish in 2 days.'
Rewrite the story with sensible time units (seconds, minutes, morning, hours).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Corrected Sensible Story:
• 'As the sun rises in the morning (not evening), Sumana wakes up. She washes her face quickly in 2 minutes (not 2 hours) and runs out to the pond. She takes 10 minutes (not 10 months) to reach the pond and catches a fish in 2 minutes/seconds (not 2 days).'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Corrected morning for sunrise 1.0 M
Corrected minutes for washing face and walking 1.0 M
Sensible duration for catching fish 1.0 M
Q5 Calendar Magic • 3x3 Magic Square Sum
On any standard monthly calendar page, draw a 3x3 square covering 9 dates.
(a) If the date in the exact center of the 3x3 box is 14, what is the sum of all 9 dates in the box?
(b) Explain the quick trick to find this sum in 2 seconds.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Calendar Magic:
(a) Sum of 9 Dates:
• Quick Magic Formula: Center Number × 9.
• Center number = 14.
• Sum = 14 × 9 = 126.

(b) The Trick Explained:
• On a calendar, the numbers symmetrically surround the center number (e.g., 14-8 and 14+8, 14-7 and 14+7, 14-1 and 14+1).
• The average of all 9 numbers is exactly the center number. Therefore, simply multiplying the center date by 9 gives the exact total of all 9 dates instantly!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct total sum (126) 1.5 M
Clear mathematical explanation of multiplying center date by 9 1.5 M

Q8 Textbook Exercise • Real-Life Math
In Chapter 7 ('Time Goes on...'), a student solves an NCERT word problem: If 1 kg of apples costs ₹60 and 1 kg of oranges costs ₹40, how much do 2 kg of apples and 3 kg of oranges cost altogether?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Cost of 2 kg apples = 2 × ₹60 = ₹120.
2. Cost of 3 kg oranges = 3 × ₹40 = ₹120.
3. Total cost = ₹120 + ₹120 = ₹240.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Apples = ₹120 1 M
Oranges = ₹120 1 M
Total = ₹240 1 M
Q9 In-Text Activity • Hands-on Investigation
In 'Time Goes on...', how do you measure and compare quantities accurately? Explain with an example using standard units.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Measurement Explanation:
• Standard units provide consistent and reliable measurements across the world.
• For length, we use metres (m) and centimetres (cm).
• For weight, we use kilograms (kg) and grams (g).
• For liquids, we use litres (L) and millilitres (mL).
• Unlike non-standard units (such as footsteps or handspans), standard units give the exact same measurement no matter who measures.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Standard units defined 1.5 M
Examples for length, weight, liquid 1.5 M
Q10 Practice Time • Two-Step Word Problem
Suresh bought a school bag for ₹175 and a pencil box for ₹45. He gave a ₹500 note to the shopkeeper. How much change did he receive?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Total money spent = ₹175 + ₹45 = ₹220.
2. Amount paid = ₹500.
3. Change returned = ₹500 - ₹220 = ₹280.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Total spent = ₹220 1.5 M
Change = ₹280 1.5 M

Q14 Time & Calendar • Date Calculation
Rohan's summer vacation starts on 15th May and ends on 30th June. How many total days of vacation does Rohan get? (Remember: May has 31 days).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Days Count:
• Days in May: From 15th to 31st = (31 - 15) + 1 = 17 days.
• Days in June: Full 30 days = 30 days.
• Total vacation days = 17 + 30 = 47 days.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
May days = 17 1.5 M
June days = 30; Total = 47 days 1.5 M
Q15 Equal Sharing • Division Problem
Mother bakes 36 cookies and wants to distribute them equally among 4 children. How many cookies does each child receive? Write the division sentence.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Equal Sharing:
• Total cookies = 36.
• Number of children = 4.
• Cookies per child = 36 ÷ 4 = 9 cookies.
• Division sentence: 36 ÷ 4 = 9.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
36 ÷ 4 = 9 cookies 2 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Family Mango Tree Planting Record

Grandfather planted a mango sapling in the family orchard in July 2016. The tree bore its first batch of mangoes in July 2021.

Analytical Task: How many years did the sapling take to bear fruit? If it is now July 2026, for how many years has the tree been bearing fruit?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Years to first fruit = 2021 - 2016 = 5 years.
2. Years bearing fruit up to 2026 = 2026 - 2021 = 5 years (or 6 harvest seasons including 2021).
Rubric: 1.5M years to first fruit + 1.5M active fruiting years.
