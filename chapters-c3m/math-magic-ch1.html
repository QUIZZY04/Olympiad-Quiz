Chapter 1: Where to Look From

Top View, Front View, Side View, Dot Grid Drawings & Mirror Halves (Lines of Symmetry) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Viewing 3D objects from different perspectives: top view, side view, and front view.
  • Using a square dot grid (Rangoli patterns) to draw shapes and objects.
  • Mirror halves: understanding symmetry and finding lines of symmetry.
  • Recognizing symmetric alphabet letters, numbers, and geometric shapes.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (19 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Perspective Drawing • Views
How does a staircase look when viewed from: (a) The side? (b) The top? (c) The front? Explain with a short description.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Perspective Descriptions:
• (a) Side View: From the side, a staircase looks like a continuous zigzag step pattern going upward like a ladder.
• (b) Top View: From directly above, you see a set of parallel rectangular stripes or bars placed one after another.
• (c) Front View: Looking straight from the front, you see a series of horizontal rectangular risers stacked vertically.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate description of side view (zigzag steps) 1.0 M
Accurate description of top view (parallel stripes) 1.0 M
Accurate description of front view 1.0 M
Q2 Mirror Halves • Symmetry Investigation
Look at the letters of the English alphabet: A, B, C, D, E, H, M, X. Which of these letters have: (a) A vertical line of symmetry? (b) A horizontal line of symmetry? (c) Both vertical and horizontal lines of symmetry?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classification of Symmetric Letters:
• (a) Vertical line of symmetry (cut down the middle | ): A, M, H, X.
• (b) Horizontal line of symmetry (cut across the middle — ): B, C, D, E, H, X.
• (c) Both vertical and horizontal symmetry: H and X (folding along either line gives matching halves).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct vertical symmetry identification 1.0 M
Correct horizontal symmetry identification 1.0 M
Correct dual symmetry identification (H, X) 1.0 M
Q3 Dot Grid Pattern • Activity Solution
Using a square dot grid, how do you construct a symmetric leaf or kite? Describe the step-by-step method using mirror halves.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-step Dot Grid Construction:
1. Draw a central vertical axis connecting 6 dots straight down.
2. On the left side, count 3 dots out to the side and draw the left curved wing/outline.
3. Count the exact same number of dots (3 dots) on the right side and draw a matching mirror curve.
4. Joining the matching dots to the top and bottom central points produces a perfectly symmetric kite or leaf.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mention of central axis and equal dot distances 1.0 M
Explanation of mirror symmetry completion 1.0 M
Q4 Perspective & Observation • Cat in the Classroom
A cat is peeping into a classroom from a high window above.
(a) What view of the classroom does the cat see: Top View, Side View, or Front View?
(b) Can you help the cat find where the teacher is sitting?
(c) Draw or describe how a staircase looks from the side versus from the top.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Cat's Perspective:
• The cat is looking down from above, so it sees the Top View (or Aerial View) of the students' desks and heads.

(b) Locating the Teacher:
• The teacher is standing/sitting at the front of the classroom near the large rectangular teacher's table facing the rows of student desks.

(c) Staircase Views:
• Side View: Shows a step-like zig-zag pattern going upward like a ladder.
• Top View: Shows only a series of parallel horizontal rectangular steps.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct perspective identification (Top View) 1.0 M
Accurate description of teacher's location in classroom 1.0 M
Clear contrast between side view and top view of staircase 1.0 M
Q5 Tit for Tat • Mirror Halves and Symmetry
In the story 'Tit for Tat', a clever painter drew only half of Amina's picture and asked for payment. Amina placed a mirror next to it.
(a) Why did Amina use a mirror?
(b) Which of these letters can be divided into two identical mirror halves by a dotted line: A, B, C, F, H, P?
(c) Give an English word whose upper and lower halves look identical in a horizontal mirror.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Mirror Trick:
• The mirror reflects the drawn half to complete the full symmetrical image, showing Amina's full face just as the painter claimed.

(b) Symmetrical Letters:
• A: Vertical mirror line down the middle.
• B: Horizontal mirror line across the middle.
• C: Horizontal mirror line across the middle.
• H: Both vertical and horizontal mirror lines.
• (F and P cannot be divided into identical mirror halves).

(c) Horizontal Mirror Words:
• Words like COOKED, DICE, CHECK, or BOOK have horizontal symmetry.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct explanation of the mirror reflection concept 1.0 M
Correct identification of symmetrical letters (A, B, C, H) 1.0 M
Valid horizontally symmetrical word example 1.0 M
Q6 Dot-Grid Rangoli • Symmetry and Patterns
Using a grid of dots to make traditional Indian rangoli designs:
(a) How can a line of symmetry help in drawing a balanced rangoli?
(b) Name four geometric shapes that can be created by joining dots on a dot grid.
(c) If you draw a diya on one side of a dotted line, how do you complete it on the other side?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Symmetry in Rangoli:
• A central line or point of symmetry ensures that every curve, petal, or corner drawn on the left is matched exactly at an equal distance on the right, creating a balanced and harmonious design.

(b) Shapes on a Dot Grid:
• Square, Rectangle, Triangle, and Rhombus (Diamond).

(c) Completing the Diya:
• Count the exact number of dots outward and downward from the mirror line for each vertex of the diya, and connect corresponding dots symmetrically on the opposite side.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of symmetry creating balance in art 1.0 M
Listing four valid polygon shapes 1.0 M
Methodical explanation of reflection across dot-grid 1.0 M
Q7 Mirror Halves Identification • Figures
State whether a dotted line through the center divides each figure into mirror halves (Write 'Yes' or 'No'):
(a) A standard butterfly with dotted line along its body.
(b) A pair of scissors with dotted line between the two blades.
(c) A watering can with a spout on one side and a handle on the other.
(d) An equilateral triangle with dotted line from top vertex to base midpoint.
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mirror Halves Check:
• (a) Butterfly: Yes (Left wing is an exact mirror image of the right wing).
• (b) Scissors: Yes (The two halves fold over each other symmetrically).
• (c) Watering Can: No (Spout on one side does not match handle on the other).
• (d) Equilateral Triangle: Yes (Bisects the triangle into two identical right-angled triangles).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct answers for (a) and (b) 2.0 M
Correct answers for (c) and (d) 2.0 M
Q8 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Reasoning
In Where to Look From, examine the practical pattern: If a student observes 5 units on Day 1 and increases by 3 units each day for 3 days, what is the sequence of values? Explain the mathematical rule applied.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Working:
1. Day 1 value = 5.
2. Day 2 value = 5 + 3 = 8.
3. Day 3 value = 8 + 3 = 11.
4. Rule: Arithmetic progression with common difference +3 units per day.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct Day 1, 2, 3 sequence (5, 8, 11) 1.5 M
Valid rule explanation (+3 daily increase) 1.5 M

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Where to Look From: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 70. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 70.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 60.
4. Divide by 2: N = 30.
5. Verification: Double of 30 is 60, plus 10 gives 70. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 70 1.5 M
Final number N = 30 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Architectural Floor Plan Perspective

An architect looks down at a school building model from above (aerial top view). She notices the roof is shaped like a giant rectangle with two circular courtyards inside.

Analytical Task: If each circular courtyard has a diameter of 10 metres and they are placed side by side with 5 metres gap between them, what is the distance from the left edge of the first courtyard to the right edge of the second?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Width of 1st courtyard = 10 m.
2. Gap between courtyards = 5 m.
3. Width of 2nd courtyard = 10 m.
4. Total span = 10 + 5 + 10 = 25 metres.
Rubric: 1.5M identifying diameter dimensions + 1.5M addition of spans and gap.
