Q1 Inverse Operations • Checking Work
Solve: 356 + 178. Then use subtraction to check whether your answer is correct.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution & Verification:
1. Addition:
  356
+ 178
-------
  534 (6+8=14, 1 carry; 5+7+1=13, 1 carry; 3+1+1=5)

2. Verification via Subtraction:
  534
- 178
-------
  356
Since 534 - 178 = 356, the addition is 100% verified correct.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct addition with carry (534) 1.5 M
Correct inverse subtraction verification 1.5 M
Q2 2-Step Financial Word Problem
Kiran had ₹500. She bought school books for ₹275 and stationery for ₹145. How much money did she spend altogether, and how much money was left with her?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-step Solution:
Step 1: Total money spent
Cost of books = ₹275
Cost of stationery = ₹145
Total spent = ₹275 + ₹145 = ₹420.

Step 2: Money left
Total money initially = ₹500
Total spent = ₹420
Money left = ₹500 - ₹420 = ₹80.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct addition for total spending (₹420) 1.5 M
Correct subtraction for remaining balance (₹80) 1.5 M
Q3 Number Puzzle • Magic Triangle
Arrange the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 along the three sides of a triangle such that the sum of the numbers along each side is 9.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
Place the smaller numbers at the corners to keep side sums low:
• Corners: 1, 2, 3.
• Between 1 and 2: place 6 → 1 + 6 + 2 = 9.
• Between 2 and 3: place 4 → 2 + 4 + 3 = 9.
• Between 3 and 1: place 5 → 3 + 5 + 1 = 9.
All three sides sum to exactly 9.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct corner placement (1, 2, 3) 1.5 M
Correct edge placements (6, 4, 5) ensuring sum = 9 1.5 M
Q4 Multi-Digit Addition • Regrouping Drill
Solve the following additions showing carrying over clearly:
(a) 247 + 185
(b) 368 + 279
(c) Estimate the sum of 247 + 185 to the nearest hundred.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solutions:
(a) 247 + 185:
• Ones: 7 + 5 = 12 (Write 2, carry 1 to tens)
• Tens: 1 (carry) + 4 + 8 = 13 (Write 3, carry 1 to hundreds)
• Hundreds: 1 (carry) + 2 + 1 = 4
• Sum = 432.

(b) 368 + 279:
• Ones: 8 + 9 = 17 (Write 7, carry 1)
• Tens: 1 + 6 + 7 = 14 (Write 4, carry 1)
• Hundreds: 1 + 3 + 2 = 6
• Sum = 647.

(c) Nearest Hundred Estimation:
• 247 rounds to 200; 185 rounds to 200.
• Estimated sum = 200 + 200 = 400 (Actual 432 is very close to 400).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct carrying and sum for (a): 432 1.0 M
Correct carrying and sum for (b): 647 1.0 M
Correct estimation explanation (400) 1.0 M
Q5 Multi-Digit Subtraction • Borrowing Across Tens
Solve the following subtractions showing borrowing steps:
(a) 512 - 278
(b) Check your subtraction answer using addition.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) 512 - 278:
• Ones: 2 - 8 cannot be done. Borrow 1 ten from 1 ten (leaving 0 tens). Ones become 12. 12 - 8 = 4.
• Tens: 0 - 7 cannot be done. Borrow 1 hundred from 5 hundreds (leaving 4 hundreds). Tens become 10. 10 - 7 = 3.
• Hundreds: 4 - 2 = 2.
• Difference = 234.

(b) Verification using Addition:
• Difference + Subtrahend = Minuend
• 234 + 278 = (200 + 200) + (30 + 70) + (4 + 8) = 400 + 100 + 12 = 512.
• Since 512 matches the starting number, the answer is Verified Correct.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct borrowing and difference (234) 1.5 M
Correct addition check formula and execution (234 + 278 = 512) 1.5 M
Q6 Two-Step Word Problem • Bakery Sales
A baker baked 350 buns in the morning. He sold 215 buns by lunchtime. In the afternoon, he baked 120 fresh buns.
(a) How many buns were left after the lunchtime sales?
(b) How many buns did he have in total in the afternoon?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Buns Left after Lunch:
• Morning buns baked = 350
• Buns sold = 215
• Leftover = 350 - 215 = 135 buns.

(b) Total Buns in Afternoon:
• Leftover buns = 135
• Fresh buns baked = 120
• Total = 135 + 120 = 255 buns.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct subtraction step (350 - 215 = 135) 1.5 M
Correct addition step (135 + 120 = 255) 1.5 M
Q7 Token Card Method • Visual Mental Math
Represent the subtraction 65 - 38 using square cards of 10 and triangle cards of 1.
(a) How many 10-cards and 1-cards are in 65?
(b) Explain how exchanging one 10-card for ten 1-cards allows you to subtract 38.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Visual Token Method:
(a) Cards for 65:
• 65 = six 10-cards and five 1-cards.

(b) Exchange and Subtraction:
• To subtract 38, we need to take away 3 ten-cards and 8 one-cards.
• We only have 5 one-cards, so we exchange one 10-card for ten 1-cards.
• Now we have: 5 ten-cards and 15 one-cards (10 + 5).
• Subtract 8 one-cards from 15 one-cards $\rightarrow$ 7 one-cards left.
• Subtract 3 ten-cards from 5 ten-cards $\rightarrow$ 2 ten-cards left.
• Remaining cards = two 10-cards + seven 1-cards = 27.
• Therefore, 65 - 38 = 27.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct card representation for 65 (6 tens, 5 ones) 1.0 M
Clear visual explanation of exchanging 1 ten for 10 ones 1.0 M
Correct final result (27) 1.0 M

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Give and Take: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 180. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 180.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 170.
4. Divide by 2: N = 85.
5. Verification: Double of 85 is 170, plus 10 gives 180. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 180 1.5 M
Final number N = 85 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Children's Book Fair Stall

At the school book fair, the storybook counter started with 450 books in the morning. They received a fresh delivery of 120 books at noon, and sold 380 books by evening.

Analytical Task: How many books were left at the storybook counter at the end of the day?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Books after morning delivery = 450 + 120 = 570 books.
2. Books sold = 380.
3. Books left at end of day = 570 - 380 = 190 books.
Rubric: 1.5M addition of delivered books + 1.5M subtraction of sold books.
