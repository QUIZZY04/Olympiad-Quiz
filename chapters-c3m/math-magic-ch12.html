Chapter 12: Can We Share?

Division as Equal Distribution, Equal Jumps on Number Line, Remainders & Word Problems | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Equal sharing among individuals: understanding dividend, divisor, and quotient.
  • Grouping objects into bundles of equal size.
  • Division on the number line using backward jumps.
  • Handling remainders: what happens when items cannot be shared equally.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (28 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Equal Sharing • Division Facts
There are 28 laddoos. Mother shares them equally among 4 plates. (a) How many laddoos are on each plate? (b) Write the related multiplication fact.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• Total laddoos = 28
• Number of plates = 4
• (a) Division Fact: 28 ÷ 4 = 7 laddoos per plate.
• (b) Related Multiplication Fact: 4 × 7 = 28 (or 7 × 4 = 28).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct division quotient (7) 1.0 M
Correct related multiplication statement 1.0 M
Q2 Division with Remainder • Problem Solving
A teacher has 35 craft ribbons. She gives 4 ribbons to each group for an art project. (a) How many groups will receive ribbons? (b) How many ribbons will be left over?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-step Solution:
1. Divide 35 by 4:
• 4 × 8 = 32
• 35 - 32 = 3
2. (a) Groups receiving ribbons: 8 groups.
3. (b) Ribbons left over (remainder): 3 ribbons.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct division calculation and quotient (8) 1.5 M
Correct remainder identification (3 ribbons) 1.5 M
Q3 Frog Jump Division • Number Line
A frog takes jumps of 3 steps at a time on a numbered track. How many jumps will it take to reach: (a) 15? (b) 27? Show the division sentences.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Jumps:
• (a) To reach 15: 15 ÷ 3 = 5 jumps.
• (b) To reach 27: 27 ÷ 3 = 9 jumps.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct jumps for 15 (5 jumps) 1.0 M
Correct jumps for 27 (9 jumps) 1.0 M
Q4 Caterpillars on Leaves • Grouping & Division

(b) Write the division sentence and multiplication check fact.

(b) Mathematical Sentences:

• Multiplication Check Fact: 3 × 7 = 21.

Correct multiplication check fact (3 × 7 = 21) 1.0 M
Q5 Sharing Grains • Mother Bird and Chicks
Mummy bird has 12 grains of corn. She distributes them equally among her 4 baby chicks.
(a) How many grains does each chick get?
(b) If Mummy bird brings 8 more grains, how many total grains does each chick get now?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Initial Sharing:
• 12 grains ÷ 4 chicks = 3 grains per chick.

(b) After Bringing 8 More Grains:
• Extra grains per chick = 8 ÷ 4 = 2 grains.
• Total grains per chick = 3 + 2 = 5 grains per chick (or 20 ÷ 4 = 5).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct initial share (3 grains) 1.5 M
Correct updated share (5 grains) 1.5 M
Q6 Money Sharing • ₹10 Notes
Grandmother gave ₹60 to be divided equally among her 3 grandchildren: Anu, Bitoo, and Chintu.
(a) How many ₹10 notes will each child get?
(b) How much money does each child receive in rupees?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Total money = ₹60.
• In ₹10 notes: ₹60 = six ₹10 notes.
(a) ₹10 Notes per Child:
• 6 notes ÷ 3 children = two ₹10 notes each.

(b) Amount in Rupees:
• 2 × ₹10 = ₹20 each (or ₹60 ÷ 3 = ₹20).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct number of ₹10 notes (2 notes) 1.5 M
Correct amount in rupees (₹20) 1.5 M
Q7 Jumps of Frog • Division on Number Line
A frog starts at 18 on a number line and jumps 2 steps backward at a time towards 0.
(a) How many jumps does it take to reach 0?
(b) Write this as a division problem.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Division as Repeated Subtraction:
(a) Jumps:
• 18 - 2 = 16 (1 jump)
• 16 - 2 = 14 (2 jumps)
• ...
• 18 ÷ 2 = 9 jumps to reach 0.

(b) Division Problem:
• 18 ÷ 2 = 9.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct jump count (9 jumps) 1.5 M
Correct division expression (18 ÷ 2 = 9) 1.5 M

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Can We Share?: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 180. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 180.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 170.
4. Divide by 2: N = 85.
5. Verification: Double of 85 is 170, plus 10 gives 180. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 180 1.5 M
Final number N = 85 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Library Bookshelf Organization

The primary section library has 54 new storybooks. Each shelf can hold exactly 9 books.

Analytical Task: How many shelves will be completely filled? If 2 more books arrive, can another shelf be filled?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Shelves filled = 54 ÷ 9 = 6 shelves completely filled.
2. If 2 more books arrive, total books = 54 + 2 = 56.
3. To fill another (7th) shelf, they would need 9 books. Since they only have 2 extra books (7 books short), no, another shelf cannot be filled.
Rubric: 1.5M division of 54 ÷ 9 = 6 + 1.5M remainder analysis for extra books.
