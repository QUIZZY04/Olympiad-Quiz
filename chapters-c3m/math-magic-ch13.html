Chapter 13: Smart Charts!

Tally Marks, Bar Charts, Tables, Traffic Surveys & Drawing Inferences | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Using tally marks (bundles of 5: four vertical lines crossed by one diagonal line).
  • Constructing and reading frequency tables from real-world surveys.
  • Reading simple bar charts with labeled axes and scales.
  • Interpreting data: finding the most popular, least popular, and total counts.

Q1 Bar Chart Interpretation • Sports Survey
Look at the bar chart above: (a) Which is the most popular sport? (b) How many more students prefer cricket over football? (c) How many total students participated in the survey?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Data Reading:
• (a) Most popular sport: Cricket (18 students, highest bar).
• (b) Difference between Cricket and Football: 18 - 12 = 6 more students.
• (c) Total students: 18 + 12 + 15 + 5 = 50 students.

Correct most popular sport identified 1.0 M
Correct difference calculation (18 - 12 = 6) 1.0 M
Correct total students sum (50) 1.0 M

Q8 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Reasoning
In Smart Charts!, examine the practical pattern: If a student observes 53 units on Day 1 and increases by 15 units each day for 3 days, what is the sequence of values? Explain the mathematical rule applied.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Working:
1. Day 1 value = 53.
2. Day 2 value = 53 + 15 = 68.
3. Day 3 value = 68 + 15 = 83.
4. Rule: Arithmetic progression with common difference +15 units per day.

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Smart Charts!: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 190. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 190.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 180.
4. Divide by 2: N = 90.
5. Verification: Double of 90 is 180, plus 10 gives 190. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 190 1.5 M
Final number N = 90 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Canteen Fruit Survey

The canteen manager tracked fruit sales on Wednesday: Bananas (45), Apples (30), Oranges (25), and Guavas (20).

Analytical Task: Which fruit was the most demanded? What fraction of the total fruit sales did bananas represent?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total fruits sold = 45 + 30 + 25 + 20 = 120 fruits.
2. Most demanded fruit: Bananas with 45 sold.
3. Fraction of bananas: 45 out of 120 = 45/120 = 15/40 = 3/8 (three-eighths).
Rubric: 1M total summation + 1M most demanded + 1M simplified fraction.
