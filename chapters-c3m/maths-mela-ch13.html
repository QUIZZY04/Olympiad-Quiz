Chapter 13: Time Goes On

Clocks, Reading Time (Hours & Minutes), AM/PM & Daily Activity Schedules | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Understanding clock hands: hour hand (short) and minute hand (long).
  • Reading time to the hour, half past (30 min), quarter past (15 min), and quarter to (45 min).
  • Difference between AM (midnight to noon) and PM (noon to midnight).
  • Calculating elapsed time between start and end of daily activities.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (22 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Clock Reading • Quarter & Half Hours
What time is shown when: (a) The hour hand is between 3 and 4, and the minute hand points to 6? (b) The hour hand is just past 8, and the minute hand points to 3? (c) The hour hand is near 12, and the minute hand points to 9?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Clock Readings:
• (a) Minute hand at 6 = 30 minutes. Time = 03:30 (Half past 3).
• (b) Minute hand at 3 = 15 minutes. Time = 08:15 (Quarter past 8).
• (c) Minute hand at 9 = 45 minutes (15 minutes to 12). Time = 11:45 (Quarter to 12).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct reading for half past 3 1.0 M
Correct reading for quarter past 8 1.0 M
Correct reading for quarter to 12 1.0 M
Q2 Elapsed Time Calculation • Daily Routines
Meera started doing her math homework at 05:15 PM and finished at 06:05 PM. How many minutes did she spend on her homework?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Start time = 05:15 PM
• End time = 06:05 PM
1. From 05:15 PM to 06:00 PM = 60 - 15 = 45 minutes.
2. From 06:00 PM to 06:05 PM = 5 minutes.
• Total duration = 45 + 5 = 50 minutes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct breakdown to the nearest hour 1.0 M
Correct total elapsed time (50 minutes) 1.0 M

Look at an analog clock face:

(c) Where do they point at 9:45 (quarter to 10)?

Q5 Calendar Reading • Days and Months
Answer the following calendar questions:
(a) How many months in a year have exactly 31 days? Name any three.
(b) How many days does February have in a leap year and in an ordinary year?
(c) If 5th September is a Tuesday, what day of the week is 12th September?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calendar Facts:
• (a) 31-Day Months: There are 7 months with 31 days (January, March, May, July, August, October, December).
• (b) February: February has 29 days in a leap year and 28 days in an ordinary year.
• (c) Day Calculation:
- Since there are 7 days in a week, adding 7 days gives the same day of the week.
- 5 + 7 = 12th September is also a Tuesday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct count (7) and names of 31-day months 1.0 M
Correct February days (29 leap, 28 ordinary) 1.0 M
Correct day of the week deduction (Tuesday) 1.0 M
Q6 Duration of Daily Routines • Elapsed Time
Aman's daily schedule is as follows:
• Leaves for school: 7:30 AM
• Returns from school: 2:00 PM
(a) How many hours does Aman spend away at school?
(b) He plays football from 5:15 PM to 6:30 PM. What is the duration of his playtime?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Elapsed Time:
(a) Time at School:
• From 7:30 AM to 12:00 noon = 4 hours 30 minutes.
• From 12:00 noon to 2:00 PM = 2 hours.
• Total school time = 4 hr 30 min + 2 hr = 6 hours 30 minutes.

(b) Playtime Duration:
• From 5:15 PM to 6:15 PM = 1 hour (60 minutes).
• From 6:15 PM to 6:30 PM = 15 minutes.
• Total playtime = 1 hour 15 minutes (or 75 minutes).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct school duration (6 hours 30 minutes) 1.5 M
Correct playtime duration (1 hour 15 minutes) 1.5 M
Q7 Age Timeline • Date of Birth Analysis
Kavya was born on 15th August 2018.
(a) How old was she on 15th August 2024?
(b) How old will she be on 15th August 2030?
(c) In which year did she celebrate her 5th birthday?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Timeline:
(a) Age in 2024:
• 2024 - 2018 = 6 years old.

(b) Age in 2030:
• 2030 - 2018 = 12 years old.

(c) 5th Birthday Year:
• 2018 + 5 = Year 2023.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct age in 2024 (6 years) 1.0 M
Correct age in 2030 (12 years) 1.0 M
Correct 5th birthday year (2023) 1.0 M
Q8 In-Text Activity • Investigation & Reasoning
In Time Goes On, examine the practical pattern: If a student observes 53 units on Day 1 and increases by 15 units each day for 3 days, what is the sequence of values? Explain the mathematical rule applied.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Working:
1. Day 1 value = 53.
2. Day 2 value = 53 + 15 = 68.
3. Day 3 value = 68 + 15 = 83.
4. Rule: Arithmetic progression with common difference +15 units per day.

Valid rule explanation (+15 daily increase) 1.5 M

Q12 HOTS & Puzzle • Logical Reasoning
Mathematical Riddle from Time Goes On: I am a number. Double me, then add 10, and you will get 190. What number am I? Verify your answer.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Algebraic Verification:
1. Let the unknown number be N.
2. Condition: (2 × N) + 10 = 190.
3. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2 × N = 180.
4. Divide by 2: N = 90.
5. Verification: Double of 90 is 180, plus 10 gives 190. Correct!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Formulating statement: 2N + 10 = 190 1.5 M
Final number N = 90 with verification 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Annual Sports Day Schedule

The 100m sprint race began at 10:20 AM and took 25 minutes. There was a 15-minute prize ceremony immediately following.

Analytical Task: At what time did the prize ceremony conclude? How many total minutes elapsed from the race start to the end of the ceremony?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Race start = 10:20 AM.
2. Race finish = 10:20 AM + 25 min = 10:45 AM.
3. Ceremony finish = 10:45 AM + 15 min = 11:00 AM.
4. Total elapsed time = 25 + 15 = 40 minutes.
Rubric: 1.5M chronological addition of times + 1.5M total duration calculation.
