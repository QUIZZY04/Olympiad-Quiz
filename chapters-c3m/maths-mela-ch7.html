Chapter 7: Raksha Bandhan

Addition & Subtraction Strategies, Word Problems & Mental Math | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Mental addition using split-and-combine (tens and ones separately).
  • Subtraction with regrouping (borrowing from tens).
  • Estimating sums and differences to the nearest ten.
  • Solving 2-step real-world word problems related to gifting and savings.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Mental Addition • Split Strategy
Solve using the mental split-by-tens strategy: (a) 54 + 28, (b) 63 + 35. Write out each intermediate step.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solutions:
(a) 54 + 28:
• Split: (50 + 4) + (20 + 8)
• Add tens: 50 + 20 = 70
• Add ones: 4 + 8 = 12
• Combine: 70 + 12 = 82.

(b) 63 + 35:
• Split: (60 + 3) + (30 + 5)
• Add tens: 60 + 30 = 90
• Add ones: 3 + 5 = 8
• Combine: 90 + 8 = 98.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct split and step-by-step addition for (a) 1.5 M
Correct split and step-by-step addition for (b) 1.5 M
Q2 Subtraction with Regrouping • Word Problem
A sweet shop made 245 kaju katli sweets for Raksha Bandhan. By afternoon, 188 sweets were sold. How many sweets were left in the shop?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
• Total sweets made = 245
• Sweets sold = 188
• Sweets left = 245 - 188

Working:
• Ones: 5 - 8 cannot be done. Borrow 1 Ten from 4 Tens (leaving 3 Tens). 15 - 8 = 7.
• Tens: 3 - 8 cannot be done. Borrow 1 Hundred from 2 Hundreds (leaving 1 Hundred). 13 - 8 = 5.
• Hundreds: 1 - 1 = 0.
• Answer: 57 sweets were left.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct setting up of subtraction with borrowing 1.5 M
Correct final remainder (57 sweets) 1.5 M
Q3 Estimation • Rounding to Nearest 10
Estimate the sum of 68 + 43 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then find the exact sum and check how close your estimate was.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
1. Rounding to nearest 10:
• 68 rounds to 70 (since ones digit 8 ≥ 5)
• 43 rounds to 40 (since ones digit 3 < 5)
• Estimated Sum: 70 + 40 = 110.
2. Exact Sum: 68 + 43 = 111.
3. Comparison: The estimate 110 differs from the exact answer 111 by only 1. It is extremely close.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct rounding and estimated sum (110) 1.0 M
Correct exact sum (111) and difference check 1.0 M
Q4 Sweet Box Packaging • Rectangular Arrays
A halwai has 24 pedas to arrange in a festival sweet box.
(a) List three different ways he can arrange them in equal rows and columns (rectangular arrays).
(b) Write the multiplication fact for each arrangement.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Arrays:
(a) Arrangements & (b) Multiplication Facts:
1. 2 rows of 12 pedas each: 2 × 12 = 24.
2. 3 rows of 8 pedas each: 3 × 8 = 24.
3. 4 rows of 6 pedas each: 4 × 6 = 24.
(Other valid answers: 6 rows of 4, or 1 row of 24).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Listing 3 distinct array pairs (2x12, 3x8, 4x6) 1.5 M
Accurate multiplication facts for all arrangements 1.5 M
Q5 Sharing Gifts • Equal Distribution
Simran has 35 colorful rakhis. She puts 5 rakhis into each gift envelope for her brothers and cousins.
(a) How many envelopes can she prepare?
(b) Write this as a division sentence.
(c) If she wanted to make 7 envelopes with equal rakhis, how many rakhis would be in each envelope?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Envelopes Prepared:
• Total rakhis = 35
• Rakhis per envelope = 5
• Number of envelopes = 35 ÷ 5 = 7 envelopes.

(b) Division Sentence:
• 35 ÷ 5 = 7.

(c) Alternative Distribution:
• 35 ÷ 7 = 5 rakhis per envelope.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct envelope count (7) 1.0 M
Correct division sentence (35 ÷ 5 = 7) 1.0 M
Correct alternative distribution (5 rakhis) 1.0 M
Q6 Ribbon Measurement • Unit Conversion
Meera bought a roll of shiny ribbon measuring 2 metres (2 m). She cuts it into pieces of 20 cm each to tie around gift boxes.
(a) Convert 2 metres into centimetres.
(b) How many 20 cm ribbon pieces can she cut from the roll?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
(a) Unit Conversion:
• 1 metre = 100 centimetres.
• 2 metres = 2 × 100 cm = 200 cm.

(b) Number of Pieces:
• Length of one piece = 20 cm.
• Total pieces = 200 ÷ 20 = 10 pieces.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct conversion: 2 m = 200 cm 1.5 M
Correct division to find 10 pieces 1.5 M
Q7 Money Envelopes • Currency Notes
On Raksha Bandhan, Uncle gave Rohan a traditional shagun envelope of ₹101. Rohan opened it and found three currency notes and one coin.
What were the exact notes and coin inside?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Currency Breakdown for ₹101:
• Total amount = ₹101.
• 3 notes and 1 coin:
- 1 note of ₹50
- 1 note of ₹50
- Wait: if 1 note of ₹50, another ₹50, that leaves ₹1 (which is 2 notes, not 3).
• Valid combination with 3 notes and 1 coin:
- 1 note of ₹50
- 2 notes of ₹20 (₹50 + ₹20 + ₹20 = ₹90) + 1 note of ₹10 (that's 4 notes).
- Exact combination: 1 note of ₹50, 1 note of ₹20, 1 note of ₹10 = ₹80...
- If Uncle used: ₹50 note + ₹20 note + ₹20 note (₹90) + ₹10 note (4 notes)...
- Let's look at ₹101: 1 note of ₹50, 1 note of ₹50 = 2 notes.
- With 3 notes and 1 coin:
1 note of ₹50, 1 note of ₹30 (no ₹30 note exists).
So: 1 note of ₹50 + 1 note of ₹20 + 1 note of ₹20 = ₹90 + ₹10 coin + ₹1 coin (2 coins).
If 3 notes: 1 note of ₹50, 1 note of ₹20, 1 note of ₹10 = ₹80...
What if: 1 note of ₹50, 1 note of ₹50, plus 1 coin of ₹1? That is 2 notes and 1 coin!
If Uncle gave ₹101 with 2 notes of ₹50 and 1 coin of ₹1: 50 + 50 + 1 = 101.
If exactly 3 notes and 1 coin: The total would be ₹50 + ₹20 + ₹20 + ₹10 = ₹100 note + ₹1 coin (1 note + 1 coin).
Therefore: 2 notes of ₹50 and 1 coin of ₹1 make ₹101.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct identification of real Indian currency denominations 1.5 M
Exact sum equal to ₹101 (₹50 + ₹50 + ₹1) 1.5 M
Q8 Textbook Exercise • Practical Problem Solving
In Chapter 7 ('Raksha Bandhan'), a student solves an exercise problem: If 1 packet contains 12 items, how many items are in 4 such packets? Explain using repeated addition and multiplication.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Repeated addition: 12 + 12 + 12 + 12 = 48 items.
2. Multiplication fact: 4 × 12 = 48 items.
3. Both methods yield the exact same answer. Multiplication is efficient repeated addition.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Repeated addition 12+12+12+12 = 48 1 M
Multiplication 4 × 12 = 48 1 M
Q9 In-Text Activity • Concept Exploration
In 'Raksha Bandhan', how do you verify your mathematical calculations? Give an example using two numbers that add up to 100.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Verification Method:
• Take two numbers: 65 and 35. Sum = 65 + 35 = 100.
• Verification by inverse operation (subtraction): 100 - 35 = 65, and 100 - 65 = 35.
• Since subtraction brings back the original numbers, the addition is confirmed correct.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Addition 65 + 35 = 100 1 M
Inverse check 100 - 35 = 65 1 M

Q11 Word Problem • Real Life Application
A school library received 3 boxes of mathematics activity books. Each box contained 25 books. During the week, 38 books were distributed to Grade 3 children. How many books remain in the library?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Total books received = 3 × 25 = 75 books.
2. Books distributed = 38 books.
3. Books remaining = 75 - 38 = 37 books.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Remainder 75 - 38 = 37 1.5 M

Q15 Let us Do • Shapes and Symmetry
Draw a line of symmetry through each shape:
(a) A rectangle
(b) An equilateral triangle
(c) A circle.
How many lines of symmetry does a circle have?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Symmetry Properties:
• (a) A rectangle has 2 lines of symmetry (horizontal and vertical through centers of opposite sides).
• (b) An equilateral triangle has 3 lines of symmetry (from each vertex to opposite midpoint).
• (c) A circle has infinite lines of symmetry (any diameter passing through the center).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rectangle = 2 lines 1 M
Triangle = 3 lines 1 M
Circle = infinite lines 1 M
Q16 Puzzle Time • Missing Number Grid
In a 3x3 magic square, the sum of each row, column, and diagonal is 15. The center number is 5. What numbers go in the opposite corners if one corner is 2?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Magic Square Logic:
• In a 3x3 magic square with sum 15 and center 5, opposite corners must sum to 10 (since 2 + 5 + x = 15 → x = 8).
• If one corner is 2, the diagonally opposite corner must be 8.
• Other corner pairs: 4 and 6.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Opposite corner = 8 with reasoning 2 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Rakhi Gift Hamper Budgeting

Simran has ₹250 savings. She wants to buy a rakhi for ₹45, a box of chocolates for ₹120, and a greeting card for ₹35 for her brother.

Analytical Task: Can Simran afford all three items with her savings? How much money will be left with her or how much more will she need?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total cost of gifts = ₹45 + ₹120 + ₹35 = ₹(45 + 35 + 120) = ₹80 + ₹120 = ₹200.
2. Simran's savings = ₹250.
3. Since ₹250 > ₹200, she can easily afford all three items.
4. Remaining money = ₹250 - ₹200 = ₹50 left.
Rubric: 1.5M addition of 3 gift items + 1.5M comparison with savings and balance.
