Maths Syllabus Weightage for JEE

Mathematics in JEE Main is notorious for being lengthy. Prioritize these high-weightage sections to maximize your attempts.

  • Calculus (35%)
    Core Analytical

    Limits, Continuity, Differentiability, Application of Derivatives, Indefinite & Definite Integration, Differential Equations. Highly conceptual.

  • Algebra (30%)
    Formula Intensive

    Matrices & Determinants, Complex Numbers, Sequence & Series, Permutations & Combinations, Binomial Theorem, Probability.

  • Coordinate Geometry & Vectors (35%)
    Highly Scoring

    Straight Lines, Conic Sections, Vectors, and 3D Geometry. These chapters are formula-heavy but guarantee high marks if practiced well.
