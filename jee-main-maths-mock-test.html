Mathematics Syllabus Weightage for JEE

Mathematics in JEE Main is notoriously lengthy. Prioritize these high-weightage sections to maximize your attempts within the time limit.

  • Calculus (30%)
    Core Analytical

    Limits, Continuity, Differentiability, Application of Derivatives, Indefinite & Definite Integrals, Differential Equations.

  • Algebra (35%)
    Highly Scoring

    Matrices & Determinants, Complex Numbers, Sequence & Series, Binomial Theorem, Probability, Permutations & Combinations.

  • Coordinate Geometry & Vectors (35%)
    Visualization Focus

    Straight Lines, Circles, Conic Sections (Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola), Vector Algebra, and 3D Geometry.
