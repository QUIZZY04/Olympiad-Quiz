Chapter 1: Good Morning & The Magic Garden

Poem by Fannie R. Buchanan & Story of School Children Loving and Watering Garden Flowers | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'Good Morning': Wishing the sky, sun, little winds, grass, and bees good morning.
  • Story 'The Magic Garden': Roses, sunflowers, marigolds, and poppies in the school garden.
  • Why flowers love little children: children bring water cans, dig the ground nicely, and bring breadcrumbs for birds.
  • Fairies in the garden: singing sweet songs that only children can hear.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem & Story
Why is the child in the poem 'Good Morning' happy? Why did the flowers love the little school children in 'The Magic Garden'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Child's Happiness: The child is happy because night has passed, morning has arrived, and the child is wide awake to run out and play with the sun, sky, wind, birds, and bees.
2. Why Flowers Loved Children: The flowers loved the little children because they brought their watering cans to pour cool water on their thirsty roots and dug the soil around the plants tenderly.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of child's joy at morning waking 1.5 M
Two reasons why flowers loved children (watering and caring) 1.5 M
Q2 Talk Time • Garden Flowers
Name five flowers that grew in the magic garden. Which flower lifted its head very high toward the sun, and why?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Flowers in Magic Garden:
1. Sunflowers, 2. Roses, 3. Marigolds, 4. Poppies, and 5. Pansies.
• The sunflower lifted its head highest because the golden morning sunshine fell on the magic garden more warmly than anywhere else.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Five flowers named accurately 1.5 M
Sunflower behavior and sunlight reason 1.5 M

Make compound words by joining words from Box A and Box B: (Sun, Play, Rain, Water) + (Fall, Ground, Flower, Drop).

Compound Words:

Q4 Reading is Fun • The Magic Garden
In 'The Magic Garden':
(a) Why did the flowers love the little children so much?
(b) Why did the birds love the children?
(c) Name five flowers that grew in the magic garden of the school.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Textbook Comprehension:
(a) Why Flowers Loved Children:
• The flowers loved the little children because they brought their watering cans and watered their thirsty roots, and dug the ground nicely.

(b) Why Birds Loved Children:
• The birds loved the children because they were kind and brought breadcrumbs for them to eat.

(c) Five Flowers in the Garden:
• 1. Sunflowers
• 2. Roses
• 3. Marigolds
• 4. Poppies
• 5. Pansies.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct reason why flowers loved children (watering roots) 1.0 M
Correct reason why birds loved children (breadcrumbs) 1.0 M
Listing 5 authentic flowers from textbook 1.0 M

Q7 Team Time • Flower Scrapbook Activity
Explain how to make a dried flower scrapbook as described in the textbook activity.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Dried Flower Scrapbook Guide:
1. Collection: Collect fallen petals and leaves from the garden (never pluck live flowers needlessly).
2. Pressing: Place the flowers carefully between the sheets of an old newspaper or heavy telephone directory.
3. Drying: Place heavy books on top and leave undisturbed for 4 to 5 days until all moisture is absorbed.
4. Mounting: Gently paste the dried flowers in a scrapbook using a dab of glue, and write their English and local names underneath.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four clear procedural steps described 2.0 M
Careful handling and labeling highlighted 1.0 M
Q8 Reading is Fun • Direct Comprehension
In 'Good Morning & The Magic Garden', describe what key lesson or character trait is highlighted when the central character faces a challenge. How does this connect to real-life friendship or cooperation?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Textual Comprehension & Analysis:
1. In the story/poem Good Morning & The Magic Garden, the central character demonstrates perseverance, kindness, and teamwork when confronting difficulties.
2. When individuals cooperate rather than act selfishly, problems are resolved much faster and everyone benefits.
3. Real-Life Connection: In our daily lives, sharing tasks with classmates or family members builds trust and mutual respect, just as seen in this lesson.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of central trait (cooperation/kindness) 1.5 M
Valid real-world connection to friendship/teamwork 1.5 M

Q10 Talk Time • Oral Expression & Personal Reflection
Have you ever had an experience similar to the events described in 'Good Morning & The Magic Garden'? Share what you did, who was with you, and how you felt afterward.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Personal Reflection:
• Experience: Yes, during our school sports day, our relay team was trailing behind. Just like the theme in Good Morning & The Magic Garden, we encouraged each other and stayed calm.
• Action: In the final lap, our teammate ran with full determination, and we secured second place.
• Feeling: I felt proud and joyful because supporting each other brought us closer together.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear narration of personal experience 1.5 M
Expression of feelings and thematic alignment 1.5 M
Q11 Grammar & Language Structure • Sentence Mechanics
Punctuate and rewrite the following sentences correctly using capital letters, commas, and full stops:
(i) rohan priya and amit went to the park on sunday
(ii) what a beautiful flower said the little girl
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Punctuation & Capitalisation:
• (i) Rohan, Priya, and Amit went to the park on Sunday. (Capitalized names, comma between items, capital S for Sunday, full stop at end).
• (ii) "What a beautiful flower!" said the little girl. (Capital W, quotation marks for speech, exclamation mark).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sentence (i) correctly punctuated 1.5 M
Sentence (ii) correctly punctuated 1.5 M

Write a short 4-sentence paragraph describing your favorite part of 'Good Morning & The Magic Garden'. Why was that particular moment memorable to you?

It taught me that small acts of courage can make a big difference. This chapter inspires me to be a thoughtful and supportive friend every day.

Relevant content reflecting chapter theme 1.5 M
Coherent 4-sentence structure and correct grammar 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Herbal Garden Care

Class 3 students decided to maintain a small botanical patch in school with tulsi, mint, aloe vera, and marigolds.

Analytical Task: What daily care routine should students establish to ensure their garden stays healthy throughout the year?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Water the plants gently in the early morning or evening so water does not evaporate in harsh midday heat.
2. Remove dried leaves, pull out wild weeds by hand, and add organic dry-leaf compost once every fortnight.
Rubric: 1.5M proper watering schedule + 1.5M weeding and natural composting.
