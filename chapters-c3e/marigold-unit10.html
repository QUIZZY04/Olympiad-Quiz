Chapter 10: How Creatures Move & The Ship of the Desert

Poem on Animals' Natural Motion & Famous Dialogue Between the Camel and the Lion | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'How Creatures Move': Lion walks on padded paws, squirrel leaps, fly crawls, seals dive, worms wiggle, and boys/girls run and leap.
  • Story 'The Ship of the Desert': The Camel explaining his desert adaptations to the King of the Forest, the Lion.
  • Camel's survival tools: Thick padded feet that do not burn on hot desert sand, hump storing fat for weeks, and eating thorny bushes without hurting thick tongue.
  • Comparative dialogue and recognizing natural biological adaptations.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem & Story
Why is the camel called 'The Ship of the Desert'? How do the padded feet of the camel help it run across burning sand dunes?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Ship of the Desert: Just as a ship travels smoothly across ocean waters carrying people and cargo, the camel is the only animal that can travel tirelessly across vast sandy deserts without food or water for days.
2. Padded Feet: The camel has thick, wide, flat padded feet. The broad pads prevent its feet from sinking into loose sand, and the thick skin prevents the burning heat of the sand (over 200°F) from scorching its soles.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of Ship of the Desert metaphor 1.5 M
Scientific explanation of wide thick padded feet 1.5 M
Q2 Animal Adaptations • Biology in English
What does the camel store in its hump, and what does it eat when there is no grass in the desert?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Adaptation Facts:
• The Hump: The camel stores fat (stored food reserve) in its hump, which its body uses when food is unavailable.
• Desert Diet: It eats prickly thorns and desert bushes. Its mouth and tongue are so thick that sharp thorns cannot prick or hurt it.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct fact: stores fat (not liquid water) in hump 1.5 M
Thorny bushes and thick mouth explanation 1.5 M

Q4 Reading is Fun • The Ship of the Desert
In 'The Ship of the Desert':
(a) Why is the camel called the 'Ship of the Desert'?
(b) How can a camel walk easily on burning desert sand where other animals cannot?
(c) Where does the camel store food and water, and for how many days can it survive without drinking?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Adaptation Science & Comprehension:
(a) Ship of the Desert:
• The camel is called the Ship of the Desert because it can navigate smoothly across endless oceans of burning desert sand dunes just like a ship sails across the sea.

(b) Walking on Hot Sand:
• Camels have broad, thick, padded feet that do not sink into loose sand, and the thick skin protects against scorching heat.

(c) Hump & Survival:
• The camel stores food as fat in its hump and can survive up to two weeks (14 days) without drinking water.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Origin of title 'Ship of the Desert' 1.0 M
Thick padded feet adaptation explained 1.0 M
Hump fat storage and 2 weeks survival fact 1.0 M
Q5 Poem Comprehension • 'How Creatures Move'
In the poem 'How Creatures Move':
(a) Match how each animal moves: Lion, Squirrel, Fly, Worm.
[Crawls, Walks on padded paws, Hops/Leaps, Creeps up a wall]
(b) How do boys and girls have much more fun than all these animals?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
(a) Animal Movements:
• Lion $\rightarrow$ Walks on padded paws
• Squirrel $\rightarrow$ Hops and leaps from limb to limb
• Fly $\rightarrow$ Creeps straight up a wall
• Worm $\rightarrow$ Wiggles all around / crawls.

(b) Boys and Girls:
• Boys and girls have much more fun because they can do all movements: they can leap, dance, walk, and run!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct animal movement matches 1.5 M
Human versatility (leap, dance, walk, run) highlighted 1.5 M

Compare how animals adapt to their environments by filling in the table:

Q7 Dialogue • Camel and Lion Conversation
In the chapter dialogue, what does the lion say when the camel invites him to walk in the desert?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Dialogue:
• When the camel proudly offers to carry the lion across the desert sand, the lion looks down at his own paws, feels the blazing heat of the sand, and says respectfully: 'No, thank you! I'd rather stay right here in my cool green forest!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate recounting of lion's humorous refusal 2.0 M
Q8 Reading is Fun • Direct Comprehension
In 'How Creatures Move & The Ship of the Desert', describe what key lesson or character trait is highlighted when the central character faces a challenge. How does this connect to real-life friendship or cooperation?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Textual Comprehension & Analysis:
1. In the story/poem How Creatures Move & The Ship of the Desert, the central character demonstrates perseverance, kindness, and teamwork when confronting difficulties.
2. When individuals cooperate rather than act selfishly, problems are resolved much faster and everyone benefits.
3. Real-Life Connection: In our daily lives, sharing tasks with classmates or family members builds trust and mutual respect, just as seen in this lesson.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of central trait (cooperation/kindness) 1.5 M
Valid real-world connection to friendship/teamwork 1.5 M

Q10 Talk Time • Oral Expression & Personal Reflection
Have you ever had an experience similar to the events described in 'How Creatures Move & The Ship of the Desert'? Share what you did, who was with you, and how you felt afterward.

• Action: In the final lap, our teammate ran with full determination, and we secured second place.

Punctuation & Capitalisation:

• (ii) "What a beautiful flower!" said the little girl. (Capital W, quotation marks for speech, exclamation mark).

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Animal Adaptations Across Biomes

While the camel thrives in hot Thar deserts, the Polar Bear thrives in freezing Arctic snow, and the Lion rules African grasslands.

Analytical Task: Why would a Lion struggle to survive in the Thar desert even though he is called the 'King of the Jungle'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The Lion does not have wide padded feet to prevent sinking in loose sand, nor does he have thick soles to tolerate burning ground.
2. The Lion requires large quantities of water daily and abundant prey animals (zebras, deer), which do not exist across waterless desert dunes.
Rubric: 1.5M physical feet limitations in hot sand + 1.5M lack of water and prey in desert.
