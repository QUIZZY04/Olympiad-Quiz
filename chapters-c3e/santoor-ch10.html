Chapter 10: Night

Night Sky, Moon, Stars, Nocturnal Animals, Quiet Slumber & Peaceful Rest | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Natural phenomena of night: sunset, darkness, twinkling stars, and changing phases of the moon.
  • Nocturnal creatures: owls, fireflies, and bats that become active after sunset.
  • The value of peaceful rest and sleep for physical growth and mental health.
  • Contrasting day activities (loud, bright, busy) with night tranquility (quiet, calm, dark).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Poem Comprehension
What changes take place in nature when day turns into night? Name two animals that stay awake during the night.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Changes in Nature: The sun sets beneath the horizon, darkness spreads over the sky, the air cools down, flowers close their petals, and stars begin to twinkle alongside the glowing moon.
2. Nocturnal Animals: Owls and bats (or fireflies) stay awake and active during the night.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Detailed description of evening and nightfall changes 1.5 M
Two correct nocturnal animals named 1.5 M
Q2 Vocabulary • Day vs Night Antonyms
Give the opposites for: (a) Day, (b) Dark, (c) Sleep, (d) Loud.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Antonyms:
• (a) Day × Night
• (b) Dark × Bright / Light
• (c) Sleep × Wake
• (d) Loud × Quiet / Silent.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct antonyms for all 4 words (0.5M each) 2.0 M
Q3 Health & Well-Being • Sleeping Habits
Why is getting 8 to 9 hours of uninterrupted sleep every night important for growing children in Class 3?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Importance of Sleep:
1. Physical Growth: Growth hormones are released primarily during deep sleep, helping bones and muscles develop.
2. Brain Memory: Sleep allows the brain to organize lessons learned during the day, improving memory, alertness, and mood in school.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Physical growth explanation 1.5 M
Brain restoration and memory consolidation 1.5 M
Q4 Poem Comprehension • 'Night'
In the poem 'Night':
(a) What heavenly body lights up the earth when the sun goes to rest?
(b) What do the stars in the dark sky look like according to the poet?
(c) Which animals become active at night (nocturnal creatures)?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
(a) Night Light:
• The Moon lights up the earth with its cool, gentle silver rays.

(b) Stars Description:
• The poet compares the sparkling stars to glittering diamonds or tiny lamps glowing across the dark velvet sky.

(c) Nocturnal Animals:
• Owls, bats, fireflies, and moths become active at night.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct identification of the Moon 1.0 M
Poetic imagery of stars explained 1.0 M
Two or more nocturnal creatures named 1.0 M
Q5 Science Connection • Day vs Night
Why does the earth have day and night? Explain in two simple sentences for a Class 3 student.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Scientific Explanation:
1. The Earth spins continuously like a top around its own axis once every 24 hours.
2. The side facing the Sun receives light and has Day, while the side turned away from the Sun is in darkness and has Night.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mention of Earth's rotation (spinning) 1.0 M
Clear relationship between facing the sun and day/night 1.0 M

Q7 Bedtime Routine • Personal Expression
Write two healthy habits you should follow every night before going to bed.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Healthy Bedtime Habits:
1. Brush your teeth properly with fluoride toothpaste to protect them from cavities overnight.
2. Turn off television, mobile screens, and tablet devices at least 30 minutes before sleeping to ensure deep, peaceful rest.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two practical, healthy bedtime habits described 2.0 M

Q11 Grammar & Language Structure • Sentence Mechanics
Punctuate and rewrite the following sentences correctly using capital letters, commas, and full stops:
(i) rohan priya and amit went to the park on sunday
(ii) what a beautiful flower said the little girl
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Punctuation & Capitalisation:
• (i) Rohan, Priya, and Amit went to the park on Sunday. (Capitalized names, comma between items, capital S for Sunday, full stop at end).
• (ii) "What a beautiful flower!" said the little girl. (Capital W, quotation marks for speech, exclamation mark).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sentence (i) correctly punctuated 1.5 M
Sentence (ii) correctly punctuated 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Light Pollution Awareness

In large cities with bright neon signs and streetlights, children rarely see more than a handful of stars at night, whereas in village skies thousands of stars are clearly visible.

Analytical Task: What is 'light pollution', and how does excessive artificial light affect both stargazers and nocturnal wildlife?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Light pollution is the excessive, misdirected artificial illumination of the night sky by human cities.
2. It hides the beauty of constellations from stargazers and disorients nocturnal creatures like migrating birds, sea turtles, and night insects who rely on darkness for navigation.
Rubric: 1.5M definition of light pollution + 1.5M ecological impact on navigation.
