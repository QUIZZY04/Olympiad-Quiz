Chapter 3: Best Friends

School Friendships, Mutual Support, Studying Together & Conflict Resolution | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Qualities of true friendship: kindness, honesty, mutual encouragement, and understanding.
  • How friends help each other during difficult lessons and creative activities.
  • Resolving petty disagreements through honest conversation rather than anger.
  • Grammar: using singular and plural nouns correctly.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Comprehension
What makes Pooja and Neha best friends in the story? How do they help each other when one finds arithmetic difficult?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Pooja and Neha are best friends because they share everything, listen respectfully to each other, and encourage one another in every school task.
2. When Neha finds arithmetic sums difficult, Pooja patiently sits beside her, explains the steps using counters, and celebrates when Neha gets the correct answer.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Characteristics of their friendship 1.5 M
Explanation of how arithmetic support is given 1.5 M
Q2 Grammar • Singular and Plural Nouns
Write the plural forms for: (a) Friend, (b) Story, (c) Class, (d) Wish. Make one sentence using 'friends'.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Plural Forms:
• (a) Friend → Friends
• (b) Story → Stories (ending in consonant + y changes to -ies)
• (c) Class → Classes (words ending in -ss take -es)
• (d) Wish → Wishes (words ending in -sh take -es).

Sentence: 'All my friends cheered when our class won the quiz competition.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct plural forms for all 4 nouns 2.0 M
Correct, meaningful sentence using friends 1.0 M
Q3 Conflict Resolution • Role Play
If two friends both want to use the same storybook during library period, what is the best way to resolve the problem peacefully?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Peaceful Solution:
1. They can sit together and read the storybook together, turning pages by turn.
2. Alternatively, they can agree that one friend reads it during the first half of the period, and the second friend reads it during the second half.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Collaborative reading suggestion 1.0 M
Fair time-sharing agreement 1.0 M
Q4 Grammar • Articles 'a' and 'an'
Fill in the blanks with the correct article 'a' or 'an':
(a) Circle saw ___ apple on the tree.
(b) Triangle drew ___ triangle with three sharp corners.
(c) Square made ___ orange box.
(d) They drew ___ umbrella to protect themselves from rain.
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Articles Rules (Vowel sounds take 'an', consonant sounds take 'a'):
• (a) Circle saw an apple on the tree ('a' sound $\rightarrow$ vowel).
• (b) Triangle drew a triangle with three sharp corners ('t' sound $\rightarrow$ consonant).
• (c) Square made an orange box ('o' sound $\rightarrow$ vowel).
• (d) They drew an umbrella to protect themselves from rain ('u' sound $\rightarrow$ vowel).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct articles for (a) and (b) 2.0 M
Correct articles for (c) and (d) 2.0 M
Q5 Shape Characters • Descriptive Vocabulary
In the story 'Best Friends', Circle, Square, and Triangle argued about who is the most important.
(a) What did Circle boast about?
(b) What did Triangle boast about?
(c) How did they realize that they are best friends when working together?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Comprehension:
(a) Circle's Boast:
• Circle said, 'I am round like the sun and the moon! Without me, wheels cannot roll and carts cannot move!'

(b) Triangle's Boast:
• Triangle said, 'I have three pointed corners and three straight sides! I make rooftops, mountains, and ice-cream cones!'

(c) Working Together:
• When they combined on paper, Triangle made the roof, Square made the walls, and Circle made the clock and doorknob to draw a beautiful house. They realized teamwork makes the best pictures.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate statement of Circle's boast 1.0 M
Accurate statement of Triangle's boast 1.0 M
Moral lesson of collaborative drawing explained 1.0 M

Look around your classroom and list two objects for each shape:

Two valid square items 1.0 M
Two valid triangle items 1.0 M
Q7 Sentence Construction • Conjunction 'and'
Combine each pair of short sentences into one sentence using 'and':
(a) Circle rolled on the ground. Square danced beside him.
(b) Triangle made a cap. Circle made a smiley face.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Combined Sentences:
• (a) Circle rolled on the ground and Square danced beside him.
• (b) Triangle made a cap and Circle made a smiley face.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct combination of sentence (a) 1.0 M
Correct combination of sentence (b) 1.0 M
Q8 Reading is Fun • Direct Comprehension
In 'Best Friends', describe what key lesson or character trait is highlighted when the central character faces a challenge. How does this connect to real-life friendship or cooperation?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Textual Comprehension & Analysis:
1. In the story/poem Best Friends, the central character demonstrates perseverance, kindness, and teamwork when confronting difficulties.
2. When individuals cooperate rather than act selfishly, problems are resolved much faster and everyone benefits.
3. Real-Life Connection: In our daily lives, sharing tasks with classmates or family members builds trust and mutual respect, just as seen in this lesson.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of central trait (cooperation/kindness) 1.5 M
Valid real-world connection to friendship/teamwork 1.5 M

Q10 Talk Time • Oral Expression & Personal Reflection
Have you ever had an experience similar to the events described in 'Best Friends'? Share what you did, who was with you, and how you felt afterward.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Personal Reflection:
• Experience: Yes, during our school sports day, our relay team was trailing behind. Just like the theme in Best Friends, we encouraged each other and stayed calm.
• Action: In the final lap, our teammate ran with full determination, and we secured second place.
• Feeling: I felt proud and joyful because supporting each other brought us closer together.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear narration of personal experience 1.5 M
Expression of feelings and thematic alignment 1.5 M
Q11 Grammar & Language Structure • Sentence Mechanics
Punctuate and rewrite the following sentences correctly using capital letters, commas, and full stops:
(i) rohan priya and amit went to the park on sunday
(ii) what a beautiful flower said the little girl
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Punctuation & Capitalisation:
• (i) Rohan, Priya, and Amit went to the park on Sunday. (Capitalized names, comma between items, capital S for Sunday, full stop at end).
• (ii) "What a beautiful flower!" said the little girl. (Capital W, quotation marks for speech, exclamation mark).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sentence (i) correctly punctuated 1.5 M
Sentence (ii) correctly punctuated 1.5 M
Q12 Let's Write & Creative Expression • Paragraph
Write a short 4-sentence paragraph describing your favorite part of 'Best Friends'. Why was that particular moment memorable to you?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Paragraph:
My favorite moment in Best Friends is when the characters unite to achieve their common goal. The vivid illustrations and dialogue make the scene feel truly alive. It taught me that small acts of courage can make a big difference. This chapter inspires me to be a thoughtful and supportive friend every day.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Relevant content reflecting chapter theme 1.5 M
Coherent 4-sentence structure and correct grammar 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Class Project Teamwork

Simran and Kabir were paired to make a science chart on 'Save Trees'. Kabir was good at drawing, while Simran wrote neat slogans and captions.

Analytical Task: How does dividing roles based on individual strengths help team projects succeed?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. By dividing work according to what each member does best, both partners enjoy their task and feel valued.
2. Kabir's creative drawings and Simran's clear slogans combine to make a high-quality chart finished on time with zero conflict.
Rubric: 1.5M explanation of role delegation + 1.5M positive outcome of combined strengths.
