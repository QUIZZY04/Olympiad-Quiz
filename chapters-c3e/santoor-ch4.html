Chapter 4: Out in the Garden

Plants, Birds, Butterflies, Rhymes, Nature Appreciation & Outdoor Observation | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Observing natural flora and fauna: flower petals, buzzing bees, caterpillars, and butterflies.
  • Poetic structure: rhyming couplets, musical rhythm, and descriptive imagery.
  • Life cycle hints: caterpillar changing into a beautiful butterfly.
  • Sensory words related to sight, smell, and sound in a lush green garden.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

Q1 Let us Think • Poetry Comprehension
What does the poet see and hear while walking out in the garden? Identify two rhyming pairs in the poem.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
1. Sights & Sounds: The poet sees bright blooming flowers, buzzing honeybees collecting nectar, and fluttering butterflies dancing in the gentle breeze.
2. Rhyming Pairs:
• Day — Play
• Breeze — Trees

Q4 Poem Rhymes • 'Out in the Garden'
In the poem 'Out in the Garden':
(a) What games do the children play out in the garden?
(b) Write three pairs of rhyming words from the poem.
(c) Why is playing outside in fresh air good for your health?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
(a) Games:
• Children play with a ball, skipping rope, running races, and hide-and-seek.

(b) Rhyming Pairs:
• Ball — All
• Play — Day
• Round — Ground.

(c) Health Benefits:
• Playing outdoors gives our body sunshine (Vitamin D), strengthens muscles and bones, and keeps our mind active and cheerful.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct outdoor games mentioned 1.0 M
Three accurate rhyming pairs 1.0 M
Clear explanation of health benefits 1.0 M

Q6 Speaking & Rules • Fair Play
(a) What should you do when you lose a game while playing with friends?
(b) Why is it important to follow rules in any game?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sportsmanship & Ethics:
(a) Grace in Defeat:
• We should smile, congratulate the winning friend with a handshake, and try harder next time without crying or arguing.

(b) Importance of Rules:
• Rules ensure that every player has an equal, fair chance to win, prevent injuries, and keep the game fun for everyone.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Pollinator Protection Campaign

Students in Class 3 notice that gardeners are spraying harsh chemical weedkillers near flowering marigold beds, causing bees and butterflies to disappear.

Analytical Task: Why are bees and butterflies essential for flowers and crops? What safe advice should students give the school gardener?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Bees and butterflies are pollinators: they carry pollen grains from flower to flower, which helps plants produce seeds, flowers, and fruits.
2. Students should advise the gardener to avoid toxic chemicals and use natural organic compost, weeding by hand to protect beneficial pollinators.
Rubric: 1.5M ecological role of pollination + 1.5M eco-friendly alternative advice.
