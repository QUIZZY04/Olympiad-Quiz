Chapter 7: The Big Laddoo

Humour, Folk Tale, Cooperation, Problem Solving & Festival Celebrations | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Folk tale humour: making an enormous festive laddoo that cannot fit through the door.
  • Cooperative problem solving: how the entire community pitches in to share and celebrate.
  • Sequencing ingredients: chickpea flour (besan), ghee, sugar, cardamom, and dry fruits.
  • Understanding size adjectives: tiny, big, huge, gigantic, enormous.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Story Comprehension
Why did the sweet-maker make such an enormous laddoo in 'The Big Laddoo'? What problem arose once the laddoo was prepared?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Reason: The sweet-maker wanted to prepare a grand sweet for the village festival that everyone would remember and talk about with pride.
2. The Problem: The laddoo turned out so gigantic that it was wider than the sweet-shop door! It could not be carried out into the courtyard without breaking the doorway.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct festival celebration motive 1.5 M
Accurate identification of the door width predicament 1.5 M
Q2 Problem Solving • Community Action
How did the villagers solve the problem of the oversized laddoo without breaking down the shop door?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solution:
Instead of forcing the giant laddoo through the doorway, the village elders suggested inviting everyone inside the spacious kitchen courtyard. The sweet-maker portioned the laddoo into hundreds of sweet bowls, sharing a portion with every child and family in the village.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clever resolution through on-site distribution and sharing 2.0 M

Q4 Recipe Sequencing • The Big Laddoo
In the story 'The Big Laddoo', Grandmother makes a special gigantic laddoo for the children:
(a) Arrange the cooking steps in correct chronological order (1 to 4):
• Shape into round balls using palms.
• Roast besan (gram flour) in pure ghee till golden brown.
• Cool slightly and mix in powdered sugar and cardamom.
• Garnish with chopped almonds and pistachios.
(b) Why did Grandmother make one huge laddoo instead of many small ones?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Cooking Sequence:
(a) Correct Order:
1. Roast besan in pure ghee till golden brown.
2. Cool slightly and mix in powdered sugar and cardamom.
3. Shape into round balls using palms.
4. Garnish with chopped almonds and pistachios.

(b) The Huge Laddoo:
• Grandmother wanted all the children to sit in a circle, break pieces from the same giant laddoo together, and learn the joy of sharing.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct chronological order (1 to 4) 2.0 M
Moral purpose of unity and sharing explained 1.0 M

(c) We should always be polite when speaking to others.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:

• (c) True: Politeness and good manners are important.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Community Festival Food Sharing

At a community Langar / Bhandara feast, volunteers cook sweet halwa for 400 people. Some children want to serve elderly visitors first.

Analytical Task: What core human values are practiced when people cook together and serve food respectfully to everyone regardless of background?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Equality & Humility: Serving everyone equally without discrimination teaches mutual respect.
2. Community Spirit & Compassion: Respecting elders and sharing resources selflessly ensures nobody in the community goes hungry.
Rubric: 1.5M concept of equality and respect + 1.5M selfless service and community solidarity.
