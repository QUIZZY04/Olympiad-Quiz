Chapter 11: Chanda Mama Counts the Stars

Moon Lore, Counting Stars, Bundles of Tens & Astronomical Curiosity | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Traditional Indian folklore: affectionate representation of the Moon as 'Chanda Mama'.
  • Counting enormous numbers by grouping: Chanda Mama grouping stars into packets of 10 and 100.
  • Connection between language arts and foundational mathematical grouping.
  • Astronomical wonder: understanding that stars are distant burning suns.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Story Comprehension
Why was Chanda Mama having trouble counting the stars in the sky? What clever system of cards did he invent to keep count?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. The Trouble: There were so many twinkling stars appearing and disappearing across the vast sky that Chanda Mama lost count whenever a star moved.
2. The Card System:
• For every single star, he put a [1] card in his pocket.
• When he collected ten [1] cards, he exchanged them for one [10] card.
• When he collected ten [10] cards, he exchanged them for one big [100] card.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of difficulty with infinite twinkling stars 1.0 M
Detailed breakdown of the base-10 card exchange system 2.0 M
Q2 Mathematical Integration • Cards to Numbers
If Chanda Mama has these cards in his pocket, what is the total number of stars counted? (a) Two [100] cards, three [10] cards, and four [1] cards. (b) Three [100] cards and eight [1] cards.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculations:
• (a) (2 × 100) + (3 × 10) + (4 × 1) = 200 + 30 + 4 = 234 stars.
• (b) (3 × 100) + (0 × 10) + (8 × 1) = 300 + 0 + 8 = 308 stars.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct calculation for (a) 234 1.0 M
Correct calculation for (b) 308 (handling zero tens) 1.0 M

Q4 Story Comprehension • Chanda Mama
In the delightful tale 'Chanda Mama Counts the Stars':
(a) Why did Chanda Mama want to count the stars in the sky?
(b) Why did he find it so difficult to count them all?
(c) What clever advice did Mother Earth give to Chanda Mama?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Breakdown:
(a) Counting Purpose:
• Chanda Mama wanted to know exactly how many little sparkling star-children he had in his sky family.

(b) The Difficulty:
• The stars kept twinkling, blinking, moving, and hiding behind passing clouds, which caused Chanda Mama to lose count again and again.

(c) Mother Earth's Advice:
• Mother Earth smiled and said, 'Do not worry about counting them all! Simply shine your silver light on them, and they will twinkle back with love.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct motive of counting star family 1.0 M
Clear reason why counting stars is impossible (twinkling/clouds) 1.0 M
Wise advice of Mother Earth explained 1.0 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Constellations & Pattern Recognition

Ancient astronomers grouped bright stars into pictures of hunters, bears, and queens called constellations (like Saptarishi / Ursa Major).

Analytical Task: How did grouping stars into recognizable patterns help ancient travelers and sailors navigate across open oceans at night?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. In ancient times before GPS or compasses, stars provided fixed landmarks in the sky.
2. By locating the Saptarishi constellation and following its pointer stars to the North Star (Dhruva Tara), sailors could always identify true North and steer ships safely without getting lost.
Rubric: 1.5M purpose of constellations as sky landmarks + 1.5M locating the North Star for navigation.
