Chapter 4: Sea Song & A Little Fish Story

Poem on Hearing Ocean in a Shell & Classic Story of a Small Fish Saved by His Size | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'Sea Song': Finding a curly seashell on the sand, taking it home, and hearing a soft song inside.
  • Story 'A Little Fish Story': A tiny fish unhappy with his small size wishing to be big like others.
  • The turning point: Getting caught in a fisherman's net, where all big fish are trapped, but the tiny fish slips through the mesh to freedom.
  • Moral: Be grateful and content with who you are; every attribute has its unique purpose.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem & Story
Where did the child find the seashell, and what surprise happened when he held it to his ear? Why was the little fish unhappy at the beginning of the story?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Sea Song: The child found a curly seashell lying cold on the sea sand. When his mother held it to his ear, a soft, sweet, clear song seemed to sing from inside the shell.
2. Little Fish's Unhappiness: The little fish was constantly complaining because he was very tiny compared to the huge fish in his school and felt that being small made him unimportant.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct shell discovery and song heard from inside 1.5 M
Little fish's complaint about his small size 1.5 M
Q2 Turning Point & Moral Discovery
What happened when the fishermen hauled their net out of the water? How did being small save the little fish's life?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Climax & Moral:
• When the fishermen pulled the great net into the boat, all the big fish flopped helplessly, trapped by the netting.
• Because the little fish was so small, he wriggled easily through the tiny gaps in the net's mesh and splashed back down into the cool, safe ocean.
• From that day on, he never wished to be a single bit bigger!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Description of net hauling and big fish trapped 1.5 M
Little fish slipping through mesh gaps to safety 1.5 M
Q3 Vocabulary • Collective Nouns
Fill in the blanks with suitable collective nouns: (A ___ of fish, A ___ of birds, A ___ of wolves, A ___ of cows).
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Collective Nouns:
• A school / shoal of fish
• A flock of birds
• A pack of wolves
• A herd of cows
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct collective nouns for all 4 groups 2.0 M
Q4 Poem Comprehension • 'Sea Song'
In 'Sea Song':
(a) Where did the child find the curly sea shell?
(b) What surprised the child when he held the shell to his ear?
(c) Write two rhyming pairs from the poem.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
(a) Shell Location:
• The child found the curly shell lying on the sandy seashore.

(b) The Surprise:
• The child was surprised and amazed to hear a soft, sweet, clear song singing inside the shell.

(c) Rhyming Pairs:
• Found — Ground
• Ear — Clear (or Hand — Sand).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Found on sand correctly mentioned 1.0 M
Hearing singing in shell described 1.0 M
Two valid rhyming pairs 1.0 M
Q5 Reading is Fun • A Little Fish Story
In 'A Little Fish Story':
(a) Why was the little fish unhappy?
(b) What happened when a huge fishing net trapped the whole school of fish?
(c) Why did the little fish never complain about being small again?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Details:
(a) Unhappiness:
• The little fish was unhappy because he was so tiny compared to all the grand, big fish in the sea.

(b) Net Trapping:
• A giant fishing net swept through the sea, trapping the school of big fish.

(c) Escape and Lesson:
• As the heavy net was being hauled out of the water, the little fish wriggled right through the tiny mesh holes and swam safely back into deep, cool water. He realized being small saved his life!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Reason for unhappiness (small size) 1.0 M
Trapped in net event 1.0 M
Life saved by small size explained 1.0 M

Q13 Vocabulary • Word Families
Add '-ing' to the following action words correctly: (a) Sing (b) Dance (c) Run (d) Play.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Adding -ing:
• (a) Sing + ing = Singing
• (b) Dance + ing = Dancing (drop the 'e')
• (c) Run + ing = Running (double the 'n')
• (d) Play + ing = Playing.

Singing, Dancing, Running, Playing 2 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Embracing Individuality & Self-Acceptance

In a school play, Kabir was sad because he was shorter than his classmates and thought he would not get a good role.

Analytical Task: How can the teacher help Kabir understand that each person has unique strengths just like the little fish in the story?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The teacher can explain that being agile, light, or quick allows children to excel in unique roles such as a fast runner, dancer, or special story character.
2. Reminding students that outer appearance does not determine talent helps children build self-worth and genuine pride in who they are.
Rubric: 1.5M recognizing diverse talents + 1.5M building healthy self-esteem.
