Chapter 2: Bird Talk & Nina and the Baby Sparrows

Poem on How Birds View Humans & Heartwarming Story of Nina Protecting Sparrow Chicks | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'Bird Talk': Robin and Jay talking in the garden about how strange humans are (no feathers, no beetles to eat, sitting on wires).
  • Story 'Nina and the Baby Sparrows': Nina's worry about baby sparrows trapped in her room during a family wedding.
  • Compassionate solution: Leaving the window wide open so mother and father sparrow can feed their babies.
  • Grammar: Describing words (adjectives: plump, tiny, worried, happy).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem & Story
What do Robin and Jay think of people in 'Bird Talk'? Why was Nina crying and refusing to buy new wedding clothes in the market?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Robin & Jay's Thoughts: Robin and Jay find human beings funny and strange because humans don't grow feathers, cannot fly, don't eat beetles, and cannot sit on wires and twigs.
2. Nina's Worry: Nina was crying because there was a sparrow's nest on her bookshelf with two tiny, featherless baby sparrows. If the house was locked for her aunt's wedding in Delhi, the mama and papa sparrows would not be able to enter to feed their hungry babies.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of Robin & Jay's view of humans 1.5 M
Nina's concern for trapped baby sparrows and closed room 1.5 M
Q2 Problem Solving • Kindness to Animals
What clever plan did Nina's mother suggest that solved Nina's problem and protected the baby sparrows?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mother's Plan:
Mother suggested removing Nina's clothes and books from the room, locking the room door from the outside, but leaving the bedroom window wide open. This kept the house secure while allowing the parent sparrows to fly in and out freely to feed the chicks.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mention of locking door from outside 1.5 M
Leaving window open for parent sparrows 1.5 M
Q3 Grammar • Adjectives (Describing Words)
Pick the suitable describing word from brackets: (a) A ___ (fat / thin) baby sparrow was flying happily. (b) Nina was very ___ (sad / angry) about the birds. (c) A ___ (beautiful / ugly) red dress was bought for the wedding.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
• (a) Two plump / fat baby sparrows were flying happily.
• (b) Nina was very sad / worried about the birds.
• (c) A beautiful red dress was bought for the wedding.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct adjectives selected for all 3 blanks 3.0 M
Q4 Reading is Fun • Nina and the Baby Sparrows
In 'Nina and the Baby Sparrows':
(a) Why was everyone in Nina's family thrilled and getting ready to go to Delhi?
(b) Why was Nina sad and crying?
(c) What clever solution did Nina's mother find so that the baby sparrows would be safe?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Details:
(a) Family Joy:
• Nina's family was going to Delhi for her aunt's grand wedding.

(b) Nina's Worry:
• Nina was crying because there was a sparrow nest on her bedroom bookshelf with two tiny baby sparrows who were just growing their feathers. If the house was locked, mother and father sparrow could not come in to feed them.

(c) Mother's Solution:
• Mother said they would remove all things from the room, leave the bedroom window wide open, and lock the main door from outside so the mother and father sparrows could fly in and out freely.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct mention of aunt's wedding in Delhi 1.0 M
Sensitive empathy for feeding baby sparrows 1.0 M
Mother's open-window solution explained 1.0 M
Q5 Poem Comprehension • 'Bird Talk'
In the poem 'Bird Talk':
(a) Name the two birds who sit in the garden and talk to each other.
(b) What three things do people not have or do, which the birds find so funny?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
(a) The Two Birds:
• Robin and Jay.

(b) What Birds Find Funny About People:
• 1. People don't grow feathers at all.
• 2. People don't eat beetles or insects.
• 3. People cannot sit on wires and tree branches like birds do.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct names: Robin and Jay 1.0 M
Three funny traits of humans from birds' perspective 2.0 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Urban Bird Conservation

House sparrows were once common in every household balcony, but modern glass buildings and lack of grains have caused sparrow numbers to drop drastically.

Analytical Task: What two simple things can apartment residents do on their balconies to help house sparrows survive in cities?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Hang an earthen shallow water bowl (bird bath) and a small wooden birdhouse out of reach of predators.
2. Place small grains (millets, bajra, broken rice) on window sills daily for food.
Rubric: 1.5M providing water bowl and safe nesting box + 1.5M providing grains daily.
