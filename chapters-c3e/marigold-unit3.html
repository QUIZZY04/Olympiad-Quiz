Chapter 3: Little by Little & The Enormous Turnip

Acorn Growing into an Oak Tree & Classic Folk Tale of Collective Strength | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'Little by Little': An acorn hidden in the earth slowly drinking dew, sending roots down and shoots up.
  • Gradual growth: How small efforts every single day lead to immense strength and majestic branches.
  • Story 'The Enormous Turnip': Old man, old woman, boy, and girl pulling together to harvest the giant turnip.
  • The moral of collective effort: 'United we stand, divided we fall'.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem & Story
What does the acorn need to grow into a mighty oak tree? Who all helped in pulling up the enormous turnip?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Acorn's Growth: The acorn needs fertile soil to hide in, cool morning dew drops to drink, warm sunshine, and fresh air to grow roots downward and slender branches upward year by year.
2. Turnip Team: Four people pulled together: the old man, the old woman, the young boy, and the young girl.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Acorn's growth requirements (soil, dew, sunlight, time) 1.5 M
All 4 characters in the turnip pulling line identified 1.5 M
Q2 Moral & Character Insight
Could the old man pull out the turnip by himself? What lesson does this teach us about doing difficult tasks?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Moral Lesson:
• No, the old man could not pull out the giant turnip alone, nor could the old woman and boy alone.
• Lesson: When a task is too big for a single individual, coming together and working as a united team achieves victory easily. Cooperation and teamwork conquer difficulties.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct statement of old man's inability alone 1.0 M
Clear moral of unity, cooperation, and teamwork 2.0 M
Q3 Vocabulary • Plant Growth Terms
Give the meaning of: (a) Slender, (b) Mighty, (c) Enormous. Use 'enormous' in a sentence.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Word Meanings:
• (a) Slender: Thin, delicate, and graceful.
• (b) Mighty: Very strong, powerful, and grand.
• (c) Enormous: Extremely large in size or quantity.

Sentence: 'The elephant walked through the forest with an enormous branch in its trunk.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate definitions for all 3 words 1.5 M
Correct grammatical sentence with enormous 1.5 M
Q4 Reading is Fun • The Enormous Turnip
In 'The Enormous Turnip':
(a) What did the old man plant in his garden?
(b) Who all helped the old man to pull up the enormous turnip? List in order.
(c) Who finally helped to eat the delicious turnip?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Details:
(a) Planted:
• The old man planted turnip seeds.

(b) People who Pulled (in Order):
• 1. The old man
• 2. The old woman
• 3. The young boy
• 4. The young girl.

(c) Who Ate the Turnip:
• All four of them (the old man, the old woman, the boy, and the girl) cooked and ate the delicious enormous turnip together.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct identification of turnip seeds 1.0 M
Accurate order of the 4 characters pulling 1.0 M
Collaborative eating described 1.0 M
Q5 Poem Comprehension • 'Little by Little'
In the poem 'Little by Little':
(a) Which tiny seed is speaking in the poem?
(b) Where does its slender root grow, and where does its threadlike shoot spring?
(c) What does the tiny acorn grow up to be eventually?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
(a) The Seed:
• An acorn (the seed of an oak tree).

(b) Growth Direction:
• The slender root grows downward deep into the dark earth.
• The tiny threadlike shoot springs upward high into the air and sunlight.

(c) Full-Grown Form:
• It grows into the mighty oak tree, the forest's pride with broad branching limbs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct acorn seed identified 1.0 M
Downwards root and upwards shoot contrast 1.0 M
Mighty oak tree named 1.0 M
Q6 Vegetable Classification • Raw vs Cooked
Classify the following vegetables into 'Can Be Eaten Raw (as salad)' and 'Must Be Cooked Before Eating':
Carrot, Potato, Cucumber, Brinjal, Radish, Raw pumpkin.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classification:
• Can Be Eaten Raw (Salad): Carrot, Cucumber, Radish.
• Must Be Cooked: Potato, Brinjal, Raw pumpkin.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct raw salad vegetables identified 1.5 M
Correct cooked vegetables identified 1.5 M
Q7 Opposites • Antonyms of Size & Direction
Write the opposites of the following words from the chapter:
(a) Enormous $\times$ _____
(b) Pull $\times$ _____
(c) Upward $\times$ _____
(d) Tiny $\times$ _____
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Opposites:
• (a) Enormous $\times$ Tiny / Small
• (b) Pull $\times$ Push
• (c) Upward $\times$ Downward

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct opposites for all 4 words 4.0 M

Q13 Vocabulary • Word Families
Add '-ing' to the following action words correctly: (a) Sing (b) Dance (c) Run (d) Play.

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Adding -ing:

• (b) Dance + ing = Dancing (drop the 'e')

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Cleanliness Drive Teamwork

The playground was covered with heavy fallen tree branches after a severe storm. No single student could lift them.

Analytical Task: How can the students apply the principle of 'The Enormous Turnip' to clean their school playground safely?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Instead of trying to lift heavy branches individually, four to five students can lift each branch together under teacher supervision.
2. Working in coordinated teams makes heavy physical tasks light, safe, and quickly accomplished.
Rubric: 1.5M collaborative lifting concept + 1.5M safety and efficiency benefits.
