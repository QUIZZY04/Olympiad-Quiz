Chapter 6: Paper Boats

Rabindranath Tagore's Poem, Monsoon Puddles, Distant Lands & Innocent Dreams | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Appreciating Rabindranath Tagore's poetic masterpiece 'Paper Boats'.
  • Writing one's name and village on boats to send messages to distant lands.
  • Loading boats with Shiuli flowers blooming in the dawn.
  • Metaphor of white bulging clouds racing with the paper boats.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Poetry Analysis
In Rabindranath Tagore's poem 'Paper Boats': (a) What does the child write on the paper boats? (b) What flowers does the child load into the boats, and why?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poetic Analysis:
• (a) Writing on boats: The child writes their name and the name of their village in big black letters, hoping someone in a strange distant land will find them and know who sent them.
• (b) Flower cargo: The child loads the boats with Shiuli blossoms gathered from the garden at dawn, hoping these blooms of the night will be safely ferried to land in the dark.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct name and village writing explanation 1.5 M
Correct identification of Shiuli flowers and dawn harvest 1.5 M
Q2 Poetic Devices • Metaphor & Imagery
What does the child imagine about the clouds in the sky? How do the clouds relate to the paper boats?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Imagery Explanation:
The child looks up and sees little white clouds with bulging sails floating in the blue sky. The child imagines that an unseen playmate in the sky sends these cloud-ships down through the air to race with their own paper boats floating down the stream.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate description of cloud playmates and air racing 2.0 M
Q3 Hands-on Activity • Origami & Recycling
What kind of paper is best suited for making paper boats, and what should students do with wet paper boats after playing in rainwater puddles?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Activity & Eco-Awareness:
1. Suitable Paper: Used drawing sheets or scrap newspaper that hold folds firmly.
2. Environmental Responsibility: Wet paper boats should never be left to clog drains or litter roads. Once play is over, the soggy paper should be collected and deposited in a recycling or compost bin.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identifies scrap/recycled paper 1.0 M
Emphasizes eco-cleanup and preventing clogged drains 1.0 M
Q4 Craft & Science • Paper Boats & Floating
In 'Paper Boats':
(a) What type of paper is best for making paper boats that do not sink quickly: thin tissue paper or thick glossy/waxed paper? Why?
(b) Write two things children write or draw on their paper boats before floating them in rain puddles.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Craft & Physics:
(a) Best Paper:
• Thick waxed or waterproof paper is best because it does not absorb water quickly. Thin tissue paper absorbs water immediately, turns soggy, and sinks.

(b) Writing on Paper Boats:
• Children write their own name and address/school, or draw a small flag or flower, hoping it sails safely to distant lands.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct paper choice with scientific reasoning (water absorption) 1.5 M
Two authentic details children write/draw on boats 1.5 M
Q5 Science Exploration • Sink or Float?
Classify the following items into 'Will Float on Water' and 'Will Sink in Water':
Dry leaf, Iron nail, Plastic bottle cap, Pebble, Wooden pencil, Glass marble.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Float or Sink Classification:
• Will Float on Water:
1. Dry leaf (Light, low density)
2. Plastic bottle cap
3. Wooden pencil (Wood floats)

• Will Sink in Water:
1. Iron nail (Heavy metal, high density)
2. Pebble (Dense stone)
3. Glass marble.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct floating items classified (3 items) 1.5 M
Correct sinking items classified (3 items) 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Cultural Heritage of Rabindranath Tagore

Rabindranath Tagore was a Nobel Prize-winning poet who wrote India's National Anthem, 'Jana Gana Mana', and founded Visva-Bharati University in Santiniketan.

Analytical Task: How does Tagore's poetry encourage young children to connect with nature, simple toys, and universal human brotherhood?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Tagore's poems celebrate simple natural wonders—rain streams, paper toys, flowers, and open skies—showing that immense happiness comes from imagination rather than expensive gadgets.
2. Sending paper boats to unknown children in faraway lands embodies his belief in universal love and peaceful connection among all human beings.
Rubric: 1.5M connection with natural imagination + 1.5M theme of universal brotherhood.
