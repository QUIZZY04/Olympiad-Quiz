Chapter 9: Madhu's Wish

Wishes, Magic Paintbrush, Helping Others, Selflessness & Moral Values | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • The classic moral theme: selfish desires vs using gifts for the public good.
  • Madhu's wish for a magic paintbrush that paints objects into reality.
  • Painting food and plows for poor villagers rather than gold for greedy kings.
  • Using modal verbs: 'could', 'would', 'should' in wishing sentences.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Story Comprehension
What special power did Madhu's paintbrush have? What did Madhu choose to paint with it?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Special Power: Whatever Madhu painted with his magic paintbrush came alive into real existence.
2. Madhu's Choice: Instead of painting gold or jewels for himself, Madhu painted water buffaloes to plow dry fields, bags of grain for hungry families, and warm blankets for shivering elders.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct explanation of the paintbrush's magical power 1.5 M
Mention of buffaloes, grain, and blankets for villagers 1.5 M
Q2 Moral Reasoning • Character Analysis
When a greedy moneylender demanded that Madhu paint a mountain of gold, what did Madhu do? What moral lesson does this teach?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Moral Analysis:
1. Madhu's Action: Madhu refused to help the greedy man become richer. When forced, he painted a deep ocean with a golden island far away and painted a boat that sailed away, stranding the greedy man without causing harm.
2. Moral Lesson: Greed leads to downfall; talents should never be surrendered to injustice or selfish exploitation.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of Madhu's clever resistance to greed 1.5 M
Clear formulation of the moral lesson 1.5 M
Q3 Grammar • Sentence Formulation
Complete the conditional wishing sentences: (a) If I had a magic paintbrush, I would paint ___. (b) If I could fly like a bird, I would visit ___.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Completions:
• (a) If I had a magic paintbrush, I would paint fresh green forests and clean rivers for endangered animals.
• (b) If I could fly like a bird, I would visit the snowy peaks of the Himalayan mountains.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Grammatically correct conditional sentence for (a) 1.0 M
Grammatically correct conditional sentence for (b) 1.0 M
Q4 Story Comprehension • Madhu's Wish
In the story 'Madhu's Wish':
(a) What magical creature appeared in Madhu's dream, and what gift was she offered?
(b) What were the three wishes Madhu thought about?
(c) What did Madhu finally wish for that brought real happiness?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Breakdown:
(a) Magical Creature:
• A glowing fairy with shimmering silver wings appeared and offered Madhu three magical wishes.

(b) Three Wishes Considered:
• 1. An endless supply of chocolates and ice cream.
• 2. A mountain of beautiful toys and dollhouses.
• 3. Wings to fly anywhere in the sky.

(c) Final Wise Wish:
• Madhu realized that toys get old and sweets finish quickly, so she wished for good health, wisdom, and happiness for her family and friends.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct fairy character identified 1.0 M
Three imaginative wishes listed 1.0 M
Selfless final wish and moral explained 1.0 M
Q5 Creative Writing • If I had Three Wishes
If a magical fairy granted you three wishes today, what would you ask for and why? Write three bullet points.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model 3-Wish Reflection:
• Wish 1: A magical tree that grows fresh fruits for poor and hungry children all over the city.
• Wish 2: A pair of magical goggles that helps me see and clean up all plastic litter on beaches and forests.
• Wish 3: That my grandparents never fall sick and live happily for 100 years.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three original, empathetic, and imaginative wishes written 3.0 M
Q6 Phonics • Long Vowel Sounds
Sort the following words into 'Long A sound (as in Cake)' and 'Short A sound (as in Cat)':
Bake, Hat, Lake, Bat, Name, Bag.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Phonics Sorting:
• Long A sound (saying the letter name 'ay'): Bake, Lake, Name.
• Short A sound ('ah'): Hat, Bat, Bag.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct classification of long 'a' words 1.5 M
Correct classification of short 'a' words 1.5 M
Q7 Synonyms • Similar Meaning Words
Match each word from the story with its synonym (same meaning):
• Wish: [Joyful, Desire, Gleaming, Huge]
• Shimmering: [Joyful, Desire, Gleaming, Huge]
• Cheerful: [Joyful, Desire, Gleaming, Huge]
• Gigantic: [Joyful, Desire, Gleaming, Huge]
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Synonyms Matching:
• Wish $\rightarrow$ Desire
• Shimmering $\rightarrow$ Gleaming
• Cheerful $\rightarrow$ Joyful
• Gigantic $\rightarrow$ Huge
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct matching for all 4 synonym pairs 4.0 M
Q8 Reading is Fun • Direct Comprehension
In 'Madhu's Wish', describe what key lesson or character trait is highlighted when the central character faces a challenge. How does this connect to real-life friendship or cooperation?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Textual Comprehension & Analysis:
1. In the story/poem Madhu's Wish, the central character demonstrates perseverance, kindness, and teamwork when confronting difficulties.
2. When individuals cooperate rather than act selfishly, problems are resolved much faster and everyone benefits.
3. Real-Life Connection: In our daily lives, sharing tasks with classmates or family members builds trust and mutual respect, just as seen in this lesson.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of central trait (cooperation/kindness) 1.5 M
Valid real-world connection to friendship/teamwork 1.5 M
Q9 Word Building • Vocabulary & Phonics
From the lesson 'Madhu's Wish', find:
(a) Two rhyming words or sound-alike pairs.
(b) Write the opposite (antonym) of: Kind, Fast, Heavy.
(c) Form a meaningful sentence using one of the new vocabulary words from this chapter.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Vocabulary Solutions:
• (a) Rhyming pairs: Day - Play, Round - Sound.
• (b) Opposites:
- Kind → Cruel / Unkind
- Fast → Slow
- Heavy → Light.
• (c) Sample Sentence: The children showed great kindness by helping the injured bird.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct rhyming pairs 1.0 M
Accurate opposites for all 3 words 1.0 M
Grammatically correct sentence 1.0 M
Q10 Talk Time • Oral Expression & Personal Reflection
Have you ever had an experience similar to the events described in 'Madhu's Wish'? Share what you did, who was with you, and how you felt afterward.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Personal Reflection:
• Experience: Yes, during our school sports day, our relay team was trailing behind. Just like the theme in Madhu's Wish, we encouraged each other and stayed calm.
• Action: In the final lap, our teammate ran with full determination, and we secured second place.
• Feeling: I felt proud and joyful because supporting each other brought us closer together.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear narration of personal experience 1.5 M
Expression of feelings and thematic alignment 1.5 M
Q11 Grammar & Language Structure • Sentence Mechanics
Punctuate and rewrite the following sentences correctly using capital letters, commas, and full stops:
(i) rohan priya and amit went to the park on sunday
(ii) what a beautiful flower said the little girl
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Punctuation & Capitalisation:
• (i) Rohan, Priya, and Amit went to the park on Sunday. (Capitalized names, comma between items, capital S for Sunday, full stop at end).
• (ii) "What a beautiful flower!" said the little girl. (Capital W, quotation marks for speech, exclamation mark).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sentence (i) correctly punctuated 1.5 M
Sentence (ii) correctly punctuated 1.5 M

It taught me that small acts of courage can make a big difference. This chapter inspires me to be a thoughtful and supportive friend every day.

Relevant content reflecting chapter theme 1.5 M
Coherent 4-sentence structure and correct grammar 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Real-Life Magic of Volunteering

During winter, a group of schoolchildren collected old woollen sweaters, cleaned them, and distributed them to homeless pavement dwellers.

Analytical Task: How does this real-life act carry the exact same 'magic' as Madhu's paintbrush in the folk tale?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. In real life, humans do not have magical paintbrushes, but our compassion, hands, and collective effort create real miracles.
2. By giving warm clothes to shivering people, the students transformed cold despair into warmth and safety, proving that empathy is the truest magic in the world.
Rubric: 1.5M connecting metaphorical magic with human kindness + 1.5M real-world social impact.
