Chapter 1: Fun with Friends

Playground Games, Sharing, Turn-Taking, New Friendships & Outdoor Sports | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • The joy of making new friends and playing traditional playground games.
  • Learning cooperation, empathy, and fair play in group games.
  • Vocabulary: playground equipment (swings, slide, see-saw) and action verbs.
  • Simple present tense sentences describing everyday play habits.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Comprehension
What games do the children play in the park in 'Fun with Friends'? Why is taking turns important when playing on playground swings?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. In the park, the children play games like hide-and-seek, tag, sliding down the slide, and swinging on swings.
2. Importance of taking turns: Taking turns ensures that every child gets a fair chance to play without arguments. It shows kindness, patience, and keeps the playground safe and friendly for everyone.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct mention of games played in the park 1.5 M
Moral explanation of fairness and safety in taking turns 1.5 M

Q4 Picture Reading • Animals & Birds
Look at the picture in Chapter 1 showing children playing near a pond:
(a) How many ducklings and chicks can you spot in the pond picture?
(b) Match each animal with its young one: Dog, Cat, Duck, Hen.
(c) What are the children doing to show friendship with animals?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Picture Reading & Vocabulary:
(a) Animal Count:
• In the picture, there are 3 yellow ducklings swimming behind mother duck and 4 little chicks pecking near the grass.

(b) Matching Animals with Young Ones:
• Dog $\rightarrow$ Puppy
• Cat $\rightarrow$ Kitten
• Duck $\rightarrow$ Duckling
• Hen $\rightarrow$ Chick.

(c) Showing Friendship:
• The children are feeding grain gently, smiling, and petting the animals without frightening or chasing them.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct picture identification of ducklings and chicks 1.0 M
Accurate matching of all 4 animal-offspring pairs 1.0 M
Thoughtful description of gentle friendship with animals 1.0 M
Q5 Poem Comprehension • 'Colours'
In the poem 'Colours':
(a) What color is the sky on a bright sunny day, and what color are the clouds when it rains?
(b) Name three green things mentioned or seen in nature.
(c) Write two pairs of rhyming words from the poem.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Analysis:
(a) Colors of Sky and Clouds:
• On a sunny day, the sky is bright blue.
• When it rains, the clouds are dark grey (or black).

(b) Three Green Things:
• 1. Grass
• 2. Leaves on trees
• 3. Parrots.

(c) Rhyming Pairs:
• Bright — White
• Seen — Green.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct colors of sky (blue) and rain clouds (grey/black) 1.0 M
Three valid natural green items listed 1.0 M
Two correct rhyming pairs identified 1.0 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Welcoming a New Student

On Monday, a new girl named Zoya joined Class 3. She sat alone during lunchtime looking shy and nervous.

Analytical Task: What two friendly actions can Rohit and his group take to help Zoya feel welcomed and happy in school?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Rohit can walk over with a warm smile and invite Zoya to sit with their group to share snacks.
2. They can show her around the classroom and playground and invite her to join their team in a friendly game of hide-and-seek.
Rubric: 1.5M first empathetic welcoming action + 1.5M second inclusive play action.
