Chapter 2: Badal and Moti

Loyalty, Care for Animals, Rural Life, Bravery & Rescuing a Pet Dog | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • The bond of affection between a village boy, Badal, and his faithful pet dog, Moti.
  • Animal intelligence, loyalty, and protective instincts toward human family members.
  • Sequencing story events: daily walks, warning bark, river crossing, and rescue.
  • Descriptive adjectives: brave, faithful, swift, muddy, rushing.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Story Comprehension
Who was Moti, and how did he show his loyalty when Badal slipped near the muddy stream?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Who was Moti: Moti was Badal's beloved pet dog who accompanied him everywhere across the village fields.
2. Act of Loyalty: When Badal slipped on the muddy bank and fell near the swollen stream, Moti immediately barked loudly to alert village elders and firmly gripped Badal's shirt collar with his teeth to prevent him from being swept into deeper water until help arrived.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate identification of Moti as Badal's dog 1.0 M
Detailed description of barking and holding collar to rescue 2.0 M
Q2 Vocabulary • Synonyms & Antonyms
Give the antonym (opposite word) for: (a) Deep, (b) Faithful, (c) Brave. Use 'faithful' in a meaningful sentence.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Antonyms:
• (a) Deep × Shallow
• (b) Faithful × Unfaithful / Disloyal
• (c) Brave × Cowardly / Timid.

Sentence: 'Moti was a faithful companion who protected the family every night.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct antonyms for all 3 words 1.5 M
Meaningful, grammatically correct sentence using faithful 1.5 M
Q3 Speaking & Listening • Pet Care
What responsibilities do pet owners have toward their pets? Mention three important duties.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Pet Care Duties:
1. Nutritious Food & Fresh Water: Providing clean drinking water and healthy meals daily.
2. Shelter & Cleanliness: Giving pets a warm, dry sleeping space and regular baths.
3. Healthcare & Exercise: Taking them to veterinary doctors for vaccinations and taking them for regular walks.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mention of food and clean water 1.0 M
Mention of shelter and healthcare/veterinary visits 2.0 M
Q4 Grammar & Verbs • Past Tense with '-ed'
In the story 'Badal and Moti', action words change to show things that happened in the past by adding '-ed'.
Change the following present verbs to past tense:
(a) Bark $\rightarrow$ _____
(b) Jump $\rightarrow$ _____
(c) Pull $\rightarrow$ _____
(d) Play $\rightarrow$ _____
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Past Tense Forms:
• (a) Bark $\rightarrow$ Barked (e.g., Moti barked loudly to alert the villagers).
• (b) Jump $\rightarrow$ Jumped (e.g., Badal jumped across the stream).
• (c) Pull $\rightarrow$ Pulled (e.g., The boy pulled the stick).
• (d) Play $\rightarrow$ Played (e.g., Badal and Moti played in the mud).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct past tense for (a) and (b) 2.0 M
Correct past tense for (c) and (d) 2.0 M
Q5 True or False • Story Retention
State whether each statement from 'Badal and Moti' is True ('T') or False ('F'):
(a) Moti was a naughty little monkey.
(b) Badal slipped and fell into deep mud near the pond.
(c) Moti ran away and did not help Badal.
(d) Badal's mother thanked Moti and gave him bread and milk.
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
True/False Evaluation:
• (a) False [F]: Moti was a faithful, brave pet dog, not a monkey.
• (b) True [T]: Badal slipped on the muddy bank and fell in.
• (c) False [F]: Moti barked loudly, caught Badal's shirt collar, and helped pull him out to safety.
• (d) True [T]: Badal's mother hugged Moti with tears of joy and fed him lovingly.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct evaluations for (a) and (b) 2.0 M
Correct evaluations for (c) and (d) 2.0 M
Q6 Speaking & Dialogue • Pet Care
Imagine your pet puppy or kitten is lost in the colony.
(a) What three things would you do to find it?
(b) How should we treat stray animals in our neighborhood?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Guide:
(a) Finding a Lost Pet:
• 1. Call out its name loudly and check familiar spots like parks, porches, and under cars.
• 2. Ask neighborhood friends, guards, and local shopkeepers if they saw it passing by.
• 3. Put up posters with its photo and a parent's phone number.

(b) Treating Stray Animals:
• We should treat stray animals with kindness, never throw stones at them, and provide clean water in earthen bowls, especially during hot summers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three sensible, proactive search actions 1.5 M
Empathetic, responsible approach to neighborhood animals 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Animal Empathy Case Study

While walking home from school, Rohan spotted a stray puppy shivering under a tree on a rainy afternoon.

Analytical Task: What safe, compassionate steps should Rohan take with the help of his parents to care for the puppy?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Rohan should gently shelter the puppy from the rain using a dry cloth or cardboard box without frightening it.
2. He should inform his parents to provide a warm bowl of milk or puppy food.
3. Together with his parents, he should contact an animal welfare shelter or vet to ensure the puppy receives healthcare and vaccination.
Rubric: 1.5M immediate protective care + 1.5M parental/veterinary consultation.
