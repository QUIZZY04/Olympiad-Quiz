Chapter 7: Puppy and I & Little Tiger, Big Tiger

A.A. Milne's Poem on Choosing a Playful Puppy & Story of a Tiger Cub Growing in the Wild | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'Puppy and I': Meeting a Man, Horse, and Puppy; choosing to go with the puppy who is going to roll and play.
  • Story 'Little Tiger, Big Tiger': Mother tigress and her cub living near a river in the shady jungle.
  • Hunting in the wild: Hunting for deer and wild boar; cub chasing a little leaping frog.
  • The roar of adulthood: The cub growing big and terrifying other jungle animals with his deep roar.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem & Story
Why did the child in 'Puppy and I' decide to go with the puppy instead of the man or horse? What happened when the little tiger cub chased a frog?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Choosing the Puppy: The man was going to the village to buy bread, and the horse was going for hay. But the puppy was going up in the hills to roll and play, which matched the child's playful spirit.
2. Chasing the Frog: The tiger cub chased a bouncing green frog far away from his mother in the dark jungle. When he caught the frog, he looked up and saw another giant tiger staring at him! Luckily, his mother tigress roared loudly, driving the stranger away.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct explanation of choosing the puppy for play 1.5 M
Details of chasing frog and encountering stranger tiger 1.5 M
Q2 Animal Homes & Sounds
Give the name of the home and young one for: (1) Tiger, (2) Horse, (3) Dog. Options: (Lair/Den, Stable, Kennel; Cub, Foal, Puppy).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Animal Facts:
• 1. Tiger: Home = Lair / Den | Young One = Cub
• 2. Horse: Home = Stable | Young One = Foal / Colt
• 3. Dog: Home = Kennel | Young One = Puppy.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct homes and young ones for all 3 animals (1M each) 3.0 M
Q3 Wildlife Conservation Awareness
The tiger is India's National Animal. Why are tigers endangered, and what is 'Project Tiger'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Tiger Conservation:
1. Why Endangered: Tigers lost their forest habitats due to deforestation and were illegally hunted by poachers for their skins and bones.
2. Project Tiger: Launched by the Government of India in 1973, Project Tiger created protected National Parks and Tiger Reserves (like Jim Corbett and Ranthambore) to save tigers and restore healthy forest ecosystems.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Reasons for endangerment (poaching and habitat loss) 1.5 M
Explanation of Project Tiger and protected reserves 1.5 M
Q4 Reading is Fun • Little Tiger, Big Tiger
In 'Little Tiger, Big Tiger':
(a) Where did mother tiger and her little tiger cub live?
(b) What did mother tiger hunt at night?
(c) What happened when the little tiger cub chased a frog deep into the jungle?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Story Details:
(a) Habitat:
• They lived in a shady jungle near a quiet river.

(b) Night Prey:
• She hunted deer and wild pigs.

(c) Chasing the Frog:
• The cub chased a tiny hopping frog far from his mother. When he caught it, he turned around and saw a huge, terrifying full-grown tiger staring at him! He was terrified until mother tiger arrived and let out a mighty roar that chased the stranger away.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Jungle habitat near river 1.0 M
Hunting deer and pigs 1.0 M
Encounter with big tiger and mother's protective roar 1.0 M
Q5 Poem Comprehension • 'Puppy and I'
In the poem 'Puppy and I':
(a) Whom did the child meet first, and where was he going?
(b) Whom did the child meet second?
(c) Why did the child choose to go with the Puppy instead of the Man or the Horse?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Poem Analysis:
(a) First Meeting:
• The child met a Man going down to the village to buy some bread.

(b) Second Meeting:
• The child met a Horse going down to the village to get some hay.

(c) Choosing the Puppy:
• The Man and Horse were going to work, but the puppy was going up into the hills 'to roll and play'. The child wanted to play, so he happily joined the puppy.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Man buying bread 1.0 M
Horse getting hay 1.0 M
Puppy going to roll and play 1.0 M

Q8 Reading is Fun • Direct Comprehension
In 'Puppy and I & Little Tiger, Big Tiger', describe what key lesson or character trait is highlighted when the central character faces a challenge. How does this connect to real-life friendship or cooperation?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Textual Comprehension & Analysis:
1. In the story/poem Puppy and I & Little Tiger, Big Tiger, the central character demonstrates perseverance, kindness, and teamwork when confronting difficulties.
2. When individuals cooperate rather than act selfishly, problems are resolved much faster and everyone benefits.
3. Real-Life Connection: In our daily lives, sharing tasks with classmates or family members builds trust and mutual respect, just as seen in this lesson.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of central trait (cooperation/kindness) 1.5 M
Valid real-world connection to friendship/teamwork 1.5 M

Q10 Talk Time • Oral Expression & Personal Reflection
Have you ever had an experience similar to the events described in 'Puppy and I & Little Tiger, Big Tiger'? Share what you did, who was with you, and how you felt afterward.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Personal Reflection:
• Experience: Yes, during our school sports day, our relay team was trailing behind. Just like the theme in Puppy and I & Little Tiger, Big Tiger, we encouraged each other and stayed calm.
• Action: In the final lap, our teammate ran with full determination, and we secured second place.
• Feeling: I felt proud and joyful because supporting each other brought us closer together.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear narration of personal experience 1.5 M
Expression of feelings and thematic alignment 1.5 M
Q11 Grammar & Language Structure • Sentence Mechanics
Punctuate and rewrite the following sentences correctly using capital letters, commas, and full stops:
(i) rohan priya and amit went to the park on sunday
(ii) what a beautiful flower said the little girl
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Punctuation & Capitalisation:
• (i) Rohan, Priya, and Amit went to the park on Sunday. (Capitalized names, comma between items, capital S for Sunday, full stop at end).
• (ii) "What a beautiful flower!" said the little girl. (Capital W, quotation marks for speech, exclamation mark).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sentence (i) correctly punctuated 1.5 M
Sentence (ii) correctly punctuated 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Forest Food Chain Balance

Tigers are apex predators at the top of the forest food chain. If tigers disappear from a forest, the deer population multiplies out of control.

Analytical Task: How does the presence of tigers maintain the ecological balance of plants and water in a jungle?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. By controlling the population of herbivores like deer and wild boar, tigers prevent overgrazing of forest grass and young tree saplings.
2. This keeps the forest canopy thick, protects topsoil from erosion, and ensures natural water springs continue flowing.
Rubric: 1.5M herbivore population control + 1.5M protecting vegetation and soil balance.
