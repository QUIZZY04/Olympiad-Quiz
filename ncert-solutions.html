Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about our free NCERT textbook solutions, CBSE marking schemes, and exam preparation.

Which classes and subjects are covered in OlympiadQuiz NCERT Solutions?

OlympiadQuiz offers comprehensive, 100% textbook question coverage for Classes 7, 8, 9, and 10 across all core CBSE subjects:

  • Class 7: Mathematics, Science (Curiosity), Social Science (Our Pasts II, Our Environment, Social & Political Life II), English (Honeycomb & An Alien Hand), and Hindi (Vasant, Bal Mahabharat Katha, Durva).
  • Class 8: Mathematics, Science, Social Science (Our Pasts III, Resources & Development, Social & Political Life III), English (Honeydew & It So Happened), Hindi (Vasant & Bharat Ki Khoj), and Sanskrit (Ruchira Bhag-3).
  • Class 9: Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Social Science (4 Books), English (Beehive & Moments), and Hindi (Kshitij, Kritika, Sparsh, Sanchayan).
  • Class 10: Mathematics (Standard & Basic), Science (All 13 Chapters), Social Science (All 4 Books), and English (Both Courses: Language & Literature [Code 184 - 28 Units] and Communicative [Code 101 - 13 Units]).
Are these solutions updated for the latest CBSE 2026-27 Rationalized Curriculum?

Yes, absolutely. Every solution is 100% updated in strict adherence to the rationalized NCERT textbooks and syllabus notifications issued by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) for the 2026-27 academic session.

All removed chapters, deleted exercises, and renumbered exercises have been factored in, guaranteeing that students study only what will actually appear in school unit tests, term exams, and Board examinations.

How do these solutions follow the official CBSE Board Marking Scheme?

Our solutions are designed using the exact step-marking rubrics used by official CBSE evaluators:

  • Given Data & Goal: Clearly established at the beginning of calculations and geometric proofs.
  • Formulae & Laws: Highlighted explicitly with mathematical justifications.
  • Step Marks (1M, 2M, 3M, 5M): Clear step-by-step mark allocations so students learn how to structure answers to secure full marks.
  • Final Conclusion & Units: Boxed concluding statements with correct SI units.
Are Competency-Based Questions (CBQs), Case Studies, and HOTS included?

Yes! In accordance with NEP 2020 and CBSE's revised exam blueprint which requires 50% competency-focused assessment, each chapter includes:

  • Case-Based / Source-Based Questions: Contextual real-life passages with sub-questions.
  • Assertion & Reasoning Questions: In-depth conceptual analysis with clear rationale.
  • Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS): Challenging multi-concept problems that test deep analytical understanding.
Is there any subscription fee, paywall, or login required to access the solutions?

No. All NCERT solutions on OlympiadQuiz are 100% free forever. There are no paywalls, no trial limits, no credit card requirements, and no mandatory account sign-up. Students, parents, and educators can access all solutions freely anytime.

How does mastering NCERT solutions help in Olympiad exams like IMO, NSO, and IEO?

NCERT textbooks form the conceptual bedrock of competitive school exams like the International Mathematics Olympiad (IMO), National Science Olympiad (NSO), and NSTSE. Olympiad questions rarely ask raw definitions; they test the application and logical extension of NCERT principles. Mastering our step-by-step solutions builds rock-solid foundational intuition, making advanced Level-1 and Level-2 Olympiad problems far easier to crack.
