Under the National Education Policy 2020 and National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE 2023), NCERT introduced brand-new textbooks for Class 6: Ganita Prakash (Mathematics), Curiosity (Science), Exploring Society: India and Beyond (Social Science), Poorvi (English), Malhar (Hindi), and Deepakam (Sanskrit). OlympiadQuiz provides complete solutions for all these new books as well as classic track references.
