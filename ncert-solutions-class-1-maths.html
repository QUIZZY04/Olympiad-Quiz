Q1 Look at the picture in the book: The furry cat is sitting in a cardboard basket. Where is the cat — inside the basket or outside? Textbook Exercise • Inside & Outside 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. The cat's entire body is enclosed within the four walls of the basket.
2. Therefore, the cat is inside the basket.
3. If the cat hops out onto the carpet, it will be outside the basket.

Sugarcane Harvesting Case Study

Real-World Context: Farmer Sham Lal ties sugarcane stalks into bundles of 10 stalks each. He has 6 full bundles and 4 loose stalks on his cart.

Analytical Task: How many sugarcane stalks does Sham Lal have in total? How many bundles will he have if he cuts 6 more stalks?

1. 6 bundles of 10 = 60. Loose stalks = 4. Total = 60 + 4 = 64 sugarcane stalks.
2. With 6 more stalks, loose stalks = 4 + 6 = 10 (which makes 1 new bundle).
3. Total bundles = 6 + 1 = 7 full bundles of 10 (70 stalks).

Pearl Necklace Project Case Study
