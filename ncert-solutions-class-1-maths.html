🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study (CBQ)

Tower Building Challenge

Real-World Context: During art class, Rohan tries to build a high tower using 5 round tennis balls, but the balls roll away and the tower falls down. Meena uses 5 matchboxes and successfully builds a tall tower.

Analytical Task: Why did Rohan's tower collapse while Meena's tower stood firmly? Which shape is best for stacking?

1. Rohan used round balls with curved surfaces which roll easily and have no flat faces to balance on.
2. Meena used matchboxes with flat faces that slide and stack stably without rolling. Flat-faced objects are best for stacking.

The School Garden Harvest Festival

Real-World Context: Grade 1 children gathered 24 radishes, 18 carrots, and 10 turnip roots from the class organic patch.

Analytical Task: Write 24 in tens and ones. Which vegetable was harvested the most, and how many carrots and radishes were there altogether?

1. 24 = 2 Tens and 4 Ones.
2. Radishes (24) were harvested the most.
3. Total carrots and radishes = 18 + 24 = 42 vegetables.
