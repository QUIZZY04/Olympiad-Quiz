Chapter 1: My Wonderful Body & Five Senses

Eyes, Ears, Nose, Tongue, Skin and Sense Functions | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • External Body Parts (Head, Limbs, Trunk)
  • Five Sense Organs and Their Functions
  • Caring for Sense Organs
  • Good Touch and Personal Safety

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Think and Tell
What are sense organs? Name the five sense organs present in our body.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sense organs are special organs that help us perceive, observe, and understand the world around us. The five sense organs are: 1. Eyes (Sight) 2. Ears (Hearing) 3. Nose (Smell) 4. Tongue (Taste) 5. Skin (Touch).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Definition of sense organs 1
Listing all 5 sense organs correctly 2
Q2 Find Out and Write
Which sense organ covers our entire body, and what is its main function?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The skin covers our entire body. Its main function is the sense of touch; it helps us feel hot, cold, soft, hard, rough, smooth, and pain.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Skin covers entire body 1
Sense of touch (hot, cold, texture) 1
Q3 Taste Buds & Tongue
What four primary tastes can our tongue identify?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Our tongue has taste buds that help us recognize: 1. Sweet (e.g., sugar, mango) 2. Salty (e.g., potato chips, salt) 3. Sour (e.g., lemon, raw mango) 4. Bitter (e.g., bitter gourd, neem).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Listing 4 tastes: sweet, salty, sour, bitter 2
Q4 Eye Care & Hygiene
Give two healthy habits to take proper care of our eyes.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Two healthy habits for eye care: 1. Wash eyes gently with clean cold water daily. 2. Do not watch television, tablet, or smartphone screens for too long or in a dark room.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Washing with clean water 1
Limiting screen time / proper lighting 1
Q5 Ear Care
Why should we never put sharp or pointed objects inside our ears?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
We should never insert hairpin, pencil, or sharp sticks into our ears because they can puncture or damage the delicate eardrum and cause permanent hearing loss.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Delicate eardrum damage 1
Risk of hearing loss 1
Q6 Body Movement
Which parts of our body help us in walking, running, and jumping?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Our legs, knees, ankles, feet, and toes (lower limbs) help us walk, run, and jump.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Legs, knees, feet 2
Q7 Hands and Fingers
Mention three daily activities that we do using our hands and fingers.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Activities done with hands and fingers: 1. Writing with a pencil 2. Holding a spoon to eat food 3. Buttoning our shirt or tying shoelaces.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three valid daily activities 3
Q8 Match the Following
Match the sense organ with its sensation: (a) Nose -> (i) Ringing bell (b) Eyes -> (ii) Hot tea cup (c) Skin -> (iii) Beautiful rainbow (d) Ears -> (iv) Fragrant perfume
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Nose -> (iv) Fragrant perfume (b) Eyes -> (iii) Beautiful rainbow (c) Skin -> (ii) Hot tea cup (d) Ears -> (i) Ringing bell.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 correct pairings 2
Q9 True or False
State True or False: (a) We smell with our ears. (b) Our tongue helps us speak clearly as well as taste food. (c) Skin protects the inner parts of our body.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) False (We smell with our nose). (b) True (Tongue helps in speech and taste). (c) True (Skin serves as a protective barrier).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
1 mark per correct answer with reason 3
Q10 Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with suitable words: (a) We have ________ (two / five) sense organs. (b) Our ________ (eyebrows / nails) protect our eyes from sweat and dust. (c) When we catch a cold, our sense of ________ (hearing / smell) becomes weak.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) five (b) eyebrows (c) smell.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
1 mark for each correct blank 3
Q11 Personal Safety (Life Skill)
What should you do if someone gives you an uncomfortable or bad touch?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
If someone gives an uncomfortable or bad touch, you should: 1. Say a firm and loud 'NO!' 2. Run away from that person immediately. 3. Tell your parents or trusted teacher right away.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Say NO and move away 1
Inform parents/teacher immediately 1
Q12 Give Two Examples
Give two examples each of: (a) Things that have a pleasant smell (b) Things that have an unpleasant (bad) smell.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Pleasant smell: Rose flower, sandalwood incense, baking cookies. (b) Unpleasant smell: Garbage heap, open sewage drain, rotten eggs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two pleasant smell examples 1
Two bad smell examples 1
Q13 Special Senses
How do visually challenged (blind) people read books?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Visually challenged people use their sense of touch to read 'Braille' script, which consists of raised dots felt with finger pads.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sense of touch 1
Braille script / raised dots 1
Q14 Scientific Reasoning
Why do we close our eyes tightly when a sudden bright light falls on them?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
It is an automatic reflex action to protect the delicate retina of our eyes from sudden intense light damage.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Reflex protection 1
Preventing damage to retina 1

[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) NECK (b) ELBOW (c) KNEE.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
1 mark per correct unscrambled word 3
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Observing with Senses in a Garden

Aman went for a morning walk in the botanical garden. He saw vibrant red roses, heard sweet cuckoo birds singing, smelled the sweet aroma of jasmine, tasted a sweet ripe berry, and felt the cool morning breeze on his cheeks.

Analytical Task: List each of the 5 senses Aman used in the garden along with the corresponding sense organ.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Aman used all five sense organs: 1. Sight (Eyes) - Seeing vibrant red roses 2. Hearing (Ears) - Listening to singing cuckoo birds 3. Smell (Nose) - Inhaling the jasmine aroma 4. Taste (Tongue) - Tasting the sweet ripe berry 5. Touch (Skin) - Feeling the cool morning breeze.
