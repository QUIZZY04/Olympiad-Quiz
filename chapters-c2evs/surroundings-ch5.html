Chapter 5: Means of Transport

Land (Bus, Train), Water (Boat, Ship), Air (Aeroplane, Helicopter) | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Class 2 Environmental Studies: Means of Transport.
  • Community relationships, safety, civic awareness, and eco-friendly habits.
  • CBSE step-by-step marking rubrics and competency evaluation.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (3 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textual Understanding • Community Life
What is the main topic of 'Means of Transport' and how does it help our society?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. In 'Means of Transport', we learn about Land (Bus, Train), Water (Boat, Ship), Air (Aeroplane, Helicopter).
2. It helps children understand how people live and work together cooperatively.
3. Value: Helping neighbors, respecting community workers, and keeping our town tidy.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Topic overview 1.5 M
Societal value 1.5 M
Q2 Identification Drill • Roles & Places
Name two important helpers or places mentioned in 'Means of Transport' and state their duties.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Example 1: Doctor — Treats sick people and gives medicines at the clinic.
• Example 2: Teacher — Teaches children good habits, reading, and mathematics at school.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two helpers/places with duties 2 M
Q3 Safety & Eco-Habits
Mention one safety rule or eco-friendly practice related to 'Means of Transport'.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Rule: Always use the zebra crossing to cross roads safely, and throw trash into dustbins instead of littering.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Valid safety or eco rule 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Means of Transport Civic Habit Task

Children apply community rules and safety guidelines to solve a practical scenario related to means of transport.

Analytical Task: Why is it important to learn and follow the community habits in 'Means of Transport'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Following good habits and rules related to land (bus, train), water (boat, ship), air (aeroplane, helicopter) keeps our neighborhood peaceful, safe, clean, and cooperative for everyone.
Rubric: 1.5 M for community value + 1.5 M for civic awareness
