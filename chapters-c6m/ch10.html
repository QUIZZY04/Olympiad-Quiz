Chapter 10: The Other Side of Zero

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Class 6) — Negative Numbers, Integers, Number Line Jumps, Additive Inverses & Integer Arithmetic | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Integer Concepts & Sign Rules
  • Integers ($mathbb{Z}$): The collection of positive natural numbers, zero, and negative natural numbers: $dots, -4, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, dots$
  • Ordering on the Number Line: Numbers to the right are always greater than numbers to the left. For any positive integer $a$, $-a < 0 < a$. As negative numbers move further left, their value decreases (e.g., $-10 < -2$).
  • Absolute Value: The numerical value of an integer regardless of its sign, denoted by $|x|$. $|-5| = 5$, $|5| = 5$.
  • Rules for Addition & Subtraction:
    • Same sign: Add absolute values and assign the common sign: $(+5) + (+3) = +8$; $(-5) + (-3) = -8$.
    • Different signs: Subtract smaller absolute value from larger and assign the sign of the larger absolute value: $(+7) + (-4) = +3$; $(-9) + (+2) = -7$.
    • Subtraction: To subtract an integer, add its additive inverse: $a - b = a + (-b)$.
NCERT Exercise 10.1: Integer Concepts & Number Line
Q1
Write integers with appropriate signs (+ or -) for the following real-life situations:
(a) An increase of 8 marks
(b) A loss of ₹ 450
(c) A submarine cruising 800 m below sea level
(d) Temperature of 15°C below 0°C
(e) Deposit of ₹ 2,000 in a bank account
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Increase of 8 marks: +8
(b) Loss of ₹ 450: -₹ 450 (or -450)
(c) 800 m below sea level: -800 m (or -800)
(d) 15°C below 0°C: -15°C (or -15)
(e) Deposit of ₹ 2,000: +₹ 2,000 (or +2000)

Q2
Represent the following integers on a number line:
(a) +5    (b) -8    (c) -1    (d) +4
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Number Line Representation:
1. Draw a horizontal straight line with arrows at both ends.
2. Mark a point in the center as 0 (reference origin).
3. Mark equal unit intervals to the right as positive integers (+1, +2, +3, +4, +5).
4. Mark equal unit intervals to the left as negative integers (-1, -2, -3, ..., -8).
5. Circle and label points: +5 (5 units right), -8 (8 units left), -1 (1 unit left), +4 (4 units right).

Q3
Compare the following pairs of integers using > or <:
(a) 0 and -8
(b) -5 and -11
(c) -100 and -101
(d) 7 and -7
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) 0 and -8: Zero is to the right of every negative integer $\implies \mathbf{0 > -8}$.
(b) -5 and -11: -5 is to the right of -11 on the number line $\implies \mathbf{-5 > -11}$.
(c) -100 and -101: -100 is closer to 0 than -101 $\implies \mathbf{-100 > -101}$.
(d) 7 and -7: Any positive integer is greater than any negative integer $\implies \mathbf{7 > -7}$.

Each correct inequality comparison0.5 Mark each (Total 2 Marks)
NCERT Exercise 10.2: Integer Addition & Subtraction
Q4
Using a number line, find the integer which is:
(a) 4 more than -1
(b) 5 less than 3
(c) 3 less than -2
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) 4 more than -1:
Start at -1 on the number line. Move 4 steps to the right (addition):
$-1 + 4 = \mathbf{+3}$.
(b) 5 less than 3:
Start at +3 on the number line. Move 5 steps to the left (subtraction):
$3 - 5 = \mathbf{-2}$.
(c) 3 less than -2:
Start at -2 on the number line. Move 3 steps to the left:
$-2 - 3 = \mathbf{-5}$.

1 Mark per correct number line step with answer3 Marks
Q5
Find the sum of:
(a) (-11) + (-12)
(b) (+37) + (-20)
(c) (-312) + 39 + 192
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) (-11) + (-12):
Both have same negative signs: $-(11 + 12) = \mathbf{-23}$.
(b) (+37) + (-20):
Different signs: Subtract absolute values: $37 - 20 = 17$.
Assign sign of larger absolute value: $\mathbf{+17}$.
(c) (-312) + 39 + 192:
Add positive integers first: $39 + 192 = 231$.
Now calculate $(-312) + 231 = -(312 - 231) = \mathbf{-81}$.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
1 Mark per sub-part with clear sign steps3 Marks
Q6
Find the value of:
(a) 35 - (20)
(b) (-15) - (-18)
(c) (-20) - (13)
(d) 23 - (-12)
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Rule: $a - b = a + (\text{additive inverse of } b)$.
(a) $35 - 20 = \mathbf{15}$.
(b) $(-15) - (-18) = -15 + 18 = \mathbf{+3}$.
(c) $(-20) - 13 = -20 + (-13) = \mathbf{-33}$.
(d) $23 - (-12) = 23 + 12 = \mathbf{35}$.

Each correct evaluation0.5 Mark each (Total 2 Marks)
Q7
Fill in the blanks with >, <, or = sign:
(a) (-3) + (-6) ______ (-3) - (-6)
(b) (-21) - (-10) ______ (-31) + (-11)
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a):
- LHS: $(-3) + (-6) = -9$
- RHS: $(-3) - (-6) = -3 + 6 = +3$
Since $-9 < +3$, the answer is <.
(b):
- LHS: $(-21) - (-10) = -21 + 10 = -11$
- RHS: $(-31) + (-11) = -42$
Since $-11 > -42$, the answer is >.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Part (a): LHS = -9, RHS = +3, sign <1 Mark
Part (b): LHS = -11, RHS = -42, sign >1 Mark
Q8
Write the opposite of each of the following statements using positive or negative integers:
(a) Increase in weight by 5 kg.
(b) 30 km north.
(c) A deposit of ₹2,000.
(d) 100 m below sea level.
(e) Temperature of 8°C below freezing point (0°C).
[2.5 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
• (a) Increase of 5 kg: Opposite is Decrease of 5 kg (represented by $-5\text{ kg}$).
• (b) 30 km north: Opposite is 30 km south ($-30\text{ km}$).
• (c) A deposit of ₹2,000: Opposite is A withdrawal of ₹2,000 ($-₹2000$).
• (d) 100 m below sea level: Opposite is 100 m above sea level ($+100\text{ m}$).
• (e) 8°C below 0°C: Opposite is 8°C above 0°C ($+8°\text{C}$).

Q9
Find the values of the following using the rules of integers:
(a) $(-13) + (+18)$
(b) $(-10) + (-19)$
(c) $(+25) - (-15)$
(d) $(-32) - (-40)$
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
• (a) $(-13) + 18 = 18 - 13 = \mathbf{+5}$.
• (b) $(-10) + (-19) = -(10 + 19) = \mathbf{-29}$.
• (c) $(+25) - (-15) = 25 + 15 = \mathbf{+40}$ (Subtracting a negative is adding a positive).
• (d) $(-32) - (-40) = -32 + 40 = 40 - 32 = \mathbf{+8}$.

Q10
Write all the integers between -5 and +3 in ascending order. Find their sum.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Integers between -5 and +3:
In ascending order: -4, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2.
Sum Calculation:
$(-4) + (-3) + (-2) + (-1) + 0 + 1 + 2$
Notice: $(-2 + 2) = 0$ and $(-1 + 1) = 0$.
Sum = $(-4) + (-3) + 0 = \mathbf{-7}$.

Q11
At Srinagar, the temperature was -2°C on Monday and then it dropped by 3°C on Tuesday. On Wednesday, it rose by 4°C. What was the temperature of Srinagar on (a) Tuesday and (b) Wednesday?
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
• Monday temperature = $-2°\text{C}$.
• (a) Tuesday Temperature: Temperature drops by 3°C:
$\text{Temp} = -2°\text{C} - 3°\text{C} = \mathbf{-5°\text{C}}$.
• (b) Wednesday Temperature: Temperature rises by 4°C from Tuesday:
$\text{Temp} = -5°\text{C} + 4°\text{C} = \mathbf{-1°\text{C}}$.

Wednesday temperature calculation (-1°C)1 Mark
Q12
A submarine is situated at 800 m below the sea level. If it ascends 250 m, what is its new position relative to the sea level? Write in integer notation.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
• Initial position = $-800\text{ m}$ (below sea level).
• Ascending distance = $+250\text{ m}$ (moving upwards).
• New position = $-800 + 250 = \mathbf{-550\text{ m}}$.
In words: The submarine is now 550 m below sea level.

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Siachen Glacier Climate Data)
Case Study: Siachen Glacier Temperature & Altitude Profile:
At a high-altitude base camp on the Siachen glacier, the midnight temperature was recorded at $-18^\circ\text{C}$. By 2:00 p.m. the following afternoon, due to clear sunshine, the temperature rose by $11^\circ\text{C}$. By midnight, a cold blizzard caused the temperature to drop by $9^\circ\text{C}$.
(a) What was the temperature recorded at 2:00 p.m.?
(b) What was the final temperature recorded at midnight?
(c) An army helicopter ascends from an altitude of 3,200 m above sea level to a ridge 5,400 m above sea level. Express the vertical altitude gain as an integer.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

(a) Temperature at 2:00 p.m.:
$\text{Temp} = -18^\circ\text{C} + 11^\circ\text{C} = \mathbf{-7^\circ\text{C}}$.

(b) Temperature at Midnight:
$\text{Temp} = -7^\circ\text{C} - 9^\circ\text{C} = \mathbf{-16^\circ\text{C}}$.

(c) Altitude Gain:
$\text{Gain} = +5,400 - (+3,200) = \mathbf{+2,200\text{ metres}}$.
