📌 Key Integer Concepts & Sign Rules
  • Integers ($mathbb{Z}$): The collection of positive natural numbers, zero, and negative natural numbers: $dots, -4, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, dots$
  • Ordering on the Number Line: Numbers to the right are always greater than numbers to the left. For any positive integer $a$, $-a < 0 < a$. As negative numbers move further left, their value decreases (e.g., $-10 < -2$).
  • Absolute Value: The numerical value of an integer regardless of its sign, denoted by $|x|$. $|-5| = 5$, $|5| = 5$.
  • Rules for Addition & Subtraction:
    • Same sign: Add absolute values and assign the common sign: $(+5) + (+3) = +8$; $(-5) + (-3) = -8$.
    • Different signs: Subtract smaller absolute value from larger and assign the sign of the larger absolute value: $(+7) + (-4) = +3$; $(-9) + (+2) = -7$.
    • Subtraction: To subtract an integer, add its additive inverse: $a - b = a + (-b)$.

Q3
Compare the following pairs of integers using > or <:
(a) 0 and -8
(b) -5 and -11
(c) -100 and -101
(d) 7 and -7
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) 0 and -8: Zero is to the right of every negative integer $\implies \mathbf{0 > -8}$.
(b) -5 and -11: -5 is to the right of -11 on the number line $\implies \mathbf{-5 > -11}$.
(c) -100 and -101: -100 is closer to 0 than -101 $\implies \mathbf{-100 > -101}$.
(d) 7 and -7: Any positive integer is greater than any negative integer $\implies \mathbf{7 > -7}$.

Using a number line, find the integer which is:
(a) 4 more than -1
(b) 5 less than 3
(c) 3 less than -2
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) 4 more than -1:
Start at -1 on the number line. Move 4 steps to the right (addition):
$-1 + 4 = \mathbf{+3}$.
(b) 5 less than 3:
Start at +3 on the number line. Move 5 steps to the left (subtraction):
$3 - 5 = \mathbf{-2}$.
(c) 3 less than -2:
Start at -2 on the number line. Move 3 steps to the left:
$-2 - 3 = \mathbf{-5}$.

Q5
Find the sum of:
(a) (-11) + (-12)
(b) (+37) + (-20)
(c) (-312) + 39 + 192
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) (-11) + (-12):
Both have same negative signs: $-(11 + 12) = \mathbf{-23}$.
(b) (+37) + (-20):
Different signs: Subtract absolute values: $37 - 20 = 17$.
Assign sign of larger absolute value: $\mathbf{+17}$.
(c) (-312) + 39 + 192:
Add positive integers first: $39 + 192 = 231$.
Now calculate $(-312) + 231 = -(312 - 231) = \mathbf{-81}$.

Q6
Find the value of:
(a) 35 - (20)
(b) (-15) - (-18)
(c) (-20) - (13)
(d) 23 - (-12)
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Rule: $a - b = a + (\text{additive inverse of } b)$.
(a) $35 - 20 = \mathbf{15}$.
(b) $(-15) - (-18) = -15 + 18 = \mathbf{+3}$.
(c) $(-20) - 13 = -20 + (-13) = \mathbf{-33}$.
(d) $23 - (-12) = 23 + 12 = \mathbf{35}$.

Q7
Fill in the blanks with >, <, or = sign:
(a) (-3) + (-6) ______ (-3) - (-6)
(b) (-21) - (-10) ______ (-31) + (-11)
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a):
- LHS: $(-3) + (-6) = -9$
- RHS: $(-3) - (-6) = -3 + 6 = +3$
Since $-9 < +3$, the answer is <.
(b):
- LHS: $(-21) - (-10) = -21 + 10 = -11$
- RHS: $(-31) + (-11) = -42$
Since $-11 > -42$, the answer is >.

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Siachen Glacier Climate Data)
Case Study: Siachen Glacier Temperature & Altitude Profile:
At a high-altitude base camp on the Siachen glacier, the midnight temperature was recorded at $-18^\circ\text{C}$. By 2:00 p.m. the following afternoon, due to clear sunshine, the temperature rose by $11^\circ\text{C}$. By midnight, a cold blizzard caused the temperature to drop by $9^\circ\text{C}$.
(a) What was the temperature recorded at 2:00 p.m.?
(b) What was the final temperature recorded at midnight?
(c) An army helicopter ascends from an altitude of 3,200 m above sea level to a ridge 5,400 m above sea level. Express the vertical altitude gain as an integer.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

(a) Temperature at 2:00 p.m.:
$\text{Temp} = -18^\circ\text{C} + 11^\circ\text{C} = \mathbf{-7^\circ\text{C}}$.

(b) Temperature at Midnight:
$\text{Temp} = -7^\circ\text{C} - 9^\circ\text{C} = \mathbf{-16^\circ\text{C}}$.

(c) Altitude Gain:
$\text{Gain} = +5,400 - (+3,200) = \mathbf{+2,200\text{ metres}}$.
