Chapter 14: Practical Geometry

NCERT Mathematics (Classic) (Class 6) — Construction of Circles with Given Radius, Construction of Line Segments & Perpendicular Bisectors, Construction of Angles (60°, 90°, 120°, 45°, 30°) and Angle Bisectors | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Tools: Ruler (straight edge), Compasses (arcs & circles), Divider, Set Squares, Protractor.
  • Perpendicular Bisector: A line dividing a segment into two equal halves at 90°.
  • Standard Angle Construction with Compass: 60° (arc radius = compass opening), 120° (two consecutive 60° arcs), 90° (bisector of 60° and 120°), 45° (bisector of 90°), 30° (bisector of 60°).
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Draw a circle of radius 3.2 cm. Draw any circle and mark points A, B and C such that: (a) A is on the circle. (b) B is in the interior of the circle. (c) C is in the exterior of the circle.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Circle Construction:
1. Open compass to 3.2 cm using a ruler.
2. Mark point O as center and rotate compass 360° to draw circle.
3. Marking points: Point A on circumference (OA = r), Point B inside (OB < r), Point C outside (OC > r).

Q2
Draw a line segment AB of length 7.3 cm and find its axis of symmetry (perpendicular bisector). Write steps of construction.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Steps of Construction:
1. Draw line segment AB = 7.3 cm using a ruler.
2. With center A and radius greater than half of AB (> 3.65 cm), draw arcs above and below AB.
3. With center B and the same radius, draw arcs cutting previous arcs at points P and Q.
4. Join PQ. Line PQ is the required perpendicular bisector (axis of symmetry) of AB.

Q3
Construct with ruler and compasses, angles of following measures: (a) 60° (b) 90° (c) 45°.
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Compass Angle Constructions:
• (a) 60°: Draw ray OA. With center O, draw arc cutting OA at P. With center P and same radius, cut arc at Q. Ray OQ forms ∠AOQ = 60°.
• (b) 90°: Cut another arc at R (120°). Draw intersecting arcs from Q and R to find bisector. The ray forms 90° with OA.
• (c) 45°: Bisect the 90° angle by drawing arcs from ray OA and ray 90°. The bisector ray forms 45°.

CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Surveying & Civil Engineering: Road Perpendicular Intersection:
Civil engineers must lay an underground pipeline perpendicular to highway segment PQ at its midpoint M. (a) Name the geometrical construct required. (b) Explain why an ordinary protractor is insufficient for large-scale field work compared to compass triangulation. (c) If PM = 45 m, what is the length of PQ?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Construct: Perpendicular Bisector.
(b) Compass Triangulation: On a physical terrain, laser distance meters and optical compass arcs guarantee millimeter precision over large distances where a plastic protractor's angular error magnifies exponentially.
(c) Length of PQ: Since M is midpoint, PQ = 2 × PM = 2 × 45 = 90 metres.
