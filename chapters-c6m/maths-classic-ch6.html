Chapter 6: Integers

NCERT Mathematics (Classic) (Class 6) — Negative Numbers & Need for Integers, Integers on Number Line, Ordering of Integers, Addition of Integers (Same Sign & Opposite Sign), Subtraction of Integers | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Integers: Z = {..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...}. Positive integers, negative integers, and zero (neither positive nor negative).
  • Opposite Situations: Gain vs Loss, Above sea level vs Below sea level, Rise vs Fall in temperature.
  • Number Line Rule: Moving right indicates addition (+); moving left indicates subtraction (-).
  • Rules: (+a) + (+b) = +(a+b); (-a) + (-b) = -(a+b); (+a) + (-b) = subtract and take sign of greater absolute value.
  • Subtraction Rule: a - b = a + (additive inverse of b).
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Write opposites of the following: (a) Increase in weight (b) 30 km north (c) 80 m east (d) Loss of ₹700 (e) 100 m above sea level.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Opposite Quantities:
• (a) Decrease in weight.
• (b) 30 km south.
• (c) 80 m west.
• (d) Gain of ₹700.
• (e) 100 m below sea level.

Q2
Find the sum of: (a) (-7) + (-9) + 4 + 16 (b) (37) + (-2) + (-65) + (-8) (c) (-312) + 39 + 192.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Integer Addition:
• (a) [(-7) + (-9)] + [4 + 16] = (-16) + 20 = +4.
• (b) 37 + [(-2) + (-65) + (-8)] = 37 + (-75) = -38.
• (c) (-312) + (39 + 192) = (-312) + 231 = -81.

Q3
Find: (a) 35 - (20) (b) 72 - (90) (c) (-15) - (-18) (d) (-20) - (13) (e) 23 - (-12).
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Integer Subtraction:
• (a) 35 - 20 = 15.
• (b) 72 - 90 = -18.
• (c) (-15) + 18 = 3.
• (d) (-20) - 13 = -33.
• (e) 23 + 12 = 35.

CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Cryogenics & Sub-Zero Storage: Lab Temperature Monitoring:
A biological vaccine storage container is kept at -18°C. During power fluctuation, temperature rises by 7°C. (a) What is the new temperature? (b) Technicians activate a cooling pump that lowers the temperature by 12°C. Find final temperature. (c) Express the total net change from initial -18°C.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) New Temperature: -18 + 7 = -11°C.
(b) Final Temperature: -11 - 12 = -23°C.
(c) Net Change: -23 - (-18) = -23 + 18 = -5°C (fell by 5°C).
