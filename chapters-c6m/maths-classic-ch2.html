Chapter 2: Whole Numbers

NCERT Mathematics (Classic) (Class 6) — Predecessor & Successor, Whole Numbers on Number Line, Properties of Addition, Multiplication & Subtraction, Distributive Property, Number Patterns | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Natural Numbers: Counting numbers 1, 2, 3, ... Whole numbers: W = {0, 1, 2, 3, ...}.
  • Properties: Closure, Commutative (a+b = b+a, a×b = b×a), Associative ((a+b)+c = a+(b+c)).
  • Distributive Property: a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c.
  • Additive Identity: 0 (a + 0 = a). Multiplicative Identity: 1 (a × 1 = a). Division by 0 is undefined.
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Find the sum by suitable rearrangement: (a) 837 + 208 + 363 (b) 1962 + 453 + 1538 + 647.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Rearrangement using Commutative & Associative Properties:
• (a) (837 + 363) + 208 = 1,200 + 208 = 1,408.
• (b) (1962 + 1538) + (453 + 647) = 3,500 + 1,100 = 4,600.

Q2
Find the product by suitable rearrangement: (a) 2 × 1768 × 50 (b) 4 × 166 × 25 (c) 125 × 40 × 8 × 25.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Product Rearrangement:
• (a) (2 × 50) × 1,768 = 100 × 1,768 = 1,76,800.
• (b) (4 × 25) × 166 = 100 × 166 = 16,600.
• (c) (125 × 8) × (40 × 25) = 1,000 × 1,000 = 10,00,000.

Q3
Find the value using distributive property: (a) 297 × 17 + 297 × 3 (b) 54279 × 92 + 8 × 54279 (c) 81265 × 169 - 81265 × 69.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Distributive Property Application:
• (a) 297 × (17 + 3) = 297 × 20 = 5,940.
• (b) 54,279 × (92 + 8) = 54,279 × 100 = 54,27,900.
• (c) 81,265 × (169 - 69) = 81,265 × 100 = 81,26,500.

Q4
A milk vendor supplies 32 litres of milk to a hostel in the morning and 68 litres in the evening. If milk costs ₹45 per litre, how much money is due to the vendor per day?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Step-by-Step Calculation:
• Total milk per day = 32 + 68 = 100 litres.
• Cost = 45 × (32 + 68) = 45 × 100 = ₹4,500.
₹4,500 is due to the vendor per day.

Q5
Study the pattern: 1 × 8 + 1 = 9; 12 × 8 + 2 = 98; 123 × 8 + 3 = 987. Write the next two steps and explain the rule.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Pattern Steps:
• Step 4: 1234 × 8 + 4 = 9876.
• Step 5: 12345 × 8 + 5 = 98765.
Rule: In the first factor, digits increase consecutively (1, 12, 123...). The added number equals the number of digits, producing consecutive decreasing digits starting with 9.

CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Commercial Energy Billing: School Solar Inverter Savings:
A school campus consumes 38 units of electricity in the morning and 62 units in the afternoon each day. Commercial tariff is ₹9 per unit. (a) Use distributive property to compute weekly commercial electricity bill (for 6 working days). (b) If rooftop solar panels generate 70 units daily, calculate net daily units billed.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Weekly Bill: Daily units = 38 + 62 = 100 units. Daily cost = 100 × ₹9 = ₹900. Weekly cost (6 days) = 6 × ₹900 = ₹5,400.
(b) Net Units Billed: 100 - 70 = 30 units daily.
