📌 Key Algebraic Concepts
  • Variable: An entity that can take different numerical values, denoted by letters like $x, y, z, m, n, p$.
  • Constant: A quantity having a fixed numerical value (e.g., $5, -8, \frac{1}{2}$).
  • Algebraic Expression: A combination of variables and constants connected by arithmetic operations ($+, -, \times, \div$), such as $2x + 7$ or $3y - 5$.
  • Equation: A mathematical condition on a variable stating that two expressions are equal, containing an equality sign ($=$).
  • Transposition: Changing the side of a term across the equality sign with inverse operation ($+ \leftrightarrow -, \times \leftrightarrow \div$).
NCERT Exercise 11.1: Matchstick Rules & Expressions
Q1
Find the rule which gives the number of matchsticks required to make the following letter patterns:
(a) A pattern of letter T as ⊤
(b) A pattern of letter Z as Z
(c) A pattern of letter U as ⨆
(d) A pattern of letter V as V
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Let $n$ be the number of letters formed.
(a) Letter T: 2 matchsticks per letter $\implies$ Rule = 2n.
(b) Letter Z: 3 matchsticks per letter $\implies$ Rule = 3n.
(c) Letter U: 3 matchsticks per letter $\implies$ Rule = 3n.
(d) Letter V: 2 matchsticks per letter $\implies$ Rule = 2n.

Q2
Cadets are marching in a parade. There are 5 cadets in a row. What is the rule which gives the number of cadets, given the number of rows? (Use n for the number of rows).
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Number of cadets in 1 row = 5.
Let the number of rows be $n$.
Total number of cadets = Number of cadets in a row $\times$ Number of rows
$= 5 \times n = \mathbf{5n}$.

Q3
Write algebraic expressions for each of the following statements:
(a) 7 added to p
(b) 7 subtracted from -m
(c) y is multiplied by -5 and the result is added to 16
(d) One-fifth of x added to 3 times y
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) 7 added to p: $\mathbf{p + 7}$
(b) 7 subtracted from -m: $\mathbf{-m - 7}$
(c) y multiplied by -5 and added to 16: $(-5 \times y) + 16 = \mathbf{16 - 5y}$
(d) One-fifth of x added to 3 times y: $\mathbf{\frac{x}{5} + 3y}$

NCERT Exercise 11.2: Evaluating Expressions & Formulas
Q4
The side of an equilateral triangle is shown by l. Express the perimeter of the equilateral triangle using l. Also express the perimeter of a regular hexagon of side s using s.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Equilateral Triangle: Has 3 equal sides of length $l$.
Perimeter = $l + l + l = \mathbf{3l}$.
Regular Hexagon: Has 6 equal sides of length $s$.
Perimeter = $6 \times s = \mathbf{6s}$.

Q5
Evaluate the expression 3x² - 5x + 8 when:
(a) x = 2
(b) x = -1
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) When x = 2:
$3(2)^2 - 5(2) + 8 = 3(4) - 10 + 8 = 12 - 10 + 8 = \mathbf{10}$.
(b) When x = -1:
$3(-1)^2 - 5(-1) + 8 = 3(1) - (-5) + 8 = 3 + 5 + 8 = \mathbf{16}$.

NCERT Exercise 11.3: Solving Simple Equations
Q6
Solve the following simple linear equations using inverse operations:
(i) x + 10 = 25
(ii) p - 8 = 17
(iii) 5m = 45
(iv) q/4 = 7
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(i) x + 10 = 25: Transpose +10 to RHS: $x = 25 - 10 \implies \mathbf{x = 15}$.
(ii) p - 8 = 17: Transpose -8 to RHS: $p = 17 + 8 \implies \mathbf{p = 25}$.
(iii) 5m = 45: Divide both sides by 5: $m = \frac{45}{5} \implies \mathbf{m = 9}$.
(iv) q/4 = 7: Multiply both sides by 4: $q = 7 \times 4 \implies \mathbf{q = 28}$.

Q7
Solve the equation 2x + 7 = 19 and verify your solution.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Solving:
$2x + 7 = 19$
$2x = 19 - 7 = 12$
$x = \frac{12}{2} = \mathbf{6}$.
Verification:
$\text{LHS} = 2(6) + 7 = 12 + 7 = 19 = \text{RHS}$. Hence verified.

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Algebraic Age Problems)
Case Study: Family Age Relationships:
Sarita's present age is y years.
(a) What will be her age 5 years from now?
(b) What was her age 3 years ago?
(c) Sarita's grandfather is 6 times her age. What is her grandfather's age?
(d) Grandmother is 2 years younger than grandfather. What is grandmother's age?
(e) Sarita's father's age is 5 years more than 3 times Sarita's age. Express father's age.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

(a) Age in 5 years: $(y + 5)$ years.

(b) Age 3 years ago: $(y - 3)$ years.

(c) Grandfather's age: $6 \times y = \mathbf{6y\text{ years}}$.

(d) Grandmother's age: $(6y - 2)$ years.

(e) Father's age: $(3y + 5)$ years.
