Chapter 4: Basic Geometrical Ideas

NCERT Mathematics (Classic) (Class 6) — Points, Line Segments, Lines, Rays, Intersecting Lines, Parallel Lines, Curves (Open & Closed), Polygons, Angles, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Circles | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Point: A location in space with no dimensions. Line: Extends infinitely in both directions. Line Segment: Portion of line between two endpoints.
  • Ray: A portion of a line starting at one point (initial point) and going endlessly in one direction.
  • Polygon: A simple closed curve made up entirely of line segments.
  • Circle: Center, Radius (r), Diameter (d = 2r), Chord, Arc, Sector (region between two radii and arc), Segment (region between chord and arc).
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Use the figure to name: (a) Five points (b) A line (c) Four rays (d) Five line segments.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Geometrical Components:
• (a) Five points: O, B, C, D, E.
• (b) A line: $\overleftrightarrow{BD}$ (or $\overleftrightarrow{DE}$ or $\overleftrightarrow{BE}$).
• (c) Four rays: $\vec{OD}, \vec{OE}, \vec{OC}, \vec{OB}$.
• (d) Five line segments: $\overline{DE}, \overline{EO}, \overline{OB}, \overline{OC}, \overline{OD}$.

Q2
Draw a rough sketch of a quadrilateral ABCD. State: (a) Two pairs of opposite sides. (b) Two pairs of opposite angles. (c) Two pairs of adjacent sides. (d) Two pairs of adjacent angles.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Quadrilateral Relations:
• (a) Opposite sides: (AB, DC) and (AD, BC).
• (b) Opposite angles: (∠A, ∠C) and (∠B, ∠D).
• (c) Adjacent sides: (AB, BC) and (BC, CD).
• (d) Adjacent angles: (∠A, ∠B) and (∠B, ∠C).

Q3
From the figure of a circle with center O, identify: (a) its centre (b) three radii (c) a diameter (d) a chord (e) two points in the interior (f) a point in the exterior (g) a sector (h) a segment.
[4 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Circle Terminology:
• (a) Centre: Point O.
• (b) Three radii: $\overline{OA}, \overline{OB}, \overline{OC}$.
• (c) Diameter: $\overline{AC}$.
• (d) Chord: $\overline{ED}$ (and diameter $\overline{AC}$).
• (e) Interior points: O and P.
• (f) Exterior point: Q.
• (g) Sector: Shaded region OAB (bounded by arc AB and radii OA, OB).
• (h) Segment: Shaded region between chord ED and arc ED.

Centre, radii, and diameter1.5 Marks
Chord and interior/exterior points1 Mark
Distinguishing sector vs segment1.5 Marks
CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Civil Engineering: Circular Traffic Rotary Design:
A municipal road junction features a circular roundabout of radius 14 m with a central statue. (a) Find diameter of the roundabout. (b) A straight pedestrian railing is installed across chord AB of length 20 m. Explain whether this railing passes through the center. (c) Distinguish between the floral sector bed and the walkway segment.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Diameter: d = 2 × 14 = 28 m.
(b) Central Passage: No, because the longest chord passing through the center is the diameter (28 m). Since length is 20 m, it does not pass through the center.
(c) Sector vs Segment: A sector is enclosed by two radii and an arc (wedge shape), while a segment is bounded by a chord and an arc.
