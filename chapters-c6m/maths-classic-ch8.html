Chapter 8: Decimals

NCERT Mathematics (Classic) (Class 6) — Tenths & Hundredths, Place Value Chart for Decimals, Comparing Decimals, Money, Length & Weight Conversions, Addition & Subtraction of Decimals | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Decimals: Fractions with denominators 10, 100, 1000... 1 tenth = 1/10 = 0.1; 1 hundredth = 1/100 = 0.01.
  • Money: 1 Rupee = 100 paise; 1 paisa = ₹0.01.
  • Length: 1 m = 100 cm (1 cm = 0.01 m); 1 km = 1000 m (1 m = 0.001 km).
  • Weight: 1 kg = 1000 g (1 g = 0.001 kg).
  • Rules for Addition/Subtraction: Align decimal points vertically before operating.
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Express as rupees using decimals: (a) 5 paise (b) 75 paise (c) 50 rupees 90 paise. Express as metres: (d) 15 cm (e) 2 m 45 cm.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Decimal Conversions:
• (a) 5 paise = 5/100 = ₹0.05.
• (b) 75 paise = 75/100 = ₹0.75.
• (c) 50 rupees 90 paise = 50 + 90/100 = ₹50.90.
• (d) 15 cm = 15/100 = 0.15 m.
• (e) 2 m 45 cm = 2 + 45/100 = 2.45 m.

Q2
Which is greater: (a) 0.5 or 0.05 (b) 1.23 or 1.2 (c) 0.099 or 0.19? Arrange in ascending order: 0.8, 0.08, 0.88, 0.808.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Decimal Comparison:
• (a) 0.50 > 0.05 -> 0.5 is greater.
• (b) 1.23 > 1.20 -> 1.23 is greater.
• (c) 0.099 < 0.190 -> 0.19 is greater.
• Ascending Order: 0.08 < 0.808 < 0.8 < 0.88.

Q3
Sunita travelled 15 km 268 m by bus, 7 km 7 m by car and 500 m on foot in order to reach her school. How far is her school from her residence?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Decimal Addition:
• By bus = 15.268 km.
• By car = 7.007 km.
• On foot = 500 m = 0.500 km.
• Total distance = 15.268 + 7.007 + 0.500 = 22.775 km.
Her school is 22 km 775 m (22.775 km) from her residence.

CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Grocery Billing & Accounting: Supermarket Audit:
Raju bought vegetables weighing 10 kg. Out of this, 3 kg 500 g is onions, 2 kg 75 g is tomatoes and the rest is potatoes. (a) Express onion and tomato weights in kg using decimals. (b) Find the weight of potatoes in decimals. (c) If potatoes cost ₹28.50 per kg, calculate total cost of potatoes.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Decimal Weights: Onions = 3.500 kg; Tomatoes = 2.075 kg.
(b) Potatoes Weight: Total onions + tomatoes = 3.500 + 2.075 = 5.575 kg. Potatoes = 10.000 - 5.575 = 4.425 kg.
(c) Cost: 4.425 × ₹28.50 = ₹126.11.
