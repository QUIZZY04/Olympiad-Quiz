📌 Key Ratio, Proportion & Unitary Rules
  • Ratio: Comparing two quantities of the same kind and with the same unit by division. Denoted by $a : b$ (read as "$a$ is to $b$"). Ratio has no units.
  • Simplest Form of Ratio: Divide both terms by their HCF: $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{a \div \text{HCF}}{b \div \text{HCF}}$.
  • Proportion: If two ratios are equal, they are in proportion: $a : b :: c : d$ (or $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$).
    • $a$ and $d$ are called extreme terms (extremes).
    • $b$ and $c$ are called middle terms (means).
    • Rule: $\text{Product of Extremes} = \text{Product of Means} \implies a \times d = b \times c$.
  • Unitary Method: The method in which first we find the value of one unit by division, and then the value of the required number of units by multiplication.
NCERT Exercise 12.1: Ratios in Simplest Form
Q1
There are 20 girls and 15 boys in a class.
(a) What is the ratio of number of girls to the number of boys?
(b) What is the ratio of number of girls to the total number of students in the class?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Number of girls = 20; Number of boys = 15.
Total number of students = $20 + 15 = 35$.
(a) Girls to Boys:
$\text{Ratio} = \frac{20}{15} = \frac{20 \div 5}{15 \div 5} = \frac{4}{3} = \mathbf{4 : 3}$.
(b) Girls to Total Students:
$\text{Ratio} = \frac{20}{35} = \frac{20 \div 5}{35 \div 5} = \frac{4}{7} = \mathbf{4 : 7}$.

Q2
Find the ratio of the following in simplest form:
(a) 30 minutes to 1.5 hours
(b) 40 cm to 1.5 m
(c) 55 paise to ₹ 1
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) 30 minutes to 1.5 hours:
Convert hours to minutes: $1.5 \times 60 = 90\text{ minutes}$.
$\text{Ratio} = \frac{30}{90} = \frac{1}{3} = \mathbf{1 : 3}$.
(b) 40 cm to 1.5 m:
Convert metres to cm: $1.5 \times 100 = 150\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Ratio} = \frac{40}{150} = \frac{4}{15} = \mathbf{4 : 15}$.
(c) 55 paise to ₹ 1:
Convert rupees to paise: ₹ 1 = 100 paise.
$\text{Ratio} = \frac{55}{100} = \frac{11}{20} = \mathbf{11 : 20}$.

Q3
Divide ₹ 60 in the ratio 1 : 2 between Kriti and Kiran.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Total parts = $1 + 2 = 3$ parts.
- Kriti's share = $\frac{1}{3} \times 60 = \mathbf{\text{₹ } 20}$.
- Kiran's share = $\frac{2}{3} \times 60 = \mathbf{\text{₹ } 40}$.
Verification: ₹ 20 + ₹ 40 = ₹ 60.

NCERT Exercise 12.2: Proportions & Cross-Products
Q4
Determine if the following numbers are in proportion:
(a) 15, 45, 40, 120
(b) 33, 121, 9, 96
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Rule: Numbers $a, b, c, d$ are in proportion if $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$ (or $a \times d = b \times c$).
(a) 15, 45, 40, 120:
- Ratio 1: $\frac{15}{45} = \frac{1}{3}$
- Ratio 2: $\frac{40}{120} = \frac{1}{3}$
Since $\frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{3}$, Yes, they are in proportion ($15 : 45 :: 40 : 120$).
(b) 33, 121, 9, 96:
- Ratio 1: $\frac{33}{121} = \frac{3}{11}$
- Ratio 2: $\frac{9}{96} = \frac{3}{32}$
Since $\frac{3}{11} \neq \frac{3}{32}$, No, they are NOT in proportion.

Q5
Find the missing value x in the proportion: 25 : 30 :: 40 : x.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
In a proportion, $\text{Product of Extremes} = \text{Product of Means}$.
$25 \times x = 30 \times 40$
$25x = 1,200$
$x = \frac{1,200}{25} = \mathbf{48}$.

NCERT Exercise 12.3: The Unitary Method
Q6
If the cost of 7 m of cloth is ₹ 1,470, find the cost of 5 m of cloth.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Step 1: Cost of 1 metre of cloth:
$\text{Cost of 1 m} = \frac{1,470}{7} = \text{₹ } 210$.
Step 2: Cost of 5 metres of cloth:
$\text{Cost of 5 m} = 5 \times \text{₹ } 210 = \mathbf{\text{₹ } 1,050}$.

Q7
Ekta earns ₹ 3,000 in 10 days. How much will she earn in 30 days?
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Earnings in 10 days = ₹ 3,000.
Earnings in 1 day = $\frac{3,000}{10} = \text{₹ } 300$.
Earnings in 30 days = $30 \times \text{₹ } 300 = \mathbf{\text{₹ } 9,000}$.

Q8
A car travels 90 km in 2 1/2 hours.
(a) How much time is required to cover 30 km with the same speed?
(b) Find the distance covered in 2 hours with the same speed.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Speed of car = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{90}{2.5} = 36\text{ km/h}$.
(a) Time to cover 30 km:
$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{30}{36} = \frac{5}{6}\text{ hours} = \frac{5}{6} \times 60 = \mathbf{50\text{ minutes}}$.
(b) Distance in 2 hours:
$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time} = 36 \times 2 = \mathbf{72\text{ km}}$.

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Industrial Manufacturing Scale)
Case Study: Solar Power Station Generation Ratio:
A solar farm generates 180 kilowatt-hours (kWh) of clean electricity every 4 hours during peak sunlight.
(a) Formulate the unit rate of energy generation per hour.
(b) How many kilowatt-hours will the solar farm generate in 7.5 hours of continuous sunshine?
(c) If a nearby village requires 720 kWh daily, how many hours of peak sunlight generation are needed to meet the full demand?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

(a) Unit Rate:
Rate = $\frac{180\text{ kWh}}{4\text{ h}} = \mathbf{45\text{ kWh per hour}}$.

(b) Generation in 7.5 hours:
Energy = $45 \times 7.5 = \mathbf{337.5\text{ kWh}}$.

(c) Hours needed for 720 kWh:
Hours = $\frac{720}{45} = \mathbf{16\text{ hours of peak sunlight}}$.
