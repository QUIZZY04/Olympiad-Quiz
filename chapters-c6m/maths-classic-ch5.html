Chapter 5: Understanding Elementary Shapes

NCERT Mathematics (Classic) (Class 6) — Measuring Line Segments, Angles (Right, Straight, Acute, Obtuse, Reflex), Clock Revolutions & Directions, Perpendicular Lines, Classification of Triangles, Quadrilaterals, 3D Shapes | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Angles & Revolutions: 1 full turn = 360°, 1/2 turn = 180° (Straight angle), 1/4 turn = 90° (Right angle).
  • Classification of Triangles by Sides: Scalene (all unequal), Isosceles (2 equal), Equilateral (all 3 equal).
  • Classification by Angles: Acute-angled (all <90°), Right-angled (one =90°), Obtuse-angled (one >90°).
  • Quadrilaterals: Parallelogram, Rectangle, Rhombus, Square, Trapezium.
  • 3D Polyhedra: Faces (F), Vertices (V), Edges (E). Euler's Formula: F + V - E = 2.
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
What fraction of a clockwise revolution does the hour hand of a clock turn through, when it goes from: (a) 3 to 9 (b) 4 to 7 (c) 7 to 10 (d) 12 to 9?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Clock Revolutions:
Total hours on clock face = 12 hours.
• (a) 3 to 9: 6 hours -> 6/12 = 1/2 revolution (2 right angles).
• (b) 4 to 7: 3 hours -> 3/12 = 1/4 revolution (1 right angle).
• (c) 7 to 10: 3 hours -> 3/12 = 1/4 revolution (1 right angle).
• (d) 12 to 9: 9 hours -> 9/12 = 3/4 revolution (3 right angles).

Q2
Name the types of following triangles: (a) Triangle with lengths of sides 7 cm, 8 cm and 9 cm. (b) ΔABC with AB = 8.7 cm, AC = 7 cm and BC = 6 cm. (c) ΔPQR such that PQ = QR = PR = 5 cm. (d) ΔDEF with m∠D = 90°. (e) ΔXYZ with m∠Y = 90° and XY = YZ.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Triangle Classification:
• (a) All sides unequal -> Scalene triangle.
• (b) All sides unequal -> Scalene triangle.
• (c) All three sides equal -> Equilateral triangle.
• (d) One angle = 90° -> Right-angled triangle.
• (e) One right angle and two equal sides -> Isosceles right-angled triangle.

Q3
Name each polygon: (a) 3 sides (b) 4 sides (c) 5 sides (d) 6 sides (e) 8 sides. Also state number of faces, edges and vertices of a triangular prism.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Polygons & 3D Shapes:
• 3 sides: Triangle; 4 sides: Quadrilateral; 5 sides: Pentagon; 6 sides: Hexagon; 8 sides: Octagon.
• Triangular Prism:
  Faces (F) = 5 (2 triangular bases + 3 rectangular sides)
  Edges (E) = 9
  Vertices (V) = 6.
  Verification: F + V - E = 5 + 6 - 9 = 2 (Euler's formula verified).

CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Aviation Navigation: Runway Compass Headings:
An aircraft flying North makes a turn. (a) If it turns clockwise by 2 right angles, what is its new heading? (b) If it turns anti-clockwise through 3/4 of a revolution from East, which direction does it face? (c) Name the angle formed between North-East and North-West.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Heading: North + 180° = South.
(b) Anti-clockwise from East: 3/4 turn (270°) anti-clockwise from East faces South.
(c) Angle: Angle between NE (45°) and NW (315°) is 90° (Right angle).
