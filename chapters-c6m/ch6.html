📌 Key Mensuration Formulas & Definitions
  • Perimeter: The total length of the continuous boundary enclosing a closed plane figure.
    • Perimeter of a Rectangle = 2 × (Length + Breadth) = 2(l + b)
    • Perimeter of a Square = 4 × Side = 4s
    • Perimeter of a Regular n-sided Polygon = n × Side length
  • Area: The measure of the surface enclosed within the boundary of a closed plane figure.
    • Area of a Rectangle = Length × Breadth = l × b
    • Area of a Square = Side × Side = s²

NCERT Exercise 6.1: Perimeter Calculations & Formulas
Q1
Find the perimeter of:
(a) A rectangle with length = 150 cm and breadth = 1 m.
(b) A regular hexagon of side 8.5 cm.
(c) An equilateral triangle of side 9.2 cm.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Rectangle Perimeter:
Convert units to same system: l = 150 cm, b = 1 m = 100 cm.
Perimeter = 2(l + b) = 2(150 + 100) = 2(250) = 500 cm (or 5 m)
(b) Regular Hexagon:
A regular hexagon has 6 equal sides.
Perimeter = 6 × side = 6 × 8.5 cm = 51 cm
(c) Equilateral Triangle:
An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides.
Perimeter = 3 × side = 3 × 9.2 cm = 27.6 cm

Q2
A rectangular piece of land measures 0.7 km by 0.5 km. Each side is to be fenced with 4 rows of wires. What is the total length of the wire needed in metres?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Step 1: Convert Dimensions to Metres:
l = 0.7 km = 0.7 × 1000 = 700 m
b = 0.5 km = 0.5 × 1000 = 500 m
Step 2: Find Perimeter (Length of 1 Row of Wire):
Perimeter = 2(l + b) = 2(700 + 500) = 2(1200) = 2400 m
Step 3: Total Wire for 4 Rows:
Total wire = 4 × Perimeter = 4 × 2400 = 9600 m (or 9.6 km)

Converting km to m (700 m, 500 m)1 Mark
Perimeter calculation 2(700 + 500) = 2400 m1 Mark
Multiplying by 4 to get 9600 m1 Mark
Q3
A room floor is 5 m long and 4 m wide. A square carpet of side 3 m is laid on the floor. Find the area of the floor that is NOT carpeted.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Step 1: Calculate Total Area of Floor:
Length of room l = 5 m, Breadth b = 4 m.
Area of floor = l × b = 5 × 4 = 20 m²
Step 2: Calculate Area of Square Carpet:
Side of square carpet s = 3 m.
Area of carpet = s² = 3 × 3 = 9 m²
Step 3: Calculate Uncarpeted Area:
Uncarpeted Area = Area of floor - Area of carpet Uncarpeted Area = 20 - 9 = 11 m²

Area of room floor: 5 × 4 = 20 m²1 Mark
Area of square carpet: 3 × 3 = 9 m²1 Mark
Subtraction: 20 - 9 = 11 m²1 Mark
CBSE Competency-Based Question (Architecture & Tiling)
Case Study: Flooring a Kitchen with Square Tiles:
How many square tiles of side 20 cm each will be required to pave a kitchen floor measuring 4 m long and 3 m wide? If the cost of tiling is ₹25 per tile, find the total cost of paving the floor.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

Step 1: Convert Floor Dimensions to cm:
l = 4 m = 400 cm
b = 3 m = 300 cm
Area of floor = 400 × 300 = 120,000 cm²

Step 2: Area of One Square Tile:
Area of tile = 20 × 20 = 400 cm²

Step 3: Number of Tiles Required:
Number of tiles = Area of floor/Area of one tile = 120,000/400 = 300 tiles

Step 4: Total Cost:
Total Cost = 300 × ₹25 = ₹7,500
