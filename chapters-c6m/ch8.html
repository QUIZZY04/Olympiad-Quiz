📌 Key Geometric Construction Rules
  • Compass and Ruler Rules: The ruler is used only to draw straight lines and measure lengths. The compass is used to mark equal lengths and draw arcs/circles.
  • Circle of Radius r: Open the compass to distance r on the ruler, place the needle at center O, and rotate complete 360°.
  • Perpendicular Bisector of AB: With centers A and B and radius greater than 1/2AB, draw intersecting arcs above and below AB. The line joining these intersection points bisects AB at 90°.
  • Standard Angles using Compass:
    • 60° Angle: The first arc cut from the baseline with the same radius gives 60°.
    • 120° Angle: The second arc cut from the 60° point gives 120°.
    • 90° Angle: Bisecting the ∠between 60° and 120° gives 90°.

NCERT Exercise 8.1: Circle & Line Segment Constructions
Q1
Write the step-by-step procedure to construct the perpendicular bisector of a given line segment AB of length 7.6 cm using ruler and compasses.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Steps of Construction:

  1. Step 1: Using a ruler, draw a line segment AB = 7.6 cm.
  2. Step 2: With point A as center and compass radius greater than half of AB (i.e., radius > 3.8 cm, say 5 cm), draw two arcs, one above AB and one below AB.
  3. Step 3: With point B as center and with the same radius, draw two more arcs intersecting the previous arcs at points P (above) and Q (below).
  4. Step 4: Using a ruler, draw a straight line passing through points P and Q. Let PQ intersect AB at point M.
  5. Conclusion: Line PQ is the required perpendicular bisector of AB. Point M bisects AB so that AM = MB = 3.8 cm and ∠PMA = ∠PMB = 90°.

Q2
Write the steps of construction to construct an ∠of 60° and then bisect it to obtain an ∠of 30° using compass and ruler only.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Steps of Construction:

  1. Step 1: Draw a ray OA.
  2. Step 2 (60° Angle): With O as center and any convenient compass radius, draw an arc intersecting ray OA at point B.
  3. Step 3: With B as center and with the same radius, draw an arc cutting the previous arc at point C. Draw ray OC. Then ∠AOC = 60°.
  4. Step 4 (Bisecting to 30°): With B and C as centers and radius greater than half of arc BC, draw two arcs intersecting each other at point D.
  5. Step 5: Join ray OD. The ray OD bisects ∠AOC. Therefore: ∠AOD = 1/2 × 60° = 30°

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Practical Geometry)
Case Study: Finding the Center of a Round Plate:
A potter creates a round circular ceramic plate but has lost the exact center point. Describe the geometric construction method using perpendicular bisectors of two chords to locate the exact center of the plate.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

Geometric Principle: The perpendicular bisector of any chord of a circle always passes through the center of the circle. Therefore, the intersection of perpendicular bisectors of any two non-parallel chords is the center of the circle.

Procedure:
1. Draw two non-parallel chords AB and CD on the circular boundary.
2. Construct the perpendicular bisector l₁ of chord AB using a compass.
3. Construct the perpendicular bisector l₂ of chord CD using a compass.
4. The point of intersection O of lines l₁ and l₂ is the exact center of the circular plate.
