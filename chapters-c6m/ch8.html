Chapter 8: Playing with Constructions

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Class 6) — Compass & Ruler Constructions, Perpendicular Bisectors, Angle Bisectors & Standard Angle Geometry | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Geometrical Construction Instruments & Rules
  • Instruments: Ruler (unmarked straightedge / calibrated scale in cm/mm), Compass (drawing arcs and circles of precise radii), Divider (comparing lengths), Set Squares (30°-60°-90° and 45°-45°-90°), Protractor (measuring/drawing angles).
  • Perpendicular Bisector: A line that divides a given line segment into two equal halves at right angles (90°). Every point on the perpendicular bisector is equidistant from the two endpoints.
  • Angle Bisector: A ray that divides an angle into two equal angles.
  • Standard Angles with Compass & Ruler:
    • 60°: First arc cut from initial point on circumference of same radius.
    • 120°: Second consecutive arc cut along circumference.
    • 90°: Bisecting the interval between 60° and 120°.
    • 30°: Bisecting 60°.
    • 45°: Bisecting 90°.
NCERT Exercise 8.1: Circle & Line Segment Constructions
Q1
Draw a circle of radius 3.5 cm using a ruler and compass. Mark its centre O, a radius OA, a diameter AB, and a chord CD.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Steps of Construction:
1. Open the compass to measure exactly 3.5 cm on the ruler scale.
2. Mark a point $O$ on paper with a sharp pencil as the centre.
3. Place the compass needle at $O$ and rotate smoothly through 360° to draw the circle.
4. Draw segment from $O$ to any point on circle $A$ $\implies$ Radius $OA = 3.5\text{ cm}$.
5. Extend $AO$ in a straight line through $O$ to point $B$ on the circle $\implies$ Diameter $AB = 7\text{ cm}$.
6. Join any two points $C$ and $D$ on the circumference not passing through $O$ $\implies$ Chord $CD$.

Q2
Draw a line segment AB of length 7.3 cm. Using ruler and compass, find its axis of symmetry (perpendicular bisector). Measure each half.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Steps of Construction:
1. Draw a line segment $AB = 7.3\text{ cm}$ using a calibrated ruler.
2. With $A$ as centre and radius more than half of $AB$ (approx. 4.5 cm), draw two arcs, one above and one below $AB$.
3. With $B$ as centre and the identical radius, draw two arcs intersecting the previous arcs at points $P$ and $Q$.
4. Join $P$ and $Q$ with a straight line. Let $PQ$ intersect $AB$ at point $M$.
5. Line $PQ$ is the required perpendicular bisector (axis of symmetry).
Measurement: $AM = MB = \frac{7.3}{2} = \mathbf{3.65\text{ cm}}$.

NCERT Exercise 8.2: Perpendiculars & Angle Constructions
Q3
Draw an angle of measure 80° using a protractor and construct its angle bisector using a compass.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Steps of Construction:
1. Draw a ray $OA$. Using a protractor, draw ray $OB$ such that $\angle AOB = 80^\circ$.
2. With $O$ as centre and any convenient radius, draw an arc cutting ray $OA$ at $P$ and ray $OB$ at $Q$.
3. With $P$ as centre and radius more than $\frac{1}{2}PQ$, draw an arc inside the angle.
4. With $Q$ as centre and the same radius, draw another arc intersecting the previous arc at point $R$.
5. Join $O$ and $R$ and produce it to form ray $OR$.
Ray $OR$ is the bisector of $\angle AOB$.
Verification: $\angle AOR = \angle ROB = \frac{80^\circ}{2} = \mathbf{40^\circ}$.

Q4
Construct an angle of 60° using only a ruler and a compass. Explain the geometric rationale for why this angle is exactly 60°.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Steps of Construction:
1. Draw a straight line and mark a point $O$ on it, with ray $OA$.
2. With $O$ as centre and any convenient radius $r$, draw an arc cutting ray $OA$ at point $P$.
3. With $P$ as centre and the same radius $r$, draw an arc cutting the first arc at point $Q$.
4. Join $O$ and $Q$ and extend it to form ray $OB$.
Angle $\angle AOB$ is exactly 60°.
Geometric Rationale: Points $O, P, Q$ form an equilateral triangle because $OP = OQ = PQ = r$. In an equilateral triangle, each interior angle measures $180^\circ / 3 = 60^\circ$.

Q5
Construct an angle of 90° at the initial point of a given ray using only a ruler and compass. Then construct its bisector to obtain a 45° angle.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Steps of Construction:
1. Draw ray $OA$. With $O$ as centre, draw a semicircle cutting $OA$ at $X$.
2. With $X$ as centre and same radius, cut the arc at $P$ (60°).
3. With $P$ as centre and same radius, cut the arc at $Q$ (120°).
4. From $P$ and $Q$, draw arcs of equal radius intersecting each other at $R$.
5. Join $OR$. Line $OR$ is perpendicular to $OA$ ($\angle AOR = \mathbf{90^\circ}$).
6. To obtain 45°, bisect $\angle AOR$: with $X$ and the intersection point on $OR$ as centres, draw intersecting arcs and join to $O$. Resulting angle = $\frac{90^\circ}{2} = \mathbf{45^\circ}$.

Q6
Explain how you would construct an angle of 30° using only a ruler and compass.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
1. Draw ray $OA$.
2. Construct an angle of 60° ($\angle AOB$) using ruler and compass as described in Q4.
3. Draw the angle bisector of $\angle AOB$ by taking arcs from points on arms $OA$ and $OB$.
4. The bisector ray divides 60° into two equal angles of $\frac{60^\circ}{2} = \mathbf{30^\circ}$.

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Pergola Pergola Blueprint)
Case Study: Botanical Garden Pergola Blueprint:
A landscape architect is designing a semi-circular wooden pergola for a school botanical garden.
(a) The foundation requires two support pillars placed at endpoints A and B of a diameter of length 8 m. What radius must be used for the semi-circular arc?
(b) A central decorative arch must stand along the perpendicular bisector of AB. Describe the geometric steps to establish this axis on the ground using two equal measuring ropes.
(c) What angle does the central pillar make with ground line AB?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

(a) Radius Required:
$\text{Radius} = \frac{\text{Diameter}}{2} = \frac{8\text{ m}}{2} = \mathbf{4\text{ metres}}$.

(b) Ground Perpendicular Bisector Construction:
1. Fix pegs at endpoints $A$ and $B$.
2. Take two equal ropes of length greater than 4 m (e.g., 6 m). Anchor one at $A$ and the other at $B$.
3. Pull the ropes taut so their ends meet at a point $P$ on one side of $AB$, and peg point $P$.
4. Repeat on the other side to establish point $Q$.
5. The straight line string between $P$ and $Q$ defines the exact perpendicular bisector axis.

(c) Angle with Ground Line:
The central axis makes an angle of exactly 90° (Right Angle) with ground line $AB$.
