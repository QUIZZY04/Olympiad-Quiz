Chapter 2: Lines and Angles

NCERT Ganita Prakash (Class 6) — Points, Lines, Rays, Angle Classifications, Clock Revolutions, Protractor Measurement, Linear Pairs & Intersecting Lines | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Geometric Concepts & Angle Classifications
  • Point, Line, Ray & Line Segment: A point marks a fixed position with zero dimensions. A line extends infinitely in both directions ($overleftrightarrow{AB}$). A line segment has two fixed endpoints ($overline{AB}$). A ray has one initial point and extends indefinitely in one direction ($ ec{AB}$).
  • Angle Classifications:
    • Acute Angle: Greater than 0° and less than 90°.
    • Right Angle: Exactly equal to 90° ($\frac{1}{4}$ of a full revolution).
    • Obtuse Angle: Greater than 90° and less than 180°.
    • Straight Angle: Exactly equal to 180° ($\frac{1}{2}$ of a full revolution).
    • Reflex Angle: Greater than 180° and less than 360°.
    • Complete Angle: Exactly equal to 360° (1 full revolution).
  • Complementary & Supplementary Angles: Two angles are complementary if their sum is 90°. Two angles are supplementary if their sum is 180°.
  • Linear Pair & Vertically Opposite Angles: When two lines intersect, adjacent angles forming a straight line add to 180° (linear pair), and vertically opposite angles are equal.

NCERT Exercise 2.1: Angle Types & Identification
Q1
Classify each of the following angles into acute, obtuse, right, straight, or reflex:
(i) 35°    (ii) 90°    (iii) 142°    (iv) 180°    (v) 245°    (vi) 89°
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(i) 35°: Since 35° < 90°, it is an Acute Angle.
(ii) 90°: Exactly 90°, it is a Right Angle.
(iii) 142°: Since 90° < 142° < 180°, it is an Obtuse Angle.
(iv) 180°: Exactly 180°, it is a Straight Angle.
(v) 245°: Since 180° < 245° < 360°, it is a Reflex Angle.
(vi) 89°: Since 89° < 90°, it is an Acute Angle.

NCERT Exercise 2.2: Clock Angles & Direction Revolutions
Q2
What fraction of a clockwise revolution does the hour hand of a clock turn through, when it goes from:
(a) 3 to 9
(b) 4 to 7
(c) 7 to 10
(d) 12 to 9
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Formula: A clock has 12 equal hours. 1 full revolution = 12 hours = 360°.
Fraction of revolution = $\frac{\text{Number of hours passed}}{12}$.
(a) From 3 to 9: Hours passed = $9 - 3 = 6$. Fraction = $\frac{6}{12} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{2}}$ revolution (180°).
(b) From 4 to 7: Hours passed = $7 - 4 = 3$. Fraction = $\frac{3}{12} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{4}}$ revolution (90°).
(c) From 7 to 10: Hours passed = $10 - 7 = 3$. Fraction = $\frac{3}{12} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{4}}$ revolution (90°).
(d) From 12 to 9 (clockwise): Hours passed = 9. Fraction = $\frac{9}{12} = \mathbf{\frac{3}{4}}$ revolution (270°).

Q3
Where will the hand of a clock stop if it:
(a) Starts at 12 and makes 1/2 of a revolution, clockwise?
(b) Starts at 2 and makes 1/2 of a revolution, clockwise?
(c) Starts at 5 and makes 1/4 of a revolution, clockwise?
(d) Starts at 8 and makes 3/4 of a revolution, clockwise?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Starts at 12, 1/2 revolution:
Hours moved = $\frac{1}{2} \times 12 = 6$ hours. $12 + 6 = 18 \equiv 6$. Stops at 6.
(b) Starts at 2, 1/2 revolution:
Hours moved = $\frac{1}{2} \times 12 = 6$ hours. $2 + 6 = 8$. Stops at 8.
(c) Starts at 5, 1/4 revolution:
Hours moved = $\frac{1}{4} \times 12 = 3$ hours. $5 + 3 = 8$. Stops at 8.
(d) Starts at 8, 3/4 revolution:
Hours moved = $\frac{3}{4} \times 12 = 9$ hours. $8 + 9 = 17 \equiv 5$. Stops at 5.

Each correct position with clear calculation0.75 Mark each (Total 3 Marks)
Q4
In Figure 2.3, two lines AB and CD intersect at point O. If ∠AOC = 50°, find the measures of ∠BOD, ∠AOD, and ∠BOC. Give reasons for each step.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Step 1: Vertically Opposite Angle:
$\angle BOD$ and $\angle AOC$ are vertically opposite angles.
Therefore, $\angle BOD = \angle AOC = \mathbf{50^\circ}$.
Step 2: Linear Pair with ∠AOC:
$AB$ is a straight line, so ray $OC$ stands on it.
$\angle AOC + \angle BOC = 180^\circ$ (Linear pair property).
$50^\circ + \angle BOC = 180^\circ \implies \angle BOC = 180^\circ - 50^\circ = \mathbf{130^\circ}$.
Step 3: Vertically Opposite Angle for ∠AOD:
$\angle AOD = \angle BOC = \mathbf{130^\circ}$ (Vertically opposite angles).
Summary: $\angle BOD = 50^\circ$, $\angle AOD = 130^\circ$, $\angle BOC = 130^\circ$.

Q5
Explain the step-by-step procedure to measure an angle ∠PQR using a protractor. Also describe how to avoid parallax error during reading.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Procedure for Protractor Measurement:
1. Place the centre point of the protractor directly on the vertex $Q$ of the angle.
2. Adjust the protractor so that the base line (zero-edge) lies precisely along one arm, $QR$.
3. Look at the scale where the zero mark coincides with arm $QR$.
4. Read the mark on that scale where the second arm, $QP$, crosses the curved edge.
Avoiding Parallax Error: Position your eye directly perpendicular above the scale reading mark rather than viewing at an angle.

Q6
(a) Find the complement of: (i) 28°    (ii) 63°
(b) Find the supplement of: (i) 105°    (ii) 82°
(c) An angle is equal to its supplement. Find the measure of this angle.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Complementary Angles (Sum = 90°):
(i) Complement of 28° = $90^\circ - 28^\circ = \mathbf{62^\circ}$.
(ii) Complement of 63° = $90^\circ - 63^\circ = \mathbf{27^\circ}$.
(b) Supplementary Angles (Sum = 180°):
(i) Supplement of 105° = $180^\circ - 105^\circ = \mathbf{75^\circ}$.
(ii) Supplement of 82° = $180^\circ - 82^\circ = \mathbf{98^\circ}$.
(c) Angle equal to its supplement:
Let the angle be $x$. $x + x = 180^\circ \implies 2x = 180^\circ \implies x = \mathbf{90^\circ}$.

Q7
You are standing facing North.
(a) If you turn clockwise to face East, what fraction of a revolution have you turned? What angle is this?
(b) If you turn anti-clockwise from North to South, what angle have you turned?
(c) If you face West and turn 270° clockwise, which direction do you face?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) North to East (Clockwise):
Turn = 1 right turn = $90^\circ$. Fraction = $\frac{90}{360} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{4}}$ revolution.
(b) North to South (Anti-clockwise):
North to South is a straight half turn = $\mathbf{180^\circ}$ (Straight angle = $\frac{1}{2}$ revolution).
(c) West turning 270° clockwise:
- West to North = 90°
- North to East = 90° (cumulative 180°)
- East to South = 90° (cumulative 270°)
Therefore, you will be facing South.

Q8
Two angles form a linear pair. If one angle is 36° more than the other angle, find the measure of both angles.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Let the smaller angle be $x$.
Then the larger angle is $(x + 36^\circ)$.
Since they form a linear pair, their sum is $180^\circ$:
$x + (x + 36^\circ) = 180^\circ$
$2x + 36^\circ = 180^\circ \implies 2x = 180^\circ - 36^\circ = 144^\circ$
$x = \frac{144^\circ}{2} = 72^\circ$.
Larger angle = $72^\circ + 36^\circ = 108^\circ$.
Answer: The two angles are 72° and 108°.

Q9
Four rays meet at a common point O, forming four angles: ∠AOB = 110°, ∠BOC = 80°, ∠COD = 95°, and ∠DOA = x. Find the value of x. Give the geometrical property used.
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Geometrical Property: The sum of all angles formed around a single point equals a complete angle ($360^\circ$).
$\angle AOB + \angle BOC + \angle COD + \angle DOA = 360^\circ$
$110^\circ + 80^\circ + 95^\circ + x = 360^\circ$
$285^\circ + x = 360^\circ \implies x = 360^\circ - 285^\circ = \mathbf{75^\circ}$.

Q11
Identify the types of angles formed by the hour hand and minute hand of a clock at the following exact times:
(a) 3:00    (b) 6:00    (c) 1:00    (d) 5:00
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Each 1-hour interval on a clock represents $\frac{360^\circ}{12} = 30^\circ$.
(a) At 3:00: Hands are 3 hours apart: $3 \times 30^\circ = 90^\circ$ $\implies$ Right Angle.
(b) At 6:00: Hands are 6 hours apart: $6 \times 30^\circ = 180^\circ$ $\implies$ Straight Angle.
(c) At 1:00: Hands are 1 hour apart: $1 \times 30^\circ = 30^\circ$ $\implies$ Acute Angle.
(d) At 5:00: Hands are 5 hours apart: $5 \times 30^\circ = 150^\circ$ $\implies$ Obtuse Angle.

Q12
Define the following geometric terms with an illustrative sketch:
(i) Collinear Points
(ii) Concurrent Lines
[2 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(i) Collinear Points: Three or more points that lie on the same straight line are called collinear points. If no single straight line can pass through all of them, they are non-collinear.
(ii) Concurrent Lines: Three or more lines that intersect at a single common point are called concurrent lines. The common point of intersection is called the point of concurrence.

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Real-World Application)
Case Study: Ship Navigation Compass & Lighthouse Bearings:
A mariner's magnetic compass has four cardinal directions (North, South, East, West) and four intercardinal directions (NE, SE, SW, NW).
(a) A ship sails facing North. What angle does the ship turn through if it turns clockwise to face South-East (SE)? What type of angle is this?
(b) If the ship is initially facing West and turns anti-clockwise through 270°, in which direction is it now facing?
(c) Two ships leave harbour along bearings of North-East and South-East. What is the angle between their routes?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

(a) North to South-East (Clockwise):
- North to East = 90°
- East to South-East = 45°
- Total angle = $90^\circ + 45^\circ = \mathbf{135^\circ}$.
Since $90^\circ < 135^\circ < 180^\circ$, it is an Obtuse Angle.

(b) West turning anti-clockwise through 270°:
- West to South = 90°
- South to East = 90° (cumulative 180°)
- East to North = 90° (cumulative 270°)
The ship is now facing North.

(c) Angle between NE and SE routes:
NE is 45° north of East. SE is 45° south of East.
Angle = $45^\circ + 45^\circ = \mathbf{90^\circ}$ (A Right Angle).
