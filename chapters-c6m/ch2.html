📌 Key Geometric Concepts & Angle Classifications
  • Point, Line, Ray & Line Segment: A point marks a position. A line extends infinitely in both directions (line AB, denoted with a double arrow ⟷). A line segment has two fixed endpoints (segment AB). A ray has one initial point and extends infinitely in one direction (ray AB, denoted with an arrow →).
  • Angle Classifications:
    • Acute Angle: Greater than 0° and less than 90°.
    • Right Angle: Exactly equal to 90° (1/4 of a full revolution).
    • Obtuse Angle: Greater than 90° and less than 180°.
    • Straight Angle: Exactly equal to 180° (1/2 of a full revolution).
    • Reflex Angle: Greater than 180° and less than 360°.
    • Complete Angle: Exactly equal to 360° (1 full revolution).
  • Clock Revolutions: 1 hour on a clock face represents 360°/12 = 30°. Moving 3 hours represents 90° (a right angle), and moving 6 hours represents 180° (a straight angle).

NCERT Exercise 2.1: Angle Types & Identification
Q1
Classify each of the following ∠s into acute, obtuse, right, straight, or reflex:
(i) 35°    (ii) 90°    (iii) 142°    (iv) 180°    (v) 245°    (vi) 89°
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Standard Definitions: An angle θ is acute if 0° < θ < 90°; right if θ = 90°; obtuse if 90° < θ < 180°; straight if θ = 180°; reflex if 180° < θ < 360°.

(i) 35°: Since 35° < 90°, it is an Acute Angle.
(ii) 90°: Exactly 90°, it is a Right Angle.
(iii) 142°: Since 90° < 142° < 180°, it is an Obtuse Angle.
(iv) 180°: Exactly 180°, it is a Straight Angle.
(v) 245°: Since 180° < 245° < 360°, it is a Reflex Angle.
(vi) 89°: Since 89° < 90°, it is an Acute Angle.

NCERT Exercise 2.2: Clock Angles & Direction Revolutions
Q2
What fraction of a clockwise revolution does the hour hand of a clock turn through, when it goes from:
(a) 3 to 9
(b) 4 to 7
(c) 7 to 10
(d) 12 to 9
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Rule: A clock has 12 equal hours. 1 full revolution = 12 hours = 360°.
Fraction of revolution = Number of hours passed/12.

(a) From 3 to 9:
Hours passed = 9 - 3 = 6 hours.
Fraction = 6/12 = 1/2 revolution (Straight angle = 180°).
(b) From 4 to 7:
Hours passed = 7 - 4 = 3 hours.
Fraction = 3/12 = 1/4 revolution (Right angle = 90°).
(c) From 7 to 10:
Hours passed = 10 - 7 = 3 hours.
Fraction = 3/12 = 1/4 revolution (Right angle = 90°).
(d) From 12 to 9 (clockwise):
Hours passed = 9 hours.
Fraction = 9/12 = 3/4 revolution (Reflex angle = 270°).

Q3
Where will the hand of a clock stop if it:
(a) Starts at 12 and makes 1/2 of a revolution, clockwise?
(b) Starts at 2 and makes 1/2 of a revolution, clockwise?
(c) Starts at 5 and makes 1/4 of a revolution, clockwise?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Starts at 12, 1/2 revolution:
Hours moved = 1/2 × 12 = 6 hours.
Starting at 12, moving 6 hours: 12 + 6 ⇒ the hand stops at 6.
(b) Starts at 2, 1/2 revolution:
Hours moved = 1/2 × 12 = 6 hours.
Starting at 2, moving 6 hours: 2 + 6 = 8 ⇒ the hand stops at 8.
(c) Starts at 5, 1/4 revolution:
Hours moved = 1/4 × 12 = 3 hours.
Starting at 5, moving 3 hours: 5 + 3 = 8 ⇒ the hand stops at 8.

Q4
In Figure 2.3, two lines AB and CD intersect at point O. If ∠AOC = 50°, find the measures of ∠BOD, ∠AOD, and ∠BOC. Give reasons for each step.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Figure 2.3: Intersecting Lines AB and CD with Vertically Opposite Angles
Step 1: Vertically Opposite Angle:
∠BOD and ∠AOC are vertically opposite ∠s.
Since vertically opposite ∠s are equal:
∠BOD = ∠AOC = 50°
Step 2: Linear Pair with ∠AOC:
AB is a straight line, so ray OC stands on it.
∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180° (Linear pair property)
50° + ∠BOC = 180°
∠BOC = 180° - 50° = 130°
Step 3: Linear Pair or Vertically Opposite Angle for ∠AOD:
∠AOD and ∠BOC are vertically opposite ∠s.
∠AOD = ∠BOC = 130°

Final Answers: ∠BOD = 50°, ∠AOD = 130°, and ∠BOC = 130°.

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Real-World Application)
Case Study: Ship Navigation Compass:
A mariner's magnetic compass has four cardinal directions (North, South, East, West) and four intercardinal directions (NE, SE, SW, NW).
(a) A ship sails facing North. What ∠does the ship turn through if it turns clockwise to face South-East (SE)? What type of ∠is this?
(b) If the ship is initially facing West and turns anti-clockwise through 270°, in which direction is it now facing?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

(a) North to South-East (Clockwise):
- North to East = 90°.
- East to South-East = 45°.
- Total angle = 90° + 45° = 135°.
Since 90° < 135° < 180°, it is an Obtuse Angle.

(b) West turning anti-clockwise through 270°:
- West to South = 90° (anti-clockwise).
- South to East = 90° (cumulative 180°).
- East to North = 90° (cumulative 270°).
Therefore, after turning 270° anti-clockwise from West, the ship is facing North.
