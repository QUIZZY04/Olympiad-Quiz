Chapter 3: Playing with Numbers

NCERT Mathematics (Classic) (Class 6) — Factors & Multiples, Prime & Composite Numbers, Divisibility Rules (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11), Prime Factorization, Highest Common Factor (HCF), Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Factor: An exact divisor of that number. Multiple: Number obtained by multiplying with an integer.
  • Prime: Exactly 2 factors (1 and itself). 2 is the only even prime number.
  • Divisibility Rules: 3 & 9 (sum of digits), 4 (last 2 digits), 8 (last 3 digits), 11 (difference of alternating sums is 0 or multiple of 11).
  • HCF (GCD): Greatest common factor. LCM: Lowest common multiple.
  • Relationship: Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM.
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Test the divisibility of the following numbers by 11: (a) 5445 (b) 10824 (c) 7138965.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Divisibility Test by 11:
• (a) 5445: Sum of odd digits = 5 + 4 = 9. Sum of even digits = 4 + 5 = 9. Difference = 9 - 9 = 0. Divisible by 11.
• (b) 10824: Sum of odd digits = 4 + 8 + 1 = 13. Sum of even digits = 2 + 0 = 2. Difference = 13 - 2 = 11. Divisible by 11.
• (c) 7138965: Sum of odd digits = 5 + 9 + 3 + 7 = 24. Sum of even digits = 6 + 8 + 1 = 15. Difference = 24 - 15 = 9 (not 0 or multiple of 11). Not divisible by 11.

Q2
Find the HCF of the following numbers: (a) 18, 48 (b) 30, 42 (c) 70, 105, 175.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Prime Factorization for HCF:
• (a) 18 = 2 × 3²; 48 = 2⁴ × 3. HCF = 2 × 3 = 6.
• (b) 30 = 2 × 3 × 5; 42 = 2 × 3 × 7. HCF = 2 × 3 = 6.
• (c) 70 = 2 × 5 × 7; 105 = 3 × 5 × 7; 175 = 5² × 7. HCF = 5 × 7 = 35.

Q3
Find the LCM of: (a) 12, 16, 24, 36 (b) 20, 25, 30.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Common Division Method for LCM:
• (a) 12, 16, 24, 36: Divide by 2: 6, 8, 12, 18 -> Divide by 2: 3, 4, 6, 9 -> Divide by 2: 3, 2, 3, 9 -> Divide by 3: 1, 2, 1, 3. LCM = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 = 144.
• (b) 20, 25, 30: Divide by 2: 10, 25, 15 -> Divide by 3: 10, 25, 5 -> Divide by 5: 2, 5, 1. LCM = 2 × 3 × 5 × 2 × 5 = 300.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27

Correct division grid for (b) = 3001.5 Marks
Q4
Three traffic lights at three different road crossings change after every 48 seconds, 72 seconds and 108 seconds respectively. If they change simultaneously at 7 a.m., at what time will they change simultaneously again?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
LCM Application in Synchronized Events:
• Required interval = LCM(48, 72, 108).
• 48 = 2⁴ × 3; 72 = 2³ × 3²; 108 = 2² × 3³.
• LCM = 2⁴ × 3³ = 16 × 27 = 432 seconds.
• 432 seconds = 432 ÷ 60 = 7 minutes 12 seconds.
• Therefore, the lights will change simultaneously at 7:07:12 a.m.

Q5
Two tankers contain 850 litres and 680 litres of kerosene oil respectively. Find the maximum capacity of a container which can measure the kerosene oil of both the tankers when used an exact number of times.
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
HCF Application in Measurement:
• Maximum capacity = HCF(850, 680).
• 850 = 2 × 5² × 17.
• 680 = 2³ × 5 × 17.
• Common factors = 2 × 5 × 17 = 170 litres.
The maximum capacity of the container must be 170 litres.

CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
Warehouse Inventory Logistics: Tile Tiling & Packaging:
A storage warehouse floor measures 360 cm by 240 cm. (a) Find the size of the largest square tile that can pave the floor without cutting. (b) Find the minimum number of such square tiles required.
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Largest Tile Size: HCF(360, 240) = 120 cm × 120 cm.
(b) Number of Tiles: Total Area / Area of 1 tile = (360 × 240) / (120 × 120) = 3 × 2 = 6 square tiles.
