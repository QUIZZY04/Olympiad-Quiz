📌 Key Fraction Concepts & Properties
  • Fraction: A number representing a part of a whole. Written as $ rac{a}{b}$, where $a$ is the numerator and $b$ is the denominator ($b e 0$).
  • Types of Fractions:
    • Proper Fraction: Numerator < Denominator (e.g., $ rac{3}{5}$). Value < 1.
    • Improper Fraction: Numerator $ge$ Denominator (e.g., $ rac{7}{4}$). Value $ge$ 1.
    • Mixed Fraction: Combination of a whole number and a proper fraction (e.g., $1 rac{3}{4}$).
  • Equivalent Fractions: Fractions having identical value, obtained by multiplying or dividing numerator and denominator by the same non-zero integer: $ rac{a}{b} = rac{a imes k}{b imes k}$.
  • Simplest Form: When numerator and denominator have no common factor other than 1 (HCF = 1).

NCERT Exercise 7.1: Equivalent Fractions & Number Line
Q1
Find the equivalent fraction of $ rac{3}{5}$ having:
(a) Denominator 20
(b) Numerator 9
(c) Denominator 30
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution

Rule: To obtain an equivalent fraction, multiply both numerator and denominator by the same integer factor $k$.

(a) Denominator 20:
To convert denominator 5 to 20, multiply by $4$ ($20 div 5 = 4$):
$$ rac{3}{5} = rac{3 imes 4}{5 imes 4} = mathbf{ rac{12}{20}}$$
(b) Numerator 9:
To convert numerator 3 to 9, multiply by $3$ ($9 div 3 = 3$):
$$ rac{3}{5} = rac{3 imes 3}{5 imes 3} = mathbf{ rac{9}{15}}$$
(c) Denominator 30:
To convert denominator 5 to 30, multiply by $6$ ($30 div 5 = 6$):
$$ rac{3}{5} = rac{3 imes 6}{5 imes 6} = mathbf{ rac{18}{30}}$$

Q2
Compare the fractions using $<, >,$ or $=$:
(i) $ rac{3}{6} ext{ and } rac{5}{6}$
(ii) $ rac{1}{7} ext{ and } rac{1}{4}$
(iii) $ rac{4}{5} ext{ and } rac{5}{6}$
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(i) $ rac{3}{6} ext{ and } rac{5}{6}$:
These are like fractions (same denominator 6). Compare numerators: $3 < 5$.
$$mathbf{ rac{3}{6} < rac{5}{6}}$$
(ii) $ rac{1}{7} ext{ and } rac{1}{4}$:
Fractions with the same numerator 1: The fraction with the smaller denominator is larger.
Since $7 > 4$, $ rac{1}{7} < rac{1}{4}$.
$$mathbf{ rac{1}{7} < rac{1}{4}}$$
(iii) $ rac{4}{5} ext{ and } rac{5}{6}$:
Convert unlike fractions to common denominator $ ext{LCM}(5, 6) = 30$:
$ rac{4}{5} = rac{4 imes 6}{5 imes 6} = rac{24}{30}$
$ rac{5}{6} = rac{5 imes 5}{6 imes 5} = rac{25}{30}$
Since $24 < 25$,
$$mathbf{ rac{4}{5} < rac{5}{6}}$$

Q3
Evaluate the following fraction operations:
(a) $ rac{5}{8} + rac{1}{8}$
(b) $ rac{5}{6} - rac{1}{4}$
(c) $2 rac{1}{5} + 3 rac{1}{2}$
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
(a) Like Fractions Addition:
$$ rac{5}{8} + rac{1}{8} = rac{5 + 1}{8} = rac{6}{8} = mathbf{ rac{3}{4}} ext{ (in simplest form)}$$
(b) Unlike Fractions Subtraction:
$ ext{LCM}(6, 4) = 12$.
$ rac{5}{6} = rac{5 imes 2}{6 imes 2} = rac{10}{12}$
$ rac{1}{4} = rac{1 imes 3}{4 imes 3} = rac{3}{12}$
$$ rac{10}{12} - rac{3}{12} = mathbf{ rac{7}{12}}$$
(c) Mixed Fractions Addition:
Convert to improper fractions:
$2 rac{1}{5} = rac{11}{5}$,   $3 rac{1}{2} = rac{7}{2}$.
$ ext{LCM}(5, 2) = 10$.
$ rac{11}{5} = rac{22}{10}$,   $ rac{7}{2} = rac{35}{10}$.
$$ ext{Sum} = rac{22 + 35}{10} = rac{57}{10} = mathbf{5 rac{7}{10}}$$

CBSE Competency-Based Question (Daily Life Measurement)
Case Study: Recipe Ingredient Fraction Balancing:
Sarita bought $ rac{2}{5} ext{ metre}$ of ribbon and Lalita bought $ rac{3}{4} ext{ metre}$ of ribbon. What is the total length of ribbon they bought together? Who bought more ribbon, and by how much?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Marking Scheme

Step 1: Total Length of Ribbon Bought:
$$ ext{Total} = rac{2}{5} + rac{3}{4}$$ $ ext{LCM}(5, 4) = 20$.
$ rac{2}{5} = rac{8}{20}$,   $ rac{3}{4} = rac{15}{20}$.
$$ ext{Total} = rac{8 + 15}{20} = rac{23}{20} ext{ m} = mathbf{1 rac{3}{20} ext{ metre}}$$

Step 2: Comparison & Difference:
Since $ rac{15}{20} > rac{8}{20}$, Lalita bought more ribbon.
$$ ext{Difference} = rac{15}{20} - rac{8}{20} = mathbf{ rac{7}{20} ext{ metre}}.$$
