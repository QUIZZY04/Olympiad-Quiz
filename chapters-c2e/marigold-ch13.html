Chapter 13: On My Blackboard I Can Draw

Artistic Drawing, Rhyming Numbers, House Architecture & Nature | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem recitation and sequential counting from 1 to 6.
  • The drawing elements: 1 little house, 2 green doors, 3 red windows, 4 chimneys, 5 steps, 6 yellow marigolds.
  • Developing spatial imagination and architectural drawing skills.
  • Color recognition and descriptive adjectives in poetic form.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Sequential Items
What does the child draw on his blackboard for numbers 1, 2, and 3?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Number 1: One little house.
• Number 2: Two green doors that open wide.
• Number 3: Three red windows shining bright.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
1 little house 1 M
2 green doors 1 M
3 red windows 1 M
Q2 Reading is Fun • Sequential Items Part 2
What does the child draw for numbers 4, 5, and 6?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Number 4: Four little chimneys painted white.
• Number 5: Five little steps leading inside.
• Number 6: Six yellow marigolds straight against the wall.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
4 chimneys 1 M
5 steps 1 M
6 yellow marigolds 1 M
Q3 Color Identification in Poem
Match each architectural item to its color in the poem:
(a) Doors (b) Windows (c) Chimneys (d) Marigolds
Colors: (1) Red (2) White (3) Green (4) Yellow
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Matching:
• (a) Doors — (3) Green
• (b) Windows — (1) Red
• (c) Chimneys — (2) White
• (d) Marigolds — (4) Yellow
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 colors correctly matched 4 M (1 M each)

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:

(c) Wall — Tall / All

Q5 Vocabulary • Chimney Function
What is a chimney on a house roof used for?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• A chimney is a vertical pipe that carries smoke, soot, and gases safely out from a fireplace or kitchen stove through the roof into the air.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Carries smoke from fireplace/stove 1 M
Exhausts outside roof safely 1 M

Q8 Flowers in Nature • Marigold
Why are marigolds (Genda phool) special in Indian homes?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Marigolds are bright golden-orange flowers with a fresh fragrance.
• They are used to make garlands for festival celebrations (like Diwali), weddings, and decorating front doorways (Toran).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Golden fragrance described 1 M
Festivals / garlands / Toran decoration 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Classroom Blackboard Art Challenge

During art class, Anuj took colourful chalks to the blackboard. Following the poem 'On My Blackboard I Can Draw', he drew: 1 cozy house, 2 green doors opening wide, 3 red windows, 4 smoking chimneys, 5 steps leading inside, and 6 golden yellow marigolds growing straight against the brick wall.

Analytical Task: How many doors, windows, and chimneys did Anuj draw? What flower was planted against the wall, and what colour were they?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Doors: 2 green doors. Windows: 3 red windows. Chimneys: 4 white/smoking chimneys.
2. Flowers: Marigolds, and they were golden yellow, standing straight against the wall.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 2 doors, 3 windows, 4 chimneys + 1.5 M for 6 yellow marigolds
