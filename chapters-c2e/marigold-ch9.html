Chapter 9: Zoo Manners

Etiquette at the Zoo, Respecting Wildlife & Gentle Observation | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem appreciation: 'Be careful what you say or do when you visit the animals at the zoo.'
  • Treating animals with dignity: not mocking the camel's proud hump or chimpanzee's wise smile.
  • The noble penguins strutting around the lake, understanding remarks.
  • Rules of visiting a zoo: no feeding, no teasing, no shouting, maintaining distance.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion

What does the poet advise us to be careful about when visiting the zoo?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The poet warns us to be very careful what we say or do when we visit the animals at the zoo.

Why should we not make fun of the camel's hump?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:

Q8 Zoo Etiquette Rules
List three important rules that every visitor must follow inside a zoo.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Zoo Rules:
1. Do not feed outside snacks to the animals.
2. Do not throw stones, tap glass, or tease the caged animals.
3. Keep the premises clean and dispose of all trash in litter bins.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Q9 Animal Adaptations • Camel Hump
Why do camels have humps on their backs?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• A camel's hump stores fat tissue.
• When walking across hot, waterless deserts for days, the camel converts this fat into energy and moisture, earning it the title 'Ship of the Desert'.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Fat storage explained 1 M
Energy in desert / Ship of the Desert 1 M

Q12 Animal Facts • Penguins
Can penguins fly in the air like parrots? How do they move in water?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• No, penguins cannot fly in the air.
• Their wings have evolved into sleek, paddle-like flippers that allow them to 'fly' underwater with astonishing speed to catch fish.

Q14 True or False
State True or False:
(a) We should shout loudly and tap on glass cages to scare zoo animals.
(b) Animals feel emotions like fear, affection, and pride.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) False. Tapping glass scares animals and is strictly prohibited in zoos.
(b) True. Animals are sentient living beings that feel feelings.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Class 2 Field Trip to National Zoological Park

Class 2 visited the National Zoological Park with their science teacher. Outside the gate, the teacher read the visitor rules: 'Do not throw plastic, do not feed bread to deer, and do not make teasing faces at monkeys.' Inside, the children watched tall camels walking proudly, and black-and-white penguins strutting gracefully around the pool.

Analytical Task: What warnings does the poem 'Zoo Manners' give about camels and penguins? Why must visitors never tease zoo animals?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Camel warning: Do not laugh at his hump, for he is proud of his noble bump.
2. Penguin warning: The penguins strutting around the lake are noble and understand every remark you make.
3. Why no teasing: Teasing stresses wild animals, makes them aggressive or unhappy, and spoils their peace. Respectful behavior ensures a safe, welcoming zoo for all.
Rubric: 1 M for camel's noble hump + 1 M for penguin's understanding + 1 M for why teasing is harmful
