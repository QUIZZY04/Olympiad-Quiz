NCERT Class 2 English • Classic CBSE

Chapter 3: I am Lucky!

Poem of Gratitude, Animal Gifts & Being Thankful to Nature | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem appreciation: Celebrating what makes each creature unique and joyful.
  • Creature gifts: Butterfly's wings, Myna's song, Fish's wriggle, Elephant's trunk, Kangaroo's hop, Octopus's eight arms.
  • Self-acceptance: Being thankful and happy for who 'I am' rather than wishing to be someone else.
  • Action verbs and sound words (flutter, sing, wriggle, raise trunk, hop).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Animal Gifts
Why does the poet say a butterfly is lucky?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The butterfly is lucky because it has beautiful, delicate wings to flutter from flower to flower.

Wings to fly identified 1 M

Q15 Poem Reflection
What is the core message of the poem 'I am Lucky!'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:
• Rather than feeling envious of others, we should count our blessings, appreciate nature's diversity, and feel proud and happy with our own unique identity.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Self-worth and gratitude emphasized 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Assembly Recitation on Self-Acceptance

During school assembly, Simi recited 'I am Lucky!'. She pointed out that a butterfly is thankful for its wings, a myna is thankful it can sing, and an octopus is thankful for its eight arms. At the end, Simi smiled and said: 'I am lucky because I am ME, not you, but just ME!'

Analytical Task: Why is the butterfly thankful? Why is the octopus thankful? What is the main message Simi shared?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The butterfly is thankful for its colorful wings.
2. The octopus is thankful for its eight strong arms.
3. Main message: Be proud and grateful for who you are. Every child has unique talents and should celebrate being themselves.
Rubric: 1 M for butterfly + 1 M for octopus + 1 M for self-acceptance message
