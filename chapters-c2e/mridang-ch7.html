Chapter 7: This is My Town

Community Life, Clean Streets, Library, Market, Park & Helpers | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem comprehension: Celebrating one's hometown—wide roads, bright markets, quiet library, and leafy parks.
  • Civic amenities: Hospital, post office, fire station, school, municipal garden.
  • Cleanliness & civic duty: Keeping public streets clean and throwing waste in trash cans.
  • Rhyming pairs: Town-brown, street-meet, park-bark, clean-green.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Poem Comprehension • The Town
What makes the town look pleasant and inviting in the poem 'This is My Town'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The town looks pleasant with its wide, clean streets, friendly neighbours, shady trees, and cheerful marketplace.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clean streets, trees, and markets 2 M
Q2 Civic Places • Reading Books
Where do children and adults go in town to borrow and read interesting books?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• They go to the town library.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Library 1 M
Q3 Emergency Helpers • Hospitals
Where do people rush when someone suddenly falls ill?
[1.5 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• They rush to the hospital or health clinic where doctors and nurses provide care.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hospital / clinic 1.5 M
Q4 Matching Exercise • Places and Services
Match each place with its role:
(a) Fire Station — (1) Teaches children
(b) School — (2) Puts out dangerous fires
(c) Market — (3) Sells vegetables and groceries
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Fire Station — (2) Puts out fires
(b) School — (1) Teaches children
(c) Market — (3) Sells groceries
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All three matched accurately 3 M
Q5 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in with words (dustbin, park, street):
(a) We play cricket in the town ______.
(b) Always throw chocolate wrappers into the ______.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) We play cricket in the town park.
(b) Always throw chocolate wrappers into the dustbin.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Park and Dustbin 2 M
Q6 Rhyming Words • Sound Identification
Find rhyming words for:
(a) Town — _______
(b) Clean — _______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Town — Brown / Down / Crown
(b) Clean — Green / Seen / Bean
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Brown/Down and Green/Seen 2 M
Q7 Vocabulary • City vs Village
Give one difference between a big city and a quiet village.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• A big city has tall buildings, heavy traffic, and shopping malls, while a village has open green fields, fewer vehicles, and fresh breeze.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear comparison of traffic and nature 2 M
Q8 Sentence Writing • My Town
Make a sentence using the word: 'Neighbour'.
[1.5 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• My neighbour Mr. Sharma always shares sweet mangoes from his orchard.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Meaningful sentence 1.5 M
Q9 Civic Responsibility • Cleanliness
What is our duty towards public parks and streets?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• It is our duty never to litter, avoid plucking flowers, and keep park benches clean for everyone.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
No littering, protecting flowers/benches 2 M
Q10 Opposites • City Life
Write the opposites of:
(a) Clean × _______
(b) Quiet × _______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Clean × Dirty
(b) Quiet × Noisy
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Dirty and Noisy 2 M
Q11 Singular and Plural • Town
Give the plural form:
(a) One street — Many _______
(b) One building — Several _______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Many streets
(b) Several buildings
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Streets and buildings 2 M
Q12 True or False • Town Life
Write True or False:
(a) Fire engines rush to put out fires with ringing sirens.
(b) We should spit on footpaths and walls.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) True
(b) False (Spitting spreads diseases).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Both answered correctly 2 M
Q13 Personal Identification • Address
Why should every child memorize their home address and phone number?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• If a child gets lost in a crowded market or fair, knowing their address and parent's phone number helps police or elders guide them home safely.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Safety in case of getting lost 2 M
Q14 Creative Expression • Town Slogan
Write a short slogan to keep your town green and clean.
[1.5 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• "Clean Town, Green Town; Spread Happiness All Around!"
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Catchy clean-town slogan 1.5 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

A Walk Through Green Valley Town

Green Valley is a tidy town with flower beds lining every avenue. In the centre stands a bright public library where children read peacefully on weekends, next to a large park where birds nest in flowering amaltas trees.

Analytical Task: What two public facilities are mentioned in the town? How do the town residents keep their town clean and beautiful?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The text mentions a public library and a large leafy park.
2. Residents plant flower beds, care for trees, and maintain tidy streets without littering.
Rubric: 1.5 M for library and park + 1.5 M for planting flowers and avoiding litter
