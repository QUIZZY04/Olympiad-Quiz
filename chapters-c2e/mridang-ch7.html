Chapter 7: Between Home and School

Daily Journey, Road Landmarks, Traffic Safety & Neighborhood | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Narrating the daily morning walk from home to the school gate.
  • Recognizing neighborhood landmarks: tea stall, post office, bakery, banyan tree.
  • Learning road safety: zebra crossings, footpath, looking both ways, traffic lights.
  • Direction words: left, right, straight ahead, across.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Comprehension • The Journey
Name three things or places Ravi sees between his home and his school.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. The red post box / post office.
2. The bustling tea stall with boiling tea.
3. The huge spreading banyan tree and the pedestrian zebra crossing.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Post box / post office named 1 M
Tea stall named 1 M
Banyan tree / crossing named 1 M
Q2 Road Safety • Zebra Crossing
What is a zebra crossing? Why does it have black and white stripes?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• A zebra crossing is a designated pathway painted across the road for pedestrians to cross safely.
• The bold black and white stripes resemble a zebra's coat and are clearly visible to approaching vehicle drivers from a distance so they slow down.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pedestrian crossing definition 1 M
Visibility of zebra stripes explained 1 M
Q3 Traffic Lights Rule
What do the three traffic light colours mean for vehicles?
(a) Red means _______
(b) Yellow (Amber) means _______
(c) Green means _______
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Red means STOP.
(b) Yellow means WAIT / SLOW DOWN / GET READY.
(c) Green means GO.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) STOP 1 M
(b) WAIT / SLOW 1 M
(c) GO 1 M
Q4 Direction Words • Left, Right, Straight
Fill in with [left, right, straight]:
(a) Walk _______ along the pavement without running.
(b) Before crossing, look right, then look _______, then right again.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Walk straight along the pavement.
(b) Look right, then look left, then right again.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) straight 1 M
(b) left 1 M
Q5 Walking Habits • Footpath Rule
Why should we always walk on the footpath (pavement) and never play on the open road?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• The road is meant for fast-moving vehicles like cars, buses, and motorcycles.
• Walking on the footpath protects pedestrians from speeding traffic and prevents accidents.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Vehicle traffic danger explained 1 M
Pavement protection explained 1 M
Q6 Neighborhood Landmarks
What is a 'landmark'? Give one example from your own neighbourhood.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Explanation:
• A landmark is an easily recognized building, tree, or place (like a big temple, bank, park, or water tank) that helps people find directions easily.
• Example: The municipal water tank near the main bus stop.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Landmark definition 1 M
Valid real-world example 1 M

Q12 Punctuality • Arriving on Time
Why is it important to reach school on time before the morning assembly bell rings?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Reaching on time teaches self-discipline.

Q14 True or False
State True or False:
(a) We should cross the road while playing on our mobile phone.
(b) A yellow traffic light tells drivers to slow down and prepare to stop.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) False. Looking at mobile phones while crossing distracts attention and can lead to dangerous accidents.
(b) True. Yellow/amber warns drivers that red light is approaching.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False with safety reason 1 M
(b) True 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Ravi's Morning Walk to School

Every morning, Ravi walks to school with his elder sister. They leave home, turn left at the red letterbox, walk past the lively tea stall, stop at the pedestrian crossing until the signal turns green, and enter through the main school gate.

Analytical Task: Which landmark did Ravi pass after turning left? Why did they stop at the pedestrian crossing? Which rule of road safety did they follow?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. After turning left, Ravi passed the red letterbox and the lively tea stall.
2. They stopped at the pedestrian crossing because the traffic light was red for pedestrians and cars were moving.
3. They followed the golden road safety rule: Wait for the green walking signal and cross only at the pedestrian/zebra crossing.
Rubric: 1 M for landmarks + 1 M for stop reason + 1 M for road safety rule
