Chapter 2: Picture Reading

Observing the School Playground, Action Words, Friends & Outdoor Games | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Visual observation: Reading detail in an energetic school playground illustration.
  • Action words: Running, skipping, jumping, sliding, kicking, reading, smiling.
  • Prepositions: On the slide, under the banyan tree, near the fountain, in the queue.
  • Peer vocabulary: Friend, sharing, playground, swings, sandbox.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Picture Observation • Playground Scene
Look closely at the playground picture. Name three things that children are playing on.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. The swings
2. The slide
3. The see-saw (or football ground)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Swings, slide, see-saw identified 2 M
Q2 Action Words • Verbs in the Picture
What is the boy in the blue shirt doing in the picture?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The boy in the blue shirt is sliding down the slide with a happy smile.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sliding down the slide 1 M
Q3 Prepositions • Where are they?
Where are the two girls sitting with their storybook?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The two girls are sitting under the shade of the big green tree.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Under the tree 1 M
Q4 Count and Write • Animals and Birds
How many birds can you spot on the branches of the tree in the picture?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• There are three little birds perched on the tree branches.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct count (three birds) 1 M
Q5 Vocabulary • Matching Actions
Match each child with their action:
(a) Child with rope — (1) Kicking
(b) Child with ball — (2) Skipping
(c) Child on swing — (3) Swinging
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Child with rope — (2) Skipping
(b) Child with ball — (1) Kicking
(c) Child on swing — (3) Swinging
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All three matched accurately 3 M
Q6 Let us Speak • Turn Taking
What should children do when there is only one swing and several children want to swing?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Children should form a queue, wait patiently, and take turns without pushing.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Take turns and wait in queue 2 M
Q7 Opposite Words • Practice
Write the opposite of:
(a) Up (on the see-saw) × _______
(b) Happy × _______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Up × Down
(b) Happy × Sad
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Down and Sad 2 M
Q8 Sentence Writing • Playground
Write one sentence about your favourite thing in your school playground.
[1.5 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• I love swinging high on the green swings during recess with my best friend.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Meaningful personal sentence 1.5 M
Q9 Find the Missing Letters • Words
Complete the words from the playground picture:
(a) S W _ N G
(b) S L _ D E
(c) F R _ E N D
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) S W I N G = SWING
(b) S L I D E = SLIDE
(c) F R I E N D = FRIEND
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All three words spelled correctly 3 M
Q10 Think and Share • Safety in Play
Why should we not run up the wrong way on a slide?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Running up a slide can cause a collision with a child sliding down and lead to falls and painful bruises.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Risk of collision and injury 2 M
Q11 Identify the Odd One Out • Groups
Which one does NOT belong to a playground: (A) Swing (B) Slide (C) Refrigerator (D) See-saw?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• (C) Refrigerator (It is a kitchen appliance, not a playground equipment).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Refrigerator identified 1 M
Q12 Grammar • A or An
Fill in with 'a' or 'an':
(a) ______ swing
(b) ______ umbrella in the rain
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) a swing
(b) an umbrella
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
'a' and 'an' applied correctly 2 M
Q13 Social Values • Including Others
If you see a new classmate standing alone by the fence, what will you say to him?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• I will smile and say, "Hello! Come and play catch with us!"
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Friendly invitation and inclusion 2 M
Q14 Colour Identification • Art
What is the colour of healthy grass in the school ground?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Healthy grass is bright green.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Green colour 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Recess Fun in the Sun

During lunch break, the school bell rang. Tara rushed to the swings, Kabir and Rohit kicked a football across the grass, while Ananya sat under the shade of a neem tree reading a colourful picture book.

Analytical Task: What three different activities were children doing during recess? Why is outdoor play essential during school hours?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The children were (a) swinging on the swings, (b) playing football, and (c) reading a picture book under the tree.
2. Outdoor play provides fresh air, physical exercise, teamwork with friends, and mental refreshment.
Rubric: 1.5 M for identifying all three activities + 1.5 M for explaining exercise, fresh air, and peer social bonding
