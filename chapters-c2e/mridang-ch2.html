Chapter 2: Picture Reading & My Name

Self-Introduction, Family Roles, Greetings & Alphabet Writing | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Introducing oneself with full name, age, class, and school.
  • Reading family pictures and talking about parents, grandparents, and siblings.
  • Using courteous greetings (Good morning, Thank you, Please, Excuse me).
  • Tracing uppercase and lowercase alphabet letters with correct stroke order.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Activity • Self Introduction
Write three sentences introducing yourself to your teacher.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sample Introduction:
1. My name is Aarav Gupta.
2. I am seven years old and I study in Class 2.
3. I like reading storybooks and playing cricket.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Name stated 1 M
Age and class stated 1 M
Hobby/favorite activity stated 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Family Picture Reading
Look at the family picture in your book. Who are the people living in a joint family?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Grandfather, grandmother, father, mother, brother, sister, and pets (like a dog or cat) living together with love and care.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Grandparents and parents listed 1 M
Siblings and pets listed 1 M
Q3 Polite Words • Magic Manners
When should you say:
(a) 'Thank you'
(b) 'Please'
(c) 'Sorry'
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Say 'Thank you' when someone helps you or gives you a gift.
(b) Say 'Please' when asking politely for help or an item.
(c) Say 'Sorry' when you make a mistake or accidentally hurt someone.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Thank you context 1 M
(b) Please context 1 M
(c) Sorry context 1 M
Q4 Grammar • Capital Letters Rule
Why do we always write the first letter of our name with a CAPITAL letter?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Explanation:
• A person's name is a Proper Noun (a special, unique naming word).
• In English grammar rules, all proper nouns always begin with a capital letter (e.g., Rohan, Meera, India).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Proper noun concept explained 1 M
Grammar capitalization rule stated 1 M
Q5 Vocabulary • Family Relations
What do you call:
(a) Your father's father: _______
(b) Your mother's brother: _______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) My father's father is my Grandfather (Dada ji).
(b) My mother's brother is my Uncle (Mama ji).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Grandfather 1 M
(b) Uncle 1 M
Q6 Alphabet Order • Alphabetical Thinking
Arrange these three names in alphabetical (A to Z) order: Chetan, Aman, Bina.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Look at first letters: C, A, B.
• Alphabetical order: A, B, C.
• Correct order: 1. Aman, 2. Bina, 3. Chetan.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
First letter comparison shown 1 M
Aman, Bina, Chetan correct 1 M

Q11 Greetings Matching
Match the greeting to the time of day:
(a) Morning (b) Afternoon (c) Night
Greetings: (1) Good night (2) Good afternoon (3) Good morning
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Matching:
• (a) Morning — (3) Good morning
• (b) Afternoon — (2) Good afternoon
• (c) Night — (1) Good night
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 time greetings correctly matched 2 M
Q12 Vowels and Consonants
How many letters are in the English alphabet? Name the 5 vowels.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• There are 26 letters in the English alphabet.
• The 5 vowels are: A, E, I, O, U.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Q15 True or False
State True or False:
(a) We should shout loudly when greeting our teacher.
(b) A loving family helps each other in daily chores.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) False. We should greet our teacher politely and respectfully, not shout.
(b) True. Family members support and care for each other.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

(b) True 1 M

[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

New Student Classroom Introduction

On the first day of Class 2, a new student stands in front of the class. She says: 'Hello everyone! My name is Ananya Sharma. I am seven years old. I live with my parents and grandparents. My favorite hobby is drawing cartoons.'

Analytical Task: What is the new student's full name? How old is she? What polite greeting did she use to start speaking?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Her full name is Ananya Sharma.
2. She is seven years old.
3. She started politely with the greeting: 'Hello everyone!'.
Rubric: 1 M for full name + 1 M for age + 1 M for greeting
