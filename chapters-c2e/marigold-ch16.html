Chapter 16: The Mumbai Musicians

Town Musicians of Bremen Adaptation, Animal Band & The Ghost Feast | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: Old donkey Gopu, dog Dopu, cat Furry, and cock Booboo set out for Mumbai.
  • Desire to become musicians when their farm masters turn them out.
  • The house in the forest: Singing together outside the window to ask for food.
  • The comical misunderstanding: Robbers inside think ghosts have attacked and flee; animals enjoy a feast.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • The Journey
Why did Gopu the donkey leave his master's house?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Gopu had worked hard carrying sacks of corn for many years, but had grown old. His master said: 'Go and see the world before you become too old!' so Gopu decided to go to Mumbai to become a musician.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Master told him to see the world 1 M
Decided to become a musician in Mumbai 1 M

[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Dopu (the dog)
2. Furry (the cat)
3. Booboo (the cock/rooster).

Q4 Reading is Fun • The Concert Noise

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

(a) Gopu: Bray (Hee-haw!)
(b) Dopu: Bark (Bow-wow!)
(c) Furry: Meow (Meow-meow!)
(d) Booboo: Crow (Cock-a-doodle-doo!)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

The Four Forest Musicians' Concert

Four elderly animal friends — Gopu the donkey, Dopu the dog, Furry the cat, and Booboo the rooster — walked toward Mumbai to become musicians. Tired and starving in the forest, they saw a lit window with a table full of delicious food. They decided to sing together to earn dinner. Gopu brayed, Dopu barked, Furry meowed, and Booboo crowed: 'COCK-A-DOODLE-DOO!'. Inside, the people thought ghosts had come, shrieked in terror, and ran into the woods.

Analytical Task: Why did the animals decide to sing outside the window? What happened when they sang all together? What did the animals do after the people fled?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The animals sang because they were starving and wanted to earn food by giving a concert.
2. When they all sang together, the loud mixed noise sounded so wild and eerie that the people thought ghosts had come and ran away in fear.
3. The happy animals sat down at the table and ate up the delicious feast until they were completely full.
Rubric: 1 M for concert to earn food + 1 M for ghost misunderstanding + 1 M for enjoying the feast
