Chapter 8: A Show of Clouds

Cloud Shapes, White Fluffy Sheep, Sailing in the Sky, Weather & Rain | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem comprehension: Watching white fluffy clouds drift across the bright blue sky like playful sheep.
  • Imaginative shapes: Clouds looking like dogs, galloping horses, soaring birds, and mountain castles.
  • Weather changes: Light white clouds bringing shade, heavy dark clouds bringing cool rain.
  • Rhyming words: Sky-high, sheep-asleep, blow-go, rain-train.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Poem Comprehension • Cloud Appearance
What do the fluffy white clouds in the poem look like as they drift across the blue hill?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The white clouds look like fluffy white sheep grazing peacefully in the blue sky.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
White sheep stated 1 M
Q2 Poem Comprehension • When the Wind Blows
What happens to the clouds when the wind blows across the sky?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• When the wind blows, the clouds move slowly and sail along together.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Move and sail along 1 M
Q3 Poem Comprehension • When the Wind Stops
What do the clouds do when the wind stops blowing?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• When the wind stops, the clouds stand still as if asleep.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Stand still / asleep 1 M
Q4 Weather Vocabulary • Describing Words
Match the weather conditions to their descriptions:
(a) Sunny — (1) Full of cool falling water
(b) Windy — (2) Bright hot sun shining
(c) Rainy — (3) Leaves dancing and blowing fast
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Sunny — (2) Bright hot sun
(b) Windy — (3) Leaves dancing
(c) Rainy — (1) Falling water
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All three matched correctly 3 M
Q5 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Cloud Watching on a Sunny Afternoon

Lying on the soft green grass, Leo and Maya looked up at the sky. 'Look!' cried Leo, 'that cloud looks like a fluffy white puppy with floppy ears!' Maya pointed, 'And that big cloud over the hill looks like a king's castle with tall towers!'

Analytical Task: What two shapes did the children spot in the drifting clouds? What happens when white clouds turn dark grey?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The children saw (a) a fluffy white puppy with floppy ears and (b) a king's castle with tall towers.
2. When clouds turn dark grey, they become heavy with water droplets and bring cool, refreshing rain.
Rubric: 1.5 M for puppy and castle shapes + 1.5 M for grey clouds bringing rain
