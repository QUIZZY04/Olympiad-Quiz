Chapter 8: This is My Town

Community Helpers, Civic Places, Cleanliness & Town Pride | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem appreciation celebrating town landmarks, streets, and community spaces.
  • Identifying community helpers: baker, doctor, teacher, potter, postman, sweeper.
  • Civic responsibility: keeping public streets clean and throwing waste in trash bins.
  • Rhyming pairs and sound patterns (town-clown, street-sweet, shop-stop).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Poem Comprehension • Town Features
What are some of the places mentioned in the poem 'This is My Town'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Clean wide streets, rows of shops, the school, the hospital/clinic, the town park, and the bakery.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Streets and shops mentioned 1 M
Public buildings / parks mentioned 1 M
Q2 Community Helpers • Match the Job
Match each helper with their work:
(a) Teacher (b) Doctor (c) Potter (d) Firefighter
Work: (1) Cures sick people (2) Extinguishes fires (3) Teaches students (4) Shapes earthen clay pots
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Matching:
• (a) Teacher — (3) Teaches students
• (b) Doctor — (1) Cures sick people
• (c) Potter — (4) Shapes earthen clay pots
• (d) Firefighter — (2) Extinguishes fires
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 helpers correctly matched 2 M (0.5 M each)
Q3 Rhyming Pairs in the Poem
Find words that rhyme with:
(a) Town — _______
(b) Street — _______
(c) Shop — _______
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Town — Brown / Down / Clown
(b) Street — Sweet / Meet / Feet
(c) Shop — Stop / Top / Hop
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Down/Brown 1 M
(b) Sweet/Meet 1 M
(c) Stop/Top 1 M
Q4 Civic Sense • Waste Disposal
Where should you throw banana peels and empty juice packets when walking in your town?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• You should always throw waste inside a public dustbin (trash can).
• If no bin is nearby, keep the wrapper in your bag until you find one, keeping the streets sparkling clean.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Public dustbin identified 1 M
Carrying waste until bin found explained 1 M

Q8 Civic Pride • Beautiful Town
What can citizens do to make their town green and beautiful?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Plant shady flowering trees and saplings along pathways and parks.
2. Never litter plastic and avoid damaging public property.

Q13 Respect for All Work
Why should we respect sanitation workers and sweepers who clean our streets?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• They do very hard and important work in all seasons to keep our surroundings disease-free and clean.
• Without them, our towns would become polluted and unlivable. All honest work deserves dignity and gratitude.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Disease prevention & hard work explained 1 M
Dignity of labor and gratitude emphasized 1 M

Q15 True or False
State True or False:
(a) A potter makes clay pots on a rotating wheel.
(b) We should scribble graffiti on public town walls.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) True. A potter uses a pottery wheel to shape wet clay.
(b) False. Scribbling on walls defaces public property and spoils the beauty of the town.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) True 1 M
(b) False with civic reason 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Community Helpers Day at Town Hall

Class 2 visited the Town Hall for Community Helpers Day. They met Uncle Paul who bakes crusty buns at the bakery, Dr. Sneha who treats sick patients, Postman Shyam who delivers letters on his bicycle, and Ramu the sanitation worker who sweeps the lanes clean every dawn.

Analytical Task: What does the baker do? Who keeps the town lanes clean? Why are all community helpers equally important?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The baker bakes fresh bread, cakes, and crusty buns for everyone.
2. Ramu the sanitation worker (sweeper) keeps the lanes clean and hygienic.
3. Importance: Every helper provides an essential service that makes town life comfortable, healthy, safe, and happy. We rely on each other in society.
Rubric: 1 M for baker + 1 M for sanitation worker + 1 M for mutual interdependence explanation
