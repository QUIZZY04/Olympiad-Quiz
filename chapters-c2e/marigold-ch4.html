Chapter 4: I Want

The Little Monkey, The Magic Wand & Being Content with Oneself | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: Little monkey wants to be big and strong; wise fairy gifts a magic wand.
  • Monkey takes a giraffe's long neck, an elephant's trunk, and a zebra's stripes.
  • Looking in the river: Monkey gets terrified thinking he is a monster!
  • Realization: Being oneself is the greatest blessing. Discarding greed and false appearances.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Monkey's Desire
What did the Little Monkey want to be in the beginning?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The Little Monkey wanted to be big and strong.

Big and strong stated 1 M
Q2 Reading is Fun • The Giraffe's Gift
What happened when the Little Monkey wished for a giraffe's neck?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• POP! His neck grew very long, just like a tall giraffe's neck, reaching the sweet leaves high in the trees.

[2 Marks]

Q6 Story Resolution • Becoming Himself
What was Little Monkey's final wish with the magic wand? What did he do with the wand?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• His final wish was: 'I want to be myself again!'
• After turning back into a happy monkey, he threw the magic wand deep into the river so he would never be tempted to change again.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Final wish: be myself again 1.5 M
Threw wand into the river 1.5 M

Q9 Moral Understanding • True Beauty
Why was Little Monkey happier as a normal monkey than as a creature with mixed animal parts?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• Monkeys are agile, light, and perfectly designed to leap through trees and eat fruits.
• Having a heavy trunk and clumsy neck made him look strange and lose his natural abilities. True beauty lies in being who you are.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Loss of natural agility explained 1 M
Acceptance of natural design emphasized 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

The Little Monkey's Magic Wand Lesson

Little Monkey was unhappy with his small size. A wise woman heard him and gave him a magic wand, saying: 'Take this wand, and all your wishes can come true!' First, Monkey wished for a giraffe's neck. Then an elephant's trunk to blow water over himself. Then a zebra's black and white stripes. When he went to the river to try his trunk, he looked in the clear water and screamed: 'Help! A monster!'

Analytical Task: Who gave Little Monkey the magic wand? What three body features did he borrow? Why did he scream 'monster' at the river?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. A wise woman (fairy) gave him the magic wand.
2. He borrowed: (a) A giraffe's long neck, (b) An elephant's trunk, and (c) A zebra's stripes.
3. He screamed 'monster' because the mismatch of different animal body parts made him look distorted and terrifying to his own eyes!
Rubric: 1 M for wise woman + 1 M for all 3 borrowed animal features + 1 M for monster reflection realization
