Chapter 15: I am the Music Man

Musical Instruments, Rhythm, Piano, Bass Drum & Faraway Lands | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem recitation: 'I am the music man, I come from far away, and I can play! What can you play? I play the piano!'
  • Musical instrument sounds: Piano ('pia-pia-piano') and big bass drum ('boom-di-boom-di-boom').
  • Identifying different musical families: strings, keys, percussion, and wind.
  • Developing rhythm, music appreciation, and expressive body movement.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

Q3 Sound Words • Musical Onomatopoeia
What sound does the piano make in the poem? What sound does the big drum make?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:

• The big drum goes: 'Boom-di-boom-di-boom-di-boom, boom-di-boom!'

Q4 Instrument Families • Classification
Classify the following musical instruments into 'String', 'Wind', or 'Percussion (Drums)':
(a) Flute (b) Tabla (c) Guitar (d) Dholak
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classification:
• Wind: (a) Flute (blown by mouth)
• Percussion: (b) Tabla, (d) Dholak (struck with hands)
• String: (c) Guitar (strummed with fingers).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Flute: Wind 1 M
Tabla & Dholak: Percussion 1 M
Guitar: String 1 M

Q14 True or False

Q15 Poem Reflection
Why does the poem 'I am the Music Man' use repetitive, rhythmic sounds?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:
• The repetitive words ('pia-pia-piano', 'boom-di-boom') imitate the actual musical rhythm and tempo of the instruments, encouraging young learners to chant, clap, and sing along.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Imitation of musical rhythm and tempo explained 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

School Annual Day Music Troupe

During the annual school concert, a lively performer dressed as the 'Music Man' walked onto the stage. He announced: 'I come from far away and I can play!'. First, his fingers danced across the black and white keys of a gleaming grand piano: 'pia-pia-piano!'. Then he struck a massive red bass drum with wooden mallets: 'BOOM-DI-BOOM-DI-BOOM!'.

Analytical Task: Which two musical instruments did the Music Man play? What sound words describe their music in the poem? Why is music joyful?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The Music Man played the Piano and the Big Bass Drum.
2. Sound words: Piano = 'pia-pia-piano, pia-piano'; Big Drum = 'boom-di-boom-di-boom'.
3. Music brings joy because rhythmic melodies stimulate our imagination, lift our spirits, and invite us to sing and dance happily together.
Rubric: 1 M for piano and drum + 1 M for 'pia-pia-piano' and 'boom-di-boom' + 1 M for emotional/rhythmic value of music
