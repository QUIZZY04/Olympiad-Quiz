Chapter 4: It is Fun!

Animal Movements, Action Verbs, Miming & Rhymes | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem recitation celebrating diverse ways animals move in nature.
  • Matching creatures with their movements: leap like a frog, swim like a fish, swing like a monkey, slither like a snake.
  • Action verbs and adverbs (swiftly, gently, playfully).
  • Creative movement games and roleplay.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Poem Comprehension • Animal Actions
According to the poem 'It is Fun!', how does:
(a) A frog move?
(b) A fish move?
(c) A bird move?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) A frog leaps.
(b) A fish swims.
(c) A bird flies.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) leaps 1 M
(b) swims 1 M
(c) flies 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Match the Movement
Match each creature with its correct movement verb:
(a) Monkey (b) Horse (c) Snake (d) Duck
Movements: (1) Slither (2) Swing (3) Waddle (4) Gallop
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Matching:
• (a) Monkey — (2) Swing
• (b) Horse — (4) Gallop
• (c) Snake — (1) Slither
• (d) Duck — (3) Waddle
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 correctly matched 2 M (0.5 M each)
Q3 Action Words • Fill in the Blanks

(a) A kangaroo can _______ high.
(b) A baby can _______ on hands and knees.
(c) A duck can _______ in the pond.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) A kangaroo can hop high.
(b) A baby can crawl on hands and knees.
(c) A duck can swim in the pond.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) hop 1 M
(b) crawl 1 M
(c) swim 1 M
Q4 Animal Adaptations • Bird Wings
What body parts help a bird fly high in the sky?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Birds have feathered wings, light hollow bones, and strong chest muscles that help them flap and soar gracefully in the air.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Feathered wings identified 1 M
Light bones / strong muscles mentioned 1 M

Find words that rhyme with:

Q6 Grammar • Present Continuous (-ing)
Add '-ing' to turn these action words into continuous actions:
(a) Jump — _______
(b) Swim — _______
(c) Fly — _______
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Jump + ing = Jumping
(b) Swim + ing = Swimming (double the 'm')
(c) Fly + ing = Flying
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Jumping 1 M
(b) Swimming 1 M
(c) Flying 1 M
Q7 Physical Activity • Fun at Play
Why is running and playing games outdoors good for growing children?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Outdoor play strengthens bones and heart muscles.
2. It gives fresh air, relieves stress, and helps children stay energetic, active, and cheerful.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Physical health / muscle strength 1 M
Fresh air and happiness 1 M

Where does a monkey like to swing and play?

Q12 Movement Comparison
Who moves faster: a snail or a rabbit? Why?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• A rabbit moves much faster.
• A snail glides slowly on a trail of slime carrying a heavy shell, whereas a rabbit has strong muscular legs for rapid running and hopping.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rabbit identified as faster 1 M
Anatomical comparison explained 1 M
Q13 Human vs Animal Movement
Can humans fly in the sky like birds? How do humans travel by air?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Humans do not have wings and cannot fly naturally.
• However, humans travel through the air using inventions like aeroplanes and helicopters.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Lack of natural wings stated 1 M
Aeroplanes/helicopters identified 1 M

Q15 True or False
State True or False:
(a) An elephant can jump over tall trees.
(b) Frogs can live both on land and in water.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) False. An elephant is too heavy and cannot jump.
(b) True. Frogs are amphibians and comfortably live in water and on land.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Playground Animal Movement Game

During sports period, the PE teacher calls out animal names. When he shouts 'Rabbit', children hop on both feet. When he shouts 'Duck', they waddle in a squat. When he shouts 'Eagle', they stretch their arms wide and glide like wings.

Analytical Task: Which movement verb matches the rabbit? Which matches the eagle? Why do different animals have different styles of movement?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Rabbit: Hop / Leap.
2. Eagle: Fly / Glide / Soar.
3. Reason: Animals have adapted unique body shapes and limbs suited for their habitat (fish have fins for swimming in water, monkeys have strong arms for swinging on trees, rabbits have powerful hind legs for hopping on land).
Rubric: 1 M for rabbit hop + 1 M for eagle fly/glide + 1 M for habitat adaptation explanation
