Chapter 2: Haldi’s Adventure

Encounter with Smiley the Giraffe, Reading Books & School Joy | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: Haldi meets Smiley, an extraordinary giraffe wearing big glasses and holding a book.
  • Smiley loves reading books and discussing stars, trees, birds, and school lessons.
  • Haldi rides on Smiley's tall back to reach her school before the morning bell.
  • The thrill of learning, reading, and discovering wonder in everyday life.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Haldi's Morning Encounter

As Haldi walked to school one morning, she met a tall giraffe. The giraffe wore big spectacles and held a book in his hand. He smiled and said: 'Good morning, Haldi! My name is Smiley. Whenever you see me, you will smile.' Haldi stared at him because she had never seen a giraffe wearing spectacles before.

Analytical Task: What was the giraffe's name? What was unusual about his appearance? Why did Haldi say she loved school?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The giraffe's name was Smiley.
2. Unusual appearance: He wore big spectacles and held a book in his hand, unlike any normal wild animal.
3. Haldi loved school because she learned exciting things about stars, trees, birds, and animals every day.
Rubric: 1 M for Smiley + 1 M for spectacles and book + 1 M for Haldi's school learning reasons
