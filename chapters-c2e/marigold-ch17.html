Chapter 17: Granny Granny Please Comb My Hair

Grandmother's Gentle Touch, Coconut Oil, Braiding & Affection | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem appreciation: A child's sweet request to her grandmother to comb and oil her hair.
  • The cozy ritual: Sitting on a yellow cushion between Granny's knees.
  • Gentle grooming: Rubbing nourishing coconut oil, gentle parting of hair, breezy breeze.
  • Grandparent affection: Granny praising the grandchild as the sweetest, best child in the world.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Child's Request
What does the little girl ask her Grandmother to do?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The little girl affectionately asks her grandmother: 'Granny Granny, please comb my hair!'.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Comb her hair stated 1 M
Q2 Reading is Fun • Special Quality of Granny
What special quality does Granny always have for her grandchild?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Granny always takes her time, and always has plenty of time for the child, never rushing through things.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Always takes her time 1 M
Never rushes / has plenty of time 1 M
Q3 Reading is Fun • Seating Arrangement
Where does Granny make the little girl sit while combing her hair?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Granny makes her sit on a soft cushion placed right between her knees.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
On a cushion 1 M
Between her knees 1 M
Q4 Reading is Fun • Oil Used
What kind of oil does Granny rub into the girl's scalp?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:

CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Coconut oil stated 1 M
Q5 Reading is Fun • Parting the Hair
How does the poet describe Granny's parting of the hair?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Soft and breezy parting described 2 M

[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:

'Now, who's a nice girl?' quoted 2 M

Q8 Grandparent Bonding

[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

2. They tell wonderful bedtime stories, teach traditional values, and make children feel deeply cherished.

Stories, values, and security 1 M

Q11 Care for Grandparents
Name two ways you can show love and care to your grandparents.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Spend unhurried time listening to their stories and reading the newspaper for them.
2. Fetch their spectacles, walking stick, or water when they need it.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Spending time / listening 1 M
Helping with small daily needs 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Sunday Morning with Grandmother

On Sunday morning, little Meera ran to her grandmother holding a wooden comb and a bottle of scented coconut oil. She climbed onto a cozy yellow velvet cushion placed between Granny's knees. Granny gently rubbed a little oil into Meera's scalp, parted her hair into two neat braids, and planted a loving kiss on her forehead, saying: 'You are the most wonderful child in the whole world!'.

Analytical Task: Where did Meera sit? What kind of oil did Granny use? What sweet praise did Granny give her?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Meera sat on a cushion between Granny's knees.
2. Granny used nourishing coconut oil.
3. Granny praised her saying: 'Now you are finished, and you are the best child in the world!'.
Rubric: 1 M for sitting on cushion between knees + 1 M for coconut oil + 1 M for grandmother's praise
