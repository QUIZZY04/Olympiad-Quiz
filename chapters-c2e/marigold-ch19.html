Chapter 19: Strange Talk

Animal Language, Onomatopoeia, Speech Manners & Active Listening | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem appreciation: A humorous look at how animals speak with distinct voices.
  • Creature sounds: Frog ('Croak/Ribbit'), Duck ('Quack, quack'), Pig ('Wee-wee/Oink'), Puppies ('Bow-wow').
  • Contrasting animal efficiency: Animals only speak when needed; humans should avoid chaotic noise.
  • Classroom discussion etiquette: Taking turns, listening without interrupting, speaking clearly.

Q8 Classroom Etiquette • Speaking Manners
Why should students raise their hand before speaking in class?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Raising hands ensures orderly communication.
• It prevents everyone from shouting simultaneously, allowing the teacher and classmates to listen to each child's thoughts clearly.

Q15 Poem Reflection
What humorous contrast does 'Strange Talk' create between animals and children?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:

• Children have the wonderful gift of language and words, so they should use it politely, speaking one at a time with clarity.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Class 2 Animal Sounds Recitation

Class 2 staged a humorous play based on 'Strange Talk'. Ananya wore a frog mask and croaked 'Croak-croak!'. Billy wore a duck beak and quacked 'Quack-quack!'. Chetan wore pig ears and grunted 'Wee-wee!'. Then the teacher asked: 'Imagine if all thirty children in this classroom shouted their animal sounds at the very same second! What would happen?'

Analytical Task: Which sounds did the frog, duck, and pig make? What would happen if all children shouted together? What classroom speech rule does this teach?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Frog: 'Croak-croak' (or 'Ribbit'); Duck: 'Quack-quack'; Pig: 'Wee-wee'.
2. If all thirty children shouted at once, it would turn into a chaotic, deafening racket where nobody could hear or understand anyone.
3. Speech rule: Speak one at a time, take turns, and listen attentively when someone else is speaking.
Rubric: 1 M for all 3 animal sounds + 1 M for chaotic noise effect + 1 M for turn-taking classroom rule
