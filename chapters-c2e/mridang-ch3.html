Chapter 3: It is Fun

Animal Movements, Flying Birds, Hopping Bunnies, Leaping Frogs & Miming | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem recitation: Delighting in animal motions—leaping, hopping, flapping, gliding.
  • Movement vocabulary: Frog leaps, rabbit hops, bird flies/flaps, fish glides/swims.
  • Rhythm and actions: Miming different animals in circle time.
  • Rhyming words: Frog-log, hop-stop, flap-clap, fun-run.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Poem Comprehension • Frog Movement
How does the frog move in the poem 'It is Fun'?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The frog moves by leaping high from one place to another.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Leaping 1 M
Q2 Poem Comprehension • Bunny Movement
What action does the little bunny (rabbit) do?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The bunny moves by hopping quickly on its hind feet.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hopping 1 M

[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:

Gliding/swimming 1 M
Q4 Matching Exercise • Animal and Motion
Match each animal with its movement word:
(a) Bird — (1) Leap
(b) Frog — (2) Flap / Fly
(c) Fish — (3) Glide
(d) Horse — (4) Gallop
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Bird — (2) Flap / Fly
(b) Frog — (1) Leap
(c) Fish — (3) Glide
(d) Horse — (4) Gallop
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All four matched correctly (1 M each) 4 M

Complete the lines with words from the poem (fun, leap, glide):

(b) It is fun to ______ like a fish.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:

(b) It is fun to glide like a fish.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Animal Action Game

The teacher told Class 2: 'When I say frog, leap! When I say fish, glide your arms! When I say bunny, hop with two paws!' All children laughed and jumped happily around the hall.

Analytical Task: What movement did the children make for the frog? What movement did they make for the bunny?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. For the frog, the children made a deep crouch and leaped high into the air.
2. For the bunny, they held up their hands like ears and hopped lightly on their toes.
Rubric: 1.5 M for leaping like a frog + 1.5 M for hopping like a bunny
