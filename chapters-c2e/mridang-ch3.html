Chapter 3: The Little Red Hen

Classic Folk Tale, Planting Wheat, Hard Work & Teamwork | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: Hen finds grains, asks friends for help, does all the work herself.
  • The stages of making bread: planting seeds, harvesting wheat, grinding into flour, baking bread.
  • Character contrast: industrious Hen vs lazy friends (Dog, Cat, Duck).
  • Moral value: Those who do not help with the work do not deserve to share the feast.

Q3 Sequencing the Steps • From Seed to Bread
Arrange the steps of making bread in the correct order:
(A) Baking bread in the oven
(B) Planting wheat seeds in soil
(C) Grinding wheat grains into flour
(D) Cutting the grown wheat crop
[3 Marks]

2. (D) Cutting the grown wheat crop

Q5 Moral Reasoning • Sharing Food
Was the Little Red Hen right in refusing to share the bread with her lazy friends? Give a reason.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• Yes, the hen was completely fair and right.
• Reason: Her friends slept and played while she worked hard in the heat. Giving them bread would encourage their laziness, whereas refusing taught them the value of labor.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Affirmative judgment (Yes) 1 M
Reason regarding hard work and laziness 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

The Little Red Hen's Harvest Dilemma

The Little Red Hen found some golden grains of wheat. She asked the dog, the cat, and the duck: 'Who will help me plant these seeds?' All three replied: 'Not I!' The hen planted, watered, cut the wheat, and baked delicious hot bread with her chicks all by herself. When the aroma spread, the dog, cat, and duck ran over and said: 'We will help you eat it!'

Analytical Task: Did the friends help the hen at any stage of making bread? What did the hen reply when they wanted to eat the bread? What is the moral lesson?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. No, the friends refused to help at every stage (planting, cutting, grinding, and baking).
2. The hen said: 'No, you will not! My chicks and I will eat it all by ourselves!'
3. Moral: Hard work brings sweet rewards. Those who are lazy and refuse to work should not expect to enjoy the fruits of someone else's labor.
Rubric: 1 M for 'No' with refusal explanation + 1 M for hen's response + 1 M for clear moral lesson
