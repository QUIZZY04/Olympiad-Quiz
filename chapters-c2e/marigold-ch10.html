Chapter 10: Funny Bunny

The Acorn Fall, The Sky is Falling Panic & Foxy Loxy's Trick | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: An acorn falls on Funny Bunny's head; he panics thinking 'The sky is falling!'.
  • Spreading rumors: Henny Penny, Cocky Locky, Ducky Lucky, and Goosey Poosey follow blindly without checking.
  • The danger of gullibility: Meeting the cunning Foxy Loxy who tricks them into his cave den.
  • Moral: Think before you panic; never believe rumors blindly, and beware of deceitful strangers.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • The Accident
One day, a nut fell on _______ (Funny Bunny / Cocky Locky / the King).
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• One day, a nut fell on Funny Bunny.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Funny Bunny chosen 1 M
Q2 Reading is Fun • The Panic Claim
What did Funny Bunny think had happened when the nut hit his head?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Funny Bunny thought that the sky was falling down and that a piece of the sky had hit him.

The sky is falling down stated 2 M

[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Q8 Moral Reasoning • Rumor Control
What should Henny Penny or Ducky Lucky have done before running off to tell the King?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• They should have stopped, looked up at the blue sky, and checked if the sky was actually falling.
• They should have asked: 'Where did the sky break?' instead of blindly panicking.

• Trusting sweet-talking strangers blindly can lead to grave danger.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

The Acorn Panic Investigation

Funny Bunny sat under a tall oak tree. Suddenly, OUCH! A ripe brown nut (acorn) dropped straight from a high branch and struck him on the head. Without looking up at the tree branches, Funny Bunny shouted: 'Oh no! The sky is falling down! I must run and tell the King!'. On the road, he repeated this false alarm to Henny Penny, Cocky Locky, Ducky Lucky, and Goosey Poosey, who all ran after him in panic.

Analytical Task: What actually fell on Funny Bunny's head? What mistake did he make? Why did the other animals follow him blindly?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. A small, ripe acorn (tree nut) fell on his head.
2. His mistake: He did not look up to investigate what hit him; he jumped to a foolish, panicked conclusion that 'the sky is falling'.
3. The other animals followed him because they believed a rumor blindly without using their own senses or reasoning.
Rubric: 1 M for acorn + 1 M for jumping to false conclusion + 1 M for blind faith in rumors
