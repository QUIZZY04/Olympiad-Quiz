Chapter 6: Come Back, Cat!

Pet Care, Searching Spaces, Prepositions of Place & Love for Animals | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: Meenu's pet cat Mittu goes missing, family searches everywhere.
  • Mastering prepositions of position: in, on, under, behind, near, between.
  • Pet care responsibilities: feeding, gentle handling, and creating safe pet shelters.
  • Building emotional awareness and affection towards domestic animals.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Story Comprehension • The Problem
Why was Meenu worried when she returned from school?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Meenu was worried because her beloved pet cat, Mittu, was missing and did not come running to greet her when called.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Cat Mittu missing identified 1 M
Lack of usual greeting explained 1 M
Q2 Prepositions • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in with [in, on, under, behind]:
(a) The cat is sitting _______ the mat.
(b) The kitten is hiding _______ the bed.
(c) Milk is _______ the red bowl.
(d) The puppy hid _______ the curtain.
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) The cat is sitting on the mat.
(b) The kitten is hiding under the bed.
(c) Milk is in the red bowl.
(d) The puppy hid behind the curtain.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) on 1 M
(b) under 1 M
(c) in 1 M
(d) behind 1 M
Q3 Story Resolution • How Mittu Returned
How did Meenu finally find Mittu?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Meenu heard a soft 'Meow!' from the terrace.
• She looked up and saw Mittu safely perched on the wall watching a butterfly, and gently carried her down.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Heard 'Meow!' sound 1 M
Found on terrace wall chasing butterfly 1 M
Q4 Animal Care Responsibilities
What two things should pet owners give their cats every day to keep them healthy?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Clean fresh drinking water and nutritious food (like warm milk or cat food).
2. A cozy clean sleeping mat and gentle love and playtime.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Water and nutritious food 1 M
Clean shelter / gentle love 1 M
Q5 Animal Sounds • Vocabulary
What sound does a cat make when it is hungry? What sound does it make when it is happy and stroked?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• When hungry, a cat says 'Meow, meow'.
• When happy and gently stroked, a cat makes a vibrating 'Purr, purr' sound.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Meow for hungry/calling 1 M
Purr for contentment 1 M

Q10 Punctuation • Exclamation Mark
Why does the title use an exclamation mark in 'Come Back, Cat!'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• An exclamation mark (!) shows strong feeling, excitement, urgency, or loud calling.
• Meenu was calling out urgently with deep love and worry for her cat.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Strong emotion / urgency explained 1 M
Loud calling connection 1 M

Q15 True or False
State True or False:
(a) Cats have sharp claws that help them climb trees and walls.
(b) A preposition tells us about the colour of an item.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) True. Retractable sharp claws allow cats to grip and climb vertical surfaces safely.
(b) False. A preposition tells us about position, place, or direction, not colour.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) True 1 M
(b) False with position explanation 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Meenu's Missing Pet Search

Meenu came home from school and called out 'Mittu! Mittu!'. Her white kitten did not appear. Meenu looked under the bed, but Mittu was not there. She checked inside the laundry basket, but found only towels. Finally, she heard a faint 'Meow!'. Mittu was sitting high on top of the terrace wall, batting at a yellow butterfly.

Analytical Task: Where did Meenu first look for Mittu? Where was Mittu actually found? Which prepositions show these locations?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Meenu first looked under the bed.
2. Mittu was found sitting on top of the terrace wall.
3. The prepositions of position used are 'under' and 'on'.
Rubric: 1 M for under the bed + 1 M for terrace wall + 1 M for identifying prepositions
