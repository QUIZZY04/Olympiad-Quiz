All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (3 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textual Comprehension • Q&A
What happens in the story/poem 'Storm in the Garden'? Summarize the main event.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:
1. In 'Storm in the Garden', we learn about Sunu-sunu the Snail Hides Under His Shell During Rain.
2. The characters experience an engaging adventure that highlights positive values and curiosity.
3. Conclusion: Good character and thoughtful actions lead to happy endings.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Event summary 1.5 M
Key conclusion 1.5 M
Q2 Vocabulary & Rhyme Bank
Give two new words you learned from 'Storm in the Garden' along with their simple meanings.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Vocabulary:
• Word 1: Wonder — To think curiously about something amazing.
• Word 2: Adventure — An exciting or unusual experience.
• Both words expand our creative reading vocabulary.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two relevant words with meanings 2 M
Q3 Grammar & Creative Expression
Form a sentence using the word 'Friend' or 'Help' based on the lesson of 'Storm in the Garden'.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sentence:
'A true friend always shares and helps when someone is in need, just like in our story.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Meaningful sentence with correct grammar 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Storm in the Garden Moral & Competency

Children analyze the practical life lesson taught in the story 'Storm in the Garden'.

Analytical Task: What does 'Storm in the Garden' teach us about behavior, friendship, or daily wisdom?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It inspires students to appreciate sunu-sunu the snail hides under his shell during rain, emphasizing virtues like gratitude, kindness, honesty, and hard work.
Rubric: 1.5 M for theme analysis + 1.5 M for moral takeaway
