Chapter 8: Storm in the Garden

Sunu-sunu the Snail, Thunderstorm in the Garden & Safety at Home | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: Sunu-sunu the little snail visits his friends, the ants.
  • The sudden arrival of a fierce storm: wind whistling, lightning flashing, thunder rolling.
  • Vivid sound words: shay-shay, zzak-zzak, gadam-gudum, sss-ta-ta.
  • Animal instinct and safety: Ants hide under flower pots, Sunu-sunu pulls into his shell and goes home safe to mother.

Q4 Reading is Fun • Snail's Body Armor
Did Sunu-sunu get wet in the storm? Explain why or why not.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• No, Sunu-sunu did not get wet at all.
• Reason: He pulled his head, feelers, and tail inside his hard spiral shell, which acted as a natural waterproof umbrella.

Q12 Nature Science • Snail Slime
Why does a snail leave a silvery shiny trail behind as it crawls?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• A snail produces a moist, sticky mucus (slime) from its foot.
• This slime smooths rough surfaces, reduces friction, and protects its soft underbelly from getting cut by sharp stones.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mucus / slime production explained 1 M
Protection from sharp surfaces 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Sunu-sunu's Stormy Garden Adventure

Sunu-sunu the snail was playing happily with his ant friends. The ants climbed all over him — hee-hee! Suddenly, black clouds arrived. Thunder rolled: GADAM-GUDUM! Lightning flashed: ZZAK-ZZAK! Rain poured: SSS-TA-TA! The ants shouted: 'Hurry, Sunu-sunu! Go home! Your mother will worry!'. Sunu-sunu pulled his head and tail into his hard spiral shell and did not get wet at all.

Analytical Task: Who was Sunu-sunu visiting? What sound did the thunder make? How did Sunu-sunu protect himself from the heavy rain?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Sunu-sunu was visiting his friends, the ants in the garden.
2. The thunder rolled with the sound: 'GADAM-GUDUM!'.
3. Sunu-sunu protected himself by pulling his head and tail completely inside his hard spiral shell, staying dry and safe.
Rubric: 1 M for visiting ants + 1 M for 'GADAM-GUDUM' sound + 1 M for pulling into shell protection
