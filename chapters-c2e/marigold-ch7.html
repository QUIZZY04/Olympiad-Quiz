Chapter 7: Rain

Poem of Rainfall, Umbrellas, Ships at Sea & Refreshing Showers | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem recitation: 'The rain is raining all around, it falls on field and tree...'
  • Universal nature of rain: showering on pastures, forests, rooftops, umbrellas, and distant ocean ships.
  • Rainy season essentials: umbrellas, raincoats, gumboots, paper boats.
  • Rhyming pairs and peaceful imagery of gentle monsoon rain.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem Locations
Where does the rain fall in the poem?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The rain falls all around:
1. On fields
2. On trees
3. On umbrellas here
4. On the ships at sea.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Field and tree 1 M
Umbrellas 1 M
Ships at sea 1 M

Q6 Importance of Rain • Farmers and Crops
Why do farmers in India wait eagerly for the monsoon rain?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Farmers need rainwater to irrigate their crops (like rice, wheat, and pulses).
• Good monsoon rains fill rivers, ponds, and wells, ensuring abundant food harvests for the country.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Irrigation for crops explained 1 M
Replenishing water bodies and food supply 1 M

Q15 Poem Reflection
Why is 'Rain' considered one of the simplest yet most beautiful nature poems?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:
• In just four gentle lines, it links nature on land (fields and trees), human daily life (umbrellas), and faraway oceans (ships at sea), showing how rain unites the entire Earth in refreshing life.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Connection between land, humans, and sea explained 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Class 2 Rainy Day Window Observation

During English period, dark silver clouds covered the sky. Cool raindrops started tapping gently on the glass window. Kabir looked out and saw rain soaking the green football ground, dripping from neem tree leaves, and bouncing off colourful umbrellas held by walking parents.

Analytical Task: Where did the rain fall outside Kabir's classroom? How does this match Robert Louis Stevenson's poem 'Rain'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Outside the classroom, rain fell on the open field (football ground), green tree leaves, and colourful umbrellas.
2. This matches the exact lines of Stevenson's poem: 'The rain is raining all around, it falls on field and tree; it rains on the umbrellas here, and on the ships at sea.'
Rubric: 1.5 M for field, trees, and umbrellas observed + 1.5 M for quoting/connecting poem lines
