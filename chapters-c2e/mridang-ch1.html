Chapter 1: Two Little Hands

Poem Recitation, Body Actions, Five Senses & Rhyming Pairs | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Reciting the action rhyme with correct rhythm and gestures.
  • Recognizing body parts: hands, feet, eyes, ears, nose, head, mouth.
  • Connecting body organs to five senses (eyes see, ears hear, nose smells).
  • Identifying rhyming words (clap-tap, wide-side, feet-meet).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Poem Comprehension • Actions
What do our hands do in the poem 'Two Little Hands'? What do our feet do?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Our two little hands go clap, clap, clap.
• Our two little feet go tap, tap, tap.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hands go clap, clap, clap 1 M
Feet go tap, tap, tap 1 M
Q2 Poem Comprehension • Head and Eyes
How many eyes do we have? How does our head move in the rhyme?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• We have two little eyes that are open wide.
• Our one little head moves from side to side.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two little eyes open wide 1 M
One little head side to side 1 M
Q3 Rhyming Words • Sound Identification
Find the rhyming pair in the poem for each word:
(a) Clap — _______
(b) Wide — _______
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Clap rhymes with Tap.
(b) Wide rhymes with Side.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) Tap 1 M
(b) Side 1 M
Q4 Body Senses Connection
Match each sense organ with its function:
(a) Eyes (b) Ears (c) Nose (d) Tongue
Functions: (1) Smell (2) See (3) Taste (4) Hear
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Matching:
• (a) Eyes — (2) See
• (b) Ears — (4) Hear
• (c) Nose — (1) Smell
• (d) Tongue — (3) Taste
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 organs correctly matched 2 M (0.5 M each)
Q5 Grammar • Singular and Plural
Write the plural form (more than one) for the following body parts:
(a) One hand — Two _______
(b) One foot — Two _______
(c) One eye — Two _______
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Two hands
(b) Two feet (irregular plural)
(c) Two eyes
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) hands 1 M
(b) feet 1 M
(c) eyes 1 M
Q6 Action Words (Verbs)
Identify the action word (verb) in each sentence:
(a) The baby claps happily.
(b) Rohan taps his shoes on the floor.
(c) Birds fly in the sky.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) Action word = claps
(b) Action word = taps
(c) Action word = fly
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) claps 1 M
(b) taps 1 M
(c) fly 1 M
Q7 Spelling and Phonics
Fill in the missing letters to complete body part words:
(a) H _ _ D (used to think)
(b) N _ _ E (used to smell)
(c) E _ _ S (used to hear)
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) H E A D = HEAD
(b) N O S E = NOSE
(c) E A R S = EARS
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) HEAD 1 M
(b) NOSE 1 M

Q9 Hygiene & Health
Why should we wash our hands with soap before eating food?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Explanation:
• Our hands touch many surfaces and collect dust and invisible germs.
• Washing hands with soap removes harmful germs and prevents us from falling sick.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Germ removal explained 1 M
Disease prevention stated 1 M
Q10 Sentence Construction
Make a simple sentence using each word: (a) Feet (b) Smile.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sentences:
(a) Feet: I wear clean white socks on my feet.
(b) Smile: Mother has a lovely, warm smile.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct sentence with feet 1 M
Correct sentence with smile 1 M

Write the opposite of:
(a) Open × _______

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:

Q12 Pronunciation Practice
Read aloud and identify which word has the short 'a' sound like in 'clap':
(A) Tape (B) Cap (C) Shape
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• (B) Cap has the short 'a' vowel sound (/æ/) exactly like 'clap' and 'tap'.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Cap identified with correct vowel explanation 1 M
Q13 Body Actions Observation
Name two things you can do with your feet other than walking.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Run / Jog
2. Jump / Hop / Kick a football.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two valid actions named 2 M
Q14 Classroom Etiquette
When do children clap their hands in the classroom?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Children clap their hands to appreciate a classmate's good presentation, when singing rhymes together, or when celebrating someone's birthday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Appreciation / singing context explained 2 M
Q15 Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks from the poem:
One little _______ goes side to side.
Two little _______ are open wide.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
One little head goes side to side.
Two little eyes are open wide.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
head 1 M
eyes 1 M
Q16 True or False
State True or False:
(a) We hear birds chirping with our nose.
(b) We have two feet.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) False. We hear sounds with our ears, not our nose.
(b) True. Humans have two feet.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False with reason 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q17 Poem Reflection
What is the message of the poem 'Two Little Hands'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Summary:
• The poem joyfully celebrates our wonderful body parts and their coordinated movements, showing that every organ has a special and helpful role in our daily life.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Celebration of body coordination explained 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Morning Circle Time Action Song

During school morning assembly, Class 2 children sing 'Two Little Hands'. Riya claps her hands 3 times and taps her feet 4 times. Kabir nods his head and winks his left eye.

Analytical Task: Which body parts did Riya use? What action does the poem describe for our eyes and our head?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Riya used her hands (to clap) and her feet (to tap).
2. According to the poem, our two little eyes are open wide, and our one little head goes side to side.
Rubric: 1.5 M for identifying hands and feet + 1.5 M for explaining eye (open wide) and head (side to side) actions
