Chapter 1: Two Little Hands

Poem • Body Parts, Actions & Daily Routines | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Action words (clap, tap, wink, nod).
  • Rhyming words and body part awareness.
  • Singing with rhythm and coordination.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (3 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Textual Analysis
What is the central theme and message of the poem 'Two Little Hands'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. The chapter 'Two Little Hands' explores Body Parts, Actions & Daily Routines.
2. It teaches students essential vocabulary, rhythmic expression, and positive values such as action words (clap, tap, wink, nod).
3. Takeaway: Engaging with nature and family brings joy and learning.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Theme identification 1.5 M
Key message / lesson 1.5 M
Q2 Vocabulary & Language Drill
Find two action words (verbs) or rhyming words featured in 'Two Little Hands' and use them in simple sentences.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Language Practice:
• Word 1: Play — 'We play in the park together.'
• Word 2: Help — 'I help my friends with their books.'
• Both words show positive daily actions.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Selected valid words 1 M
Grammatically correct sentences 1 M
Q3 Speaking & Self Expression
How would you share your thoughts about 'Two Little Hands' with your teacher during Circle Time?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Response:
'I enjoyed learning about Two Little Hands because it reminds me of my own experiences with body parts, actions & daily routines. I love practicing the rhymes and actions with my classmates.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Expressive complete answer 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Two Little Hands Life-Skill Task

Children connect the themes of 'Two Little Hands' with their daily home and school experiences.

Analytical Task: What important lesson or value does the chapter 'Two Little Hands' teach young learners?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
It develops language skills, builds observational awareness about body parts, actions & daily routines, and encourages kindness, cooperation, and active learning.
Rubric: 1.5 M for core theme + 1.5 M for value articulation
