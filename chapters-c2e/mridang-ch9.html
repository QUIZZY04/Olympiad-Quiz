Chapter 9: Out in the Garden

Outdoor Play, Ball Games, Nature Care, Seasons & Sunshine | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem recitation on active garden play: bouncing, throwing, catching, and rolling a ball.
  • Observing natural garden life: blooming flowers, green grass, buzzing bees, and fluttering butterflies.
  • The value of outdoor active exercise vs screen time.
  • Nurturing plants by regular watering and protecting garden greenery.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Poem Comprehension • Ball Actions
What four actions with a ball are mentioned in the poem 'Out in the Garden'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. Bounce the ball
2. Throw the ball
3. Catch the ball
4. Roll the ball.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 actions named (bounce, throw, catch, roll) 2 M
Q2 Poem Comprehension • Weather
On what kind of day do children like to go out in the garden to play?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Children like to go out in the garden on each fine, sunny day when the sky is clear and the weather is pleasant.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Fine/sunny day identified 1 M

Q6 Plant Care • Green Garden
How can children help take care of their school garden?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Water the plants regularly using a watering can in the morning or evening.
2. Never pluck flowers or step on delicate young plant saplings.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Regular watering 1 M
Not plucking flowers/stepping on plants 1 M

Q8 Outdoor vs Indoor Play
Why is playing outdoors with friends better than sitting alone staring at mobile screens?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
1. Outdoor play involves running, throwing, and jumping, which keeps the body fit, agile, and healthy.
2. It builds real friendships and team spirit, and prevents eye strain caused by excessive screen exposure.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Physical fitness and agility 1 M
Friendship and eye strain prevention 1 M

Q14 True or False
State True or False:
(a) A heavy stone bounces higher than a rubber ball.
(b) Sunlight and water help garden plants grow healthy.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answers:
(a) False. A rubber ball is elastic and bounces high, while a heavy stone thuds on the ground without bouncing.
(b) True. Plants need sunlight, water, and soil to prepare food and grow.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False with reason 1 M
(b) True 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Class 2 Springtime Garden Activity

On a bright sunny afternoon, Rohan and Priya took a red rubber ball to the school herbal garden. Rohan bounced the ball on the paved garden path, and Priya caught it with both hands. Nearby, worker honeybees buzzed around yellow marigold blossoms, and green grasshoppers hopped on soft dew-covered grass.

Analytical Task: Which ball actions did Rohan and Priya perform? Which insects were observed in the garden? Why is playing in the garden refreshing?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Ball actions: Bouncing and catching.
2. Insects observed: Honeybees (drinking nectar from marigolds) and grasshoppers.
3. Refreshment: Playing in the open garden provides fresh oxygen, warm sunlight (Vitamin D), physical exercise, and connects children directly with the peace and beauty of nature.
Rubric: 1 M for ball actions + 1 M for insects observed + 1 M for health & nature benefits
