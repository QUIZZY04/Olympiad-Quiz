Geometry Questions for Olympiads

Boost your Math Olympiad (IMO) score by mastering properties of shapes, angles, and spatial reasoning.

Q1. In a right-angled triangle, if the two smaller sides are 3 cm and 4 cm, what is the length of the hypotenuse?

A) 5 cm
B) 6 cm
C) 7 cm
D) 25 cm

Correct Answer: A (Apply Pythagoras Theorem: 3² + 4² = 5²)

Q2. How many diagonals does a regular hexagon have?

A) 6
B) 9
C) 12
D) 15

Correct Answer: B [Formula: n(n-3)/2 where n=6]
