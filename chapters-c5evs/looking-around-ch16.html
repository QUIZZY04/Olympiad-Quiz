Chapter 16: Who will do this Work?

Sanitation Workers, Dignity of Labour, Mahatma Gandhi's Ashram Rules & Dr. Ambedkar | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Sanitation workers perform essential public hygiene duties (cleaning gutters, garbage collection, sweeping streets) under hazardous conditions.
  • Untouchability and caste discrimination forced specific communities into cleaning jobs for generations.
  • Mahatma Gandhi's Sabarmati Ashram strictly enforced that every resident—guest or leader—must clean their own toilet.
  • Dr. B.R. Ambedkar fought for the eradication of caste discrimination, establishing constitutional equality (Article 17 abolition of untouchability).
  • Modern technology: robotic sewer cleaners, suction machines, and protective safety suits must replace manual scavenging.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (24 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise
Why did Mahatma Gandhi insist that every person living in his ashram must clean their own toilet?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Mahatma Gandhi wanted to break the deeply rooted caste prejudice and untouchability in Indian society. In those days, cleaning toilets was viewed as low, dirty work reserved only for so-called lower castes. By making every resident, lawyer, and guest clean their own toilet, Gandhi promoted the dignity of all labour and taught that no honest work is beneath any human being.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Eradication of caste stigma and untouchability 1.5 Marks
Promotion of universal dignity of labour 1.5 Marks
Q2 Textbook Exercise
What hardships do sanitation workers face daily, and how can modern technology eliminate manual cleaning of sewers?
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Hardships Faced: Sanitation workers face grave health risks from toxic gases (hydrogen sulphide) in sewer pits, skin infections, respiratory diseases, social stigma, and lack of safety gear.
Technological Solutions:
  • Sewage Suction Trucks: Mechanised vacuum pumps clean deep drain blockages without human entry.
  • Manhole-Cleaning Robots: Robots (such as India's Bandicoot) equipped with cameras and robotic arms descend into manholes to clear sludge.
  • Protective Gear: Heavy waterproof suits, oxygen masks, and gas detectors ensure complete worker safety.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Health hazards and social discrimination faced 2 Marks
Robotic cleaners and mechanised suction trucks as solutions 2 Marks
Q3 Textbook Exercise
What does 'Dignity of Labour' mean? Why is every profession—from a sanitation worker to an engineer—equally essential for society?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Dignity of Labour means that all forms of honest work are honourable, valuable, and deserve equal human respect, with no job considered superior or inferior based on prejudice.
If engineers design bridges, sanitation workers keep towns free from deadly cholera and plague epidemics. If sanitation workers stopped cleaning for just one week, cities would overflow with garbage and disease. Therefore, every job that serves society has equal dignity.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Definition of dignity of labour and equal human respect 1.5 Marks
Crucial role of sanitation in public disease prevention 1.5 Marks
Q4 Textbook Exercise
Who was Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar? What historic provisions did he incorporate in the Indian Constitution to fight untouchability?
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Babasaheb) was the Chief Architect of the Constitution of India, a visionary legal scholar, and a champion of human rights from the Mahar community who suffered severe caste discrimination in childhood.
Constitutional Provisions:
  • Article 14: Guarantees equality before the law for all citizens irrespective of religion, caste, gender, or birth.
  • Article 17: Completely abolishes the practice of Untouchability in any form and declares it a punishable criminal offense.
  • Prohibited discrimination in public places, temples, schools, and water wells.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of Dr. Ambedkar as father of the Constitution 1.5 Marks
Article 17 abolition of untouchability as a punishable crime 1.5 Marks
Equal access to public facilities without caste discrimination 1 Mark
Q5 Textbook Exercise
How can every household practice waste segregation (wet vs dry waste) at home to help municipal sanitation workers?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Households should maintain two separate color-coded waste dustbins:
  • Green Bin (Wet Biodegradable Waste): Fruit peels, vegetable trimmings, leftover food, tea leaves, and eggshells. This waste can be turned into rich organic compost.
  • Blue Bin (Dry Recyclable Waste): Paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, metal cans, glass, and wrappers. This waste is recycled by scrap industries.
Segregating at source prevents sanitation workers from having to manually sift through hazardous, rotting garbage with bare hands.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Green bin for biodegradable wet food waste (composting) 1.5 Marks
Blue bin for dry recyclable materials & worker safety 1.5 Marks

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Eradicating Manual Scavenging & Upholding Dignity of Labour

Mahatma Gandhi and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar strongly condemned the caste-based practice of forcing specific communities to clean dry latrines and carry human excreta. In modern times, the government has banned manual scavenging and promotes robotic sewer cleaners like 'Bandicoot'.

Analytical Task: Why was manual scavenging an inhumane practice, and how does modern robotic technology uphold human dignity and worker safety?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Why Manual Scavenging is Inhumane: Manual scavenging forced marginalized caste groups to enter toxic underground sewer lines and septic tanks without protective gear, inhaling deadly sewer gases (methane, hydrogen sulphide) that caused fatal asphyxiation and chronic lung diseases. It reinforced untouchability and violated the fundamental constitutional right to life and dignity (Article 21). Role of Robotic Mechanization: 1. Zero Human Entry: Modern robotic units (such as 'Bandicoot') use multi-axis robotic arms and high-resolution cameras to break sludge and extract clogs remotely. 2. Rehabilitation into Skilled Technicians: Sanitation workers are trained to operate robotic control panels, transitioning from hazardous manual labour to respected technological professionals.
Rubric: 2.5 Marks for social justice and health hazard critique + 2.5 Marks for robotic sewer technology and dignity of labour = 5 Marks
