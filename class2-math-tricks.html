Class 2 Math Olympiad Tricks and Strategies

Learn smart techniques to solve Class 2 Olympiad math questions quickly and accurately.

Understanding Class 2 Olympiad Mathematics

In Class 2, Olympiad mathematics questions become slightly more advanced than Class 1. Students begin working with two-digit numbers, number patterns, addition and subtraction, and basic multiplication concepts.

The key goal at this level is to improve number sense and mental calculation ability. Students should focus on understanding numbers rather than memorizing formulas.

Trick 1: Tens and Ones Strategy

Large numbers can be simplified by separating them into tens and ones.

Example: 47 + 25 Break into tens and ones 40 + 20 = 60 7 + 5 = 12 Answer = 72

Trick 2: Quick Subtraction Trick

Instead of subtracting directly, round the number first and adjust later.

Example: 63 − 29 63 − 30 = 33 Add back 1 Answer = 34

Trick 3: Doubling Method

Multiplying by 2 is simply doubling the number.

Example: 2 × 14 14 + 14 = 28

Trick 4: Number Pattern Recognition

Many Olympiad questions test pattern recognition.

Example: 5, 10, 15, 20, ? Pattern = +5 Answer = 25

Trick 5: Estimation Method

Students can estimate answers quickly before calculating.

Example: 98 + 21 ≈ 100 + 20 ≈ 120 Actual answer = 119

Trick 6: Comparing Numbers Quickly

Students should practice identifying the largest and smallest numbers quickly.

Which number is greater? 56 or 65 Look at tens digit: 6 > 5 Answer = 65

Trick 7: Skip Counting

Skip counting helps in solving multiplication problems.

Example: 3 × 6 Count by 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 Answer = 18

Practice Tips for Students

  • Practice mental addition every day.
  • Learn number patterns.
  • Practice subtraction using number lines.
  • Improve observation skills.
  • Try solving Olympiad style puzzles.
