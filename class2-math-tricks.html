➖ Trick: Subtraction by Compensation

Class 2 students often struggle with "borrowing." compensation is the faster way: add the same value to both numbers to reach a friendly ten.

Problem: 53 - 19
The Trick: Add 1 to both sides.
1. 19 becomes 20.
2. 53 becomes 54.
3. 54 - 20 = 34.
Answer: 34.

🪞 Spatial Mastery: Mirror Symmetry

Mirror images test spatial intelligence. Teach the Mirror Law: Left becomes Right, but Top stays Top.

[Image of line of symmetry in geometric shapes]
Shortcut: In paper fold questions, a single fold "clones" the shape. Look for symmetrical partners in the answer options to eliminate 50% of the wrong answers.

💡 Guideline: Reverse Checking

Teach the child that $20 - 5 = 15$ is only true if $15 + 5 = 20$. Checking "backwards" is the fastest way to avoid silly mistakes on the final paper.
