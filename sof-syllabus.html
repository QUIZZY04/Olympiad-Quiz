Preparation Strategy & Recommended Books

  • Understand the Exam Pattern: Familiarize yourself with the marking scheme. Prioritize the Achievers Section as it carries more marks.
  • Focus on Logical Reasoning: This section often acts as a rank decider. Practice coding-decoding, blood relations, and spatial reasoning daily.
  • Take Timed Mock Tests: Accuracy under pressure is key. Use CBT interfaces to simulate the real exam environment.

Recommended Books:

  • NCERT / School prescribed textbooks for core concepts.
  • MTG Workbooks & Previous Years' Papers.
  • Reasoning & Aptitude books by R.S. Aggarwal.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is there negative marking in these Olympiads?

Generally, preliminary levels of Olympiads do not have negative marking for Class 1 to 10. However, always verify with the official guidelines for specific advanced levels.

What is the Achievers Section?

The Achievers Section contains Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions. These are more complex and carry a higher weightage (usually 2 or 3 marks per question), making them crucial for securing top ranks.
