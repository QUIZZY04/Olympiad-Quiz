Class 1 Math Olympiad Mastery

Building Visual Foundations: Number Sense, Spatial Logic, and Patterns.

🔢 Strategic Trick: The "Bridge-to-10" Method

At the Class 1 level, counting on fingers is the biggest time-waster. We teach students the Bridge-to-10 method to perform mental addition instantly.

Problem: 8 + 5
Trick: 8 needs 2 to make 10. Take 2 from the 5 (leaving 3).
Result: 10 + 3 = 13.

🧩 The "Nested Shape" Visualization

Olympiads love "Hidden Shape" questions. Students must learn to see shapes within shapes.

Problem: How many triangles are in a square with an 'X' inside?
Trick: Count the 4 small triangles first. Then, look for 4 large triangles made by combining two small ones. Total = 8.

💡 Preparation Roadmap

1. Active Reading: Circle action words like "Altogether" (+) or "Left" (-).
2. Pattern Units: Identify the unit of repeat in sequences (e.g., Red-Blue-Red).
3. 60-Second Rule: If a question takes too long, skip it and come back.
