Class 6 NSO Syllabus & Exam Pattern

The Class 6 Science Olympiad assesses students across three major sections aligned with the updated school syllabus.

  • Section 1
    Logical Reasoning

    Verbal and Non-Verbal Reasoning, Series Completion, Analogy, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Logical Venn Diagrams.

  • Section 2
    Science

    Food and its Components, Sorting Materials, Separation of Substances, Getting to Know Plants, Body Movements, Living Organisms, Motion and Measurement, Light, Shadows and Reflections, Electricity and Circuits, Fun with Magnets, Air and Water.

  • Section 3
    Achievers Section

    Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions based on the Science syllabus. These carry 3 marks each.

Why Science Olympiads are Crucial in Class 6

Class 6 is a transitional year where science splits into fundamental Physics, Chemistry, and Biology concepts. Participating in the Class 6 Science Olympiad builds a robust analytical foundation.
