Your privacy is important to us

At Olympiad Quiz, protecting the privacy of our users is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information that users provide when using our website.

The information collected from users may be used for several purposes, including:

Cookies

Olympiad Quiz may use cookies to improve website functionality and user experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the website remember certain information about your visit.

Cookies may be used to:

  • Remember user preferences

Users can choose to disable cookies through their browser settings if they prefer.

Our website may use third-party tools or services such as analytics providers to better understand how users interact with the platform. These third-party services may collect certain anonymous information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We take appropriate measures to protect user data and maintain the security of the website. However, no method of online data transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

Olympiad Quiz is designed for students preparing for academic competitions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate supervision.

Updates to This Policy
