Q1
What made Mrs. Amelia Slater assume that her elderly father, Abel Merryweather, was dead?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Mrs. Amelia Slater went upstairs to take her father a cup of tea. She found him lying motionless, cold as stone, with his eyes closed and no sound of breathing. Instead of calling a doctor to verify death, she jumped to the conclusion that he was dead, immediately ordering her husband to put on black and telegramming her sister Elizabeth to come divide his belongings.

Q3
How do Elizabeth and Ben Jordan behave upon arriving? How is their hypocrisy exposed?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Elizabeth and Ben Jordan arrive dressed in formal mourning, putting on a show of grief and weeping loudly. However, their concern is entirely monetary: without even going upstairs to look at their father's 'corpse', they immediately demand to search for his will, discuss whether he paid his premium insurance, and squabble over who gets his gold watch and mourning clothes.

Q5
How is 'The Dear Departed' a biting satire on filial ingratitude, middle-class hypocrisy, and commercialized family relationships? What bombshell does Abel drop at the climax?
8 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Satire on Filial Ingratitude:
Stanley Houghton satirizes the degrading decay of moral values in suburban middle-class society. The daughters, Amelia Slater and Elizabeth Jordan, view their seventy-two-year-old father not with loving reverence, but as an inconvenient financial encumbrance whose death is celebrated as an opportunity to plunder furniture, jewelry, and insurance money.

2. Hypocrisy and Pettiness:
Both sisters compete in vulgar rivalry, fighting tooth and nail over an old man's clock, bureau, and flannel trousers before his body has even cooled. They care nothing for his welfare, passing him back and forth like a parcel.

3. Abel's Masterstroke (The Bombshell):
Realizing that his daughters are heartless vultures who wished him dead, Abel delivers poetic justice. He announces three life-altering decisions:

  • He will alter his will at his lawyer's on Monday, leaving all his money, estate, and possessions entirely to whoever he is living with at the time of his death.
  • He will pay his insurance premium himself.
  • At twelve o'clock on Monday, he will marry Mrs. John Shorrocks, the cheerful landlady of the 'Ring-o'-Bells' pub, who genuinely cares for his comfort.

Abel walks out triumphant, leaving both greedy sisters stunned, humiliated, and empty-handed.

Dramatic Irony & Humor
Analysis: How does the innocent child Victoria serve as the moral conscience of the family in 'The Dear Departed'?

Ten-year-old Victoria is the only character untouched by greed. When Amelia orders her to help steal the bureau, Victoria asks innocently: 'Are we pinching it?' When Abel descends, Victoria is overjoyed that her grandfather is alive. Her uncorrupted innocence acts as a mirror that magnifies the grotesque greed and moral rot of her parents.
