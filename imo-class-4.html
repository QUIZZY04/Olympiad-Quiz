Class 4 IMO Syllabus & Exam Pattern

The Class 4 Math Olympiad test assesses students across four major sections. Our practice perfectly aligns with this syllabus.

  • Section 1
    Logical Reasoning

    Patterns, Alphabet Test, Coding-Decoding, Ranking Test, Mirror Images, Geometrical Shapes, Direction Sense, Analogy and Classification.

  • Section 2
    Mathematical Reasoning

    Numerals, Number Names, Computation Operations, Fractions, Length, Weight, Capacity, Time, Money, Geometry, Perimeter of Various Shapes, Symmetry, Data Handling.

  • Section 3
    Everyday Mathematics

    Word problems applying Mathematical Reasoning concepts to real-world scenarios.

  • Section 4
    Achievers Section

    Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions based on the syllabus. These questions carry 2 marks each.
