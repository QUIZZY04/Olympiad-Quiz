Class 11 JEE Main Core Syllabus Highlights

Approximately 45-50% of the final JEE Main paper consists of the Class 11 syllabus. A strong grip on these subjects is vital for a top percentile.

  • Physics
    Mechanics & Thermodynamics

    Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work Energy and Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, Properties of Solids and Liquids, Thermodynamics, Kinetic Theory of Gases.

  • Chemistry
    Physical & Organic Foundations

    Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry, Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Redox Reactions, Basic Principles of Organic Chemistry.

  • Mathematics
    Algebra & Coordinate Geometry

    Sets, Relations and Functions, Complex Numbers, Quadratic Equations, Permutations and Combinations, Binomial Theorem, Sequence and Series, Straight Lines, Conic Sections.

Why Start JEE Prep in Class 11?

Class 11 acts as the critical bridge between basic school science and advanced engineering concepts. Concepts like Calculus, Mechanics, and Organic Chemistry introduced in Class 11 serve as the prerequisite for the entire Class 12 syllabus.

By utilizing our Class 11 JEE Main free mock tests, students acclimatize to the NTA CBT (Computer Based Test) environment early. This eliminates exam anxiety and builds the stamina required to sit for 3-hour long technical exams.

Our platform ensures you never repeat mistakes by providing detailed step-by-step solutions for both MCQs and Numerical Value Type questions.
