Chapter 9: Merry-Go-Round

Poem: Fairground Fun, Riding Up and Down | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of Merry-Go-Round: Understanding key narrative actions and poetic rhythm.
  • Vocabulary building, rhyming words, and simple sight word recognition.
  • Sentence formation and moral values: friendship, courage, and cleanliness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Poem Opening
What ride did the child climb up on in the poem 'Merry-Go-Round'?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The child climbed up on the merry-go-round.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Merry-go-round 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • Movement of Ride
How did the merry-go-round move?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
It went round and round.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Round and round 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • Ride Animal
What did the child climb upon on the merry-go-round?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The child climbed up on a big brown wooden horse.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Big brown horse 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • Horse Movement
How did the big brown horse move?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
It went up and down.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Up and down 1 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • Joyful Motion
Recite the line describing the combined motion of the ride.
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
'Around and round, and up and down, around and round, and up and down!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Around and round and up and down 1 M
Q6 Reading Comprehension • High Riding
Where did the child sit while riding?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The child sat up high on the big brown horse.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sat up high 1 M
Q7 Let us Listen • Rhyming Words
Find words that rhyme with:
(a) Round
(b) Down
(c) Brown
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Rhyme with Round: Ground, Sound, Bound, Found.
(b) Rhyme with Down: Town, Clown, Crown.
(c) Rhyme with Brown: Drown, Frown, Town.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Round rhyme 1 M
Down rhyme 1 M
Brown rhyme 1 M
Q8 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct poem words:
(a) I climbed up on the merry-go-round and it went _______ (round and round / fast and slow).
(b) I climbed up on a big brown _______ (horse / elephant).
(c) And it went _______ (up and down / backwards).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) I climbed up on the merry-go-round and it went round and round.
(b) I climbed up on a big brown horse.
(c) And it went up and down.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
round and round 1 M
horse 1 M
up and down 1 M
Q9 Let us Write • Opposites
Write the opposite word for:
(a) Up → ________
(b) High → ________
(c) Big → ________
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Up → Down
(b) High → Low
(c) Big → Small
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Down 1 M
Low 1 M
Small 1 M

Q11 Let us Speak • Fair Experience
Have you ever sat on a merry-go-round in a village fair or park? How did it feel?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Yes! It felt very exciting, breezy, and dizzy with laughter as the world spun around.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Q12 Let us Speak • Shapes in Rides
What geometric shape path does the merry-go-round follow as it turns?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
It turns in a circle (round shape).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Circle / circular path 1 M
Q13 True or False • Poem Facts
State True or False:
(a) The horse on the merry-go-round was made of wood and painted brown.
(b) The ride went straight in a long line without turning.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) True. It was a big brown wooden horse.
(b) False. It went round and round in a circle.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) True 1 M
(b) False 1 M

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Spiral / circles identified 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Merry-Go-Round Real-World Analysis

In 'Merry-Go-Round', children engage with everyday life lessons, discovering how friendship, family, nature, and positive actions bring happiness and solve daily challenges.

Analytical Task: What is the key lesson or joyful message conveyed in 'Merry-Go-Round'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The chapter teaches children the importance of kindness, observation, and creative problem solving through engaging storytelling and rhythmic poetry.
Rubric: 1.5 M for core concept identification + 1.5 M for value application
