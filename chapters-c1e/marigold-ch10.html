Chapter 10: Circle

Story: Grandmother and Mohini Drawing Shapes | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of Circle: Understanding key narrative actions and poetic rhythm.
  • Vocabulary building, rhyming words, and simple sight word recognition.
  • Sentence formation and moral values: friendship, courage, and cleanliness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Story Characters
Who were sitting together drawing pictures in the story 'Circle'?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mohini and her Grandmother were sitting together.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mohini and Grandmother 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • First Drawing
What shape did Grandmother draw first on the paper?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Grandmother drew a circle.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
A circle 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • Transformation 1
What did Grandmother turn the circle into, and how?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
She drew three lines on the circle and turned it into a ball!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Drew three lines 1 M
Turned into a ball 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • Mohini's Turn
What did Mohini draw after seeing Grandmother's ball?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mohini drew three lines on her circle and made a real ball that really bounced!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Drew a ball that bounced 1 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • Transformation 2
What did Grandmother draw next from a circle?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Grandmother added a zig-zag line to the circle and made a balloon!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Added zig-zag line 1 M
Made a balloon 1 M
Q6 Reading Comprehension • Colorful Balloons
What colours of balloons did Mohini draw all over her paper?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mohini drew red, blue, green, and yellow balloons.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Red, blue, green, yellow 2 M
Q7 Reading Comprehension • Other Circle Objects
What other round things did Mohini draw using circles?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mohini drew a wheel, the moon, the sun, a rabbit, and her own face!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Wheel, moon, sun 1 M
Rabbit and her own face 1 M
Q8 Let us Speak • Round Objects Around Us
Look around your classroom or kitchen. Name three things that are round like a circle.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A clock, a dinner plate, a coin, and a chapati (roti) are round like a circle.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three round objects named 2 M
Q9 Let us Listen • Rhyming Words
Find words that rhyme with:
(a) Ball
(b) Moon
(c) Sun
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Rhyme with Ball: Call, Fall, Tall, Wall.
(b) Rhyme with Moon: Soon, Spoon, Noon.
(c) Rhyme with Sun: Fun, Run, Bun.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Ball rhyme 1 M
Moon rhyme 1 M
Sun rhyme 1 M
Q10 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct story words:
(a) Grandmother drew a _______ (circle / square).
(b) Grandmother drew three lines on the circle to make a _______ (ball / book).
(c) Mohini drew a zig-zag line to make a _______ (balloon / snake).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Grandmother drew a circle.
(b) Grandmother drew three lines on the circle to make a ball.
(c) Mohini drew a zig-zag line to make a balloon.

circle 1 M

Q13 True or False • Story Facts
State True or False:
(a) Mohini could not draw a circle even after trying.
(b) Mohini drew her own face using a circle.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. Mohini drew many beautiful circles.
(b) True. She drew eyes, nose, mouth and made her own face.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q14 Let us Do • Creative Drawing
How can you turn a circle into a smiling sun?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Draw a circle, draw eyes and a smiling mouth inside, and draw straight rays of sunshine all around the edge!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear steps to draw smiling sun 1 M
Q15 Think and Share • Family Bonding
What do you like to draw or make when sitting with your grandmother or grandfather?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
I like to draw flowers, animals, and houses, and listen to interesting childhood stories from them.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Thoughtful child response 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Circle Real-World Analysis

In 'Circle', children engage with everyday life lessons, discovering how friendship, family, nature, and positive actions bring happiness and solve daily challenges.

Analytical Task: What is the key lesson or joyful message conveyed in 'Circle'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The chapter teaches children the importance of kindness, observation, and creative problem solving through engaging storytelling and rhythmic poetry.
Rubric: 1.5 M for core concept identification + 1.5 M for value application
