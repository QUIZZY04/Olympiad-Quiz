NCERT Class 1 English • Classic CBSE

Chapter 1: A Happy Child

Poem: My House is Red - Play & Joy | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of A Happy Child: Understanding key narrative actions and poetic rhythm.
  • Vocabulary building, rhyming words, and simple sight word recognition.
  • Sentence formation and moral values: friendship, courage, and cleanliness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Poem Details
What colour is the happy child's house in the poem 'A Happy Child'?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The happy child's house is red ('My house is red, a little house').
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Red 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • Daily Activity
What does the child do the whole day long?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The child laughs and plays the whole day long ('I laugh and play the whole day long').
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Laughs and plays all day 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • Child's Nature
Does the happy child cry often?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
No, the child hardly ever cries ('A happy child am I, I hardly ever cry').
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hardly ever cries 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • Nature Around
What is there near the child's house to give shade from the hot sun?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
There is a green, green tree to provide shade from the hot sun.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Green tree gives shade 1 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • Rest Time
What does the child do when all the play is done?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The child sits under the green tree when all the play is done.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sits under the green tree 1 M
Q6 Let us Speak • Colors Around Us
What is the colour of:
(a) The happy child's house?
(b) The tree?
(c) The sun?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) The house is Red.
(b) The tree is Green.
(c) The sun is Yellow / Golden.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Red 1 M
Green 1 M
Yellow 1 M
Q7 Let us Speak • Personal Connection
What is the colour of your own house, and do you have a tree near your home?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The colour of my house is white/yellow (child's own colour), and yes, there is a green neem/mango tree near my home.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
States house colour and tree presence 1 M
Q8 Let us Listen • Rhyming Words
Find words in the poem that rhyme with:
(a) Cry
(b) Sun
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Rhyme with Cry: I, Sky, My, Fly.
(b) Rhyme with Sun: Done, Run, Fun, Bun.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Cry rhyme 1 M
Sun rhyme 1 M
Q9 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct poem words:
(a) My house is _______ (red / blue), a little house.
(b) And under it I often sit, when all my play is _______ (done / begun).
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) My house is red, a little house.
(b) And under it I often sit, when all my play is done.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
red 1 M
done 1 M
Q10 Let us Write • Opposites
Write the opposite word for:
(a) Happy → ________
(b) Cry → ________
(c) Little → ________
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Happy → Sad
(b) Cry → Laugh
(c) Little → Big
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sad 1 M
Laugh 1 M
Big 1 M

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) H O U S E (HOUSE)

(c) P L A Y (PLAY)

Q12 Let us Write • Matching
Match the words with their meanings or colors:
1. Tree    (A) Red
2. House    (B) Shade from sun
3. Sun     (C) Hot & bright
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:

• 2. House → (A) Red
• 3. Sun → (C) Hot & bright
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme

Q13 True or False • Poem Facts
State True or False:
(a) The child cries all through the day.
(b) The green tree gives shade when it is hot.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. The child laughs and plays all day.
(b) True. The tree shades the child from the sun.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q14 Think and Share • Emotions
What makes you feel happy like the child in the poem?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Playing with my friends, eating sweet fruits, and listening to bedtime stories make me feel very happy.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Child-friendly personal response 1 M
Q15 Let us Do • Drawing Smiles
Draw a happy smiling face and a sad face. Which face do you like to wear every day?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
I like to wear a happy smiling face every day because a smile spreads joy to everyone around!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Happy face with reason 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

A Happy Child Real-World Analysis

In 'A Happy Child', children engage with everyday life lessons, discovering how friendship, family, nature, and positive actions bring happiness and solve daily challenges.

Analytical Task: What is the key lesson or joyful message conveyed in 'A Happy Child'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The chapter teaches children the importance of kindness, observation, and creative problem solving through engaging storytelling and rhythmic poetry.
Rubric: 1.5 M for core concept identification + 1.5 M for value application
