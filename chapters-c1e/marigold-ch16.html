NCERT Class 1 English • Classic CBSE

Chapter 16: The Tiger and the Mosquito

Story: Pride, Humility & Not Being Proud | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of The Tiger and the Mosquito: Understanding key narrative actions and poetic rhythm.
  • Vocabulary building, rhyming words, and simple sight word recognition.
  • Sentence formation and moral values: friendship, courage, and cleanliness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Story Opening
What was the tiger doing when the story starts in 'The Tiger and the Mosquito'?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A tiger was dozing under a tree.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Dozing under a tree 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • The Intruder
Who came buzzing by while the tiger was resting?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A tiny mosquito came buzzing by ('Buzzzz, buzzzz!').
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
A mosquito buzzing by 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • Tiger's Pride
What did the angry tiger tell the mosquito?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The tiger growled: 'Hey! Mosquito! Go away!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Go away growl 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • Mosquito's Reply
What did the mosquito say back to the proud tiger?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The mosquito said: 'Why should I go away? I am not afraid of you!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Why should I go away? 1 M
Not afraid of you 1 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • The First Strike
What happened when the furious tiger struck out with his paw?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The mosquito flew off, and the tiger's heavy paw struck his own cheek!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mosquito flew off 1 M
Paw struck own cheek 1 M
Q6 Reading Comprehension • Pain and Bleeding
What happened to the tiger's cheek after he hit himself?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The blow scraped his cheek, the skin broke, and it began to bleed.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Scraped cheek 1 M
Began to bleed 1 M
Q7 Reading Comprehension • Second Attempt
What happened when the tiger struck with his other paw?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The mosquito buzzed off again, and the tiger hit his other cheek too!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hit other cheek 2 M
Q8 Reading Comprehension • Tiger's Defeat
What did the helpless tiger finally do?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The tiger was helpless. He quietly got up and walked away in shame.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Walked away quietly 1 M
Q9 Reading Comprehension • The Mosquito's Parting Words
What valuable advice did the mosquito call out to the walking tiger?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The mosquito said: 'Don't be so proud, my friend. Everyone is great in their own way!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Don't be proud quote 1 M
Everyone is great in their own way 1 M
Q10 Let us Listen • Sound Words
What sound does a mosquito make when it flies around our ears?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A mosquito makes a high-pitched 'Buzzzz, buzzzz!' sound.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Buzzzz sound 1 M
Q11 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct story words:
(a) A tiger was _______ (dozing / dancing) under a tree.
(b) The tiger struck out with his _______ (paw / tail).
(c) Everyone is great in his own _______ (way / size).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) A tiger was dozing under a tree.
(b) The tiger struck out with his paw.
(c) Everyone is great in his own way.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
dozing 1 M
paw 1 M
way 1 M
Q12 Let us Write • Missing Letters
Complete the spellings:
(a) T _ G E R
(b) P _ W
(c) C H _ _ K
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) T I G E R (TIGER)
(b) P A W (PAW)
(c) C H E E K (CHEEK)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
TIGER 1 M
PAW 1 M
CHEEK 1 M
Q13 True or False • Story Facts
State True or False:
(a) The tiny mosquito was terrified and bowed before the tiger.
(b) Being very big and strong does not mean you can bully smaller creatures.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. The mosquito was fearless.
(b) True. True strength lies in humility, not bullying.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q14 Think and Share • Moral of the Story
What is the main lesson taught by 'The Tiger and the Mosquito'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Never be vain or overly proud of your size or power. Every living creature has unique strengths and deserves respect.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Never be too proud 1 M
Respect everyone / unique strengths 1 M
Q15 Let us Write • Opposites
Write the opposite word for:
(a) Proud → ________
(b) Quiet → ________
(c) Friend → ________
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Proud → Humble
(b) Quiet → Noisy / Loud
(c) Friend → Enemy
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Humble 1 M
Noisy/Loud 1 M
Enemy 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

The Tiger and the Mosquito Real-World Analysis

In 'The Tiger and the Mosquito', children engage with everyday life lessons, discovering how friendship, family, nature, and positive actions bring happiness and solve daily challenges.

Analytical Task: What is the key lesson or joyful message conveyed in 'The Tiger and the Mosquito'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The chapter teaches children the importance of kindness, observation, and creative problem solving through engaging storytelling and rhythmic poetry.
Rubric: 1.5 M for core concept identification + 1.5 M for value application
