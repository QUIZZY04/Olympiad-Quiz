Chapter 9: Anandi's Rainbow

Colours of Nature, VIBGYOR, Joy of Painting & After-Rain Sky | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: Anandi dreaming of rainbows and painting her garden.
  • The seven colours of the rainbow: Violet, Indigo, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red.
  • Observing natural phenomena after rainfall.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Story Details
What was Anandi doing when the story starts, and what was she dreaming about?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Anandi was fast asleep and dreaming of a huge, bright rainbow.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Dreaming of a rainbow 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • The View Outside
What did Anandi see when she woke up and looked out of her window?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
She saw a huge, bright rainbow stretching across the clear blue sky.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Huge bright rainbow across blue sky 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • Pet Companion
Who was Anandi's favourite companion who ran out into the garden with her?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Her favourite pet cat named Milly ran out with her.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pet cat Milly 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • Anandi's Idea
What did Anandi want to do with the flowers in her garden using her paint box?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
She wanted to paint the flowers of her garden with the colours of the rainbow.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Paint flowers with rainbow colours 2 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • Colors of Flowers
Which colors did Anandi paint on the garden flowers? Name at least three colors.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
She painted one flower with violet, one with indigo, one with blue, one with orange, and one with red.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Names at least three rainbow colors correctly 2 M
Q6 Reading Comprehension • The Sun's Color
Which colour did Anandi leave behind, and why?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
She left the yellow colour so that the sun could shine bright, warm, and yellow in the sky.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Yellow left 1 M
For the sun to shine bright 1 M
Q7 Reading Comprehension • The Garden Finale
How did the garden look after Anandi finished painting the flowers?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The garden looked glorious and beautiful with beautiful, colourful flowers all over!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Glorious and beautiful with colorful flowers 1 M
Q8 Let us Speak • Rainbow Science
How many colours are there in a rainbow? Name all seven colours in order (VIBGYOR).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
There are seven colours in a rainbow:
1. V - Violet
2. I - Indigo
3. B - Blue
4. G - Green
5. Y - Yellow
6. O - Orange
7. R - Red
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
7 colors stated 1 M
VIBGYOR acronym explained 2 M
Q9 Let us Listen • Rhyming Rainbow Words
Find words that rhyme with:
(a) Bright
(b) Cat
(c) Blue
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Rhyme with Bright: Light, Night, White.
(b) Rhyme with Cat: Hat, Mat, Bat, Fat.
(c) Rhyme with Blue: Glue, Clue, True, Shoe.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bright rhyme 1 M
Cat rhyme 1 M
Blue rhyme 1 M
Q10 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct story words:
(a) Anandi loved to draw and _______ (sing / paint).
(b) Milly was a pet _______ (cat / parrot).
(c) The rainbow was gone, but the flowers remained _______ (colourful / dead).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Anandi loved to draw and paint.
(b) Milly was a pet cat.
(c) The rainbow was gone, but the flowers remained colourful.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
paint 1 M
cat 1 M
colourful 1 M
Q11 Let us Write • Missing Letters
Complete the spellings of words from the chapter:
(a) R _ I N B _ W
(b) G _ R D _ N
(c) F L _ W _ R
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) R A I N B O W (RAINBOW)
(b) G A R D E N (GARDEN)
(c) F L O W E R (FLOWER)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
RAINBOW 1 M
GARDEN 1 M
FLOWER 1 M
Q12 Let us Write • Matching Colours
Match each natural object with its colour in nature:
1. Leaf    (A) Yellow
2. Sky     (B) Green
3. Sun     (C) Blue
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• 1. Leaf → (B) Green
• 2. Sky → (C) Blue
• 3. Sun → (A) Yellow
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 color matches correct 2 M
Q13 Think and Share • When Rainbow Appears
When do we see a rainbow in the sky? What two things must happen?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
We see a rainbow when the sun shines right after or during light rain showers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sun shines 1 M
After/during rain showers 1 M
Q14 True or False • Rainbow Facts
State True or False:
(a) A rainbow has nine different colours.
(b) Anandi used her paint box to colour the garden flowers.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. A rainbow has 7 colours.
(b) True. Anandi painted the flowers with her paints.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q15 Let us Do • Color Mixing
What color do you get when you mix:
(a) Yellow + Blue?
(b) Red + Yellow?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Yellow + Blue = Green
(b) Red + Yellow = Orange
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Green 1 M
Orange 1 M
Q16 Let us Speak • Creative Imagination
If you could paint anything in your classroom with rainbow colours, what would you paint?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
I would paint my desk, pencil box, and classroom door with bright rainbow colours!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Imaginative child-friendly response 1 M
Q17 Let us Write • Sentence Completion
Fill in the blank: 'The rainbow disappeared into the ________ (clouds / water) after the sun went down.'
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
'The rainbow disappeared into the clouds after the sun went down.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
clouds 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Anandi's Garden Flowers

When Anandi woke up, she saw a huge, bright rainbow arching across the clear blue sky. She ran outside with her paintbrushes to paint the flowers in her garden with the rainbow colours.

Analytical Task: What did Anandi see in the sky? What did she decide to paint with those colours?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Anandi saw a huge, bright rainbow arching across the clear blue sky.
2. She decided to paint the flowers in her garden with the colours of the rainbow.
Rubric: 1 M for bright rainbow + 1 M for painting garden flowers
