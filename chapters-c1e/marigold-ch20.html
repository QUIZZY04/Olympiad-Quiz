NCERT Class 1 English • Classic CBSE

Chapter 20: The Tailor and his Friend

Story: Kalu the Tailor & Appu the Elephant | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of The Tailor and his Friend: Understanding key narrative actions and poetic rhythm.
  • Vocabulary building, rhyming words, and simple sight word recognition.
  • Sentence formation and moral values: friendship, courage, and cleanliness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Story Characters
Who were the two friends in the story 'The Tailor and his Friend'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Kalu the tailor and Appu the elephant were good friends.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Kalu the tailor 1 M
Appu the elephant 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • Kalu's Shop
Where was Kalu's shop located, and what clothes did he stitch?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Kalu's shop was near the river. He stitched colourful pants and shirts for children.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Near the river 1 M
Colourful pants and shirts 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • Daily Friendship
What did Kalu give to Appu every day when Appu visited the shop?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Kalu always gave Appu many nice things to eat (fruits and sugarcane).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Nice things to eat 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • The Mischievous Trick
What naughty trick did Kalu play on Appu one day instead of giving him food?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Kalu took out a needle and pricked Appu's trunk with the needle!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Took out a needle 1 M
Pricked Appu's trunk 1 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • Appu's Pain
What did Appu do when Kalu pricked him with the needle?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Appu ran away in great pain.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Ran away in pain 1 M
Q6 Reading Comprehension • The River Revenge
What did Appu do at the river two days later?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Appu went to the river, filled his long trunk with muddy water, and walked back to Kalu's shop.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Filled trunk with muddy water 1 M
Walked back to Kalu's shop 1 M
Q7 Reading Comprehension • The Water Splash
What did Appu do inside Kalu's shop with the water in his trunk?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Appu squirted water everywhere, and all the new clothes in the shop became wet and muddy!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Squirted water everywhere 1 M
All new clothes became wet and muddy 1 M
Q8 Reading Comprehension • Kalu's Realization
What did Kalu shake his head and say to Appu?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Kalu said: 'An elephant never forgets! Let's be friends again, Appu.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
An elephant never forgets quote 1 M
Let's be friends again 1 M
Q9 Reading Comprehension • Friendship Restored
Did Kalu ever prick Appu with a needle again?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
No, Kalu never pricked Appu again and they remained good friends forever.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Never pricked again / good friends 1 M
Q10 Let us Listen • Rhyming Words
Find words that rhyme with:
(a) Shop
(b) Wet
(c) Friend
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Rhyme with Shop: Hop, Pop, Top, Stop.
(b) Rhyme with Wet: Pet, Set, Get, Net.
(c) Rhyme with Friend: Bend, Send, End, Mend.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Shop rhyme 1 M
Wet rhyme 1 M
Friend rhyme 1 M
Q11 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct story words:
(a) Kalu the _______ (tailor / cobbler) had a shop near the river.
(b) Appu the _______ (elephant / horse) came to his shop every day.
(c) Kalu pricked Appu's trunk with a _______ (needle / pin).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Kalu the tailor had a shop near the river.
(b) Appu the elephant came to his shop every day.
(c) Kalu pricked Appu's trunk with a needle.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
tailor 1 M
elephant 1 M
needle 1 M
Q12 Let us Write • Missing Letters
Complete the spellings:
(a) T _ I L O R
(b) E L _ P H A N T
(c) N _ _ D L E
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) T A I L O R (TAILOR)
(b) E L E P H A N T (ELEPHANT)
(c) N E E D L E (NEEDLE)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
TAILOR 1 M
ELEPHANT 1 M
NEEDLE 1 M
Q13 True or False • Story Facts
State True or False:
(a) It is good and funny to hurt an animal with sharp objects.
(b) Appu taught Kalu that friends should never hurt or play mean tricks on each other.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. We should never hurt animals.
(b) True. True friends treat each other with kindness.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q14 Think and Share • Moral of the Story
What lesson do we learn about friendship from Kalu and Appu?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
We learn that we should never tease, trick, or hurt our friends. If we make a mistake, we must say sorry and make peace.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Never tease or hurt friends 1 M
Say sorry and restore peace 1 M
Q15 Let us Speak • Community Helpers
What does a tailor do, and what tools does he use?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A tailor stitches clothes. He uses a sewing machine, needle, thread, scissors, and measuring tape.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Stitches clothes 1 M
Lists tailor tools 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

The Tailor and his Friend Real-World Analysis

In 'The Tailor and his Friend', children engage with everyday life lessons, discovering how friendship, family, nature, and positive actions bring happiness and solve daily challenges.

Analytical Task: What is the key lesson or joyful message conveyed in 'The Tailor and his Friend'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The chapter teaches children the importance of kindness, observation, and creative problem solving through engaging storytelling and rhythmic poetry.
Rubric: 1.5 M for core concept identification + 1.5 M for value application
