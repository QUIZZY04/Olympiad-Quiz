Chapter 2: Greetings

Everyday Manners: Good Morning, Thank You, Please & Introductions | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Appropriate greetings for different times of day: Good morning, Good afternoon, Good night.
  • Courteous magic words: Please, Thank you, Sorry, Excuse me.
  • Introducing oneself clearly: Name, age, and class.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Speak • Morning Greeting
What do you say to your teacher when you reach school in the morning?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
We say: 'Good morning, Teacher!' with a cheerful smile.

Q10 Let us Write • Time of Day Matching

3. Night      (C) Good morning
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

First Day at School

On the first day of Class 1, Aarav meets his class teacher Mrs. Sen at the door. He smiles and politely greets her.

Analytical Task: What greeting should Aarav say to his teacher in the morning? What should he say when she hands him a new pencil?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Aarav should say, 'Good morning, Teacher!'
2. When given a pencil, he should say, 'Thank you, Teacher!'
Rubric: 1 M for Good morning + 1 M for Thank you
