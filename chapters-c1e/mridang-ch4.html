Chapter 4: The Cap-Seller and the Monkeys

Classic Folktale: Cleverness, Animal Mimicry & Problem Solving | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: sequence of events and problem-solution.
  • Understanding monkey behavior: imitation and mimicry.
  • Moral of clever thinking in difficult situations.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Story Fact
What was the cap-seller carrying in his bag to sell in the village fair?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
The cap-seller was carrying colourful caps in his bag.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Colourful caps 1 M
Q2 Comprehension • The Problem
What happened when the cap-seller woke up from his nap?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
When he woke up, he saw that his bag was empty and all the monkeys on the tree branches were wearing his caps.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bag was empty 1 M
Monkeys wearing caps 1 M
Q3 Clever Solution • Mimicry
How did the cap-seller get his caps back from the monkeys?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
He knew that monkeys copy what people do. He took off his own cap and threw it on the ground. Seeing this, all the monkeys also took off their caps and threw them down!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Monkeys imitate actions 1.5 M
Threw cap on ground and monkeys copied 1.5 M
Q4 Vocabulary • Sound Words
What sound does a monkey make?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
A monkey makes a chatter-chatter / whoop / gibber sound.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Chatter / whoop 1 M
Q5 Moral and Life Skill
What lesson do we learn from 'The Cap-Seller and the Monkeys'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
We learn that calm and clever thinking can solve difficult problems when shouting or getting angry does not work.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clever thinking solves problems 2 M
Q6 Phonics • Rhyming Pairs
Write three rhyming words for 'CAP'.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Rhyming words for Cap: Map, Lap, Tap, Nap.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three valid rhymes provided 2 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Under the Banyan Tree

A tired cap-seller placed his bag of red, blue, and green caps under a banyan tree and fell asleep. Mischievous monkeys living on the tree branches climbed down and each took a colourful cap.

Analytical Task: Where did the cap-seller keep his bag? What did the monkeys do while he was sleeping?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The cap-seller kept his bag under the big banyan tree.
2. The monkeys climbed down, opened the bag, took the caps, and wore them on their heads.
Rubric: 1 M for under the tree + 1 M for wearing caps
