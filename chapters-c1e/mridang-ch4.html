Chapter 4: The Cap-Seller and the Monkeys

Classic Folktale: Cleverness, Animal Mimicry & Problem Solving | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Story comprehension: sequence of events and problem-solution.
  • Understanding monkey behavior: imitation and mimicry.
  • Moral of clever thinking in difficult situations.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Story Details
What did the cap-seller carry in his basket to sell in the village?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The cap-seller carried many colourful caps in his basket (red, blue, green, and yellow caps).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Colourful caps in basket 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • Setting
Why did the cap-seller sit down under a large banyan tree?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
He was tired and sleepy after walking in the hot sun, so he sat in the cool shade to take a rest.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tired/sleepy and rested in cool shade 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • Characters
Who lived on the branches of the big banyan tree?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A group of naughty, playful monkeys lived on the branches of the tree.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Playful monkeys 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • Plot
What did the monkeys do while the cap-seller was fast asleep?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The monkeys climbed down quietly, took all the caps from the basket, put them on their heads, and climbed back up!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Took caps from basket 1 M
Wore them on heads on the tree 1 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • Conflict
What did the cap-seller see when he woke up from his nap?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
He saw that his basket was completely empty and all the monkeys on the tree were wearing his caps!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Empty basket 1 M
Monkeys wearing his caps 1 M
Q6 Reading Comprehension • Action
What did the cap-seller do when he got angry and shouted at the monkeys?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
He shouted, shook his fist, and stamped his feet at the monkeys.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Shouted and shook his fist 1 M
Q7 Reading Comprehension • Monkey Behavior
What did the monkeys do when the cap-seller shook his fist at them?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The monkeys copied him! They also shook their fists and chattered back at him.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Copied him and shook fists 1 M
Q8 Reading Comprehension • Resolution
How did the clever cap-seller finally get his caps back?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The cap-seller took off his own cap and threw it down on the ground. The monkeys copied him immediately and threw all their caps down too!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Threw his own cap down 1 M
Monkeys copied and threw all caps 1 M
Q9 Let us Listen • Animal Sounds
What sound do monkeys make when they chatter in the trees?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Monkeys make a 'Chatter, chatter! Whoop, whoop!' sound.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Chatter chatter sound 1 M
Q10 Let us Listen • Rhyming Words
Find words that rhyme with 'cap': Name three words.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Words rhyming with cap: map, tap, nap, clap, trap.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two or three accurate rhyming words 2 M
Q11 Let us Write • Sequencing
Arrange the story events in correct sequence (1 to 4):
(A) Monkeys took the caps.
(B) Cap-seller fell asleep under a tree.
(C) Cap-seller collected the caps and went away happily.
(D) Cap-seller threw his cap on the ground.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
1. (B) Cap-seller fell asleep under a tree.
2. (A) Monkeys took the caps.
3. (D) Cap-seller threw his cap on the ground.
4. (C) Cap-seller collected the caps and went away happily.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct order B -> A -> D -> C 2 M
Q12 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct words from the story:
(a) The cap-seller rested under a _______ (banyan tree / stone).
(b) The monkeys liked to _______ (copy / bite) the man.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) The cap-seller rested under a banyan tree.
(b) The monkeys liked to copy the man.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
banyan tree 1 M
copy 1 M
Q13 Let us Write • Opposites
Match the words with their opposites:
1. Sleep    (A) Sad
2. Full     (B) Empty
3. Happy    (C) Wake up
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
• 1. Sleep → (C) Wake up
• 2. Full → (B) Empty
• 3. Happy → (A) Sad
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 opposites correctly matched 2 M
Q14 Let us Write • Missing Letters
Complete the spelling of these story words:
(a) M _ N K _ Y
(b) B _ S K _ T
(c) C _ P
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) M O N K E Y (MONKEY)
(b) B A S K E T (BASKET)
(c) C A P (CAP)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
MONKEY 1 M
BASKET 1 M
CAP 1 M
Q15 Think and Share • Story Moral
What lesson do we learn from the story of the cap-seller and the monkeys?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
We learn that wisdom and calm thinking can solve tricky problems better than getting angry.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Wisdom/calm thinking solves problems 2 M
Q16 True or False • Story Facts
State True or False:
(a) The monkeys gave the caps back as soon as the cap-seller politely asked them.
(b) Monkeys are known to imitate what humans do.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. The monkeys did not give them back until copied.
(b) True. Monkeys are known to imitate actions.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q17 Let us Speak • Creative Expression
If you were the cap-seller, what would you have done to get your caps back?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
I would offer the monkeys bananas to drop the caps, or do the same clever trick of throwing down my hat!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Reasonable creative answer 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Under the Banyan Tree

A tired cap-seller placed his bag of red, blue, and green caps under a banyan tree and fell asleep. Mischievous monkeys living on the tree branches climbed down and each took a colourful cap.

Analytical Task: Where did the cap-seller keep his bag? What did the monkeys do while he was sleeping?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The cap-seller kept his bag under the big banyan tree.
2. The monkeys climbed down, opened the bag, took the caps, and wore them on their heads.
Rubric: 1 M for under the tree + 1 M for wearing caps
