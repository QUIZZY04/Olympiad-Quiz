Chapter 8: Mittu and the Yellow Mango

Story: Clever Parrot, Red Balloon & Crow | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Theme of Mittu and the Yellow Mango: Understanding key narrative actions and poetic rhythm.
  • Vocabulary building, rhyming words, and simple sight word recognition.
  • Sentence formation and moral values: friendship, courage, and cleanliness.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading Comprehension • Story Hero
Who was Mittu, and what did he look like in 'Mittu and the Yellow Mango'?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mittu was a green parrot with a bright red beak.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Green parrot 1 M
Red beak 1 M
Q2 Reading Comprehension • Favourite Activity
What did Mittu love to do?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mittu loved to fly high in the sky.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Loved to fly 1 M
Q3 Reading Comprehension • The Discovery
What did Mittu see on a tree when he looked down while flying?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mittu saw a big yellow mango hanging on a tree.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Big yellow mango 1 M
Q4 Reading Comprehension • The Crow's Shout
Who lived on the mango tree, and what did he shout at Mittu?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
A big black crow shouted: 'Caw, caw, go away! This is my tree!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Big black crow 1 M
Shouted 'Caw, caw, go away!' 1 M
Q5 Reading Comprehension • Mittu's Reaction
How did Mittu feel when the crow shouted with a loud, harsh voice?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mittu was afraid of the crow and flew away.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Afraid and flew away 1 M
Q6 Reading Comprehension • The Clever Plan
What did Mittu see under another tree that gave him a clever idea?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mittu saw a red balloon lying under a tree.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Red balloon under tree 1 M
Q7 Reading Comprehension • The Trick
How did Mittu carry the red balloon to the mango tree?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
He carefully picked up the balloon by its string in his red beak without bursting it.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Picked by string in beak 1 M
Flew behind the tree carefully 1 M
Q8 Reading Comprehension • The Pop Sound
What did Mittu do behind the crow, and what sound did it make?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mittu pecked the balloon with his red beak. It burst with a loud: 'POP!'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pecked balloon with beak 1 M
Burst with loud 'POP' 1 M
Q9 Reading Comprehension • The Crow's Fear
What did the crow think when the balloon popped, and what did he do?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
The crow thought: 'A big gun is after me!' He screamed 'Caw!' and flew away, never to return.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Thought a big gun was firing 1 M
Flew away in fear 1 M
Q10 Reading Comprehension • The Reward
What did Mittu do after the crow flew away?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
Mittu came to the tree and ate the big, delicious yellow mango ('Yummy, yummy! What a nice mango!').
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Came to tree 1 M
Ate delicious mango happily 1 M
Q11 Let us Listen • Rhyming Words
Find words that rhyme with:
(a) Crow
(b) Pop
(c) Tree
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Rhyme with Crow: Blow, Grow, Slow, Know.
(b) Rhyme with Pop: Hop, Stop, Top, Drop.
(c) Rhyme with Tree: See, Free, Three, Bee.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Crow rhyme 1 M
Pop rhyme 1 M
Tree rhyme 1 M
Q12 Let us Write • Fill in the Blanks
Fill in the blanks with correct story words:
(a) Mittu was a green _______ (parrot / pigeon).
(b) The crow was _______ (black / white) and had a loud voice.
(c) Mittu found a red _______ (balloon / ball) under a tree.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) Mittu was a green parrot.
(b) The crow was black and had a loud voice.
(c) Mittu found a red balloon under a tree.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
parrot 1 M
black 1 M
balloon 1 M
Q13 Let us Write • Missing Letters
Complete the spellings:
(a) P A R R _ T

[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:

(b) M A N G O (MANGO)
(c) B A L L O O N (BALLOON)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
PARROT 1 M
MANGO 1 M
BALLOON 1 M
Q14 True or False • Story Facts
State True or False:
(a) Mittu was a selfish, rude bird.
(b) Mittu used his intelligence to chase away the loud crow.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
(a) False. Mittu was a clever, gentle parrot.
(b) True. He used his cleverness with the balloon.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
(a) False 1 M
(b) True 1 M
Q15 Think and Share • Moral of the Story
What does Mittu's story teach us about dealing with loud bullies?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Model Answer:
It teaches us that clever thinking and calm planning can overcome big, loud bullies without fighting.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clever thinking overcomes bullies 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Mittu and the Yellow Mango Real-World Analysis

In 'Mittu and the Yellow Mango', children engage with everyday life lessons, discovering how friendship, family, nature, and positive actions bring happiness and solve daily challenges.

Analytical Task: What is the key lesson or joyful message conveyed in 'Mittu and the Yellow Mango'?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The chapter teaches children the importance of kindness, observation, and creative problem solving through engaging storytelling and rhythmic poetry.
Rubric: 1.5 M for core concept identification + 1.5 M for value application
