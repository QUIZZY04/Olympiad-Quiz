Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is there negative marking in these Olympiads?

Generally, preliminary levels of Olympiads do not have negative marking. However, always verify with the official guidelines for specific advanced levels.

What is the Intelligence Based Questions section?

This section tests logical and analytical reasoning, forming a substantial part of the exam score and testing critical thinking alongside subject knowledge.
