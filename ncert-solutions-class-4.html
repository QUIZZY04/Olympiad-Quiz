Class 4 NCERT Solutions FAQ

Which textbooks should a Class 4 CBSE student follow in 2026-27? ▾
Under the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and NCF-SE 2023, NCERT has released new textbooks for Class 4 including Maths Mela (Mathematics), Santoor (English), Veena (Hindi), and Our Wondrous World (The World Around Us). Many CBSE affiliated schools also continue to use Math-Magic, Marigold, Rimjhim, and Looking Around during the transition period. OlympiadQuiz provides 100% verified solutions for both sets of books so students never face any gaps regardless of which book their school prescribes.
Are in-text questions and hands-on activities included? ▾
Yes. Our solutions cover 100% of the textbook exercises including "Find Out", "Let Us Think", "Try This", creative writing prompts, group discussions, and experiment observations.
How do these solutions help in Olympiad exams like IMO and NSO? ▾
Olympiad exams test deep conceptual foundation and logical problem-solving. By mastering our step-by-step NCERT explanations, students build strong analytical intuition in mathematics and science, enabling them to tackle higher-level competitive questions effortlessly.
