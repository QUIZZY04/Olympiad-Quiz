Physics Syllabus Weightage for JEE

Physics in JEE Main balances conceptual clarity with numerical speed. Prioritize these high-weightage sections to maximize your attempts.

  • Mechanics (30%)
    Core Analytical

    Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work Energy & Power, Rotational Dynamics, Gravitation. The foundation of Physics scoring.

  • Electromagnetism (35%)
    Formula Intensive

    Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, EMI & AC, Electromagnetic Waves. Focus on circuit solving and vectors.

  • Modern Physics & Optics (25%)
    Highly Scoring

    Dual Nature of Matter, Atoms and Nuclei, Electronic Devices, Ray Optics, Wave Optics. Direct NCERT applications.
