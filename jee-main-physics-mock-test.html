Physics Syllabus Weightage for JEE

Physics often acts as the rank decider in JEE Main. Prioritize these high-weightage sections for maximum percentile gains.

  • Mechanics (30%)
    Core Foundation

    Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work Energy and Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation. (Requires strong calculus application).

  • Electromagnetism (25%)
    Class 12 Heavyweight

    Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Current, EMI & AC. (Formula intensive and highly conceptual).

  • Modern Physics & Optics (20%)
    High Scoring & Direct

    Dual Nature of Matter, Atoms and Nuclei, Electronic Devices, Ray and Wave Optics. (Usually straightforward application questions).
