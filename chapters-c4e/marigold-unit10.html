Chapter 10: The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow

Humorous Rhyme on Kindness to Animals & Classic Story of Gentleness Succeeding where Brute Force Fails | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'The Donkey': If I had a donkey that wouldn't go, would I wallop him? No, no, no! I'd feed him hay and corn.
  • Story 'The Milkman's Cow': A stubborn cow sits right in the middle of a busy highway and refuses to budge.
  • Failed brute force: Milkman pulling, policeman blowing whistle, grocer offering goods, wrestler flexing muscles.
  • The triumph of gentleness: A little boy arrives holding a sheaf of sweet green grass; the cow gets up and chews peacefully.
  • Moral: Kindness, empathy, and understanding psychology achieve what shouting, anger, and violence can never accomplish.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem 'The Donkey'
What does the poet say he would do if he had a donkey that refused to go? What would he NOT do?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
What He Would NOT Do: The poet declares that he would never 'wallop' (hit, beat, or whip) the donkey, because cruelty to animals is unkind and wrong.

What He Would Do: Instead, he would feed the donkey a bundle of sweet green hay and juicy ears of corn, treating the animal with love, patience, and nourishment until it happily trotted forward.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rejects walloping/beating the donkey 1.5 M
Advocates feeding hay and corn with kindness 1.5 M
Q2 Reading is Fun • 'The Milkman's Cow'
Who all tried to make the milkman's cow move from the road, and what methods did they use?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. The Milkman: Tugged, pleaded, and pulled hard on the lead rope.
2. The Policeman: Twirled his baton, puffed his chest, and blew his loud brass whistle.
3. The Grocer: Rattled bags of potatoes, peas, and shiny cans.
4. The Wrestler: Flexed his enormous biceps and tried to haul the cow by brute physical force.
All four grown adults failed completely.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Lists milkman, policeman, grocer, and wrestler 1.5 M
Describes brute force and noisy methods used by each 1.5 M
Q3 Reading is Fun • 'The Milkman's Cow'
How did the little boy succeed in moving the cow where four strong grown men had failed? What is the moral lesson?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The Little Boy's Method: The little boy did not shout, pull, or use force. Smiling gently, he held out a sheaf of tasty, crisp green grass right before the cow's nose. Chewing happily, the cow got to her feet and peacefully followed the boy to the side of the road.

Moral Lesson: Gentleness, patience, and understanding the needs of others achieve what shouting, anger, and muscle power can never accomplish.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Details little boy offering bundle of tasty green grass 1.5 M
States moral: gentleness and empathy surpass anger and force 1.5 M
Q4 Word Building • Sensory Action Verbs
Match the character with the action verb from the story: (a) Policeman, (b) Wrestler, (c) Cow, (d) Boy. [Options: Chewed, Flexed, Whistled, Smiled].
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
(a) Policeman: Whistled.
(b) Wrestler: Flexed.
(c) Cow: Chewed.
(d) Boy: Smiled.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four correct character-verb pairings (0.5 M each) 2.0 M
Q5 Reading is Fun • Thematic Inquiry & Character Study
In 'The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow', analyze how the central characters overcome their primary challenge. What life lesson or core value does this convey to young learners about empathy, patience, or perseverance?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Character Analysis & Thematic Understanding:
1. In The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow, the characters face moments of uncertainty where teamwork, quick thinking, and kindness play pivotal roles in finding a resolution.
2. By listening to advice, respecting others, and not giving up when confronted with obstacles, they achieve success and bring happiness to those around them.
3. Core Value: The chapter reinforces that true strength lies in humility, perseverance, and mutual support during times of difficulty.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Detailed character insight and challenge identification 1.5 M
Clear deduction of core values (empathy/perseverance) 1.5 M

From the lesson 'The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow', complete the language exercise:
(a) Find two compound words or rhyming pairs.
(b) Give antonyms for: Brave, Generous, Polite.
(c) Form a complex descriptive sentence using new vocabulary introduced in this unit.
[3 Marks]

Vocabulary Solutions:

• (b) Antonyms:

- Generous → Selfish / Stingy

• (c) Sample Sentence: The children expressed immense gratitude to the generous village elder who guided them through the dense woods.

Q7 Grammar in Context • Parts of Speech & Tenses
Read the grammatical situation based on 'The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow':
(a) Identify whether the following sentence is in past continuous or simple past tense: 'The children were walking through the green orchard when the bell rang.'
(b) Underline the prepositions of place and time in: 'The robin perched on the branch at dawn.'
(c) Change this affirmative sentence into a question: 'Neha woke up at six o'clock.'
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Grammar Analysis:
• (a) Tense Identification: 'were walking' is in the Past Continuous Tense (was/were + verb-ing), while 'rang' is in the Simple Past Tense.
• (b) Prepositions: 'on' (preposition of place) and 'at' (preposition of time).
• (c) Interrogative Transformation: 'Did Neha wake up at six o'clock?'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of past continuous tense 1.0 M
Correct identification of prepositions 'on' and 'at' 1.0 M
Accurate question formation with helping verb 'Did' 1.0 M
Q8 Talk Time • Experiential Sharing & Perspective Taking
Imagine you experienced a situation similar to the one depicted in 'The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow'. Describe how you felt, what decision you made, and what you learned from the final outcome.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Experiential Reflection:
• Situation: While participating in our school's science fair, our display model unexpectedly broke just thirty minutes before the judges arrived.
• Decision: Remembering the teamwork depicted in The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow, my teammates and I stayed calm instead of panicking. We reassembled the parts using eco-friendly materials from the craft box.
• Learning Outcome: The judges appreciated our resourcefulness and teamwork. I learned that composure and collaborative problem-solving turn setbacks into triumphs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear narrative of personal dilemma/experience 1.5 M
Maturity of reflection and actionable learning 1.5 M
Q9 Sentence Mechanics • Punctuation & Direct Speech
Punctuate and rewrite the following dialogue correctly:
mother asked rahul why are you late from school today rahul replied my cycle had a flat tire near the post office
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Corrected Dialogue with Punctuations:
Mother asked, "Rahul, why are you late from school today?"
Rahul replied, "My cycle had a flat tire near the post office."

Explanation: Added capital letters at the beginning of each sentence and proper nouns, commas after introductory reporting verbs, quotation marks for spoken words, and question mark inside quotation marks.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate quotation marks and question mark for Mother's speech 1.5 M
Accurate capitalization and punctuation for Rahul's response 1.5 M
Q10 Creative Writing • Diary Entry & Paragraph Writing
Write a short diary entry (40–50 words) reflecting on the most inspiring thought presented in 'The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow'. Express how you will practice this thought in your school or neighborhood.

Today we read 'The Donkey & The Milkman's Cow' in our English class. It inspired me deeply to notice small acts of kindness around me. Starting tomorrow, I have decided to help my younger sister with her reading homework and share my water bottle with classmates during physical education periods. Small deeds truly make our world brighter!
— Ananya

Content relevance to chapter theme and personal resolution 2.0 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Conflict Resolution & Non-Violent Communication

Two 4th-grade students began shouting, pulling, and fighting over who gets to play with the basketball first during the sports hour.

Analytical Task: How does 'The Milkman's Cow' offer a practical lesson in conflict resolution? How can shouting be replaced with empathy to resolve classroom arguments?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Lesson from the Story: Shouting and aggressive pulling make people (and animals) dig their heels in more stubbornly, causing resistance and deadlock. Just as the four strong men failed with shouting and muscle, fighting never solves student disputes.

Empathetic Resolution: The little boy showed that offering a cooperative solution (sharing or taking turns) satisfies everyone's needs. Students should take turns (e.g. 15 minutes each) or join together in a unified team game.
Rubric: 1 Mark for identifying failure of brute force in human conflicts; 1 Mark for practical win-win collaborative resolution.
