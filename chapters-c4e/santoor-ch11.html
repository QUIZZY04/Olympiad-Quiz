Chapter 11: A Journey to the Magical Mountains

Adventure Narrative in the Himalayas: Family Bonds, Mountain Ecology & Environmental Stewardship | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Young girl Minam and her grandfather, a retired Sherpa who loves the Himalayan peaks.
  • Preparing for a mountain trek: Packing essentials (rotis, achaar, water bottles, woolen caps).
  • Experiencing the changing altitude, crisp alpine air, crystal-clear glacial streams, and prayer flags.
  • First encounter with snow: Joy of building a snowman and touching powdery snowflakes.
  • The sacred pledge: Respecting fragile mountain ecosystems and leaving zero plastic litter behind.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Let us Think • Comprehension
Who was Minam, and what special invitation did her grandfather give her one sunny morning?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Who Minam Was: Minam was an adventurous young girl who lived in a scenic village nestled at the foothills of the mighty Himalayas with her grandfather, who was once an experienced mountain Sherpa.

Grandfather's Invitation: One bright sunny morning, her grandfather noticed how strong and healthy she had grown and said: 'Minam, your legs are strong now. Today, I will take you on a magical journey high up into the heart of the mountains.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identifies Minam and grandfather's Sherpa background 1.5 M
Explains morning invitation to climb the Himalayas 1.5 M
Q2 Let us Think • Comprehension
What supplies did Minam pack in her rucksack, and where did they pause for their afternoon lunch?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Rucksack Supplies: Minam thoughtfully packed freshly baked rotis wrapped in cloth, spicy homemade lemon pickle (achaar), two stainless steel water bottles, and crisp mountain apples.

Lunch Spot: They paused beside a bubbling, ice-cold mountain stream bordered by smooth river pebbles. Minam dipped her tired feet into the cool water and joyfully watched silver fish dart across the riverbed while eating her lunch.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Lists rotis, achaar, water bottles, and apples 1.5 M
Describes riverside lunch spot with cool glacial water 1.5 M
Q3 Let us Think • Comprehension
How did Minam react when she touched snow for the very first time in her life? What did she build with her grandfather?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
When Minam reached the snowline, she gasped in pure wonder. She scooped up a handful of powdery snow, delighted by its cool, soft texture, and laughed aloud with excitement.

Together with her grandfather, she rolled large snowballs and built a friendly little snowman, using pine twigs for its arms and small black river stones for its eyes and smiling mouth.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Describes wonder and sensory excitement of touching snow 1.5 M
Details building a snowman using twigs and river pebbles 1.5 M
Q4 Let us Think • Comprehension
What solemn promise did Minam make to her grandfather and the mountains before descending?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Before descending to their valley home, Minam looked up at the towering, snow-capped Himalayan peaks and placed her hand over her heart. She solemnly promised her grandfather that she would forever respect, love, and protect nature, and ensure that no traveler ever litters or harms the pristine beauty of the mountains.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Mentions pledge of environmental respect and protection 1.5 M
Highlights preserving mountain purity and zero littering 1.5 M

Q14 Review Questions • True or False
State True or False:

(b) In the English alphabet, every vowel always produces only one sound.
(c) A paragraph should revolve around a single central topic.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Eco-Tourism & Clean Himalayan Trails

Every summer, thousands of tourists leave discarded plastic bottles, chip bags, and aluminum cans along high-altitude trekking trails in Ladakh and Himachal Pradesh, endangering grazing mountain goats (Ibex) and contaminating melting glaciers.

Analytical Task: How does Minam's mountain promise oppose the habits of careless tourists? State two practical rules every eco-trekker must follow.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Contrast with Minam's Pledge: While careless tourists treat sacred peaks as dumping grounds, Minam internalized her grandfather's golden Sherpa ethos: nature must be revered and left untouched for future generations.

Two Eco-Trekker Rules:
1. Leave No Trace: Carry back 100% of non-biodegradable waste in personal trash bags to plains municipal recycling centers.
2. Use Reusable Canteens: Refill durable stainless steel water bottles from verified boiled/filtered spring points instead of buying single-use commercial plastic bottles.
Rubric: 1 Mark for contrasting Minam's reverence with irresponsible tourism; 1 Mark for two actionable waste-mitigation trekking rules.
