Chapter 6: Hiawatha & The Scholar's Mother Tongue

Poem by H.W. Longfellow on Nature Lore & Mughal Court Tale of Birbal Discovering Mother Tongue | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'Hiawatha': The young Native American boy raised by grandmother Nokomis who learned the language of all birds and animals.
  • Calling birds 'Hiawatha's Chickens' and beasts 'Hiawatha's Brothers'.
  • Story 'The Scholar's Mother Tongue': A learned pundit challenges Akbar's courtiers to identify his mother tongue.
  • Birbal's nocturnal trick: Whispering and tickling the pundit's ear with a dry grass straw during deep sleep.
  • The psychological truth: In moments of sudden fright or emergency, a person always speaks their true mother tongue.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem 'Hiawatha'
Who was Hiawatha, and what wonderful secrets did he learn from the birds and animals of the forest?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Hiawatha was a young Native American boy who lived with his wise grandmother Nokomis in a forest wigwam.

He learned all the intimate secrets of the wilderness:
1. How the birds built their nests in spring and where they hid themselves in winter.
2. How the beavers built their river lodges, how squirrels hid their acorns, and how the reindeer ran so swiftly.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identifies Hiawatha and grandmother Nokomis 1.0 M
Details secrets of birds' nests, beaver lodges, and squirrels' acorns 2.0 M
Q2 Reading is Fun • 'The Scholar's Mother Tongue'
What challenge did the learned Pundit present to Emperor Akbar's royal court? Why was it difficult to guess his mother tongue?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The Pundit challenged Emperor Akbar and his courtiers to identify his real mother tongue within one day.

It was extraordinarily difficult because the scholar was a master linguist who spoke multiple languages—including Sanskrit, Persian, Arabic, and Hindi—with such flawless fluency and native accent that no one could tell which language was his original mother tongue.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
States 24-hour court challenge to identify mother tongue 1.5 M
Explains scholar's flawless multilingual fluency 1.5 M
Q3 Reading is Fun • 'The Scholar's Mother Tongue'
How did Birbal discover the scholar's mother tongue? What was the scholar's actual mother tongue?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
At dead of night when the scholar was sound asleep, Birbal quietly entered his bedchamber and gently tickled the scholar's ear with a dry grass straw. Half-awake and startled, the scholar cried out sudden curses and shouts in Telugu.

Next morning, Birbal announced to Emperor Akbar that the scholar's mother tongue was Telugu, which the astonished scholar humbly admitted was true.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Describes midnight straw tickle provoking subconscious reaction 1.5 M
Identifies Telugu as the scholar's true mother tongue 1.5 M

Add '-ly' to convert the following adjectives into adverbs and write one sentence: (a) Fluent, (b) Quiet, (c) Sudden.

(a) Fluent: Fluently.
(b) Quiet: Quietly.
(c) Sudden: Suddenly.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Psycholinguistics of the Mother Tongue

Modern psychologists confirm that our first acquired native language (mother tongue) is deeply encoded in the subconscious limbic system of the brain, making it our automatic involuntary response during fright or pain.

Analytical Task: How did Birbal leverage this subconscious psychological reflex? Why are mother tongues so vital to cultural identity?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Birbal's Insight: Birbal understood that conscious intellect and learned accents drop their guard when startled out of deep sleep. An involuntary reflexive shout will always emerge from the earliest childhood linguistic memory.

Vital to Identity: The mother tongue is not just words; it carries our emotional intimacy, childhood folklore, cultural heritage, and maternal bonding. Preserving native languages safeguards community identity and linguistic diversity.
Rubric: 1 Mark for subconscious reflex mechanism; 1 Mark for cultural and emotional preservation significance.
