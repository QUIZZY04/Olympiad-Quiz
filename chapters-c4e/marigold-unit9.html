Chapter 9: The Naughty Boy & Pinocchio

John Keats' Whimsical Verse & Carlo Collodi's Classic Puppet Tale of Honesty and Integrity | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Poem 'The Naughty Boy': An English boy runs away to Scotland only to discover ground is as hard, cherries as red, and lead as heavy as in England.
  • Story 'Pinocchio': Old carpenter Gepetto carves a talking wooden puppet and names him Pinocchio.
  • The mischievous puppet: Pulling wigs, laughing impudently, and running away from school.
  • The magical consequence: Every time Pinocchio tells a lie, his wooden nose grows longer, longer, and longer.
  • Moral: Deception cannot be concealed; truthfulness brings true humanity and freedom.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (20 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Reading is Fun • Poem 'The Naughty Boy'
Why did the naughty boy run away to Scotland? What surprising discovery did he make when he arrived there?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The naughty boy ran away to Scotland because he was restless and foolishly imagined that Scotland would be an exotic, magical place totally different from England.

He was astonished to discover that:
1. The Scottish ground was just as hard as English ground.
2. A yard was just as long, a song was just as merry, cherries were just as red, and lead was just as heavy.
Human nature and the physical world were identical everywhere.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explains running away to Scotland out of restless curiosity 1.5 M
Lists discoveries: hard ground, long yard, red cherries, heavy lead 1.5 M
Q2 Reading is Fun • 'Pinocchio'
How did Gepetto create Pinocchio? What mischievous tricks did the puppet play as soon as its features were carved?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Gepetto carved Pinocchio from an enchanted piece of talking firewood. As soon as he finished carving the eyes, they began rolling at him. The moment he carved the mouth, it laughed mockingly and stuck out its tongue.

Before Gepetto could even finish carving the legs, Pinocchio snatched the carpenter's brown wig right off his head, jumped down to the floor, and ran out the front door into the street.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Describes carving from enchanted talking wood 1.5 M
Details rolling eyes, mocking laughter, and snatching Gepetto's wig 1.5 M
Q3 Reading is Fun • 'Pinocchio'
What bizarre physical phenomenon happened to Pinocchio every time he told a falsehood or made a false excuse?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Every single time Pinocchio spoke a lie, broke a promise, or made a false excuse to avoid school, his wooden nose immediately began growing longer, longer, and longer.

It grew so enormous that he could not turn his head inside the room without hitting walls, until he was forced to confess the honest truth to shrink it back.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identifies nose growing visibly longer with every lie 1.5 M
Explains that only speaking honest truth shrank it back 1.5 M
Q4 Word Building • Word Families
Name three things made of wood from your home or classroom: (a) ___, (b) ___, (c) ___. What tool does a carpenter use to smoothen wood?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Three wooden objects: (a) Study Table, (b) Door Frame, (c) Cricket Bat.

A carpenter uses a plane (or sandpaper) to smoothen rough wood surfaces.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three wooden objects correctly named 1.5 M
Identifies carpenter's plane/sandpaper 0.5 M
Q5 Reading is Fun • Thematic Inquiry & Character Study
In 'The Naughty Boy & Pinocchio', analyze how the central characters overcome their primary challenge. What life lesson or core value does this convey to young learners about empathy, patience, or perseverance?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Character Analysis & Thematic Understanding:
1. In The Naughty Boy & Pinocchio, the characters face moments of uncertainty where teamwork, quick thinking, and kindness play pivotal roles in finding a resolution.
2. By listening to advice, respecting others, and not giving up when confronted with obstacles, they achieve success and bring happiness to those around them.
3. Core Value: The chapter reinforces that true strength lies in humility, perseverance, and mutual support during times of difficulty.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Detailed character insight and challenge identification 1.5 M
Clear deduction of core values (empathy/perseverance) 1.5 M

Q7 Grammar in Context • Parts of Speech & Tenses
Read the grammatical situation based on 'The Naughty Boy & Pinocchio':
(a) Identify whether the following sentence is in past continuous or simple past tense: 'The children were walking through the green orchard when the bell rang.'
(b) Underline the prepositions of place and time in: 'The robin perched on the branch at dawn.'
(c) Change this affirmative sentence into a question: 'Neha woke up at six o'clock.'
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Grammar Analysis:
• (a) Tense Identification: 'were walking' is in the Past Continuous Tense (was/were + verb-ing), while 'rang' is in the Simple Past Tense.
• (b) Prepositions: 'on' (preposition of place) and 'at' (preposition of time).
• (c) Interrogative Transformation: 'Did Neha wake up at six o'clock?'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identification of past continuous tense 1.0 M
Correct identification of prepositions 'on' and 'at' 1.0 M
Accurate question formation with helping verb 'Did' 1.0 M
Q8 Talk Time • Experiential Sharing & Perspective Taking
Imagine you experienced a situation similar to the one depicted in 'The Naughty Boy & Pinocchio'. Describe how you felt, what decision you made, and what you learned from the final outcome.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Experiential Reflection:
• Situation: While participating in our school's science fair, our display model unexpectedly broke just thirty minutes before the judges arrived.
• Decision: Remembering the teamwork depicted in The Naughty Boy & Pinocchio, my teammates and I stayed calm instead of panicking. We reassembled the parts using eco-friendly materials from the craft box.
• Learning Outcome: The judges appreciated our resourcefulness and teamwork. I learned that composure and collaborative problem-solving turn setbacks into triumphs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear narrative of personal dilemma/experience 1.5 M
Maturity of reflection and actionable learning 1.5 M
Q9 Sentence Mechanics • Punctuation & Direct Speech
Punctuate and rewrite the following dialogue correctly:
mother asked rahul why are you late from school today rahul replied my cycle had a flat tire near the post office
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Corrected Dialogue with Punctuations:
Mother asked, "Rahul, why are you late from school today?"
Rahul replied, "My cycle had a flat tire near the post office."

Explanation: Added capital letters at the beginning of each sentence and proper nouns, commas after introductory reporting verbs, quotation marks for spoken words, and question mark inside quotation marks.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Accurate quotation marks and question mark for Mother's speech 1.5 M
Accurate capitalization and punctuation for Rahul's response 1.5 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Psychological Metaphor of Pinocchio's Nose

Modern behavioral scientists observe that when people tell deliberate lies, micro-expressions, increased blink rates, vocal pitch changes, and autonomic nervous system stress reactions involuntarily reveal the deceit.

Analytical Task: Why is Pinocchio's growing nose one of the greatest moral metaphors in children's literature? How does one lie inevitably lead to another?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
The Metaphor: Pinocchio's nose externalizes an internal psychological truth: deceit cannot remain secret. Even if invisible physically, lying distorts a person's dignity and character visibly to those around them.

The Trap of Lies: To protect the first lie, a person must fabricate three more lies. Soon, the web of deception becomes so cumbersome and absurd (just like an enormous wooden nose) that confession and honest apology are the only way back to freedom.
Rubric: 1 Mark for explaining externalization of guilt; 1 Mark for compound snowball effect of continuous deceit.
