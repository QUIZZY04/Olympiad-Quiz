Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are H.O.T.S. questions in Olympiad exams?

H.O.T.S. stands for Higher Order Thinking Skills. These questions test advanced analytical, logical, and problem-solving abilities beyond the standard school curriculum, often appearing in the Achievers Section of exams like SOF IMO and NSO.

Are these HOTS mock tests free?

Yes, all H.O.T.S. problems and mock tests on Olympiad Portal are completely free for students from Class 1 to 10.

Which subjects are covered in the HOTS section?

We offer chapter-wise and mixed HOTS questions for Mathematics (IMO), Science (NSO), English (IEO), and Logical Reasoning (IRO).

Why is the Achievers Section important?

The Achievers Section usually contains HOTS questions that carry higher weightage (2 or 3 marks per question). Scoring well in this section is crucial for securing top international ranks and breaking ties.

Do you provide solutions for HOTS questions?

Absolutely. Every HOTS question comes with a detailed, step-by-step solution to help you understand the core concepts and logic required to solve complex problems.
