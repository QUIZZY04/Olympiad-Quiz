🏆 How to Maximize Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is your central command center for Olympiad success. To get the most out of your preparation, we recommend following a structured daily routine.

  • Daily Chapterwise Practice: Dedicate at least 30 minutes every day to master individual topics before jumping into full-length tests.
  • Analyze Your Mistakes: Always visit the Performance Analytics section after a test to identify your weak areas.
  • Weekend Live Arenas: Participate in our live weekend quizzes to experience real exam pressure and benchmark your national ranking.
  • Review Study Material: If you consistently score low in a specific topic, go back to the Study Material section and review the core concepts.

Consistency is the key to unlocking your true potential. Keep practicing, keep improving!
