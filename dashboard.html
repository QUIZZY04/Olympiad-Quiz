Frequently Asked Questions (JEE & NEET)

Are the JEE and NEET mock tests based on the latest NTA pattern?

Yes, all our full-length and chapter-wise mock tests are strictly aligned with the latest NTA and NMC syllabuses, including the updated marking schemes and CBT interfaces.

How should I balance board exams with competitive prep?

Focus on mastering the NCERT textbook concepts first, as they form the foundation for both Boards and JEE/NEET. Allocate weekends strictly for competitive mock tests and higher-level problem solving.

Do you provide solutions for the mock tests?

Absolutely. Detailed, step-by-step solutions and performance analytics are available immediately after you submit any mock test to help you understand your mistakes.

How is the All India Rank (AIR) calculated in Live Mock Tests?

Your AIR is calculated dynamically based on your relative performance (percentile scoring) against all other students who participated in that specific Live Arena session across the country.

Are there negative marks in the mock tests?

Yes, our tests simulate the exact marking scheme of the real exams. JEE Main and NEET tests carry a -1 negative mark for incorrect answers, while JEE Advanced tests feature complex partial marking and negative marking per the actual exam format.

Can I attempt the tests on a mobile phone?

Yes, our platform is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices. However, for the best experience resembling the actual CBT (Computer Based Test), we highly recommend taking full-length mock tests on a desktop or laptop.

What are Multi-Correct Questions in JEE Advanced mock tests?

Multi-correct questions are those where one or more options can be correct. Our testing engine fully supports this format, including the intricate partial marking rules (+1 for each correct option chosen, provided no incorrect option is selected, and -2 if any incorrect option is selected).

Is there any specific strategy for attempting the NEET biology section?

Since Biology accounts for 50% of the NEET score, we advise students to attempt this section first and aim to complete it within 40-45 minutes. Our chapter-wise Botany and Zoology quizzes are designed to improve both your accuracy and speed.
