NEET Physics Syllabus Breakdown & Scoring Strategy

Physics is often the rank-deciding section in NEET UG, consisting of 45 compulsory questions worth 180 marks (+4 for each correct answer and -1 for each incorrect answer). Unlike JEE Main, NEET Physics prioritizes speed, conceptual clarity, and clean single-step or two-step numerical execution.

High-yield Class 11 topics include Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work-Energy-Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, and Thermodynamics. From Class 12, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Moving Charges and Magnetism, Ray and Wave Optics, Dual Nature of Matter, Atoms, Nuclei, and Semiconductor Electronics consistently contribute the largest share of questions.

Keep a dedicated formula sheet for constants, unit conversions, and dimensional formulas, and practice solving 45 Physics questions within 50–55 minutes during timed chapter-wise and mock drills.

  • Class 11 Priority Units: Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work-Energy-Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, Properties of Matter, Thermodynamics, and Oscillations & Waves.
  • Class 12 Priority Units: Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Current, EMI & AC, Ray & Wave Optics, Dual Nature, Atoms, Nuclei, and Semiconductor Devices.
  • Accuracy Strategy: Verify SI units and powers of 10 on every numerical before bubbling/clicking an option to avoid -1 negative marking penalties.
