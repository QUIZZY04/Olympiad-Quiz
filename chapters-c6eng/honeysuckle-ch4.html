Chapter 4: An Indian-American Woman in Space: Kalpana Chawla & Beauty

NCERT Honeysuckle (Class 6) — Biography of astronaut Kalpana Chawla (Columbia STS-107) & E-Yeh-Shure's lyrical ode to beauty. | CBSE 2026-27

📌 Key Concepts & Formulas
  • Kalpana Chawla: Born in Karnal, Haryana; aeronautical engineer from Punjab Engineering College; NASA astronaut.
  • First Indian-born woman in space aboard Space Shuttle Columbia in 1997; tragic disintegration of STS-107 in 2003.
  • Inspirational message from space: 'The path from dreams to success does exist. May you have the vision to find it, the courage to get on to it...'
  • Poem 'Beauty': Beauty is seen in sunlight, corn growing; heard in wind, rain, singer chanting; and felt in good deeds and happy thoughts.
NCERT Textbook Exercise & Practice Questions
Q1
Where was Kalpana Chawla born? Why is she called an Indian-American? What was her message to students from space?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Biographical Details:
• Kalpana was born in Karnal, Haryana, India.
• She is called an Indian-American because she was born in India and graduated with an engineering degree there, but later immigrated to the United States, married flight instructor Jean-Pierre Harrison, and became a naturalized US citizen.
• Her message from Columbia: 'The path from dreams to success does exist. May you have the vision to find it, the courage to get on to it, and the perseverance to follow it.'
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Karnal birth and naturalized US citizenship1.5 Marks
Accurate inspirational quote from orbit1.5 Marks
Q2
According to the poet E-Yeh-Shure, where can beauty be seen, heard, and felt?
[3 Marks]
✅ CBSE Standard Step-by-Step Solution
Poetic Interpretation:
• Seen: In the sunlight, trees, birds, growing corn, and hardworking farmers dancing for their harvest.
• Heard: In the night, when wind sighs, rain falls, or a singer chants in earnest.
• Felt in Oneself: In good deeds, noble actions, and happy thoughts that repeat in our dreams, work, and quiet moments.
CBSE Board Marking Scheme 2026-27
Visual beauty in nature and human effort1 Mark
Auditory beauty in wind, rain, song1 Mark
Inner beauty in good deeds and conscience1 Mark
CBSE Competency-Based Case Study (NEP 2020)
STEM Education & Women in Aerospace Engineering:
Despite facing conservative societal reservations against female higher education in aeronautics, Kalpana Chawla persevered. (a) What qualities of character enabled her to achieve her dream? (b) How does she continue to inspire young girls in developing nations?
Detailed Analytical Solution & Rubric
(a) Qualities: Unshakable determination, intellectual discipline, boundless curiosity about aviation, and the courage to chart an unprecedented career path.
(b) Inspiration: She proved that geographical, economic, and gender barriers can be overcome through dedication, establishing that the sky is not the limit—it is only the beginning.
