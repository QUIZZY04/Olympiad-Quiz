Chapter 1: My Wonderful Body & Five Senses

Eyes, Ears, Nose, Tongue, Skin and Sense Functions | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Foundational environmental awareness for Class 2: My Wonderful Body & Five Senses.
  • Understanding eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin and sense functions through observation and activities.
  • CBSE step-by-step marking rubrics and competency-based questions.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (3 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Conceptual Understanding • Core Facts
What are the key facts and lessons taught in 'My Wonderful Body & Five Senses'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Key Lessons:
1. In 'My Wonderful Body & Five Senses', we learn about Eyes, Ears, Nose, Tongue, Skin and Sense Functions.
2. It helps us understand our body, our family, and the natural world around us.
3. Important Habit: We must always respect nature, practice hygiene, and care for living beings.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Core lesson points 1.5 M
Practical application 1.5 M
Q2 Daily Observation & Activity
Give two everyday examples related to 'My Wonderful Body & Five Senses' that you observe at home or school.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Observations:
• Example 1: Practicing handwashing with soap before eating meals.
• Example 2: Watering potted plants and closing leaking water taps.
• Both actions show responsible daily citizenship.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two valid daily examples 2 M
Q3 True or False • Fact Check
True or False: We should waste water and leave lights turned on when leaving a room. Explain.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer: False. We should always turn off lights and close water taps tightly to conserve electricity and natural resources.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
False with correct justification 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

My Wonderful Body & Five Senses Eco-Awareness Task

Children examine a daily real-life situation involving my wonderful body & five senses and discuss responsible environmental choices.

Analytical Task: How can young children practice good habits related to 'My Wonderful Body & Five Senses' in their daily life?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
By practicing mindful habits like eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin and sense functions, children keep themselves healthy, respect their peers and protect natural resources.
Rubric: 1.5 M for habit identification + 1.5 M for environmental value
