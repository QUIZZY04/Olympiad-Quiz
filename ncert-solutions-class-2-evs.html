Chapter 1: My Wonderful Body & Five Senses

Eyes, Ears, Nose, Tongue, Skin and Sense Functions | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • External Body Parts (Head, Limbs, Trunk)
  • Five Sense Organs and Their Functions
  • Caring for Sense Organs
  • Good Touch and Personal Safety

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (15 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Think and Tell
What are sense organs? Name the five sense organs present in our body.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sense organs are special organs that help us perceive, observe, and understand the world around us. The five sense organs are: 1. Eyes (Sight) 2. Ears (Hearing) 3. Nose (Smell) 4. Tongue (Taste) 5. Skin (Touch).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Definition of sense organs 1
Listing all 5 sense organs correctly 2
Q2 Find Out and Write
Which sense organ covers our entire body, and what is its main function?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
The skin covers our entire body. Its main function is the sense of touch; it helps us feel hot, cold, soft, hard, rough, smooth, and pain.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Skin covers entire body 1
Sense of touch (hot, cold, texture) 1
Q3 Taste Buds & Tongue
What four primary tastes can our tongue identify?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Our tongue has taste buds that help us recognize: 1. Sweet (e.g., sugar, mango) 2. Salty (e.g., potato chips, salt) 3. Sour (e.g., lemon, raw mango) 4. Bitter (e.g., bitter gourd, neem).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Listing 4 tastes: sweet, salty, sour, bitter 2
Q4 Eye Care & Hygiene
Give two healthy habits to take proper care of our eyes.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Two healthy habits for eye care: 1. Wash eyes gently with clean cold water daily. 2. Do not watch television, tablet, or smartphone screens for too long or in a dark room.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Washing with clean water 1
Limiting screen time / proper lighting 1
Q5 Ear Care
Why should we never put sharp or pointed objects inside our ears?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
We should never insert hairpin, pencil, or sharp sticks into our ears because they can puncture or damage the delicate eardrum and cause permanent hearing loss.

Risk of hearing loss 1

Q1 Think and Tell
What is a 'Tree'? Give two examples of trees found in India.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
A tree is a very big, tall, and strong plant with a thick, woody stem called the 'trunk'. Examples: Neem tree, Banyan tree (or Mango tree).

Q2 Shrubs vs Herbs
How are shrubs different from herbs? Give one example of each.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
• Shrubs: Medium-sized bushy plants with thin, hard, woody stems branching near the ground (e.g., Rose, Hibiscus). • Herbs: Very small plants with soft, tender green stems; they live only for one season (e.g., Mint, Coriander, Spinach).

Q3 Climbers and Creepers

[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
• Climbers have thin and weak stems; they use tendrils to wind around supports like walls or sticks to climb upward (e.g., Money plant, Pea). • Creepers also have weak stems, but they bear big, heavy fruits (like Pumpkin, Watermelon) so they crawl along the ground.

Evaluation & Model Answer:
Plastic litter is deadly because marine animals mistake plastic pieces for jellyfish, eat them, and choke or starve to death, or get tangled in plastic nets and drown. Three eco-friendly picnic habits: 1. Carry stainless steel reusable water bottles and steel tiffin boxes instead of single-use plastic bottles. 2. Carry a jute or cloth trash bag to bring all garbage back home for proper segregation. 3. Never throw any plastic wrappers or bottles into rivers, lakes, beaches, or open grounds.
