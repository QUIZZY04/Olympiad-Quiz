Q1 What are sense organs? Name the five sense organs present in our body. Think and Tell 3 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Sense organs are special organs that help us perceive, observe, and understand the world around us. The five sense organs are: 1. Eyes (Sight) 2. Ears (Hearing) 3. Nose (Smell) 4. Tongue (Taste) 5. Skin (Touch).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Definition of sense organs 1
Listing all 5 sense organs correctly 2
Q2 Which sense organ covers our entire body, and what is its main function? Find Out and Write 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
The skin covers our entire body. Its main function is the sense of touch; it helps us feel hot, cold, soft, hard, rough, smooth, and pain.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Skin covers entire body 1
Sense of touch (hot, cold, texture) 1
Q3 What four primary tastes can our tongue identify? Taste Buds & Tongue 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Our tongue has taste buds that help us recognize: 1. Sweet (e.g., sugar, mango) 2. Salty (e.g., potato chips, salt) 3. Sour (e.g., lemon, raw mango) 4. Bitter (e.g., bitter gourd, neem).

Q4 Give two healthy habits to take proper care of our eyes. Eye Care & Hygiene 2 Marks
✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
Two healthy habits for eye care: 1. Wash eyes gently with clean cold water daily. 2. Do not watch television, tablet, or smartphone screens for too long or in a dark room.

✅ Step-by-Step Model Answer & Solution:
• Shrubs: Medium-sized bushy plants with thin, hard, woody stems branching near the ground (e.g., Rose, Hibiscus). • Herbs: Very small plants with soft, tender green stems; they live only for one season (e.g., Mint, Coriander, Spinach).

Q3 Why do climbers need support to grow upright, whereas creepers spread on the ground? Climbers and Creepers 3 Marks

• Climbers have thin and weak stems; they use tendrils to wind around supports like walls or sticks to climb upward (e.g., Money plant, Pea). • Creepers also have weak stems, but they bear big, heavy fruits (like Pumpkin, Watermelon) so they crawl along the ground.
