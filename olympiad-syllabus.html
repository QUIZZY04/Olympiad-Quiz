Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the general syllabus for Olympiad exams?

The syllabus for most Olympiad exams like SOF, SilverZone, and CREST is strictly based on the respective class curriculum prescribed by CBSE, ICSE, and various state boards.

Do Olympiads have a different syllabus than school exams?

While the core topics remain the same as the school curriculum, the difficulty level and application of concepts are much higher in Olympiad exams, especially in the Achievers or Scholar's zone.

How can I view the Olympiad syllabus for my class?

You can easily view the detailed class-wise and subject-wise syllabus for SOF, CREST, SilverZone, and NSTSE by selecting the respective Olympiad card above.

Are the mock tests aligned with the latest syllabus?

Yes, all our mock tests and chapter-wise practice quizzes are continuously updated to align perfectly with the latest 2026 exam patterns and syllabus guidelines.
