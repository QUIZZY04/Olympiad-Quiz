Q2
Describe the fire that destroyed the author's house. What heroic act did his mother perform?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. The Fire Outbreak: While Zan was doing homework on a cold Sunday afternoon with his red tabby cat purring on his papers, smoke began pouring through the ceiling seams. Within seconds, thick smoke filled the room, forcing Zan and his mother to flee into the front yard as flames engulfed the roof.

2. Mother's Heroic Act: Zan's mother bravely sprinted back into the burning inferno to retrieve a small metal box containing vital family documents. Then, crazed with grief, she ran back inside a second time to rescue letters and photographs of his late father, the only tangible memories of his existence, before being pulled out by firemen.

Q4
How did the students and teachers of the new high school surprise the author? What does the title 'A House Is Not a Home' mean? (Max Word Count)
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Generous Outpouring of Compassion:
When news of the fire spread, the students and teachers gathered at a table in the gym to present Zan with a massive collection of school supplies, notebooks, backpacks, and clothes—jeans, tops, sweats. Students who had never spoken to him introduced themselves, invited him to their homes, and welcomed him warmly into their community. For the first time, Zan felt hope and made real friends.

2. Meaning of the Title:
The title draws a profound philosophical distinction:

  • A 'house' is merely a physical structure of brick, wood, and mortar that can easily burn to ashes.
  • A 'home' is an emotional sanctuary forged by love, family, companionship, community, and belonging.

When his cat was safely returned by a kind woman and friends surrounded him, Zan realized that his physical house was being rebuilt, but his true emotional 'home' was restored within his heart.

🎯 Competency-Based & Value-Based Questions — Unit 25CBSE 2026-27
Emotional Resilience
Adolescent Vulnerability: How does Zan Gaudioso capture the fragile mental state of teenagers navigating loss?

Gaudioso captures the painful insecurity of adolescence with raw honesty. Moving to a new school already induces anxiety; losing one's home, clothes, and childhood cat compounds this into existential dread. Zan feels naked, humiliated, and disconnected from life. The narrative shows that teenage suffering often stems from feeling unloved or invisible. When the school community embraces him with tangible kindness, his emotional armor dissolves, demonstrating that empathy and belonging are the ultimate remedies for youth depression.

Value-Based Reflection
Kindness of Strangers: What role did the kind woman play who found the cat?

The woman who found the terrified cat went to extraordinary lengths to trace its owner. Even though the phone number on the collar was disconnected due to the fire, she persistently researched and located Zan. Her selfless integrity mirrors the generosity of the high school students, reinforcing the theme that human goodness surrounds us even in our darkest hours, ready to restore what was lost.
