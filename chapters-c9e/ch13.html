Poem 4: The Lake Isle of Innisfree

William Butler Yeats — Solitude, Nature & Longing for Peace — CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27

I will arise and go now, and go to Innisfree, And a small cabin build there, of clay and wattles made; Nine bean-rows will I have there, a hive for the honey-bee, And live alone in the bee-loud glade. And I shall have some peace there, for peace comes dropping slow, Dropping from the veils of the morning to where the cricket sings; There midnight's all a glimmer, and noon a purple glow, And evening full of the linnet's wings. I will arise and go now, for always night and day I hear the lake water lapping with low sounds by the shore; While I stand on the roadway, or on the pavements grey, I hear it in the deep heart's core.
— William Butler Yeats

Q4
Contrast the city life of the poet with his vision of life at Innisfree. (Max Word Count)
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Grim Reality of Urban Life:
The poet portrays contemporary city life through drab, somber imagery. The city is characterized by grey, artificial pavements, monotonous roadways, ceaseless traffic noise, mechanical routine, and emotional emptiness, creating intense mental fatigue.

2. Paradise of Innisfree:
In contrast, Innisfree is an idyllic sanctuary of pristine, sensory-rich nature. There, peace 'comes dropping slow' like morning mists; crickets sing, bees hum, mornings wear gentle veils, noons glow with purple blossoms, and evenings shimmer with linnet wings. One enjoys complete self-sufficiency through nine bean-rows and a beehive. The contrast reveals a romantic longing to flee artificial urbanization for tranquil communion with mother nature.

🎯 Competency-Based & Value-Based Questions — Unit 13CBSE 2026-27
Visual & Auditory Imagery
Literary Analysis: How does W.B. Yeats blend visual and auditory imagery in 'The Lake Isle of Innisfree'?

Yeats crafts an immersive musical sanctuary by intertwining sound and sight. Visual imagery includes 'veils of the morning' (dew and mist), 'glimmering midnight', 'purple glow' of midday, and 'linnet's wings' in twilight skies. Auditory imagery complements this with 'bee-loud glade', the cricket's chirp, flutter of wings, and the haunting, hypnotic alliteration of 'lake water lapping with low sounds by the shore'. The liquid /l/ sounds create an auditory trance that echoes the speaker's yearning.

Philosophical Reflection
Theme of Inner Peace: Is Innisfree merely a physical place, or a state of mind?

Innisfree is both a real geographical island in County Sligo, Ireland, and a profound psychological sanctuary. The fact that Yeats hears its waters while standing on London's grey pavements shows that Innisfree exists permanently within his consciousness—his 'deep heart's core'. It symbolizes an inner spiritual oasis that modern humans can retreat into when overwhelmed by urban materialism, reminding us that tranquility is cultivated within the spirit.
