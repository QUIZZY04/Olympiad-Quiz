Q1
What havoc did the super cyclone of October 1999 wreak in Orissa? How did Prashant survive the initial two days?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Havoc Wrought: On 27 October 1999, a super cyclone with monstrous winds of 350 km/h struck coastal Orissa. Ancient trees were uprooted, houses washed away, and a raging surge of saltwater inundated Ersama. Dead bloated human bodies and animal carcasses floated everywhere in dark floodwaters.

2. Initial Survival: Prashant and his friend's family survived by taking refuge on the open cement roof of their house for two freezing days, subsisting solely on tender green coconuts that had fallen from two coconut trees uprooted onto their roof.

Q2
How did Prashant make the perilous journey back to his native village Kalikuda?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Equipped with a sturdy long stick to determine the depth of floodwaters, Prashant embarked on a dangerous eighteen-kilometer trek through swirling, neck-deep waters. He frequently had to push away floating bloated corpses of humans and animals. Occasionally losing the road, he had to swim until he met two friends of his uncle with whom he waded together to his devastated village, Kalikuda.

Q3
How did Prashant organize the youth and community to solve the food and sanitation crisis at the shelter? (Max Word Count)
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Mobilizing Youth to Secure Food:
Seeing 2500 survivors starving on green coconuts at the Red Cross shelter, 19-year-old Prashant formed a youth volunteer team. They successfully pressured the local grain merchant to release his stored rice, allowing the camp to cook and eat their first hot meal in four days.

2. Sanitation and Medical Care:
He organized volunteers to clear the shelter of filth, vomit, urine, and floating carcasses, and to tend to the festering wounds and broken limbs of injured survivors.

3. Attracting Military Relief Helicopters:
When military helicopters dropped small rations and did not return, Prashant had children lie on the open sand with empty bowls on their stomachs to signal their desperate hunger. The pilots received the signal and began dropping regular food packets and medicines.

Q4
How did Prashant help the devastated widows and orphaned children overcome their grief?
4 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. For Orphaned Children: Prashant brought all orphaned children together and constructed a polythene sheet shelter for them. He mobilized women to look after them, and later resisted government institutions, successfully resettling orphans within their own community alongside childless widows, creating foster families filled with love and belonging.

2. For Grief-Stricken Widows: Seeing women sinking into depression, he persuaded them to join the 'food-for-work' program started by an NGO, and organized cricket matches and sports for children, lifting the community out of the dark chasm of grief.

Disaster Management & Youth Leadership
Leadership in Crisis: What leadership qualities make 19-year-old Prashant an inspiring role model for the youth of India?

Prashant exemplifies visionary grassroots leadership. In the face of personal trauma, he rose above his own grief to assume responsibility for 2500 helpless survivors. He showed organizational genius: forming task forces, delegating duties, utilizing psychological insight (engaging widows to care for orphans), and devising peaceful yet firm solutions for food. Prashant proves that real leadership does not depend on age, official power, or money, but on initiative, empathy, and the courage to act when others are paralyzed.

Social Rehabilitation
Community-Based Rehabilitation: Why did Prashant oppose putting orphans and widows in separate government institutions?

Prashant possessed deep sociological insight. He understood that institutionalizing children in impersonal orphanages causes emotional stunting without love, while isolating widows creates lifelong stigma and loneliness. By uniting childless widows with orphaned children into community foster families, both found emotional healing and mutual purpose, proving that community-based rehabilitation preserves emotional warmth and social dignity.
