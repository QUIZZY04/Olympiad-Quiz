Part I: Santosh Yadav — Exercise Q&A
Q1
Why was the holy man who gave Santosh's mother his blessings surprised? What does the name 'Santosh' mean?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Holy Man's Surprise: In the traditional patriarchal village society of Haryana, the birth of a son was considered a blessing while a daughter was not welcomed. A visiting wandering holy man assumed the family wanted a boy. However, Santosh's grandmother requested blessings for a daughter, astonishing the holy man who readily granted the unconventional blessing.

2. Meaning of 'Santosh': The girl was named 'Santosh', which means contentment. Yet Santosh was not always content with traditional societal limits placed on women.

Q3
How did Santosh Yadav take up mountaineering? Describe her training at Uttarkashi.
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

While studying at Maharani College in Jaipur, Santosh's hostel room faced the Aravalli Hills. She used to watch villagers climb up the hills and vanish over the crest. Curious, she went there herself one day and found a few mountaineers. They encouraged her to join them.

Santosh saved money, enrolled without her parents' permission in the Nehru Institute of Mountaineering at Uttarkashi, and wrote an apology letter to her father. She underwent rigorous expeditions every year, developing extraordinary physical endurance, resistance to extreme cold and altitude, and an iron will.

Q4
What unique mountaineering records did Santosh Yadav set? What incident showed her deep humanity?
5 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Global Mountaineering Records:
In 1992, at barely twenty years of age, Santosh Yadav scaled Mount Everest, becoming the youngest woman in the world to achieve the feat. Within twelve months, she joined an Indo-Nepalese Women's Expedition and scaled Everest a second time, becoming the only woman in mountaineering history to conquer Mount Everest twice.

2. Exemplary Humanity on the Slopes:
During her 1992 Everest expedition, Santosh displayed extraordinary compassion. At the South Col, she desperately tried to save a dying climber, though she was unsuccessful. However, she successfully saved the life of another teammate, Mohan Singh, by selflessly sharing her own precious oxygen bottle with him—a sacrifice without which Mohan Singh would have certainly perished.

3. Environmental Commitment:
Santosh was also an ardent environmentalist who collected and brought down 500 kilograms of garbage from the fragile slopes of the Himalayas.

Part II: Maria Sharapova — Exercise Q&A
Q5
What sacrifices did young Maria Sharapova make to train in Florida? How did she handle bullying?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Heartbreaking Sacrifices: At barely nine years old, Maria was separated from her mother, Yelena, who was forced to stay back in Siberia due to visa restrictions. Maria traveled to Florida, USA, with her father Yuri, who worked grueling shifts to fund her tennis training. Maria endured profound homesickness and loneliness for two long years.

2. Handling Bullying: Because Maria went to bed at 8 PM, older pupils returned at 11 PM, woke her up, and ordered her to clean the room. Instead of breaking down into tears, Maria became more quietly determined, tough, and mentally resilient. She learned how to take care of herself and never considered quitting.

Comparative Character Study
Determination & Resilience: Compare the life journeys of Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova. What common message do they offer young women?

Both Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova achieved pinnacle global success through unyielding grit, sacrifices, and refusal to conform to societal handicaps. Santosh defied rural patriarchal expectations in Haryana, resisted child marriage, and conquered Mount Everest twice. Maria endured painful maternal separation at age nine in Siberia, worked through poverty in Florida, and overcame locker-room bullying to capture Wimbledon at seventeen. Both women demonstrate that unwavering focus, mental toughness, and hard work can overcome gender bias, poverty, and isolation, proving that excellence is earned through persistent struggle.
