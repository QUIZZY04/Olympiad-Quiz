II. Long Answer Questions — In-Depth Emotional Analysis (100–120 Words / 6 Marks)
Q6
How did Kezia's perception of her father change from a terrifying giant to a loving, hardworking human being? (Max Word Count)
6 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Initial Fear and Estrangement:
Initially, Kezia perceived her father as an authoritarian figure—cold, gigantic, impatient, and harsh. Her fear was reinforced when he beat her with a ruler for accidentally destroying his speech papers, despite her tearful explanation that it was intended as a birthday surprise.

2. The Turning Point (Night of the Nightmare):
One night, when both Kezia's mother and grandmother were away at the hospital, Kezia woke up screaming from a terrifying nightmare of a butcher with a knife and a rope. She expected to be alone and terrified, but to her astonishment, her father stood beside her bed holding a candle. He blew out the candle, scooped her gently into his strong arms, and carried her to his own large bedroom.

3. Tenderness and Protection:
He tucked her in carefully with warm blankets and lay down beside her. When Kezia still felt cold, he told her to rub her cold feet against his legs to get warm. Resting her head close beneath his armpit and clutching his shirt, Kezia listened to his beating heart.

4. Epiphany and Deep Empathy:
Kezia realized that her father worked excessively hard every day to provide for the family, leaving him too exhausted in the evenings to play like Mr. MacDonald. Her resentment melted into boundless compassion and love, concluding with her heartfelt murmur: 'What a big heart you've got, Father dear.'

🎯 Competency-Based & Value-Based Questions — Unit 3CBSE 2026-27
Value-Based / Parenting
Parent-Child Dynamics: Why is open communication crucial between parents and children? How does Katherine Mansfield illustrate this through Kezia and her father?

Mansfield illustrates that authoritarian parenting without verbal affection creates profound emotional distance and unnecessary trauma. Kezia's father genuinely loved his daughter, but his gruff demeanor, exhausted silence, and harsh discipline made Kezia view him as a monster. Had he communicated his fatigue gently or explained the significance of his work calmly, Kezia would not have suffered from fear and stuttering. The story underscores that parents must express warmth visibly, and children need an emotionally safe environment where mistakes are corrected with guidance rather than corporal punishment.

Character Sketch
Character Analysis: Write a character sketch of Kezia's grandmother.

Kezia's grandmother is the gentle emotional pillar of the household. She possesses deep understanding, patience, and empathetic wisdom. Unlike Kezia's parents who are preoccupied with social standing and business, grandmother provides Kezia with unconditional love, comforting lap, and emotional solace. She actively attempts to cultivate filial affection by sending Kezia to converse with her parents on Sundays and suggesting the thoughtful birthday cushion. When Kezia is weeping after the ruler beating, grandmother wraps her in a shawl and rocks her in the rocking chair, assuring her that pain will pass with sleep.
