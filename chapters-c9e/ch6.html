Chapter 6: My Childhood

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam — Values, Secular Harmony & Upbringing — CBSE Marking Scheme 2026-27

I. Thinking about the Text — Short Questions (30–40 Words / 2–3 Marks)
Q1
Where was Abdul Kalam's house? What characteristics does he say he inherited from his parents?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. House Location: Kalam's ancestral house was located on the Mosque Street in the island town of Rameswaram (in erstwhile Madras State). It was a fairly large pucca house built of limestone and brick in the middle of the nineteenth century.

2. Inherited Values: From his father, Jainulabdeen, Kalam inherited honesty and self-discipline. From his mother, Ashiamma, he inherited faith in goodness and deep kindness, as well as an expansive generosity of spirit.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Location: Mosque Street, Rameswaram1 Mark
From Father: Honesty and self-discipline1 Mark
From Mother: Faith in goodness and deep kindness1 Mark
Q2
Who were Abdul Kalam's school friends? What did they later become?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

Kalam had three close childhood friends from orthodox Hindu Brahmin families:

  1. Ramanadha Sastry: Son of Pakshi Lakshmana Sastry (head priest of the Rameswaram Temple). He later took over the priesthood of the famous Rameswaram temple from his father.
  2. Aravindan: Entered the business of arranging transport and logistics for visiting Hindu pilgrims.
  3. Sivaprakasan: Became a prominent catering contractor for the Southern Railways.
CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Ramanadha Sastry -> Head priest of Rameswaram temple1 Mark
Aravindan -> Transport contractor for pilgrims1 Mark
Sivaprakasan -> Catering contractor for Southern Railways1 Mark
Q3
How did Abdul Kalam earn his first wages? How did he feel about it?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

When World War II broke out in 1939, emergency was declared and the train halt at Rameswaram station was suspended. Newspaper bundles thrown from the moving train between Rameswaram and Dhanuskodi needed to be collected. Kalam's cousin Samsuddin, who distributed newspapers, engaged Kalam to catch the bundles. For this work, Kalam earned his first wages. Even half a century later, Kalam recalled that he felt an immense surge of personal pride in earning his own money for the very first time.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Context: WWII emergency, train halt cancelled at Rameswaram1 Mark
Task: Helping cousin Samsuddin catch newspaper bundles from moving trains1 Mark
Emotional impact: Tremendous surge of pride in earning first wages1 Mark
Q4
What happened when a new teacher arrived at Rameswaram Elementary School? How was it resolved?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

When Kalam was in fifth standard, a new teacher arrived who could not stomach a Hindu priest's son (Ramanadha Sastry) sitting beside a Muslim boy wearing a cap. He ordered Kalam to go and sit on the back bench. Both boys felt deeply dejected and wept. Ramanadha reported the humiliation to his father, Pakshi Lakshmana Sastry.

Lakshmana Sastry immediately summoned the teacher and bluntly reprimanded him, telling him not to spread the poison of social inequality and communal intolerance in the minds of innocent children. He gave the teacher the choice to apologize or leave the school and island. The teacher repented, reformed, and learned a lasting lesson in communal harmony.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Prejudice: New teacher segregated Kalam to back bench due to cap/religion1 Mark
Intervention: Pakshi Lakshmana Sastry summoned and reprimanded teacher1 Mark
Resolution: Teacher reformed; island's secular unity upheld1 Mark
Q5
How did Sivasubramania Iyer's wife react when Kalam was first invited for dinner? How did she change on his second visit?
3 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. First Visit: Science teacher Sivasubramania Iyer, a rebel Brahmin, invited Kalam home for a meal. His conservative wife was horrified at the idea of a Muslim boy dining in her ritually pure kitchen and adamantly refused to serve him. Iyer did not lose his temper; he served Kalam with his own hands and sat beside him to eat.

2. Second Visit: The following weekend, when Kalam revisited, Iyer's wife took him inside her kitchen herself and served him food with her own hands, having observed his cultured manners and overcome her communal prejudice.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
First visit: Wife refused to serve Muslim boy in her kitchen; teacher served him1½ Marks
Second visit: Wife reformed, welcomed Kalam inside kitchen, served him herself1½ Marks
II. Long Answer Questions — Communal Harmony and Parental Wisdom (100–120 Words / 6 Marks)
Q6
What did Kalam's father say when Kalam asked permission to leave Rameswaram to study at Ramanathapuram? Discuss the profound wisdom of Jainulabdeen's response. (Max Word Count)
6 Marks
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

1. Father's Philosophical Blessing:
When Kalam sought permission to pursue higher education at the district headquarters in Ramanathapuram, his father Jainulabdeen encouraged him without hesitation, stating: 'Abul! I know you have to go away to grow. Does the seagull not fly across the sun, alone and without a nest?'

2. Consoling the Hesitant Mother:
Kalam's mother was hesitant and tearful about sending her young son away. To console her, Jainulabdeen quoted the Lebanese poet-philosopher Khalil Gibran:

'Your children are not your children. They are the sons and daughters of Life's longing for itself. They come through you but not from you, and though they are with you yet they belong not to you. You may give them your love but not your thoughts, for they have their own thoughts.'

3. Significance:
This response highlights Jainulabdeen's extraordinary progressive wisdom. Despite possessing no formal schooling, he understood that parental love must foster independence rather than possessive attachment. He granted Kalam the freedom to soar beyond the horizons of Rameswaram, instilling wings of self-reliance that eventually made him India's 'Missile Man' and President.

CBSE Marking Scheme Breakdown 2026-27
Father's seagull metaphor: Must fly alone across the sun to grow2 Marks
Quoting Khalil Gibran on children belonging to the future, not parents2 Marks
Philosophical depth: Progressive mindset, selfless parenting, nurturing freedom2 Marks
Q7
How did science teacher Sivasubramania Iyer's progressive attitude and dinner invitation challenge orthodoxy and transform his conservative wife's mindset?
4 Marks (60–80 Words)
✅ CBSE Standard Answer (Max Word Count)

A.P.J. Abdul Kalam's science teacher, Sivasubramania Iyer, was an enlightened Brahmin rebel against rigid communal orthodoxy:

  1. The Dinner Invitation: He invited young Muslim Kalam to his home for a meal. His conservative wife, horrified by orthodox notions of ritual purity, refused to serve a Muslim boy inside her kitchen.
  2. Exemplary Calm Leadership: Rather than losing his temper, Sivasubramania Iyer served Kalam with his own hands and sat beside him to eat, demonstrating that food and humanity transcend religious barriers.
  3. The Second Invitation & Transformation: When Kalam felt hesitant, the teacher invited him again the following weekend, advising: 'Once you decide to change the system, such problems have to be confronted.' When Kalam arrived the next week, the wife escorted him into her kitchen and served him food with her own hands, proving that quiet conviction dissolves bigotry.

Wife's orthodox refusal to serve a Muslim boy in kitchen1.0 Mark
Teacher's calm response: Serving Kalam personally and dining together1.5 Marks
Transformation on second visit: Wife serves Kalam in kitchen1.5 Marks
🎯 Competency-Based & Value-Based Questions — Unit 6CBSE 2026-27
Value-Based / Secularism
Theme of Social Harmony: How does Kalam's childhood demonstrate that communal harmony is built through individual courage and enlightened leadership?

Kalam's upbringing in Rameswaram proves that communal peace requires proactive defense. When religious bigotry threatened the town through the new schoolteacher, the high priest Lakshmana Sastry directly confronted the perpetrator, demanding reformation rather than appeasement. Similarly, science teacher Sivasubramania Iyer challenged domestic orthodoxy by serving Kalam himself, transforming his wife's rigid heart through patient example. These episodes show that societal prejudice cannot survive when respected elders and teachers champion equality, proving that India's unity relies on active empathy and moral courage.

Character Sketch
Character Analysis: Write a character sketch of Sivasubramania Iyer.

Sivasubramania Iyer was an orthodox Brahmin science teacher who acted as a radical catalyst for social transformation. He was a visionary who believed that merit transcends caste and religion. When his conservative wife refused to serve Kalam, Iyer remained composed, avoiding domestic conflict while firmly standing by his convictions. He broke caste taboos by dining beside Kalam and mentoring him: 'Once you decide to change the system, such problems have to be confronted.' His broadminded mentorship provided Kalam with intellectual ambition and secular values.
