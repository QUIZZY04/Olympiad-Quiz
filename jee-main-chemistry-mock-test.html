Chemistry Syllabus Weightage for JEE

Chemistry is universally considered the most scoring section in JEE Main if you have thoroughly memorized NCERT and practiced numerical applications.

  • Physical Chemistry (35%)
    Formula & Numerical Focus

    Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Solutions. Practice the numerical value section heavily here.

  • Organic Chemistry (35%)
    Reaction Mechanisms

    GOC, Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes, Alcohols, Aldehydes & Ketones, Amines, Biomolecules. Focus on intermediate stability and reagents.

  • Inorganic Chemistry (30%)
    Factual & NCERT Based

    Chemical Bonding, Coordination Compounds, p-block, d-block, and f-block elements. Focus heavily on periodic trends and exceptions.
