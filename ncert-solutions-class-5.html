Class 5 NCERT Solutions FAQ

Which textbooks should a Class 5 CBSE student follow in 2026-27? ▾
Under the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and NCF-SE 2023, NCERT provides dual curriculum pathways for Class 5: Maths Mela & Math-Magic for Mathematics, Santoor & Marigold for English, Veena & Rimjhim for Hindi, and Our Wondrous World & Looking Around for Environmental Studies. OlympiadQuiz provides 100% verified solutions for both sets of books so students never face any gaps regardless of which textbook their school prescribes.
Are in-text exercises, fill-in-the-blanks, and activities included? ▾
Yes. Our solutions cover 100% of textbook exercises, including "Find Out", "Try These", "Think and Discuss", puzzle challenges, word problems, and experiment observation tables aligned with Tiwari Academy standards.
How do these solutions help in Olympiad exams like IMO and NSO? ▾
School Olympiads like IMO and NSO require deep conceptual understanding and multi-step deduction. Our detailed mathematical workings and scientific explanations develop the foundational intuition needed to excel in competitive exams.
