Library Badge Code Challenge

Real-World Math Context: The school librarian gives each child a library code based on their name: 10 points for each letter in the first name plus 5 points for each vowel (A, E, I, O, U).

Analytical Task: Calculate the total library badge score for 'KAVITA'. How many vowels are in her name, and what is her final code value?

1. Name: K-A-V-I-T-A contains 6 letters.
2. Letter points = 6 × 10 = 60 points.
3. Vowels in KAVITA: A, I, A = 3 vowels.
4. Vowel bonus points = 3 × 5 = 15 points.
5. Total library badge score = 60 + 15 = 75 points.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1M letter points + 1M vowel identification + 1M final calculation.

Toy Store Clearance Sale

Real-World Math Context: During Diwali sale, the shop offers a 'Buy 2 Get ₹10 Off' coupon on toy purchases above ₹50.

Analytical Task: Arjun selects a toy drum for ₹35 and a toy car for ₹42. Does he qualify for the discount? What is his final bill amount?

1. Total cost before discount = ₹35 + ₹42 = ₹77.
2. Since ₹77 is greater than ₹50 and he bought 2 toys, Arjun qualifies for the discount.
3. Discount = ₹10.
4. Final bill amount = ₹77 - ₹10 = ₹67.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1M qualification logic + 1M addition + 1M discount subtraction.

Train Journey Schedule Analysis

Real-World Math Context: The Shatabdi Express leaves New Delhi station at 06:00 AM and arrives in Amritsar at 12:30 PM on the same day.

Analytical Task: How long is the train journey in hours and minutes? If the train is delayed by 45 minutes, at what time will it reach?

1. From 06:00 AM to 12:00 Noon = 6 hours.
2. From 12:00 Noon to 12:30 PM = 30 minutes.
• Scheduled journey time = 6 hours and 30 minutes.
3. With a 45-minute delay: 12:30 PM + 45 minutes = 12:30 + 30 min (1:00 PM) + 15 min = 01:15 PM.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1.5M journey duration calculation + 1.5M delayed arrival time.

Classroom Floor Tiling Project

Real-World Math Context: The school is laying square tiles of side 1 metre in an activity hall that is 6 metres long and 4 metres wide.

Analytical Task: How many 1-metre square tiles are needed to pave the entire floor? If each tile costs ₹50, find the total cost of tiles.

1. Area of floor = Length × Width = 6 m × 4 m = 24 square metres.
2. Area of 1 tile = 1 m × 1 m = 1 square metre.
3. Number of tiles needed = 24 ÷ 1 = 24 tiles.
4. Total cost = 24 × ₹50 = ₹1,200.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1.5M tile count calculation + 1.5M total cost multiplication.

Rakhi Gift Hamper Budgeting

Real-World Math Context: Simran has ₹250 savings. She wants to buy a rakhi for ₹45, a box of chocolates for ₹120, and a greeting card for ₹35 for her brother.

Analytical Task: Can Simran afford all three items with her savings? How much money will be left with her or how much more will she need?

1. Total cost of gifts = ₹45 + ₹120 + ₹35 = ₹(45 + 35 + 120) = ₹80 + ₹120 = ₹200.
2. Simran's savings = ₹250.
3. Since ₹250 > ₹200, she can easily afford all three items.
4. Remaining money = ₹250 - ₹200 = ₹50 left.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1.5M addition of 3 gift items + 1.5M comparison with savings and balance.

Analytical Task: How many total runners participated? If Rahul wore bib 789 and Sanya wore bib 814, how many runners were between them?

Community Kitchen Lemonade Preparation

Real-World Math Context: To make lemonade for 50 people at a sports day, students use 8 litres of water, 1 litre of lemon juice, and 500 mL of sugar syrup.

Analytical Task: What is the total volume of lemonade prepared in millilitres? If each glass holds 200 mL, can they serve 45 glasses?

1. Convert all volumes to millilitres (mL):
• Water = 8 L = 8,000 mL
• Lemon juice = 1 L = 1,000 mL
• Sugar syrup = 500 mL
• Total volume = 8,000 + 1,000 + 500 = 9,500 mL.
2. Volume needed for 45 glasses of 200 mL each = 45 × 200 mL = 9,000 mL.
3. Since 9,500 mL > 9,000 mL, yes, they can easily serve 45 glasses (with 500 mL left over).
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1.5M litre to millilitre conversion and summation + 1.5M capacity comparison.

Handicrafts Stall Revenue Summary

Real-World Math Context: A pottery artisan brought 80 clay diyas to the fair. He sold 55 diyas on Day 1 for ₹10 each. On Day 2, he reduced the price to ₹8 each and sold all remaining diyas.

Analytical Task: How many diyas were sold on Day 2? What was the total revenue earned by the artisan from selling all 80 diyas?

1. Diyas sold on Day 2 = Total - Day 1 = 80 - 55 = 25 diyas.
2. Day 1 revenue = 55 × ₹10 = ₹550.
3. Day 2 revenue = 25 × ₹8 = ₹200.
4. Total revenue = ₹550 + ₹200 = ₹750.
CBSE Assessment Rubric: 1M Day 2 count + 1M Day 1 & Day 2 revenues + 1M total revenue sum.

Analytical Task: How much water was in the tank at the end of the day? Can the tank take another 200 litres without overflowing?

1. Water left after morning usage = 850 - 475 = 375 litres.
