Math Olympiad Tricks (Class 1–10)

Learn powerful problem-solving strategies based on Olympiad syllabus

Introduction

Olympiad mathematics questions require logical thinking, pattern recognition and creative problem solving. Instead of solving problems using long methods, students can use smart mathematical tricks to reach the answer quickly.

Class 1 Math Tricks

Counting Forward Trick

6 + 3 Count after 6 → 7,8,9 Answer = 9

Counting Backward Trick

9 − 4 Count backward → 8,7,6,5 Answer = 5

Pattern Recognition

2,4,6,8,? Pattern = +2 Answer = 10

Class 2 Math Tricks

Tens and Ones Method

47 + 26 40 + 20 = 60 7 + 6 = 13 Answer = 73

Quick Subtraction

63 − 29 63 − 30 = 33 Add back 1 Answer = 34

Doubling Trick

2 × 14 14 + 14 = 28

Class 3 Math Tricks

Class 4 Math Tricks

Distributive Property

24 × 13 24 × (10 + 3) = 240 + 72 = 312

Fraction Simplification

8/12 Divide by 4 Answer = 2/3

Class 5 Math Tricks

Divisibility Rule for 9

729 7 + 2 + 9 = 18 18 ÷ 9 = 2

LCM Trick

LCM of 6 and 8 6 = 2 × 3 8 = 2 × 2 × 2 LCM = 24

Class 6 Math Tricks

Integer Addition Trick

−8 + 13 13 − 8 Answer = 5

Ratio Simplification

Class 7 Math Tricks

Algebra Identity

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Difference of Squares

49 − 25 (7 − 5)(7 + 5) = 2 × 12 = 24

Class 8 Math Tricks

Square Shortcut

19² (20 − 1)² = 400 − 40 + 1 = 361

Cube Trick

12³ = 1728

Class 9 Math Tricks

Factorization Trick

x² − 9 = (x − 3)(x + 3)

Midpoint Formula

Midpoint of (2,4) and (6,8) (2+6)/2 , (4+8)/2 Answer = (4,6)

Trigonometric Identity

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

Quadratic Formula
