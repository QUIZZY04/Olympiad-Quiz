Math Olympiad Tricks (Class 1–10)

Learn smart techniques and shortcuts to solve Olympiad mathematics problems faster.

Class 1 Math Tricks

Class 1 Olympiad questions focus on counting, number recognition and simple addition. Students should develop observation and number sense.

Counting Trick

Example: 4 + 3 Count after 4 → 5, 6, 7 Answer = 7

Pattern Trick

2, 4, 6, 8, ? Pattern = +2 Answer = 10

Class 2 Math Tricks

Students work with two-digit numbers and simple mental calculations.

Tens and Ones Method

48 + 23 40 + 20 = 60 8 + 3 = 11 Answer = 71

Quick Subtraction

63 − 28 63 − 30 = 33 Add back 2 Answer = 35

Class 3 Math Tricks

Multiplication and division become important concepts.

Skip Counting Trick

6 × 4 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24

Multiply by 5 Trick

5 × 18 10 × 18 = 180 180 ÷ 2 = 90

Class 4 Math Tricks

Students solve fraction problems and larger multiplications.

Distributive Property

24 × 13 24 × (10 + 3) = 240 + 72 = 312

Class 5 Math Tricks

Divisibility rules help solve many Olympiad questions quickly.

Divisibility Rule for 9

729 7 + 2 + 9 = 18 18 ÷ 9 = 2 So 729 is divisible by 9.

Class 6 Math Tricks

Students learn integers and algebraic expressions.

Integer Addition Trick

−6 + 10 10 − 6 = 4 Answer = 4

Class 7 Math Tricks

Algebraic identities help simplify expressions quickly.

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Class 8 Math Tricks

Squares and factorization shortcuts improve solving speed.

18² = (20 − 2)² = 400 − 80 + 4 = 324

Class 9 Math Tricks

Factorization techniques simplify polynomial expressions.

a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)

Class 10 Math Tricks

Trigonometry identities help solve Olympiad problems faster.

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
