🧠 The "Olympiad Thinking" Framework

Success in exams like IMO, iOM, and NSTSE is not about how many formulas you know; it's about how efficiently you apply them. Traditional school math rewards the long way—competitive math rewards the smart way.

1. Pattern Spotting

Olympiad problems often look impossible at first glance. We teach you to identify the underlying "Unit of Repeat" that simplifies complex data into a 5-second solution.

2. Logical Elimination

With four options, the answer is already on the page. Use "Value Substitution" and "Last Digit Checks" to eliminate wrong answers without solving the whole problem.

3. Time Optimization

The "First Pass" strategy ensures you never leave easy marks on the table. Learn when to pivot from a calculation to a logical guess.
