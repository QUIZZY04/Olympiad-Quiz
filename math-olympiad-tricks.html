Math Olympiad Tricks (Class 1–10)

Smart problem-solving techniques for Olympiad mathematics

Class 1 Math Tricks

Focus on counting, number recognition and simple addition.

Example: 3 + 4 Count forward → 4,5,6,7 Answer = 7

Class 2 Math Tricks

Break numbers into tens and ones.

48 + 23 40 + 20 = 60 8 + 3 = 11 Answer = 71

Class 3 Math Tricks

Use multiplication tables and skip counting.

6 × 4 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24

Class 4 Math Tricks

25 × 12 25 × (10 + 2) = 250 + 50 = 300

Class 5 Math Tricks

Divisibility rule for 9 Sum of digits divisible by 9 → number divisible by 9

Class 6 Math Tricks

−7 + 10 = 3

Class 7 Math Tricks

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Class 8 Math Tricks

18² = (20 − 2)² = 400 − 80 + 4 = 324

Class 9 Math Tricks

a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)

Class 10 Math Tricks
