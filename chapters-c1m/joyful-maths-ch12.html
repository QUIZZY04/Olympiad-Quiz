Chapter 12: How Much Can We Spend?

Indian Currency: Coins ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10, Notes ₹10, ₹20 & Toy Bills | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Recognizing Indian coins: ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10.
  • Recognizing currency notes: ₹10 and ₹20 notes.
  • Combining coins to pay an exact amount (e.g. ₹7 = ₹5 + ₹2).
  • Calculating total cost of items bought at a stationary/toy shop.
  • Calculating return change after payment.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Identifying Coins
Name the four coins commonly used in India shown in your textbook.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• The four common Indian coins are: ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, and ₹10.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 coins named (₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10) 2 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Making ₹6 with Coins
How can you pay ₹6 using two coins?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Take one ₹5 coin and one ₹1 coin.
• ₹5 + ₹1 = ₹6.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
₹5 + ₹1 = ₹6 2 M

Q4 Let Us Do • Making ₹10 with ₹5 Coins
How many ₹5 coins do you need to make ₹10?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• ₹5 + ₹5 = ₹10.
• You need two ₹5 coins.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two ₹5 coins 1 M
₹5 + ₹5 = ₹10 1 M

Q6 Let Us Think • Buying an Ice Candy
An orange ice candy costs ₹10. Simran pays with two ₹5 coins. Did she pay the exact amount?

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

₹5 + ₹5 = ₹10 1 M
Q7 Textbook Exercise • Total Bill at Toy Shop
A toy top costs ₹6 and a balloon costs ₹2. What is the total cost?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Cost of top = ₹6
• Cost of balloon = ₹2
• Total cost = ₹6 + ₹2 = ₹8.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
₹6 + ₹2 = ₹8 2 M
Q8 Textbook Exercise • Return Change Calculation
Kabir buys a notebook for ₹15. He hands the shopkeeper a ₹20 note. How much change does Kabir get back?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Money given = ₹20
• Cost of notebook = ₹15
• Change returned = ₹20 - ₹15 = ₹5.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
₹20 - ₹15 = ₹5 1 M
Answer: ₹5 1 M
Q9 Let Us Do • Coin Rubbing Activity
How do you make a coin print on paper using a pencil?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Place a coin underneath a sheet of paper.
2. Rub gently over the paper with a soft pencil.
3. The design and number (₹1, ₹2, ₹5) appears like magic on paper!
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Place coin under paper 1 M
Rub with pencil 1 M

Q11 Let Us Think • Can You Buy Both?
Aman has ₹10 in his pocket. A toy car costs ₹8 and a ball costs ₹5. Can Aman buy both toys together?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Total cost of both = ₹8 + ₹5 = ₹13.
2. Aman only has ₹10.
3. Since ₹13 is more than ₹10, No, Aman cannot buy both toys together (he is short by ₹3).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
No, total is ₹13 > ₹10 1 M
Short by ₹3 noted 1 M
Q12 Textbook Exercise • Match Money to Amount
Match the coin combinations to their total:
(a) ₹5 + ₹5
(b) ₹5 + ₹2 + ₹1
(c) ₹2 + ₹2 + ₹1
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• (a) ₹5 + ₹5 = ₹10
• (b) ₹5 + ₹2 + ₹1 = ₹8
• (c) ₹2 + ₹2 + ₹1 = ₹5
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 correctly evaluated 3 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Stationery Shop Purchase Case Study

Rohan goes to the stationery shop with a ₹20 note. He buys a wooden pencil for ₹5 and an eraser for ₹3.

Analytical Task: What is the total cost of the pencil and eraser? How much change should the shopkeeper return to Rohan from his ₹20 note?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total cost = ₹5 + ₹3 = ₹8.
2. Change returned = ₹20 - ₹8 = ₹12 (e.g., a ₹10 coin and a ₹2 coin).
Rubric: 1.5 M for ₹5 + ₹3 = ₹8 + 1.5 M for ₹20 - ₹8 = ₹12 change
