Chapter 10: How do I Spend my Day?

Daily Routine, Sequencing Events, Day and Night, Clock Awareness | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Sequencing daily activities in chronological order: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night.
  • Recognizing activities that take longer time vs shorter time.
  • Understanding days of the week and daily rhythms.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Sequencing • Morning Routine
Arrange these actions in the correct order for the morning: (A) Going to school, (B) Brushing teeth, (C) Waking up from bed, (D) Eating breakfast.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Correct Order:
1. (C) Waking up from bed
2. (B) Brushing teeth
3. (D) Eating breakfast
4. (A) Going to school.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct sequence C → B → D → A 2 M
Q2 Time Duration • Longer vs Shorter
Which activity takes a longer time: (a) Blinking your eyes once, or (b) Taking a bath?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Taking a bath takes a longer time (a few minutes).
• Blinking your eyes takes a very short time (less than a second).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Taking bath identified as longer 1 M
Explanation of duration 1 M
Q3 Parts of the Day • Matching
Match the activity with the time of day:
(a) Seeing stars and the moon
(b) Having lunch
(c) Eating dinner
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Seeing stars and the moon → Night
(b) Having lunch → Afternoon
(c) Eating dinner → Night / Late Evening
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 3 matched correctly 3 M
Q4 Daily Life • Cooking Steps
What takes more time: Boiling a pot of milk or drinking a sip of milk?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Boiling a pot of milk takes more time because it requires heating on the gas stove for several minutes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Boiling milk takes more time 1 M
Q5 Days of the Week
How many days are there in a week? Which day is usually a school holiday?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• There are 7 days in a week (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
• Sunday is usually a school holiday.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
7 days in a week 1 M
Sunday identified as holiday 1 M
Q6 Observation • Sun Movement
From which direction does the Sun rise in the morning, and where does it set in the evening?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The Sun rises in the East in the morning.
• The Sun sets in the West in the evening.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rises in the East 1 M
Sets in the West 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Chintu's Sunday Schedule

On Sunday, Chintu has breakfast at 8:00 in the morning, plays cricket in the park at 5:00 in the evening, studies for school at 6:30 PM, and sleeps at 9:00 PM at night.

Analytical Task: Which activity does Chintu do in the evening? Arrange breakfast, sleeping, and cricket in order from earliest to latest in the day.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Playing cricket is done in the evening (5:00 PM).
2. Earliest to latest order: Breakfast (Morning) → Playing Cricket (Evening) → Sleeping (Night).
Rubric: 1.5 M for evening cricket + 1.5 M for correct chronological order
