Q1 Tens and Ones • Place Value
What number is 6 Tens and 4 Ones?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 6 Tens = 60
• 4 Ones = 4
• 60 + 4 = 64 (Sixty-four).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
60 + 4 1 M
64 1 M
Q2 Number Names • 51 to 100
Write the numeral for:
(a) Seventy-eight
(b) Ninety-five
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Seventy-eight = 78
(b) Ninety-five = 95

Q4 Before and After • Late 90s
What comes just before 100, and what comes just after 89?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Just before 100: 99
• Just after 89: 90

Q5 Century Concept • Making 100
How many tens are needed to make 100?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
10 tens make 100 (10 × 10 = 100).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
10 tens 1 M
Q6 Comparison • Two-Digit Numbers
Which number is greater: 87 or 78? Explain why.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Explanation:
• 87 has 8 tens.
• 78 has 7 tens.
Since 8 tens is greater than 7 tens, 87 is greater than 78 (87 > 78).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
87 > 78 1 M
Reasoning with tens digit 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Centurions on the Cricket Pitch

Batsman Virat has scored 90 runs. He hits a boundary (4 runs) and then two singles (1 run each).

Analytical Task: What is Virat's score now? How many more runs does he need to reach a century (100 runs)?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Virat's score: 90 + 4 + 1 + 1 = 96 runs.
2. Century = 100 runs. Runs needed: 100 - 96 = 4 runs.
Rubric: 1.5 M for score 96 + 1.5 M for 4 runs needed
