Numbers 51 to 100, Sugarcane Bundles of 10 & 100-Chart | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Extending place value to numbers from 51 to 100.
  • Grouping by 10s: 5 tens = 50 up to 10 tens = 100.
  • Writing numerals and number names up to One Hundred.
  • Completing missing numbers in the 51-100 grid.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Sugarcane Harvesting Case Study

Farmer Sham Lal ties sugarcane stalks into bundles of 10 stalks each. He has 6 full bundles and 4 loose stalks on his cart.

Analytical Task: How many sugarcane stalks does Sham Lal have in total? How many bundles will he have if he cuts 6 more stalks?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. 6 bundles of 10 = 60. Loose stalks = 4. Total = 60 + 4 = 64 sugarcane stalks.
2. With 6 more stalks, loose stalks = 4 + 6 = 10 (which makes 1 new bundle).
3. Total bundles = 6 + 1 = 7 full bundles of 10 (70 stalks).
Rubric: 1.5 M for 64 stalks + 1.5 M for 7 full bundles
