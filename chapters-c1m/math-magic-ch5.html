Chapter 5: Numbers from Ten to Twenty

Bundles of 10, Counting 10-20, Tens and Ones, Addition & Subtraction | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Making groups of 10 from loose objects (matchsticks, pencils).
  • Counting from 10 to 20: 10 + 1 = 11 up to 10 + 10 = 20.
  • Partitioning numbers into Tens and Ones.
  • Comparing numbers from 10 to 20: greater than and smaller than.
  • Addition and subtraction within 20.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (24 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Bundle of 10 Matchsticks
How many loose matchsticks make 1 bundle of 10? What do we call 10 ones?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Exactly 10 loose matchsticks tied together make 1 bundle.
• 10 ones is called 1 Ten.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
10 loose matchsticks 1 M
10 ones = 1 Ten 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Make a Group of 10
Look at 14 ice-cream cups. Circle a group of 10 cups. How many cups remain uncircled?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Total cups = 14
• Circled group = 10 (1 ten)
• Uncircled cups = 14 - 10 = 4 cups.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Group of 10 circled 1 M
4 remaining 1 M

Q13 Textbook Exercise • Addition within 20 (4 + 5)
Solve: 4 + 5 = __ and 14 + 5 = __.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• 4 + 5 = 9
• 14 + 5 = 10 + (4 + 5) = 10 + 9 = 19.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
4+5 = 9 1 M
14+5 = 19 1 M
Q14 Textbook Exercise • Addition 8 + 7
Solve: 8 + 7 = __ using the 'Make 10' method.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. 8 needs 2 to make 10.
2. 7 = 2 + 5.
3. 8 + 2 = 10.
4. 10 + 5 = 15.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Make 10 steps shown 1 M
Answer: 15 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Matchstick Bundling Project Case Study

Deepak has 14 loose matchsticks on his work table. His teacher asks him to make a bundle of 10 matchsticks using a red rubber band.

Analytical Task: How many bundles of 10 can Deepak make? How many loose matchsticks will remain outside the bundle?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. 14 has 1 ten and 4 ones.
2. Deepak can make 1 bundle of 10 matchsticks.
3. 4 loose matchsticks will remain outside.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 1 bundle of 10 + 1.5 M for 4 loose sticks
