Chapter 12: Money

Coins, Currency Notes, Combining Coins to Pay Exact Price & Change | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Coins used in India: ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10.
  • Notes used in India: ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100.
  • Making different amounts using combinations of coins.
  • Calculating the cost of buying toys and stationery.
  • Calculating balance change returned by shopkeeper.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Common Coins in India
Name the four coins shown in the textbook that we use to buy things in India.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• ₹1 coin, ₹2 coin, ₹5 coin, and ₹10 coin.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
All 4 coins named 2 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Make ₹1 Using Coins
How do you pay ₹1 using coins?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Pay with one ₹1 coin.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
One ₹1 coin 2 M
Q3 Textbook Exercise • Make ₹2 in Two Different Ways
Show two different ways to pay ₹2 using coins.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Way 1: One ₹2 coin.
• Way 2: Two ₹1 coins (₹1 + ₹1 = ₹2).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Way 1: One ₹2 coin 1 M
Way 2: Two ₹1 coins 1 M

Q5 Textbook Exercise • Make ₹4 Using Coins
Show two ways to pay ₹4 using coins.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Q6 Textbook Exercise • Make ₹5 in Different Ways

[3 Marks]

• Way 3: Five ₹1 coins.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Village Fair Toy Stall Case Study

At the village fair, a clay flute costs ₹8 and a paper pinwheel costs ₹4. Meena has a ₹20 note in her purse.

Analytical Task: What is the total cost of the flute and pinwheel? How much change should the stall owner give back to Meena?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total cost = ₹8 + ₹4 = ₹12.
2. Change returned = ₹20 - ₹12 = ₹8 (e.g. a ₹5 coin, a ₹2 coin, and a ₹1 coin).
Rubric: 1.5 M for ₹8 + ₹4 = ₹12 + 1.5 M for ₹20 - ₹12 = ₹8 change
