Q1 Coin Combination • Make ₹8
How can you make ₹8 using ₹2 coins?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8.
• You need four ₹2 coins to make ₹8.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Four ₹2 coins identified 2 M
Q2 Shopping Sum • Pen and Notebook
A notebook costs ₹10 and a pencil costs ₹3. How much do both items cost together?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Notebook = ₹10
• Pencil = ₹3
• Total cost = ₹10 + ₹3 = ₹13.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
₹10 + ₹3 1 M
Total ₹13 1 M
Q3 Change Calculation
Tina gives a ₹10 note to buy a packet of biscuits that costs ₹8. How much change does the shopkeeper give her back?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Money given = ₹10
• Cost of biscuits = ₹8
• Change = ₹10 - ₹8 = ₹2.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
₹10 - ₹8 = ₹2 2 M
Q4 Currency Symbol
What is the official currency symbol of India? Draw it.
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
The official currency symbol of India is ₹ (Indian Rupee). It was designed by Udaya Kumar Dharmalingam.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Symbol ₹ correctly drawn/named 1 M
Q5 Different Ways to Pay ₹10
Show two different ways to pay ₹10 using coins.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Way 1: One single ₹10 coin.
• Way 2: Two ₹5 coins (₹5 + ₹5 = ₹10).
• Way 3: Five ₹2 coins (2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = ₹10).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two valid methods provided 2 M
Q6 Mental Math • Notes
How many ₹20 notes make ₹100?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 = 100.
It takes five ₹20 notes to make ₹100 (5 × ₹20 = ₹100).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Five ₹20 notes 2 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Buying Kites for Makar Sankranti

A shopkeeper sells colourful kites for ₹6 each and rolls of string (charkhi) for ₹10 each. Aman buys one kite and one roll of string.

Analytical Task: How much total money must Aman pay to the shopkeeper? If he gives a ₹20 note, how much change will he receive?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total cost = ₹6 + ₹10 = ₹16.
2. Change from ₹20 = ₹20 - ₹16 = ₹4 (or two ₹2 coins).
Rubric: 1.5 M for total ₹16 + 1.5 M for change ₹4
