Chapter 8: Fun with Numbers

Numbers from 21 to 99: Tens, Ones, Place Value & Patterns | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Counting in groups of tens from 20 up to 99.
  • Understanding place value in 2-digit numbers: Tens place and Ones place.
  • Finding predecessor, successor, and ordering numbers up to 99.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (6 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Tens and Ones • Place Value
What is the number for: 5 Tens and 3 Ones? Write the numeral and number name.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 5 Tens = 50
• 3 Ones = 3
• Numeral = 53
• Number Name = Fifty-three.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Numeral 53 1 M
Number name Fifty-three 1 M
Q2 Counting by 10s • Skip Counting
Complete the tens chain: 10, 20, 30, __, __, 60, __, 80, __.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Counting by tens:
10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Filled in 40, 50, 70, 90 correctly 2 M
Q3 Before and After • Numbers up to 50
Write the number that comes:
(a) Just before 30
(b) Just after 49
(c) Between 39 and 41
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) Just before 30 is 29.
(b) Just after 49 is 50.
(c) Between 39 and 41 is 40.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
29 1 M
50 1 M
40 1 M
Q4 Number Comparison • Greater or Smaller
Compare using > or <: 64 __ 46. Which number is greater and why?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Comparison:
• In 64, the tens digit is 6 (6 tens = 60).
• In 46, the tens digit is 4 (4 tens = 40).
• Since 6 tens > 4 tens, 64 > 46.
64 is greater than 46.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sign: 64 > 46 1 M
Explanation using tens digit 1 M
Q5 Expansion • Decomposing Numbers
Expand the following numbers into tens and ones:
(a) 28
(b) 75
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) 28 = 20 + 8 (2 Tens + 8 Ones)
(b) 75 = 70 + 5 (7 Tens + 5 Ones)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 20 + 8 1 M
Part (b): 70 + 5 1 M
Q6 Riddle Time • Who Am I?
I have 9 in the ones place and 8 in the tens place. What number am I?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Tens place = 8
• Ones place = 9
• The number is 89 (Eighty-nine).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct number 89 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Garlands for the School Annual Function

Students are stringing marigold garlands. Each garland has 10 flowers. Team Alpha makes 4 full garlands and has 6 loose flowers left over.

Analytical Task: How many flowers does Team Alpha have in all? What is the digit in the tens place and the ones place?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. 4 garlands of 10 = 4 × 10 = 40 flowers.
2. 6 loose flowers = 6 ones.
3. Total flowers = 40 + 6 = 46 flowers.
Tens digit = 4, Ones digit = 6.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 46 total + 1.5 M for Tens:4, Ones:6
