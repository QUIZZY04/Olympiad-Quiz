Chapter 6: Vegetable Farm

Addition & Subtraction within 20, Double Ten-Frames & Word Problems | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Extending addition and subtraction to teen numbers (10 to 20).
  • Using double ten-frames to bridge 10 and add single digits.
  • Partitioning numbers: 14 = 10 + 4, 17 = 10 + 7.
  • Real-life harvest and market word problems with vegetables.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Carrot Harvest Addition
Rani pulls 9 carrots from the first garden patch and 4 carrots from the second patch. How many carrots did Rani pull altogether?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. First patch = 9 carrots
2. Second patch = 4 carrots
3. 9 + 4 = 9 + 1 + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13 carrots.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Making 10 strategy (9+1+3) 1 M
Answer: 13 carrots 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Pumpkin Subtraction
There were 15 pumpkins lying in the farm shed. 4 pumpkins were loaded onto a wooden cart. How many pumpkins remain in the shed?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Total pumpkins = 15
2. Loaded pumpkins = 4
3. Pumpkins remaining = 15 - 4 = 11 pumpkins.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
15 - 4 = 11 1 M
Answer with unit: 11 pumpkins 1 M
Q3 Let Us Do • Double Ten-Frame Counting
One ten-frame is completely full (10 dots), and the second ten-frame has 6 dots. What total number do they represent?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Full ten-frame = 10
• Second frame = 6
• Total = 10 + 6 = 16 (Sixteen).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
10 + 6 = 16 1 M
Number name Sixteen 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Vegetable Basket Distribution Case Study

Farmer Kalu harvests 12 orange carrots and 6 red radishes from his organic garden beds. He puts them into a large jute sack.

Analytical Task: How many total vegetables did Farmer Kalu harvest? If he sells 5 carrots at the morning market, how many carrots remain?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total vegetables harvested = 12 + 6 = 18 vegetables.
2. Carrots remaining = 12 - 5 = 7 carrots left.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 12 + 6 = 18 vegetables + 1.5 M for 12 - 5 = 7 carrots
