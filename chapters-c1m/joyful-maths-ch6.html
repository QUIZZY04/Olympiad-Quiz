Chapter 6: Vegetable Farm

Story Problems, Word Scenarios & Practical Arithmetic | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Solving contextual addition and subtraction problems from farm life.
  • Comparing quantities of different vegetables harvested.
  • Formulating mathematical questions based on visual pictures.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (6 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Farm Story • Addition
A farmer plucked 9 cucumbers from one plant and 8 cucumbers from another plant. How many cucumbers did he pluck altogether?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Cucumbers from 1st plant = 9
• Cucumbers from 2nd plant = 8
• Total cucumbers = 9 + 8 = 17 cucumbers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Number sentence 9 + 8 1 M
Correct sum: 17 cucumbers 1 M
Q2 Farm Story • Subtraction
There were 16 carrots in the vegetable basket. Bunnies ate 7 carrots. How many carrots were left in the basket?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Total carrots = 16
• Carrots eaten = 7
• Remaining carrots = 16 - 7 = 9 carrots.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Subtraction 16 - 7 1 M
Answer: 9 carrots 1 M
Q3 Comparison • More or Fewer
Radha picked 15 tomatoes and 10 potatoes. Which vegetable did she pick more of, and by how many?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Comparison:
• Tomatoes = 15
• Potatoes = 10
• 15 > 10, so she picked more tomatoes.
• Difference = 15 - 10 = 5 more tomatoes.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identified more tomatoes 1.5 M
Difference calculated as 5 1.5 M
Q4 Make a Math Problem
Look at this number sentence: 10 + 4 = 14. Make up your own farm story for this sentence.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sample Story:
'Farmer Hari planted 10 chili plants in the morning. His daughter planted 4 chili plants in the evening. Together they planted 14 chili plants.'
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Valid creative story context 1 M
Math aligns with 10 + 4 = 14 1 M
Q5 Two-Step Story • Real Life
A vegetable cart had 18 watermelons. 5 were sold in the morning and 3 were sold in the afternoon. How many watermelons were left on the cart?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Total watermelons sold = 5 + 3 = 8 watermelons.
2. Watermelons left = 18 - 8 = 10 watermelons.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Sum of sold: 5 + 3 = 8 1.5 M
Remaining: 18 - 8 = 10 1.5 M
Q6 Mental Arithmetic • Quick Facts
Quickly answer:
(a) 10 + 10 = __
(b) 20 - 5 = __
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) 10 + 10 = 20
(b) 20 - 5 = 15
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 20 1 M
Part (b): 15 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Harvest Day at Kaka's Farm

Kaka harvested 11 red pumpkins and 7 green cabbages in the morning. In the afternoon, he sold 6 pumpkins at the mandi.

Analytical Task: How many total vegetables did Kaka harvest in the morning? How many pumpkins are left with him?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Total vegetables harvested = 11 + 7 = 18 vegetables.
2. Pumpkins left = 11 - 6 = 5 pumpkins.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 11 + 7 = 18 + 1.5 M for 11 - 6 = 5
