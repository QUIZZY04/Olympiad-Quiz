Chapter 4: Subtraction

Take Away, Crossing Out, How Many Left & Subtraction Facts | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Understanding subtraction as 'Taking away' from a collection.
  • Crossing out pictures/dots to find the remaining count.
  • Using the minus sign (-) and equal to (=) signs.
  • Subtraction facts within 9 and subtracting zero.
  • Subtracting a number from itself (e.g. 5 - 5 = 0).

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Take Away Leaves
A twig has 6 leaves. Take away 2 leaves. How many leaves are left?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• 6 - 2 = 4 leaves left.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
6 - 2 = 4 2 M

Q21 Competency Review • Checking by Addition
How can you check if 8 - 3 = 5 is correct using addition?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Add the answer (5) to the number subtracted (3):
• 5 + 3 = 8 (the original number).
• This proves the subtraction is correct.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
5 + 3 = 8 1 M
Proves inverse operation 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Leaves on a Twig Case Study

A green twig had 6 tender leaves. A hungry caterpillar came and ate 2 leaves.

Analytical Task: How many leaves are left on the twig? If the caterpillar comes back and eats 4 more leaves, how many leaves remain?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Leaves left initially = 6 - 2 = 4 leaves left.
2. After eating 4 more: 4 - 4 = 0 leaves left.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 6 - 2 = 4 leaves + 1.5 M for 4 - 4 = 0 leaves
