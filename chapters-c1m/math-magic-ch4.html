Chapter 4: Subtraction

Take Away, How Many Left, Subtraction Facts up to 9 | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Concept of subtraction as 'taking away' or 'separating'.
  • Using the minus sign (-) and equal sign (=).
  • Finding differences and remaining items in everyday situations.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (6 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Subtraction Concept • Take Away
There were 5 cups on the dining table. 2 cups broke. How many cups are left?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Total cups = 5
• Cups broken = 2
• Cups left = 5 - 2 = 3 cups.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
5 - 2 = 3 cups 2 M
Q2 Subtracting All
A boy had 3 balloons. All 3 balloons burst with a pop. How many balloons are left with him?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 3 - 3 = 0 balloons.
When you take away all items from a group, zero items are left.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
3 - 3 = 0 1 M
Explanation of zero remainder 1 M

Q4 Missing Subtrahend
Fill in the blank: 7 - __ = 2.
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
7 - 5 = 2 (since 7 - 2 = 5).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
5 1 M
Q5 Word Problem • Frogs on a Rock
9 frogs were sitting on a river rock. 4 frogs jumped into the water. How many frogs are still sitting on the rock?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Frogs on rock = 9
• Frogs jumped in = 4
• Frogs remaining = 9 - 4 = 5 frogs.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
9 - 4 statement 1 M
Answer: 5 frogs 1 M
Q6 Comparison • Difference
Tina has 7 pencils and Meenu has 4 pencils. How many more pencils does Tina have than Meenu?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Tina = 7 pencils
• Meenu = 4 pencils
• Difference = 7 - 4 = 3 pencils.
Tina has 3 more pencils.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Difference 7 - 4 = 3 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Leaves on a Branch

A twig had 6 fresh green leaves. In the autumn breeze, 2 leaves fell off onto the garden grass.

Analytical Task: How many leaves remained attached to the twig? Write the subtraction fact.
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Leaves remaining = 6 - 2 = 4 leaves.
2. Subtraction fact: 6 - 2 = 4.
Rubric: 1 M for 4 leaves remaining + 1 M for fact 6 - 2 = 4
