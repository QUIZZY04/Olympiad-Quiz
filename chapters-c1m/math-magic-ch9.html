Chapter 9: Data Handling

Counting Shapes in Pictures, Letter Counts in Names & Frequency | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Counting distinct geometric shapes (triangles, circles, squares, rectangles) in a composite picture.
  • Identifying which shape occurs the most and which occurs the least.
  • Counting letters in names of friends.
  • Finding which letter repeats most frequently across names.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Joker's Geometric Costume Case Study

In the circus picture, the joker's funny costume is made entirely of shapes: 8 triangles for the hat and collar, 5 circles for buttons and eyes, 6 rectangles for arms and legs, and 4 squares for the body pockets.

Analytical Task: Which shape is used the most in the joker's costume? Which shape is used the least? How many shapes were used in all?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Triangles (8) are used the most.
2. Squares (4) are used the least.
3. Total shapes = 8 + 5 + 6 + 4 = 23 shapes in all.
Rubric: 1 M for Triangles (most) + 1 M for Squares (least) + 1 M for total 23 shapes
