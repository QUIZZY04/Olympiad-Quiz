Chapter 9: Data Handling

Counting Shapes, Picture Charts, Frequency & Name Length Analysis | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Counting different geometric shapes within a composite drawing.
  • Comparing which shape occurs the most and the least.
  • Counting letters in names and identifying most common letters.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (6 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Counting Shapes • Picture Reading
Look at the robot drawing. It has 4 circles for joints, 2 rectangles for legs, 1 square for head, and 1 rectangle for body. How many rectangles are there in total?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Legs = 2 rectangles
• Body = 1 rectangle
• Total rectangles = 2 + 1 = 3 rectangles.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Legs: 2, Body: 1 1 M
Total 3 rectangles 1 M
Q2 Letter Count in Names
Count the number of letters in each friend's name:
(a) RAM
(b) GEETA
(c) SUBHASH
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) RAM = 3 letters
(b) GEETA = 5 letters
(c) SUBHASH = 7 letters
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
RAM = 3 1 M
GEETA = 5 1 M
SUBHASH = 7 1 M
Q3 Data Comparison • Highest Frequency
In the names RAM, GEETA, and SUBHASH, which name has the highest number of letters?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
SUBHASH has the highest number of letters (7 letters).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Subhash identified 1 M
Q4 Finding Common Letters
How many times does the letter 'A' appear in the names RAM, GEETA, and SUBHASH combined?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Counting:
• RAM → 1 time
• GEETA → 1 time
• SUBHASH → 1 time
• Total = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3 times.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Occurrences shown 1 M
Total: 3 times 1 M
Q5 Shape Comparison
In a diagram, there are 9 circles and 5 triangles. How many more circles are there than triangles?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Circles = 9
• Triangles = 5
• Difference = 9 - 5 = 4 more circles.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
9 - 5 = 4 circles 2 M
Q6 Simple Tally Observation
Why do we make a table when counting many items in a room?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
We make a table so that we do not miss any item or count the same item twice. A table makes counting organized, clear, and easy to read.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Organization and avoiding mistakes explained 2 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Scenery Shape Count

Children drew a landscape featuring a house, mountains, and the sun. The drawing contains 12 Triangles (mountains and roof), 4 Circles (sun and flowers), 6 Squares (windows), and 3 Rectangles (doors).

Analytical Task: Which shape appears the most times in the drawing? Which shape appears the least times? How many total shapes were used?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Most: Triangles (12 times).
2. Least: Rectangles (3 times).
3. Total shapes: 12 + 4 + 6 + 3 = 25 shapes.
Rubric: 1 M for most + 1 M for least + 1 M for total 25
