Q1 Addition Concept • How Many Altogether
Two cows are grazing in the field. 1 more cow joins them. How many cows are there altogether?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 2 + 1 = 3 cows altogether.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
2 + 1 = 3 cows 1 M
Q2 Number Pairs • Make 6
Write three different addition pairs that equal 6.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Pair 1: 5 + 1 = 6
• Pair 2: 4 + 2 = 6
• Pair 3: 3 + 3 = 6 (also 6 + 0 = 6).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three valid pairs provided 3 M
Q3 Adding Zero
What is 4 + 0? Explain why adding zero does not change the number.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 4 + 0 = 4.
• Adding zero means adding nothing to the group, so the original quantity stays exactly the same.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
4 + 0 = 4 1 M
Explanation of adding nothing 1 M
Q4 Word Problem • Combining Marbles
Rohan has 4 blue marbles and 3 green marbles. How many marbles does Rohan have in total?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Blue marbles = 4
• Green marbles = 3
• Total marbles = 4 + 3 = 7 marbles.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
4 + 3 statement 1 M
Answer: 7 marbles 1 M

Q6 Visual Problem • Ants on a Log
There are 6 ants marching in a line. 2 more ants join the back of the line. How many ants are in line now?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Existing ants = 6
• Joined ants = 2
• Total ants = 6 + 2 = 8 ants.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
6 + 2 = 8 ants 2 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

One More Parrot Rhyme

Two green parrots flying in the sky, another little parrot joined them as they flew high. Then one more joined to sing and play.

Analytical Task: How many parrots were flying after the first one joined? How many parrots were flying when another one joined after that?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. 2 parrots + 1 parrot = 3 parrots.
2. 3 parrots + 1 more parrot = 4 parrots altogether.
Rubric: 1 M for 2+1=3 + 1 M for 3+1=4
