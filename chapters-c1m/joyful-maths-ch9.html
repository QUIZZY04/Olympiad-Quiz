Chapter 9: Utsav

Repeating & Growing Patterns, Toran Beads & Rangoli Shapes | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Identifying repeating units in decorative borders, torans, and fabrics.
  • Extending shape patterns: alternating colors, sizes, and rotations.
  • Growing patterns in numbers and blocks (e.g. 1, 2, 3... or 2, 4, 6...).
  • Patterns in nature: day and night, seasons, animal stripes.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Extending Shape Patterns
Look at the sequence: Circle, Square, Circle, Square, Circle, __. What shape comes next?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. The repeating rule is alternating between Circle and Square.
2. After Circle comes Square.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identified Square 1 M
Alternating rule explained 1 M

Q4 Textbook Exercise • Growing Pattern
Look at the dots: •, ••, •••, ••••, _____. Draw the next group of dots.
[2 Marks]

Q6 Textbook Exercise • Rangoli Tile Pattern
In a rangoli grid, Priya colors tiles: Yellow, Green, Yellow, Green. What color should she use for the 5th tile?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Odd positions (1st, 3rd, 5th) are Yellow.
• Even positions (2nd, 4th) are Green.
• The 5th tile will be Yellow.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Yellow color 1 M
Odd position reasoning 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Diwali Toran Decoration Case Study

During Diwali utsav, Priya makes a door toran using green mango leaves and yellow marigold flowers in the pattern: Leaf, Flower, Leaf, Flower, Leaf, Flower.

Analytical Task: What comes next in Priya's toran pattern after the flower? How many leaves will she need if her toran has 6 flowers?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. After the flower, the next item in the pattern is a green mango leaf.
2. Since the pattern pairs 1 leaf with 1 flower, for 6 flowers she will need 6 mango leaves.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 'Leaf' identification + 1.5 M for 6 leaves calculation
