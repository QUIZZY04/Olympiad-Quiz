Chapter 2: What is Long? What is Round?

Exploring 3D & 2D Shapes: Rolling, Sliding, and Sorting Objects | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Distinguishing between long objects and round objects.
  • Understanding rolling vs sliding based on flat and curved surfaces.
  • Sorting daily life objects by shape and building towers.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (17 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Long vs Round
Classify the following items into 'Long' and 'Round': Ruler, Cricket Ball, Flute (Bansuri), Orange, Carrot, Coin.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classification:
• Long Objects: Ruler, Flute (Bansuri), Carrot.
• Round Objects: Cricket Ball, Orange, Coin (circular face).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correctly classified long objects 1.5 M
Correctly classified round objects 1.5 M
Q2 Action Investigation • Rolling and Sliding
Will a football roll or slide on a smooth slide in the park? What about a wooden block?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• A football rolls because it has a curved round surface.
• A wooden block slides because it has flat surfaces.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Football rolls explained 1 M
Wooden block slides explained 1 M
Q3 Hands-on Math • Both Roll and Slide
Name two objects that can both roll and slide.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
1. A Coin: It rolls when pushed on its round edge, and it slides when pushed flat on its face.
2. A Water Bottle or Glass Tumbler: It rolls when kept on its curved side, and slides when standing on its flat bottom.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two objects identified 1 M
Explanation of how both happen 1 M
Q4 Let Us Do • Making Towers
Can you stack coins to make a tower? Why does a coin tower stand, but a marble tower cannot be made?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Yes, coins can be stacked into a tower because coins have flat top and bottom surfaces.
• Marbles cannot be stacked because they are completely round with only curved surfaces, so they roll off.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Coins stack due to flat faces 1.5 M
Marbles roll off due to curved surfaces 1.5 M
Q5 Riddle Time • Guess the Shape
'Round all around, has no corners, I can roll in your hand.' What am I?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
You are a Ball (or an Orange / Lemon / Marble / Ladoo).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct answer (Ball/Circle) 1 M
Q6 Creative Thinking • Drawing Shapes
Draw or describe an object that is long and pointed at one end.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
A Pencil or a Crayon or a Chili. It has a long cylindrical body with a sharp pointed tip at one end.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Object named and described 2 M
Q7 In-Text Activity • Blindfold Touch Game
In Uncle Golu's blindfold game, Kabir touches an object with no corners and a smooth curved body that rolls easily in his hands. What shape is it?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. An object with no corners and a curved body that rolls is round (spherical).
2. Examples: A tennis ball, cricket ball, orange, or marble.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identified as round / sphere 1 M
Valid real-life example 1 M
Q8 Let Us Do • Rolling vs Sliding
Test these four items on a wooden desk ramp: (a) A round tomato, (b) An eraser, (c) A glass marble, (d) A flat ruler. Which roll and which slide?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Roll: Tomato and Glass marble (because they have curved surfaces).
• Slide: Eraser and Flat ruler (because they have flat faces that glide along the surface).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tomato & marble roll 1 M
Eraser & ruler slide 1 M
Q9 Let Us Think • Both Rolls and Slides
Take a ₹5 coin. When does it roll, and when does it slide? Name another object that can do both.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Rolls: When placed upright on its thin curved edge and gently pushed, it rolls like a wheel.
• Slides: When placed flat on its face (Heads or Tails) and pushed, it slides.
• Another object: A carrom striker or a cylindrical water bottle.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rolls on curved edge 1 M
Slides on flat face 1 M
Valid second example 1 M
Q10 Hands-on Construction • Tower Making
Three children try to make towers: Tina uses wooden blocks, Priya uses matchboxes, and Aman uses oranges. Whose tower will fall down immediately, and why?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Aman's tower will fall down immediately.
2. Reason: Oranges are round with curved surfaces that roll off when stacked.
3. Wooden blocks and matchboxes have flat faces that rest securely on top of each other.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Aman's tower falls 1 M
Curved surface rolls off 1 M
Flat surfaces allow stable stacking 1 M
Q11 Picture Observation • Long vs Round
Look at the vegetable basket: Carrot, Onion, Cucumber, Lemon, Bitter gourd, Tomato. Classify them into 'Long' and 'Round'.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Long Vegetables: Carrot, Cucumber, Bitter gourd.
• Round Vegetables: Onion, Lemon, Tomato.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct long vegetables 1 M
Correct round vegetables 1 M
Q12 Let Us Do • Coin Spinning Trick
Spin a ₹2 coin like a top on a smooth table. What shape does the spinning coin look like while it spins fast?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• When a flat round coin spins rapidly on its edge, it looks like a round ball (sphere).
• Once it slows down and stops, it falls flat on the table.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Looks like a round ball / sphere 1 M
Observation of spinning motion 1 M
Q13 Shape Tracing • Matchbox vs Bangle
What 2D shape do you get if you trace the outline of: (a) A round plastic bangle, (b) The bottom face of a matchbox?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• (a) Tracing a round bangle gives a Circle.
• (b) Tracing the bottom face of a matchbox gives a Rectangle.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bangle = Circle 1 M
Matchbox = Rectangle 1 M
Q14 Creative Thinking • Pipe Stacking
Can you stack hollow cardboard pipes to make a tall standing tower? How must you place them so they don't roll?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. If pipes are placed on their curved sides, they will roll and scatter.
2. To stack them securely, stand them vertically on their flat circular circular ends, or place them in a pyramid row inside a tray.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Must stand on flat ends 1 M
Recognized curved side rolls 1 M
Q15 Summary Drill • Corners and Edges
How many straight edges does a post-it square have? Does a cricket ball have any straight edges?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• A square post-it note has 4 straight edges and 4 corners.
• A cricket ball has 0 straight edges (it has only 1 continuous curved surface).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Square: 4 straight edges 1 M
Ball: 0 straight edges 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Tower Building Challenge

During art class, Rohan tries to build a high tower using 5 round tennis balls, but the balls roll away and the tower falls down. Meena uses 5 matchboxes and successfully builds a tall tower.

Analytical Task: Why did Rohan's tower collapse while Meena's tower stood firmly? Which shape is best for stacking?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Rohan used round balls with curved surfaces which roll easily and have no flat faces to balance on.
2. Meena used matchboxes with flat faces that slide and stack stably without rolling. Flat-faced objects are best for stacking.
Rubric: 1.5 M for curved surfaces roll + 1.5 M for flat surfaces stack
