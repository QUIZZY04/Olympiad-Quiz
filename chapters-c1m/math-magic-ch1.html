Chapter 1: Shapes and Space

Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom, Rolling-Sliding & Shapes | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • The Arab and his Camel: understanding Inside and Outside.
  • Comparing sizes: Bigger vs Smaller, Biggest vs Smallest.
  • Relative positions: Top vs Bottom, Nearer vs Farther, On vs Under, Above vs Below.
  • Movement properties of 3D objects: Rolling vs Sliding.
  • Sorting shapes by colour, size, and type into boxes.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Story • The Arab and his Camel
In the desert winter, the Arab pitched his tent and went inside. The camel was outside in the cold. What did the camel ask first?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• The camel asked: 'May I put my neck inside? It is too cold outside.'
• The Arab allowed him to put his neck inside the tent.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Asked to put neck inside 1 M
Reason: too cold outside 1 M
Q2 Textbook Story • Who is Inside at the End?
At the end of 'The Arab and his Camel' story, who was finally inside the tent, and who was outside?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Inside: The Camel was inside the tent.
• Outside: The Arab was outside in the cold desert night because the tent was too small for both.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Camel is inside 1 M
Arab is outside 1 M

Q18 Textbook Story • The Lamb and the Dholak
How did the grandmother help the lamb escape from the hungry wolf?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Grandmother put the little lamb inside a hollow, round dholak.
2. She rolled the dholak down the slope.
3. Because the dholak was round, it rolled fast and took the lamb safely home.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Put lamb inside dholak 1 M
Rolled down hill safely 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Wise Grandmother's Rolling Dholak Case Study

A little lamb was going to visit his grandmother. In the forest, a hungry wolf stopped him to eat him. The lamb requested, 'Let me go to my grandmother, I will grow fat and then you can eat me.' On returning, grandmother hid the lamb inside a wooden dholak and rolled it down the grassy hill.

Analytical Task: Why did the dholak roll smoothly down the hill instead of sliding? Did the wolf catch the lamb?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The dholak has a curved cylindrical surface, which rolls easily down a slope.
2. The dholak rolled very fast past the waiting wolf, and the lamb reached home safely without getting caught.
Rubric: 1.5 M for curved surface rolls + 1.5 M for lamb reaches home safely
