Q1 Counting • Place Value Review
Look at 3 bundles of 10 pencils and 6 loose pencils. How many pencils are there altogether? Write in tens and ones.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 3 bundles = 3 Tens (30)
• 6 loose = 6 Ones (6)
• Total = 36 pencils (3 Tens + 6 Ones).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
36 pencils 1 M
3 Tens and 6 Ones 1 M
Q2 Addition Story Sum
In an orchard, there are 13 guava trees and 6 lemon trees. How many fruit trees are there in all?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Guava trees = 13
• Lemon trees = 6
• Total trees = 13 + 6 = 19 trees.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
13 + 6 statement 1 M
19 trees 1 M
Q3 Subtraction Story Sum
A vendor had 20 coconuts. He sold 8 coconuts by noon. How many coconuts were left with him?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Total coconuts = 20
• Coconuts sold = 8
• Coconuts left = 20 - 8 = 12 coconuts.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
20 - 8 statement 1 M
12 coconuts 1 M
Q4 Number Name to Numeral
Write the numeral for: Forty-seven.
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Forty-seven = 47.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
47 1 M
Q5 Comparison • Smallest to Largest
Arrange these numbers from smallest to largest: 34, 12, 45, 23.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
Smallest to largest (Ascending order):
12, 23, 34, 45.

Q6 Mental Math • Tens
What is 10 more than 30? What is 10 less than 30?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 10 more than 30 is 40 (30 + 10 = 40).
• 10 less than 30 is 20 (30 - 10 = 20).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
40 1 M
20 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

The School Garden Harvest Festival

Grade 1 children gathered 24 radishes, 18 carrots, and 10 turnip roots from the class organic patch.

Analytical Task: Write 24 in tens and ones. Which vegetable was harvested the most, and how many carrots and radishes were there altogether?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. 24 = 2 Tens and 4 Ones.
2. Radishes (24) were harvested the most.
3. Total carrots and radishes = 18 + 24 = 42 vegetables.
Rubric: 1 M for 2 tens 4 ones + 1 M for radishes most + 1 M for sum 42
