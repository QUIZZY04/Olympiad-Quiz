Chapter 7: Lina's Family

Non-Standard Lengths, Handspans, Footsteps, Heavy vs Light | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Measuring length using non-standard body units: handspan, fingers, and footsteps (paces).
  • Understanding why non-standard units vary between children and adults.
  • Comparing lengths: longer vs shorter, longest vs shortest.
  • Comparing heights: taller vs shorter, tallest vs shortest.
  • Comparing weights qualitatively: heavier vs lighter.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Measuring with Handspan
What is a 'handspan'? Measure the length of your mathematics textbook using your handspan. How many handspans long is it?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. A handspan is the distance from the tip of the thumb to the tip of the little finger when the hand is stretched wide open.
2. When measured by a Class 1 student, the Joyful Mathematics book is approximately 2 to 3 handspans long.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Definition of handspan 1 M
Measurement: ~2 to 3 handspans 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Longer and Shorter Pencils
Look at two pencils: Pencil A has not been sharpened, and Pencil B is a small stub after many days of use. Which pencil is longer?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Pencil A is longer (new, unsharpened pencil).
• Pencil B is shorter (used stub).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pencil A is longer 1 M
Reason based on sharpening 1 M
Q3 Let Us Do • Measuring Classroom with Footsteps
Walk from the classroom door to the teacher's blackboard counting your footsteps (paces). If it takes you 15 footsteps and your teacher takes 10 footsteps, explain why.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. The distance between the door and board is the same.
2. The teacher is an adult with longer legs, so her footsteps are bigger (takes 10 steps).
3. A student's steps are shorter, requiring more footsteps (15 steps).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Teacher steps are longer 1 M
Student needs more steps 1 M

Q15 Let Us Do • Comparing Rope Lengths
Rope Red is 8 footsteps long. Rope Blue is 11 footsteps long. Which rope is longer and by how many footsteps?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• 11 is greater than 8, so Rope Blue is longer.
• Difference = 11 - 8 = 3 footsteps longer.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rope Blue is longer 1 M

Q16 Competency Review • Standard vs Non-Standard
Why do we need a standard ruler to measure cloth in a shop instead of using the shopkeeper's handspan?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Every person's hand has a different size.
2. A standard wooden or plastic ruler is fixed and equal everywhere, ensuring fair and accurate measurement.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Handspans differ per person 1 M
Ruler is fixed and equal 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Measuring the Study Table Case Study

Lina measures the length of her dining table using her handspan. She finds it is 10 handspans long. Her father measures the same table and finds it is only 6 handspans long.

Analytical Task: Why did Lina get 10 handspans while her father got only 6 handspans? Whose handspan is bigger?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Father's handspan is bigger because he is an adult.
2. Because father's handspan is bigger, fewer handspans (6) are needed to cover the same table.
3. Lina's hand is smaller, so she needed more handspans (10).
Rubric: 1.5 M for father's handspan is bigger + 1.5 M for fewer spans needed
