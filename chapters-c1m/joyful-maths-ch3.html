Chapter 3: Mango Treat

Counting 1 to 9, Number Names, One-to-One Matching & Comparison | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Counting physical objects from 1 to 9 with one-to-one correspondence.
  • Writing numerals and matching with number names: One to Nine.
  • Comparing sets: More than, Less than, and Equal to.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (18 Questions)

Count the objects and write the number and number name: 🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎 (5 apples).
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Count: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Q2 Comparison • More or Less
Set A has 6 butterflies. Set B has 4 flowers. Which set has more objects? Can every butterfly sit on a flower?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Set A = 6 butterflies
• Set B = 4 flowers
• 6 is more than 4 (6 > 4). Set A has more objects.
• No, only 4 butterflies will get a flower; 2 butterflies will be left without a flower.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Set A has more (6 > 4) 1.5 M
Explanation of 2 left without flower 1.5 M
Q3 Number Sequence • Missing Numbers
Fill in the missing numbers in the train: 1, 2, __, 4, __, 6, 7, __, 9.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
The missing numbers are 3, 5, and 8.
Complete sequence: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
3, 5, 8 identified correctly 3 M
Q4 Concept of Zero • Empty Plate
There were 3 laddoos on a plate. Chintu ate all 3 laddoos. How many laddoos are left on the plate?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Starting laddoos = 3
• Laddoos eaten = 3
• Remaining laddoos = 3 - 3 = 0 (Zero).
There are no laddoos left.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Calculation 3 - 3 = 0 1 M
Concept of Zero explained 1 M

Q6 Mental Math • Make 5
Show two different ways to make the number 5 using two groups.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Way 1: 2 + 3 = 5 (2 red dots and 3 blue dots)
• Way 2: 1 + 4 = 5 (1 green leaf and 4 yellow leaves)
• Way 3: 0 + 5 = 5.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two valid combinations given 2 M
Q7 In-Text Activity • As Many As
There are 5 hungry kittens and 5 bowls of warm milk. Is there a bowl for every kitten? Explain using 'As many as'.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Number of kittens = 5.
2. Number of milk bowls = 5.
3. Since 5 = 5, there are as many bowls as there are kittens. Every kitten gets exactly 1 bowl.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Recognized 5 = 5 matching 1 M
Used 'as many as' correctly 1 M

Q9 Let Us Think • More or Less
Group A has 7 ripe red apples. Group B has 4 ripe red apples. Tick the group that has more apples. How many more?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Group A has 7 apples and Group B has 4 apples.
• 7 is greater than 4, so Group A has more apples.
• Difference = 7 - 4 = 3 more apples.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Group A has more 1 M
3 more apples calculated 1 M
Q10 Dot Card Pairing • Missing Dots
A domino tile has 4 dots on the left half and 2 dots on the right half. How many dots are on the tile in all?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Left dots = 4
• Right dots = 2
• Total dots = 4 + 2 = 6 dots.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Addition sentence 4 + 2 1 M
Correct answer (6 dots) 1 M
Q11 Visual Drill • Making Groups of Given Number
Draw 9 mangoes. Circle a group of 6 mangoes. How many mangoes remain uncircled?

Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Total mangoes = 9.
2. Circled group = 6.
3. Uncircled mangoes = 9 - 6 = 3 mangoes.

Correct grouping of 6 1 M
Remaining 3 calculated 1 M

Q13 Concept of Zero • Empty Basket
There were 3 sweet mangoes in a cane basket. Rahul ate 1, Simran ate 1, and Grandpa ate 1. How many mangoes are left in the basket?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Initial mangoes = 3.
2. Mangoes eaten = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3.
3. Mangoes left = 3 - 3 = 0 (Zero).
4. Zero means nothing is left in the basket.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Calculation 3 - 3 = 0 1 M
Zero explained as 'none left' 1 M

Q16 Word Problem • Birds on a Branch
Four little sparrows are sitting on a tree branch. Two more sparrows fly in and sit with them. How many sparrows are on the branch now?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working

Q17 Count and Match • Fingers and Dots
A hand shows 5 outstretched fingers. How many more fingers must you raise from the other hand to show 8 fingers in total?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
1. Starting fingers = 5.
2. Target total = 8.
3. Count forward from 5: 6 (one), 7 (two), 8 (three).
4. You must raise 3 more fingers.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Counting forward logic 1 M
Answer: 3 fingers 1 M
Q18 Competency Check • Comparing Collections
Neeraj has 6 crayons and Tarun has 8 crayons. Who has fewer crayons? How many more does he need to have equal crayons?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• Comparing: 6 is less than 8, so Neeraj has fewer crayons.
• To be equal: 8 - 6 = 2 more crayons needed by Neeraj.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Neeraj has fewer 1 M
Needs 2 more crayons 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Grandmother's Mango Basket

Dadi brings a basket of ripe mangoes from the orchard. Ankit counts 7 mangoes in the basket, while Simran counts 5 mangoes in her bag.

Analytical Task: Who has more mangoes? How many more mangoes are there in the basket than in the bag?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Ankit has more mangoes (7 > 5).
2. 7 - 5 = 2 more mangoes in Dadi's basket.
Rubric: 1.5 M for Ankit / 7 > 5 + 1.5 M for 2 more mangoes
