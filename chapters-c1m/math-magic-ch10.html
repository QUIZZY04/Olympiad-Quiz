Chapter 10: Patterns

Extending Sequences, T-Shapes, Arrow Patterns & Number Sequences | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Extending sequences of basic 2D shapes (circles, squares, triangles).
  • Alternating letter and symbol patterns (T, ⊥, T, ⊥).
  • Arrow direction patterns (Up, Down, Up, Down).
  • Number patterns: counting in 1s, 2s, 5s, 10s.
  • Patterns with dots, leaf prints, and growing geometric shapes.

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Iron Grill Window Pattern Case Study

A blacksmith creates an ornamental window grill using alternating upright hearts and inverted hearts: ♡, ♢, ♡, ♢, ♡, ♢.

Analytical Task: What symbol comes next in the grill sequence? If the window frame has 10 upright hearts, how many total symbols are in the row?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Following the alternation, the next symbol is an upright heart (♡).
2. Since upright and inverted hearts alternate equally, 10 upright + 10 inverted = 20 total symbols.
Rubric: 1.5 M for upright heart + 1.5 M for 20 total symbols
