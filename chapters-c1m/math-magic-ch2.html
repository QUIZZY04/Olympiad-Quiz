Chapter 2: Numbers from One to Nine

Counting 1 to 9, As Many As, More or Less, Make Groups & Zero | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • One-to-one matching: 'As many as'.
  • Counting physical objects from 1 to 9.
  • Comparing collections: More vs Less.
  • Counting and writing numerals and number names.
  • Grouping objects into given numbers and concept of Zero.

Q1 Textbook Exercise • As Many As Cats and Mice
In the textbook illustration: 5 cats are paired with 5 mice by drawing connecting lines. Is every cat matched to a mouse?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Yes, there are 5 cats and 5 mice.
• Since 5 = 5, there are as many mice as cats.

Q21 Textbook Exercise • Concept of Zero Flower Pot
There are 3 pretty flowers in a flower pot. A child plucks 1 flower, then another, then the last one. How many flowers are left in the pot?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Total flowers = 3
2. Flowers plucked = 3
3. Flowers remaining = 3 - 3 = 0 (Zero).
4. Zero means no flowers are left in the pot.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
3 - 3 = 0 1 M
Concept of zero explained 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Playground Matchsticks Counting Case Study

Five children are playing in the school garden. Teacher gives 1 biscuit to each child. Then she counts the remaining biscuits in the packet: there are 3 biscuits left.

Analytical Task: How many biscuits were given to the 5 children? How many total biscuits were in the packet originally?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. 5 children each got 1 biscuit, so 5 biscuits were given out.
2. Total biscuits originally = 5 given + 3 left = 8 biscuits.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 5 biscuits given + 1.5 M for 5 + 3 = 8 total biscuits
