Q1 Addition • Counting On
Solve: 8 + 5 using the 'count on' method.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Start at 8.
• Count on 5 numbers: 9, 10, 11, 12, 13.
• 8 + 5 = 13.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Showing counting on sequence 1 M
Correct sum: 13 1 M
Q2 Subtraction • Taking Away
There were 14 apples in a fruit crate. Ravi took 6 apples for his friends. How many apples are left in the crate?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Total apples = 14
• Apples taken = 6
• Apples left = 14 - 6 = 8 apples.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct subtraction formula 14 - 6 1 M
Final answer with unit (8 apples) 1 M
Q3 Number Pairs • Addition Sentences
Find the missing number in each sentence:
(a) 9 + __ = 15
(b) 12 - __ = 7
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) 9 + 6 = 15 (since 15 - 9 = 6)
(b) 12 - 5 = 7 (since 12 - 7 = 5)
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Part (a): 6 1 M
Part (b): 5 1 M
Q4 Word Problem • Combining Groups
Pooja has 7 crayons. Her mother gave her 7 more crayons on her birthday. How many crayons does Pooja have now?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Calculation:
• Existing crayons = 7
• New crayons = 7
• Total crayons = 7 + 7 = 14 crayons (Double fact: 7 + 7 = 14).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Addition 7 + 7 1 M
Result: 14 crayons 1 M
Q5 Mental Math • Subtracting Zero
What is 18 - 0? What happens when you subtract zero from any number?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 18 - 0 = 18.
• When you subtract zero from any number, the number remains unchanged because zero means taking away nothing.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Result 18 1 M
Rule explanation 1 M
Q6 Let Us Do • Subtraction on Number Line
Use the number line to calculate 16 - 4. Draw or explain the hops.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Explanation:
• Start at 16 on the number line.
• Take 4 hops backward: 15, 14, 13, 12.
• You land on 12. Therefore, 16 - 4 = 12.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Backward hops shown 1 M
Answer 12 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Birdwatching at the Lake

Sonu saw 9 ducks swimming in the pond. 4 more ducks flew in and joined them. Later, 3 ducks walked out to rest on the bank.

Analytical Task: How many ducks were in the pond after 4 joined? How many ducks remained swimming when 3 walked out?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Ducks after 4 joined: 9 + 4 = 13 ducks.
2. Ducks remaining swimming: 13 - 3 = 10 ducks.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 9 + 4 = 13 + 1.5 M for 13 - 3 = 10
