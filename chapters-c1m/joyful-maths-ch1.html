Chapter 1: Finding the Furry Cat

Pre-Number Spatial Concepts: Inside/Outside, Top/Bottom, Nearer/Farther, On/Under | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Identifying spatial positions: inside and outside, on and under, top and bottom.
  • Comparing distances: nearer to and farther from a reference object.
  • Developing spatial vocabulary through visual observation and classroom games.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (6 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Activity • Inside or Outside
In the picture on Page 4, the furry cat is sleeping in a basket. Is the cat inside the basket or outside the basket?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
The furry cat is inside the basket because its entire body is resting within the borders of the basket.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct position identified as 'inside' 1 M
Explanation using boundary 1 M
Q2 Concept Check • Top and Bottom
Look at the big mango tree. A red bird is sitting on the highest branch and a puppy is playing on the grass below. Who is at the top and who is at the bottom?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Answer:
• The red bird is at the top (highest branch of the tree).
• The puppy is at the bottom (on the grass under the tree).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bird at the top 1 M
Puppy at the bottom 1 M
Q3 Spatial Comparison • Nearer and Farther
Two children, Rohan and Priya, are walking to school. Rohan is 5 steps away from the school gate, while Priya is 25 steps away. Who is nearer to the school gate and who is farther from it?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Comparison:
• Rohan is 5 steps away, so Rohan is nearer to the school gate.
• Priya is 25 steps away, so Priya is farther from the school gate.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Rohan identified as nearer 1.5 M
Priya identified as farther 1.5 M
Q4 Observation • On and Under
A book is placed on the table, and a ball is kept under the table. What will happen if you drop an eraser from above the table? Where does it fall?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
The eraser starts from above the table. If dropped, it will land on the table or bounce onto the floor under the table.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identifying 'above' and 'on/under' table 2 M
Q5 Think & Discuss • Bigger or Smaller
Draw or name two fruits found in your kitchen: one that is bigger and one that is smaller.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Bigger fruit: Watermelon (तरबूज) or Papaya (पपीता).
• Smaller fruit: Lemon (नींबू) or Grape (अंगूर).
Watermelon is much bigger in size than a lemon.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct example of bigger fruit 1 M
Correct example of smaller fruit 1 M
Q6 Everyday Math • Above and Below
A ceiling fan is spinning above the bed, and a pair of slippers is kept below the bed. Which object is above and which is below?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• The ceiling fan is above the bed.
• The slippers are below (under) the bed.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Fan identified as above 1 M
Slippers identified as below 1 M
🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Playground Spatial Clues

Lina and Kabir are playing hide and seek in the school garden. Lina spots her kitten sitting under a wooden bench, Kabir is standing on top of the slide, and a squirrel is inside a hollow log.

Analytical Task: Which animal is underneath a playground object? Where is Kabir located relative to the slide?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. The kitten is underneath the playground object (sitting under the wooden bench).
2. Kabir is at the top of the slide (on top).
Rubric: 1 M for kitten under bench + 1 M for Kabir on top of slide
