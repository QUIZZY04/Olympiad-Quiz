Chapter 1: Finding the Furry Cat

Pre-Number Concepts, Spatial Vocabulary & Orientation | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Inside and Outside: recognizing objects within boundaries vs outside.
  • Top and Bottom: identifying highest and lowest positions.
  • Nearer and Farther: comparing relative distances from a reference point.
  • On and Under, Above and Below: positional relationships.
  • Bigger and Smaller: comparing physical sizes visually.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Textbook Exercise • Inside & Outside
Look at the picture in the book: The furry cat is sitting in a cardboard basket. Where is the cat — inside the basket or outside?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. The cat's entire body is enclosed within the four walls of the basket.
2. Therefore, the cat is inside the basket.
3. If the cat hops out onto the carpet, it will be outside the basket.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Identified 'inside' 1 M
Clear spatial explanation 1 M
Q2 Textbook Exercise • Top & Bottom
A little bird sits on the highest branch of a guava tree, while a snail is on the grass at the foot of the tree. Tick the animal at the top.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Top: The bird is at the top of the tree because it is perched on the uppermost branch.
• Bottom: The snail is at the bottom of the tree on the ground.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bird is at the top 1 M
Snail is at the bottom 1 M
Q3 Let Us Do • Nearer & Farther
Two mice are running toward a hole in the kitchen wall. Mouse Chintu is 2 steps away from the hole. Mouse Pintu is 8 steps away. Who is nearer to the hole?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Compare the distances: 2 steps is less than 8 steps.
2. Nearer: Mouse Chintu is nearer to the hole.
3. Farther: Mouse Pintu is farther from the hole.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Chintu is nearer 1 M
Distance comparison stated 1 M
Q4 Let Us Do • Bigger & Smaller
Tick (✓) the bigger animal between an elephant and a goat. Cross (✗) the smaller animal.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• Elephant: Tick (✓) — the elephant has a huge body, so it is bigger.
• Goat: Cross (✗) — compared to the elephant, the goat is smaller.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Elephant ticked as bigger 1 M
Goat crossed as smaller 1 M
Q5 In-Text Prompt • On & Under
A water bottle is kept on the study table. A red football is lying on the floor under the table. Describe their positions using 'on' and 'under'.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
• The water bottle is on the table (resting on the upper flat surface).
• The red football is under the table (resting on the floor beneath the table).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Bottle is on the table 1 M
Football is under the table 1 M
Q6 Let Us Think • Above & Below

✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:

• Below: The rug is on the floor below the bed level.

Q7 Let Us Do • Footprints in Sand
Look at two footprints left on the wet river sand: one is a tiny sparrow claw mark and the other is a large camel hoof print. Which print is bigger?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Large animals have broad feet to walk on sand.
2. Therefore, the camel's hoof print is bigger.
3. The sparrow's claw mark is smaller.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Camel print is bigger 1 M
Sparrow print is smaller 1 M

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Hiding in the Playroom Case Study

Aarav is playing hide-and-seek with his furry kitten, Mitu. Aarav looks under the bed, inside the toy trunk, and on top of the study bookshelf.

Analytical Task: If Mitu is found inside the trunk, what spatial position does this represent? Where would Aarav look if Mitu were sleeping on a flat elevated surface?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. Finding Mitu inside the trunk represents the 'Inside' position (enclosed by container walls).
2. For a flat elevated surface, Aarav should look 'On' the study bookshelf or on top of the bed.
Rubric: 1.5 M for 'Inside' concept + 1.5 M for 'On' concept
