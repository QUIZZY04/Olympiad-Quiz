Q1 Tens and Ones • Identification
What number is formed by 4 Tens and 5 Ones?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• 4 Tens = 40
• 5 Ones = 5
• 40 + 5 = 45 (Forty-five).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
40 + 5 1 M
45 1 M
Q2 Missing Numbers • 21 to 30
Fill in the missing numbers: 21, 22, __, 24, 25, __, 27, 28, __, 30.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
The missing numbers are 23, 26, and 29.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
23, 26, 29 identified 3 M
Q3 Number Names • 30 to 50
Write the number name for:
(a) 32
(b) 48
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
(a) 32 = Thirty-two
(b) 48 = Forty-eight
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Thirty-two 1 M
Forty-eight 1 M
Q4 Place Value Table
In the number 42, which digit is in the tens place and which is in the ones place?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Tens place digit = 4 (value = 40)
• Ones place digit = 2 (value = 2).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Tens = 4 1 M
Ones = 2 1 M
Q5 Before and After • 21 to 50
Write the number just before 40 and the number just after 40.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
• Number just before 40 is 39.
• Number just after 40 is 41.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Before: 39 1 M
After: 41 1 M
Q6 Mental Arithmetic • Group of 10
How many tens are there in the number 50?
[1 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Answer:
There are 5 tens in 50 (5 × 10 = 50).

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Counting Matchstick Bundles

Deepak has 3 bundles of 10 matchsticks and 7 loose matchsticks. His friend gives him 3 more loose matchsticks.

Analytical Task: How many total matchsticks did Deepak have at first? How many bundles can he make after receiving 3 more loose matchsticks?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
1. At first: 3 tens + 7 ones = 37 matchsticks.
2. 7 loose + 3 loose = 10 loose matchsticks, which makes 1 new bundle.
3 + 1 = 4 full bundles of 10 (40 matchsticks).
Rubric: 1.5 M for 37 matchsticks + 1.5 M for 4 bundles (40)
