📋 How to Start the Live Quiz

  • 1. Unlock the Test: Ensure you have registered and unlocked the test in the Arena. The status will change to "Access Granted".
  • 2. Wait for the Timer: Watch the countdown timer on the card. When the timer reaches exactly 00:00:00, the system will verify the start time.
  • 3. Click 'Join Quiz': Once the timer hits zero, the bottom button will turn into a vibrant Green "Join Quiz" button. Click this to immediately enter the exam interface.
  • 4. Time Limit: Your individual timer starts as soon as you enter the quiz. It cannot be paused.
  • 5. Single Attempt: Only 1 attempt is allowed. Do not refresh your browser during the test or you may lose your progress.
  • 6. Auto-Submit: If the timer runs out while you are testing, your saved answers will be submitted automatically.

❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

The timer reached 00:00:00 but the button didn't turn green. What should I do?

Sometimes, local network delays can cause a slight lag. Wait a few seconds. If it still doesn't change, try refreshing the Live Arena page manually.

I unlocked the test, but the status still says "Locked".

Wait a few seconds on the dashboard for your payment verification to sync securely with the database, then refresh the page.

Can I join the quiz late?

Yes, you can join after the start time, as long as the session has not officially "Ended". However, your time may be reduced depending on the strict session end time.
