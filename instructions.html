❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

I paid but the button still says "Unlock Test". What do I do?

Go back to the Dashboard and wait for 2-3 seconds. The system needs a moment to update your status from the database.

Can I take the quiz on my phone?

Yes, the portal is fully mobile-responsive. Ensure you have a stable internet connection.

What happens if my internet disconnects?

Refresh the page immediately. The timer will keep running, but you can resume from where you left off.
