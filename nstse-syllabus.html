Preparation Strategy & Recommended Books

  • Understand the Exam Pattern: Familiarize yourself with the marking scheme and syllabus layout.

  • Take Timed Mock Tests: Accuracy under pressure is key. Use CBT interfaces to simulate the real exam environment.

Recommended Books:

  • NCERT / School prescribed textbooks for core concepts.
  • Brain Mapping Academy (BMA) prep books for NSTSE.
  • Reasoning & Aptitude books for critical thinking.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is there negative marking in these Olympiads?

Generally, preliminary levels of Olympiads like NSTSE do not have negative marking. However, always verify with the official guidelines for specific advanced levels.

What is the Critical Thinking Section?

The Critical Thinking Section contains Logical Reasoning and problem-solving questions. These are designed to test your core analytical skills and act as a differentiator.
