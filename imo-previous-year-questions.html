Class 6 IMO Prep Guide

IMO Previous Year Questions: Section-Wise Preparation Plan

The SOF International Mathematics Olympiad (IMO) tests four key areas across Classes 1 to 10: Logical Reasoning, Mathematical Reasoning, Everyday Mathematics, and the 3-mark Achievers Section.

Solving past IMO papers helps students build familiarity with word-problem phrasing, number-system tricks, fraction/ratio puzzles, and multi-step geometry questions that go beyond routine textbook exercises.

After solving each PYQ set, note down recurring shortcuts and re-attempt missed Achievers questions after 3–4 days to verify true mastery.

  • Focus Areas by Grade: Classes 1–4 emphasize number sense, shapes, time, and money; Classes 5–8 emphasize fractions, ratios, mensuration, and algebra; Classes 9–10 focus on polynomials, geometry, trigonometry, and probability.
  • Achievers Section Strategy: Each Achievers question carries 3 marks—verify edge cases carefully and eliminate impossible options before committing.
