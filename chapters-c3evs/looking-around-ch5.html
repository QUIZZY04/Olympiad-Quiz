Chapter 5: Chhotu's House

Turning a Shelter into a Home: Uninvited Animal Guests, House Cleanliness, and Decoration | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • The difference between a physical shelter (roof/walls) and a home (warmth, family love, comfort).
  • How Chhotu repurposed a large cement drainage pipe into a comfortable home with separate living, cooking, and sleeping zones.
  • Uninvited animal guests in our homes: Lizards, spiders, mosquitoes, flies, ants, and mice.
  • Keeping homes clean: Sweeping, mopping, waste disposal, and festive decorations (rangoli, torans).

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (16 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Shelter Adaptation
How did Chhotu divide the cement pipe into different spaces for his daily living?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Chhotu smartly divided the pipe and the area around it into functional zones:
1. Inside the pipe was his sleeping space shielded from rain and heat.
2. Outside behind the pipe was his cooking space with a small clay stove.
3. An area next to it was for drying clothes on a rope.
4. A corner was set aside for storing drinking water in an earthen pitcher.
5. The front open space was for welcoming guests and chatting with his friend Monu.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Detailed division into sleeping, cooking, water storage, and guest spaces 3 Marks
Q2 Uninvited Guests in Homes
Name four uninvited animals that live in our homes and explain where they are usually found.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Lizards (Geckos): Crawling on walls and ceilings near electric lights to hunt flies.
2. Spiders: Weaving webs in dusty corners and behind heavy cupboards.
3. Ants: Marching in straight lines toward sugary crumbs dropped in the kitchen.
4. Mice (Rats): Hiding in dark basements, store rooms, or kitchen drains.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Naming four uninvited creatures 1.5 Marks
Accurate micro-locations within the house 1.5 Marks
Q3 Cleanliness Habits
Why is daily sweeping and wet mopping necessary for household health?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Daily sweeping removes dust and food crumbs that attract cockroaches and mice, while mopping with disinfectant cleans floor bacteria and prevents the breeding of germs that cause infectious illnesses.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pest prevention by removing food crumbs 1 Mark
Bacterial disinfection for family health 1 Mark
Q4 Shelter Adaptation • Chhotu's House
When Chhotu came to Mumbai, he had no house. He saw a large cement pipe lying on the road.
(a) How did Chhotu use the cement pipe as his home?
(b) How did he divide the pipe and surrounding area into different parts for living?
[4 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Chhotu's Shelter:
(a) Pipe as Shelter:
• Chhotu used the large cement pipe as a safe, dry home that protected him from rain, scorching heat, and cold winds without having to pay rent.

(b) Functional Zones:
• Inside the pipe: Sleeping area.
• Near the pipe entrance: Cooking area.
• On a string outside: Drying clothes.
• In a bucket outside: Water storage.
• Open space: Sitting area for chatting with friend Monu.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Protection from weather without rent 1.5 M
Zoning of pipe into sleeping, cooking, drying areas 2.5 M
Q5 Uninvited Guests • Household Pests
In the poem 'Home Sweet Home', which creatures are called 'uninvited guests' that live in our houses without our permission?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Uninvited Guests in Houses:
• Lizards (crawling on walls)
• Mice / Rats (running races in cupboards)
• Mosquitoes and Flies (buzzing around)
• Cockroaches (creeping out from drains at night).
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
At least three accurate household pests named 3.0 M
Q6 Home Cleanliness • Sanitation Rules
Why is it important to sweep floors and empty dustbins daily in a house?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Sanitation Rationale:
• Leftover food crumbs, dust, and trash attract mice, cockroaches, and flies, which carry harmful bacteria that contaminate food and cause illnesses. Daily sweeping and mopping keep the house healthy and pleasant.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Pest attraction and bacterial contamination explained 1.5 M
Hygienic living environment emphasized 1.5 M
Q7 Decorating the House • Festive Rangoli
How do families decorate their houses on special occasions and festivals? Give two examples.
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
House Decoration:
1. Drawing colorful rangolis with flower petals and rice powder on the front porch.
2. Hanging fresh mango leaf torans (bandanwar) and lighting clay diyas along balconies.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two authentic Indian house decoration methods described 2.0 M
Q8 Observational Field Study • Nature Journal
In 'Chhotu's House', describe how students can observe this environmental phenomenon in their own neighborhood. What three things should they note down in their field notebook?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Neighborhood Field Observation:
1. Direct Observation: Students can observe the living ecosystem and community activities related to Chhotu's House during a morning nature walk.
2. Three Field Log Entries:
• (a) Physical appearance, color, and behavior of the species or object.
• (b) Interactions with surroundings (e.g., sources of water, food, shade, or shelter).
• (c) Human impact or community care practices observed.
3. Conclusion: Recording firsthand observations builds sharp environmental awareness and respect for biodiversity.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Observation methodology clearly described 1.5 Marks
Three concrete notebook parameters listed 1.5 Marks
Q9 Scientific Classification • Sorting Activity
Based on the concepts in 'Chhotu's House', classify the following into two distinct categories and explain the reason for your classification:
(Item A, Item B, Item C, Item D).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classification & Reasoning:
• Category 1 (Natural / Sustainable): Elements directly provided by nature that replenish naturally and cause zero pollution.
• Category 2 (Man-Made / Processed): Products manufactured by human tools that require energy and produce waste.
• Reason: Understanding the difference helps us reduce our ecological footprint and practice green habits.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct grouping into two categories 1.5 Marks
Scientific explanation for classification 1.5 Marks
Q10 Discussion & Value Reflection • Care & Compassion
How can young children practice kindness and empathy towards living beings or community helpers, as taught in 'Chhotu's House'? Give two practical daily examples.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Practicing Everyday Kindness:
1. Daily Habit 1: Keep a shallow earthen bowl of clean fresh water on the balcony or under a tree for thirsty birds and small animals during warm afternoons.
2. Daily Habit 2: Speak respectfully to community sanitation workers and delivery persons, offering them clean drinking water and never littering on roads.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two practical, student-led empathy habits 2.0 Marks
Alignment with chapter moral values 1.0 Mark
Q11 Environmental Riddle • Who Am I?
Environmental Riddle from 'Chhotu's House': I have no color of my own, yet I make the world green. You drink me when thirsty, and clouds carry me across mountains. Who am I? Mention two ways we can save me from wastage.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solving the Nature Riddle:
• Answer: WATER (जल / पानी).
• Two Ways to Save Water:
1. Turn off the running tap while brushing teeth or soaping hands (saves up to 6 liters per minute).
2. Reuse kitchen vegetable-wash water to water garden pots and indoor plants.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct identity (Water) 1.0 Mark
Two practical conservation methods 2.0 Marks
Q12 Hands-on Project & Craft • Eco-Friendly Living
Describe a simple hands-on craft or reuse activity related to 'Chhotu's House' that can be done using discarded household materials. What are the steps?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Eco-Craft Project: Upcycled Planter / Feeder:
• Materials Needed: Discarded clean coconut shell or plastic bottle, jute string, potting soil, vegetable seeds.
• Steps:
1. With adult help, make 2 small holes on opposite sides for hanging.
2. Fill with garden soil mixed with dry compost leaves.
3. Plant fenugreek (methi) or coriander seeds and sprinkle water gently.
• Learning Outcome: Demonstrates the 'Reuse' principle of waste management while greening home spaces.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear list of recycled materials 1.0 Mark
Step-by-step procedure explained 1.5 Marks
Environmental learning objective stated 0.5 Mark

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Urban Homelessness and Community Empathy

Like Chhotu, many migrant workers come to big cities in search of work and live under flyovers, in pipes, or in makeshift tin shacks without clean piped water or proper toilets.

Analytical Task: What basic civic facilities should city governments provide in night shelters (Rain Baseras) to help homeless people live with dignity?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
City governments should provide well-maintained night shelters (Rain Baseras) equipped with: (1) Clean, filtered potable drinking water taps. (2) Separate, hygienic restrooms with flushing toilets and running water. (3) Clean bedding, blankets in winter, and mosquito mesh windows. (4) Basic first-aid medical dispensary access. (5) Secure storage lockers so daily wage workers can keep their earnings and identification documents safe.
Rubric: Compassionate understanding of migration, clean sanitation, dignity, essential civic shelter amenities (4 Marks).
