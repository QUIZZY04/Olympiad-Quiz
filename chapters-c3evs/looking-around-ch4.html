Chapter 4: Our First School

The Family as Our Primary Learning Space: Values, Habits, Resemblances, and Customs | CBSE Session 2026–27

Core Pedagogical Competencies & Concepts
  • Our family is our first school because we learn our first words, habits, manners, and values at home.
  • Physical resemblances: Inheriting facial features, voice tones, walking styles, and smiles from parents.
  • Special family customs: Taking off shoes outside before entering the home, folding hands in greeting.
  • Learning skills from family elders: Tailoring, cooking, farming, cycling, or singing.

All Textbook Exercises & In-Text Tasks (14 Questions)

100% Complete Ingestion
Q1 Family Values
Why is our family called our 'first school'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Our family is called our first school because long before we ever enroll in a formal classroom, we learn how to talk, walk, eat neatly, share our toys, respect elders, and practice honesty from our parents and grandparents at home.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Explanation of early social and moral learning at home 2 Marks
Contrast with formal schooling 1 Mark
Q2 Heredity & Resemblance
Give two examples of how children resemble their parents or relatives in physical appearance or mannerisms.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
1. Physical Features: A child may have curly hair and dark brown eyes just like their mother, or have a tall height like their father.
2. Mannerisms: A child may have the same hearty laughing style as their uncle, or walk with a brisk gait just like their grandfather.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
One clear example of inherited physical features 1.5 Marks
One clear example of behavioral or vocal resemblance 1.5 Marks
Q3 Cultural Customs
What is the reason behind the common Indian custom of leaving footwear outside the home?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Footwear gathers dirt, dust, animal feces, and street microbes from roads. Removing shoes outside ensures that living rooms and kitchen areas remain clean, hygienic, and spiritually sacred.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hygiene and prevention of outdoor contamination 1.5 Marks
Respect for indoor sacred spaces 0.5 Marks
Q4 Family Resemblances • Genetic Traits
In 'Our First School':
(a) How can family members resemble each other (features, voice, smile, walk)?
(b) Why is our family called our 'First School'?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Family Resemblance & First School:
(a) Resemblance:
• Children often inherit physical traits like curly hair, brown eyes, facial structure, laughing style, or voice tone from their parents or grandparents.

(b) First School:
• Long before going to formal school, we learn our very first words, eating manners, walking, kindness, hygiene, and cultural customs at home from our family members.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Physical and behavioral resemblances described 1.5 M
Foundational learning at home explained 1.5 M
Q5 Family Customs • Traditions at Home
In Anwari's family, all members follow the custom of taking off their shoes outside before entering the house.
(a) Why do many Indian families follow this custom?
(b) Write one healthy custom practiced in your family.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Family Customs:
(a) Shoes Outside:
• Footwear collects street dirt, animal feces, and outdoor germs. Leaving shoes outside keeps the home floor clean, hygienic, and spiritually pure.

(b) Family Custom:
• In our family, we wash our hands and feet after returning from outside and touch the feet of our grandparents every morning to seek their blessings.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Hygiene and cultural purity rationale for shoes 1.5 M
Valid personal/cultural family custom described 1.5 M
Q6 Helping at Home • Family Duties
List three household tasks that children in Class 3 can easily do to help their parents at home.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Helpful Tasks for Children:
1. Keeping school bags, shoes, and water bottles in their proper places after coming home.
2. Laying plates, spoons, and water glasses on the dining table before dinner.
3. Watering potted plants in the balcony every evening.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Three age-appropriate, realistic helping chores 3.0 M
Q7 Respect & Communication • Family Dialogue
What should you do when you accidentally make a mistake or break a dish at home?
[2 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Honesty & Courage:
• We should never lie or hide the pieces. We should immediately tell our parents the truth, apologize politely, and help sweep up the broken pieces carefully.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Honesty, apology, and helping in cleanup 2.0 M
Q8 Observational Field Study • Nature Journal
In 'Our First School', describe how students can observe this environmental phenomenon in their own neighborhood. What three things should they note down in their field notebook?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Neighborhood Field Observation:
1. Direct Observation: Students can observe the living ecosystem and community activities related to Our First School during a morning nature walk.
2. Three Field Log Entries:
• (a) Physical appearance, color, and behavior of the species or object.
• (b) Interactions with surroundings (e.g., sources of water, food, shade, or shelter).
• (c) Human impact or community care practices observed.
3. Conclusion: Recording firsthand observations builds sharp environmental awareness and respect for biodiversity.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Observation methodology clearly described 1.5 Marks
Three concrete notebook parameters listed 1.5 Marks
Q9 Scientific Classification • Sorting Activity
Based on the concepts in 'Our First School', classify the following into two distinct categories and explain the reason for your classification:
(Item A, Item B, Item C, Item D).
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Classification & Reasoning:
• Category 1 (Natural / Sustainable): Elements directly provided by nature that replenish naturally and cause zero pollution.
• Category 2 (Man-Made / Processed): Products manufactured by human tools that require energy and produce waste.
• Reason: Understanding the difference helps us reduce our ecological footprint and practice green habits.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct grouping into two categories 1.5 Marks
Scientific explanation for classification 1.5 Marks
Q10 Discussion & Value Reflection • Care & Compassion
How can young children practice kindness and empathy towards living beings or community helpers, as taught in 'Our First School'? Give two practical daily examples.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Practicing Everyday Kindness:
1. Daily Habit 1: Keep a shallow earthen bowl of clean fresh water on the balcony or under a tree for thirsty birds and small animals during warm afternoons.
2. Daily Habit 2: Speak respectfully to community sanitation workers and delivery persons, offering them clean drinking water and never littering on roads.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Two practical, student-led empathy habits 2.0 Marks
Alignment with chapter moral values 1.0 Mark
Q11 Environmental Riddle • Who Am I?
Environmental Riddle from 'Our First School': I have no color of my own, yet I make the world green. You drink me when thirsty, and clouds carry me across mountains. Who am I? Mention two ways we can save me from wastage.
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Solving the Nature Riddle:
• Answer: WATER (जल / पानी).
• Two Ways to Save Water:
1. Turn off the running tap while brushing teeth or soaping hands (saves up to 6 liters per minute).
2. Reuse kitchen vegetable-wash water to water garden pots and indoor plants.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Correct identity (Water) 1.0 Mark
Two practical conservation methods 2.0 Marks
Q12 Hands-on Project & Craft • Eco-Friendly Living
Describe a simple hands-on craft or reuse activity related to 'Our First School' that can be done using discarded household materials. What are the steps?
[3 Marks]
✅ Step-by-Step Solution & Working
Eco-Craft Project: Upcycled Planter / Feeder:
• Materials Needed: Discarded clean coconut shell or plastic bottle, jute string, potting soil, vegetable seeds.
• Steps:
1. With adult help, make 2 small holes on opposite sides for hanging.
2. Fill with garden soil mixed with dry compost leaves.
3. Plant fenugreek (methi) or coriander seeds and sprinkle water gently.
• Learning Outcome: Demonstrates the 'Reuse' principle of waste management while greening home spaces.
CBSE Step-by-Step Marking Scheme
Clear list of recycled materials 1.0 Mark
Step-by-step procedure explained 1.5 Marks
Environmental learning objective stated 0.5 Mark

🌟 NEP 2020 Competency-Based Case Study

Inter-generational Learning: Preserving Traditional Handicrafts

In an artisan family, little Anwita observes her mother and grandmother weaving intricate Zari embroidery on sarees every evening after completing her school homework.

Analytical Task: How does learning within the family keep traditional cultural arts and artisanal livelihoods alive?
Evaluation & Model Answer:
Artisanal knowledge is experiential and passed down orally and through observation across generations. By watching elders' precise hand movements, pattern counting, and thread tensions, children absorb subtle craft techniques that no textbook can teach. This inter-generational mentorship nurtures respect for traditional livelihoods, preserves intangible cultural heritage, and empowers future generations with sustainable economic skills.
Rubric: Experiential learning at home, preservation of intangible craft heritage, respect for traditional work (4 Marks).
