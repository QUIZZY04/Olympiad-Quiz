Class 3 Math Olympiad Roadmap

Advanced Logic: Fraction Benchmarking and Elapsed Time Jumping.

🚀 The Logical Leap

Class 3 introduces **Fractions** and **4-Digit Numbers**. Success depends on "Number Benchmarking"—the ability to estimate and compare values rather than performing long, tedious calculations for every question.

🍕 Strategy: The Pizza Fraction Benchmark

Comparing fractions like 1/4 and 1/8 is easy if you imagine "Sharing a Pizza." The larger the denominator (bottom number), the smaller the slice.

Problem: Which is bigger, 1/3 or 1/6?
The Trick: Imagine 1 pizza shared by 3 friends versus 6 friends. If only 3 people share, your slice is much bigger!
Answer: 1/3.

⏰ Strategy: The Hour-Jump Method

For elapsed time (e.g., "What time is it 75 minutes after 2:30?"), counting by ones causes errors. Jump by full hours first, then "bridge" the remaining minutes to the nearest 60.

Example: 2:30 PM + 75 minutes.
1. Jump 60 minutes (1 hour) to reach 3:30.
2. You have 15 minutes left (75 - 60).
3. 3:30 + 15 = 3:45.
Answer: 3:45 PM.

📋 Professional Guidelines

1. Knuckle Trick: Use knuckles to instantly find months with 31 days. This saves critical time in calendar questions.
2. Scaling: If 3 toys cost $30, don't find the cost of 1; just recognize that 6 toys will cost exactly double ($60).
3. Face vs Place: Face value is the name (stays same), Place value is the address (changes with position).
