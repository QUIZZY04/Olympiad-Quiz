Class 3 Math Olympiad Tricks and Strategies

Learn powerful techniques to solve Class 3 Olympiad mathematics problems faster.

Understanding Class 3 Olympiad Mathematics

In Class 3, Olympiad mathematics introduces more advanced topics such as multiplication, division, fractions, geometry and logical number patterns. Students must begin developing strong problem solving skills.

Instead of solving problems using long methods, smart tricks and strategies can help students solve questions quickly during Olympiad exams.

Trick 1: Skip Counting for Multiplication

Skip counting helps students understand multiplication quickly. Instead of repeated addition, count numbers in steps.

Example: 4 × 6 Skip count by 4 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 Answer = 24

Trick 2: Multiply by 5 Shortcut

Multiplying by 5 can be done easily using a shortcut. Multiply the number by 10 and divide by 2.

Example: 5 × 18 10 × 18 = 180 180 ÷ 2 = 90 Answer = 90

Trick 3: Multiply by 9 Trick

To multiply a number by 9, multiply it by 10 and subtract the original number.

Example: 9 × 34 10 × 34 = 340 340 − 34 = 306 Answer = 306

Trick 4: Division Check Method

You can check your division answer by multiplying the quotient and divisor.

Example: 24 ÷ 6 = 4 Check: 4 × 6 = 24 So the answer is correct.

Trick 5: Number Pattern Recognition

Many Olympiad questions involve recognizing patterns in numbers.

Example: 3, 6, 9, 12, ? Pattern = +3 Answer = 15

Trick 6: Doubling and Halving Method

Multiplication problems can sometimes be simplified by doubling and halving numbers.

Example: 4 × 25 Double 25 → 50 Double again → 100 Answer = 100

Trick 7: Understanding Fractions

Fractions represent equal parts of a whole. Understanding fractions visually makes solving problems easier.

Example: 1/2 means one part out of two equal parts.

Trick 8: Estimation Strategy

Estimation helps eliminate wrong answers quickly.

Example: 198 + 102 Estimate 200 + 100 = 300 Actual answer = 300

Practice Tips for Students

  • Memorize multiplication tables up to 12.
  • Practice mental math daily.
  • Solve pattern problems regularly.
  • Practice division problems.
  • Understand fractions using diagrams.
