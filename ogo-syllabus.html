Preparation Strategy & Recommended Books

  • Understand the Exam Pattern: Familiarize yourself with the marking scheme. Ensure you pay attention to the logical reasoning component.
  • Focus on Logical Reasoning: This section often acts as a rank decider. Practice coding-decoding, blood relations, and spatial reasoning daily.

  • OGO official prep materials.
  • Reasoning & Aptitude books for extra practice.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is there negative marking in these Olympiads?

Generally, preliminary levels of Olympiads like OGO do not have negative marking. However, always verify with the official guidelines for specific advanced levels.

What is the Logical Reasoning Section?

The Logical Reasoning Section contains questions that test your analytical and critical thinking skills, ensuring holistic assessment of a student's cognitive abilities.
