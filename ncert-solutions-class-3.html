Frequently Asked Questions

Class 3 NCERT Solutions FAQs

Which textbooks are prescribed by CBSE for Class 3 in academic session 2026–27? ▾
For Class 3, schools across India are transitioning under the National Curriculum Framework (NCF-FS / NCF-SE 2023) to new textbooks: Maths Mela (Mathematics), Santoor (English), Veena (Hindi), and Our Wondrous World (The World Around Us / EVS). To ensure no student is left behind, OlympiadQuiz provides complete solutions for both the new NCF books and the classic CBSE textbooks (Math-Magic, Marigold, Rimjhim, and Looking Around).
Are the solutions available completely free without registration? ▾
Yes! All solutions on OlympiadQuiz are 100% free with no login walls, subscriptions, or credit card requirements. Students and parents can access them directly on smartphones, tablets, or laptops.
How do these solutions help in Olympiad exam preparation? ▾
Olympiad exams such as IMO, NSO, IEO, and UIEC test deep conceptual clarity derived from NCERT fundamentals. Our solutions break down the underlying mathematical principles, scientific inquiries, and grammatical rules, giving students the strong conceptual foundation needed to crack achiever-level Olympiad problems.
